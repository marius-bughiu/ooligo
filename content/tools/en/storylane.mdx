## What it is

Storylane is a demo automation platform: you capture your product once and publish it as a clickable demo on the website, in an email, inside a deal room, or in front of a prospect on a live call. It covers all three capture types in one product — screenshot, video, and HTML/CSS clones that behave like a sandbox — so marketing's website tour and the SE's leave-behind come from the same library. It is independent and says it has 5,000+ customer teams and more than 200,000 demos built. In December 2024 it acquired PreSkale, a presales management tool, and its product has since grown from demos into selling: presenter demos, deal intelligence, and RepX, a conversational AI SDR that runs on the website and pulls up demos mid-conversation.

## Why it shows up in RevOps stacks

- **One demo library for three teams.** Product marketing builds the website tour, sales sends a personalized copy after discovery, and presales runs a sandbox on the call. Personalization tokens swap in the prospect's name, logo, and data without anyone rebuilding the demo.
- **Demo engagement becomes pipeline data.** Lead forms, account reveal (company identification of anonymous viewers), and CRM sync push who watched what into [HubSpot](/en/tools/hubspot/) or [Salesforce](/en/tools/salesforce/). The HubSpot app supports workflows triggered on demo engagement, so a finished tour can enroll a contact in a sequence or alert the owner.
- **Wide GTM integration list.** Documented connectors include HubSpot, Salesforce, Dynamics 365, Freshsales, Marketo, Pardot, [Gong](/en/tools/gong/), [Outreach](/en/tools/outreach/), [Chili Piper](/en/tools/chili-piper/), [Calendly](/en/tools/calendly/), [Slack](/en/tools/slack/), [Intercom](/en/tools/intercom/), Clearbit, [6sense](/en/tools/6sense/), [Seismic](/en/tools/seismic/), Highspot, Segment, Google Analytics, [Zapier](/en/tools/zapier/), and webhooks.
- **Agents can build and send demos.** The Storylane MCP server (Starter plan and above, OAuth sign-in) lets [Claude](/en/tools/claude/) and [ChatGPT](/en/tools/chatgpt/) build, personalize, share, and report on demos without opening the app. In ChatGPT, write actions need Developer Mode, which is still in beta.

## Pricing

Storylane publishes list prices. Annual billing is 20% below monthly, and price does not scale with how many people view your demos.

- **Free — $0.** 1 seat, 1 live demo at a time, screenshot and video demos only, unlimited views.
- **Starter — $40/month billed annually** ($50 monthly), 1 seat plus $40 per extra seat. Unlimited demos, custom branding, HubSpot and Zapier, the MCP server, and 250 account reveals a month. No HTML capture.
- **Growth — $500/month billed annually** ($625 monthly), 5 seats plus $100 per extra seat. Adds the HTML editor, personalization tokens, A/B testing, 2,500 account reveals a month, and team workspaces. Hubs (deal rooms) cost extra.
- **Premium — $1,200/month billed annually** ($1,500 monthly), 10 seats. Adds sandbox and presenter demos, Hubs, deal intelligence, offline demos, multi-language, SSO, a white-label URL, and the Salesforce integration.
- **Enterprise — custom.** Adds API access, private demo links, and professional services.
- **RepX is priced separately.** Growth starts at $2,000/month for up to 10,000 monthly website visitors, Premium at $3,000/month for up to 40,000 visitors with video-avatar conversations and multiple agents; both include a Demo Suite license and a 30-day free trial.
- **What teams pay.** Vendr puts the median Storylane contract at $15,330 a year, in a $3,000 to $50,000+ range, with negotiated deals landing 15-25% below list. That median sits just above Premium list ($14,400 a year), so plan for Premium as soon as Salesforce or sandbox demos are on the requirements list.

## Best for

- Product marketing and demand-gen leaders at B2B SaaS companies who want a clickable demo on the website and a leave-behind for reps, from the same tool, on a HubSpot budget
- Presales and SE teams at mid-market software vendors who want sandbox demos and personalized follow-up without an engineering-built demo environment
- Not for teams whose product has to be shown with live data and real integrations (a demo environment or a real trial beats any capture), or for one-off tutorial GIFs, where a free screen-capture tool is enough

## Alternatives — and when to pick them instead

- **Navattic** — the rival in nearly every head-to-head. HTML capture is the only capture type on its paid plans, and Salesforce and Marketo arrive on Base at $500/month billed annually (5 seats) rather than on a $1,200 tier. Pick Navattic for a PLG website demo on Salesforce where the HTML demo is the whole job. Its $125/month Startup plan is limited to bootstrapped or seed-stage companies with 30 or fewer employees.
- **Arcade** — the widest free footprint (20,000+ companies, per company-profile data). Growth is $42.50/month billed annually for up to 10 seats but has no HTML capture, which Arcade reserves for Enterprise along with its native Salesforce integration. Pick Arcade when the demo is a polished screenshot or video tour for launches and social posts rather than a sales asset.
- **Supademo** — the fastest-growing entrant, ranked by G2 among its fastest-growing products; it launched its own AI Demo Agent in June 2026. Scale is $38 per creator a month billed annually, and HTML demos start on Growth at $350/month for 5 creators. Pick it when you want Storylane's feature set at a lower seat price and can live with a smaller integration list.
- **Walnut** — sales-led and enterprise-priced: a Vendr median of $17,000 a year across 36 purchases, with no free tier. Pick Walnut when SEs need heavy, data-edited sandbox demos across a large sales team and budget is not the constraint.
- **[Qualified](/en/tools/qualified/)** — if RepX is the reason you are looking, compare it against a dedicated AI SDR. Pick Qualified when Salesforce-native routing and pipeline attribution matter more than showing the product inside the chat.

## Watch-outs

- **Salesforce is a Premium feature.** Starter includes HubSpot and Zapier; the Salesforce integration first appears on Premium at $14,400 a year list. Guard: if Salesforce is your CRM, price Premium from the start, or confirm in writing that a Zapier or webhook sync meets your attribution needs before you buy Growth.
- **The plan decides the capture type.** Free and Starter publish screenshot and video demos only; HTML editing starts on Growth and sandbox demos on Premium. Guard: decide which capture type your buyer needs to see before shortlisting a tier, and build the hardest screen of your product during the trial.
- **Design control runs out on complex brands.** Customization depth is the complaint that recurs most in Storylane's G2 reviews. Guard: rebuild your most branded page as a pilot demo and have design sign off before you commit to an annual contract.
- **RepX is a second product and a second contract.** At $24,000 a year and up, it competes with the chat agent you may already run. Guard: use the 30-day trial against a baseline of meetings booked per 1,000 visitors, and never run two AI agents on the same page.
- **Agents can publish and share.** The MCP server has write access with the connected user's permissions. Guard: connect MCP with a creator seat scoped to a sandbox workspace, and keep publishing to the live website behind a human review in the editor.
- **Captured demos go stale when your UI changes.** HTML captures show whatever the product looked like on capture day. Guard: give every live demo a named owner and re-capture after each major UI release; retire any demo that has not been updated in two quarters.
