## What it is

Navattic is a no-code interactive demo platform. A Chrome extension captures your product's HTML and CSS, and the result is a clickable clone of the app that a prospect can use on your website, in an email, or through a shared link, with no login and no trial account. Paid plans build demos from HTML captures; the free Starter plan also allows one uploaded-media demo, and there is no video or GIF export. Every HTML demo therefore behaves like the product rather than a recording of it. Navattic says it has more than 1,000 customers and holds a 4.7/5 rating on G2 across roughly 824 reviews. It remains an independent, Y Combinator-backed company with no 2026 acquisition or funding round on record. Its 2026 product line has three parts: the demo platform, Copilot (AI demo building, voiceovers, and avatars), and Navattic Agents, conversational AI agents that guide and qualify website visitors and click through captured demos while they answer questions, sold as a separate product.

## Why it shows up in RevOps stacks

- **PLG website demos that behave like the product.** Because each demo is an HTML clone, a visitor can type into fields, open menus, and move between screens. Marketing puts it behind "Take a tour" next to "Book a demo" and reaches the self-serve evaluator who would never fill in a form.
- **Demo engagement becomes routing data.** Account identification (Base and above) names the companies viewing a demo. The integrations sync engagement to [HubSpot](/en/tools/hubspot/) Contacts and Companies and to [Salesforce](/en/tools/salesforce/) Leads, Contacts, and Accounts, checking for an existing account before creating one. Playbooks fire a [Slack](/en/tools/slack/) or email alert when a target account finishes a high-intent flow. Navattic routes its own product-qualified leads this way with [Default](/en/tools/default/), per a Default case study.
- **Salesforce on the $500 tier.** Navattic's pricing page lists Salesforce, Marketo, "and 25+ more" on Base, at $500/month billed annually for 5 seats. [Storylane](/en/tools/storylane/) charges the same $500 for its 5-seat Growth plan but holds Salesforce back until Premium at $1,200/month, and Arcade keeps native Salesforce on Enterprise.
- **Growth now covers the sales-call demo.** Growth adds sandbox demos, demo translation, the Gong, Salesloft, Outreach, Seismic, and Highspot connectors, and Launchpad tools for reps: requests and approvals, personal intro videos, and buyer-circle discovery. A team that bought Navattic for the website can now hand the same capture library to sales without a second vendor.
- **An MCP server with write access.** The pricing page lists the MCP server on Base; Navattic's docs say it is available "on the Startup plan and above". [Claude](/en/tools/claude/), [ChatGPT](/en/tools/chatgpt/), Gemini Enterprise, and Microsoft Copilot Studio connect over OAuth; [Cursor](/en/tools/cursor/), VS Code, and Codex use a personal access token with selectable scopes and a 7- to 180-day expiry. Agents can read analytics, visitor data, and Navattic Agents conversations, build and publish demos, and manage Launchpad share links. Growth adds "MCP suggested demos".

## Pricing

Navattic publishes list prices for Base and Growth on its pricing page (checked 2026-11-23). Annual billing is about 14-17% below monthly.

- **Starter — $0.** 1 seat, 1 demo (HTML or media), Starter Copilot, unlimited views, basic analytics.
- **Base — $500/month billed annually** ($600 monthly), 5 seats. Unlimited HTML demos; Salesforce, HubSpot, Marketo, and 25+ more integrations with unlimited playbooks; the MCP server; account identification, A/B testing, pipeline-impact reporting, premium AI voiceovers, 100 AI avatar minutes, and a dedicated CSM.
- **Growth — $1,200/month billed annually** ($1,400 monthly), 10 seats. Adds Gong, Salesloft, Outreach, Gmail, Outlook, Seismic, and Highspot, sandbox demos, demo translation, custom domains, SSO and directory sync, AI avatar cloning, and 300 avatar minutes. That is a 20% rise on the $1,000 annual rate Navattic published in mid-2026.
- **Enterprise — custom.** Adds offline demos, HTML capture translation, custom permissions, audit logs, professional services, and priority support.
- **Startup — quote only.** For bootstrapped and seed-stage teams of 30 or fewer. It was last published at $125/month billed annually; the page now says "Contact us for pricing".
- **Navattic Agents — quote only.** Priced on the number of conversations and agents, and includes access to the Base plan.
- **What teams pay.** Vendr puts the median Navattic contract at $8,500 a year across 99 purchases, in a $5,000 to $18,000 range, with 15-30% below list achievable on annual prepay and multi-year terms. That median sits between Base ($6,000 a year at list) and Growth ($14,400). Budget for Base if Salesforce is your CRM, and for Growth once sandboxes or sales-engagement sync are on the requirements list.

## Best for

- Product marketing and growth leaders at PLG or hybrid B2B SaaS companies who want a hands-on HTML demo on the website feeding HubSpot or Salesforce routing, with 5-10 demo builders
- RevOps and demand-gen teams who need the Salesforce integration on a $6,000-a-year list budget rather than a $14,400 one
- Not for teams that want video or GIF tours for launch posts and social channels (Navattic exports no video), not for enterprise presales orgs where SEs build data-edited sandboxes for every prospect across a large team (look at Consensus or [Walnut](/en/tools/walnut/)), and not for products whose UI changes weekly, because HTML captures do not update themselves

## Alternatives — and when to pick them instead

- **[Storylane](/en/tools/storylane/)** — the rival in nearly every head-to-head, independent, with 5,000+ customer teams. It covers screenshot, video, and HTML capture in one library, starts at $40/month billed annually, and sells an AI SDR (RepX) from $2,000/month. At 10 seats its Premium plan and Navattic Growth now share the same $1,200/month annual list. Pick Storylane when marketing needs screenshot or video tours and sales needs presenter demos from the same tool. Pick Navattic when HTML website demos are the core job and Salesforce has to be on a 5-seat plan.
- **Arcade** — the widest free footprint (20,000+ companies, per company-profile data), and now also selling an AI video generator as a separate product. Demo Growth is $50 per seat a month, 15% less on annual billing, for up to 10 seats, with MP4 and GIF export; HTML capture and native Salesforce are Enterprise-only. Pick Arcade when the demo is a polished screenshot or video tour for launches and social posts rather than a pipeline asset.
- **[Supademo](/en/tools/supademo/)** — the fastest-growing entrant, ranked by G2 among its fastest-growing products, with a metered AI Demo Agent. HTML demos start on Business at $199/month billed annually for 1 creator, and Growth at $350/month covers 5 creators with sandbox demos, against $500 for Navattic Base and $1,200 for Navattic Growth. Pick Supademo when you want HTML and sandbox demos at a lower price and can live with a shorter integration list.
- **[Consensus](/en/tools/consensus/)** — presales-first: video and interactive demos sent to the buying committee, with analytics on which stakeholders watched what. It agreed to acquire Peel, a conversational demo agent, in April 2026, and no longer publishes list prices; its Vendr median is $53,296 a year. Pick Consensus when the buyer is an enterprise committee and SE capacity, not website conversion, is the bottleneck.
- **[Walnut](/en/tools/walnut/)** — sales-led and enterprise-priced: Ignite at $575/month billed annually and a Vendr median of $17,000 a year, with no free tier. Pick Walnut when SEs need data-edited sandbox demos across a large sales team.

## Watch-outs

- **Growth went up 20%.** The 10-seat plan moved from $1,000 to $1,200 a month on annual billing, and to $1,400 monthly. Guard: if a renewal quote is priced off the new list, bring the $8,500 Vendr median and a competing Storylane or Supademo quote to the call, and push for a multi-year lock; Vendr reports 15-30% off list on annual and multi-year terms.
- **One capture type, no video.** Paid plans output HTML clones only. Guard: if the same tour must also run as a video on LinkedIn, a GIF in a launch email, or a clip in the help center, budget a second tool (Arcade or Supademo) instead of forcing Navattic into that job.
- **Captures go stale when your UI ships.** An HTML clone shows the product as it looked on capture day, and re-capture is manual. Guard: give every live demo a named owner, re-capture after each major UI release, and retire any demo untouched for two quarters.
- **The Startup plan is quote-only and ends at 30 people.** It no longer carries a published price, and the next published tier is Base at $6,000 a year. Guard: get the Startup rate in writing, and if you expect to raise or pass 30 employees inside the term, negotiate the Base price at signing.
- **Agents are early, and the MCP server can publish.** Navattic has published no customer results for Navattic Agents, which are priced per conversation, and the MCP server's Demo Publishing scope can publish and archive demos. Guard: pilot Agents on one page against a baseline of meetings booked per 1,000 demo visitors with a conversation cap in the contract, and grant MCP tokens only the read scopes (Analytics, Demo Browsing) until a human reviews what agents change.
