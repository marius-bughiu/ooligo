## What it is

Beamery is the enterprise talent CRM positioned as the strategic-talent-acquisition platform for global Fortune 500 organizations. London-headquartered, it differentiates on the Talent Graph — an AI-native data layer that represents people, skills, organizations, and movements in a navigable graph rather than a flat database. Spans candidate sourcing, talent marketing, internal mobility, and workforce planning.

## Why it shows up in Recruiting stacks

- **Talent Graph data model.** People as nodes with skill, organization, and movement edges — enables queries that flat CRMs can't (find candidates whose career trajectory matches our top-performing employees; identify internal candidates for external roles).
- **Internal mobility first-class.** Beamery treats internal candidates as a pipeline category alongside external — supports the strategic shift many enterprises are making toward internal-mobility-first hiring.
- **Built for global scale.** Multi-region data residency, GDPR-native, multi-language candidate-facing surfaces. Fits the deployment profile of global enterprises that find US-built talent CRMs awkward outside North America.

## Pricing

- **Custom only.** Per-employee or per-recruiter pricing depending on packaging; effective entry point in the high-five figures annually for mid-market scaling into seven figures for global enterprise.
- **Module packaging.** Beamery Recruit (sourcing/CRM), Beamery Marketing (talent marketing), Beamery Mobility (internal mobility), Beamery Plan (workforce planning) all separately licensed.
- Implementation typically 4-9 months — partner-led for global rollouts.

## Best for

- Global Fortune 500 enterprises with multi-region talent acquisition complexity
- Companies pursuing internal-mobility-first hiring strategies
- Organizations whose CRM-side talent investment exceeds their ATS-side investment (i.e., long-cycle relationship management matters more than funnel throughput)

## Watch-outs

- Heavyweight to deploy — not the right call below ~5,000 employees or for companies that don't have an enterprise ATS to pair it with
- Pricing is significantly higher than [Gem](/en/tools/gem/) or [Findem](/en/tools/findem/) for the CRM-only use case
- AI-augmented sourcing depth depends on the Talent Graph being well-populated with the customer's specific talent context — implementation work, not turnkey
- For mid-market and growth-stage companies, [Gem](/en/tools/gem/) typically delivers similar value at much lower cost
