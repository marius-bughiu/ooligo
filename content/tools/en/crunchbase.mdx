## What it is

Crunchbase is a private-company database. It started in 2007 as TechCrunch's list of startups, was spun out with Emergence Capital backing in 2015, and is still independent. Its core data is the record of who raised what from whom: funding rounds, investors, acquisitions, headcount, leadership and news, on what its own pages call more than 4 million private companies (its October 2026 release says "more than six million companies"). The data comes from analysts, news and filings, venture partners and its own users.

Since 2025 it has been repositioning from a lookup site into a prediction product. Three 2026 releases matter for GTM teams:

- **Market Insights (June 2, 2026).** Over 5,100 "micro-industries", AI-generated competitor lists with reasons, and a signal that labels segments as emerging, growing or declining.
- **Crunchbase MCP (July 21, 2026).** A hosted MCP server at `https://mcp.crunchbase.com` with OAuth sign-in. It exposes company, person, investor, funding, acquisition and prediction data, plus list management, to [Claude](/en/tools/claude/), [ChatGPT](/en/tools/chatgpt/), [Gemini](/en/tools/gemini/), [Cursor](/en/tools/cursor/) and other MCP clients. Every account can connect; how much data you can pull depends on your tier's data-token allowance.
- **Crunchbase AI (October 6, 2026).** The umbrella name for the MCP, the ChatGPT app and the API, sold as a "headless" data layer for agents.

## Why it shows up in RevOps stacks

The question this page answers: is Crunchbase a real GTM data source for account scoring and trigger events now, or still just a funding lookup?

**It is a trigger-event source, not a contact source.** A new funding round, an acquisition, a leadership hire or a headcount jump is a reason to call, and Crunchbase records those events for private companies better than general B2B databases do. Its predictions (likely to raise, likely to be acquired, growth scores) are a reasonable input to an account score for startup and scale-up markets. Crunchbase claims its models predicted 84% of funding rounds and 73% of acquisitions correctly. It does not publish how "correct" is measured, so treat those numbers as marketing until you backtest them on your own closed-won accounts.

**What it does not give you:** verified emails and direct dials at scale, technographics, or intent. You still need [ZoomInfo](/en/tools/zoominfo/), [Apollo](/en/tools/apollo/) or an enrichment waterfall to reach the people at the accounts Crunchbase flags.

**The rule.** If you sell to venture-backed companies and "just raised" or "just got acquired" is your best timing signal, Crunchbase earns a place in the stack. If your market is mid-market or enterprise companies that rarely raise venture money, a funding feed adds little and you should spend the budget on contacts or intent.

## Pricing reality

Crunchbase does not post a full price table, and we could not load its checkout page directly. The figures below come from 2026 third-party price checks and Vendr's deal data:

- **Pro: $99 a month billed monthly.** Annual list is reported at about $79 a month ($948 a year per seat), with $49 a month offered to new users for the first year. Pro covers AI search, growth and heat scores, alerts, a tracker board and exports of up to 2,000 rows a month. It does not include predictions.
- **Business: sales-led.** Third-party checks put it near $199 a month billed yearly. It adds the prediction models, CRM integrations with auto-enrichment and exports of up to 5,000 rows a month.
- **API and data access: quoted.** No public price.

**What teams pay:** a solo founder or seller pays **about $600 to $1,200 a year** for one Pro seat. Team and API contracts are a different scale. Vendr's data from 71 purchases puts the **median at about $20,000 a year**, with a range of roughly $4,400 to $51,000.

## Best for

A **RevOps lead or SDR manager at a company that sells to venture-backed startups and scale-ups** (dev tools, fintech infrastructure, recruiting and HR software for growing teams) who wants new funding rounds and acquisitions to route straight to reps. The scoped case where Crunchbase wins: a weekly Business-tier export or MCP query of Series A to C companies in your micro-industries that raised in the last 30 days, scored and pushed into [Salesforce](/en/tools/salesforce/) or [HubSpot](/en/tools/hubspot/) as a trigger for an SDR sequence.

## Not for

Teams whose buyers are established, profitable companies, or teams that need contact data first. Crunchbase's people records are leadership-level and thin compared with a contact database.

## Versus the alternatives

- **ZoomInfo**: the market-share leader in B2B data, with funding events as one filter among contacts, org charts and intent. Pick ZoomInfo when reps need verified contacts and the funding signal is secondary. Its median contract is about $33,500 a year (Vendr). See [ZoomInfo](/en/tools/zoominfo/).
- **Apollo**: the other high-share option, a contact database and sequencer with funding filters built in, on a self-serve plan. Pick Apollo when one tool must find, contact and sequence. See [Apollo](/en/tools/apollo/).
- **Crustdata**: the fastest-growing entrant we cover for agent-first company data. It returns funding, headcount and hiring signals over an API and an MCP server, with webhook "Watchers" for changes. Pick Crustdata when an agent or workflow, not a person, consumes the data and you want event webhooks. See [Crustdata](/en/tools/crustdata/).
- **People Data Labs**: published per-record prices from $98 a month. Pick PDL when you need bulk company and person enrichment in code and funding history is a side field. See [People Data Labs](/en/tools/people-data-labs/).

If you only need funding data as one column in a longer enrichment flow, check whether your existing enrichment tool, such as [Clay](/en/tools/clay/), already returns funding fields before you buy another seat.

## Watch-outs

- **The January 2026 breach.** ShinyHunters claimed more than 2 million records and published a leak archive after a voice-phishing attack on employee SSO, and Crunchbase confirmed a breach (SecurityWeek, Security Affairs). Researchers reported personal data and contracts in the leak. **Guard:** before signing, ask for the incident report and the remediation steps, and keep Crunchbase out of systems that hold your own customer data until security review signs off.
- **Prediction accuracy is self-reported.** **Guard:** export a year of predictions for accounts you already know, compare them with what actually happened, and only weight the score in your model if it beats your current signals.
- **Predictions and CRM sync sit in Business, not Pro.** **Guard:** decide which tier you need before the trial ends. If the use case is CRM triggers, budget for Business from day one.
- **MCP usage is metered in data tokens, and allowances per tier are not published.** Lookups cost 10 tokens per returned field, and requests stop when the balance runs out. **Guard:** check your balance (a zero-token call) before connecting an agent that loops over large account lists, and get the allowance in writing.
