## What it is

Phenom is the talent experience platform (TXP) positioned as a layer above the [ATS](/en/learn/ats-vs-recruiting-crm/) — owning the candidate-facing surfaces (career site, application flow, candidate portal) plus the AI-augmented recommendation and personalization across all of them. Phenom acquired SmashFly in 2021 to extend into talent marketing; the result is the most comprehensive talent-experience platform serving the enterprise market.

## Why it shows up in Recruiting stacks

- **Personalized career site.** Each candidate sees role recommendations based on their profile, browsing behavior, and inferred skills. Materially improves application conversion vs static career sites.
- **Internal mobility surface.** Same recommendation engine serves internal employees looking at internal roles — supports the internal-mobility-first hiring shift many enterprises are pursuing.
- **AI-augmented across the surface.** Chatbot for application support, AI matching for job recommendations, AI-augmented sourcing, AI-driven candidate engagement campaigns.

## Pricing

- **Custom only.** Per-employee pricing typical; effective entry point in the mid-six figures annually for enterprise.
- **Module packaging.** Phenom Hire (TXP core), Phenom Mobility (internal mobility), Phenom Talent Marketing (formerly SmashFly), Phenom Studio (chatbot) all separately licensed.
- Implementation typically 6-12 months for enterprise deployments.

## Best for

- Enterprise organizations (5,000+ employees) where the candidate-facing experience is a competitive differentiator
- Consumer-brand hiring profiles (retail, hospitality, healthcare networks) where the career site is part of the brand surface
- Companies pursuing internal-mobility-first hiring strategies at scale

## Watch-outs

- Heavyweight to deploy and expensive — not the right call below ~5,000 employees
- Compete head-to-head with [Beamery](/en/tools/beamery/) on enterprise talent CRM and with [Eightfold](/en/tools/eightfold/) on talent intelligence — choice depends on which capability is the primary need
- Personalization quality depends on data-richness of candidate and job records; thin data produces poor recommendations
- For mid-market companies, [SmartRecruiters](/en/tools/smartrecruiters/) plus [Gem](/en/tools/gem/) typically delivers similar capability at significantly lower cost
