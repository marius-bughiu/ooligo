## What it is

QuickBooks Online (QBO) is Intuit's cloud general ledger for small businesses, and the default accounting system for US companies before they need an ERP. It handles invoicing, bills, bank feeds, payroll add-ons, basic inventory and standard financial reports. Intuit (NASDAQ: INTU) sells it in four tiers: Simple Start, Essentials, Plus and Advanced. Above Advanced sits Intuit Enterprise Suite, a separate multi-entity product.

This page covers QBO as the endpoint other ops tools post to. Twelve tools on ooligo list it as an integration, including [QuotaPath](/en/tools/quotapath/), [Nue](/en/tools/nue/), [PandaDoc](/en/tools/pandadoc/), [Clio](/en/tools/clio/), [LawPay](/en/tools/lawpay/) and [Smokeball](/en/tools/smokeball/). The question it answers is what "syncs to QuickBooks" means in practice, and whether QBO is still the cheap option.

## What actually flows into it

"Syncs to QuickBooks" usually means one of three things, and vendors rarely say which:

- **Invoices and payments.** CPQ and billing tools ([Nue](/en/tools/nue/), [Chargebee](/en/tools/chargebee/), [Stripe](/en/tools/stripe/)) create customers, invoices and payments in QBO. Legal-billing tools such as [Clio](/en/tools/clio/) and [PracticePanther](/en/tools/practicepanther/) push invoices and operating-account payments. Trust (IOLTA) ledgers usually stay in the practice-management tool, and QBO gets summary entries.
- **Payouts as bills or journal entries.** Commission tools like [QuotaPath](/en/tools/quotapath/) post approved payouts as journal entries or bills, mapped to an expense account. Payroll still pays the rep.
- **Read-only reporting pulls.** Tools that read revenue or AR back out for dashboards.

Classes and locations, the fields most integrations use to tag revenue by segment or office, start at the Plus tier. QBO has no native ASC 606 schedules below Advanced, so subscription companies usually run revenue recognition in a billing tool.

On the AI side, Intuit has added agents to QBO for bookkeeping, payments, finance and customer management since 2025. It signed a multi-year partnership with Anthropic in February 2026 that put a QuickBooks connector inside [Claude](/en/tools/claude/), US customers only at last check. A separate deal with OpenAI, worth over $100M and announced November 2025, covers its apps in [ChatGPT](/en/tools/chatgpt/). Intuit also publishes an open-source QBO MCP server for developers.

## Pricing reality

Intuit raised renewal prices from August 1, 2026. Most independent trackers now list Simple Start at $38/month (1 user), Essentials at $85 (3 users), Plus at $140 (5 users) and Advanced at $340 (25 users). Earlier 2026 baselines differ by source, so confirm on intuit.com before quoting. Plus went up about 22% and Advanced about 24% versus the $115 and $275 widely listed before August.

Real-world cost is higher than list. Payroll is a separate subscription. Bill-pay and payment processing carry their own fees. Advanced is where most companies with a dozen finance and ops users land, so budget about $4,100/year before add-ons. Intuit Enterprise Suite has no public price. Consultancy estimates run about $8,000/year for one entity and $12,000-$15,000 for two to five.

App developers pay too. Under the Intuit App Partner Program, data-in calls are free but data-out ("CorePlus") calls are metered beyond 500,000 credits/month on the free Builder tier. Integration vendors may pass that cost through.

## Best for

A finance lead or RevOps lead at a single-entity US company, or a small-to-mid law firm, that needs CRM, CPQ, commission or legal-billing tools to post into one ledger an outside accountant already knows.

**Not for you if** you run several legal entities that need consolidation, need ASC 606 schedules across thousands of contracts, or have more than 25 people who need ledger access. Those are the signals for Intuit Enterprise Suite, [Sage Intacct](/en/tools/sage-intacct/) or [NetSuite](/en/tools/netsuite/).

## Versus the alternatives

**Xero** is the other cloud ledger with real market share, at about 4.6 million subscribers in its H1 FY2026 results. US plans run about $27-$97/month after an October 2026 increase, with unlimited users on every plan. Pick Xero when you're outside the US, have many occasional users, or your accountant works in Xero. Pick QBO when your integrations list it first, which in US RevOps and legal tools they almost always do.

**Rillet** is the fastest-growing entrant, with a $100M Series C at a $1B valuation in August 2026. It is an AI-native ledger for venture-backed SaaS companies, built around revenue recognition and a faster close. Pick it when your revenue is subscription and you're about to outgrow QBO anyway. **Puzzle** and **Digits** sell AI-native ledgers to startups at the small end. In September 2026 Puzzle agreed to sell its accounting-firm business to Accrual and kept the direct product.

Move to [Sage Intacct](/en/tools/sage-intacct/) or [NetSuite](/en/tools/netsuite/) when multi-entity consolidation or ASC 606 at volume becomes the bottleneck.

## Watch-outs

- **"Syncs to QuickBooks" hides one-way, summary-only connectors.** Many tools push invoices but never read payments back, so the CRM shows deals unpaid that finance has collected. *Guard:* before buying, ask each vendor for its object list (customers, invoices, payments, journal entries) and the direction of each. Test a refund and a partial payment in a QBO sandbox.
- **Renewal prices rose in 2026, and auto-renew locks in the new rate.** Some trackers report an earlier spring adjustment as well as the August one. *Guard:* check your renewal date now. If Advanced at $340 is close to Intuit Enterprise Suite or Xero cost for your user count, price both before the renewal date.
- **Your accounting firm's tooling is changing.** Intuit is retiring QuickBooks Online Accountant in favor of Intuit Accountant Suite, moving firms in cohorts through 2026. *Guard:* ask your outside bookkeeper when their migration lands, and don't schedule a chart-of-accounts change or integration cutover in the same month.
- **The Claude connector and MCP server can write, not just read.** One tester found Intuit's MCP server exposes create, update and delete tools by default. *Guard:* connect AI clients through a QBO user with the narrowest role available. Keep a human approval step before anything posts to the ledger.
