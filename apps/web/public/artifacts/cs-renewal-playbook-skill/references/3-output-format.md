# Renewal review worksheet

- Account, as-of date and confirmed renewal date
- Dated evidence and the source ID for each statement
- Calculation showing comparable units
- Review priority from the team's rubric and why it applies
- Known facts, open questions and who can resolve each question
- Actions with an owner, due date and completion evidence
- Condition and date for escalation to the CS lead
- CSM approval before creating a task or contacting anyone

A reader should be able to recompute the figures and trace each action to an observation. Evaluate a real trial by recording preparation time, review time, corrected facts and which actions the CSM actually approved.
