## What it is

HappyFox is a ticketing-first help desk from HappyFox Inc., a privately held, founder-led vendor run by CEO Shalin Jain. The core product turns email, web forms, chat, social and phone into tickets with categories, SLAs, canned actions, smart rules and a self-service knowledge base. Around that core it now sells a family of separately priced modules: HappyFox AI (agent copilot and AI resolution), Autopilot (autonomous AI agents billed per successful action), a Chatbot billed per resolution, Workflows, Business Intelligence, a Service Desk for internal IT, and Assist AI for employee support inside Slack and Microsoft Teams.

It is the quiet option in this market. It rarely tops a review roundup, but it shows up on r/sysadmin and mid-market shortlists as the help desk that is cheaper and simpler to run than [Zendesk](/en/tools/zendesk/), with a published price list for every plan.

One thing to clear up before you shortlist it. In 2026 a single trade-press item reported that HappyFox had absorbed Thena, the Slack-native B2B support startup, and that Thena's standalone app would be retired. On 17 November 2026 we could not confirm it: happyfox.com and the HappyFox newsroom do not mention Thena, and thena.ai still sells its own Standard ($79) and Enterprise ($119) seats with live sign-up. If you are a Thena customer planning a migration, get the answer from Thena in writing before acting on the rumour.

## Where it fits

- **Mid-market email and portal support.** HappyFox's strength is a rules-heavy ticket queue: category-based routing, SLA escalations, smart rules, and asset fields on Pro. Teams of 10 to 60 agents handling B2B or B2C email support get most of what Zendesk Suite Professional does at a lower seat price.
- **Teams that outgrow per-seat pricing.** The unlimited-agent plans are unusual. Few mid-market help desks publish a flat monthly price where adding agents costs nothing and you pay for ticket volume instead.
- **Slack and Teams, with a limit.** Help Desk lists Slack, Microsoft Teams and Teams meeting integrations from the Team plan up, so agents can get notified and collaborate in chat. That is not shared-channel customer support of the kind [Pylon](/en/tools/pylon/) and [Unthread](/en/tools/unthread/) are built for, and Assist AI is aimed at internal IT and HR requests, not customers.

## Pricing reality

Published on happyfox.com and checked 17 November 2026. Help Desk is per agent per month (monthly / annual / two-year prepaid):

- **Basic**: $29 / $24 / $21, capped at 5 agents
- **Team**: $69 / $49 / $39
- **Pro**: $119 / $99 / $89, the tier HappyFox marks as most popular
- **Enterprise Pro**: quote only

The unlimited-agent plans are flat monthly prices on annual billing with a yearly ticket allowance: **Growth $1,999** (20,000 tickets, $0.03 per extra ticket), **Scale $3,999** (150,000), **Scale Plus $5,999** (300,000), and Ultimate by quote (1,000,000). HappyFox's own FAQ points teams above about 20 to 25 agents toward them, and the arithmetic agrees: Growth equals about 20 Pro agents or 41 Team agents.

AI costs extra. **HappyFox AI** sits on top of the help desk plan at $14 (Standard), $29 (Premium) or $39 (Enterprise) per agent per month, with monthly caps on copilot interactions and AI resolutions. Autopilot bills from 2 cents per successful action and the Chatbot $0.33 per resolution.

Real numbers: a 10-agent team on Team pays $5,880 a year. A 25-agent team on Pro pays $29,700; add HappyFox AI Premium for everyone and it reaches about $38,400. The same 25 agents on Growth pay $23,988 before AI, as long as they stay under 20,000 tickets a year. Every annual Help Desk subscription includes free white-glove migration, which is worth real money when leaving Zendesk.

## Best for

Support operations leads running a 10-60 agent email and portal help desk who want SLA and routing depth near Zendesk's without Zendesk's add-on stack, and teams with many occasional agents who can use the flat unlimited-agent plans.

## Versus the alternatives

- **[Zendesk](/en/tools/zendesk/)**: the market-share leader. Pick it when you need the widest marketplace, mature voice, or Zendesk's AI agents at scale, and can budget north of $200 per agent once add-ons land.
- **[Freshdesk](/en/tools/freshdesk/)**: the other high-share option, with a free tier and Pro at $55 per agent on annual billing. Pick it when you also run Freshservice or want a cheaper per-seat entry than HappyFox Pro.
- **[Zoho Desk](/en/tools/zoho-desk/)**: the price floor at $7 to $40 per agent. Pick it when budget decides and you already run Zoho apps.
- **[Pylon](/en/tools/pylon/)**: the fastest-growing entrant in B2B support. Pick it when your customers talk to you in shared Slack or Teams channels, which HappyFox does not handle as customer conversations.
- **[Help Scout](/en/tools/help-scout/)**: pick it for a small team that values a clean shared inbox over a configurable rules engine.

## Watch-outs

- **The per-agent price is not the bill.** AI, Workflows, BI, web messaging and the Chatbot are separate products with their own meters. The guard: price the help desk plan, HappyFox AI tier, Workflows and Chatbot as separate line items for your agent count and ticket volume before comparing against Zendesk or Freshdesk.
- **Unlimited-agent plans move the risk to ticket volume.** Growth's 20,000 tickets a year is about 1,670 a month; a spike or a spam-heavy inbox eats it fast. The guard: pull 12 months of ticket counts, size to the peak, and ask whether HappyFox AI is billed per agent on unlimited plans before signing.
- **Not built for shared-channel B2B support.** Slack and Teams here mean agent notifications and internal assist, not customer conversations in Slack Connect. The guard: if more than a small share of your support happens in customer channels, shortlist Pylon or Unthread, and treat the Thena report as unconfirmed until either company publishes it.
