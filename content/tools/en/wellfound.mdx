## What it is

Wellfound (formerly AngelList Talent, rebranded 2022) is the dominant job platform for early-stage and growth-stage startups — both as a candidate-discovery channel for startup-curious candidates and as a lightweight ATS for founder-led hiring at companies that haven't yet adopted a full ATS. Roughly 25,000 startups actively hire on Wellfound; 8M+ candidates have profiles.

## Why it shows up in Recruiting stacks

- **The startup-hiring channel.** Candidates explicitly looking for startup roles browse Wellfound rather than LinkedIn or generic job boards. Strongest fit for hiring engineers, designers, and PMs interested in early-stage opportunities.
- **Free for hiring.** Posting jobs is free; Wellfound monetizes through paid promotion and the candidate-side. Material cost advantage for resource-constrained startups.
- **Pre-built candidate profiles with startup-relevant signal.** Profiles include startup interests, role preferences, salary expectations, and compensation transparency in ways LinkedIn doesn't surface.

## Pricing

- **Free** — basic job posting, applicant tracking
- **Recruit Pro** — $349/month, advanced search, candidate outreach, branded company page
- **Custom** — for higher-volume hiring teams
- Pricing economics dramatically favorable vs [LinkedIn Recruiter](/en/tools/linkedin-recruiter/) for startup hiring

## Best for

- Pre-Series-B startups hiring 5-25 roles per year
- Founder-led hiring at companies without dedicated recruiters
- Engineering, product, and design hiring at startups

## Watch-outs

- Wrong scope for established mid-market and enterprise hiring — candidate population is startup-oriented
- Candidate quality varies — high signal for startup-curious candidates, lower signal for general talent searches
- Less mature ATS functionality than [Workable](/en/tools/workable/) or [Ashby](/en/tools/ashby/) — fine as a primary platform for early-stage but typically replaced by full ATS at Series B+
- Outside the US, candidate inventory is sparser; works best for US-based startup hiring
