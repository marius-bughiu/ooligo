## What it is

PracticePanther is a cloud practice management platform for solo and small law firms: matters, contacts, calendaring, time and expense tracking, invoicing, a client portal, document templates and task workflows, with its own payment processor (PantherPayments) built in. Since April 2026 its top plan also includes PantherAccounting Plus, a native trust and operating accounting suite with a general ledger, a three-way reconciliation wizard and Plaid bank feeds. Of the two small-firm platforms that publish every price, it is the one that puts trust and operating accounting inside a plan instead of selling it as an add-on.

It is a Miami company founded in 2012 and owned by Paradigm, the legal-software group formed by Alpine Investors' ASG. Francisco Partners made a strategic growth investment in Paradigm in October 2021, when the group also ran Bill4Time and MerusCase and served 11,000 firms. Paradigm bought trust-accounting startup TrustBooks in 2022, and TrustBooks' co-founder is on the PantherAccounting Plus team. PracticePanther ships no generative AI of its own: its 2026 release notes cover accounting, billing analysis and bank-transaction batching, and nothing else.

## Why it shows up in Legal Ops stacks

- **Accounting is inside the subscription, not bolted on.** PantherAccounting Plus blocks a client's trust balance from going below zero, forces every trust transaction onto a client and matter, keeps a bank ledger and a client-matter ledger side by side, and produces P&L, balance sheet, cash flow and trial balance reports. LawNext reports the beta ran for over a year across firms in every U.S. state. A firm that today pays for practice management plus QuickBooks plus a trust add-on can drop to one bill.
- **Every tier has a number on the website.** Four plans, each priced per user, with a 7-day trial and no credit card required. Clio publishes only its entry tier; Smokeball publishes none.
- **Payments are flat-rate.** PantherPayments charges 2.95% on all cards with no per-transaction fee, 1% on eChecks, and $0 a month, and fees are never debited from trust. Card-brand network fees pass through at month end where they apply.

## Pricing reality

practicepanther.com/pricing, checked 2026-10-09, per user per month:

- **Solo — $49 billed annually, $59 monthly.** Matter and contact management, time and expense tracking, client portal, unlimited storage, PantherPayments, document templates, task and event workflows, internal chat.
- **Essential — $69 annually, $79 monthly.** Adds custom fields, custom security roles, invoice-read alerts and multiple bank accounts.
- **Business — $89 annually, $99 monthly.** Adds native 2-way texting, intake forms, unlimited e-signature, LEDES billing and the attorney revenue report.
- **Business Pro — $114 annually, $124 monthly.** Adds PantherAccounting Plus: trust and operating accounting, reconciliation, general ledger, AR and expense management.

Annual billing saves $120 per user per year. In practice a solo lawyer on Solo pays $588 a year, a five-lawyer firm on Business pays $5,340, and the same firm on Business Pro pays $6,840. For comparison, five lawyers on MyCase Advanced with its $39 accounting add-on pay $10,140 a year, so PracticePanther's accounting-inclusive tier comes in about $3,300 cheaper at that size. Calendar rules need a separate LawToolBox subscription. Payment processing is the other real cost: a firm collecting $400,000 a year by card pays about $11,800 at 2.95% before any network fees.

## Best for

- Managing partners and office managers at 1-15 lawyer firms who want billing, payments, trust accounting and operating books in one system at a price they can read off a web page, and who do not need AI inside the matter today.
- Firms paying a bookkeeper to reconcile trust in QuickBooks every month — Business Pro is built to replace that loop.
- **Not for** in-house legal teams (this is firm-side software built on time and trust), firms that want AI drafting or matter Q&A in the core platform, or plaintiff firms running thousands of templated matters.

## Versus the alternatives

- **vs [Clio](/en/tools/clio/).** The market-share leader. After its vLex acquisition, Clio puts legal research and drafting inside the matter, but only Starter ($49 per user per month) has a published price; Core, Signature and Elite are quote-only. Pick Clio when AI research and drafting are part of what you are buying or the firm is heading past 30 lawyers; pick PracticePanther when a fixed published price and built-in accounting matter more.
- **vs [MyCase](/en/tools/mycase/).** The other price-transparent option: $50 / $100 / $130 per user per month annually, with 8am IQ AI on Pro and Advanced and accounting as a $39 add-on. Pick MyCase when you want matter-level AI and OCR; pick PracticePanther when trust and operating accounting in the base price outweigh AI.
- **vs [Smokeball](/en/tools/smokeball/).** Quote-only, strongest on automatic time capture and Word document automation, with its Archie AI as an add-on. Pick it for document-heavy practices willing to go through a sales process.
- **vs [Filevine](/en/tools/filevine/).** The fastest-growing platform in the category, valued at an estimated $3B after a $400M raise in September 2025, built for high-volume plaintiff work. At that volume PracticePanther is the wrong shape.
- **Alongside [Lawmatics](/en/tools/lawmatics/).** Firms that outgrow Business-tier intake forms add a dedicated legal CRM for marketing and nurture; PracticePanther stays the system of record for matters and money.

## Watch-outs

- **No native AI.** There is no drafting assistant, no matter Q&A and no document OCR in the product, and none of the 2026 releases through August added any. **Guard:** if AI on your own files is a requirement this year, price MyCase Advanced or Clio against PracticePanther plus a standalone legal AI seat, and decide on the total, not the base subscription.
- **API access is by request, and there is no MCP server.** The OAuth 2 REST API exists, but access goes through a support request, and most integrations route through Zapier. **Guard:** file the API request on day one of the 7-day trial and confirm the endpoints you need before committing to an annual plan.
- **Accounting only lives on Business Pro.** It costs $25 per user per month more than Business. **Guard:** compare $300 per user per year against what you pay today for QuickBooks plus bookkeeper reconciliation time; if trust is your only need, run that reconciliation in Business Pro's wizard for one month before switching off the old tools.
- **Trust compliance has one state exception and one payments trade-off.** North Carolina still requires a separate attorney-review document outside the software, and firms that keep LawPay instead of PantherPayments keep their integration but lose access to new payment features. **Guard:** NC firms keep that review in their monthly close checklist; LawPay firms model 2.95% flat against their current blended rate on last year's actual card volume before migrating.
- **Reviews flag sync glitches.** Capterra rates it 4.7 from roughly 520 reviews, but recurring complaints include Google Calendar sync issues and a mobile app thinner than the web app. **Guard:** test calendar sync and the mobile app with real matters during the trial, not demo data.
