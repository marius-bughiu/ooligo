## What it is

ContractPodAi is an end-to-end CLM platform built on the Microsoft stack, combining contract authoring, repository, workflow, and AI-driven review under a single enterprise tenant. Their "Leah" agent reads, drafts, and risk-scores contracts inside the same environment that holds the executed paper.

## Why it shows up in legal-ops stacks

- **Microsoft-native.** Deep ties to Azure, M365, Word, and Teams mean less duct tape for enterprises that already standardized on the stack.
- **One platform, full lifecycle.** Intake, drafting, negotiation, signature, repository, and obligations management live in one tenant — fewer SaaS handoffs for legal ops to babysit.
- **Leah AI agent.** Goes beyond extraction with redlining suggestions, clause comparison against playbook, and Q&A across the contract repo.
