## What it is

Crayon is a competitive intelligence platform from Boston, co-founded by Jonah Lopin, who still runs it as CEO. Its core job is automated monitoring: it tracks competitor websites (with before-and-after views of page changes), news, press releases, and review sites, then classifies what it finds so a product marketing or CI team can turn it into battlecards. Those cards then reach sellers in [Salesforce](/en/tools/salesforce/), [Slack](/en/tools/slack/), Microsoft Teams, and enablement tools such as [Seismic](/en/tools/seismic/) and Highspot. In April 2026 Gartner named Crayon a Leader in its first Magic Quadrant for Competitive and Market Intelligence Platforms, alongside [Klue](/en/tools/klue/). It holds 4.6/5 on G2 across roughly 385 reviews, most from mid-market companies.

Crayon is independent. A Dealroom profile lists a 2021 acquisition by Highspot, but Crayon's own May 2021 announcement of a $22 million Series B led by Baird Capital contradicts it, and every 2026 source treats Crayon as a standalone company.

## Why it shows up in RevOps stacks

- **Monitoring breadth without a researcher.** Crayon's pitch is coverage: many competitors, many public sources, checked continuously, with a daily digest to the CI owner. If your problem is that nobody noticed a rival's pricing page change until a deal was lost, this is the part of Crayon that fixes it.
- **AI that writes the first draft.** Sparks is Crayon's analysis agent. It runs templated or custom prompts (a SWOT from recent news and reviews, objections mined from [Gong](/en/tools/gong/) or [Chorus](/en/tools/chorus/) calls, a win-loss summary), can be scheduled weekly or monthly, and turns the output into battlecard updates. Answers lets reps ask a competitive question in Slack with `/crayon` and get a reply drawn from the curated card set.
- **Field intel captured at close.** Field Agent for Slack (June 2026) watches Salesforce: when a competitive deal moves to Closed Won or Closed Lost, it messages the rep in Slack, takes a voice note, text, or screenshot, asks follow-up questions, and files a structured Insight tagged to the competitor. This is the closest Crayon gets to Klue's win-loss interviewer, and it is cheaper to run because no buyer is involved.
- **Built to feed other AI tools.** Crayon shipped what it calls the first competitive intelligence MCP server on September 4, 2025, three months before Klue. Alongside it sit a Content API, an Answers API, and an Insights API (October 2026). A native [Glean](/en/tools/glean/) integration followed on July 14, 2026; Glean's connector indexes Battlecards and Sparks, though only Sparks from the last 30 days. If your reps already ask [ChatGPT](/en/tools/chatgpt/), [Microsoft 365 Copilot](/en/tools/microsoft-365-copilot/), or Glean their questions, Crayon is designed to be the source behind the answer rather than another tab.

## Pricing

Crayon publishes no prices, offers no free plan or trial, and quotes every contract.

- **What teams pay.** Vendr puts the median Crayon contract at $30,000 a year across 93 purchases, in a range of $12,700 to $46,000, with buyers saving about 20% against the first quote. Klue's Vendr median is also $30,000, so price rarely decides between the two finalists.
- **What moves the quote.** The number of competitors tracked, seats, and contract term drive price. Adding tracked competitors mid-contract can cost extra, so size the list before you sign.
- **Contract terms.** Crayon subscriptions auto-renew unless you give 60 days' written notice, and cancelling mid-term does not end the payment obligation.
- **Budget beyond the license.** Like every tool in this category, Crayon needs an owner. Plan for a PMM or CI lead spending at least a quarter of their week on it.

## Best for

- Product marketing and CI leads at mid-market B2B companies who face 5 or more active competitors, need automated tracking of their websites and pricing pages, and want cards inside Salesforce and Slack
- RevOps teams that already run Glean, Copilot, or ChatGPT internally and want competitive answers to come from a curated source through MCP or an API
- Not for teams under about 20 reps without a dedicated CI owner, and not for strategy or corporate development teams researching markets, filings, and expert calls (look at AlphaSense)

## Alternatives — and when to pick them instead

- **[Klue](/en/tools/klue/)** — the other Gartner Leader and the rival in nearly every Crayon deal, at the same $30,000 median. Klue's edge is win-loss: an AI interviewer that talks to buyers, built from three acquisitions. Pick Klue when buyer-side win-loss interviews matter more than monitoring breadth, and only after its security team has answered for the June 2026 breach of its Salesforce integration.
- **AlphaSense** — the largest vendor in the category by revenue and also in the Gartner Magic Quadrant. It searches filings, earnings calls, broker research, and expert transcripts. Pick it when the buyer is strategy or product leadership asking market questions, not reps trying to win a deal.
- **DIY on your own call data** — the fastest-growing option in practice. Our [competitive-intel-tracker-n8n](/en/workflows/competitive-intel-tracker-n8n/) watches competitor pages and sends a weekly digest for a few dollars a month in model costs, and [competitive-battlecard-skill](/en/workflows/competitive-battlecard-skill/) drafts cards from your own transcripts. Pick DIY when you have a technical RevOps owner, fewer than 20 reps, and no budget for a $30,000 platform. You give up the Salesforce-native card UI, Field Agent, and the managed source coverage.

## Watch-outs

- **Noisy feeds.** Reviewers repeatedly say the AI deduplication and relevance filtering let too much through, so the feed fills with minor page edits and reposted news. Guard: track only competitors that appear in at least 5% of opportunities, mute low-value sources in the first month, and review the daily digest instead of the raw feed.
- **Agents write content reps will trust.** Sparks turns analysis into battlecard updates, and Field Agent turns a rep's voice note into an Insight that can feed Sparks and Answers. Crayon's own description of Field Agent shows no formal approval step. Guard: require the CI owner to approve any Sparks output before it reaches a published card, and tag Field Agent Insights as single-rep opinion until a second deal confirms them.
- **Another OAuth path into your CRM.** Crayon reads Salesforce, Gong, Slack, and SharePoint, the same kind of integration footprint attackers used against Klue customers in June 2026. Guard: connect Crayon through a dedicated Salesforce integration user with read access to opportunities and competitor fields only, and ask Crayon for its token lifetime, egress IP list, and most recent SOC 2 report before go-live.
- **Auto-renewal with a 60-day notice window.** Miss the date and you owe another full term. Guard: put the notice date in the contract tracker the day you sign, ask for a cap on renewal increases, and bring a parallel Klue quote to every renewal.
