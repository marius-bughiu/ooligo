Regie.ai vs Outreach is a question of philosophy: do you want an AI SDR that runs the outbound function autonomously, or a sequence platform with humans in the loop and AI assists bolted on? Regie pitches autonomous outbound at near-zero marginal cost. Outreach pitches a coached, governed engagement engine for human sellers. Both are real. The choice depends on what kind of RevOps team you're building.

## Where Regie.ai wins

- **AI SDR and autonomous outbound.** Regie's "Auto-Pilot" aims to research, write, and send personalized outbound at volume without a human writing each email. Outreach's AI helps an SDR write — it doesn't replace one.
- **Personalization at scale.** Regie ingests context from your knowledge base and generates per-prospect openers and value props. Outreach's templated sequences are still mostly hand-written by SDRs at scale.
- **Cost per touch.** Replacing or augmenting SDR headcount with AI agents is the entire pitch. For lean teams, Regie can deliver outbound volume that would otherwise require 5-plus SDRs.

## Where Outreach wins

- **Enterprise governance and compliance.** Role-based permissions, sequence approval flows, brand controls, and the kind of audit trails that legal and CRO offices require. Regie's autonomy makes governance harder.
- **Conversation intelligence and coaching.** Outreach (with Kaia and acquired call intelligence) gives managers a coaching layer. Regie has no human to coach.
- **CRM and dialer maturity.** Outreach's Salesforce integration, dialer, and reporting are battle-tested at thousand-rep orgs. Regie is a younger product still maturing on these.

## When to use both / Pricing reality

Plenty of teams run Regie inside Outreach. Regie writes the personalized first touch (and sometimes the follow-ups), then drops them into Outreach sequences for sending and tracking. This keeps SDRs in their existing UI and gives ops the audit trail. Pricing: Outreach is around 130 to 200 USD per seat. Regie is consumption-priced and varies wildly with volume — model an actual outbound load before signing.

## Verdict

- **Pick Regie.ai** if you're a small team trying to scale outbound without hiring 10 SDRs, or you need radical personalization volume.
- **Pick Outreach** if you have human SDRs and AEs and your bottleneck is governance, coaching, or sequence quality.
- **Use both** if you want AI-generated personalization on top of a governed, human-in-the-loop sending platform.

The single mistake to avoid: replacing Outreach with Regie before your AI SDR has actually proven it can book qualified meetings. The personalization-versus-deliverability tradeoff bites in month three.
