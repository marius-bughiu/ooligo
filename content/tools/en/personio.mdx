## What it is

Personio is the dominant HR-and-recruiting platform for European SMB and mid-market companies — a single platform spanning HRIS, recruiting (ATS), payroll integration, time tracking, and performance management. Munich-headquartered, founded in 2015, Personio serves 14,000+ companies primarily in DACH (Germany, Austria, Switzerland), UK, and broader EMEA.

## Why it shows up in Recruiting stacks

- **One-vendor HR + recruiting consolidation.** SMB and mid-market European companies often want to consolidate HR systems; Personio's combined HRIS+ATS+performance approach reduces vendor count from 4-5 to 1.
- **Strong DACH region fit.** Built for German labor law, social-security integration, multi-language teams. Material advantages over US-built tools (Workday, BambooHR) for German-headquartered companies.
- **Recruiting integrated with HR data.** Hired candidates flow seamlessly into HRIS — onboarding, payroll, performance review setup all happen in the same system without integration work.

## Pricing

- **Custom only.** Per-employee pricing typical; effective rates depend heavily on modules selected.
- **Module packaging.** Personio HR (HRIS), Personio Recruiting (ATS), Personio Payroll, Personio Performance, Personio Whistleblower all separately licensed.
- Implementation typically 60-120 days for combined HR+recruiting deployments.

## Best for

- European SMB and mid-market companies (50-2,000 employees)
- DACH-region headquartered companies
- Organizations consolidating HR vendor count rather than running specialist tools per function

## Watch-outs

- Recruiting features are competent but not category-leading vs specialist ATS ([Ashby](/en/tools/ashby/), [Greenhouse](/en/tools/greenhouse/), [Workable](/en/tools/workable/), [Recruitee](/en/tools/recruitee/))
- Less brand recognition outside Europe; US-headquartered companies typically default elsewhere
- AI features lag specialist tools — pair with [juicebox](/en/tools/juicebox/) or [hireEZ](/en/tools/hireez/) for serious sourcing
- Outgrown by enterprise scale (5,000+ employees, multi-jurisdiction complexity beyond Europe); migration to Workday or SAP SuccessFactors typical at that point
