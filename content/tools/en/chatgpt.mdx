## What it is

ChatGPT is OpenAI's consumer and team-facing AI assistant — chat, voice, image, file analysis, custom GPTs, and a Projects/Workspace surface for sustained work. Plus and Business tiers unlock the frontier models, longer context, and connectors to Drive, Gmail, and more.

## Why it shows up in ops stacks

- **Most teams already use it.** It's the default on-ramp for any AI workflow — distribution and habit beat product purity.
- **Custom GPTs.** Codify recurring ops prompts (ICP scoring rubric, contract redline checklist, candidate scorecard) into shareable, callable agents.
- **Connectors and Tasks.** Connect Drive, Gmail, GitHub; schedule recurring runs. The product is moving toward background agents.

## Pricing

- **Free** — $0; capped messages, and US free users now see ads ("Sponsored Tips")
- **Go** — $8/mo; higher limits than Free, still ad-supported in the US
- **Plus** — $20/mo; frontier GPT-5-series models, custom GPTs, higher limits
- **Pro** — $200/mo for top limits and Deep Research volume; a lighter $100/mo Pro tier launched April 2026
- **Business** (renamed from Team in Aug 2025) — $20/seat/mo billed annually, $25-30/seat/mo monthly, 2-seat minimum; shared workspace, admin controls
- **Enterprise** — custom; roughly $60/seat/mo floor with an annual commitment, SSO, audit, expanded context
- API billed separately, usage-based

For an ops team the real entry is Plus at $20 or Business at $20/seat annual — Go's ad-supported consumer tier isn't built for shared work.

## Best for

- Ops leaders codifying repeatable analysis as Custom GPTs
- Anyone needing a fast, broadly-connected general-purpose assistant
- Teams that want prebuilt connectors over building MCP servers

## Watch-outs

- For coding and structured agentic work, [Claude](/en/tools/claude/) often pulls ahead
- Enterprise data governance lags Anthropic's enterprise posture in some buyer reviews
- Custom GPT quality is prompt-dependent; treat them like products, not toys
