## What it is

ModernLoop is the interview scheduling platform that automates the operational nightmare of coordinating multi-person, multi-stage interview loops — interviewer availability, panel composition, candidate timezone, scorecards, debrief logistics. AI-driven scheduling figures out the right combination of interviewers and time slots that satisfies all constraints in seconds rather than the hours a recruiting coordinator would spend manually.

## Why it shows up in Recruiting stacks

- **Eliminates the scheduling tax.** A 4-round on-site loop with 6 interviewers across 3 timezones used to take a recruiting coordinator 2-4 hours to schedule. ModernLoop does it in minutes by understanding interviewer availability, role requirements, panel-diversity rules, and candidate preferences simultaneously.
- **Interviewer load balancing.** Tracks who's interviewed how much; surfaces overloaded interviewers; rotates load across the eligible interviewer pool. Material at scale where interviewer fatigue affects candidate experience.
- **Native ATS integration.** Reads candidate stage, scorecards, and required interview types from [Ashby](/en/tools/ashby/), [Greenhouse](/en/tools/greenhouse/), [Lever](/en/tools/lever/), or workday; writes back interview events, debrief notes, and scheduling history.

## Pricing

- **Custom only.** Per-recruiter or per-coordinator pricing typically; effective rates from $200-500/coordinator/month at mid-market scale.
- **Volume pricing for high-volume hiring teams** — works out to a per-interview-loop cost at the largest deployments.
- Implementation typically 30-60 days.

## Best for

- Mid-market and enterprise companies running 50+ interview loops per month
- Engineering and sales hiring where multi-round loops with rotating interviewer panels are standard
- Recruiting teams where scheduling coordinator capacity is the bottleneck on hiring throughput

## Watch-outs

- Wrong scope for SMB or low-volume hiring — Calendly or native ATS scheduling suffices below ~20 loops/month
- Compete with [GoodTime](https://goodtime.io/), [Prelude](https://prelude.co/) (Calendly), and increasingly native ATS scheduling improvements (Ashby ships strong scheduling out-of-the-box)
- Quality of automation depends on accurate interviewer availability + skills tagging — garbage-in produces wrong panel composition
- Pair with [BrightHire](/en/tools/brighthire/) for the interview-recording layer once the scheduling tax is eliminated; the next bottleneck moves to interview quality
