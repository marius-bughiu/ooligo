## What it is

Avoma records sales calls, transcribes them in 70+ languages, writes notes and action items, and saves the result to Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho, Pipedrive or Copper. Capture runs either as a bot that joins the call or as bot-less native recording; the pricing page lists both. That part is [Fireflies](/en/tools/fireflies/) or [Fathom](/en/tools/fathom/) with a revenue layer on top.

The part that isn't: Avoma also sells the meeting *before* the meeting. Scheduling and Lead Router — booking pages, group and round-robin scheduling, inbound form qualification, routing on CRM owner or custom fields, SDR-to-AE handoff — ship inside the same product. No other conversation-intelligence vendor in this catalog does that. [Gong](/en/tools/gong/), [Chorus](/en/tools/chorus/), [Attention](/en/tools/attention/) and [Sybill](/en/tools/sybill/) all start at the recorder and work forward. Avoma starts at the calendar.

The product is a base plan plus three priced add-ons: Conversation Intelligence, Revenue Intelligence and Lead Router. On 5 November 2026 Avoma added Home, a per-user daily workspace with a Morning Brief, a Daily Manager Brief and a Coaching Rundown; it is included in every plan, but which widgets work depends on the add-ons assigned. Reading Avoma as "the cheap Gong" is the mistake that runs the evaluation off the road. The base seat is a notetaker, and the Gong-shaped job is three line items.

## Why it shows up in RevOps stacks

Consolidation, and for once it is a defensible reason. A 30-rep team running one vendor for booking, another for inbound routing, a third for notes and nothing at all for coaching is carrying four renewal dates for one funnel. Avoma collapses that into one contract and one admin surface, and it is the only vendor here that can.

The second reason is the MCP server. An admin publishes the connector to Claude or ChatGPT once for the org, then each user connects their own account over OAuth 2.0, with no API keys to manage. The read side covers transcripts, notes, summaries and action items, meeting search by participant, topic or outcome, call-scoring results, and Ask Avoma questions across meeting history, which are also exposed through the External API. The write side is exactly three operations: `set_meeting_purpose`, `set_meeting_outcome` and `set_meeting_privacy`. A Settings page lists every connected user, their scope, and calls per tool. Set that against Attention's 68 tools and a write scope that includes `delete_user`: Avoma's connector cannot damage anything outside meeting metadata. If you want an assistant reading call history without holding write access to your system of record, that narrowness is the feature.

## Pricing reality

Published per recorder seat, checked 12 November 2026. Viewers and collaborators are free.

- **Startup** — $19/seat/month annual, $29 monthly. Capped at 25 paid seats. Recording, transcription, AI notes, 1:1 scheduling, AI follow-up emails, notes saved to CRM records, dialer integration.
- **Organization** — $29/seat/month annual, $39 monthly. Capped at 100 paid seats. Adds custom AI templates, group and round-robin scheduling, limited conversation intelligence, smart playlists, transcript translation, org-level policies, API and webhooks.
- **Enterprise** — $39/seat/month, annual only, 10-seat minimum. Adds SSO (SAML/OIDC), SCIM, HIPAA, custom data retention, signed DPAs, a designated success manager and concierge onboarding.

Add-ons, per seat, on the same billing cycle as the plan: **Conversation Intelligence** $29 annual / $35 monthly (AI scorecards, auto call scoring, coaching recommendations, custom trackers), **Revenue Intelligence** $29 / $35 (deal risk alerts, methodology tracking, forecasting, win-loss, and two-way CRM field updates), **Lead Router** $19 / $25. The whole recording team must sit on the same plan; add-ons can go to individuals. No platform fee, no setup fee. Expert onboarding below Enterprise is an optional $1,000 one-time. The entry point is a 14-day Organization trial with every add-on enabled and no card.

Organization was $24 annual in September; it is now $29, up 21%. Two consequences. Startup's monthly price and Organization's annual price are now the same $29, so any team that can commit for a year should not be on monthly Startup. And Enterprise is now only $10/seat above Organization, so a team over 10 seats that needs SSO or a retention policy should price Enterprise before negotiating Organization down.

The number that matters is the one for the job you are buying. Recording plus coaching plus CRM-aware deal tracking — the Gong-equivalent scope — is Organization + CI + RI: **$87/seat/month annual**, about **$1,044/seat/year**, or $109 on monthly billing. Twenty reps in that configuration is roughly **$20,900/year**.

Against the comparables, honestly: Gong runs approximately $1,400-$2,000 per seat/year on standard-tier deployments, plus a mandatory platform fee reported in the $5,000-$50,000 range. Avoma at full scope is **roughly 50-75% of Gong's per-seat band with no platform fee**, not a tenth of it, whatever the comparison blogs say. At the other end, Fathom Business is $25/seat/month annual and now includes CRM field sync, coaching metrics and AI scorecards, so Avoma fully configured costs about 3.5x a competent notetaker. The $19 base seat is priced against Fathom; the $87 configuration is priced against Gong. Those are two different purchases and only one of them is on your quote.

## Best for

A RevOps or sales leader on a **10-60 seat revenue team running Salesforce, HubSpot or Pipedrive** who owns inbound meeting booking *and* call coaching and wants one vendor for both. The case where Avoma wins outright: a team already paying separately for a scheduling tool and a notetaker, where inbound demo requests need routing to the right AE by CRM owner and those same calls need scorecards. Avoma is the only vendor in this segment that sells both on one contract.

It also fits a smaller team that needs a published number and no platform fee. Ten reps on Organization plus Conversation Intelligence is $58/seat/month on a card. Gong is not a customer conversation at that size.

## Not for

Teams past roughly 100 reps whose mandate is coaching depth and cross-market intelligence. Organization caps at 100 paid seats, which forces the Enterprise conversation, and at that scale you are weighing Gong's deal inspection against a base plan with three add-ons attached. Gong's economics improve exactly where Avoma's advantage runs out.

It is also the wrong pick when the requirement is notes, CRM writeback and basic scorecards and nothing else. Fathom Business does that job for $25 annual, and Fathom Enterprise lifts its 3-users-per-domain CRM sync cap at a published $35. Avoma's two-way field updates sit in the $29 Revenue Intelligence add-on, so the same job lands at $58 on Organization. Pay that only when scheduling, routing or forecasting is also in scope.

And if the honest requirement is notes for eight people, the base seat is not competitive with a $0 [Granola](/en/tools/granola/) or Fathom tier.

## Versus the alternatives

- **[Gong](/en/tools/gong/)** — the share leader and the name your buying committee already knows, at a roughly $55,000 median annual contract. Pick Gong at 40+ quota-carrying reps when coaching is a funded program with a named owner. Pick Avoma under 60 seats, when the budget has no room for a five-figure platform fee, and when booking and routing are half the reason for the purchase.
- **[Attention](/en/tools/attention/)** — quote-only, estimated in the $70-140/seat/month band, with agents that send the follow-up and fill the CRM fields themselves and an MCP server of 68 tools with a real write scope. Pick Attention when agents taking the action is the deliverable. Pick Avoma when you want a price you can read on a website and a connector that cannot touch the CRM.
- **[Sybill](/en/tools/sybill/)** — the other published-price pole: $90/user/month annual on Business, CRM Autofill capped at 10 fields, API and MCP on the same tier, no seat minimum. Pick Sybill when CRM hygiene is the single measured problem. Pick Avoma when scheduling and inbound routing are in scope, because Sybill does not sell them.
- **[Fathom](/en/tools/fathom/), Granola and [Fireflies](/en/tools/fireflies/)** — $0-35/seat/month. Pick any of them when the requirement really is notes, and pick Fathom Business or Enterprise when it is notes plus writeback plus light scoring. The gap to Avoma is the calendar half of the product and the forecasting add-on, not the recorder.

If none of these fit, the tell is usually that meetings are not the constraint. Buy a $20 notetaker, spend the difference on the process problem, and revisit next quarter.

## Watch-outs

- **Add-on stacking is where the budget goes.** Modeling on the $19 headline and then finding that coaching is a second $29 line, CRM field writes a third and routing a fourth is the most common Avoma surprise. **Guard:** build the seat count for each module separately *before* the first demo, price that grid yourself, and make the vendor quote a discount against the whole grid. A discount on the cheapest line is not a discount.
- **The seat caps are hard product limits, and one plan covers every recorder.** Startup stops at 25 paid seats and Organization at 100, and you cannot mix plans across the recording team. A team that hires past a cap is migrating tiers mid-contract. **Guard:** if your 12-month hiring plan crosses 25 seats, start on Organization. If it approaches 100, get Enterprise pricing into the first paper — the tier change is a repricing event and you have nothing to bargain with in month eight.
- **Capture mode decides what your prospects see.** The bot-based recorder joins Zoom, Meet and Teams as a visible attendee, and some enterprise buyers block external bots outright; the bot-less option avoids that but records from the rep's side. **Guard:** decide the default capture mode per meeting type during the trial, run it against your noisiest recurring call and one known-hard call, and read the raw transcript before signing. Confirm the bot is admitted in at least one target account.
- **MCP has no named tier and Startup has no API.** The pricing page lists API and webhooks from Organization up and does not mention MCP at all; the assistant sees whatever the connected user can see. **Guard:** get MCP access named by plan in the order form, and set meeting privacy defaults *before* publishing the connector — the write scope includes `set_meeting_privacy`, so the order matters.
