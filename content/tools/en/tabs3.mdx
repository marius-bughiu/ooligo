## What it is

Tabs3 is billing, trust accounting and practice management software for US law firms, made in Lincoln, Nebraska, and now part of ProfitSolv, the group that also owns CosmoLex, Rocket Matter, TimeSolv and Mango. It is a suite of separately named modules. **Tabs3 Billing** covers time, rates, fee arrangements and statements. **Tabs3 Financials** is the firm's general ledger, trust accounting and accounts payable. **PracticeMaster** handles matters, documents, calendars and conflict checks. **Tabs3Pay** takes card, ACH, PayPal, Venmo, Apple Pay and Google Pay payments, with Autopay. **Tabs3 Connect** gives browser and mobile access to time entry, case files and the firm calendar.

For most of its life Tabs3 was Windows software installed on a firm's own server. Tabs3 Cloud launched for billing and accounting on 26 October 2023, and PracticeMaster joined it on 23 September 2024 (vendor press releases). Both editions are still sold. That history explains who uses it: firms of roughly 10 to 50 timekeepers that bought Tabs3 years ago, run their trust and operating books in it, and now have to decide whether to move to its cloud edition or to a different vendor.

## Why it shows up in Legal Ops stacks

- **The firm's books live with billing.** Financials gives you a general ledger, accounts payable and trust ledgers in the same database as time and statements, so a firm can run without QuickBooks. The QuickBooks integration exists for firms whose accountant insists on it.
- **Billing depth built for hourly work.** Rate tables, fee arrangements, custom statement design and detailed billing reports are the reason long-time users stay. Reviewers on Capterra and Software Advice praise the support team in particular; some say it is the reason they renewed.
- **Document-management tie-ins midsize firms need.** PracticeMaster integrates with NetDocuments (including conflict searches) and iManage. Tabs3's February 2026 newsletter put the iManage sync in its pre-release program: a client added in Billing, PracticeMaster or Trust creates the matching iManage workspace. The same pre-release added Tabs3Pay payments scheduled automatically when statements are generated.
- **An on-premises option that still exists.** Firms with data-residency rules or an in-house IT team can keep the OnSite edition on their own servers, or have a hosting partner such as Uptime Legal or CyberlinkASP run it.

## Pricing reality

tabs3.com/pricing, checked 2026-11-20, per user per month, with volume discounts available:

- **Tabs3 Cloud — from $89.** Billing, Financials, Tabs3Pay and Connect. **PracticeMaster adds $35.**
- **OnSite subscription — from $69.** Billing, Financials and Connect; Tabs3Pay is not in the bundle. **PracticeMaster adds $32.**

A "user" is anyone who tracks time or works in the software, so legal assistants and bookkeepers count. Firms of 10 or more users are told to ask for a custom quote. Add-ons with published prices: Tabs3 CRM from $147 a month (annual, up to 3 PracticeMaster users), Websites at $159 a month plus a $599 setup fee, LexShare file sharing and e-signature from $30 a month. LEDES and UTBMS e-billing needs a separate Taskbill license with no published price, and Tabs3Pay processing rates are quoted, not listed.

In practice, 10 users on Tabs3 Cloud with PracticeMaster pay $14,880 a year at list ($124 per user per month). The same firm OnSite pays $12,120, before the server, backups and IT time the cloud price covers. For comparison, 10 users on CosmoLex Pro, Tabs3's own sister product with a general ledger built in, pay $15,480. [SurePoint](/en/tools/surepoint/) and [Centerbase](/en/tools/centerbase/) do not publish prices, so get written quotes before you treat Tabs3 as the cheap option. Capterra's Canadian listing shows Tabs3 Cloud from $124, so confirm the currency on any quote.

## Best for

Firm administrators and billing managers at US firms of about 10 to 50 timekeepers that already run Tabs3 on a server and want to keep their billing setup, trust history and general ledger while moving to the cloud. It also fits an insurance-defense or business-law firm that bills hourly, needs detailed statements and real trust accounting, and doesn't want a separate accounting system.

**Not for:** solo lawyers (Lawyerist lists "not suited to solo practices" among its cons and rates Tabs3 Cloud 4.1 out of 5), firms that want an app-marketplace ecosystem, and firms past about 75 lawyers with multi-office or multi-entity books.

## Alternatives

- **[Clio](/en/tools/clio/)** — the market-share leader, from $49 per user per month. Pick it when the client portal, integrations and modern UX matter more than having the general ledger inside the billing system; you will keep QuickBooks or Xero for the firm's books.
- **[MyCase](/en/tools/mycase/)** — the other large incumbent. Pick it for a smaller firm that wants intake, client texting and payments in one bill and can add MyCase Accounting.
- **[SurePoint](/en/tools/surepoint/)** — the like-for-like successor for firms of 50 to 150 lawyers. Pick it when you also want firm analytics and integrations with AI time-capture tools and can absorb a custom-quoted implementation.
- **[Centerbase](/en/tools/centerbase/)** — the cloud-native rival for the same midsize band. Pick it when partner self-service dashboards and a single cloud database matter more than keeping your existing Tabs3 data model.
- **[Filevine](/en/tools/filevine/)** — the fastest-growing platform in the category. Pick it for litigation-heavy or plaintiff practices where case pipelines matter more than billing depth.

If you are staying in the ProfitSolv family, [CosmoLex](/en/tools/cosmolex/) is the simpler, all-in-one cloud product for firms under about 10 lawyers.

## Watch-outs

- **The headline price leaves out PracticeMaster.** $89 buys billing and accounting; matter management, documents and conflicts are $35 more per user, and every person who works in the system counts. *Guard:* count every timekeeper and staff user, price Cloud plus PracticeMaster, and ask for the volume discount in writing.
- **E-billing is a separate license.** LEDES and UTBMS output needs Taskbill, which has no public price. *Guard:* if any client requires e-billing, put Taskbill on the same quote before you sign.
- **No named AI features and no public API.** Tabs3's site and recent newsletters describe no AI tools, and no developer API is published, while Clio and sister brand CosmoLex have shipped AI features. *Guard:* if AI time capture matters, budget for a third-party tool and confirm it connects to Tabs3 before you buy it.
- **The cloud edition is younger than the product.** One Software Advice reviewer reports lag in the cloud version and says it lacks features they used on-premises, and a Capterra reviewer describes a steep learning curve. *Guard:* run a full billing cycle and a trust reconciliation on the trial with your heaviest users before you retire the server.
- **Conversion help comes from outside.** Tabs3 points firms to consultants for setup, data migration and training. *Guard:* get a fixed-fee conversion quote from a certified consultant, and a test conversion of your trust ledgers, before setting a go-live date.
