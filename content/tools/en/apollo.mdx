## What it is

Apollo bundles a B2B contact database, a sales-engagement layer (sequences, dialer, email tracking), and light AI assistance into one tool. It's the "good enough at everything" pick for sub-$10M ARR teams that don't want to buy [ZoomInfo](/en/tools/zoominfo/) for data, [Outreach](/en/tools/outreach/) for sequencing, and a dialer on top. Think of it as a compressed ZoomInfo-plus-Outreach at a fraction of the combined price — you trade depth on each axis for one contract and one login.

## Why it shows up in RevOps stacks

- **Database + sequencer in one.** Search Apollo's database, push results into a sequence, dial out — no glue code between tools.
- **Lowest-cost tier with real API access.** Paid plans open at $49/seat and expose an API most data competitors gate behind an enterprise contract.
- **Decent CRM sync.** Bi-directional with HubSpot and Salesforce — shallower than a native sequencer, adequate for most SMB teams.

## Pricing reality

Four plans, priced per seat, cheaper on annual:

- **Free** — $0, ~250 emails/day, 5 mobile + 10 export credits/mo, 1,200 data credits/yr, 2 active sequences.
- **Basic** — $49/seat/mo annual ($59 monthly), 75 mobile + 1,000 export credits/mo, 5,000 data credits/yr, unlimited sequences.
- **Professional** — $79/seat/mo annual ($99 monthly), dialer, AI assist, 100 mobile + 2,000 export credits/mo, 10,000 data credits/yr.
- **Organization** — $119/seat/mo annual ($149 monthly), 3-seat minimum, 200 mobile + 4,000 export credits/mo, 15,000 data credits/yr, advanced security and reporting.

The sticker is the per-seat annual rate; three things push real spend above it. Monthly billing runs about 20-25% over annual. Organization carries a 3-seat floor, so its true entry is $4,284/yr, not $119/mo. And mobile-number and bulk-export credits are metered on every tier — the overage line is where Apollo makes its margin. A 5-rep Professional team is $4,740/yr in seats alone; add a few hundred dollars a month once export volume is real.

## Best for

- Early-stage and growth teams (5-25 reps) wanting integrated prospecting + outreach on one contract.
- SDR teams that don't need enterprise sequencing governance — the [Outreach](/en/tools/outreach/) / [Salesloft](/en/tools/salesloft/) tier.
- Teams pairing Apollo with [Clay](/en/tools/clay/): Apollo for the dialer and cheap base data, Clay for higher-quality enrichment and orchestration.

**Not for** enterprise teams whose pipeline lives or dies on contact-data accuracy, or 100+ rep orgs with mature RevOps governance. At that scale ZoomInfo's data depth and Outreach's admin controls earn their premium, and Apollo's metered-credit model turns expensive and noisy.

## Versus the alternative

- **vs [ZoomInfo](/en/tools/zoominfo/).** ZoomInfo leads on data accuracy, coverage, and intent signals — and costs several times more, usually $15K+/yr on an annual platform contract. Pick ZoomInfo when data quality is the whole point; pick Apollo when "80% as accurate at a fifth of the price" wins.
- **vs [Outreach](/en/tools/outreach/) / [Salesloft](/en/tools/salesloft/).** These are enterprise sales-engagement platforms with deeper sequencing logic, reporting, and RevOps governance. Pick them at 100+ reps with mature process; pick Apollo for the SMB / mid-market band where that overhead doesn't pay back.
- **vs [Clay](/en/tools/clay/).** Clay is the fastest-growing entrant in GTM data — an enrichment and orchestration workbench, not a bundle. Most teams run both: Apollo for cheap base data and the dialer, Clay for waterfall enrichment and custom plays. Go Clay-first when data quality and bespoke workflows matter more than an all-in-one.
- **vs [Amplemarket](/en/tools/amplemarket/).** The AI-native bundle peer, at a higher ~$300/seat entry. Pick Amplemarket for stronger AI-personalization quality; pick Apollo for the cheaper entry and the bigger raw database.

## Watch-outs

- **Data accuracy sits a step below ZoomInfo.** **Guard:** run Apollo records through a verification or enrichment pass ([Clay](/en/tools/clay/) waterfall) before high-value sends; treat Apollo's bundled data as a starting list, not ground truth.
- **Metered credits surprise at scale.** Mobile and export credits cap per seat, and overages stack fast. **Guard:** model mobile/export usage at real outbound velocity before committing — the Organization 3-seat minimum and the ~20-25% monthly-billing premium both inflate the sticker.
- **AI features are basic** next to [Claude](/en/tools/claude/), Gong, or Clay-with-AI-columns. **Guard:** don't buy Apollo for the AI; buy it for data + dialer + sequencing and run AI drafting elsewhere.
- **The Pocus acquisition isn't packaged yet.** Apollo acquired [Pocus](/en/tools/pocus/) in March 2026 to fold product-led and signal-based scoring in, but none of it appears in the published tiers. **Guard:** don't buy Apollo today expecting Pocus-grade PLS signals — evaluate what ships in your contract term, not the roadmap.
