## What it is

Outreach is the enterprise sales engagement platform for outbound at scale. As of its Spring 2026 relaunch it markets itself as the "agentic AI platform for revenue teams" and moved its site from outreach.io to outreach.ai. Underneath, it is still the incumbent that defined the category: sequences, a dialer, email tracking, conversation intelligence (Kaia), and a deal-management layer over the CRM — now with AI agents sitting on top. The default choice for $50M+ ARR sales orgs running structured outbound across many reps.

## Why it shows up in RevOps stacks

- **Built for scale.** Hundreds of seats, complex permissions, deep Salesforce integration, audit trail, and an admin UI that survives org changes. Most $50M+ ARR teams running outbound have it deployed.
- **Sequence sophistication.** Branching logic on opens/clicks/replies, A/B testing on send times, dialer integration with click-to-call, and reporting that ties activity to pipeline.
- **The 2026 agent layer (Amplify).** Outreach Omni is a conversational agent in Slack and mobile that answers deal questions, analyzes accounts, and drafts email in one thread. Agent Studio lets RevOps build and schedule custom agents on a visual canvas. Named agents — Meeting Prep Agent, Deal Agent (writes AI-suggested CRM field updates back for rep approval), AI Topics Explorer, and a Kaia coaching agent — automate prep, coaching, and pipeline hygiene. This is a real step up from the old "the AI is fine but not the reason to buy" story, but validate the agents on your own data before you pay for the tier that unlocks them.

## Pricing

- **Quote-gated, seat-based, with a new AI-credit layer.** The core Engage plan (sequencing) runs roughly $100-140 per seat/month on annual contracts. AI agents live in the Amplify tiers — Core (~$100/seat, 25,000 AI credits), Plus (~$130/seat, 50,000 credits), Pro (~$160/seat, 100,000 credits) — where credit consumption is billed on top of the seat.
- Module add-ons stack: Meet (conversation intelligence) and Deal (pipeline) run ~$30-50/seat each; Forecast ~$20-40/seat, usually managers only.
- Implementation is $5,000-25,000+; budget a platform/services fee of ~$2,000-5,000/year on top.
- A fully loaded 50-seat deployment clears ~$150-165K in year one; Engage-only is closer to ~$72K/year. Minimum commitments typically start at 25-50 seats — not viable sub-$10M ARR.

## Best for

- $50M+ ARR B2B SaaS with 50+ outbound reps
- Sales orgs that need deep Salesforce coupling, complex permissions, and audit trails
- Teams where outbound is a primary channel, the engagement layer is central, and reps will actually deploy the Amplify agents rather than pay for AI they won't use

## Alternatives — and when to pick them instead

- **Salesloft** — the peer incumbent. Pick it if you want a comparable engagement + conversation-intelligence + forecasting suite and prefer its Rhythm signal-to-action model over Outreach's agent framing.
- **Apollo** — pick it under ~$25M ARR. It bundles the B2B contact database with sequencing at a fraction of the seat cost, so you aren't paying enterprise TCO for a mid-market motion.
- **11x or Artisan** — the fastest-growing challengers. Pick one only if you want an autonomous AI SDR that runs outbound itself, not an engagement layer your reps drive. Different job; don't cross-shop on price alone.

## Watch-outs

- **The bill compounds through modules and credits.** Engage is the floor; Meet, Deal, Forecast, and Amplify credit consumption quietly double it. Guard: price Engage-only first, negotiate each add-on separately, and cap/monitor AI-credit usage before committing to a Pro tier.
- **Wrong tool below ~$25M ARR.** Under ~25 seats the TCO never pencils out. Guard: default to Apollo or Salesloft until outbound is a core primary channel with a dedicated RevOps owner.
- **Migration lock-in.** Annual contracts, hard mid-term seat reductions, and sequence/reporting/integration assets that all need rebuilding. Guard: plan a 90-day transition and negotiate seat-flexibility into the contract before you sign.
- **"AI-native" is a rebrand, not a rebuild.** The outreach.ai positioning ships real product, but the core is still a 2014-era sequencer with agents layered on. Guard: pilot the specific agents you would actually use on your data — don't buy the Amplify tier on the marketing.
