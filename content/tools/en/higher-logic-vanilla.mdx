## What it is

Higher Logic Vanilla is a hosted customer community platform for B2B and B2C companies: Q&A and discussion forums with accepted answers, knowledge bases, a product-feedback (ideation) process, events, polls, and a badges-points-leaderboards reputation layer, all arranged in a drag-and-drop layout editor. It started as Vanilla Forums in Montreal and was acquired by Higher Logic in 2021; it is now one of two community products Higher Logic sells, next to Higher Logic Thrive, which is the separate, association-oriented social product with its own sales and support teams. The vendor cites 300+ organizations on Vanilla, with D2L, ChurnZero, 6sense, Anaplan, TeamViewer and Smartsheet among the named logos. It is the incumbent the self-serve challengers measure themselves against: our [Bettermode](/en/tools/bettermode/) page calls it the category leader Bettermode most resembles.

## Why it shows up in Customer Success stacks

- **It is built around the question-to-answer-to-article loop.** Discussions and questions are separate post types, questions get accepted answers, and AI-suggested answers offer the asker up to three generated replies drawn from existing community content before a human sees the thread. A resolved question can be promoted into a knowledge-base article. That is the deflection mechanism most CS business cases rest on, and FeverBee's June 2026 enterprise review scored Vanilla's discussions-and-Q&A 8/10 and called its Zendesk integration the best of the platforms it reviewed. Smartsheet reports that 80% of questions in its Vanilla community are answered by other members rather than moderators.
- **The integration list covers the support desk, the CRM and the CS platform.** Native connectors include [Zendesk](/en/tools/zendesk/), [Salesforce](/en/tools/salesforce/), [HubSpot](/en/tools/hubspot/), and escalation to [Freshdesk](/en/tools/freshdesk/), [ServiceNow](/en/tools/servicenow/) and Jira; [Productboard](/en/tools/productboard/) for feedback; and external integrations with [ChurnZero](/en/tools/churnzero/) and [Fivetran](/en/tools/fivetran/), the latter syncing community data into your warehouse. [Totango](/en/tools/totango/) connects through Salesforce middleware, and [Slack](/en/tools/slack/) through [Zapier](/en/tools/zapier/).
- **The MCP server is free on every tier and can act, not just read.** Higher Logic Vanilla MCP launched on 26 May 2026 for Claude, ChatGPT and Cursor. It runs on Vanilla's REST API under each user's existing roles and permissions, every action is logged to the audit trail, and it can write: send a DM to event attendees, award a badge, add posts to a collection. A CS lead can ask Claude for the fastest-growing contributors who are not yet in the advocate program, or the articles with 500+ views in 90 days that nobody has updated in six months, without exporting a CSV.

## Pricing reality

Higher Logic publishes no prices. The packages page lists three plans — Corporate, Enterprise and Enterprise Plus — all with unlimited users, page views and API calls, and every tier ends in "Book Demo".

- **Starting price:** Capterra, Software Advice and GetApp all list Vanilla from **$24,000 per year, flat rate**.
- **What teams pay:** Vendr reports a **$20,400/year median** across 14 Higher Logic purchases. That figure covers both Higher Logic products, not Vanilla alone, so treat it as a sanity check on the listed floor, not a Vanilla quote.
- **Contract shape:** FeverBee describes Vanilla as enterprise-priced and sold on multi-year contracts.

The tier, not the list price, decides what you get. **Corporate** caps you at 5 automation rules, 2 federated-search connectors, 6 custom webhooks, 4 knowledge bases and a 1-month audit log, with no subcommunities. **Enterprise** removes the automation and webhook caps, raises federated search to 6 connectors and adds a dedicated cluster. **Enterprise Plus** is the only tier with Advanced AI RAG Search, the AI Search Assistant and a dedicated AI model. Budget from roughly $24,000/year for Corporate; if your deflection case depends on generative search across your docs, you are pricing Enterprise Plus, and you should not sign until that number is in writing.

## Best for

A Customer Success or support leader at a B2B software company with a large installed base — roughly 10,000 users and up — who needs a branded, SEO-indexed support community that deflects tickets into Zendesk-grade workflows, with deep permissioning (private customer spaces, beta groups, category-level access) and, often, several brands or languages on one instance.

**Not for you if** your community is under a few thousand members or your budget is under $20,000/year: [Bettermode](/en/tools/bettermode/) at $4,788–$18,000/year or [Discourse](/en/tools/discourse/) from $100/month does that job. It is also the wrong pick when the community is built around events or a mobile app — FeverBee scored Vanilla 3/10 on both.

## Versus the alternatives

- **[Gainsight](/en/tools/gainsight/) Customer Communities** is the other share leader for enterprise B2B communities. Pick it when you already run Gainsight CS and want community activity to write into health scores, or when ideation weighted by account value matters; FeverBee rates Gainsight well ahead on ideation (Vanilla scored 5/10). Pick Vanilla for the Zendesk loop, layout control and multi-brand setups.
- **[Khoros](/en/tools/khoros/)** was the enterprise incumbent; it has been owned by IgniteTech since May 2025, and FeverBee reports Vanilla winning many customers moving off it. Stay on Khoros only if your contract and migration cost make leaving more expensive than the risk; if you are evaluating fresh, Vanilla is the safer bet.
- **[Discourse](/en/tools/discourse/)** wins on raw forum depth, open source and data ownership. Pick it for a technical community where you want to self-host or own the exit; you give up packaged CRM and help-desk integrations.
- **[Bettermode](/en/tools/bettermode/)** is the published-price challenger at $399–$1,500/month with MCP on every plan. Pick it when you need a budget number this week and your community fits under 25,000 members.
- **Circle** is the fastest-growing entrant in adjacent territory, built for courses and paid memberships. Pick it when the community is a product you sell, not a support channel.

## Watch-outs

- **The AI search you heard about in the demo may be an Enterprise Plus feature.** RAG search and the AI Search Assistant sit only in the top tier. *Guard:* ask for the quote as a tier-by-tier feature list and map each item in your deflection business case to a line in it before procurement starts.
- **Corporate's caps bite earlier than the member count.** Five automation rules and two federated-search connectors run out fast once Zendesk, your docs site and Salesforce all need wiring. *Guard:* list every automation and search source you plan to run in year one, then pick the tier that covers it; do not plan to upgrade mid-contract on a multi-year deal.
- **MCP availability and write scope need checking for your instance.** The May 2026 launch said it is available to all customers, but FeverBee's June 2026 review described access as beta, gated behind a beta agreement. Because MCP acts with the connecting user's permissions, an admin's Claude session can message members at scale. *Guard:* confirm MCP is enabled on your instance in writing, and connect it through a dedicated role with only the write permissions you intend to use.
- **Getting your data out is easier through the API than by hand.** FeverBee flags clean manual export as a weak spot. *Guard:* turn on the Fivetran sync, or schedule an API export into your warehouse, from day one, so your exit at renewal does not depend on the vendor.
- **Vanilla and Thrive are different products with different teams.** *Guard:* name Higher Logic Vanilla explicitly in your RFP and in the order form, so you are not demoed or quoted the association-focused Thrive.
