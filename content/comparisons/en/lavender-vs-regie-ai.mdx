[Lavender](/en/tools/lavender/) and [Regie.ai](/en/tools/regie-ai/) used to be an easy call: one graded the email a rep was already writing, the other wrote the sequence for them. That gap closed in 2026. Lavender now sells two products — the Email Coach browser extension and Ora, an agent that researches, writes, and sends on its own. Regie.ai folded its Co-Pilot assistant and Auto-Pilot agents into RegieOne and sells it as a replacement for your sales engagement platform, not a layer on top of one.

So the question is no longer "coach or generator." It is how much of the outbound stack you are willing to hand over, and whether you already own a contact list.

## Where Lavender wins

- **The rep gets better.** Coach scores each draft 0-100 inside the inbox — sentence length, spam triggers, subject-line grade, tone, personalization depth — and shows why a draft is a 58 and not an 82. Regie.ai's agents write the email; nobody on the team writes better six months later. When the reply-rate problem is a skill problem, that distinction is the whole decision.
- **You can start at zero and stay month-to-month.** Coach has a Free Forever tier (5 analyzed emails and 5 personalizations per month), then $29/mo Starter, $49/mo Individual Pro, and $99/seat/mo Team on monthly billing — $27, $45, and $89 on annual. No seat minimum, no sales call, 7-day trial without a credit card. Every published Regie.ai plan requires a sales conversation and an annual contract.
- **Ora is priced per agent, not per seat.** One Ora agent is $500/month at launch volume — 1,000 emails a month reaching 4,000+ contacts a year — with no annual commitment, and agents are free to build until you launch them. Regie.ai's closest equivalent, Force Multiplier Rep, is $499/user/month with a 5-seat minimum on an annual contract. Near-identical headline price, 5× different entry cost.
- **Nothing to migrate.** Coach installs as a Chrome extension against Gmail or Outlook 365 and runs inside Outreach, Salesloft, and HubSpot. Reps keep their sequencer and their workflow. Regie.ai's entire pitch is that it replaces the sequencer.

## Where Regie.ai wins

- **It brings the data.** RegieOne includes access to 220+ million contacts with verified mobile numbers at no add-on cost, plus over 100 built-in signals pulled from LinkedIn, 10-K filings, G2, Crunchbase, BuiltWith, company news, call transcripts, and CRM records. Force Multiplier Rep bundles 120,000 AI and enrichment credits (1,000 accounts / 4,000 contacts) into the seat price. Ora takes a CSV upload or a Salesforce connection and will not source prospects for you — without a list and a data vendor already in place, a $500/month Ora agent has nothing to send to.
- **Multichannel from one surface.** Email, sequencing, social, and an AI parallel dialer run inside RegieOne with reporting across all of them, and Force Multiplier ships 10 mailboxes with domain warming included. Lavender is email-only in both products; a phone-heavy motion still needs a separate dialer next to it.
- **Agents and reps share one queue.** RegieOne assigns tasks to Auto-Pilot agents and to human reps in the same workflow, so an account an agent warms up lands in a rep's task list instead of a second tool. Ora runs its campaign beside your SEP rather than inside it, which means two systems of record for outbound activity.
- **Consolidation is the budget argument.** At 10 seats, Regie.ai displaces an SEP license, an enrichment contract, and a dialer. Measured against Outreach or Salesloft plus ZoomInfo plus a dialer, $180/user/month reads as a line-item reduction. That argument is real — it just isn't an argument against Lavender.

## Pricing reality

**Coaching layer, 10 reps.** Coach Team at $89/seat/month on annual billing is $10,680/year, or $11,880 billed monthly.

**Sales engagement platform, 10 reps.** Regie.ai AI SEP at $180/user/month against its 10-seat minimum is $21,600/year — 2× the Coach bill, for a different job. Add the AI parallel dialer at $1,800/user/year and the same 10 seats reach $39,600/year, 3.7× Coach. Mailbox rotation is $50/user/month for 5 mailboxes or $100/user/month for 10, and data packages run from Bronze (525,000 credits, 5k accounts / 20k contacts) to Platinum (6,080,000 credits, 40k accounts / 300k contacts) on top of that. The dialer is not sold standalone — it only ships in a package that includes agents.

**Autonomous outbound.** One Ora agent is $6,000/year at $500/month, cancellable monthly, which works out to $0.50 per email at the launch allowance. Regie.ai Force Multiplier Rep's 5-seat minimum puts its floor at $29,940/year on an annual contract. That is a 5× gap at entry, and it narrows as you launch more Ora agents and as Regie's included credits displace a separate data spend.

A 10-rep team running Coach Team plus one Ora agent pays $16,680/year — still under Regie.ai's AI-SEP-plus-dialer configuration, and that stack has no dialer, no contact database, and no sequencer.

## Implementation effort

Lavender Coach: install the extension, authenticate the mailbox, done in an afternoon. Security-conscious organizations will route the mailbox authentication through an IT review first, and that review is the long pole, not the setup.

Ora is heavier than Coach and lighter than a platform. Upload the list or connect Salesforce, let the agent read the inbox to learn what has worked, then correct its understanding and give it instructions before launch. Budget a week of supervised sending before it runs unattended.

Regie.ai: 3-6 weeks. Sequences move onto RegieOne, agents get configured against your ICP and messaging, the data package gets provisioned, and the dialer needs numbers and compliance setup. You are replacing the system of record for outbound activity, and the rollback path is re-migrating every sequence.

## Verdict

- **Pick Lavender** when the sequencer you have works and the binding constraint is email quality; when you want a per-agent, month-to-month way to test autonomous sending without signing an annual contract; or when you already buy contact data from ZoomInfo, Clay, or Apollo and need something that writes and sends well against it.
- **Pick Regie.ai** when you are replacing an SEP anyway; when you do not own a contact database and would otherwise buy one separately; when the motion is multichannel with real phone volume; or when 10+ seats and an annual commitment are already approved.
- **Pick neither** when you need a sequencer with light AI and nothing more. Apollo, Smartlead, and lemlist cover generate-and-send at a fraction of both, and if you are already on Outreach or Salesloft, check what their native AI assist covers before adding a third writing tool.

Choosing in a vacuum, start with Lavender. Coach is free to trial and reversible, and Ora is $500 you can stop next month. Regie.ai is a $21,600 floor and a 3-6 week migration, and the decision it forces — replace the SEP — deserves to be made deliberately, not as a side effect of buying an AI writer.
