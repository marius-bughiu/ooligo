## What it is

Wonderlic is the Chicago-area assessment company behind the 50-question, 12-minute cognitive test that has been a hiring shorthand since the 1930s. It still sells that idea, now packaged as **Wonderlic Select** (the product formerly called WonScore): one candidate session that measures cognitive ability, personality, and motivation (Wonderlic calls the last one "interests"), then rolls the three into a single job-fit score against a scoring profile for the role. The cognitive section is short and timed, typically about 12 minutes; the personality and motivation questionnaires are untimed.

The second product is **Wonderlic Develop**, a post-hire module that reuses the same results to help managers coach and retain people. Since June 2026 it includes **Ask Wonderlic**, an AI coach built into Develop at no extra cost that draws on an employee's results and role to suggest 1-on-1 talking points.

The company is still independent and family-controlled: Charlie Wonderlic, grandson of founder E.F. Wonderlic, is executive chairman, and Becca Callahan, formerly head of product at Hogan Assessments, is CEO. We found no acquisition or wind-down reports in the last 12 months.

Wonderlic is **not** an ATS, a video-interviewing tool, or a skills-test library. It scores candidates at one point in the funnel and hands the result to your ATS.

## What changed in 2026

- **Select redesign (announced June 4, 2026; live for all customers by July 27, 2026).** Eight updates: an openings dashboard, combined candidate and job search, AI-generated job summaries on scoring profiles (Wonderlic states these do not change scoring), the five most predictive indicators at the top of each result, a candidate list with one overall fit score, side-by-side finalist comparison, a per-candidate testing timeline, and a **standardized assessment battery** so every candidate for a job takes the same tests whether they come in by email, Quick Apply link, or onsite.
- **AI-cheating signal (February 2026).** Cognitive results now carry an "accuracy warning" when the response pattern suggests outside help, including AI tools. It cannot be switched off, and Wonderlic calls it "a signal, not a verdict."
- **ATS-free automation (August 2026).** Quick Apply links with external candidate IDs and daily or weekly data reports by email or SFTP, for teams without an integration.

## Pricing

- **Quote only.** Wonderlic publishes no prices. Its own FAQ says pricing depends on the package and your employee count, and that every package includes implementation, training, and support. It sells packages rather than per-assessment credits, so you can test every applicant.
- **Real-world band (third-party, not vendor-confirmed):** Software Advice and GetApp list Select from about **$6,000 a year**. Treat that as the floor for a small organization; packages grow with headcount.
- **Renewal risk is real.** A GetApp reviewer reported a renewal at "over 300%" of the prior fee, and G2 small-business reviewers say the flat rate is hard to justify in slow hiring months.
- **Anchor:** [TestGorilla](/en/tools/testgorilla/)'s 2026 Core plan is about $2,580 a year for 750 candidate credits. Below a few hundred tested candidates a year, Wonderlic has to win on validation and job-fit scoring, not price.

Review scores at time of checking: 4.2/5 on G2 (189 reviews) and 4.5/5 on Software Advice (166 reviews).

## Best for

TA and HR leads at small and mid-sized employers (roughly 50 to 2,000 staff) hiring repeatedly into hourly, sales, service, and early-career roles, who want one short, validated screen per applicant and a single fit score a hiring manager can read without training. It fits best where the same results will be reused after hire through Develop.

**Do not buy Wonderlic if** you mainly need coding or role-specific skills tests, you want async video interviews from the same vendor, or you test fewer than about 200 candidates a year.

## Versus the alternatives

- **[Criteria](/en/tools/criteria-corp/)**: the closest rival. The CCAT is the same shape (50 questions, 15 minutes) and Criteria adds video and AI interviewing plus 75+ ATS partners. Pick Criteria when you want assessments and interviews on one contract; pick Wonderlic when you want cognitive, personality, and motivation fused into one job-fit score and a post-hire coaching layer.
- **[SHL](/en/tools/shl/)**: the market-share leader in enterprise psychometrics. Pick it for global, high-stakes selection that must survive legal challenge in many countries.
- **[HireVue](/en/tools/hirevue/)**: the share leader in enterprise video interviewing with assessments attached. Pick it when the interview, not the test, is the core of the screen.
- **[TestGorilla](/en/tools/testgorilla/)**: the fastest-growing entrant, with self-serve plans and a large test library. Pick it for SMB budgets, many role-specific skills tests, and month-to-month buying.
- **[Plum](/en/tools/plum/)** and **[Harver](/en/tools/harver/)**: pick Plum for talent-potential matching across internal mobility, Harver for frontline programs hiring thousands a month with job simulations.

If none fit because volume is low, a rubric-scored work sample followed by a [structured interview](/en/learn/structured-interviewing/) beats any annual package. For the broader shift, see [skills-based hiring](/en/learn/skills-based-hiring/).

## Watch-outs

- **Prep material and AI help are everywhere.** The classic test is the most practiced cognitive test on the internet, and the accuracy warning only flags patterns; it does not proctor. *Guard:* treat a flagged result as a prompt to retest in a supervised setting, as Wonderlic itself recommends, and re-probe high scorers with an interview question that tests the same reasoning. See [AI interview fraud](/en/learn/ai-interview-fraud/).
- **Cognitive tests carry adverse-impact risk.** Timed cognitive screens are the assessment type most often linked to group score gaps, and the fused fit score can hide which component drives a reject. *Guard:* run a quarterly pass-rate check by segment on your own data (the [screening score variance audit](/en/workflows/screening-score-variance-audit-skill/) is built for this), keep a human on every reject, and check bias-audit duties under [NYC Local Law 144](/en/learn/nyc-local-law-144/).
- **Integrations are thin.** Wonderlic names [Greenhouse](/en/tools/greenhouse/), [JazzHR](/en/tools/jazzhr/), and [iCIMS](/en/tools/icims/); we found no public API or MCP server. *Guard:* test invite, completion, and score write-back on your own ATS before signing, and budget for the SFTP report path if your ATS is not listed.
- **Price moves at renewal.** *Guard:* negotiate a renewal cap and a headcount band in the first contract, and get the package's test-volume terms in writing.
