## What it is

SurveySparrow is a survey and customer-experience suite built around conversational surveys: one question per screen in a chat-style layout, designed to keep mobile respondents from abandoning a long form. The same account covers classic surveys, an NPS/CSAT/CES "CX" module, offline kiosk surveys, 360 assessments and review management. The company is independent (founded 2017) and, as checked on 21 November 2026, has no acquisition or sunset notice. It markets itself directly to teams leaving Delighted, which Qualtrics shut on 30 June 2026, and GetFeedback Direct, which SurveyMonkey ends on 31 December 2026.

The AI layer has three named parts. **Echo**, launched September 2026, is an agent that asks a respondent follow-up questions and can then alert an owner, open a ticket or update the CRM; every plan gets a quota (2 Echo agents on Free, 25 on paid plans). **CogniVue** is text analytics for themes, emotion-level sentiment and key-driver analysis, and the pricing page lists Key Driver Analysis on Enterprise only. **Copilot** answers plain-language questions about the data. Generative features run on OpenAI (US) and Azure OpenAI (EU), and SurveySparrow says customer data is not used for training. There is no MCP server as of this check.

It is not a customer success platform (no health scores or renewal playbooks) and not a research panel.

## Why it shows up in Customer Success stacks

- **It is the cheapest credible restart for a small NPS program.** A forever-free plan and published paid tiers mean a two-person CS team can relaunch a post-Delighted survey without a sales call. Our [Delighted alternatives comparison](/en/vs/alternatives-to-delighted/) names it the route for low seat count and lumpy volume.
- **The score reaches the tools CS already runs.** [HubSpot](/en/tools/hubspot/) is included from Starter, [Zendesk](/en/tools/zendesk/) from Business, [Slack](/en/tools/slack/) from Basic, with [Freshdesk](/en/tools/freshdesk/), [Microsoft Teams](/en/tools/microsoft-teams/), [Zapier](/en/tools/zapier/), webhooks and a REST API covering the rest.
- **Enterprise is a real option, not a dead end.** [Salesforce](/en/tools/salesforce/) and [ServiceNow](/en/tools/servicenow/) sync, SSO (Okta, Entra ID, PingOne), SMS and WhatsApp sending, and a choice of hosting region (US, Canada, EU, UK, UAE, India or Australia) are available, though only on Enterprise and CX plans. Warehouse exports include [Snowflake](/en/tools/snowflake/), BigQuery and Databricks.

## Pricing reality

Checked 21 November 2026. The pricing page shows plan limits but loads prices client-side, and the navigation links to a quote form. SurveySparrow's own fact sheet confirms paid plans "from $19 per month" and Enterprise "from $5,000 per year" on annual contracts. Independent price trackers consistently report, billed annually: **Basic $19/month** (2,500 responses, 1 user), **Starter $39** (15,000 responses, 1 user, HubSpot), **Business $79** (36,000 responses, 3 users, custom branding, webhooks, Zendesk). Quarterly billing costs more; there is no monthly option. The paid response counts read as yearly pools rather than monthly caps, though the page does not state the period. **Free** gives 75 responses per quarter, 3 active surveys and 10 questions per survey (the page's FAQ still says 50 a month, so treat 75 per quarter as the current figure).

The fee that decides the bill is **Additional Users at $49 per user per month**. Business with 3 users is about $950 a year; add five more managers who want logins and you add $2,940. The second jump is the enterprise gate: Salesforce sync, SSO, SMS/WhatsApp and CogniVue driver analysis start at roughly $5,000 a year. Read the realistic band as $0–$950 a year for a small email-and-link NPS program, and $5,000–$15,000 once CRM sync or SSO is required. HIPAA is a paid add-on with a signed BAA.

## Best for

A CS operations lead or CX manager at a 10–200-person company relaunching a one-question NPS or CSAT program after Delighted, with one to three people who need logins, email and link distribution, and a HubSpot or Zendesk sync. It fits when the budget line is under $1,000 a year and nobody needs Salesforce.

## Versus the alternatives

- **[SurveyMonkey](/en/tools/surveymonkey/)** — the volume leader in general surveys and GetFeedback Direct's designated successor. Pick it when one license must also cover market research and internal surveys.
- **[Qualtrics](/en/tools/qualtrics/)** — the market-share leader in experience management. Pick it when CX is a funded, multi-journey program with a budget of $30K or more.
- **[Retently](/en/tools/retently/)** — the fastest-moving entrant in B2B NPS, with account-level scoring and its own MCP server. Pick it when the program is B2B, account-based and you want scores rolled up per company rather than per respondent.
- **[Zonka Feedback](/en/tools/zonka-feedback/)** — pick it when you survey many locations or channels and need closed-loop cases with per-location permissions.
- **[Typeform](/en/tools/typeform/)** — the other conversational-format option, from $28/month for 100 responses a month. Pick it when the survey doubles as a lead-capture form for marketing.
- **[Simplesat](/en/tools/simplesat/)** — pick it when the job is agent-level support CSAT inside the help desk.

## Watch-outs

- **The seat fee outgrows the plan.** At $49 per extra user per month, eight logins on Business cost about three times the plan itself. **Guard:** keep one or two editor seats, route results to managers through Slack, Teams or a shared dashboard, and price the user count you will have in 12 months before you buy.
- **The features CS teams ask for sit behind Enterprise.** Salesforce sync, SSO, SMS/WhatsApp and key-driver analysis are not on Basic, Starter or Business. **Guard:** list the integrations you need before the trial, and if any of them is Enterprise-only, compare the $5,000-a-year floor against Retently or Zonka quotes instead of the $79 plan.
- **Published numbers disagree with each other.** The free plan reads 75 per quarter on the plan card and 50 a month in the FAQ, paid response periods are unstated, and prices load only in the browser. **Guard:** get the response allowance, its period and the overage rate written into the order form, and screenshot the plan page on the day you buy.
- **No standard uptime SLA.** SurveySparrow's fact sheet says so directly, and 24×6 support is a paid add-on. **Guard:** do not put a survey on a customer-facing critical path (checkout, onboarding gate) without a negotiated SLA; trigger surveys asynchronously from your CRM or help desk instead.
- **The NPS series breaks at migration.** New wording, a conversational layout or a different send time moves scores with no change in customers. **Guard:** keep the question text and scale identical to Delighted's, mark the cutover date on every chart, and compare one full cycle before reporting a trend.
