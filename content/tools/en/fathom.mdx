## What it is

Fathom is an AI notetaker that records, transcribes, and summarizes Zoom, Meet, and Teams calls, then pushes the summary and action items into [Salesforce](/en/tools/salesforce/), [HubSpot](/en/tools/hubspot/), Slack, Notion, or Asana. It started as the freemium answer to [Gong](/en/tools/gong/): free forever for one person, paid when you need CRM writeback or search across a team's calls.

That framing is now only half true. Fathom's Business tier has grown a light coaching layer (Deal View, which rolls every call on a deal into one summary, plus coaching metrics and AI scorecards), and the vendor openly courts Gong customers: teams switching from Gong get Business free for the rest of their Gong contract, with data migration. Capture changed too. Every plan offers a choice of bot capture or bot-free capture, the latter still a Mac beta. A public API, an MCP server, and first-party ChatGPT and Claude integrations let an agent query call history directly. The domain moved from fathom.video to fathom.ai; the help center still lives on the old one.

## Why it shows up in RevOps stacks

- **The free tier has no recording cap.** Unlimited recording, transcription, summaries, clips, and search at $0 is why reps adopt it before procurement hears about it.
- **Summaries are usable on the first call.** 15+ expert templates, including BANT and Sandler frameworks, with no prompt-tuning pass before the output is worth reading.
- **Agent access is first-party.** Premium and every paid Team plan get unlimited MCP access, so transcripts wire into a Claude or ChatGPT workflow without a third-party connector. "Unlimited" refers to access duration, not request volume.
- **Recruiting crossover.** TA teams run it on candidate intake calls: same workflow, different ICP.

## Pricing reality

Per user per month, checked 2026-11-20 at fathom.ai/pricing and the Fathom help center.

- **Free** — $0. Unlimited recording, transcription, AI summaries, clips, and search.
- **Premium** — $20 monthly, $16 annual. Single seat. Advanced summaries, AI action items, a conversational meeting assistant, a custom meeting bot.
- **Team** — $19 monthly, $15 annual, 2-user minimum. Shared workspace, trackers, Ask Fathom across team calls, team security controls, and HIPAA compliance with a standard BAA.
- **Business** — $34 monthly, $25 annual, 2-user minimum. CRM field sync, Deal View, coaching metrics and AI scorecards, custom summaries for your organization.
- **Enterprise** — $35, annual only. SSO, Okta SCIM provisioning, custom data retention, custom BAA eligibility, Launch Assist implementation, a dedicated success manager, priority support SLAs.

Three things the price list does not say out loud. **Team costs less per seat than Premium** ($19 against $20 monthly, $15 against $16 annual), so any pair of users belongs on Team. Premium is only right for a solo operator.

The second is the CRM ceiling. On Free, Premium, and Team, **at most 3 users per email domain** can connect to a CRM; Business and Enterprise are unlimited. A 20-rep team that wants every rep writing back to the pipeline is on Business, not Team: 20 seats at $25 is $6,000 a year, not the $3,600 the Team line suggests. Count writeback seats before you pick the tier.

The third is that Enterprise is no longer a quote. At $35 annual it is $10 a seat over Business, which turns "do we need SSO?" from a procurement project into a line-item decision: $2,400 a year extra for that same 20-rep team.

## Best for

RevOps and sales leaders at 5-50 rep teams who want call summaries attached to the opportunity record, deal-level rollups, and basic rep scorecards, without buying a forecasting platform. Also TA teams capturing structured intake notes without signing an interview-intelligence contract.

**Do not use it if** you need forecast roll-ups, pipeline inspection, or deal-risk scoring. Business adds coaching metrics and scorecards, but no forecasting or deal-risk feature appears on any tier. That is the line where [Gong](/en/tools/gong/) or [Clari](/en/tools/clari/) still earns its contract.

## Versus the alternatives

- **[Gong](/en/tools/gong/)** — Fathom records, summarizes, and now scores calls; Gong forecasts. Pick Gong when a VP Sales owns the budget and wants forecast risk and pipeline inspection, and can defend a five-figure annual contract against Fathom Business at $300 per seat per year. If you are leaving Gong mostly for cost, take Fathom's free-through-your-contract offer and run both in parallel before the renewal date.
- **[Otter.ai](/en/tools/otter-ai/) and [Fireflies](/en/tools/fireflies/)** — the two highest-volume notetakers by seat count. Otter has repositioned as an org-wide knowledge layer rather than a per-user notetaker; Fireflies meters AI usage against a monthly credit ceiling and tiers storage. Pick Fireflies when you want cheaper seats and can live with the credit ceiling; pick Fathom when you want per-seat cost flat and AI usage uncapped inside your own calls.
- **[Granola](/en/tools/granola/)** — the fastest-growing entrant, built around augmenting the notes you type yourself, with no bot in the room. Pick Granola when the meetings are internal and a recording bot is socially awkward. Pick Fathom when the calls are external, need CRM writeback, and someone will ask for the recording later. Fathom's bot-free capture narrows that gap, but it is a Mac-only beta: Granola is note-first, Fathom is recording-first.

## Watch-outs

- **The 3-users-per-domain CRM cap below Business.** Guard: count the seats that genuinely need writeback before signing. Above 3, budget Business at $25 a seat from day one instead of finding the ceiling in month two.
- **Scorecards are not forecasting.** Guard: if a forecast-accuracy mandate is anywhere in next year's plan, treat Fathom as the call-capture layer and keep the export path open. The public API and MCP server make the call history portable to [Gong](/en/tools/gong/) or [Chorus](/en/tools/chorus/) later.
- **Bottom-up adoption outruns recording policy.** Guard: reps switching on a notetaker with no policy behind it is how two-party-consent states turn into a legal problem. Write the consent and retention rule before the free tier spreads, and note that custom retention exists only on Enterprise.
- **Bot-free capture is a beta, and Mac-only.** Guard: if the reason you are buying is "no bot in the call," test it on your Windows users first, or plan for a mixed fleet where some calls still carry the bot.
