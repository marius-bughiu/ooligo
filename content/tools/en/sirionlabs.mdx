## What it is

SirionLabs is the AI-native enterprise CLM that pitched itself on contract intelligence — extracting obligations, risks, and value leakage from existing contract repositories — before generative AI was mainstream. The platform now spans pre-signature workflow (drafting, negotiation, approvals) and post-signature performance (obligation tracking, supplier scorecards, value realization). Strong traction in industries where post-signature contract performance is a board-level metric: telecom, manufacturing, financial services, energy.

## Why it shows up in Legal Ops stacks

- **Post-signature is the differentiator.** Most CLMs end at signature; Sirion treats signature as the start of the contract's value cycle. Tracks deliverables, SLAs, obligations, and price changes against the executed contract.
- **AI-native architecture.** Built post-2017 with ML at the core, not retrofitted onto a legacy CLM database. Clause extraction, risk scoring, and obligation parsing benefit from this foundation.
- **Buy-side strength.** Procurement and supplier-management use cases are first-class, not afterthoughts. Useful for organizations whose biggest contract risk lives in the supplier portfolio.

## Pricing

- **Custom only.** Enterprise pricing typically starts in the low six figures for mid-size deployments, scaling into seven for global rollouts.
- **Module packaging.** Sirion Pre-Signature, Sirion Post-Signature, and Sirion AI typically purchased together but can be unbundled.
- Implementation usually 4-8 months — faster than Icertis at comparable enterprise scale, slower than mid-market plays like Concord or LinkSquares.

## Best for

- Enterprises whose contract risk is heavily post-signature (long-tail vendor obligations, telecom MSAs, complex outsourcing contracts)
- Procurement-heavy organizations needing supplier-side contract intelligence
- Mid-market and enterprise teams that find Icertis too heavyweight but Ironclad too sales-side-focused

## Watch-outs

- Less Salesforce-native than Conga; not the natural fit for sales-led organizations
- Smaller partner ecosystem than Icertis or Conga; verify implementation partner availability for your region
- Brand recognition lower than Ironclad in US growth-stage tech; stronger fit in industries that already prioritize contract performance management
- Generative-AI feature pace is competitive — verify current capabilities against your specific clause types and contract volume during pilot
