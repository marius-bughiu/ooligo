## What it is

Cloudingo is a deduplication and data-management tool for [Salesforce](/en/tools/salesforce/), built by Symphonic Source Inc. of Dallas, Texas (founded 2010, per G2). Unlike [DemandTools](/en/tools/demandtools/), it is a web app: you create a Cloudingo account, connect your org over OAuth, and install a small Cloudingo agent from the AppExchange. You build filters (match rules from exact to fuzzy), review the duplicate groups each filter finds, set master-record and field-survival rules, and merge by hand, in bulk, or on a schedule. The same app mass-updates, mass-deletes, standardizes, converts leads, and imports CSV files while matching them against existing records. Cloudingo says it does not export or store your records, only an index of the fields used for matching.

Two newer pieces matter for buyers. Cloudingo Intelligence, included on every tier, monitors org health and security. And Cloudingo now lists a HubSpot app on the HubSpot Marketplace covering contacts, companies, deals, tickets and custom objects; it had 10+ installs and no reviews when we checked on 2026-11-24, so treat it as early. Cloudingo also covers Marketo data. G2 shows 4.4 out of 5 from 37 reviews, with support the most praised item.

What it is not: an enrichment source, a routing engine, or an AI product. It does not fill empty fields from a third-party database, and it names no AI features beyond its matching algorithms.

## The question it answers

**Cloudingo, DemandTools or Insycle, now that RingLead is part of ZoomInfo?** ZoomInfo bought RingLead in September 2021 and folded it into ZoomInfo Operations, which leaves Cloudingo and DemandTools as the two independent Salesforce dedupe specialists. Pick Cloudingo when you want a browser-based tool with a published price that does not grow with your Salesforce seat count. Pick DemandTools when you want the deepest rule set and run the jobs from an admin's desktop.

A typical scoped job: a RevOps admin at a 250-seat org with 450,000 leads, contacts and accounts builds a fuzzy company-name filter plus an email filter, previews the groups, merges 18,000 duplicate contacts in a weekend, then turns on real-time merge so new duplicates are merged as they are created instead of piling up until the next batch.

## Pricing

Cloudingo publishes its prices. Checked 2026-11-24 on cloudingo.com:

- **Standard, $2,500 a year**: 1 user, standard objects only, mass merge, convert, update and delete, scheduled jobs, the standard import wizard, email support.
- **Professional, $6,000 a year**: 3 users, up to 3 custom objects, real-time merge, undo and restore of merges, the rapid import wizard, personal onboarding.
- **Enterprise, $10,000 a year**: 8 users, unlimited custom objects, API access (1,000 calls a day), a customer success manager, security audits and contract changes.
- **Record fee.** Prices are per Salesforce org, not per user. Above 300,000 records (leads, contacts, accounts, person accounts, plus custom objects in scope) you pay $100 per extra 100,000. A 500,000-record org on Professional pays $6,200; a 1.2 million-record org pays $6,900.
- **HubSpot**: $500 a year per HubSpot account, with the same $100-per-100,000 fee above 300,000 records (Marketplace pricing updated 2026-10-28).
- **Terms**: annual only, no monthly plans. One license covers one production org and one sandbox. Address validation is a paid add-on. A 10-day trial lets you act on 25 duplicate groups.

The real-world band for a Salesforce team is $6,000-$7,000 a year, because the Professional features (undo, real-time merge, custom objects) are the ones most teams need.

## Best for

- A Salesforce admin or RevOps lead at a 100-1,000 seat org with a duplicate backlog in the tens to hundreds of thousands, who wants a browser tool with a fixed published price instead of seat-based licensing
- Teams that need duplicates merged as they are created, not only in batches
- Not for HubSpot-only teams that need a proven tool today (the HubSpot app is new), and not for teams whose real problem is empty fields or routing

## Alternatives — and when to pick them instead

- **[DemandTools](/en/tools/demandtools/)**: priced per Salesforce seat, so Vendr's median of $16,764 a year at 200 seats compares with about $6,200 for Cloudingo Professional at 500,000 records. Pick DemandTools for its deeper rule set and DupeBlocker, which third-party guides report is included; pick Cloudingo when seat count is high and you want a web app.
- **[Insycle](/en/tools/insycle/)**: priced per record, unlimited users, HubSpot-first. 100,000 records on Growth costs $1,800 a year, under Cloudingo Standard; at 500,000 records Growth costs $9,000, above Cloudingo Professional. Pick Insycle for small or HubSpot-centered databases.
- **[ZoomInfo](/en/tools/zoominfo/) Operations** (formerly RingLead) or **[Openprise](/en/tools/openprise/)**: pick them when dedupe is one part of a larger job that also covers enrichment, normalization and routing, and the budget is in the tens of thousands.
- **Salesforce duplicate and matching rules**: free, and enough when the backlog is a few thousand records.
- **An agent against the Salesforce API**: the fastest-growing approach in the category. The [Salesforce data cleanup skill](/en/workflows/salesforce-data-cleanup-skill/) drafts match rules and merge plans from Claude Code. Pick it for a one-off cleanup by a technical admin; keep Cloudingo for scheduled and real-time merging that a non-developer can run.

## Watch-outs

- **Standard has no undo.** Merge undo and restore start at Professional, and Salesforce itself cannot unmerge records. Guard: buy Professional if anyone other than one careful admin will merge, or export the affected objects before every mass merge on Standard.
- **Standard ignores custom objects and allows one user.** Teams find this after the trial. Guard: list the objects and the people who will run jobs before you pick a tier; three custom objects or a second user means Professional.
- **The record fee follows imports.** A purchased list or an acquisition can push the org past a new 100,000 band. Guard: delete or archive dead leads before renewal and model the fee in the budget for any planned import.
- **Mass jobs use your Salesforce API allocation.** Cloudingo works through the Salesforce API, so a large automated merge competes with your other integrations. Guard: schedule big jobs overnight, and preview each automation on a filtered sample of 50 groups before letting it run on the whole org.
