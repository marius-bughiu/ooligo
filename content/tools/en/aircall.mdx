## What it is

Aircall is a cloud phone system for SMB and mid-market sales and support teams: business numbers, IVR and routing, a shared SMS and WhatsApp inbox, call recording, a power dialer, and an app-marketplace model that drops calls straight into the CRM or help desk. It is Paris-founded, privately held, serves more than 23,000 businesses by its own count, and crossed $200M in ARR according to coverage of its 2026 deals.

The reason to look at it now is that Aircall has turned itself into an AI-agent vendor in five months. It bought Vogent, a San Francisco voice-AI company, on 6 May 2026 for its speech models, turn-taking, and interruption handling. It launched AI Messaging Agents for SMS and WhatsApp on 27 May. It bought Piper AI, a revenue-intelligence and sales-orchestration startup, in early June. And its 22 September update added outbound AI voice campaigns, agent templates, Salesforce Knowledge lookups, and support for 38 languages. The pitch is that the AI agent lives on the same numbers your team already answers, so there is no second telephony vendor to wire in.

## Where it fits

- **The AI Voice Agent answers, qualifies, and transfers on your existing lines.** It reads knowledge from Zendesk, Salesforce, HubSpot, or Document360, looks the caller up in the CRM during the greeting, reads and writes records such as cases and opportunities mid-call, and hands off with Agent Memory: caller details, transfer reason, and history passed to the human. Aircall's only published metric is from a pilot, 69% less handling time after a transfer; it has published no containment rate.
- **Outbound campaigns run the same agent against a list.** Upload contacts, pick an agent, and it works the list, books meetings, and pulls in a rep when needed. Campaigns ship on every plan and draw on the same minute pool.
- **The integrations are the moat.** More than 250 native integrations, with HubSpot, Salesforce, Zendesk, Intercom, and Pipedrive the ones support and sales teams actually buy for. Aircall publishes a REST API but no official MCP server; the MCP servers that exist for it are third-party (CData, MCPBundles, community builds), so audit one before giving it call and contact data.

## Pricing reality

Seats, billed annually: **Essentials $30 per license per month** (unlimited US/Canada calling, SMS, shared inbox, recordings and AI transcripts, IVR, 6 months of analytics) and **Professional $50** (adds AI call summaries and conversation intelligence, smart routing, power dialer, monitoring and barge, full API). Both have a 3-license minimum. Enterprise is quote-only with a 25-license minimum and is where SSO, SCIM, HIPAA, and SLA terms live. Third-party guides put month-to-month billing at about $40 and $70.

AI sits on top. The Voice Agent is metered: $0.49 per minute pay-as-you-go, bundles of 500 minutes for $175 and 2,500 for $725 (about $0.29 per minute), and down to $0.19 per minute above 10,000 per Aircall's FAQ, with a $100 monthly floor if you run voice agents without seats. Plans include 50 agent minutes a month. AI Messaging Agents cost $0.15-$0.35 per conversation; AI Assist Pro (live coaching) is $49 per seat. Other add-ons stack fast: Analytics+ $15 per seat, WhatsApp $10 per seat, Salesforce Voice $30 per seat.

Real numbers: a 10-seat Essentials support desk is about $3,600 a year. A 25-seat Professional team with AI Assist Pro and 2,500 voice-agent minutes a month is about $38,400 a year ($15,000 seats + $14,700 coaching + $8,700 minutes), before numbers and international calling.

## Best for

Support and sales ops leaders running 5 to roughly 150 phone seats on HubSpot, Salesforce, Zendesk, or Intercom who want an AI agent to take after-hours calls, FAQs, and lead qualification on the numbers customers already dial, without buying and integrating a separate voice-AI platform.

Skip it if you run a 200+ seat contact center with workforce management, QA, and complex routing needs; that is CCaaS territory.

## Versus the alternatives

**RingCentral** and **Dialpad** hold the largest share of business cloud phone. Pick RingCentral when you need video, UCaaS, and a phone system for the whole company rather than for customer-facing teams; pick Dialpad when you want transcription and AI summaries bundled from its cheaper entry tier instead of gated at Professional. Pick Aircall over both when the buyer is the sales or support team and depth of CRM and help-desk integration decides the deal.

At contact-center scale, [Five9](/en/tools/five9/) and [Genesys Cloud](/en/tools/genesys-cloud/) are the step up: pick them past roughly 200 seats or when outbound compliance and WEM matter.

The real question this page answers is native agent versus specialist. Retell AI, one of the fastest-growing voice-agent platforms (named to Brex's 25 fastest-growing startups in September 2026), and enterprise specialists [PolyAI](/en/tools/polyai/) and [Parloa](/en/tools/parloa/) build deeper agents. Rule: if your call reasons are FAQs, booking, order status, and qualification, run Aircall's native agent for 60 days and measure containment yourself. Buy a specialist only when your calls need multi-step authenticated transactions or regulated scripts, and the specialist's resolution rate beats native by enough to pay for a second contract and the integration work.

## Watch-outs

- **Three acquisitions in five months means product churn.** Vogent and Piper are being merged into Aircall's stack, and templates are English-only for now. Guard: date-stamp what you pilot, get the features you depend on named in the order form, and re-test after each monthly update.
- **AI costs are metered and add-ons compound.** The seat price on the pricing page is not the bill. Guard: model your cost from three months of real call volume by reason, buy a minute bundle sized to that, and get an overage alert configured before launch.
- **Call quality and billing are the recurring review complaints.** Aircall averages 4.4 on G2 and 4.2 on Capterra, but connection drops, choppy audio, and charges after cancellation recur. Guard: test call quality on your own network (Aircall recommends 100 Kbps per concurrent call, wired over Wi-Fi), and get cancellation and renewal-notice terms in writing; third-party reviews report 30 days' written notice for accounts over 10 seats.
- **No published containment benchmark.** One pilot metric on post-transfer handle time is not proof the agent resolves calls. Guard: set your own containment and transfer-rate targets for the top five call reasons and make any annual AI commitment contingent on hitting them.
