## What it is

Perplexity is an AI answer engine: ask a question, get a synthesized answer with inline citations you can click through to the source. Around that core sit Spaces (persistent research projects with pinned sources), the Comet browser with an on-page assistant, the Sonar API for developers, and Enterprise tiers with SSO and internal-data connectors. It's the default tool for ops folks who want grounded, cited answers — not raw model output — when researching companies, regulations, or candidates.

## Why it shows up in ops stacks

- **Grounded by default.** Every answer lists its sources, which matters for ICP research, competitor intel, and any output headed to a CRO or GC who will ask "where did this come from?"
- **Spaces for project research.** Pin sources, re-run queries against them, and share the Space with the team — better suited to an ongoing research workstream than a one-off [ChatGPT](/en/tools/chatgpt/) thread.
- **Real-time web, built in.** [Claude](/en/tools/claude/) and ChatGPT both search now, but Perplexity's whole UX is organized around retrieval and citation rather than bolting it onto a chat.
- **Official MCP server.** Perplexity ships a first-party MCP server (`@perplexity-ai/mcp-server`), so you can wire Sonar search and deep research into Claude, [Cursor](/en/tools/cursor/), or your own agent instead of copy-pasting answers out of the app.

## Pricing

- **Free** — roughly 5 Pro searches/day plus unlimited basic search. Fine for occasional lookups.
- **Pro** — $20/mo ($200/yr): unlimited Pro Search, ~20 deep-research runs/day, access to the frontier model picker, and the Comet assistant. This is the tier most individual operators want.
- **Max** — $200/mo ($2,000/yr): removes research/Labs limits and adds Model Council (one query fanned across multiple frontier models with a synthesized answer) plus the highest file and storage caps. A power-user tier, not a team default.
- **Enterprise Pro** — $40/seat/mo ($400/seat/yr): SSO, SCIM, admin controls, SOC 2 Type II, Internal Knowledge Search over your own docs, and — critically — your data is not used for training.
- **Enterprise Max** — $325/seat/mo ($3,250/seat/yr): Enterprise Pro plus the highest limits and model access.
- **Sonar API** — usage-based: ~$1 per million tokens at the base tier, up to $3 input / $15 output per million for Sonar Pro, plus a per-request web-retrieval fee (~$5-12 per 1,000 requests).
- The **Comet** browser is free; Comet Plus (~$5/mo, bundled into Pro and Max) adds premium publisher content.

## Best for

- RevOps building account-research dossiers with sources a rep can defend on a call
- Recruiters validating market and comp data before it goes into a hiring plan
- Legal ops scanning regulatory updates and case summaries — with the click-through-to-source discipline below

## Alternatives — and when to pick them instead

- **ChatGPT (with search)** — the market-share leader. Pick it if your team already lives in ChatGPT and wants one tool for both retrieval and long-form drafting rather than a dedicated answer engine.
- **Google Gemini / AI Mode** — the other giant, and the fastest-growing entrant in consumer answer-search. Pick it if you're standardized on Google Workspace and want AI search inside the same account and data boundary.
- **Claude** — pick it when the job is reasoning, synthesis, or drafting over sources you supply, not open-web retrieval. Different job; pair the two rather than cross-shopping on price.

## Watch-outs

- **Citations look authoritative but can misattribute.** The model sometimes cites a source that doesn't actually support the sentence. Guard: click through every load-bearing citation before it lands in a board deck, GC memo, or candidate brief.
- **Retrieval engine, not a writing engine.** For long-form reasoning and polished drafting, Claude/ChatGPT are stronger. Guard: use Perplexity to gather and cite, then hand the raw material to a writing model for the deliverable.
- **The $20 → $200 jump is steep.** Max's Model Council is real, but most ops users never hit Pro's research limits. Guard: stay on Pro until you're routinely capping out deep-research runs before paying 10x.
- **Only Enterprise tiers wall off your data.** Free and Pro don't come with the no-training guarantee, SSO, or admin controls. Guard: never put company or candidate data into Free/Pro; require Enterprise Pro before any team rollout.
