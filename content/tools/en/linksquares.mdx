## What it is

LinkSquares is a CLM that started life as an AI-powered contract repository — drop in your back catalog of executed PDFs, get extracted terms, dates, parties, and obligations searchable on day one. They've since added drafting (Finalize) and AI assist (LinkSquares AI) on top of the original Analyze product.

## Why it shows up in legal-ops stacks

- **Repository-first.** Most CLMs make you start by re-papering everything in their template engine. LinkSquares lets you ingest the existing pile and search it.
- **AI extraction that actually works.** Renewal dates, auto-renew clauses, indemnity, MFN — pulled at scale and exported to dashboards.
- **Strong reporting.** Legal ops loves the analytics layer for board-ready obligations reports.

## Pricing

- **Custom** — quote-based, tiered by contract volume and modules
- Analyze (repository + AI) and Finalize (drafting) are separately licensed
- Implementation included with most enterprise deals

## Best for

- Legal ops teams inheriting a backlog of executed contracts
- Mid-market companies that want analytics, not just storage
- Sales-led orgs needing fast turnaround on standard NDAs and MSAs

## Watch-outs

- Drafting/workflow is newer than the repository product — kick the tires
- Integrations beyond Salesforce/Slack/DocuSign get thinner fast
- Pricing tied to contract volume can scale unpredictably
