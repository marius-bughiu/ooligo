The default stack for a modern tech-company recruiting team in 2026 — built around the assumption that AI handles the high-volume, low-judgment work (sourcing, scheduling, screening synthesis) while humans focus on the high-judgment work (relationships, evaluation, closing). Optimized for the 100-1,000 employee tech company hiring at meaningful but not enterprise scale.

## How the pieces fit

- **[Ashby](/en/tools/ashby/) is the [ATS](/en/learn/ats-vs-recruiting-crm/) backbone.** Best-in-class native analytics, modern API, [structured interview](/en/learn/structured-interviewing/) scorecards as a first-class primitive. Where the funnel data lives and where every stage transition gets recorded.
- **[Gem](/en/tools/gem/) is the recruiting CRM.** Long-cycle candidate pipelining, outbound sequence management, talent community building. Pairs natively with Ashby — sourcers work in Gem, recruiters work in Ashby, the data syncs.
- **[juicebox (PeopleGPT)](/en/tools/juicebox/) is the [AI sourcing](/en/learn/ai-sourcing/) layer.** Natural-language candidate discovery against 800M+ candidates. Replaces hours of Boolean LinkedIn search with conversational queries.
- **[BrightHire](/en/tools/brighthire/) is the [interview intelligence](/en/learn/structured-interviewing/) layer.** Records and AI-analyzes every interview; produces decision-grade evidence for debriefs; flags interviewer-behavior patterns that hurt [candidate experience](/en/learn/candidate-experience/) and quality.
- **[ModernLoop](/en/tools/modernloop/) is the interview-scheduling layer.** Automates multi-person multi-stage loop coordination; eliminates the recruiting-coordinator scheduling tax that otherwise consumes 1-2 FTE at this scale.
- **[Claude](/en/tools/claude/) is the horizontal AI surface.** [JD writing](/en/workflows/jd-writer-claude-skill/), [interview loop design](/en/workflows/interview-loop-builder-skill/), [debrief synthesis](/en/workflows/interview-debrief-summary-skill/), [rejection feedback](/en/workflows/rejection-feedback-claude-skill/), [offer prep](/en/workflows/offer-prep-claude-skill/), [weekly recruiting digest](/en/workflows/weekly-recruiting-digest-skill/). The horizontal AI that connects everything else.

## Why this combination

Pre-AI recruiting stacks at this scale were Greenhouse + LinkedIn Recruiter + Calendly + Zoom + maybe Gem — five tools that did their individual jobs but didn't compound. The AI layer (juicebox + BrightHire + Claude) inverts the model: each piece extends the recruiter's reach, and the data flows between them produce insights no individual tool surfaces.

The combination accepts six tools (~$200K-$500K annual all-in for a 100-500 employee company hiring 50-150 roles/year) because each does one thing well. Trying to do this with one all-in-one platform (Greenhouse Enterprise, SmartRecruiters Talent Cloud) gives up the AI workbench depth that the specialist tools uniquely provide.

## Common variations

- **Earlier-stage variant.** Replace Ashby with [Workable](/en/tools/workable/); drop ModernLoop (use Ashby's native scheduling); use [Dover](/en/tools/dover/) instead of Gem for the smaller candidate-CRM scope. Cuts cost by 60-70% for the under-100-employee stage.
- **Enterprise variant.** Replace Ashby with [Greenhouse](/en/tools/greenhouse/) Enterprise or [SmartRecruiters](/en/tools/smartrecruiters/); add [Beamery](/en/tools/beamery/) or [Phenom](/en/tools/phenom/) for talent CRM at enterprise scale; add [HireVue](/en/tools/hirevue/) for high-volume video screening.
- **Engineering-heavy hiring.** Add [HackerRank](/en/tools/hackerrank/) or [CodeSignal](/en/tools/codesignal/) for technical assessment; add [Holly](/en/tools/holly/) for autonomous AI engineering sourcing if recruiter capacity is constrained.
- **High-volume hourly-hiring variant.** Different category entirely — see [Fountain](/en/tools/fountain/) plus [Harver](/en/tools/harver/) plus a corporate ATS for the salaried/professional layer above the hourly-hiring stack.

## What this stack does NOT replace

- A real [recruitment marketing](/en/learn/recruitment-marketing/) function — career site, content, employer brand work that drives inbound application flow
- A formal [structured interviewing](/en/learn/structured-interviewing/) discipline — the tools enable it but don't enforce it; manager and interviewer training still required
- A [diversity recruiting](/en/learn/diversity-recruiting/) program — the tools surface candidates and signals, but the strategic and operational diversity work remains human
- A [bias audit](/en/learn/ai-screening-bias/) infrastructure — required by NYC Local Law 144, [EU AI Act](/en/learn/eu-ai-act-for-legal-teams/), and other regulatory frameworks for AI-augmented hiring at scale
