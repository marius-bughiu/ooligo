## What it is

Bitscale is a spreadsheet-style GTM data workbench: the same shape as [Clay](/en/tools/clay/), sold on a different meter. You build a "grid" from a source (company or people search, a Sales Navigator search URL, a HubSpot list, a webhook, Google Maps, LinkedIn post engagers), then add columns that enrich each row, research it with AI, and push the result to your CRM or sequencer. The vendor says it connects to 100+ data sources and runs 18+ contact-data providers in a single email-and-phone [waterfall](/en/learn/waterfall-enrichment/), charging only for the provider that returns a valid result.

Bitscale is a Gurugram, India company founded in 2023 by Sanket Goyal, Abhinay Kumar, and Yash Sharma. Its only reported round is an undisclosed pre-seed led by First Cheque in July 2024 (Entrackr, Inc42). It names DevRev, Delhivery, Scrut Automation, Yellow.ai, and Phyllo as customers and claims SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001.

## Why teams look at it

- **No separate action meter.** Clay has billed two meters since March 2026: Data Credits for provider lookups and Actions for AI runs, integrations, and table operations. On Bitscale's paid plans, actions are unlimited and you pay only in credits. Failed lookups, formula columns, and CRM syncs are free. For tables heavy on formulas, routing, and syncs, that removes the meter that surprises Clay buyers.
- **Bigger credit allowance per dollar, on paper.** Growth gives 15,000 credits for $349 a month, about $0.023 per credit. Clay Growth gives 6,000 data credits plus 40,000 actions for $495, about $0.083 per data credit. A Bitscale credit and a Clay data credit do not buy the same lookup, so treat this ratio as a reason to run a test, not as a result.
- **Credit rollover.** Unused credits roll over up to 3x the monthly plan allowance on Growth and Booster.
- **Pre-built playbooks and research agents.** BitAgent does web research per row (tech stack, news, funding, decision makers), alongside GPT-4, Claude, DeepSeek, and Perplexity columns. Prospect lists, hiring signals, competitor-customer lists, and inbound enrichment all ship as playbooks.

## A concrete use case

A 10-rep outbound team wants inbound website visitors and new hires at target accounts turned into sequenced contacts. On Growth, the team pipes RB2B or Factors visitors into a grid by webhook. The grid matches each company to the ICP, waterfalls the buying-committee emails, and has a Claude column write a two-line opener from the account's latest hiring signal. It then pushes qualified rows to [Instantly](/en/tools/instantly/) and writes them back to [HubSpot](/en/tools/hubspot/) with a two-way sync. Bitscale does not send email or run LinkedIn steps itself, so the sequencer is always a second line item.

## Pricing

Checked on bitscale.ai/pricing on 2026-10-09:

- **Free**: $0, 200 lifetime credits, 2,000 company/people searches a month, 50,000 rows per grid, 20 action columns per grid. No CRM integration.
- **Growth**: $349 a month billed monthly, or about $314 a month billed annually (10% off). 15,000 credits and 15,000 searches a month, 100,000 rows per grid, 30 action columns, two-way HubSpot, webhooks, and the HTTP API.
- **Booster**: $799 a month billed monthly, or about $719 a month annually. 50,000 credits and searches, 50 action columns, Salesforce integration, and a private Slack channel.
- **Enterprise**: annual only, from $15,000 a year. Unlimited rows, SSO, custom CRM sync, and the MCP server, which is in beta for beta users and Enterprise customers.

What teams actually pay is **$3,800 to $9,600 a year** self-serve. HubSpot shops land on Growth. Salesforce shops are forced onto Booster, so the effective floor for a Salesforce org is about $8,600 a year. Add your sequencer on top. The free tier's 200 lifetime credits run out on a single test list, so it is a UI preview, not a trial. An $89 Starter plan still appears in some 2026 reviews, but it is not on the current pricing page.

## Best for

A RevOps lead or GTM engineer at a HubSpot-based, 5-to-30-rep outbound team who builds enrichment tables every week and has hit Clay's Actions ceiling with formula-, routing-, and sync-heavy workflows. It suits a lead who wants a waterfall and AI research in one grid without managing two meters.

## Alternatives, and when to pick them instead

- **[Clay](/en/tools/clay/)**: the segment leader. Pick it when you need its larger provider marketplace, niche data sources, or highly customized AI research agents, or when your team already has Clay templates and a GTM engineer fluent in them. Clay Launch at $185 a month is also the cheaper entry if your monthly volume is small.
- **[Apollo](/en/tools/apollo/)**: the other high-share default. Pick it when you want a contact database and a sequencer in one per-seat product, and don't need a build-your-own enrichment table.
- **[Unify](/en/tools/unify/)**: the fastest-growing entrant (revenue up 8x in the year before its $40M Series B in July 2025). Pick it when you want signal-to-sequence plays that run the outreach too, not a grid you assemble.
- **[FullEnrich](/en/tools/fullenrich/)** or **[Prospeo](/en/tools/prospeo/)**: pick one when all you need is verified emails and mobiles. A packaged waterfall or a single database is cheaper than a workbench.

## Watch-outs

- **Credit burn is hard to predict.** Reviewers report that credits drain faster than expected and that there is no cost estimate before a grid runs. Per-provider credit costs live in the docs' Providers and Partners Directory, not on the pricing page. *Guard:* run a 50-row test grid with each column you plan to use, read the credit delta, and multiply by your monthly row volume before choosing between Growth and Booster.
- **CRM sync is tier-gated.** HubSpot two-way sync requires Growth, and Salesforce requires Booster at $799 a month. *Guard:* price the plan your CRM requires, not the plan your credit volume needs. If you run Salesforce and use under 15,000 credits a month, compare against Clay Launch plus a CSV import before you commit.
- **A small vendor.** One undisclosed pre-seed round and roughly a dozen G2 reviews, against a much larger leader. *Guard:* keep the system of record in your CRM, export grid templates and prompts to a doc you own, and choose monthly over annual billing for the first quarter.
- **AI copy is a first draft.** One August 2026 review estimates experienced SDRs rewrite 30-50% of generated openers. *Guard:* spot-check 20 rows per new prompt and gate the push to the sequencer on a reviewed column.
