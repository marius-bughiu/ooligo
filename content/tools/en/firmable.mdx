## What it is

Firmable is a B2B company and contact database with buying signals, built in Melbourne and now also based in San Francisco. Reps search for accounts and people, unlock profiles with work emails and mobile numbers, push them to a CRM, and set "signal agents" to watch for job changes, funding rounds and hiring. It resembles [ZoomInfo](/en/tools/zoominfo/) or [Lusha](/en/tools/lusha/) in shape. What sets it apart is where the data is deepest: Firmable says its strongest coverage is Australia, New Zealand and South-East Asia, with "strong datasets for select high-value verticals" in the US and Canada rather than full North American parity.

The company announced a Series A led by Airtree on 2 March 2026, reported as $14M in the release (Firmable's own site says US$9M, likely the same round in Australian dollars). At the time it served 1,000+ customers across Australia, New Zealand and eight other APAC markets, and the money was earmarked for US expansion. Its headline coverage claims: 21M+ companies and 141M+ people across the US, Canada and APAC, of which 16.3M company profiles and 110M contacts are in the US and Canada. These are vendor figures, not audited ones.

## Why it shows up in RevOps stacks now

- **APAC coverage the US vendors lack.** Teams selling into ANZ and South-East Asia routinely find US-built databases thin on local mobiles and local company registers. Firmable includes local company register data from the Small Teams plan up.
- **An MCP server, launched 29 July 2026.** It ships through Firmable Connect (connect.firmable.ai), a hub that also holds webhooks, CRM sync and API access. The app server uses an account-specific URL with OAuth sign-in, and the docs cover [Claude](/en/tools/claude/), [ChatGPT](/en/tools/chatgpt/), Microsoft 365 Copilot via Copilot Studio, [Claude Code](/en/tools/claude-code/), [Codex](/en/tools/openai-codex/), [Cursor](/en/tools/cursor/), VS Code and Windsurf. The help centre still labels it beta. Firmable also claims 540 pre-built skills and prompts in the Connect marketplace. See [MCP servers explained](/en/learn/mcp-server-explained/) for what this connection does and does not let an agent do.
- **A separate pay-as-you-go developer server.** `https://agents.firmable.com/developer/mcp` takes OAuth or an API key, gives 100 credits at sign-up and 40 free credits a month, and charges US$0.25 per company or person enrichment. Search is free; one credit buys a profile, and re-fetching it plus its email and phone costs nothing more.

## Pricing

Checked today on Firmable's North America pricing page (US$, per user per month, billed annually):

- **Individual:** US$55 (US$660 a year), 1 user, 1,200 profiles a year. Monthly billing is US$110. AI search, core data, CSV export and the Chrome extension. No CRM integration and no MCP.
- **Small Teams:** US$75 (US$900 a year), up to 3 users, 3,000 profiles a year. Adds CRM integrations, shared lists, local company registers, up to 3 signal agents and MCP access.
- **Teams Pro:** US$110 (US$1,320 a year), 3 or more users, 6,000 profiles a year. Adds two-way CRM sync with [HubSpot](/en/tools/hubspot/), [Salesforce](/en/tools/salesforce/), [Pipedrive](/en/tools/pipedrive/) or Dynamics 365, CRM enrich and action agents, unlimited signal agents and phone support. Tech intelligence is an add-on.
- **Enterprise:** US$170, custom seats and credits. Adds API access, Okta SSO and search-intent data as an add-on.

A profile includes every email and mobile Firmable has for that record, and it is consumed when you unlock or export it (to CRM or XLSX). Once your organisation has paid for a record, re-downloads are free for everyone in it. Unused subscription credits roll over when you renew on the same or a higher plan. There is a 14-day free trial.

What it costs in practice: a 5-rep team cannot use Small Teams (capped at 3 users), so it lands on Teams Pro at US$6,600 a year. Firmable's own page quotes about US$4,500 a year for five people, which is Small Teams arithmetic. For scale, our [Cognism](/en/tools/cognism/) entry puts a 5-seat team at roughly $22K-$37K a year, and Firmable cites a Vendr median of about US$33.5K a year for [ZoomInfo](/en/tools/zoominfo/).

## Best for

SDR and AE teams of 3 to 50 reps selling into Australia, New Zealand or South-East Asia, who need mobile numbers that connect and want the data inside HubSpot or Salesforce without a ZoomInfo-sized contract. The second fit is a RevOps or GTM engineer who wants an agent in Claude or ChatGPT to look up APAC accounts and contacts through MCP, on Small Teams or above.

## Not for

Teams whose territory is mostly the US or Europe. Firmable describes its North American data as strong in select verticals, not broad. A US-only motion should test ZoomInfo or [Apollo](/en/tools/apollo/) first, and an EMEA motion should test Cognism. Also not for solo founders who need CRM sync or MCP: neither is on the Individual plan.

## Versus the alternatives

- **[ZoomInfo](/en/tools/zoominfo/)** — the share leader, with the deepest US coverage, intent data and a mature admin layer on custom annual contracts. Pick ZoomInfo when most of your pipeline is North American enterprise. Pick Firmable when APAC accounts are the majority and a five-figure contract is out of reach.
- **[Apollo](/en/tools/apollo/)** — the other share leader: database, sequencer and dialer in one per-seat app with a free tier. Pick Apollo for a US-heavy, self-serve outbound motion where sequencing matters. Pick Firmable when Apollo's APAC mobiles fail your test list.
- **[Clay](/en/tools/clay/)** — the fastest-growing platform in the segment, which runs many providers as a [waterfall](/en/learn/waterfall-enrichment/). Pick Clay when you sell across regions and want each region's best provider per row; Firmable is listed as a Clay integration, so it can be one step in that waterfall.
- **[Cognism](/en/tools/cognism/)** — pick Cognism for EMEA, phone-verified mobiles and GDPR-led compliance. Pick Firmable for ANZ and South-East Asia at roughly a fifth to a third of the 5-seat cost.
- **[Lusha](/en/tools/lusha/)** — pick Lusha for a cheap, self-serve Chrome-extension workflow in the US and Europe. Pick Firmable when the contacts are in APAC.

## Watch-outs

- **North American coverage is the open question.** The vendor claims 58.8M US mobiles, "48% more than ZoomInfo", but its own site calls the US and Canada data strong in select verticals. Guard: during the 14-day trial, pull 100 contacts from your real target list in each region you sell into, dial or email-verify a sample, and buy only if the connect rate in your main region beats your current provider.
- **Seat caps decide the plan, not features.** Small Teams stops at 3 users, the plan card does not say whether the 3,000 or 6,000 annual profiles are per seat or per account, and API access is Enterprise-only. Guard: get the profile allowance in writing as a per-account number, model a year of unlocks and CRM pushes against it, and confirm which plan you need for API before you sign.
- **Agent use spends credits the web app does not.** Viewing a work email in the web app is free, but revealing it through MCP or the API costs one credit per person, and tech-intelligence lookups cost one credit per technology, up to five per company. Guard: run agents on a scoped account or role, start with search (free) and decision tools, and check the credit dashboard after the first week before you roll MCP out to the team.
- **Compliance claims are self-reported.** Firmable says it is ISO 27001 certified and SOC 2 compliant. Guard: request the ISO certificate, the SOC 2 report and a DPA during procurement, and check how it handles Australian Privacy Act and Do Not Call obligations (a DNC check costs one credit) before reps dial.
