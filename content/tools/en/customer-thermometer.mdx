## What it is

Customer Thermometer is a one-click feedback tool. The recipient sees four icons in an email (by default gold, green, amber, red), clicks one, and that click is the response. An optional comment page follows. You can send Thermometers as a standalone email blast, embed them in helpdesk and CRM emails, or put them in every agent's Outlook, Gmail, Apple Mail or iPhone signature so each reply asks for a rating. It reports CSAT and NPS trends, CSAT drivers, and a comment cloud. The UK company behind it, Customer Thermometer Technology Ltd, was acquired by the email-signature vendor Exclaimer in February 2021 and is still sold as a standalone product with its own pricing page.

It does one thing: capture a single rating at the moment of contact and route it somewhere useful. It is not a survey builder for multi-question research, and checked 21 November 2026 its feature list carries no AI analysis of comments.

## Why it shows up in Customer Success stacks

- **It is the cheapest like-for-like Delighted replacement with published prices.** Qualtrics shut Delighted down on 30 June 2026, and GetFeedback Direct follows on 31 December 2026. A team that used either for one CSAT or NPS question can be live on Customer Thermometer the same day, with no sales call.
- **Seat-free pricing fits support teams.** Every tier includes unlimited users and agents, so a 40-agent support desk pays the same as a 4-agent one. You pay only for responses.
- **The score lands where work happens.** Two-way integrations with [Salesforce](/en/tools/salesforce/), [Zendesk](/en/tools/zendesk/) and [Freshdesk](/en/tools/freshdesk/) trigger surveys from workflow rules and write the rating back to the case, contact or ticket. Ratings and comments stream to [Slack](/en/tools/slack/) or [Microsoft Teams](/en/tools/microsoft-teams/). [HubSpot](/en/tools/hubspot/) and [Intercom](/en/tools/intercom/) support embedding a Thermometer in outgoing emails. A REST/SOAP API, webhooks and [Zapier](/en/tools/zapier/) cover the rest, and the long integration list leans toward MSP tools such as ServiceNow, ConnectWise and Autotask.

## Pricing reality

Checked 21 November 2026, plans are priced by responses per month, monthly, in USD: Jumper $49 (200 responses), Flyer $99 (500), Mach 1 $159 (1,000), Mach 2 $259 (5,000), Mach 3 $400 (10,000), Mach 4 $700 (20,000). Enterprise is quote-only and adds multiple accounts, bespoke reporting and invoicing terms. A dormant "At the Beach" plan holds your data for $9/mo while you are not sending.

Three mechanics set the real price. Unused responses roll over, but an account can hold at most double its monthly allowance. There is no overage fee: when the balance reaches zero, new clicks are not recorded and the recipient sees a "survey closed" page, unless you switch on auto-upgrade. Annual and bi-annual billing are available by invoice from Flyer up, and the site publishes no annual discount figure. A team collecting 1,000–5,000 responses a month lands on Mach 1 or Mach 2, so $1,900–3,100 a year at monthly rates. Qualtrics, by comparison, carries a Vendr median near $30K a year.

## Best for

A support or CS operations lead at a 10–300-person company, often an MSP or B2B service business, who needs a ticket-level CSAT or a simple relationship NPS number in the helpdesk and CRM this week, and does not want to pay per seat. It is the default pick for a former Delighted customer who sat on the low tiers.

## Versus the alternatives

- **[Qualtrics](/en/tools/qualtrics/)** — the market-share leader in experience management, and where Delighted's sunset notice sends people. Pick it when you are funding a multi-journey CX program with an owner and a budget line, not for one question.
- **[SurveyMonkey](/en/tools/surveymonkey/)** — the volume leader in general surveys. Pick it when the same license must also cover market research and internal surveys.
- **[Retently](/en/tools/retently/)** — the growth pick in this segment: $99/mo Ecommerce Basic and $299/mo Pro, AI topic and sentiment tagging, account-level B2B scores and an MCP server. Pick it when you want AI on the comments or an agent querying feedback.
- **[Simplesat](/en/tools/simplesat/)** — Starter $149/mo for 300 responses and 5 users. Pick it when agent-level reporting inside Zendesk or Freshdesk matters more than price per response.
- **[AskNicely](/en/tools/asknicely/)** — quote-only frontline coaching. Pick it when the job is coaching staff on each response, not reporting a score.

## Watch-outs

- **Running out of credits silently drops feedback.** At a zero balance, clicks are not recorded and customers see "survey closed", often during the busy month you most wanted data from. **Guard:** turn on auto-upgrade at setup, or alert an owner when the balance falls below one week of average volume.
- **No AI on the comments.** Open-text answers get a word cloud and type grouping, not sentiment or topic models. **Guard:** if you need themes, export comments on a schedule through the API or Zapier into your own analysis, or pay for Retently instead of building a pipeline.
- **The score series breaks at migration.** A four-icon scale is not Delighted's 0–10 or 1–5 scale, and the trend moves when wording and scale change. **Guard:** keep NPS questions on the NPS template, mark the cutover date on every chart, and do not compare old and new CSAT as one series.
- **Compliance evidence is on request, not published.** Data is stored on Azure in the UK and the vendor states GDPR and CCPA compliance, but its security page names no SOC 2 or ISO 27001 report. **Guard:** request the pentest and audit certificates before sending surveys to regulated customers, and confirm UK storage suits your data-residency terms.
