Legal intake turns a request for legal help into a record with a question, an owner and a next action. For a vendor agreement, “Please review by Friday” tells counsel when the buyer wants an answer. It leaves open which version to review, which clause needs a decision and what happens if the date slips.

## Start with a form the requester can finish

[Download the editable legal intake form and routing worksheet](/downloads/legal-intake-form.md). Save a copy of the Markdown file and fill in the blank section. The same file includes an example routing table and a completed request you can use to check your own setup.

Ask for a stable request ID, the requester and team, a contact, the counterparty, the decision needed and the business event behind the requested review date. Link to the exact draft in the approved matter system. Add contract dates, currency or prior agreements when they affect the question. Give the requester a way to say “unknown” and name the person who can supply the answer.

## Follow JH-042 from request to reviewer

In this fictional case, Juniper Harbor is buying a SaaS service from Alder Relay. Maya Chen in Procurement owns the business request. Priya Shah is commercial counsel.

| When | What the coordinator records | Who acts next |
| --- | --- | --- |
| October 5, 2026 | Maya submits draft v1 of the master services agreement (MSA). The vendor asks for 90 calendar days’ notice before renewal. Maya requests a review by October 9 but omits the proposed start and first renewal dates. | The coordinator marks JH-042 “Waiting for requester” and asks Maya for both dates. |
| October 6, 2026 | Maya confirms a proposed December 1, 2026 start and December 1, 2027 first renewal. The coordinator adds them to JH-042 and links the draft. | The coordinator assigns Priya and gives Maya the reviewer’s name and the next update. |
| During negotiation | Juniper Harbor’s example playbook targets 30 days’ notice. A 45-day fallback requires Priya’s explicit exception decision. | Priya records the proposed term, reason and decision. Until she decides, the exception stays pending. |

October 9 is the requested review date. It is separate from the proposed contract dates and from any deadline for sending a notice. The [MSA negotiation prompt pack](/en/workflows/msa-negotiation-prompt-pack/) follows the same agreement through the buyer’s proposal and the vendor’s counteroffer.

## Adapt the routing table to your team

Give the coordinator responsibility for following up on incomplete requests. Keep one ID when the requester replies. For a complete request, name the reviewer and tell the requester when to expect a status update. When a concession exceeds the reviewer’s authority, record who can decide it under your playbook. Record the final decision and any separate signature step in the matter record. Assigning a reviewer does not approve the contract.

To see whether intake is working, count how many requests needed more information and why. Separate time spent waiting for the requester from time waiting for a reviewer. Use those observations to change a confusing question or an overloaded queue. The [contract review procedure](/en/learn/contract-review-sop/) covers what happens once counsel has the draft.

## If you want to connect n8n

The [legal request intake router](/en/workflows/legal-request-intake-router-n8n/) is a separate n8n export. Its form webhook reads fields such as `submission_id`, `summary` and `needed_by`. In JH-042, `needed_by` is the requested October 9 review date. The proposed start and renewal dates belong in the case details. The download includes an example field mapping. The worksheet itself sends nothing. Configure the export’s credentials and policy, then check the missing-information and reviewer paths with fictional data before publishing a webhook.
