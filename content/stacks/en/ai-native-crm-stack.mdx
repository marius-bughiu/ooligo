The stack a modern AI-native B2B SaaS startup adopts at Series A or B instead of defaulting to Salesforce or HubSpot. Every layer is API-first, AI-aware, and assumes the team's GTM engineers will customize as much as they run off the shelf. The point is not that any single tool wins in isolation — it is that the data moving between them produces a CRM a two-person RevOps team can bend to their motion in an afternoon.

## How the pieces fit

- **[Attio](/en/tools/attio/) is the CRM substrate.** API-first, a Notion-like customizable data model, fast UI. It is the source-of-truth database every other tool reads from and writes to. Plus runs $29/user/mo and Pro $69/user/mo (annual), and Pro now bundles **Call Intelligence** — a native recorder that transcribes Zoom, Google Meet, and Teams calls straight onto the record. That overlaps Fathom's job and changes the stack's shape (more below).
- **[Clay](/en/tools/clay/) is the data + AI workbench.** Same role as in the [outbound stack](/en/stacks/ai-outbound-prospecting-stack/) — list-build, enrichment, scoring — with Attio as the CRM destination instead of HubSpot or Salesforce. Clay's **March 2026 pricing overhaul** split billing into two meters, Data Credits and Actions, and cut per-lookup costs 50-90%; self-serve teams land on Launch ($185/mo) or Growth ($495/mo).
- **[Claude](/en/tools/claude/) is the horizontal AI surface.** Lead summarization, account research, opportunity-stage validation, [QBR prep](/en/workflows/qbr-prep-skill/), [activity summarization](/en/workflows/activity-summarizer-skill/), plus custom Skills built against Attio's API. It reads the other tools' output and turns it into drafts a human edits rather than writes.
- **[n8n](/en/tools/n8n/) is the integration layer.** A webhook from a form fill → Clay enrichment → Attio insert → Slack notification. Every cross-tool flow runs through n8n rather than a hard-wired vendor connector. Cloud Starter is $20/mo, Pro $50/mo; self-hosting drops the software cost to near zero and trades it for ops time.
- **[Slack](/en/tools/slack/) is the operational surface.** Lead routing, deal alerts, churn-risk warnings, competitive intel. Most reps work from Slack notifications more than from the CRM UI.
- **[Fathom](/en/tools/fathom/) records and summarizes external meetings.** Auto-attached to Attio meeting records; transcripts available to Claude for downstream synthesis. Its Business tier ($25-34/user/mo) is the one that carries CRM sync.

## The handoffs that make it a stack

The value is the chain, not the six logos. A form fill hits an n8n webhook, which fires a Clay enrichment run, which writes the finished row into Attio and posts the routed lead to Slack — one event, four tools, no human in the loop. When a rep takes the call, Fathom (or Attio Call Intelligence) captures it and lands the transcript on the Attio record. Claude then reads that transcript plus the account's activity and drafts the follow-up, the QBR deck, or the stage-validation note back onto the record. Each arrow is an event that triggers the next action, which is why the stack compounds instead of just co-existing.

## Why this combination

Salesforce and HubSpot are excellent CRMs for organizations that already run one. For an AI-native team starting fresh in 2026, both feel heavy — too many features, too many surfaces, too much config to do simple things. Attio inverts the model: ship a clean data model, expose everything via API, and let the team build the workflow that fits their motion rather than fitting the team to the platform.

The trade-off is that every integration is a custom build, not a vendor-supported connector. n8n is the glue that makes that tractable for a team without a large eng org; engineering-heavy teams get the same shape with custom code against Attio's API. Either way, the stack assumes the CRM is an evolving codebase, not a once-configured purchase.

## What it costs

Budget roughly **$18K-$45K a year all-in** for a 10-25 seat GTM team. Attio seats (Plus $29 / Pro $69 per user per month) and Clay ($2.2K-6K self-serve, more at Enterprise where contracts run a ~$30K/yr median) are the two biggest levers. Claude Team ($25/seat/mo, 5-seat minimum), Fathom ($15-34/user/mo), Slack ($8.75-15/user/mo), and n8n ($20-50/mo cloud, or self-host) each add a few thousand a year. The range is wide because Attio and Fathom scale with seats while Clay scales with data-and-action volume — model those two Clay meters against real list sizes before you commit, or that line surprises you.

## The Fathom vs Attio Call Intelligence call

Attio's 2026 Call Intelligence overlaps Fathom directly. If everyone who takes calls is already an Attio seat and Pro's per-seat cost is acceptable, native recording removes a tool and keeps capture inside the CRM. Keep Fathom when non-CRM users — founders, CS, partners — need recording, when you want its free individual tier for light users, or when its summary quality and playlist/search UX matter more to you than consolidation. Do not pay for both across the same seats without a reason you can name.

## Common variations

- **Heavy outbound motion.** Add [Apollo](/en/tools/apollo/) and [Outreach](/en/tools/outreach/) — see the [outbound stack](/en/stacks/ai-outbound-prospecting-stack/) for the full picture. Trigger: outbound volume outgrows what Clay-into-Attio handles by hand.
- **Sales-led with high deal value.** Add [Gong](/en/tools/gong/) for conversation intelligence — Fathom and Attio Call Intelligence serve transactional motions; Gong earns its price only at enterprise deal complexity and coaching scale.
- **Engineering team that prefers code over n8n.** Replace n8n with custom TypeScript/Python against Attio's API. Same shape, more control, more maintenance.
- **Outgrowing Attio at enterprise scale.** When ops complexity exceeds Attio's data model — multi-business-unit, multi-currency, complex CPQ — migration to [Salesforce](/en/tools/salesforce/) is the typical path. Plan it as a Series-D event, not a forever choice.

## When this stack is the right pick — and when it isn't

Pick it when you are a founder or RevOps leader at a Series A-B AI-native company, starting CRM fresh, with at least one person who enjoys building workflows rather than filing tickets to an admin. That is its center of gravity.

Skip it if any of these holds. If you already run Salesforce or HubSpot with working process on top, rip-and-replace rarely pays — layer Clay and Claude onto what you have instead. If nobody on the team will own the n8n and API glue, the custom-build assumption breaks and an all-in-one like HubSpot serves you better. And if you need CPQ, multi-currency, or heavy territory management on day one, Attio is the wrong substrate — start where you will end up.

## What this stack does NOT replace

- A CPQ for complex pricing — Attio does not pretend to be one.
- An ABM platform — see the [ABM stack](/en/stacks/abm-revenue-stack/) for that motion.
- A customer success platform at scale — see the [customer success expansion stack](/en/stacks/customer-success-expansion-stack/).
- A marketing automation platform (Marketo, HubSpot Marketing) for high-volume nurture — Attio is sales-side, not full GTM.
