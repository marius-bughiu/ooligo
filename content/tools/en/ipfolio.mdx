## What it is

IPfolio is Clarivate's cloud IP management system (IPMS) for corporate IP departments: one record for inventions, patents, trademarks, domain names, and copyrights, with a country-laws engine that calculates statutory dates and deadlines, task assignment, inventor award tracking, and reports and dashboards. It runs on the Salesforce platform. Customers log in to a Clarivate-hosted Salesforce org, and Clarivate pushes updates automatically instead of selling upgrade projects.

It is a different product from [Clarivate IPOne](/en/tools/clarivate-ipone/). IPOne puts AI agents and MCP servers over Derwent, Darts-ip, and CompuMark data. IPfolio is the docket and portfolio record. The two connect, because IPfolio verifies and enriches records against Clarivate's patent and trademark data, but you buy them separately. Clarivate also sells two older IPMS lines, FoundationIP for corporations and Inprotech for law firms, so ask which one a Clarivate rep is actually quoting.

If [Anaqua](/en/tools/anaqua/) is the heavyweight corporate IPMS, IPfolio is the configurable middle option: smaller in scope, faster to stand up, and built for teams that do not have a dedicated system administrator.

## Why it shows up in Legal Ops stacks

- **The renewals bundle.** Clarivate owns CPA Global's annuity and trademark-renewal business, and its 2026 wins pair the two. IGT bought IPfolio together with patent annuity and trademark renewal services (21 May 2026). Mitsubishi Fuso's release names integrated annuity and renewal management (15 June 2026). Topcon pays annuities through Clarivate's Japanese agent (30 September 2026). If renewal spend is the line item under review, one vendor for the docket and the payment removes a reconciliation step.
- **Configuration without upgrade projects.** Mitsubishi Fuso cites changes rolled out in phases after go-live. Clarivate's product page claims more than 200 IPfolio clients onboarded with zero failed implementations. That is a vendor figure with no audit behind it, but no comparable claim exists for the heavier platforms.
- **A Japan delivery bench.** Daikin (November 2024), Fujifilm (November 2025), Mitsubishi Fuso, and Topcon are all Japanese manufacturers, implementation is supported by Clarivate teams in Japan, and IPfolio has been listed on Salesforce AppExchange Japan since 2023. Topcon moved off an on-premises system. For a Japan-headquartered IP department, that track record outweighs any single feature.

## Pricing reality

Clarivate publishes no price, no edition list, and no self-serve tier: every deal is a quote based on user count and modules. G2 also lists no price.

The band below is an estimate, not a sourced quote. Entry-tier cloud IPMS subscriptions in this segment start near $25K/year, and IPfolio competes in that range for small and mid-size corporate teams, well under the $150K+ TCO reported for mid-size Anaqua deployments. Three line items come on top of the IPMS subscription:

- annuity and renewal fees, priced per payment
- Forecast, Clarivate's AI budgeting tool, sold as a separate product
- Derwent or CompuMark data, if you want more than the record-enrichment feed

Get each one quoted as its own line.

## Best for

In-house IP managers and IP operations leads at manufacturers and tech companies with roughly 100 to 2,000 active patent families plus a trademark portfolio, who want one cloud docket live this year, will configure it themselves, and are open to outsourcing renewals to the same vendor.

**Not for you if** you are a law firm (look at Inprotech, or IPfolio Law once Clarivate ships it), trademarks are the whole job, or you need automated docketing from office correspondence today. IPfolio's own page promises automated data verification and enrichment but does not name correspondence auto-processing, and Clarivate sells that as a separate Docket product.

## Alternatives — and when to pick them instead

- **[Anaqua](/en/tools/anaqua/)**: the other half of the incumbent pair, and the depth leader. Pick it when you have 500+ families across many jurisdictions, need native iManage or NetDocuments sync and correspondence auto-docketing, and will staff a system owner for a 6–12 month implementation.
- **[Questel](/en/tools/questel/)** (Equinox): run by more than 1,000 law firms and corporations as of March 2025. Pick it when European prosecution is the center of gravity, or when you want Orbit search and the Qthena drafting assistant from the same vendor as the docket.
- **[Tradespace](/en/tools/tradespace/)**: the fastest-growing entrant, managing over 440,000 patents for 80+ organizations after its January 2026 Series A. Pick it when you want AI drafting, invention harvesting, and flat-fee outside counsel in the same system as the docket, and can accept a young vendor's support model.
- **[Alt Legal](/en/tools/alt-legal/)**: pick it when trademarks are the only docket.

**The rule:** pick IPfolio over Anaqua when speed to go-live and self-administration matter more than integration depth. Pick it over Tradespace when renewals outsourcing and an incumbent's balance sheet matter more than AI.

## Watch-outs

- **The AI story is mostly elsewhere in Clarivate.** Topcon's September 2026 release points to AI enhancements still to come, while Forecast, Docket, and IPOne are sold separately. **Guard:** list the AI and auto-docketing capabilities you are paying for as named line items in the order form, each with its current release. Do not accept roadmap language as part of the deal.
- **Bundled renewals weaken your negotiating position.** Once the docket and the annuity payments sit with one vendor, switching either one means switching both. **Guard:** quote the IPMS and the renewals separately, keep the right to use a third-party annuity provider, and get a full data export (records, deadlines, documents) in a documented format written into the contract.
- **The parent is restructuring.** Clarivate sold its Life Sciences & Healthcare segment to Altaris for $600M in October 2026 to pay down debt, and the IP segment's organic revenue fell 1.9% in 2025. Clarivate calls IP a core segment. **Guard:** cap renewal price increases at a fixed percentage for the initial term, and get a written support commitment for IPfolio itself rather than for "Clarivate IPMS."
- **The Salesforce platform has limits.** It brings security and automatic updates, but your data lives in Clarivate's org, not yours, so it will not join your company's own Salesforce reporting without an integration project. **Guard:** before signing, ask how API access and bulk export work for your records, and whether your ERP and HR integrations are included or billed as professional services.
