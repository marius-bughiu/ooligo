## What it is

Icertis is the enterprise CLM of choice for global Fortune 500 deployments. Built natively on Microsoft Azure, it specializes in massive contract repositories (millions of agreements), deep ERP integration (SAP, Oracle, Workday), and the kind of governance and security profile required by global financial services, pharma, and industrial customers. Icertis ICI Copilot adds an AI layer for clause analysis, risk scoring, and contract authoring on top of the platform.

## Why it shows up in Legal Ops stacks

- **Built for global enterprise complexity.** Multi-entity, multi-currency, multi-language contract repositories with row-level access controls. The fit when a single CLM has to serve legal, procurement, sales, and HR across 30+ countries.
- **ERP-grade integrations.** Bidirectional flows with SAP and Oracle for procurement contracts; Salesforce and Microsoft Dynamics for sales contracts. Other CLMs claim integrations; Icertis ships them at the depth large enterprises need.
- **Microsoft partnership depth.** Native Azure deployment, Microsoft 365 deep integration, and a co-sell relationship that lands Icertis in many "Microsoft-first" enterprise deals.

## Pricing

- **Custom only.** Enterprise pricing typically starts in the low six figures and scales into seven for the largest deployments.
- **Module-based packaging.** Buy ICI core, then add modules (ICI Copilot AI, ICI Insights analytics, supplier or sales accelerators) as needed.
- **Implementation cost is significant.** Plan for 6-12 months with Icertis professional services or a partner (Accenture, Deloitte, Cognizant); not a quick rollout.

## Best for

- Global Fortune 500 enterprises with multi-business-unit contract complexity
- Procurement-heavy organizations where the CLM has to serve buy-side as much as sell-side
- Microsoft-first enterprises where Azure-native deployment matters

## Watch-outs

- Heavyweight to deploy; not the right call below ~$1B revenue or ~30 legal headcount
- AI features (ICI Copilot) are competitive but not category-leading; pair with Harvey or Spellbook for the highest-bar drafting and review work
- Per-user UX feels enterprise-software dated next to AI-native CLMs (SirionLabs, Ironclad's newer surfaces)
- Direct competitors (SirionLabs, Conga, Ironclad enterprise tier) close on capability — the win is increasingly about ecosystem and trust, not pure feature parity
