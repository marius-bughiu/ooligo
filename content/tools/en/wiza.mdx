## What it is

Wiza is a contact finder built around LinkedIn. You open a LinkedIn, Sales Navigator, or Recruiter profile, click the Wiza icon, and a side panel returns the person's work email, personal email, direct dial, or mobile, verified at the moment you ask rather than pulled from a cache. Behind the extension sit Wiza Prospect, a searchable database the vendor sizes at 850M+ contacts, plus CSV enrichment, CRM sync, and an API. The company is an independent Toronto startup (founded around 2019). It claims 500,000+ users and a 99%+ deliverability rate on the emails it marks valid, and you pay only for valid emails.

What it is not, any more: a Sales Navigator list scraper. On August 19, 2026, Wiza removed bulk export and multi-select from LinkedIn, Sales Navigator, and Recruiter search, list, and company pages, for every plan. Wiza's help center says LinkedIn tightened what third-party extensions may do, support cannot switch the feature back on, and older extension versions are not a supported workaround. You can still reveal one full profile at a time; Wiza no longer reads Sales Navigator preview panes. That change ended the use case most teams bought Wiza for, and parts of the homepage and Chrome Web Store listing still describe the old bulk export.

## Why it still shows up in RevOps stacks

- **Accurate one-at-a-time reveals.** On G2's head-to-head scores, Wiza rates 8.3 out of 10 for contact data accuracy against 7.7 for ZoomInfo and 7.6 for Lusha. For an AE or recruiter working a short account list profile by profile, the extension still does that job well.
- **Valid-only billing.** Credits come out for valid emails only. Emails Wiza flags as risky are free, so a bad list does not burn the balance the way a pay-per-lookup tool does.
- **CRM sync on every paid plan.** Native syncs cover [HubSpot](/en/tools/hubspot/), [Salesforce](/en/tools/salesforce/), [Pipedrive](/en/tools/pipedrive/), Zoho CRM, [Outreach](/en/tools/outreach/), and [Salesloft](/en/tools/salesloft/), with [Zapier](/en/tools/zapier/) for anything else.
- **A provider inside Clay.** [Clay](/en/tools/clay/) lists Wiza as a waterfall source, paid with Clay credits or your own Wiza account, so you can use it as one step in a cascade without seats.

## Pricing

Checked 2026-11-15 on wiza.co/pricing. All prices are per user.

- **Free** — $0, 20 valid emails and 5 phone numbers.
- **Starter (monthly only)** — $49 a month, 100 valid emails and 100 phone numbers, then $0.15 per extra email and $0.35 per extra phone.
- **Email** — $99 a month with 500 valid emails, or $990 a year ($83 a month) with "unlimited" email reveals. Phones cost $0.35 each on both.
- **Email + Phone** — $199 a month with 500 emails and 500 phones, or $1,990 a year ($166 a month) with unlimited emails and phones.
- **Team** — custom annual pricing for 3+ users, unlimited emails and phones, and the only plan that lists Wiza Prospect, CSV import, advanced fields, and API access.
- **What "unlimited" means.** Annual plans cap list exports at 2,500 a month, and a third-party review that tested the limits found reveals throttled at 500 per 24 hours and 50 per five minutes.
- **What it costs in practice.** Monthly Email works out to about $0.20 per email inside the allowance. Email + Phone annual beats Email annual plus $0.35 phones only above about 2,860 phones a year, roughly 11 a working day. Ten seats on the individual annual plan run $19,900 a year before a Team quote. Per phone, Wiza's $0.35 sits between [Prospeo](/en/tools/prospeo/) at about $0.15 and [Lusha](/en/tools/lusha/) at about $0.76.

## Best for

- AEs, recruiters, and founders working fewer than about 30 named prospects a day from LinkedIn profiles, who want a verified email or phone on the profile in front of them and a push to the CRM
- RevOps teams that already run [Clay](/en/tools/clay/) and want Wiza as one accuracy-weighted step in a waterfall
- Not for SDR teams that built lists by bulk-exporting [Sales Navigator](/en/tools/sales-navigator/) searches; that workflow is gone on every plan. Not for buyers who want database search and CSV enrichment without a Team contract: on the public pricing page, both sit only on Team.

## Alternatives — and when to pick them instead

- **[ZoomInfo](/en/tools/zoominfo/)** — the share leader, with the deepest database, intent, and org charts on custom contracts. Pick ZoomInfo when account intelligence is the job. Pick Wiza when you only need a verified contact for the profile you are looking at.
- **[Apollo](/en/tools/apollo/)** — the other share leader: database, sequences, and a dialer from $49 per seat per month annual. Pick Apollo when you lost Sales Navigator export and need list building plus sending in one seat. Pick Wiza only for its per-profile accuracy on top.
- **[Clay](/en/tools/clay/)** — the fastest-growing platform here (a $115M Series D at a $7.1B valuation in September 2026). Pick Clay when you want Wiza, Prospeo, and others in one ordered waterfall under your control.
- **[FullEnrich](/en/tools/fullenrich/)** — a packaged waterfall over 20+ providers, Wiza among them, at $55 for 1,000 credits and charging only for hits. Pick FullEnrich when you have a list of LinkedIn URLs and want contacts back in bulk without per-seat pricing.
- **[Prospeo](/en/tools/prospeo/)** — a weekly-refreshed database with a cheaper mobile and public self-serve API from its $49 Starter plan. Pick Prospeo when cost per contact and bulk enrichment matter more than profile-by-profile work.

## Watch-outs

- **Old guides describe a product that no longer exists.** Reviews, comparison pages, the homepage, and the Chrome Web Store listing still mention bulk Sales Navigator export. Guard: read Wiza's August 19, 2026 help-center update before you buy, and test the free plan on your real workflow, not a demo.
- **"Unlimited" is throttled.** Annual plans cap exports at 2,500 a month, and reveals are rate-limited per day and per five minutes. Guard: estimate your daily reveal count; above 500 a day per rep, price a bulk-enrichment tool instead.
- **The features that replace bulk export sit on Team.** Prospect search, CSV import, and the API appear only on the custom-priced Team plan. Guard: if you need lists rather than single reveals, get the Team quote in writing before signing an individual annual plan.
- **Phones get expensive off the top plan.** At $0.35 each, 300 phones a month adds $1,260 a year per seat. Guard: reveal phones only for accounts someone will dial this quarter, and move to Email + Phone only above about 11 phones a working day.
