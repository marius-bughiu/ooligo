## What it is

Workable is the SMB-and-mid-market applicant tracking system (ATS) used by 30,000+ companies worldwide. Founded in 2012, it's the ATS most commonly chosen by 50-1,000 employee companies that have outgrown spreadsheet-tracked hiring but aren't yet at the enterprise scale that demands [iCIMS](/en/tools/icims/) or workday. Workable AI (released 2023) adds AI-assisted sourcing, candidate matching, and interview scheduling on top of the core platform.

## Why it shows up in Recruiting stacks

- **The right scope for SMB-to-mid-market.** Job posting, applicant tracking, interview scheduling, offer management — without the enterprise complexity that breaks first-time ATS deployments.
- **One-click multi-board posting.** Distribute jobs to LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, ZipRecruiter, and 200+ free job boards from one workflow. Removes the manual posting overhead that consumes early-stage recruiter time.
- **Workable AI sourcing.** Searches the integrated database of 400M+ candidates, scores fit against the job description, surfaces candidates the team would otherwise miss.

## Pricing

- **Standard** — $169/month for up to 50 employees
- **Premier** — $360/month, includes advanced reporting and Workable AI features
- Per-job-slot model also available for high-volume hiring teams
- Annual plans typically discounted 15-20% vs monthly

## Best for

- 50-500 employee SMB and growth-stage companies hiring 5-50 roles per quarter
- Companies replacing first-time ATS choices (Lever, Greenhouse felt too expensive or complex)
- HR-led recruiting teams that want one tool for the whole hiring workflow

## Watch-outs

- Less suited for enterprise scale — multi-business-unit hiring, complex approval workflows, and global compliance push teams toward [iCIMS](/en/tools/icims/) or [Greenhouse](/en/tools/greenhouse/) Enterprise above ~1,500 employees
- AI features are improving but lag specialist sourcing tools ([Gem](/en/tools/gem/), [hireEZ](/en/tools/hireez/), [juicebox](/en/tools/juicebox/)) on candidate-discovery quality
- Reporting depth is mid-tier; teams that want sophisticated funnel analytics often layer a BI tool on top
