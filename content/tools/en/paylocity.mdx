## What it is

Paylocity (NASDAQ: PCTY) sells a single-database HCM suite for US mid-market employers: payroll, benefits, time and scheduling, HR, and talent management, which includes recruiting. Its fiscal 2026 annual report (year ended 30 June 2026) lists about 44,400 clients, more than 6.7 million employees on the platform, and $1.771B in revenue, up 11%. OutSail's July 2026 buyer guide puts its sweet spot at 50–750 employees.

**Paylocity Recruiting** is the applicant tracking module inside that suite: requisitions, career site and job posting, an applicant tracker, offers, and a handoff into onboarding and payroll on the same employee record. Over 2026 Paylocity added an AI layer on top, in three steps:

- **7 April 2026: it bought Grayscale Labs**, a text-recruiting platform built for employers that hire at volume. Terms were not disclosed.
- **21 July 2026: it launched Ignite AI**, its platform-wide agent layer. The recruiting agents in that release were a **Candidate Fit Agent** and a **Resume Summary Agent**. An **Ignite AI Hub** lets admins switch individual agents on or off, control who has access, and watch usage.
- **22 October 2026: it launched Recruiting Optimizer**, an add-on with two more agents. The **Candidate Engagement Agent** runs conversational AI over web and SMS: it helps applicants find roles, asks screening questions, and schedules interviews against criteria the recruiter sets. The **Talent Rediscovery Agent** surfaces past applicants who match open requisitions and summarises their earlier application and interview history for a recruiter to review.

Paylocity claims Recruiting Optimizer raises recruiter productivity 35%, fills roles 25% faster, and cuts candidate drop-off 15%. Those are vendor figures with no published method. Paylocity also says it ranked #1 in G2's AI Recruiting category in the Fall 2026 reports. That is a vendor announcement we could not check against G2's own grid.

This page answers one question: **we run payroll on Paylocity, so do its new recruiting agents replace a standalone ATS, or a texting and screening tool like Paradox or Humanly?**

## Why it shows up in mid-market hiring stacks

- **One record from applicant to first paycheck.** A hire in Paylocity Recruiting becomes the employee that Paylocity onboards and pays, with nothing to sync. For a 300-person company with one HR generalist doing the recruiting, removing that integration matters more than any AI feature.
- **The conversational layer is now native.** Before 2026, a Paylocity customer that wanted SMS screening and self-scheduling bought a separate tool. Grayscale plus the Candidate Engagement Agent puts that inside the suite, under the same login and the same contract.
- **Your applicant history becomes a sourcing pool.** Talent Rediscovery works on candidates already in your Paylocity database. A company hiring the same roles every quarter, such as clinic staff or warehouse associates, gets value from it without buying a sourcing tool.

## Pricing reality

Paylocity publishes no prices. Vendr reports a median contract of **$33,541 a year** across 37 deals, with a range of $16,000 to $445,082 (page checked 12 November 2026, data last updated February 2026). Vendr says talent modules, recruiting included, are billed either as a bundled per-employee-per-month (PEPM) fee or as separate PEPM fees per module. OutSail puts the full suite at **$26–33 PEPM** and core payroll and HR alone at $18–22 PEPM. It puts implementation at 10–20% of the annual software fee.

Recruiting Optimizer is priced separately. UsagePricing's reading of Paylocity's contract terms (checked 2 November 2026) says it is metered per **action**. Each automated screen, interview booking, bulk-engagement message, or rediscovery counts as one action against an allocation set in your order form. Above the allocation, Paylocity can charge an overage at a rate set in a separate ordering document, or restrict the feature. It also requires an active Recruiting subscription.

Worked example: a 400-employee company at $28 PEPM pays about $134,000 a year for the suite, plus $13,000–27,000 to implement it. A high-volume hirer that turns on the Candidate Engagement Agent can use thousands of actions a month, so the action allocation, not the PEPM, decides what the AI costs.

## Best for

HR and TA leads at 100–1,000-employee US companies that already run payroll on Paylocity, hire 20–200 people a month into repeat roles, and have no recruiting-ops team to run a second system. Concretely: a 450-person home-health agency hiring caregivers across six branches, where one recruiter currently texts applicants from a personal phone.

**Not for you if** you hire mostly salaried and technical roles with structured interview loops, scorecards, and a recruiting team of five or more. Paylocity Recruiting is a payroll-suite ATS. It is not [Greenhouse](/en/tools/greenhouse/) or [Ashby](/en/tools/ashby/).

## Versus the alternatives

**[Paradox](/en/tools/paradox/)** is the best-known conversational hourly-hiring platform, and Workday has owned it since October 2025. Pick it over the Candidate Engagement Agent when you hire thousands of people a month across many sites and need its depth in scheduling and multi-language chat. Confirm the depth of its Paylocity connector in writing, because we found no published integration. **[Humanly](/en/tools/humanly/)** is the fastest-growing independent alternative. Pick it when you want screening that writes back to your ATS from a vendor no HCM suite owns. Again, get its Paylocity write-back confirmed before you sign.

**[ADP Workforce Now](/en/tools/adp/)** is the largest payroll-native rival in the same mid-market band. **[UKG](/en/tools/ukg/)** sells conversational hourly hiring as UKG Rapid Hire, and **[Dayforce](/en/tools/dayforce/)** relies on partners for it. **[Rippling](/en/tools/rippling/)** and **[BambooHR](/en/tools/bamboohr/)** are the picks under about 200 employees. None of them is a reason to move payroll for recruiting alone. Pick the recruiting option your payroll vendor ships, and add a specialist only where it fails.

## Watch-outs

- **Action metering can turn a flat contract into a variable one.** Each automated text, screen and booking draws down an allocation, and the overage rate sits in a separate document. Guard: before you sign, get the action allocation, the overage rate and the definition of one action in the order form. Then model one peak hiring month against it.
- **The agents are weeks old.** Recruiting Optimizer launched on 22 October 2026, and its performance figures are Paylocity's own. Guard: pilot it on one requisition family for 60 days, and measure apply-to-interview time and drop-off against the previous quarter before you switch off any texting tool you pay for.
- **The screening and fit agents need a bias check.** The Candidate Fit Agent and the screening questions shape who reaches a recruiter. Paylocity says it audited its recruiting agents for bias, but the audit is not published. Guard: run your own adverse-impact check by requisition family, keep a human decision on every rejection, and remember that NYC Local Law 144 requires a bias audit for tools that rank candidates.
- **Third-party ATS integrations are partner-gated.** Integrator documentation says Paylocity issues recruiting API credentials only through its partner program, with a customer sponsor, and some connectors, such as [Pinpoint](/en/tools/pinpoint/)'s, are one-way. Guard: if you keep a separate ATS, confirm in writing which fields sync in which direction before you plan around it.
