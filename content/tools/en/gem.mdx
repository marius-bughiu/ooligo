## What it is

Gem is a Talent CRM and outbound platform for recruiters. It sits on top of LinkedIn and your ATS, captures every candidate touch, and runs sourcing sequences with AI-assisted personalization. Think Outreach, but rebuilt for TA.

## Why it shows up in Recruiting/TA stacks

- **LinkedIn extension that actually works.** One-click sourcing into a real CRM with auto-enriched contact info, sequence enrollment, and dedupe against your ATS.
- **Pipeline analytics by source.** Real funnel data — pass-through rates, time-to-hire, source ROI — without exporting CSVs from [Greenhouse](/en/tools/greenhouse/) and gluing them in a sheet.
- **Outreach that recruiters actually send.** Multi-step email sequences with AI rewrite, A/B testing, and reply detection. Response rates beat hand-rolled InMails for most teams.

## Pricing

- **Sourcing & CRM** — custom, typically per-seat annual
- **Talent Engagement** — adds full sequences and analytics, custom pricing
- **Enterprise** — adds DEI dashboards, advanced reporting

Public pricing is not listed. Expect $8–15K/year per recruiter seat depending on modules and volume. Annual contracts are the norm.

## Best for

- Series B–public companies with a dedicated sourcing function
- Teams running outbound at scale (50+ candidates per req per recruiter)
- Orgs already on Greenhouse, Lever, or [Ashby](/en/tools/ashby/) that want CRM on top

## Watch-outs

- Pricing climbs fast once you turn on Engagement and DEI modules
- LinkedIn TOS friction — Gem stays compliant, but heavy automation still gets accounts flagged
- Overlap with [hireEZ](/en/tools/hireez/) and SeekOut for sourcing; Gem wins on CRM and outreach, loses on raw search depth
