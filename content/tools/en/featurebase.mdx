## What it is

Featurebase started as a feedback board — the Canny shape of voting, a public roadmap, and a changelog — and spent 2026 turning into a full helpdesk. The same workspace now runs live chat, email, and Slack conversations in one inbox, a help center, proactive messaging, and Fibi, an AI agent that answers customers and bills per resolution. The pitch is consolidation: one per-seat tool in place of a feedback board plus an Intercom-style support stack. The meter to understand first is the opposite of Canny's. Featurebase charges for **your team's seats and for Fibi's resolutions**, not for the end users who post and vote.

## Why it shows up in Customer Success stacks

- **Feedback and support share one record.** Feedback 2.0 (24 September 2026) renamed posts to Requests and backs each one with Insights pulled from support conversations and call recorders — Fathom, tl;dv, Grain, Gong, Fireflies, and Granola. Agents link a chat to a Request from the inbox, and status changes flow back to the conversation where the customer first asked. That is the loop-closer a separate board and helpdesk can only approximate through an integration.
- **The support surface is now real.** Outbound (7 July) folded the changelog, banners, email, surveys, and targeted chat into one module. Slack became a native support channel on 10 August, with SLAs and conversion to Tickets. Salesforce two-way sync and HubSpot data sync shipped on 20 August, with requests sortable by Opportunity Value.
- **AI is priced on outcome, not seats.** Fibi bills only when it resolves a conversation; hand-offs to a human are free. Featurebase's own fleet figure after Fibi 3.0 (18 June) is a 65% resolution rate, up from 50% — a vendor number, not an independent benchmark.
- **Agent access is on the entry paid plan.** API, webhooks, and MCP sit on Growth at $29/seat, and the July Reports release lets you query support data through the MCP server.

## Pricing reality

Four plans, priced per seat; end users, posts, and comments are not metered.

- **Free** — $0, one seat, one feedback board, one roadmap, live chat and inbox. No Fibi.
- **Growth** — $29/seat/month billed yearly, $37 month-to-month. Fibi, Copilot, API/MCP, reporting.
- **Professional** — $59/seat/month yearly, $75 monthly. Adds workflows, SLAs, round-robin assignment, Salesforce and HubSpot, and 20 free Lite (read-only) seats.
- **Enterprise** — $99/seat/month yearly, $129 monthly. SSO, multi-brand, 50 Lite seats.

The seat price is not the bill. Five meters stack on top: Fibi at **$0.49 per resolution** (up from $0.29 on 1 July 2026, for new and existing customers alike); Copilot at 10 free conversations per seat per month, then $19/seat/month; outbound email at 5,000 free, then an unpublished rate; and $69/month to remove Featurebase branding. A worked example: three Growth seats plus 400 Fibi resolutions a month is about $283/month ($87 + $196). The same load on Intercom — Essential seats from $29 plus Fin at $0.99 per outcome — lands near $483. Early-stage companies (under two years old, fewer than six employees) qualify for 86% off.

## Best for

Founders and first CS hires at B2B SaaS companies with 2-15 support seats who want feedback voting, a public roadmap, a help center, and an AI first responder in one tool for under $300/month. It fits best when your public board is large or consumer-heavy, because Featurebase's cost does not move when 20,000 users start voting.

Do not buy Featurebase if you run 25+ support agents, need voice or workforce scheduling, or need a vendor whose AI agent has been benchmarked outside its own marketing. That is [Zendesk](/en/tools/zendesk/) territory, or [Intercom](/en/tools/intercom/) when Fin's deflection quality and ecosystem justify paying double per resolution.

## Versus the alternatives

- **[Canny](/en/tools/canny/)** — pick it when you only need the feedback board and want the more mature one; Canny's Autopilot intake is unmetered, but its bill climbs with tracked users, so a large public board flips the math to Featurebase.
- **[Productboard](/en/tools/productboard/)** — pick it when the buyer is a product org that needs scored prioritization and feature hierarchies, not a support inbox.
- **[Intercom](/en/tools/intercom/)** and **[Zendesk](/en/tools/zendesk/)** — the two market-share leaders in support; pick either once support volume, channel breadth, or compliance review outgrows a small vendor.
- **[Pylon](/en/tools/pylon/)** — the fastest-growing B2B support entrant; pick it when enterprise accounts live in shared Slack Connect channels and you need account-level health views. [Plain](/en/tools/plain/) and [Help Scout](/en/tools/help-scout/) are the lighter API-first and shared-inbox options.

## Watch-outs

- **The AI rate moved 69% on 13 days' notice.** The $0.29-to-$0.49 change was announced 18 June and applied to existing customers on 1 July. **Guard:** on an annual contract, get the per-resolution rate written into the order form with a cap, and model your budget at $0.49 plus headroom rather than today's number.
- **"Resolution" has no published definition.** Featurebase has not documented a quiet period, reopen rule, or how partial escalations bill, where Zendesk and HubSpot publish 72-hour rules. **Guard:** get the definition in writing before you switch Fibi on, then reconcile one month's invoice against conversation logs.
- **Revenue-sorted feedback needs Professional.** The Salesforce and HubSpot sync that lets you sort requests by deal value sits on the $59 tier, not Growth. **Guard:** if ARR-weighted triage is why you are buying, price Professional from day one.
- **Fibi is a black box to admins.** Featurebase's own feedback board carries user requests for visibility into which tools Fibi calls and why. **Guard:** review a sample of Fibi transcripts weekly for the first month and keep sensitive topics routed to humans by workflow.
