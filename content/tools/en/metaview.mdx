## What it is

Metaview is the Gong-for-recruiting. It joins your interviews on Zoom, Meet, or Teams, transcribes them, and writes a structured scorecard draft straight into Greenhouse, Lever, or Ashby. Interviewers stop typing notes and start actually interviewing.

## Why it shows up in Recruiting/TA stacks

- **Scorecards that write themselves.** Drafts competency-aligned scorecards from the transcript. Hiring managers edit instead of starting from a blank page — debrief quality jumps.
- **ATS-native output.** Pushes notes, summaries, and scorecard fields directly into Greenhouse, Lever, or Ashby. No Notion middleman, no copy-paste.
- **Calibration insight.** Surfaces interview quality patterns — who asks leading questions, who talks 70% of the call, where rubrics drift across panels.

## Pricing

- **Free** — limited interviews per month, basic transcription
- **Starter** — $30/user/month, full Metaview AI features
- **Business** — custom, adds analytics, calibration, SSO
- **Enterprise** — custom, adds advanced security and dedicated support

Real value kicks in on Business when you turn on calibration analytics. Most teams land $15–30K/year for a 30–50-person hiring panel.

## Best for

- Series A through public companies hiring across panels
- Engineering, product, and GTM teams with structured rubrics
- TA leaders trying to fix interviewer drift and bias

## Watch-outs

- Calibration features need a few hundred interviews to be statistically meaningful
- Interviewer adoption is the real risk — leaders need to mandate it or it stays shelfware
- Greenhouse / Lever / Ashby integration depth varies; test your stack before committing
