## What it is

Avaya is the legacy enterprise contact-center and telephony vendor behind Aura, Elite and the on-premises call centers still running in many banks, hospitals, airlines and government agencies. Its current product line is **Avaya Infinity**, launched in April 2025 as the next step for the Avaya Experience Platform (AXP). Infinity is built on Edify, a cloud contact-center and UC platform Avaya bought in 2024, and runs in three models: cloud, hybrid, and on-premises. Avaya's product leadership has said the plan is to fold its whole portfolio into Infinity over time.

The company behind it matters as much as the product. Avaya went through Chapter 11 twice in six years, most recently in 2023, and has been privately held since. After that exit it narrowed its focus to roughly its 1,500 largest global customers, ended new sales of SIP trunking and its communications APIs (both retired 28 April 2025), and set a 200-seat monthly minimum on its AXP public cloud from 30 June 2025. Jeff Clarke, previously CEO of Insurity, became CEO on 3 August 2026, with Patrick Dennis moving to executive chair. ZK Research's Zeus Kerravala described the hire as one that "positions Avaya defensively": balance sheet, margins and retention first.

## Why it shows up in support stacks

- **It keeps the voice core you already run.** Avaya's pitch is "innovation without disruption": put AI, orchestration and cloud routing on top of an existing Aura or Elite estate instead of ripping it out. For a 2,000-seat center with years of call flows and regulatory sign-off on its telephony, that is the cheapest-looking path on paper.
- **MCP is the AI integration point.** Avaya says Infinity acts as both an MCP server and an MCP client, was among the first contact-center platforms to commit to MCP (July 2025), and is a member of the Linux Foundation's Agentic AI Foundation. In practice this means you can bring outside AI agents, or your own, and let them reach billing and CRM data through MCP; Avaya calls the human-plus-AI model "Tandem Care". Its CTO has described exposing more Infinity capabilities as MCP tools as roadmap, so confirm which tools exist today.
- **Databricks is the data layer.** Governance for Infinity's AI and zero-copy access to live engagement data (Delta Sharing) run on [Databricks](/en/tools/databricks/). If your data team already lives in Databricks, that is a real advantage; if not, it is one more dependency.
- **Regulated, high-availability voice.** Avaya Nexus, announced 18 March 2026 and targeted for general availability in Q4 2026, is a high-availability voice platform for emergency services, healthcare, utilities and government, deployable on Azure, Google Cloud or locally. It is a companion to Infinity, not a replacement for it.

Evidence of AI in production is thin. The one 2026 reference is avatarin (May 2026), an ANA Holdings spin-off using Infinity to coordinate robots, chat and phone; Infinity also received a mention in the Omdia Universe for Customer Engagement Platforms 2026, with no published rating. Neither is a containment figure.

## Pricing reality

Avaya publishes no list price for Infinity; every deal is quoted on agent count, deployment model, channels and AI modules. The only per-agent figures in public view come from a UK G-Cloud filing for AXP bundles, reported second-hand at £40 per named agent per month for voice only and £57.50 for all media, with concurrent licences 25-30% higher. Treat those as a public-sector floor, not what a commercial buyer pays. For comparison, Five9 lists voice at $159 per concurrent seat per month. Budget separately for carrier costs (Avaya no longer sells SIP trunking, so you bring your own carrier), workforce engagement, AI usage, and migration services, which on a multi-site Aura estate typically outweigh a year of licences.

## Best for

Heads of contact-center operations at large regulated enterprises (1,000+ seats, often on-premises or hybrid by policy) that already run Avaya Aura or Elite, have a reason to keep voice on-site or in a private cloud, and want to add AI agents without a full CCaaS re-platform in the next 24 months.

Skip it if you run fewer than a few hundred seats, are not already an Avaya customer, or need a vendor with a published AI-containment track record today.

## Versus the alternatives

**[Genesys Cloud](/en/tools/genesys-cloud/)** and **[NICE CXone](/en/tools/nice-cxone/)** are the two largest enterprise CCaaS platforms and the most common migration targets for Avaya estates. Pick Genesys when you want one orchestration layer over several vendors' AI agents; pick NICE when workforce management and quality management drive the decision. **[Talkdesk](/en/tools/talkdesk/)** is the fastest climber in the segment (Visionary to Leader in Gartner's 2025 CCaaS Magic Quadrant); pick it for faster mid-market rollout. **[Five9](/en/tools/five9/)** wins when outbound volume matters, and **[Amazon Connect](/en/tools/amazon-connect/)** for AWS-native teams. Keep **[Verint](/en/tools/verint/)** in view if you stay on Avaya: it remains a common WEM layer on Avaya estates.

The rule: stay on Avaya and adopt Infinity only if a regulatory or architectural reason keeps voice on-premises or in a private cloud, and you can sign a contract that survives the vendor's refinancing. Otherwise, run your Avaya renewal as a migration RFP, and add AI voice agents such as [Parloa](/en/tools/parloa/) or [PolyAI](/en/tools/polyai/) on the destination platform. The [voice support agent stack](/en/stacks/voice-support-agent-stack/) shows that pattern.

## Watch-outs

- **Restructuring and refinancing risk.** Two bankruptcies since 2017, repeated layoff rounds and a company-wide voluntary exit offer in August 2025, and one trade report puts remaining debt at about $800M with obligations due in August 2028. Guard: negotiate a change-of-control and insolvency exit clause, source-code or data escrow for on-premises components, and keep your term inside the refinancing window or price the exit into it.
- **Product end-of-sale churn.** SIP trunking, CPaaS, AXP voice recording bundles and Agent for Desktop (end of new sales 20 July 2026, expansions end 23 October 2028) have all been retired in 18 months. Guard: before signing, get Avaya's written end-of-sale and end-of-support dates for every component in your bill of materials, and require 24 months' notice for future retirements.
- **Small-customer exit.** The 200-seat floor on AXP public cloud shows how Avaya treats accounts outside its top tier. Guard: if you are below that size or shrinking, start a migration plan now rather than at renewal.
- **AI claims ahead of evidence.** MCP support and Tandem Care are real announcements, but there is no published containment rate. Guard: pilot on your top five call reasons with your own containment, transfer and repeat-contact data before committing to Infinity's AI modules.
