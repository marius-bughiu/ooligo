## What it is

The Informa TechTarget Portal is the intent-data product of Informa TechTarget (Nasdaq: TTGT), the company formed in December 2024 when Informa merged its digital businesses into TechTarget and took a majority stake. In September 2025 the Portal replaced **Priority Engine**, the product most buyers still know it by. Existing Priority Engine customers were moved onto the matching Portal modules, and G2 still lists it as "Informa TechTarget Portal (Priority Engine)."

What sets it apart is where the signal comes from. TechTarget owns the publications. Its intent is built from what registered members read and download across its own network of IT and B2B tech media, which third-party reviews citing the company's investor materials put at **220+ editorial properties and a 57.6 million-member permissioned audience**. In the same September 2025 announcement, the company said combining the Informa Tech and TechTarget properties grew its intent data by **41%**. Because readers register, the output is not only an account score. It goes down to **named prospects**, with the topics each one researched.

The Portal packages that data at four levels: market-level purchase analytics, account-level intent feeds, buying-group identification ("Always-On Buying Groups"), and prospect-level intent exports. The sales module, now sold as **Intent Data for Sales**, shows Prospect-Level Intent inside the Portal and inside Salesforce. It has an "Add to SEP" button that drops active prospects into Outreach sequences or Salesloft cadences, and **IntentMail AI**, which drafts a rep email from the prospect's research. Informa TechTarget was named a Leader in The Forrester Wave: Intent Data Providers for B2B, Q1 2025, alongside Bombora, 6sense, Demandbase and Intentsify.

## When named prospects beat a surge score

The question this page answers: Priority Engine became the Portal, so is TechTarget's first-party intent worth paying for next to a Bombora feed or the intent already inside 6sense or Demandbase?

**Buy it when you sell technology to IT buyers and your reps need a person to call this week.** A co-op feed like [Bombora](/en/tools/bombora/) tells you an account is surging on a topic. The Portal tells you which architect at that account downloaded three white papers on the topic, and lets the rep put that person into a sequence in one click. For a field or inside sales team selling security, cloud, data or networking products, that is the difference between a list of accounts to research and a queue of people to work.

**Skip it when your buyers are not IT.** The network covers technology topics. If you sell to finance, HR, legal or operations buyers, the coverage thins out, and a broader co-op feed or an ABM suite will find more of your market.

## Pricing reality

Informa TechTarget publishes no prices for any product, and there is no trial. Everything goes through a sales conversation and an annual contract. Outside numbers:

- **Vendr's TechTarget page, updated February 2026, puts the median buyer at $95,292 a year across 34 purchases**, with a range of $27,000 to $593,267. That figure covers all TechTarget spend, including content syndication and display, not the Portal alone.
- **For the intent product alone, Vendr's estimate is $30,000-$50,000 a year for mid-market deployments**, $40,000-$80,000 when monitoring 500-2,000 accounts, and over $100,000 for enterprise. Pricing scales with accounts monitored, topics tracked and integrations, and prospect-level access is priced above account-level access.
- **Onboarding and integration services run $5,000-$15,000**, per Vendr. Multi-year terms or a bundle with syndication or display are reported to bring 10-25% off.

Worked estimate, marked as one: a mid-market vendor monitoring 1,000 accounts at $50,000, plus a $10,000 integration fee, pays about **$60,000 in year one** before any media spend. That is more than twice the Vendr median for a direct Bombora license. The premium buys the named contacts.

## Best for

A **sales or marketing ops leader at a B2B tech vendor selling to IT buyers**, with an SDR or inside sales team on Outreach or Salesloft and a named account list. The scoped case where the Portal wins outright: routing the specific people at target accounts who are researching your category on TechTarget sites into rep sequences each week, with the research history attached for the first touch.

## Not for

Companies whose buyers sit outside IT. Teams selling mostly to SMB accounts, where readers on enterprise IT publications are sparse. And teams that want a general contact database for outbound: the Portal gives you the people it has observed researching, not a searchable directory, so you still need a data vendor such as [ZoomInfo](/en/tools/zoominfo/) for the rest of the account.

## Versus the alternatives

- **6sense** — one of the two market-share leaders in ABM platforms. It blends Bombora and its own signals into predictive stages and runs ads and orchestration on top. Pick it when you want one model and one place to run plays across every buyer type. Pick the Portal for named IT researchers 6sense cannot show you. The two connect, so some teams run both. See [6sense](/en/tools/6sense/).
- **Demandbase** — the other large ABM suite, with its own intent, account identification and advertising. Pick it for one vendor across ads and sales intelligence. Pick the Portal when your reps need people, not accounts. See [Demandbase](/en/tools/demandbase/).
- **Bombora** — the wholesale co-op feed resold inside most GTM tools. Pick it for coverage beyond IT and for account scores you can push to ads and models at a lower price. Pick the Portal when the account score alone does not tell your reps who to call. See [Bombora](/en/tools/bombora/).
- **Intentsify** — intent plus buying-group contacts and managed media. Pick it for broader topic coverage with contacts attached. See [Intentsify](/en/tools/intentsify/).
- **The signal-stack approach** — the fastest-growing alternative composes first-party visitor ID from [Warmly](/en/tools/warmly/) with job changes, hiring and tech installs pulled through [Clay](/en/tools/clay/). Pick it when your motion runs on triggers from your own site. Pick the Portal when buyers do their research on third-party IT media long before they reach your site.

## Watch-outs

- **Contact accuracy varies.** G2 reviewers report outdated titles and wrong contact details on some prospects. **Guard:** in the pilot, verify a random 50 prospects against LinkedIn and your enrichment vendor, and write the measured accuracy rate into the renewal terms.
- **The headline price is often a bundle.** Vendr's $95,000 median mixes intent with syndication and display media. **Guard:** ask for the intent license, onboarding fee and any media as separate line items, and benchmark the intent line against a direct Bombora quote before you sign.
- **Coverage stops at IT and tech topics.** **Guard:** before contracting, send your target account list and ask how many accounts and prospects showed activity on your topics in the last 90 days. If fewer than one in five accounts show any, the data will not move pipeline.
- **One-click sequencing can burn good prospects.** Dropping every active reader into a cadence produces generic outreach at volume. **Guard:** require the IntentMail draft to cite the specific asset the prospect read, cap auto-adds per rep per week, and track reply rate on Portal-sourced sequences separately from the rest.
