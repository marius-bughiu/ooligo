[Smartlead](/en/tools/smartlead/) and [lemlist](/en/tools/lemlist/) both send cold email at volume, both include warmup on every plan, and — as of 2026 — both ship an official MCP server, so an agent can drive campaigns from Claude on either side. Smartlead's exposes 116+ tools across six categories; lemlist ships MCP alongside a CLI. If you last compared these two on "which one an agent can operate", that axis is closed and you need a different one.

The axis that still decides it is channel mix and who the bill scales with. Smartlead is sending infrastructure: unlimited mailboxes and unlimited users on every tier, priced on send volume, with placement testing, dedicated IPs, and white-label client workspaces sold as add-ons. lemlist is a multichannel sequencer with the data included: LinkedIn, SMS, WhatsApp, and calling run as native steps next to email, and a 650M-lead database sits under every plan. So: is email the whole motion and the buyer an agency or a volume sender, or does a LinkedIn touch do real work in your cadence?

## Where Smartlead wins

- **Agency multi-tenancy is a product, not a workaround.** White-label client workspaces run $29/mo each from the Pro plan up, with three or more included on Unlimited Prime, so clients log in under your brand with campaigns and inboxes isolated per account. lemlist has no white-label equivalent — an agency running ten clients on lemlist is running ten seats or ten accounts.
- **The deliverability control surface.** SmartDelivery inbox-placement testing is sold in three tiers ($49/mo for 120 sequence tests, $174/mo unlimited across 200 sender accounts, $599/mo across 500), SmartServers add a dedicated IP at $39/server/mo, and the warmup pool exposes its own strength with an in-app upgrade path. These are knobs lemlist does not expose.
- **The bill ignores headcount.** Every Smartlead plan includes unlimited users and unlimited email accounts, with no per-seat and no per-mailbox fee. Adding your eighth rep changes nothing on the invoice. That is the structural difference from lemlist's multichannel line.

## Where lemlist wins

- **LinkedIn is a real step, not a handoff.** Connection requests, messages, voice notes, SMS, WhatsApp, and dialer steps run inside the same sequence with one reply inbox. Smartlead markets LinkedIn as part of its multichannel story, but the LinkedIn actions execute in a partner tool — Expandi documents the integration as importing leads from Smartlead and exporting them back — so the cadence and the reply handling sit in two products with two reporting surfaces.
- **Data on the same invoice.** The 650M-lead database, unlimited contacts, and the email and phone finder are on every plan, metered by credits at $0.01 each: 5 credits for a verified email, 20 for a phone number. On Smartlead, verified prospect emails are a $59/mo add-on on Base and Pro, included only from Unlimited Smart up.
- **Cheaper per sent email at matched volume.** On the email-only plan, lemlist undercuts Smartlead at the tiers most teams actually buy — see below.

## Pricing reality

Smartlead prices on sends and stored contacts: Base **$39/mo** (6K sends, 2K contacts), Pro **$94/mo** (90K sends, 30K contacts), Unlimited Smart **$174/mo** (150K sends, unlimited contacts), Unlimited Prime **$379/mo** (500K sends). Annual billing cuts 17%, taking those to $32.50, $78.30, $144.50, and $314.60. API and webhooks start at Pro — Base has none.

lemlist prices on two different axes. The **Email** plan has unlimited users and unlimited email senders and bills purely on monthly send volume: $69 for 50K, $89 for 100K, $159 for 200K, $359 for 500K, $659 for 1M, with annual billing cutting 20% to $55, $80, $127, $287, and $527. **Multichannel** is per seat at $109/user/mo, $87 on annual, with five senders per user and the LinkedIn, SMS, and dialer steps the Email plan omits. Enterprise is quote-only from five seats.

Match them at 150K sends a month, email only. Smartlead's Unlimited Smart is $144.50/mo on annual; lemlist's 200K tier is $127/mo and carries the lead database and unlimited contacts. lemlist is the cheaper line item before you add Smartlead's $59 verified-email add-on or SmartDelivery, and buying mailboxes through SmartSenders adds $3.99–$5 per mailbox per month for fresh inboxes ($9 pre-warmed) plus $13–$19 per domain per year.

The inversion runs the other way on multichannel. lemlist Multichannel bills per seat while Smartlead's bill is flat against headcount, so at two seats lemlist Multichannel ($174/mo annual) has already passed Unlimited Smart ($144.50). At six seats it is $522/mo against $144.50 — 3.6x. At ten seats it is 6x. You are buying the LinkedIn channel, and the premium scales linearly with rep count while Smartlead's does not. Price the channel mix and the headcount together, not separately.

## Implementation and risk

The warmup ramp differs in kind, and it is the thing teams under-budget. lemwarm is identical on every lemlist plan and deliberately conservative: roughly 40 sends per day per mailbox as the working ceiling, three to four weeks to ramp there, then about two weeks holding steady before real campaigns — call it five to six weeks from signature to first send at volume. Smartlead's warmup volume is unlimited on every plan, but pool quality is tiered by plan: Foundation on Base, Growth on Pro, Premium with Ultra Premium eligibility on the Unlimited tiers. Guard: do not read "unlimited warmup" as "the same warmup" — if you are on Base or Pro you are on the lower pool tier, and the plan upgrade is the fix, not a setting.

The rest of the risk is shared, because the sending mechanism is. Warmup does not fix deliverability on either tool: keep per-mailbox volume conservative at 20–30 a day, rotate domains instead of pushing one harder, and verify lists before sending. Two tool-specific guards. lemlist's database-size claim is not consistent across its own pages, so run your actual ICP through the 14-day trial and count what comes back verified, and keep a dedicated data vendor for named accounts. And Smartlead's $39 Base tier has no API or webhooks, so if you plan to trigger campaigns from [Clay](/en/tools/clay/) or n8n, Pro at $94 is your real floor — budget from there.

## Verdict

- **Pick Smartlead** when you run an agency that needs white-label client workspaces, when email is the only channel and you send at volume, or when deliverability is the job and you need placement testing, dedicated IPs, and per-mailbox throttling. It also wins outright when headcount is growing faster than your channel count, because the bill does not move with seats.
- **Pick lemlist** when a LinkedIn, SMS, or WhatsApp touch does real work in the cadence and you want it in one sequence with one reply inbox, or when you would otherwise buy a separate data vendor. Keep the per-seat math honest: past roughly ten reps on Multichannel, re-run it against Smartlead plus a dedicated LinkedIn tool.
- **Pick neither** if you send under 1,000 emails a month from a single domain — whatever sequencer ships with your CRM already covers it. And if you need email at volume plus LinkedIn but Multichannel's per-seat math does not work at your headcount, run Smartlead for email alongside a per-sender LinkedIn tool and accept the two reporting surfaces as the price.

Default pick: **lemlist.** For a team choosing specifically between these two and unable to break the tie, the Email plan is already competitive with Smartlead's on price at matched volume while including the database, and it leaves the LinkedIn channel open without a migration. Move to Smartlead when you are an agency selling under your own brand, or when email at volume is the entire motion and the deliverability control surface is what you are actually buying.
