## What it is

Fullstory is an Atlanta-based session-replay and behavioral-analytics platform, still independent and run by co-founder and CEO Scott Voigt. The core product captures every click, scroll, rage-click and error on a web or mobile app without manual event tagging, then lets you replay any session or query the captured data as funnels, journeys and metrics.

Two 2026 changes are why it belongs on a Customer Success shortlist rather than only a product-analytics one:

- **It now acts on what it sees.** Fullstory bought Usetiful, an Estonian digital-adoption startup, on 2025-11-03 and relaunched it on 2026-02-19 as **Guides and Surveys**: no-code in-app walkthroughs and embedded surveys, targeted by user properties and live behavior, with every survey response linked to the session that produced it. That puts Fullstory in the same conversation as [Pendo](/en/tools/pendo/) for the first time.
- **AI agents can read it.** The Fullstory MCP server (announced in beta on 2026-06-16, now on by default for all paying customers per Fullstory's help center) connects Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Gemini Enterprise and other MCP clients to sessions, analytics and StoryAI Opportunities. **Agentic Session Review**, shipped in July 2026 through the same MCP, lets an agent open a session, step through its accessibility tree, request pixel screenshots at any timestamp and pull console and network logs, so a CSM can ask "why did this account's admin abandon SSO setup?" without watching a replay.

Fullstory's September 2026 H1 release reports multi-product ARR growing more than 100% year over year and weekly MCP tool calls up 10x in Q2. Both are vendor numbers; read them as direction, not proof.

## Pricing reality

Fullstory publishes no paid prices. Every paid product says "Request pricing & demo" on the plans page (checked 2026-11-26):

- **FullstoryFree** — $0, permanent. 30,000 sessions a month, 12 months of retention, up to 10 users. Session replay, basic analytics and debugging only; dashboards and StoryAI need a paid plan.
- **Business, Advanced, Enterprise** — quote-only, sized by monthly session volume. Session caps and retention per tier are not published.
- **Add-ons on every tier:** StoryAI, Guides and Surveys, Mobile, Multi-Org Management. Fullstory Anywhere (warehouse sync and activation) is a separately quoted product line.

Real-world numbers come from procurement data. Vendr's marketplace puts the median Fullstory contract at **$26,856/year** across 313 purchases, with observed deals from $8,287 to $98,981 and an average negotiated saving of 19%. Sub-$10k deals are small-traffic Business contracts; the high end is Enterprise with add-ons. Budget the add-ons explicitly: the MCP requires StoryAI to be switched on, and Guides and Surveys is never included in the base plan.

## Best for

Customer Success and digital-experience leads at mid-market and enterprise B2B or e-commerce companies who need to see *why* specific accounts struggle in the product, want their CS agents and coding assistants to query that evidence over MCP, and are open to running in-app nudges from the same tool that detected the friction. Best economics when product, engineering and CS share one contract.

## Do not buy it if

You only need replay for a small site: FullstoryFree or Microsoft Clarity (free) covers it, and the jump to a five-figure quote buys you nothing. Skip it too if in-app guidance is the primary purchase. Guides and Surveys is under a year old inside Fullstory; [Pendo](/en/tools/pendo/), [Userpilot](/en/tools/userpilot/) and [Appcues](/en/tools/appcues/) have years more depth on onboarding flows, resource centers and NPS programs.

## Versus the alternatives

- **[Contentsquare](/en/tools/contentsquare/)** is the market-share leader and the main head-to-head. It owns Hotjar and [Heap](/en/tools/heap/), bundles support-conversation intelligence, and publishes a $49/mo Growth entry tier, with a Vendr median near $20k/year. Pick Contentsquare when you want replay, retroactive product analytics, surveys and support-ticket analysis on one invoice. Pick Fullstory when replay fidelity and agent-readable session data are the purchase.
- **[PostHog](/en/tools/posthog/)** is the fastest-growing entrant and the price discipline: open source, usage-priced, replay plus product analytics plus feature flags. Pick it when engineering owns the decision and the CFO wants a metered bill instead of an annual commit.
- **Quantum Metric and Glassbox** hold the regulated-enterprise end (banks, airlines, telcos). Pick one when compliance-driven capture is the requirement.
- **[Pendo](/en/tools/pendo/)** is the pick when guides, resource centers and NPS are the main job and replay is secondary.
- **Smartlook buyers need a new home.** Cisco's end-of-life notice set Smartlook's end of sale at 2026-05-31 and the last renewal at 2026-08-31. Fullstory offers migration planning but no migration discount, and event definitions and funnels must be rebuilt by hand.
- None of these run the customer lifecycle. Fullstory feeds [Gainsight](/en/tools/gainsight/) or [Vitally](/en/tools/vitally/); it does not own renewals or health-score playbooks.

## Watch-outs

- **Quote-only pricing with a mandatory add-on chain.** The MCP needs StoryAI, and the action layer needs Guides and Surveys, so the AI story in the demo is three line items. *Guard:* ask for base plan, StoryAI, Guides and Surveys, and Mobile priced in one proposal, and benchmark against the Vendr median before you sign.
- **Session-metered contracts punish traffic growth.** A marketing win becomes a CS budget overrun. *Guard:* write the session ceiling and per-block overage rate into the order form, not a referenced rate card.
- **Guides and Surveys is a 2026 product built on an acquisition.** Fullstory's claim that embedded surveys lift response rates from 5–7% to over 30% is vendor-sourced. *Guard:* pilot one onboarding guide and one survey on a single segment for a quarter, and measure completion against your current tool before replacing it.
- **Agents reading sessions widen your data exposure.** Every MCP client you connect can pull user behavior. Fullstory carries existing masking rules into the MCP and offers separate EU and US endpoints, and the MCP toggle is on by default. *Guard:* audit masking rules for PII fields before enabling StoryAI, restrict API keys to named admins, and pin EU customers to the `eu1` endpoint.
- **MCP throughput is capped at 3 requests per second (burst 20) per org.** Fine for a CSM asking questions; tight for an agent sweeping hundreds of accounts. *Guard:* batch account reviews off-hours and use Fullstory Anywhere warehouse sync, not the MCP, for bulk scoring.
