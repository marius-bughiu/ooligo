## What it is

MyCase is a legal practice management platform optimized for solo and small firm-side practices — primarily personal injury, family law, criminal defense, immigration, and other plaintiff or consumer-facing practice areas where intake volume and client engagement matter as much as matter management. Owned by AffiniPay (the LawPay parent), MyCase pairs naturally with LawPay for payments and Docketwise for immigration-specific workflows.

## Why it shows up in Legal Ops stacks

- **Built for plaintiff and consumer practices.** Lead management, intake forms, marketing automation, and client portal are first-class — different priorities than [Clio](/en/tools/clio/)'s broader practice coverage.
- **Aggressive pricing.** $39/user/month entry point makes MyCase the most affordable real practice-management option, especially for solo practices that don't need accounting depth.
- **Native LawPay integration.** Payments, trust accounting, and client billing flow through one ecosystem with minimal reconciliation overhead.

## Pricing

- **Basic** — $39/user/month, core practice management and time tracking
- **Pro** — $79/user/month, billing, payments, custom fields
- **Advanced** — $99/user/month, workflow automation, business intelligence
- **Premium** — $109/user/month, advanced reporting and unlimited e-signatures
- AffiniPay payment processing fees separate

## Best for

- Solo practitioners and small firms in plaintiff, family, criminal defense, and immigration practice areas
- Practices where lead intake and conversion matter as much as matter management
- Firms standardized on LawPay for payments

## Watch-outs

- AI features lag [Clio](/en/tools/clio/) Duo and the firm-AI specialists; not the platform of choice for firms prioritizing AI adoption
- Less suited for transactional or corporate practice areas — fewer document-management and CLM-adjacent features than [Clio](/en/tools/clio/)
- Limited fit for in-house Legal Ops; MyCase is a firm-side product
