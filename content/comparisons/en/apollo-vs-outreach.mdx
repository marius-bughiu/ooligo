Apollo and Outreach aren't really direct competitors — Apollo is the "good enough at everything" all-in-one for small teams, and Outreach is the enterprise sales engagement platform. But they get compared because growing teams hit the upgrade question: when do we leave Apollo and move to Outreach?

## Where Apollo wins

- **All-in-one pricing.** Apollo bundles a contact database, sequencer, dialer, and basic CRM sync from $49/seat/month. Outreach is sequencer-only at $130-180/seat/month, and you still need ZoomInfo or similar for data.
- **Time to first sequence.** Apollo gets you sending in an hour. Outreach onboarding typically runs 2-4 weeks.
- **Free tier.** Apollo's free tier is genuinely usable for a 1-2 person SDR team. Outreach has no free tier.
- **Database included.** Apollo's contact database is built in, so you skip the ZoomInfo contract for most use cases.

## Where Outreach wins

- **Sequence sophistication.** Branching logic, A/B testing on send times, deeply customizable activity reporting. Apollo's sequences are simpler and the reporting is shallower.
- **Salesforce integration depth.** For teams running heavy Salesforce customization, Outreach's integration handles edge cases Apollo doesn't.
- **Scale.** 200+ reps with complex permissions, audit requirements, and multi-region operations stress Apollo's UI. Outreach was built for this.
- **Ecosystem.** Native integrations with Gong, Chorus, ZoomInfo, LinkedIn Sales Navigator are deeper.

## When does the upgrade trigger?

The natural breakpoints:

- **5-15 reps:** Apollo is clearly the right call. Don't spend Outreach money.
- **15-30 reps:** Either works. Most teams stay on Apollo unless they're also on Salesforce and wanting deeper integration.
- **30-100 reps:** It becomes a question of how complex the motion is. Single-motion AE org → Apollo is still fine. Multi-motion (outbound + inbound + PLG) → Outreach earns its price.
- **100+ reps:** Almost always Outreach (or Salesloft). Apollo's UI doesn't scale to that many cooks in the kitchen.

## Pricing reality

10-rep team, all-in:

- **Apollo Professional + dialer** — ~$80-100/seat/month → ~$10K/year
- **Outreach Standard + Kaia + dialer** — ~$155/seat/month → ~$18K/year, plus ~$15-25K/year for ZoomInfo or equivalent

Outreach + ZoomInfo runs roughly 3-4x Apollo's all-in cost at this scale. The premium is justified above ~30 reps; below that, you're paying for capabilities you won't use.

## Verdict

- **Use Apollo** if you're under 30 reps, your motion is straightforward, and you'd rather buy one tool than three.
- **Use Outreach** if you're 50+ reps, need deep Salesforce coupling, or have multi-motion complexity that demands a real sales engagement platform.
- **Use Apollo + Clay** as a step-up from raw Apollo before considering the Outreach migration. Adding Clay for enrichment + AI columns covers most of what teams think they need Outreach for, at a fraction of the cost.

The single mistake to avoid: jumping to Outreach because you "feel like" you've outgrown Apollo. Reach for Clay first; the data quality jump is bigger than the sequencer jump for most teams.
