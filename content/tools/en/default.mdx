## What it is

Default is an AI-native inbound platform that collapses the demo-request workflow into one product: forms, enrichment, qualification, routing, and meeting booking. A lead lands on your site, Default enriches them in real time, qualifies against your ICP, and books an AE on the spot — without bouncing through Chili Piper, Calendly, and a Marketo flow.

The pitch is "kill your inbound stack." It mostly delivers.

## Why it shows up in RevOps stacks

- **Real-time enrichment + routing.** Default pulls firmographics at form submit and routes by territory, segment, or round-robin in under a second.
- **AI qualifier.** Replaces the "company size" dropdown with inferred data, so junk-fill rates drop and qualified leads route faster.
- **One vendor instead of five.** Forms + enrichment + scheduling + routing in a single contract. RevOps gets fewer Zaps to debug.

## Pricing

- **Custom only** — quoted by inbound volume and seat count.
- Typical mid-market deals start around $20K-$30K ARR.
- Annual contracts; no public free tier.

## Best for

- B2B SaaS teams with high-intent inbound demo requests
- RevOps teams replacing 3+ point tools (Chili Piper, Clearbit forms, etc.)
- Companies with clear ICP rules they want enforced at form submit

## Watch-outs

- Younger company; expect feature gaps versus mature stacks
- Less useful if your motion is outbound-heavy or PLG self-serve
- Enrichment quality depends on third-party providers underneath; test with your ICP
