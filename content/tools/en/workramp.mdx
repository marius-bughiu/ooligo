## What it is

WorkRamp is a Y Combinator–backed learning platform that sold two products on one codebase: a **Customer Learning Cloud** for external academies and an **Employee Learning Cloud** for onboarding, compliance, and sales enablement. Learning Pool, the Marlin Equity–backed UK learning company, bought it on 15 October 2025. On 10 August 2026 Learning Pool renamed itself **Confirm**, and the WorkRamp products were renamed with it: the customer side is now **Academy** and the employee side is now **Learn:Up**. workramp.com now redirects to confirm.com. The login (app.workramp.com) and the help center still carry the old name, and most buyers still search for it as WorkRamp.

The renaming matters more than usual in customer education. [Gainsight](/en/tools/gainsight/) owns both Northpass and Skilljar. WorkRamp was the third name on most academy shortlists, and it now sits inside a five-company consolidation (Learning Pool, WorkRamp, Elucidat, WorkStep, Confirm HR) that sells by customer segment. You are no longer buying from a focused startup. You are buying one module of a PE-owned suite.

## What you get

- **Academy (customer and partner education).** Branded hubs on your own domain with SSO from your app, white-label theming, and per-segment or per-partner-tier experiences. Paid and free course tiers, certification ecommerce, revenue reporting on training, and SEO-indexed academy pages. Certifications can be issued automatically on completion and published to LinkedIn. Content can be embedded in your product or help center. 2026 releases added custom roles for Academy hubs (June), prerequisites in an early-adopter program, and bulk CSV invites of up to 5,000 contacts (August).
- **Learn:Up (employees and GTM teams).** Onboarding paths, compliance, a skills engine that maps skills to trainings, SCORM import, and **AI Simulations**: role-plays and pitch certifications that sales managers can put inside a path and set to repeat. Integrations listed include Salesforce, Slack, Zoom, Okta, Google Workspace, and HRIS connectors.
- **An MCP connector, for Learn:Up only.** Released 21 October 2026, it is read-only and runs on each user's own permissions. It lets Claude or another assistant look up learners, assignments, paths, SCORM courses, and certifications. "MCP for Academy" is still listed as coming soon.

## Pricing reality

Confirm now publishes floor prices for the employee side only. **Learn:Up starts at $9 per user per month, billed annually, based on a 200-person company.** That is $21,600 a year at list for 200 seats. **Practice**, the standalone AI role-play product formerly called AI Sims, starts at $4 per user per month, which adds $9,600 a year at the same headcount. **Academy is quote-only.**

Vendr's contract data, last updated February 2026 and so from before the repackaging, puts the median WorkRamp deal at **$19,300 a year**, ranging from $4,990 to $69,154. Implementation and content migration add 10–20% to year one, and renewals typically carry 3–7% annual uplifts. A realistic first-year number for a mid-market customer academy is **$20,000 to $45,000**. That puts it below Intellum's $41,000 median, and it is the main reason WorkRamp makes shortlists.

## Best for

A Customer Education or Enablement lead at a B2B SaaS company with roughly 500 to 20,000 external learners who wants a branded academy live in one quarter, and who would like the same vendor to run CSM and sales onboarding with AI role-play.

**Not for you if** your CS org runs on Gainsight and you want completions feeding health scores natively, or if you need multi-tenant partner portals with deep commerce. In both cases, other platforms are built for exactly that.

## Versus the alternatives

- **Skilljar by Gainsight** has the largest installed base in customer education. Pick it when Gainsight is already your CS system of record. Pick WorkRamp/Academy when you specifically do not want your education platform owned by your CS-platform vendor.
- **[Docebo](/en/tools/docebo/)** is the broadest enterprise learning platform of the set. Pick it when employee learning is most of your volume and you need extended-enterprise depth across many audiences.
- **[Intellum](/en/tools/intellum/)** is the pick for certification that has to survive a compliance or contractual audit, and for one instance covering customers, partners, and staff at enterprise scale.
- **[Thought Industries](/en/tools/thought-industries/)** is the pick for multi-tenant partner and reseller portals and paid course catalogs as a revenue line.
- **[Mindtickle](/en/tools/mindtickle/)** or **[Seismic](/en/tools/seismic/)** Learning is the better choice if what you actually need is sales readiness rather than a customer academy. Both go deeper on rep coaching than Learn:Up.

## Watch-outs

- **Roll-up risk.** The product has been renamed once in 10 months, and it now shares a roadmap with four other acquired products. *Guard:* put a price-lock for the full term, a data-export clause (courses, completions, certifications in a documented format), and a termination right if Academy is end-of-lifed or merged into another Confirm product into the order form.
- **Reporting is the most-cited complaint.** G2 reviewers (4.4 of 5 across about 620 reviews) repeatedly name basic analytics, limited filtering, and having to export to a BI tool. *Guard:* during the trial, rebuild your top 3 executive reports from the admin console. If any of them needs an export, budget for your BI tool to own them and confirm API access is included at your tier.
- **AI Simulations is sold twice.** Confirm lists AI Simulations as a core Learn:Up feature and also sells Practice separately at $4 a user. *Guard:* get the simulation entitlements (seat count, voice vs. text, languages) named in writing.
- **The MCP story is employee-side.** The live connector is read-only and covers Learn:Up. *Guard:* do not count agent access to academy data in the business case until MCP for Academy ships and you have tested it.
