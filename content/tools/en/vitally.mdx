## What it is

Vitally is the customer-success platform built around CSM productivity and notion-style flexibility — pulls customer health data (product usage, support tickets, NPS, billing), surfaces accounts at risk, and gives the CSM a workflow surface to manage their book without bouncing between Salesforce, the product analytics tool, and Slack. Used by CS teams at $20-300M ARR SaaS companies that have outgrown spreadsheet-tracking but find [Gainsight](/en/tools/gainsight/) too heavy and [ChurnZero](/en/tools/churnzero/) too narrow.

## Why it shows up in RevOps stacks

- **CSM workflow over CSM dashboard.** Most CS platforms are dashboard-heavy and workflow-light; Vitally inverts. The CSM lives in Vitally for daily account work.
- **Customer health that includes product-usage signal.** Pulls from Segment / [Mixpanel](/en/tools/mixpanel/) / Amplitude (the product analytics) directly, not via a one-off ETL. Health scores reflect what customers actually do in the product.
- **Mid-market positioning.** Cheaper than Gainsight, deeper than ChurnZero, more flexible than [Catalyst](/en/tools/catalyst/) for teams that want notion-like custom layouts.

## Pricing reality

Vitally is custom-quoted; no public pricing on the public site. Customer-side reports place mid-market deployments (10-30 CSMs) at $30K-$70K annually; enterprise deployments (50+ CSMs) at $80K-$200K+. Per-seat-plus-platform-fee structure; volume discounts exist.

The economics work for CS teams of 10+ CSMs where the platform spend is meaningfully less than the CSM productivity lift. They don't work for sub-5-CSM teams where the manual workflow + Salesforce is fine.

## Best for

- $20-300M ARR B2B SaaS with 10-50 CSMs and a defined customer-health practice.
- CS teams whose product has a tracked product-usage signal worth incorporating into health scoring.
- RevOps-CS partnerships where the CS team needs platform autonomy without burning the RevOps roadmap.

## Versus the alternative

- **vs [Gainsight](/en/tools/gainsight/).** Gainsight is the enterprise standard — heavier, more configurable, more expensive. Pick Gainsight for 50+ CSM teams with mature CS programs and budget. Pick Vitally for the mid-market sweet spot where Gainsight's complexity is overkill.
- **vs [ChurnZero](/en/tools/churnzero/).** ChurnZero is workflow-focused like Vitally but narrower in customization. Pick ChurnZero for plug-and-play simplicity; pick Vitally for flexibility.
- **vs [Catalyst](/en/tools/catalyst/).** Catalyst is the most direct competitor at the mid-market tier. Pick Catalyst for stronger renewal-forecasting (Catalyst's ARR-management is a differentiator). Pick Vitally for stronger CSM daily-workflow.
- **vs Salesforce + spreadsheets.** Default at smaller CS teams. Workable up to ~5 CSMs; stalls at scale.

## Watch-outs

- **Implementation depth depends on health-score design.** Vitally is only as good as the health-scoring logic the CS team configures. **Guard:** budget 4-8 weeks for the health-score design + tuning; rolling out without a thoughtful health-score is rolling out a dashboard with no signal.
- **Notion-style flexibility = configuration drift.** Different CSMs build different layouts; the team's view diverges. **Guard:** designate a single Vitally admin who maintains team-standard layouts; allow per-CSM overlays but not full redesigns.
- **Product-usage signal requires the analytics tool to exist first.** Vitally pulls from Segment / Mixpanel / Amplitude; if the product team doesn't track meaningful events, Vitally's health scores fall back to lagging indicators (login count, support tickets). **Guard:** confirm the product-analytics layer is mature before contracting Vitally.
- **Per-seat cost climbs at scale.** **Guard:** at 50+ CSMs, re-evaluate against Gainsight; the cost crossover narrows.
