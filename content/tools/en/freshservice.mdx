## What it is

Freshservice is Freshworks' IT service management (ITSM) suite, sold to mid-market companies that want a ServiceNow-style service desk without a ServiceNow-style implementation. It covers the ticket portal, service catalog, SLAs, change and problem management, asset management, and on-call. **Freshservice for Business Teams** extends the same desk to HR, legal, finance and facilities intake. Freshworks is steering its retired Freshteam HR customers there: it stopped Freshteam renewals from 7 March 2026.

It is not a side product. Freshworks reported on 3 November 2026 that its employee-experience business, which is mostly Freshservice, reached $588 million in ARR, up 22% year on year and 59% of company ARR. The earnings call put the base at about 20,000 Freshservice customers.

2026 has been an acquisition year. Freshworks closed its purchase of FireHydrant (incident management and status pages) in January, bought Robusta, the maker of the open-source HolmesGPT investigation agent, on 28 October, and signed a deal for Torii (SaaS and AI spend management) in October, expected to close by year end. At its Refresh event on 14 May it launched **Freddy AI Agent Studio**, a no-code builder for service agents that run in Slack, Microsoft Teams or the portal, and an **MCP Gateway** in early access.

## Where it fits

- **Mid-market IT desks going live in weeks.** Companies with roughly 20 to 200 fulfillers that need incidents, changes, a service catalog and an asset inventory, and have no budget for a partner-led rollout.
- **One intake for IT, HR and legal.** Business Teams workspaces give legal ops a request form and queue (NDAs, contract reviews, policy questions) on the same platform as IT, with separate SLAs and permissions.
- **Incident response next to the desk.** FireHydrant brings runbooks, retrospectives and status pages into the ServiceOps bundle, which matters if you are leaving Opsgenie before its April 2027 end of support.
- **Agent access in both directions.** Outbound MCP lets Freddy agents act in tools like Atlassian, Notion and Linear (Growth and above, with Agent Studio). Inbound MCP, which lets Claude or Microsoft Copilot query Freshservice data, is Enterprise only.

## Pricing reality

The official pricing table lists **Starter $19, Growth $49 and Pro $99 per agent per month**, billed annually. Enterprise is quote-only. Monthly billing costs more: Freshworks' own earlier price list put Starter at $29 and Pro at $119. Requesters do not need licenses.

AI is priced on top. **Freddy AI Copilot** (drafting, summaries, suggested resolutions) is an add-on at about $29 per agent per month on annual billing, according to several 2026 pricing guides. The autonomous **Freddy AI Agent** is tied to Enterprise, which third-party breakdowns say includes 1,200 sessions per license per year, pooled, with overage packs sold by quote. Agent Studio is included on Growth and Pro from its May launch.

Real numbers: a 25-agent IT desk on Growth pays about $14,700 a year. Move to Pro for asset management depth and project tracking and it is $29,700; add Copilot for every agent and it reaches about $38,400. That is the range where Jira Service Management Standard (around $27 per agent a month on monthly billing after Atlassian's October increase) is cheaper, and where ServiceNow is not yet a serious option.

## Best for

IT and ops leaders at 200 to 3,000-employee companies with 15 to 150 fulfillers, who want ITSM plus HR and legal intake on one platform, live in under a quarter, run by an internal admin rather than a consultancy.

## Versus the alternatives

The two largest players in ITSM are [ServiceNow](/en/tools/servicenow/) and [Jira Service Management](/en/tools/jira-service-management/). Pick ServiceNow above roughly 200 fulfillers or 5,000 employees, when IT, HR and customer service need one data model and you can fund a partner. Pick Jira Service Management when engineering already works in Jira and service requests must link to engineering work items without a sync tool.

The fastest-growing entrants are Slack-native desks such as [Unthread](/en/tools/unthread/). Pick Unthread when most requests already start as Slack threads, the team is under about 20 agents, and you do not need change management or a CMDB.

If the job is external customer support rather than internal service, Freshservice is the wrong Freshworks product: look at [Freshdesk](/en/tools/freshdesk/) or [Zendesk](/en/tools/zendesk/).

## Watch-outs

- **Reporting runs out early.** Capterra's 2026 review summary and G2 reviewers both flag basic, hard-to-customize reporting, and the better analytics sit on higher tiers. Guard: before signing, rebuild your three most important monthly reports (SLA breach rate, backlog age, cost per ticket) in a trial instance, and budget for a BI export if you cannot.
- **The AI you saw in the demo may not be in your plan.** Agent Studio is on Growth and Pro, the Freddy AI Agent is Enterprise, Copilot is a per-agent add-on, and inbound MCP is Enterprise only, with the gateway still in early access. Guard: ask for a line-item quote that names each Freddy capability and its session allowance, and get overage pricing in writing.
- **Three acquisitions in a year.** FireHydrant, Robusta and Torii will change packaging and pricing as they are folded in. Guard: cap renewal increases in the contract and avoid a multi-year term that bundles modules you have not tested.
- **CMDB accuracy needs work.** PeerSpot reviewers report discovery gaps and inaccurate asset records. Guard: run discovery against a known device list during the pilot and reconcile the counts before you rely on it for audits.
