## What it is

Deel is the global HR, payroll, employer-of-record (EOR) and contractor platform, now reporting more than $1.5B in revenue, a $17.3B valuation and 40,000+ customers on its own press page. Until this year it had no applicant tracking system. At its Big Deel event in March 2026 it announced general availability of a native ATS built on Deel HR, which makes it a direct answer to [Rippling](/en/tools/rippling/) Recruiting: one data model from approved headcount to the first payroll run.

The ATS is not sold on its own. Deel's FAQ says it is available to all customers "with HRIS as a required foundation." If you don't run Deel HR, this page is not for you.

## What the ATS actually does

- **Headcount becomes a requisition.** Roles approved in Deel's Workforce Planning module are pushed into the ATS as job requisitions, so the req and the plan cannot drift apart.
- **AI on job creation, matching and first-round screens.** AI drafts job postings and compensation ranges, matches candidates against the criteria you set, and can run first-round screens. Deel states the matching compares the CV and application answers to your criteria, uses no external data sources, and leaves the decision to a human. Screening is optional.
- **Scheduling and interview notes.** Google Workspace and Microsoft Teams are embedded for scheduling. An AI Interview Assistant records with consent, transcribes and summarizes; candidates can opt out.
- **Distribution and career pages.** One posting reaches what Deel calls 1,000+ connected job boards. Career pages generated by the ATS include AI-disclosure text automatically.
- **Offer to onboarding.** Offers are generated in the ATS, and the hired candidate moves into Deel HR for onboarding, payroll and compliance setup without re-entry.

An ATS Pro tier adds premium AI for sourcing, screening and interview intelligence. Deel does not publish a price for it.

## Pricing reality

Deel's pricing page lists "Find talent" (job posting, AI screening, offers, scheduling, interview summaries) from $14 per worker per month, and Deel HR Core at $5 per person per month. Deel's HRIS FAQ says hiring is priced separately from HR. Because the ATS needs the HRIS, budget at least $19 per person per month for HR plus recruiting until a quote says otherwise.

The open question is what "per worker" counts. If it is your whole headcount, a 300-person company pays about $50,400 a year for recruiting alone, which is more than many mid-market standalone ATS contracts. If it covers only hiring users or open roles, it is cheap. Get the unit in writing before you compare it with anything. EOR ($599 per employee per month) and contractor ($49 per contractor per month) fees sit on top for anyone you hire through them.

## Best for

People Ops leaders at 50 to 500-person companies already on Deel HR, hiring a few roles at a time across several countries, where the person who runs recruiting also runs onboarding and payroll. The case is strongest when many hires land on Deel EOR or contractor agreements, because the handoff you remove is a cross-border one.

Do not buy it if outbound sourcing drives a large share of your hires, if structured interviewing and scorecard discipline are the problem you are fixing, or if your HRIS is not Deel.

## Versus the alternatives

[Rippling](/en/tools/rippling/) is the closest analog: an ATS module that only exists on its HRIS, with a longer track record and IT provisioning on the same trigger. Pick Rippling when US-heavy hiring and device and app provisioning matter; pick Deel when most hires are international or EOR. [BambooHR](/en/tools/bamboohr/), [HiBob](/en/tools/hibob/) and [Personio](/en/tools/personio/) offer the same HRIS-plus-ATS bundle for companies that run HR there instead.

Running a dedicated ATS on top of Deel HR is the option to price honestly. [Greenhouse](/en/tools/greenhouse/) leads the mid-market on structured hiring and assessment integrations. [Ashby](/en/tools/ashby/) is the fastest-growing challenger, with analytics and a talent CRM for outbound. [Workable](/en/tools/workable/) is the cheaper SMB default. Pick one of them over Deel's ATS when hiring quality, not handoff speed, is the bottleneck.

## Watch-outs

- **The pricing unit is ambiguous.** "Per worker" can mean everyone on Deel or only hiring users. Guard: get a written quote that names the unit and model it at your full headcount for year two.
- **It is a 2026 product.** Public reviews of Deel mostly rate EOR and payroll, not the ATS. Guard: pilot two or three live requisitions end to end, including a scorecard debrief and an offer, before you cancel your current ATS.
- **Sourcing is thin.** Deel names "connected external sources" and a talent marketplace, not a searchable candidate database or outbound sequences. Guard: count last year's hires from outbound; above roughly 30%, budget for [LinkedIn Recruiter](/en/tools/linkedin-recruiter/) or [Gem](/en/tools/gem/) up front.
- **You cannot keep the ATS without the HRIS.** Guard: require an itemized recruiting line, a module-level exit clause and a full candidate-data export format at signature.
- **AI disclosure is not a compliance programme.** Auto-added disclosure text does not cover a NYC LL 144 bias audit or EU AI Act obligations for high-risk hiring systems. Guard: ask Deel for its audit documentation, or keep AI screening off for jurisdictions where you cannot meet the rules.
