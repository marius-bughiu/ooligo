## What it is

Eightfold is an enterprise talent intelligence platform. Its skills graph — built from a billion+ public profiles — powers sourcing, internal mobility, succession planning, and DEI analytics on top of your HCM. It's HR-tech-as-a-platform, not a point tool.

## Why it shows up in Recruiting/TA stacks

- **Skills-based matching.** Maps candidates and employees to skills (not titles), then projects career trajectories. The matching is genuinely better than keyword search for senior and cross-functional roles.
- **Internal mobility, finally working.** Surfaces internal candidates for open reqs against external pipeline. CHROs love the metric; recruiters get a real second pool.
- **Enterprise-grade.** Workday and SAP integrations are deep. DEI reporting, audit trails, and global compliance are built for Fortune 500.

## Pricing

- **Talent Acquisition** — custom, typically priced per employee or per req volume
- **Talent Management** — internal mobility and skills modules, custom
- **Enterprise Suite** — full platform, custom

No public pricing. Six- to seven-figure annual deals are standard. Implementation usually takes 4–9 months with services.

## Best for

- Enterprise (10K+ employees) running Workday or SAP SuccessFactors
- Orgs treating skills and internal mobility as a strategic priority
- TA teams reporting to a CHRO, not a CFO

## Watch-outs

- Heavy implementation; this is a platform play, not a quick win
- Recruiter-facing UX is dense — sourcers often prefer Gem or hireEZ for daily work
- ROI is real but slow — 12–18 months before the skills graph pays back
