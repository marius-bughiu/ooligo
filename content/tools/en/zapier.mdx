## What it is

Zapier is the original no-code workflow automation platform, with 7,000+ app integrations and a growing AI surface (Zapier Agents, Zapier AI Actions). Ops teams use it for the long tail of "when X happens in Tool A, do Y in Tool B" automations that don't justify a custom build.

## Why it shows up in ops stacks

- **Integration breadth.** If a SaaS tool exists, Zapier probably integrates with it. n8n and Make can't match the catalog.
- **Non-technical accessible.** Ops leaders who can't write code can ship automations the same day.
- **AI Agents and Actions.** Natural-language workflow building is real now — describe the automation, get a draft Zap.

## Pricing

- **Free** — 100 tasks/mo, 5 Zaps
- **Professional** — from $19.99/mo, multi-step Zaps
- **Team** — from $69/mo, shared workspace
- **Enterprise** — custom, SSO, advanced governance
- Tasks are the consumption unit; complex Zaps burn through quotas fast

## Best for

- Ops teams shipping internal automations without engineering
- Glue between SaaS tools that don't have native integrations
- Lightweight notifications, syncs, and form-to-CRM flows

## Watch-outs

- Task-based pricing scales painfully with high-volume workflows
- For complex logic, Make and n8n offer better visual flow control
- Latency on triggers (especially polling-based) isn't real-time
