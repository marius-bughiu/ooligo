## What it is

LexisNexis Protégé is the AI assistant layered on top of Lexis+ AI, built to answer legal research questions, summarize cases, draft arguments, and analyze documents — all grounded in the LexisNexis content library with citations. It's positioned as a personalized AI for lawyers, learning from a firm's matter history and drafting style.

## Why it shows up in legal-ops stacks

- **Citations to authoritative sources.** Answers link back to Lexis content, which matters for any output that has to survive a partner review.
- **Personalization layer.** Protégé surfaces a firm's own templates and prior work alongside research, not just generic model output.
- **Drafting + research in one.** Brief writing, contract review, and case law Q&A under one subscription instead of three.

## Pricing

- **Custom** — bundled with Lexis+ subscriptions, seat-based
- Typically negotiated as part of a broader Lexis content deal
- Solo/small-firm tiers exist via Lexis+ AI

## Best for

- Law firms already on Lexis content
- In-house legal teams doing research-heavy work
- Litigation and regulatory practices needing cite-checked output

## Watch-outs

- Tied to the Lexis content stack — switching costs are real
- Less popular than Harvey in the boutique-firm tier; check market fit
- Hallucinations have been documented industry-wide; verify cites
