## What it is

Zoom Contact Center is Zoom's cloud contact-center platform (CCaaS): voice, video, chat, SMS, email and social routing, a no-code flow editor and IVR, and an AI layer that Zoom now sells as an agentic CX suite. Three parts of it matter for an AI evaluation. **Zoom Virtual Agent (ZVA)** is the customer-facing AI agent for chat and voice. **AI Expert Assist** guides and acts for human agents during live interactions. **CX Insights** answers natural-language questions about contact-center data. It is sold mostly into companies that already run Zoom Workplace or Zoom Phone, and that is the question this page answers: is the Zoom you already pay for a serious contact center and AI agent, or do you still need [Talkdesk](/en/tools/talkdesk/) or [Five9](/en/tools/five9/) plus [Sierra](/en/tools/sierra/) or [Decagon](/en/tools/decagon/)?

2026 is when Zoom started selling it as an AI agent platform. ZVA 3.0 shipped on 24 February 2026 with an execution framework that runs multi-step workflows across CRM, billing and order systems, plus admin visibility into the data sources and decision logic behind each automated action. Zoom said multimodal input, continuous learning from human-agent resolutions and proactive outbound would follow, expected to be generally available in spring 2026. At Enterprise Connect on 10 March 2026 Zoom announced AI Expert Assist 3.0, which moves from suggesting next steps to executing them (updating records, triggering follow-up workflows) inside approved guardrails, along with natural-language customer-workflow orchestration across Workplace, Phone and CX.

## Why it shows up in support stacks

- **One vendor for phones, meetings and the contact center.** Teams already on Zoom Phone get one admin console, one identity layer and one contract. Video and screen sharing are first-class contact-center channels, which matters for remote troubleshooting, onboarding and regulated advice.
- **The AI agent works outside Zoom's own CCaaS.** ZVA's FAQ lists Salesforce, Zendesk, Microsoft Dynamics and Genesys Cloud as places it can run, and the Receptionist plan works with no Zoom Phone. You can pilot the AI agent without migrating the contact center.
- **Momentum is real.** Zoom reported ZVA's customer count up 256% year over year in its Q2 fiscal 2027 results (25 August 2026), and paid AI in nine of its top ten CX deals that quarter. A year earlier, on the Q2 fiscal 2026 call, contact-center customers above $100K ARR were up 94% to 229. IDC named Zoom a Leader in its 2026 MarketScape for agentic CCaaS platforms (August 2026).
- **The analyst picture is split.** Gartner placed Zoom Contact Center as a Niche Player in its 2026 CCaaS Magic Quadrant. Read that as a breadth gap against the enterprise suites, not a verdict on the AI.

## Pricing reality

Zoom publishes seat prices, per agent per month: **Essentials** $69 billed annually ($85 monthly), inbound voice, video, chat and SMS with VoC surveys and AI summaries; **Premium** $99 ($119), which adds email, social, work items, progressive and preview outbound dialing and cobrowse; **Elite** $149 ($179), which adds AI Expert Assist, workforce management, advanced quality management (including QM for ZVA) and the Live AI Interpreter. On Essentials and Premium, Expert Assist 2.0 is an add-on, and the pricing page lists Expert Assist 3.0 for Elite only.

Virtual Agent, AI Intent Routing, CX Insights and the Agentless Dialer are add-ons on every tier, and Zoom publishes no price for any of them. The ZVA pricing page lists plans and features but no per-resolution, per-conversation or per-minute rate, apart from noting that the Receptionist plan includes 100 voice minutes. A 50-seat team on Premium is $59,400 a year at list; 200 seats on Elite is $357,600 before ZVA and telephony. Vendr reports observed outcomes of roughly $90-$130 per agent per month for 50-200 agent deployments, and existing Zoom accounts can use the rest of their Zoom spend to negotiate.

## Best for

Heads of support or CX operations running roughly 20 to 300 agents who already standardised on Zoom Phone or Zoom Workplace, want voice, video and digital channels under one contract, and want to test an AI agent on their top contact reasons before buying a specialist.

Skip it as the primary platform if you run a 1,000+ seat, multi-site operation with complex routing and workforce planning, or if high-volume predictive outbound is the core motion.

## Versus the alternatives

**[Genesys Cloud](/en/tools/genesys-cloud/)** and **[NICE CXone](/en/tools/nice-cxone/)** hold the largest share of enterprise CCaaS. Pick Genesys when you need deep routing and plan to orchestrate several AI-agent vendors behind one front door; pick NICE when workforce engagement and quality management drive the decision. Pick Zoom over both when you are mid-market, already on Zoom Phone, and value one vendor over the deepest feature set. **[Talkdesk](/en/tools/talkdesk/)** wins when an industry edition matches your business. **[Five9](/en/tools/five9/)** wins when predictive outbound volume matters. **[Amazon Connect](/en/tools/amazon-connect/)** fits AWS-native teams that will build on per-minute primitives, and **[Dialpad](/en/tools/dialpad/)** is the cheaper cloud-phone-plus-AI option for smaller teams not on Zoom.

The fastest-growing competition is AI-native agent vendors that sit on top of any CCaaS: [Sierra](/en/tools/sierra/), [Decagon](/en/tools/decagon/), [Parloa](/en/tools/parloa/) and [PolyAI](/en/tools/polyai/). Rule: pilot ZVA on your top five contact reasons with your own containment, transfer and CSAT targets. Add a specialist only where it beats ZVA by enough to fund a second contract. If you buy Zoom's revenue-side tooling as well, [Zoom Revenue Accelerator](/en/tools/zoom-revenue-accelerator/) is a separate product with its own seat price.

## Watch-outs

- **The AI agent has no public price.** ZVA, AI Intent Routing and CX Insights all route to sales. Guard: get the ZVA unit (minute, conversation or resolution), the committed volume, the overage rate and a usage alert written into the order form before you sign seats.
- **Roadmap items were announced as "spring 2026".** Multimodal input, continuous learning and proactive outbound were not generally available at the ZVA 3.0 launch. Guard: contract only for features live on your instance, and have sales confirm GA status for each in writing.
- **The proof points are mostly Zoom's own.** Zoom cites its billing team going from 0% to 30% deflection in three months, and customers Cricut (50% self-service containment) and Vensure. These are vendor figures, and Zoom's own pages quote two different CSAT gains for its internal deployment (25 and 28 points). Guard: run a 60-day pilot against your own baseline and make any AI commit contingent on it.
- **Bundling cuts both ways.** Co-terming the contact center with Zoom Workplace makes leaving one product harder. Guard: negotiate a separate renewal date and escalator cap for Contact Center, and confirm recordings, transcripts and flows export in a usable format.
- **Enterprise depth lags the leaders.** The Gartner Niche Player placement and the dialer list (progressive and preview, no predictive dialer on the pricing page) point the same way. Guard: script your three hardest routing and WFM scenarios into the demo and score them side by side with Genesys or NICE.
