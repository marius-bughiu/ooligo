## What it is

Zoho Desk is the ticket-first helpdesk from Zoho Corporation, the privately held Indian software company behind [Zoho CRM](/en/tools/zoho-crm/), [Zoho Recruit](/en/tools/zoho-recruit/), Books and the Zoho One suite. It covers the Zendesk-shaped feature set: email, web forms, social and chat channels feeding one ticket queue, SLAs and multi-level escalations, a knowledge base and community forum, stage-gated processes (Blueprint), multi-department routing, and telephony on the higher editions. The AI layer is Zia, Zoho's in-house assistant, plus Zia Agents, Zoho's agent builder.

The question it answers: "We need a real helpdesk, our budget is closer to $10 a seat than $50, and maybe we already run Zoho CRM. Is Zoho Desk good enough, and what AI do we actually get on our edition?" For budget support teams and Zoho-stack companies, the answer is usually yes. The catch is that the AI you get depends heavily on the edition you buy.

## What changed in 2026

- **Zia AI moved down the price ladder on 2026-07-07.** Per Zoho's community announcement, sentiment analysis, auto-tagging and the Answer Bot now start on Standard. Anomaly detection, field prediction and the Zoho-hosted Zia model start on Professional. Zia Actions and the Deluge scripting assistant are Enterprise-only. Bring-your-own-key generative AI through ChatGPT starts on Express.
- **AI agents on every paid edition.** Zoho's pricing page lists AI Agents from Express up, running on your own LLM key or on Zoho's models with "up to 30M free tokens/month." Zoho's own Desk release notes describe two support agents, Support Specialist (drafts knowledge-base replies and reassigns to a human when the answer is missing) and Resolution Expert (writes the root cause onto the ticket), and listed both as available on request.
- **MCP access through Zoho MCP.** Zoho MCP lets you build your own MCP server, choose which Desk tools to expose, and connect it to Claude as a custom connector over OAuth. Calls count against Desk's normal API limits.

## Pricing

USD list prices per agent per month, billed annually, as published on zoho.com (the INR price list on the same page scales the same way):

- **Free**: $0 for up to 3 agents, with a limited feature set built around email ticketing. The 3 free seats don't carry over into a paid plan.
- **Express**: $7. Capped at 5 agents. Email, social and web forms, workflows, escalations, AI agents and BYOK generative AI. No chat, telephony, Blueprint or multi-department.
- **Standard**: $14. Adds the chat widget, knowledge base, community forum, and the Answer Bot plus sentiment and auto-tag AI.
- **Professional**: $23. Adds telephony, Blueprint, multi-department, round-robin assignment, webhooks, the Zoho-hosted Zia model and light agents.
- **Enterprise**: $40. Adds Zoho SalesIQ live chat, multi-level IVR, sandbox, custom modules, skill-based assignment, Zia Actions, and 50 free light agents.

Zoho says annual billing saves up to 34%, so monthly billing costs noticeably more per seat. Telephony runs through Zoho Voice or a third-party provider, and call minutes bill separately.

Real-world band: Professional is the tier most teams need, because that is where telephony, routing across departments and the hosted Zia model start. A 10-agent team pays $2,760 a year on annual billing. The same team pays $6,600 on [Freshdesk](/en/tools/freshdesk/) Pro ($55) or Zendesk Suite Team ($55), before either vendor's AI meter. Enterprise for 10 agents is $4,800 a year, still under the Freshdesk Pro bill.

## Best for

A support lead or CS operations manager running a 3-to-50-agent team that needs proper ticketing with SLAs and routing on a budget, especially a company already paying for Zoho CRM or Zoho One. The scoped use case: run email, web form and chat requests through one SLA-tracked queue, have the Answer Bot deflect the routine questions from the knowledge base, and see the customer's deals and invoices from Zoho CRM and Books on the ticket without a sync tool in between.

**Not for you if** your agents will only adopt a modern, lightweight inbox, or if you need a deep app marketplace and workforce management above about 100 agents.

## Versus the alternatives

- **[Zendesk](/en/tools/zendesk/)** is the market leader. Pick it above roughly 100 agents, or when you need workforce management, multi-brand routing and the largest marketplace. Expect to pay 2.4x to 5x Zoho's seat price, and per-resolution fees for its AI agents.
- **[Freshdesk](/en/tools/freshdesk/)** is the number-two ticket-first helpdesk and the nearest like-for-like rival. Pick it when you want a cleaner UI and Freddy AI. Note that Freddy Copilot is a per-agent add-on and the Freddy AI Agent bills per session.
- **[Intercom](/en/tools/intercom/)** is the AI-first entrant the segment now benchmarks against, at $0.99 per Fin resolution. Pick it when chat-led, high-volume deflection is the whole problem and you'll pay per outcome for a stronger agent.
- **[Help Scout](/en/tools/help-scout/)** and **[Hiver](/en/tools/hiver/)** win for small, email-led teams that care more about a friendly shared inbox than ticket admin depth. **[Front](/en/tools/front/)** wins when conversations are account threads across many channels.

The rule: pick Zoho Desk when price per seat or Zoho-suite integration decides the deal, and the team can live with a dense admin UI.

## Watch-outs

- **The edition decides the AI.** The Zoho-hosted Zia model starts at Professional. On Express and Standard, generative replies and summaries run on your own OpenAI key, which adds a second bill and a second data processor. **Guard:** list the AI features in your business case, map each to the 2026-07-07 edition table, and price the tier that holds all of them, not the $7 headline.
- **Express caps at 5 agents and drops core helpdesk features.** No chat, telephony, Blueprint or multi-department on Express, and Standard still lacks telephony and Blueprint. **Guard:** if you expect to pass 5 agents or add phone support within a year, budget for Professional from day one.
- **The free AI token allowance has no published overage rate on the Desk pricing page.** **Guard:** before an annual commitment, get Zoho's per-token or per-credit price above 30M tokens a month in writing, and set an alert on token usage in the first month.
- **Pre-built support agents are on request.** Zoho listed Support Specialist and Resolution Expert as on-request rather than self-serve. **Guard:** ask your Zoho rep to enable them in the trial org, and run them on a week of real tickets before you count deflection in the ROI.
- **Admin depth comes with a dense UI.** Blueprint, Deluge custom functions and custom modules give admins real control, and reviewers consistently describe the interface as cluttered compared with Freshdesk or Help Scout. **Guard:** run the trial with two frontline agents, not just the admin, and time how long the 10 most common ticket actions take.
- **The MCP route is build-your-own.** Through Zoho MCP, Claude can only use the Desk tools you expose, and every call draws on the same API quota as your integrations. **Guard:** expose read tools first, keep reply-sending tools off until you've reviewed agent output, and check your edition's daily API limit before connecting an assistant.
