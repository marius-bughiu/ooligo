## What it is

HERP Hire is an applicant tracking system from HERP Inc. (株式会社HERP, Tokyo), built for Japanese mid-career hiring. Its organizing idea is "scrum recruiting" (スクラム採用): instead of HR and executives running every step, line engineers, managers and other employees join sourcing, screening and interviews, and the ATS assigns each candidate to the people working on them. It is the closest Japanese equivalent to [Greenhouse](/en/tools/greenhouse/) in philosophy, with a stronger bias toward startups and digital-talent hiring.

Four pieces make up the product family in late 2026:

- **HERP Hire**, the ATS: candidate records, job pages, talent pool, scheduling with Google Calendar and Outlook, and dashboards through HERP DataHub.
- **HERP AI Recruiter**, a paid add-on that went generally available on June 23, 2026 after a beta that started in October 2025 and passed 200 customer companies. It does a first-pass document review against job requirements, transcribes and summarizes interviews, drafts evaluations and candidate emails, and analyzes past screening data to suggest requirement changes.
- **HERP Agent Network**, launched April 22, 2026, a rebrand of the job-distribution feature of HERP's Jobmill agency platform. You post a job from your ATS in one click and receive referrals from more than 1,000 recruitment agents, including agencies you have no contract with.
- **HERP Career**, a job site with 100,000+ monthly users.

On August 25, 2026 HERP announced a ¥1.9 billion (about $12 million) Series C led by JIC Venture Growth Investments, with DNX Ventures and Japan Finance Corporation, part equity and part bank loans. HERP Hire and Jobmill together count 4,000+ companies cumulatively, and the company plans to grow from about 100 to 300 staff by 2029. Independent and alive.

## Why it shows up in Recruiting stacks

- **Job-board ingestion is the Japanese table stakes, and HERP does it.** Applications from BizReach, Wantedly and roughly 20-30 other Japanese job boards and scout services flow into one record automatically. Third-party listings disagree on the exact count, which is why it is a guard below.
- **Hiring managers actually use it.** Progress syncs to [Slack](/en/tools/slack/) and Chatwork, with a separate recruiting timeline so candidate discussion stays out of general channels. On ITreview (Summer 2026) HERP Hire scores 4.3 overall from 145 reviews, with 4.6 for ease of rollout and 4.5 for usability, ahead of HRMOS採用 (4.2, 105 reviews) and Jobcan Recruitment (3.7, 33 reviews).
- **Agencies run through the ATS.** In Japan, agency referrals are a large share of mid-career hires. The Agent Network removes the manual re-keying of each job into each agency portal; HERP reports a 20% valid-application rate on its referrals, on par with directly contracted agencies (vendor figure).

## Pricing

- **Free plan:** ¥0, with job pages, talent pool and candidate and screening management. ITreview notes it does not auto-convert to paid.
- **Paid plans:** quoted by hiring volume; no public price list. One third-party catalog (imitsu) lists a Light plan from ¥20,000 a month (about $130) plus a ¥100,000 setup fee. Treat that as a floor, not a quote.
- **Real-world band (estimate):** roughly ¥240,000 to ¥1,000,000+ a year for a startup to mid-size company, driven by job and hire volume, before the AI add-on.
- **AI Recruiter:** priced separately, as a flat-rate plan or a usage-based plan. No public numbers.
- **Agent Network:** fee terms are not in the launch release. Agencies still charge their own placement fees.

## Best for

A Japanese startup or scale-up hiring 20 to 200 mid-career people a year, mostly engineers and other digital roles, that wants hiring managers inside the process and sources through BizReach, Wantedly, scout services and agencies. HR teams of one to five people get the most out of it.

Not for you if your hiring is mostly new-graduate (新卒) volume campaigns, if the recruiting team works in English with non-Japanese-speaking hiring managers, or if you need one global ATS across Japan and other countries.

## Versus the alternatives

- **HRMOS採用 (Visional / BizReach)** — the most common incumbent alternative. Pick it when you want recruiting data to flow into HRMOS core HR after the hire.
- **Jobcan Recruitment (ジョブカン採用管理)** — pick it when you already run other Jobcan modules and price matters more than hiring-manager experience.
- **sonar ATS** — pick it for new-graduate programs with large event and seminar volume.
- **[Greenhouse](/en/tools/greenhouse/)** — pick it for a foreign-capital company or a Japan entity of a global firm that needs one ATS, structured scorecards and an English UI across countries.
- **[Ashby](/en/tools/ashby/)** — the fastest-growing global ATS. Pick it when analytics and an all-in-one sourcing and scheduling suite matter more than Japanese job-board coverage.

The rule: if most of your candidates come from Japanese job boards and agencies and your interviewers are Japanese-speaking engineers, HERP Hire is the default. If any of your hiring sits outside Japan, start from Greenhouse or Ashby and test their Japanese job-board coverage first.

## Watch-outs

- **Language.** The site, docs and press material are Japanese-only, and we found no published English UI. Guard: during the trial, have every non-Japanese-speaking hiring manager complete one full scorecard; get the vendor's answer on English support in writing.
- **Job-board coverage varies by source.** Listings cite anywhere from about 20 to 30 integrated job boards. Guard: list every board and scout service you used in the last 12 months and confirm each one with HERP before signing.
- **The AI add-on changes the bill.** AI Recruiter has flat and usage-based plans, and screening volume swings by quarter. Guard: price both plans on last year's applicant count and write the usage cap into the order form. HERP says inputs are not used to train the AI provider's models; get that in the contract and check retention under Japan's APPI.
- **Scrum recruiting widens access to candidate data.** More employees see more candidate records. Guard: use per-candidate assignment so interviewers only see the people they meet, and review user access each quarter.
- **Agency fees are not the platform's fees.** The Agent Network makes posting free of effort, not free of cost. Guard: confirm each agency's fee rate and refund terms before accepting a referral from one you have not contracted with.
