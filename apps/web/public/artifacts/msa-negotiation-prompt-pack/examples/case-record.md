# JH-042 · Source record

Juniper Harbor, a fictional buyer, is evaluating a SaaS agreement from fictional vendor Alder Relay. The extracts and internal rules below were written for this exercise. Keep the playbook and decision log inside the buyer's team.

## Intake handoff

On October 5, 2026, procurement requester Maya Chen asked for a commercial review by October 9. The proposed start and first renewal dates were missing. The request went back to Maya. On October 6 she supplied the proposed dates: December 1, 2026 and December 1, 2027. Commercial counsel Priya Shah received the request for review. October 9 is the requested internal review date. No agreement has been signed.

## Source PB1 · Juniper Harbor's fictional playbook for this exercise

| Rule | Position and authority |
| --- | --- |
| REN-01 | Ask for written nonrenewal notice at least **30 calendar days** before renewal. Exactly **45 calendar days** may be offered only after Priya Shah records approval for JH-042. Hold any other proposed period for Priya. There is no authority to accept 60 or 90 days. |
| DELIVERY-01 | The template calls for notices by email to the notices address designated in the order form. Verify the full agreement, notices clause and order form before proposing an insertion. |
| PAY-01 | Ask for payment of undisputed service invoices within **30 calendar days of receipt**. The buyer may propose **20 calendar days**, conditional on retaining **30 days** for nonrenewal notice. This concession does not authorize combining 20-day payment with a different notice period. |

## Round 1 · Vendor V1 and buyer proposal B1

**V1, §4.1 · Payment.** “Buyer will pay undisputed service invoices within 15 calendar days of receipt.”

**V1, §7.1 · Renewal.** “The subscription renews for one year unless Buyer gives Vendor written notice of nonrenewal at least 90 calendar days before renewal.”

**Scope.** For this exercise the reviewed excerpts are §§4.1 and 7.1. V1 does not specify how written notice is delivered in those excerpts. No other agreement sections or order form are supplied.

**B1, §4.1.** “Buyer will pay undisputed service invoices within 30 calendar days of receipt.”

**B1, §7.1.** “The subscription renews for one year unless Buyer gives Vendor written notice of nonrenewal at least 30 calendar days before renewal.” Buyer also asks Alder Relay to confirm the notice method and its location in the full documents.

## Round 2 · Vendor reply V2

**V2, §4.1.** “Buyer will pay undisputed service invoices within 20 calendar days of receipt.”

**V2, §7.1.** “The subscription renews for one year unless Buyer gives Vendor written notice of nonrenewal at least 60 calendar days before renewal. Send notice by email to the notices address designated in the order form.”

**Vendor message V2.** “We can discuss a shorter notice period if payment is due within 20 calendar days.” This invites discussion. It does not set a new notice period or confirm an order-form email address.

## Decision at the end of the record

Priya Shah has not approved the 45-day exception. Maya Chen is responsible for obtaining the order form and confirming the notices address. The record contains no third-round vendor response, signed terms, final notice date or authorization to send a further draft.
