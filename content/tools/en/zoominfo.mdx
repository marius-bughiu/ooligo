## What it is

ZoomInfo is the legacy heavyweight of B2B contact and intent data. Phone numbers, emails, firmographics, technographics, and Bombora-style intent signals — all behind one of the largest known sales-intelligence contracts on the planet. If you have heard "the data is good but the rep is aggressive," they are talking about ZoomInfo.

It is the default for enterprise outbound, and increasingly the default everyone resents.

## Why it shows up in RevOps stacks

- **Coverage is real.** For NA mid-market and enterprise, contact coverage and accuracy is still hard to beat in raw numbers.
- **Intent + WebSights.** Intent topics and de-anonymized site visitors feed ABM playbooks and outbound triggers.
- **Bundled stack.** ZoomInfo SalesOS plus Chorus plus Engage offers a one-vendor GTM platform — appealing to procurement, less so to operators.

## Pricing

- **Custom only** — multi-year contracts the norm, six-figure commits common
- Pricing scales by seats, intent topics, credits, and modules
- Reputation for aggressive renewals and auto-renewal clauses; read your contract carefully

## Best for

- Enterprise outbound orgs with NA-heavy ICP and big budgets
- ABM teams needing intent + de-anonymized web traffic
- Companies that want one vendor for data, dialer, and conversation intelligence

## Watch-outs

- Pricing is opaque and contracts notoriously sticky
- For startups and SMB, Apollo or Clay routing usually wins on value
- International data quality (LATAM, APAC, parts of EMEA) lags the NA dataset
