## What it is

Boomi is an enterprise integration platform — iPaaS, API management, master data management and data integration sold together as the Boomi Enterprise Platform — that has spent 2026 bolting an agent control plane on top. Gartner placed it a Leader in the Magic Quadrant for iPaaS dated 16 March 2026, its 12th consecutive time, and positioned it highest for Ability to Execute. Boomi reports more than 30,000 customers and 800-plus partners. It has been owned by Francisco Partners and TPG since Dell sold it in 2021.

The architectural fact that still separates it from [Workato](/en/tools/workato/) and [Tray.ai](/en/tools/tray-ai/) is the runtime. Boomi processes run on Atoms — lightweight runtimes you can install inside your own network, cluster into Molecules, or leave in Boomi's cloud. An on-prem ERP, a database that never touches the internet, and SaaS apps can sit in one process without a VPN tunnel to someone else's cloud.

## What it shipped for agents

**Agentstudio** is the agent layer. Agent Designer, the no-code builder, is included in the Agentstudio Base edition that ships with every Enterprise Platform edition, so every customer already has it. At Boomi World in Chicago on 13 May 2026 the company centred the release on **Agent Control Tower**, which inventories and monitors agents across more than 30 agentic platforms — Boomi-built and third-party — and an **MCP Registry** inside the API control plane that catalogues MCP servers from Boomi and other registries and applies security policy before agents reach them. An integration with [Amazon Bedrock](/en/tools/amazon-bedrock/) feeds Bedrock agents into Control Tower.

Three acquisitions fill out the picture. Boomi announced an intent to acquire Lunar.dev, an AI and MCP gateway, on 13 May 2026 to sit inside Boomi Connect, its managed MCP offering; Techzine has since reported the deal closed. On 19 October 2026 it bought Elementary Data, an Israeli data-observability company whose open-source project reports more than 1.1 million monthly downloads, for a reported $43 million, to check data quality before agents act on it. The Q3 2026 roadmap adds agent runtimes in US East, UK, Japan and Australia for data residency, and ingests [Claude Code](/en/tools/claude-code/) token, cost and active-time telemetry into Control Tower.

## A use case worth buying it for

One customer record across [Salesforce](/en/tools/salesforce/), [NetSuite](/en/tools/netsuite/) and a support desk, where at least one system sits on-premises. Boomi DataHub, the MDM module, holds the golden record and its match rules; integration processes running on an Atom inside the firewall push changes out to each system; the API gateway exposes the cleaned record to internal apps. Workato and Tray.ai can move the data, but neither sells a master-data hub, so the deduplication logic ends up hand-built inside recipes. When an ops team then puts an agent in front of that record, Control Tower is the place its tool calls get logged.

## Pricing

Boomi publishes one real number. **Pay-as-you-go** costs **$99 a month in base fees plus $0.05 per Boomi Message**, with no contract, per its documentation updated 16 July 2026. Every committed edition — Professional, Professional Plus, Enterprise, Enterprise Plus, plus API Management and DataHub — is quote-only.

Vendr is the better anchor for committed deals: a **median of $45,147 a year** across 101 purchases, an observed range of about $11,700 to $184,389, and an average of 11% off first quote. Vendr's buyer guide puts the average near $130,000 across 72 deals with a maximum of $1.8 million, which tells you how far the large estates pull the tail. For scale against peers, Vendr's medians are about $64,500 for Workato and $37,600 for Tray.ai.

Committed editions are licensed by **connection**, counted separately for production and test. Boomi's edition documentation lists 5/5 standard connections on Professional, 7/7 on Professional Plus, 10/20 on Enterprise and 15/30 on Enterprise Plus. Enterprise connectors such as SAP and Oracle are included at 1/2 on Enterprise and 2/4 on Enterprise Plus, and cost extra on the Professional tiers.

## Best for

IT and integration leaders at 1,000-plus-person companies with a hybrid estate — an on-prem ERP or database next to SaaS systems of record — who want integration, API management and master data under one contract, and who need to govern agents from several vendors rather than build them all in one tool.

## Alternatives, and when to pick them instead

- **[Workato](/en/tools/workato/)** — pick it when the estate is all SaaS, business-side builders will own recipes, and you want the more developed agent-building layer rather than the governance layer.
- **MuleSoft** — Salesforce-owned, the other incumbent by share. Pick it when API-led architecture is the main requirement and Salesforce is your strategic platform.
- **[n8n](/en/tools/n8n/)** — the fastest-growing entrant. Pick it when you have engineers, can self-host, and want agent workflows without a five-figure floor.
- **[Tray.ai](/en/tools/tray-ai/)** — pick it when a smaller team wants a cheaper iPaaS with agent tooling and does not need on-prem runtimes or MDM.
- **[Microsoft Power Automate](/en/tools/microsoft-power-automate/)** — pick it when the integrations are mostly Microsoft 365 and Dynamics and the licences are already paid for.

## Watch-outs

- **Connections, not workflows, drive the bill — and test counts separately.** A Salesforce connector deployed against production and a sandbox uses a connection in each pool. Guard: before the first quote, list every endpoint times every environment, flag which need enterprise connectors, and get the definition of a billable connection plus the per-connection true-up price written into the order form.
- **The agent layer is young and partly acquired.** Control Tower, the MCP Registry, Lunar.dev and Elementary all arrived within six months, and the cross-platform monitoring claims are vendor-reported. Guard: pilot Control Tower against one third-party agent you already run, and confirm in writing which Agentstudio capabilities sit above the Base edition and what they cost.
- **Private-equity ownership and an 11% average discount leave little room at renewal.** Guard: negotiate a renewal price cap and a multi-year rate on connections at signature, before the platform is embedded in your processes.
- **The build canvas assumes a trained developer.** Boomi processes are low-code, not no-code, and ops managers rarely own them. Guard: budget for a certified Boomi developer or a partner from day one rather than discovering the need after go-live.
