## What it is

ZipRecruiter (NYSE: ZIP) is a US job-ad marketplace built for employers who want to post once and have the platform find applicants for them. You publish a job, ZipRecruiter distributes it across its own site, app and partner job boards, and its matching engine pushes the job to candidates it scores as a fit and invites them to apply. It is the distant number three behind [Indeed](/en/tools/indeed/) and [LinkedIn Recruiter](/en/tools/linkedin-recruiter/): Q2 2026 revenue was $118.1 million, up 5% year on year, from 70,721 quarterly paid employers paying an average of $1,669 per quarter.

The 2026 product story is AI matching. A rebuilt search and matching engine launched in Q1 2026 lifted application volume 37% for job seekers on it, and management reported a 34% quarter-on-quarter rise in qualified applications in Q2 once it rolled out fully. Employer-side, **Smart Outreach** automates multi-step messaging campaigns into a resume database of 50 million+ profiles, and **Be Seen First** lets high-intent candidates attach an audio note to stand out. Job-seeker-side, ZipRecruiter shipped a ChatGPT app in March 2026 and a Claude connector on 9 June 2026; both let seekers search and click through to apply, so your listings reach AI-assistant search with no extra spend. Neither is an employer tool.

## Why it shows up in recruiting stacks

Because owner-operators and one-person TA teams want predictable monthly spend and a candidate queue, not a bidding dashboard. ZipRecruiter's core product is a per-job-slot subscription: you pay to keep a role live, and the platform does distribution and matching. That is the opposite of Indeed's per-click auction, and it is why ZipRecruiter keeps landing on SMB shortlists next to it.

It also shows up through the ATS. ZipRecruiter lists 200+ ATS and HCM integrations, including [Greenhouse](/en/tools/greenhouse/), [BambooHR](/en/tools/bamboohr/), [JazzHR](/en/tools/jazzhr/) and [Workable](/en/tools/workable/), so many teams buy it as a posting destination from inside the system they already run.

## Pricing reality

ZipRecruiter publishes no price list; every employer gets a quote based on location, industry, role and volume. Third-party guides from mid-2026 put the plans at roughly **$299-$399 per job slot per month (Standard)**, **$419-$519 (Premium)** and **$719-$899 (Pro)**, with annual billing about 15-20% cheaper. A single always-on Standard slot therefore costs about $3,600-$4,800 a year. Resume database access is limited on Standard and priced separately; TrafficBoost for an underperforming job is a paid add-on. Enterprise accounts buy custom slot bundles and performance campaigns — a pay-per-click model that grew 15% year on year in Q2 2026, faster than subscriptions (2%).

The trial is short — commonly two to four days, card up front, auto-converting to paid. Treat it as a billing event, not a free evaluation.

## Best for

A hiring manager, office manager or solo recruiter at a US SMB filling one to ten hourly, frontline or general-professional roles at a time, who wants a fixed monthly cost and an inbox of matched applicants without running campaigns. That reader should buy one slot per live role, tie every applicant source back to hires in the ATS, and kill slots that do not produce.

Skip it for senior, specialist or engineering roles, and skip it outside the US — the marketplace is US-centric and the matching data is thin elsewhere.

## Versus the alternatives

**[Indeed](/en/tools/indeed/)** is the market-share leader. Pick it when you need the biggest active-seeker audience and can manage a per-click budget against cost per hire; pick ZipRecruiter when you value a fixed per-slot bill over reach.

**[LinkedIn Recruiter](/en/tools/linkedin-recruiter/)** is the other leader. Pick it for passive professional candidates; ZipRecruiter rarely reaches them.

**[Appcast](/en/tools/appcast/)** and **[Joveo](/en/tools/joveo/)** are programmatic buyers that place your jobs on ZipRecruiter, Indeed and thousands of other sites by cost per applicant. Once you run roughly 30+ concurrent roles, buy media through one of them instead of stacking per-slot subscriptions. **[Talroo](/en/tools/talroo/)** sells per-application pricing for frontline hiring. The fastest-growing channel is AI-assistant job search: ChatGPT and Claude now surface ZipRecruiter and Indeed listings directly.

If applicants arrive but do not convert, the problem is after the apply — see [Workstream](/en/tools/workstream/), [Paradox](/en/tools/paradox/) and the [high-volume recruiting stack](/en/stacks/high-volume-recruiting-stack/).

## Watch-outs

- **Per-slot billing charges for idle roles.** A filled or paused job still costs a full month if the slot stays open. *Guard:* keep a slot register next to your requisitions and close or reassign slots the day a role fills; review it before each billing date.
- **Opaque, rep-negotiated pricing.** Two employers in the same city can pay different rates for the same plan. *Guard:* get the per-slot price, billing unit, renewal date and auto-renew terms in writing, and benchmark against the third-party bands above before signing annual.
- **Matched applicants are not qualified applicants.** "Invite to apply" volume can flood a queue with low-fit candidates. *Guard:* add two knockout screening questions per job and track [cost per hire](/en/learn/cost-per-hire/) by source monthly, not applicant count.
- **The trial auto-converts.** *Guard:* set a calendar cancel reminder on day one, and post your hardest-to-fill role during the trial so you see the real match quality.
