## What it is

Dice is the tech-only job board and resume database run by DHI Group (NYSE: DHX). Employers buy two things from it: job slots that put IT and engineering roles in front of a technologist audience, and TalentSearch, a searchable candidate database that also pulls in open-web profiles and passive candidates. It is a posting-and-search product with AI layered on, not an AI sourcing agent.

The AI that ships today is incremental and recruiter-facing: an AI job description generator and an AI Boolean enhancer (Q3 2025, part of the New Employer Experience), knockout screener questions (Q1 2026), and an AI Applicant Score with filters for work authorization, location and fit status (Q2 2026). Dice also launched an MCP server in June 2026, but it lets job seekers search Dice listings from ChatGPT or Claude. There is no recruiter-side MCP access to TalentSearch.

## Why it shows up in Recruiting stacks

- **The audience is pre-filtered to tech.** Every applicant arrives from a board that lists nothing but tech roles. For contract IT, infrastructure and enterprise-software roles, it is the channel recruiters most often weigh against LinkedIn, as an August 2026 r/recruiting thread on exactly that choice shows.
- **Search is bundled with posting at a fixed price.** Starter and Standard include a monthly allowance of TalentSearch profile views alongside the job slots, so one subscription covers both inbound and outbound for a small team.
- **ATS posting is native.** Easy Post and apply integrations exist for Greenhouse, Bullhorn, iCIMS and SmartRecruiters, so jobs flow out of the ATS and applicants flow back in without copy-paste.

## Pricing

Verified against [dice.com/hiring/pricing](https://www.dice.com/hiring/pricing) on 2026-10-09. Prices are for new customers only.

- **Single Job Slot — $399** for one job live for 30 days. No search access.
- **Starter — $549/month billed annually**, or $649/month month-to-month. 3 job slots, 1 user, 250 candidate views a month.
- **Standard — $749/month billed annually** on an annual contract. 5 job slots, 2 users, 500 shared candidate views a month, branded jobs, a branded company profile and a dedicated account manager.
- **Dice for Scaling Teams — custom.**

Dice also lists 12-month packages on AWS Marketplace: Bronze $6,995 (unlimited tech job posting), Silver $14,995 (adds ATS integrations and automatic job retrieval), Gold $34,995 and Platinum $79,995.

Real-world band: DHI's Q2 2026 results put average annual revenue per Dice Recruitment Package customer at $15,899, which is about 1.8× the Standard list price of $8,988/year. The typical paying account buys more slots or views than the published tiers include. Budget $6,600 to $9,000 a year for one recruiter on Starter or Standard, and expect a quote in the $15K range once a team needs more than two users.

## Best for

- Staffing-agency and in-house recruiters filling contract and full-time IT roles (systems, DevOps, enterprise software, data) who need a second sourcing channel next to LinkedIn
- Small tech-hiring teams that want a fixed monthly cost for postings and search instead of a pay-per-click budget

Not the pick for non-technical roles, for teams that need AI to source and outreach on its own, or for employers who cannot sponsor visas and lack the time to filter for work authorization.

## Versus the alternatives

- **[LinkedIn Recruiter](/en/tools/linkedin-recruiter/)** is the default for tech sourcing. Its UK G-Cloud rate card lists £8,925 per seat per year at 1-2 licences. Pick LinkedIn Recruiter when you need full-time senior engineers and InMail reach matters. Pick Dice when the roles are contract or mid-level IT, and the budget is closer to $7K than $12K a seat.
- **[Indeed](/en/tools/indeed/)** wins on volume and pay-per-click flexibility, with resume search at $520/month for 100 contacts. Pick Indeed for mixed or non-tech hiring. Pick Dice when tech-only targeting matters more than total applicant count.
- **[ZipRecruiter](/en/tools/ziprecruiter/)** sells quoted job slots, around $299-$399 per slot per month on Standard. Pick it for broad distribution across many role types.
- **[Juicebox](/en/tools/juicebox/)** is the fastest-growing entrant in AI sourcing at $99 per seat per month billed annually. Pick it when you want an agent to find and email passive candidates across the open web. Pick Dice when you want inbound applicants and a database of people who have opted in to tech job search.

## Watch-outs

- **The customer base is shrinking.** Dice ended Q2 2026 with 3,702 Recruitment Package customers, down 15% year over year, and revenue fell 14% to $15.8M. Guard: sign annual terms rather than multi-year, and before renewal, pull your own cost-per-hire from Dice against every other channel.
- **Candidate quality complaints are about third-party and fake profiles.** In an August 2026 r/recruiting thread comparing Dice with LinkedIn Recruiter, recruiters said the pool skews toward corp-to-corp (C2C) contractors and said they had seen fabricated applicants. Guard: turn on the Q1 2026 knockout screeners and the work-authorization filter on every posting, and require a live video screen before any submittal.
- **Candidate views are a hard monthly cap.** 250 views on Starter runs out within a week of heavy sourcing. Guard: track views used per req during the first month, and move to Standard's 500 shared views only if the cap is actually binding.
- **No recruiter-side MCP or published sourcing API.** Guard: if your agents need to read Dice data, rely on ATS sync through the native integrations and treat anything beyond that as unsupported.
