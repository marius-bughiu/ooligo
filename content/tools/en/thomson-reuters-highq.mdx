## What it is

Thomson Reuters HighQ is the collaboration and workflow platform law firms use to put matters in front of clients: client portals and extranets, deal rooms, structured trackers, and the approval and intake workflows that sit behind them. It was a London start-up until Thomson Reuters bought it in July 2019, when it served more than 400 customers including over half of the Global 100 law firms. The core building blocks are sites, files, tasks, and iSheets, which are configurable grids that act as matter trackers, CP lists, or intake registers. Contract Express document automation ships inside it as HighQ DocAuto.

The live question is whether HighQ still earns its place now that firms are buying [Harvey](/en/tools/harvey/), [Legora](/en/tools/legora/), or [CoCounsel](/en/tools/thomson-reuters-cocounsel/) as an AI front end. Our answer: yes, if clients log into it. HighQ is where the client-facing record of a matter lives, and the AI tools are now reading from it rather than replacing it.

## What changed in 2026

- **A quarterly release cadence.** Thomson Reuters moved HighQ to four feature releases a year, v5.10 in March through v5.13 with production upgrades from 28 November to 13 December, with maintenance releases still every two weeks.
- **An MCP server, live since v5.10.** HighQ MCP gives AI assistants read-only, real-time access to sites, documents, and iSheets under each user's own permissions, authenticated by per-user OAuth and logged in HighQ's audit trail. Thomson Reuters lists full support for Claude Desktop, Claude Code, ChatGPT Enterprise, Cursor, and VS Code Copilot. It is included for HighQ Premium clients at no extra charge, but only on Azure-migrated instances.
- **CoCounsel inside the platform.** Self-Service Q&A powered by CoCounsel answers client questions from approved folders, and the document review skill moved from GPT-4o to GPT-4.1 in the Q3 release. Due diligence guided workflows run intake, risk flags, and reports against a data room.
- **A seat in Microsoft's legal plugin registry.** When [Microsoft Copilot for Legal](/en/tools/microsoft-copilot-for-legal/) launched on 15 October 2026, HighQ was a partner plugin, and the only route by which Thomson Reuters appeared at all; Westlaw and CoCounsel were absent.

## Pricing

Quote-only. The one published unit price we found is a UK G-Cloud 13 listing at £85 per user per month, a public-sector framework rate that should be read as a ceiling reference, not a law-firm quote. Capterra's listing notes that the extranet option grants five external users for every internal licence, which is the ratio that decides the cost of a client portal. Papermark, a competitor, estimates $20,000-50,000+ a year for mid-sized firms and $100,000-500,000+ for large ones; treat that as a rough band from a rival, not a price list. Premium add-ons, such as the Power BI and Azure Maps dashboard panels, sit on top. Budget implementation in weeks to months, not days.

## Best for

Knowledge, innovation, and client-service leads at mid-size and large law firms who run repeat client programmes, such as panel reporting, matter dashboards, and transaction checklists, and need clients to see live status without email. It is also a fit for in-house teams running legal intake and self-service portals for the business.

Not for: firms that only need a one-off virtual data room, or practices under about 20 lawyers, where a Firmex or [Datasite](/en/tools/datasite/) room per deal, or a SharePoint site, costs less than HighQ's implementation effort alone.

## Alternatives

- **[iManage](/en/tools/imanage/) and [NetDocuments](/en/tools/netdocuments/), the two largest law-firm DMS vendors.** Pick them when the need is sharing documents securely from the system of record and clients do not need trackers or workflows. HighQ wins once the portal carries structured data and approvals.
- **[Harvey](/en/tools/harvey/), the fastest-growing entrant.** Shared Spaces, launched in December 2025, give clients guest accounts into shared Vaults, review tables, and workflows; Harvey reported in June 2026 that more than 90% of its legal teams had created one. Pick Harvey when the shared object is AI work product. Legora's Portal is the parallel option.
- **[Intapp](/en/tools/intapp/) Collaboration** offers client-branded external workspaces on SharePoint. Pick it when Intapp already runs your intake, conflicts, and walls. Infodash is the lighter SharePoint-native extranet.
- **[Microsoft SharePoint](/en/tools/microsoft-sharepoint/)** when volume is low and a site per client is enough.

## Watch-outs

- **The MCP server does not reach Copilot inside Word or Outlook.** Thomson Reuters lists no MCP support for Copilot in Microsoft 365 apps, and only limited support in Copilot Studio, with app-level authentication that drops per-user permissions. Guard: before relying on the Copilot for Legal plugin, test it with two users who have different site access and confirm each sees only their own matters. Keep Copilot Studio agents off client sites.
- **Older tenants are locked out of the AI features.** MCP needs Premium and an Azure-migrated instance. Guard: get your tenant's hosting status and migration date in writing before you price the AI layer into a renewal.
- **The interface is dense and takes training.** Capterra reviewers rate it about 4.4/5 but repeatedly call the UI dated and the learning curve steep. Guard: pilot the new collapsible sidebar navigation and one rebuilt dashboard with three real clients before rolling out firm-wide, and assign a site owner for each client programme.
- **Fees grow with every add-on.** Dashboard panels, DocAuto, and CoCounsel features are priced separately from the core licence. Guard: list every module in the order form with its unit price, and cap annual uplift on multi-year terms.
