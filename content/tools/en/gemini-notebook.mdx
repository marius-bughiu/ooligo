## What it is

Gemini Notebook is Google's source-grounded research notebook: you load a fixed set of documents, and every answer, summary, audio overview, slide deck or report is drawn from those sources with citations back to the passage. Google renamed it from NotebookLM on 2026-07-16 and kept it a standalone product. At the rename Google reported 30M+ users and 600,000+ organizations on it.

For an ops team there are two different products behind the one name:

- **Gemini Notebook in Google Workspace.** A core service on Business Standard, Business Plus, Enterprise Standard and Enterprise Plus, so most Workspace customers already pay for it. Admins can turn it on or off for users from the Admin console.
- **Gemini Notebook Enterprise** (Google's docs also call it "Gemini Notebook for business" and NotebookLM Enterprise). A Google Cloud product, bought as a standalone license or included in Gemini Enterprise Standard and Plus. Sources are stored in your own Cloud project in the US or EU multi-region, under VPC Service Controls and customer-managed encryption keys, and Google states the data is not used to train models.

## Why it shows up in ops stacks

- **A bounded answer surface.** Legal ops loads the playbook, the fallback-clause library and three precedent agreements, and reviewers ask questions that can only be answered from those. That bound is the point: a notebook won't wander into the open web or into a stale Drive folder nobody curated.
- **Onboarding packs in one place.** RevOps builds one notebook per product line from the pricing sheet, the battlecards and the discovery guide. New AEs get the audio overview on day one and ask follow-ups with cited answers instead of messaging the enablement lead.
- **It runs inside Workspace automation.** Since 2026-05-12, [Google Workspace Studio](/en/tools/google-workspace-studio/) has an "Ask NotebookLM" step, so a flow can answer an inbound question from a curated notebook and post the cited reply to Chat or Gmail.
- **A wide source list.** Google Docs and Slides, PDF, DOCX, PPTX, XLSX, public URLs, YouTube, audio, images and pasted text, uploaded directly or from [Google Drive](/en/tools/google-drive/).

## Pricing

- **Workspace editions** — included. Business Standard is $14/user/mo and Business Plus $22 on annual terms; Google's core-service list starts at Business Standard, so confirm coverage before counting on it for Starter seats. The AI Expanded Access add-on raises the notebook limits; Google publishes no list price for it.
- **Gemini Notebook Enterprise** — $9 per license per month on Google Cloud's product page, with a discount for annual commitment and a free trial.
- **Gemini Enterprise Standard and Plus** — Gemini Notebook Enterprise is included; the seat price is a quote (see [Gemini Enterprise](/en/tools/gemini/)).

Real-world band: $0 incremental for a team already on Workspace Business Standard or above, $108 per user per year at list for the enterprise SKU, before any annual discount.

## Best for

- Legal ops, enablement and CS leads who need answers grounded in a curated, fixed set of documents, with citations reviewers can check
- Workspace shops that want team research notebooks without a new vendor or a new procurement cycle
- Regulated teams that need the enterprise SKU's Cloud-project storage, US or EU residency and CMEK for the source files, at $9 a license rather than a full enterprise-search seat

## Alternatives — and when to pick them instead

- **[Microsoft 365 Copilot](/en/tools/microsoft-365-copilot/)** — the Microsoft pole on any shortlist. Pick it when the source documents live in SharePoint and OneDrive; at $30/user/mo it answers across the tenant, and Gemini Notebook has no SharePoint connector.
- **[ChatGPT](/en/tools/chatgpt/) Business** — the market-share leader in general assistants, $20/seat/mo on annual terms. Pick it when the team wants one general assistant with Projects and connectors, and grounded notebooks are an occasional need.
- **[Claude](/en/tools/claude/) Projects** — the fastest-growing challenger for document-heavy work. Pick it when the job is drafting and long-document analysis against the sources rather than audio overviews and study aids.
- **[Glean](/en/tools/glean/)** — pick it when the question spans live systems (Salesforce, Jira, Slack) rather than a curated pack. It costs $40-50/user/mo with a 100-seat minimum, roughly five times the enterprise notebook license.

## Watch-outs

- **The Workspace version and the enterprise SKU are not the same product.** The enterprise SKU keeps sources in your Cloud project with US or EU residency; the Workspace version follows Workspace's own storage terms. Guard: if a data-residency or DPA clause applies to the source documents, put those notebooks on Gemini Notebook Enterprise and keep the Workspace version for non-restricted material.
- **Cloud project roles open every notebook.** On the enterprise SKU, anyone holding project-level Viewer, Editor or Owner can open every notebook in the project. Guard: run notebooks in a dedicated Cloud project, grant users only the Gemini Notebook user role, and audit project IAM before the first sensitive upload.
- **Sources are static copies.** The enterprise SKU stores each upload as a copy, so an updated playbook in Drive does not reach the notebook on its own. Guard: give every notebook a named owner and a monthly re-upload date, and put the source date in the notebook title.
- **Limits are per user per day.** The enterprise SKU caps each user at 500 queries, 20 audio overviews, 15 slide decks and 15 infographics a day, with 300 sources per notebook and 500,000 words per source. Guard: split a large contract archive by counterparty or year before upload, and route high-volume Q&A through Workspace Studio or a search tool instead of one shared notebook.
- **No live connectors to third-party systems.** Notebooks hold only what you upload; SharePoint, Jira and ServiceNow connectors belong to Gemini Enterprise search, not to notebooks. Guard: if the answer has to reflect today's CRM or ticket data, use Gemini Enterprise or Glean and keep notebooks for reference packs.
