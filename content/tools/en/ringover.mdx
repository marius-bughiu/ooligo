## What it is

Ringover is a Paris-founded cloud phone system for sales and recruiting teams: business numbers in dozens of countries, IVR and routing, a power dialer, call recording, and 100+ native CRM and ATS integrations, with HubSpot, Salesforce and Bullhorn the ones its buyers actually come for. It raised a €20M Series B in 2023 led by Orange Ventures and Bpifrance, and Le Journal des Entreprises put it at roughly 350 employees and €36M in 2024 revenue when it bought the French conversational-AI company Target First in 2025.

The phone line is the commodity. What makes Ringover worth a page in 2026 is the AI it has stacked on top, now sold as three products on one pricing page. **`Empower`** is the conversation-intelligence layer: transcripts, summaries, call scoring, topic and signal detection, live AI coaching, Pitch Room call simulations with AI personas (launched October 2025), and `Ask Empower`, an assistant that since version 2.0 (March 2026) queries all past calls and meetings rather than one at a time. `Empower` also records Zoom, Google Meet, Teams and Webex, not only Ringover calls. **AIRO** is a voice agent that answers, qualifies, books and transfers inbound calls in 11 languages. **Meet.us** is its own video-meeting product.

That bundle is the question this page answers. An r/RevOps thread in August 2026 put it plainly: the stack is Ringover plus HubSpot, so do we still need Gong or Fireflies on top?

## Pricing reality

Ringover publishes prices. Checked on 10 October 2026, per user per month, excluding tax, with a 3-user minimum on every self-serve plan:

| Plan | Annual (12-month commitment) | Monthly | What it adds |
|---|---|---|---|
| Talk | $19 | $29 | Web-only purchase. Calls, recordings, transcription and summaries, IVR. No power dialer; the 100+ integrations start on Business. |
| Business | $49 | $69 | Power dialer, monitoring and whisper, advanced IVR, the 100+ integrations, CRM Autofill, Ask AI search over calls. |
| Unified | $69 | $89 | WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram in one inbox, shared email, Meet.us Business. |
| Enterprise | Quote | Quote | 50-user minimum. Multi-site teams, local presence, bring-your-own-carrier. |

`Empower` is priced separately: **Business $15** and **Max $39** per user per month annual ($19 and $49 monthly). Max is the tier with call scoring, live coaching, simulations and AI workflows. AIRO is a flat platform fee, not per seat: $100/month annual for 20 inbound calls a day, $500 for 100 a day, then $0.39 per extra incoming call and $0.19 per minute on answered outbound calls.

Add-ons are where the invoice grows. The add-ons page lists **CRM Autofill at $5 per license, billed on every license** (despite appearing on the Business card), a **Parallel Dialer at $49 per user** (UK, US and Spain only), Campaigns at $10, and Workforce Management at $6. Add-on commitments run to the end of your contract.

Worked for the thread's case, a 20-rep team on HubSpot, annual billing: Business seats are $11,760 a year, CRM Autofill $1,200, `Empower` Max $9,360. That is **about $22,300 a year, or $93 per rep per month**, for phone, dialer and conversation intelligence on one contract. `Empower` Max also lists CRM Autofill, so ask whether the $5 add-on is still required once Max is on the contract; dropping it saves $1,200. Gong for the same 20 seats triangulates at $1,400-2,000 per seat per year plus a $5,000-50,000 platform fee, so roughly **$33,000-90,000** for intelligence alone, before you pay for a phone system. Keeping Ringover Business and adding [Fireflies](/en/tools/fireflies/) Business at $19 a seat instead of `Empower` costs about $17,500, before the metered AI credits Fireflies charges for its CRM Autofill and agent features.

## Best for

A **RevOps or sales leader, or a staffing-agency ops lead, running 10-80 reps or recruiters whose work happens mostly on the phone**, on HubSpot, Salesforce or Bullhorn, who wants dialing, recording, CRM logging and coaching on one vendor. The scoped case where it wins: an SDR or recruiting desk making high-volume calls, where `Empower` Max scores every call already sitting in Ringover instead of shipping audio to a second vendor.

## Not for

Teams whose deals are won in multi-stakeholder video meetings and inspected in a weekly forecast call. `Empower` can record those meetings, but deal boards, pipeline inspection and forecasting at Gong or [Clari](/en/tools/clari/) depth are not what it sells. It is also a poor fit for one or two-person teams: the 3-user minimum means you pay for seats you don't use.

## Versus the alternatives

- **[Aircall](/en/tools/aircall/)** — the closest like-for-like, another Paris-founded cloud phone with more than $200M ARR. Its Professional plan is $50 a seat annual with conversation intelligence, but live coaching is AI Assist Pro at $49 a seat. Pick Aircall when you need its 250+ integration marketplace or US-centric support. Pick Ringover when you want scoring, coaching and simulations from the same vendor at $39 instead of $49.
- **RingCentral** — with Dialpad, the share leader in business cloud phone. Pick it when the phone system has to serve the whole company with video and UCaaS, not just customer-facing teams.
- **[Gong](/en/tools/gong/)** — pick it on top of Ringover only when you have 40+ reps, a funded coaching program with an owner, and forecast inspection is the deliverable. Below that, `Empower` Max covers the call-level value for a fraction of the cost.
- **[Fireflies](/en/tools/fireflies/)** — pick it over `Empower` when most conversations are video meetings and you want notes, CRM Autofill and deal flags at $19 a seat plus metered AI credits, with Ringover handling only the phone.
- **[Nooks](/en/tools/nooks/)** — the AI-native dialer challenger ($70M raised, 1,500+ customers). Pick it over Ringover's dialer when connect rate on cold outbound is the metric that matters and parallel dialing plus a virtual salesfloor is worth a higher seat price.

If none of these fit, you probably don't need conversation intelligence yet: use the transcripts and summaries included in Ringover Business and run the analysis in Claude.

## The MCP server

Ringover runs an MCP endpoint, documented on developer.ringover.com, that its August 2026 guide says exposes **36 read-only tools** covering contacts, calls, transcriptions, users, groups, numbers and configuration, and names Claude, ChatGPT and Cursor as clients. It authenticates with the public API key, and the key's scopes decide what the assistant sees; transcripts are reachable only if transcription is enabled for that team. Nothing writes, so Claude can find the calls where pricing came up last month, but it cannot change a contact or place a call.

## Watch-outs

- **Add-ons lock in until contract end.** Ringover's own add-ons page says an add-on's commitment extends to the end of the contract, and CRM Autofill bills on every license, not only the reps who use it. **Guard:** list every add-on on the order form with its billing scope, and buy `Empower` or Parallel Dialer for a pilot group at renewal, not mid-term.
- **Transcription accuracy varies by language and accent.** G2 reviewers report transcripts that need manual correction, and every `Empower` score is built on them. **Guard:** before buying Max, run two weeks of real calls in each language you sell in, add your product names and acronyms to the custom vocabulary, and spot-check 20 transcripts against the audio.
- **Bullhorn needs Business or above and a Bullhorn admin.** Activation goes through a Bullhorn support case for credentials, which can take days. **Guard:** open the Bullhorn case the day you sign and keep recruiters on their current dialer until the first logged call shows up on the candidate record.
- **The MCP key sees everything its scopes allow.** One API key with call and transcript scopes gives an assistant the whole team's conversations. **Guard:** issue a dedicated read-only key per use case, start with contacts and calls only, and add transcription scope after security reviews the first workflow.
