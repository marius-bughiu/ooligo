A renewal review is easier to act on when the CSM can point to the observation behind each task. This worksheet starts with a local account brief, identifies what needs checking, and assigns each action to a person with a date. The CSM reviews the draft before adding tasks to an account system.

## Work the Lantern Works example

[Download the bundle](/artifacts/cs-renewal-playbook-skill.zip) and open the [fictional account brief](/artifacts/cs-renewal-playbook-skill/examples/account.md). The sample files are in English. On October 5 there are 60 days until the December 4 renewal. Weekly active seats fell from 12 of 20 to 8 of the same 20, using the same seven-day definition. Adoption went from 60% to 40%, a decline of **20 percentage points**. The cause is unknown. The previous sponsor changed roles, and the successor is unconfirmed.

Apply the [example review rubric](/artifacts/cs-renewal-playbook-skill/references/1-segment-playbook-config.md) before opening the [expected plan](/artifacts/cs-renewal-playbook-skill/examples/expected-plan.md). Those two observations produce the label `Owner review`. The rubric chooses who should look at an account next. There is no renewal-outcome dataset behind it from which to estimate a probability.

| Owner | Due | What closes the task |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Adoption lead | October 12 | Reconcile the usage counts and document the cause, or record what is still unknown |
| CSM | October 12 | Confirm the decision owner's role and the confirmation date |
| CS lead | October 19 | If the sponsor is still unknown, agree on an owner and next review date |

The dates come from the team schedule in the brief. If the seat denominator changes or a week is missing, the rubric calls for checking the inputs before drawing a trend.

## Use it on your own account

Prepare a dated extract of approved account records. Identify the contract source, renewal date, usage definition and most recent stakeholder evidence. Keep quotations separate from interpretation. A CSM can fill in the [output template](/artifacts/cs-renewal-playbook-skill/references/3-output-format.md) manually or use [Claude](/en/tools/claude/) to draft from approved notes.

If the data lives in [Gainsight](/en/tools/gainsight/) or [Gong](/en/tools/gong/), your team supplies the export or implements a connector with appropriate access. The download contains the worksheet, example and instructions. A CSM checks terms, recipients and owners, then creates any approved tasks or outreach in the team's system.

## Evaluate a trial

Compare the fictional input and expected plan first. On a real account, track preparation and review time, corrected observations, and actions the CSM approves. This exercise checks the arithmetic and decision path. No live account run or measured renewal lift underlies the example.
