## What it is

CrewAI is two products under one name. The first is an open-source Python framework, MIT-licensed, with about 61,000 GitHub stars. You define agents with a role, a goal and tools, group them into a **Crew** that splits a task between them, and wrap crews in **Flows**: event-driven workflows with state, branching and routing (`@start`, `@listen`, `@router`) for the steps that must happen the same way every time. Agents can consume MCP servers directly over stdio, SSE or streamable HTTP, though only MCP tools are supported, not prompts or resources.

The second is **CrewAI AMP**, the commercial platform (launched as "AOP" in late 2025 and since renamed). It adds Studio, a visual editor with an AI copilot that generates crews from a description, plus hosted deployment, tracing with OpenTelemetry export, guardrails, human-in-the-loop steps, cron scheduling, and triggers from Gmail, Outlook, Slack, Teams, Salesforce, HubSpot, Drive and OneDrive. Every deployed crew gets its own URL with `POST /kickoff` and `GET /status` endpoints, so a CRM workflow or an [n8n](/en/tools/n8n/) flow can call it. A crew can also be exported as an MCP server or as a UI component.

The company raised $18M in October 2024 across a seed round led by boldstart ventures and a Series A led by Insight Partners. It ships constantly: the framework reached v1.15.44 on November 19, 2026, about forty point releases since July. Recent ones added a `crewai eval` command, conversational agent sessions, versioned file artifacts and an Oracle Integrations connector. The site claims use by "65% of the Fortune 500" and shows DocuSign, PepsiCo and Experian logos; those figures are vendor-reported and we could not verify them.

## Why it shows up in ops stacks

- **Role-based decomposition matches how ops work is split.** An account-research crew with a researcher, an ICP scorer and a writer is easy to explain to a RevOps leader, and the case studies on the site are this shape: Gelato enriching 3,000+ leads a month, DocuSign cutting time to first contact with leads by 75%.
- **Flows give you a deterministic spine.** For a contract-intake or candidate-screening process, the routing and approvals live in a Flow and only the judgment steps (clause extraction, résumé scoring) go to a crew. That split is what makes CrewAI usable for work an auditor might later ask about.
- **Deployment options are wide.** Enterprise runs on CrewAI's cloud, your own VPC or your own infrastructure, which matters for Legal Ops teams that cannot send documents to another vendor's cloud.

## Pricing reality

From crewai.com/pricing, checked 2026-11-23. There are two plans.

- **Basic, free.** Studio with the AI copilot, GitHub integration, 50 workflow executions a month, tracing, guardrails, human-in-the-loop, MCP-server export and cron scheduling.
- **Enterprise, custom.** Higher execution limits with paid overage, enterprise connectors, a dedicated VPC, SSO through Microsoft Entra or Okta, role-based access control, PII redaction, a 45-day onboarding and forward-deployed engineers sold à la carte. No price is published.

A $25/month Professional tier that older reviews still quote is gone, so there is no paid self-serve step between 50 free runs and a sales contract. The open-source framework costs nothing to self-host. You pay for compute and model tokens, and multi-agent crews use far more tokens than a single prompt, because each agent reasons, calls tools and hands its output to the next. The realistic paths are: run the open-source framework on your own infrastructure with [LangSmith](/en/tools/langsmith/) or another tracer (platform cost $0, plus $39 per seat per month if you buy LangSmith Plus), or budget for an annual Enterprise contract once a crew is in production. We found no reliable current price data for Enterprise, so treat it as a negotiation.

## Best for

- **Best for:** a RevOps or Legal Ops engineer who writes Python and needs to put two or three multi-step agent processes into production (account research before outbound, first-pass contract review, candidate shortlisting) and wants the option to move them onto a governed, hosted platform later without a rewrite.
- **Not for** an ops team with no Python skills. Studio is real, but debugging a crew that misbehaves still means reading traces and code. A no-code builder fits better.

## Versus the alternatives

- **LangGraph with [LangSmith](/en/tools/langsmith/):** the share leader among code-first agent frameworks. Pick it when you want explicit graph control over every state transition and the most mature tracing and evaluation. Pick CrewAI when role-based crews describe the job and you want to ship faster with less orchestration code.
- **[Microsoft Copilot Studio](/en/tools/microsoft-copilot-studio/):** the share leader for business-user agents, at $200 per 25,000-credit pack. Pick it when the company lives in Microsoft 365 and IT wants agents inside that tenant's governance, with no Python involved.
- **[Sim](/en/tools/sim/):** the fastest-growing open-source agent canvas, Pro at $25 per user per month. Pick it when the builders want a visual-first tool with published self-serve pricing.
- **[n8n](/en/tools/n8n/) or [Gumloop](/en/tools/gumloop/):** pick these when the work is moving records between SaaS apps with one AI step in the middle. Plenty of teams call a CrewAI crew from n8n through the kickoff API.
- **[Dify](/en/tools/dify/):** pick it when the job is a retrieval assistant grounded in your own documents rather than a team of agents.
- **[Relevance AI](/en/tools/relevance-ai/) or [Lyzr](/en/tools/lyzr/):** pick these when you want pre-built sales or enterprise agents instead of building your own.

## Watch-outs

- **The framework changes under you.** v1.15.29 on October 23, 2026 removed `CrewAgentExecutor` and changed how every agent runs: oversized tool results now go to temp files, long conversations get summarised, and the fifth identical tool call ends the task. **Guard:** pin an exact version in production, and run `crewai eval` against a fixed set of saved inputs before every upgrade.
- **Nothing sits between free and Enterprise.** Basic stops at 50 executions a month, which a daily lead-research crew across a few reps uses up in days. **Guard:** prove the use case on the open-source framework first, measure executions per month, and get the execution cap and overage rate in writing before you sign.
- **Autonomous crews are slow, costly and non-deterministic.** **Guard:** keep routing, approvals and CRM writes in a Flow, use crews only for steps that need judgment, and put a human-in-the-loop step before anything a customer or counterparty will see.
- **The headline customer numbers are vendor-reported.** **Guard:** ask for two references in your own function (RevOps, Legal Ops or Recruiting) running a crew in production, not a pilot.
