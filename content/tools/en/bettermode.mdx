## What it is

Bettermode is a hosted B2B customer community platform: discussion spaces, a knowledge base, a wishlist and roadmap for ideation, events, direct messages, and a badge-and-leaderboard reputation system, all assembled in a visual layout builder the vendor calls Design Studio. Everything behind it is reachable through a GraphQL API with webhooks and a React SDK. The category leader it most resembles is Higher Logic Vanilla — a branded customer community that sits next to the help center rather than inside it — with one structural difference that decides most evaluations: Bettermode publishes its prices, and Higher Logic Vanilla, Khoros, and Gainsight Customer Communities do not.

## Why it shows up in Customer Success stacks

- **It is the only self-serve entry in a quote-only segment.** A CS leader building a deflection business case can price Bettermode in an afternoon — $4,788 or $18,000 a year — while the three enterprise incumbents require a discovery call before a number exists. That difference is worth more than any feature on the page when the budget cycle is short.
- **The meter is community members, not CS seats.** Plans cap members (10,000 / 25,000 / 100,000+) and collaborators — admins and moderators — separately at 5, 10, and 20+. Your CSMs do not each need a paid seat to answer in the community; your moderator bench is what the tier governs.
- **MCP ships on every plan, including the entry tier.** The connector went live on 27 August 2026 and exposes 11 read tools across spaces, posts, members, badges, post types, and analytics, so Claude or ChatGPT can generate a weekly digest, list members who have gone quiet, or flag suspected spam against live community data. Writes are a short list — publish a post, send a direct message, assign a badge, add an existing member to a space, hide a post — and each one asks for approval individually. It is admin-only and cannot delete spaces, members, or the community.
- **Federated search reaches outside the community.** On Growth and above, search spans [Zendesk](/en/tools/zendesk/) help-center content and Salesforce, so a member asking a question hits your documentation and your community in one query instead of choosing a silo first.

## Pricing reality

Bettermode repriced in February 2026 into three plans, and the entry point moved a long way up. Anything on a review aggregator quoting $49 or $199 a month is the retired plan set.

- **Starter** — $399/month billed annually ($4,788/year), $499 month-to-month. Up to 10,000 members, 5 collaborators. No Ask AI, no federated search, and MCP is capped at 10 requests per day per connector.
- **Growth** — $1,500/month billed annually ($18,000/year), $1,750 month-to-month. Up to 25,000 members, 10 collaborators. Adds Ask AI, federated search, and unlimited MCP within the standard API rate limits.
- **Premium** — quote only. 100,000+ members, 20+ collaborators.

A 14-day trial runs without a credit card. The free plan is gone: free communities were removed on 15 March 2026.

The number to budget against is **$18,000/year, not $4,788**. Starter's 5-collaborator ceiling is the binding constraint for a real CS org — two community managers, a docs owner, a support lead, and one product manager consume it before the member count is anywhere near 10,000 — and the AI search that most deflection business cases rest on does not exist below Growth. Teams that buy Starter to prove the case largely re-sign at Growth within a year, which makes the honest entry price the higher one.

## Best for

A Customer Success or support leader at a B2B SaaS company with roughly 2,000 to 25,000 users, running a 3-10 person CS team, who wants a branded self-service community for ticket deflection and structured feature requests — and who needs a defensible budget number before procurement will open a vendor evaluation.

**Not for you if** your community is under about 500 members: with the free plan retired, $4,788/year against a few hundred participants does not clear, and a hosted Discourse instance at $100/month or a customer Slack channel is the correct answer. It is also the wrong pick if you need moderation across millions of members, or if your actual requirement is feature-request intake alone — [Canny](/en/tools/canny/) does that job from $79/month.

## Versus the alternatives

- **Higher Logic Vanilla and Khoros** hold the enterprise B2B community share. Pick either when you need moderation at scale, deep SEO control over community content, or a member base in the millions, and you have the procurement patience for a quote-only cycle. Their floor is well above Bettermode's; their ceiling is far higher too.
- **[Gainsight](/en/tools/gainsight/) Customer Communities** is the pick when community activity has to write back into health scores in a Gainsight CS platform you already run. Buying Bettermode instead means building that link yourself over the GraphQL API and webhooks.
- **Discourse** is the pick for data sovereignty, self-hosting, or a small community — hosted plans start at $100/month as of August 2026. You trade Design Studio and the packaged integrations for a plugin ecosystem you maintain.
- **Circle** is the fastest-growing entrant in adjacent territory, but it is built for creators selling courses and paid memberships. Pick it when you are monetizing an audience, not deflecting support tickets; its branding control and CRM depth are behind Bettermode's for B2B customer communities.

## Watch-outs

- **The repricing has a second act at renewal.** Existing Pro customers were renamed "Pro - Legacy" and keep their features only until their renewal date; Enterprise and Advanced customers hold current terms to renewal as well. *Guard:* if you are on a legacy plan, request the renewal quote in writing 90 days out and model Growth as the landing tier, not your current line item. If you are new, sign annually — the monthly rate is 25% higher on Starter.
- **The AI you are buying the platform for is a tier above the price you saw first.** Ask AI and federated search are Growth-and-up. *Guard:* run the 14-day trial against the Growth feature set with your own imported content, and measure answer quality on your ten most-asked support questions before committing $18,000/year.
- **MCP writes are deliberately slow.** Every write action prompts for approval one at a time — a request to message twelve quiet members surfaces twelve separate approvals. *Guard:* scope MCP for reads and analysis, and route any bulk action through the GraphQL API or webhooks where you control batching.
- **Reviewers consistently flag in-community search and the mobile app** as behind the rest of the product. *Guard:* import a representative slice of your existing forum or help-center corpus during the trial and search it as a member would; treat mobile as a browser experience and do not promise an app in your rollout plan.
