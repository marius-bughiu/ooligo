## What it is

Juicebox (branded PeopleGPT) is the AI-native sourcing tool that replaces Boolean search with natural-language queries against an 800M+ profile database compiled from dozens of public sources: LinkedIn, GitHub, conference speaker lists, patent and paper authorship, and other signals. Launched in 2023, it was the first widely adopted recruiting product built around an LLM primitive rather than retrofitting one onto a legacy search engine. The vendor now claims 25,000 teams.

In late 2026 it is no longer a search box with outreach bolted on. The product center of gravity is **Juicebox Agents**: always-on sourcing agents that live inside a project, pull context from your ATS, intake docs and call transcripts, map the talent market, shortlist, and send outreach from a connected mailbox. The second shift is that Juicebox is now reachable from outside its own UI: an official **MCP server** lets Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor or Gemini CLI query recruiting analytics and create, calibrate, pause or close Agents. That puts Juicebox squarely in the agent-first sourcing field next to [Pin](/en/tools/pin/), [SeekOut](/en/tools/seekout/) and [goperfect](/en/tools/goperfect/).

## Why it shows up in Recruiting stacks

- **Natural-language sourcing.** "Senior Python engineers in Berlin who have contributed to open-source ML libraries and worked at fintech startups" returns a ranked list in seconds, with an AI spotlight explaining why each profile matched. The Boolean string that took a senior sourcer 30 minutes is one sentence.
- **Agents with context.** An Agent reads connected sources (Google Drive, Notion, Granola, [Metaview](/en/tools/metaview/) transcripts and, on Business, your ATS's past hires) before it searches, then learns from the profiles you approve and reject. You choose the autonomy: hands-off, or checkpoints at shortlist or at sequencing.
- **Reachable from your AI client.** The remote MCP server (`https://mcp.juicebox.ai/v1`, per-user OAuth) runs on every paid plan. Analytics and Agent tools work on all of them; shortlist, candidate-detail and project tools are Business-only. A recruiter working in [Claude](/en/tools/claude/) can spin up an Agent for a new req without opening the app.
- **Broad ATS coverage.** 40+ ATSes (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, iCIMS, SmartRecruiters and more) plus 20+ CRMs for import-a-job, export-to-ATS and rediscovery, all gated to the Business plan.

## Pricing

Freemium, per seat, billed monthly or annually. Juicebox advertises the annual toggle as 15% off, but the realized discount differs by plan: Starter drops 17%, Growth only 10%. Verified on juicebox.ai/pricing on 2026-11-09:

- **Free**: $0, limited searches, 1 seat, 5 one-off contact credits, no exports
- **Starter**: $119 per seat/month, or **$99 per seat/month billed annually**; 1 seat, unlimited searches, 500 contact credits and 500 export credits per month, 1 mailbox
- **Growth**: $199 per seat/month, or $179 per seat/month annually; up to 5 seats, each paid; 1,500 contact and 1,500 export credits per seat/month, talent insights, 3 mailboxes per seat
- **Business**: custom, annual only; unlimited contact credits, 2,250 exports per seat/month, ATS/CRM integrations, hiring-manager seats, network sourcing, usage analytics, SSO and SCIM, 6 mailboxes per seat
- **Juicebox Agents add-on**: $199 per agent/month on any paid plan, with unlimited contact and email credits

**Real-world cost:** the "$99" headline is one annual seat with no automation. A solo recruiter running Juicebox the way it is marketed (one seat plus one always-on Agent) pays **$298–318/month** ($99–119 seat + $199 agent). Two details move the bill. First, an Agent occupies a paid agent seat while it is sourcing, paused or awaiting action; only calibrating and closed Agents are free, so a paused Agent you forgot about still costs $199. Second, the compare table prices a phone number at 3 contact credits against 1 for an email, so phone-heavy outreach drains Starter's 500 credits about three times faster. ATS sync and the MCP shortlist tools both sit on custom Business, so integration-dependent teams should price that tier in from day one.

## Best for

- Growth-stage tech companies hiring engineers, designers and ML researchers, where achievement-level signal beats keyword matching
- Lean TA teams (one to three recruiters) who want an always-on [AI sourcing](/en/learn/ai-sourcing/) agent per evergreen req rather than another headcount
- Teams already working inside Claude or ChatGPT who want to launch and monitor sourcing from that client through MCP

## Watch-outs

- **Incumbents out-integrate it on the funnel.** [hireEZ](/en/tools/hireez/) and [Gem](/en/tools/gem/), the two tools most teams already run, carry deeper CRM workflow and pipeline reporting, and Juicebox locks its ATS sync behind Business. *Guard:* keep your ATS as system of record, treat Juicebox as discovery plus first touch, and get Business pricing in writing before you count on ATS export.
- **Sparse-signal roles underperform.** Sales, ops and traditional management candidates leave thin public footprints. *Guard:* reserve Juicebox for roles with strong online signal and keep a referral or agency channel for the rest.
- **Coverage varies by geography.** US, UK and EU data is strongest; some APAC and LATAM markets are thinner. *Guard:* run a 20-candidate test search in your real target region on the Free plan and compare recall against [Findem](/en/tools/findem/) or your current tool before paying.
- **Agents send real email from your domain.** An auto-email Agent on a bad calibration can burn sender reputation. *Guard:* start every Agent in auto-shortlist mode for a week and switch to auto-email only once its shortlists match what you would have picked.
- **MCP sessions expire every 30 days**, and SSO-enforced orgs reject connections not approved through SSO. *Guard:* document the re-auth step for anyone scripting against the MCP server so a silent expiry does not stall a workflow.

## Alternatives

- **hireEZ / Gem**: pick these when ATS-native sequencing and pipeline reporting matter more than search UX. They are the incumbents; Juicebox wins on natural-language discovery and agent autonomy, they win downstream.
- **[Pin](/en/tools/pin/)**: the fastest-growing agent-first rival, also freemium from $99. Pick it when you want one agent to run source-to-schedule end to end; see [Pin vs Juicebox](/en/vs/pin-vs-juicebox/).
- **[SeekOut](/en/tools/seekout/)**: pick it when you want a vendor-delivered shortlist service rather than running searches yourself.
- **[goperfect](/en/tools/goperfect/)**: weigh it when continuous multi-channel autopilot across many ATSes is the core need.

Not a replacement for an [ATS](/en/learn/ats-vs-recruiting-crm/): pair it with [Ashby](/en/tools/ashby/), [Greenhouse](/en/tools/greenhouse/) or another ATS for funnel tracking.
