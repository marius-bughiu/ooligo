## What it is

ChartMogul is a subscription analytics platform. It pulls invoices and subscriptions from your billing systems, normalizes them into one customer-level revenue ledger, and reports MRR and ARR, MRR movements (new, expansion, contraction, churn, reactivation), churn, LTV, cohorts and a revenue forecast. It also includes a built-in CRM at no extra charge, and **ChartMogul AI**, an in-app assistant that builds reports and answers questions over the same data.

The company is Berlin-based, founded in 2014 and independent. Its status page logged a resolved data-processing delay in August 2026, and its help docs were last updated on 20 November 2026.

## The question it answers

Most teams arrive with one question: do we need ChartMogul for MRR, NRR and renewal analytics, or can Salesforce or the billing tool do it?

- **Your billing tool** reports its own data. [Stripe](/en/tools/stripe/) and [Chargebee](/en/tools/chargebee/) both show MRR, and Chargebee exposes MRR history through its MCP server. That is enough while one billing system holds all revenue.
- **[Salesforce](/en/tools/salesforce/)** reports bookings from opportunities, not recognized subscription revenue from invoices. Building per-customer MRR movements there means custom objects and a sync you maintain. ChartMogul lists Salesforce as a CRM source, but its pricing page still marks it "coming soon".
- **ChartMogul earns its fee** when revenue sits in more than one place (Stripe for self-serve, Chargebee or invoiced contracts for sales-led, app stores for mobile), or when the board wants NRR and GRR by cohort that tie back to individual invoices. Background: [NRR vs GRR](/en/learn/nrr-vs-grr/) and [unit economics](/en/learn/unit-economics/).

## Pricing reality

Price is banded by the ARR ChartMogul tracks, set with a slider on the pricing page. Annual billing saves 17%.

- **Free**: $0 up to $10K MRR ($120K ARR). One billing source, two standard destinations.
- **Starter**: $57 to $707 a month billed annually ($69 to $849 monthly), up to $10M ARR. Three team members, one billing source, two custom or third-party sources, no warehouse export.
- **Pro**: $99 to $1,199 a month billed annually ($119 to $1,439 monthly), up to $10M ARR. Unlimited users, five billing sources, two-way CRM sync, and two advanced destinations (Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift, S3 and others).
- **Enterprise**: from $19,900 a year, for companies above $10M ARR or with high data volumes. Fifteen billing sources and a named support contact.

So the real band for a multi-source team is Pro at roughly $1,200 to $14,400 a year, rising with ARR, and $19,900 or more past $10M. The FAQ says the account moves to the next tier as ARR grows. The API, the MCP server, standard CSV exports, forecasting and the CRM are on every plan, Free included. A startup program takes $50 a month off any paid plan for 12 months. We could not retrieve independent negotiated-price data, so treat the published list as the reference.

## AI and MCP

The remote MCP server at `https://mcp.chartmogul.com` uses OAuth 2.0 and is listed in the Claude and ChatGPT directories. The help article counts 81 tools: metrics, cohorts, benchmarks, scenario analysis, customers, invoices, pipelines and dashboards. ChartMogul AI uses the same toolset in the app. Ask Claude "which accounts drove last quarter's contraction MRR" and it answers from the ledger, not from a CSV you exported.

## Best for

A RevOps or finance lead at a **B2B SaaS company between $1M and $10M ARR with revenue in two or more billing systems**, who owns the monthly board pack and needs MRR movements, NRR and churn cohorts that reconcile to invoices. It also fits as the renewal-data layer in a [customer success expansion stack](/en/stacks/customer-success-expansion-stack/) next to [Vitally](/en/tools/vitally/) or [ChurnZero](/en/tools/churnzero/).

## Not for

A Stripe-only company under about $1M ARR: Stripe's own dashboard or ProfitWell Metrics covers it for free. Also not for a company past $10M ARR with a data team and a warehouse, where subscription metrics modeled in [Snowflake](/en/tools/snowflake/) avoid a second source of truth.

## Versus the alternatives

- **ProfitWell Metrics (Paddle)**: free, works with Stripe and Chargebee without Paddle Billing. Pick it when the budget is zero and one billing source covers you. Pick ChartMogul when you need several sources, a CRM, warehouse export or an MCP server.
- **Baremetrics**: Launch $49 a month up to $360K ARR, Growth $189 to $3.6M, Scale $749 above. Pick it when you also want failed-payment recovery and cancellation insights, sold as add-ons. Pick ChartMogul for deeper segmentation and five-plus sources.
- **Stripe Billing analytics and Sigma**: pick them when every dollar runs through Stripe and you have someone who writes SQL.
- **Warehouse-native modeling**: the route more data teams take as subscription data lands in Snowflake or BigQuery anyway. Pick it when you have an analytics engineer.

If none fit because revenue is a few dozen annual contracts, keep MRR in a spreadsheet tied to the ERP and revisit at 100 customers. See also [LTV:CAC](/en/learn/ltv-cac/).

## Watch-outs

- **The numbers are only as good as the mapping.** Plan mapping, annual-to-monthly normalization and duplicate-customer merges decide your MRR. A 2026 G2 reviewer reported Recurly sync errors that forced manual checks. **Guard:** reconcile ChartMogul MRR against billing-system invoice totals for three closed months before any number goes to the board, and write down your metric definitions.
- **Starter caps bite early.** Three users, one billing source, no warehouse export. **Guard:** count billing sources and report readers before choosing a plan; with two sources you are buying Pro.
- **The fee climbs with ARR.** Tiers move automatically as tracked ARR grows. **Guard:** price the plan at 2x current ARR before signing, and lock the band in an annual contract.
- **The MCP server is not fully read-only.** The docs call access read-only, but the tool list includes writes for tags, tasks and custom attributes, and access is on by default. **Guard:** have an Owner or Admin decide under Settings > Security & Access > Team Security whether MCP access stays on, and keep custom attributes that feed reports out of agent workflows.
