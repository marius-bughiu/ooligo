## What it is

Zonka Feedback is a survey and feedback platform for NPS, CSAT and CES programs that run across more than one channel. One survey can go out by email, SMS, WhatsApp, a website or in-app widget, a link, a QR code or an offline kiosk app, and the responses land in one dashboard with closed-loop case management: a low score opens a case, assigns an owner and tracks it to resolution. The company behind it, Zonka Technologies Pvt. Ltd., is founder-led (Rajiv Mehta, CEO) and claims 2,000+ customer brands and surveys in 30 languages.

Since 2025 the product comes in two parts. **Feedback Management** is the survey, distribution and case-management core. **AI Feedback Intelligence** is a separate analytics product that pulls in surveys, support tickets, chats and public reviews, then runs theme and sub-theme detection, sentiment and emotion, entity recognition (agents, locations, products, competitors), impact analysis against NPS/CSAT/CES, and an "Ask AI" assistant that answers questions about the feedback. A hosted MCP server at `https://mcp.zonkafeedback.com` exposes the same scores, themes and verbatims to Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and other clients. Checked 21 November 2026, it is read-only, with write actions announced but not shipped.

It is not a research-grade survey tool for conjoint or panel studies, and it is not a customer success platform: it does not hold health scores, renewals or playbooks.

## Why it shows up in Customer Success stacks

- **It is the mid-market landing spot for Delighted and GetFeedback refugees.** Qualtrics shut Delighted down on 30 June 2026, and SurveyMonkey ends GetFeedback Direct on 31 December 2026. Zonka runs migration programs for both, including moving Salesforce-triggered survey workflows off GetFeedback.
- **Omnichannel without Qualtrics overhead.** Email, SMS and WhatsApp sending are built in, so a team that surveys retail locations, field visits and SaaS users does not need a second tool per channel.
- **The score lands in the systems CS already uses.** Native integrations cover [Salesforce](/en/tools/salesforce/), [HubSpot](/en/tools/hubspot/) (scores written to contact properties, surveys triggered from workflows), [Zendesk](/en/tools/zendesk/) (post-ticket surveys, score sync, ticket creation), [Intercom](/en/tools/intercom/), [Freshdesk](/en/tools/freshdesk/), [Help Scout](/en/tools/help-scout/), [Front](/en/tools/front/) and [Pipedrive](/en/tools/pipedrive/), with alerts in [Slack](/en/tools/slack/) and [Microsoft Teams](/en/tools/microsoft-teams/). [Zapier](/en/tools/zapier/), [Make](/en/tools/make/), webhooks and a REST API cover the rest.
- **Role-based access goes all the way down.** A store manager or regional lead sees only their own location's responses, and the MCP server enforces the same permissions, which matters once an AI assistant starts answering questions about the data.

## Pricing reality

Checked 21 November 2026, Zonka publishes no prices. Its pricing page shows two products with a "Get a Custom Quote" button each, and its FAQ says Feedback Management is priced mainly on response volume and AI Feedback Intelligence on "data credits", with the two sold separately or bundled. A 14-day free trial is available; there is no free plan.

The vendor-supplied listings on Capterra and G2 put Feedback Management at **$199/month** and AI Feedback Intelligence at **$999/month** as starting points. Older multi-tier prices ($29, $199 and $499 a month) still circulate on aggregator sites; treat them as retired. Read the real band as roughly $2,400 a year for a single-channel NPS program at low volume, and $14,000 or more a year once you add the AI add-on, before any volume uplift. SSO, data-residency choice (US, EU, Australia or India hosting), historical data import and priority support sit in an "Enterprise-Scale Capabilities" block whose plan placement the page does not state, so price them explicitly in the quote. The MCP server, per Zonka, carries no extra fee on any plan.

For scale: Delighted's old Premium tier topped out at $249/month, and Qualtrics' Vendr median sits near $30K a year. Zonka without AI lands much closer to the first; with AI, it is about halfway to the second.

## Best for

A CX or customer success operations lead at a 100–2,000-person company running NPS, CSAT and CES across several channels (email plus SMS, WhatsApp, kiosk or in-app) and several locations or teams, who needs closed-loop follow-up and per-location permissions but cannot justify a Qualtrics contract. It is the default pick for a former Delighted Premium or GetFeedback Direct customer whose program had grown past one question.

## Versus the alternatives

- **[Qualtrics](/en/tools/qualtrics/)** — the market-share leader in experience management. Pick it when CX is a funded, multi-journey program with an owner, a research team and a budget line of $30K or more.
- **[SurveyMonkey](/en/tools/surveymonkey/)** — the volume leader in general surveys, and where SurveyMonkey points GetFeedback Direct customers. Pick it when the same license must cover market research and internal surveys too, and omnichannel distribution is not the point.
- **[Retently](/en/tools/retently/)** — the fastest-moving entrant in this segment, with published B2B plans at $100 and $299 a month, AI topic and sentiment tagging, account-level scores and its own MCP server. Pick it when the program is B2B, email-first and you want a card-swipe purchase instead of a sales call.
- **[Customer Thermometer](/en/tools/customer-thermometer/)** — one-click CSAT and NPS from $49/month with unlimited users. Pick it when all you need is one rating per ticket or email signature.
- **[Simplesat](/en/tools/simplesat/)** — Starter at $149/month for 300 responses. Pick it when the job is agent-level support CSAT inside Zendesk or Freshdesk.
- **[Medallia](/en/tools/medallia/)** — pick it over Zonka only when you are an enterprise with contact-center speech analytics and a multi-year VoC budget.

## Watch-outs

- **No public price means the quote is the product.** Two products, response tiers and credit packs give the sales team a lot of room, and the AI add-on alone can be five times the survey base. **Guard:** get both products quoted at your 12-month response forecast, ask for the per-response and per-credit overage rates in writing, and cap annual uplift at renewal in the order form.
- **The AI lives in a separate product.** A Feedback Management contract does not include theme, sentiment or entity analysis. **Guard:** decide before the demo whether you need AI on comments, and run your own 3–6 months of exported verbatims through the trial to check that the themes match how your team already tags issues.
- **The MCP server reads but does not write.** You can ask Claude or ChatGPT about scores and themes, but an agent cannot yet open cases, send surveys or tag responses through it. **Guard:** keep write automations on Zapier, Make or the REST API, and turn the MCP toggle on only for roles whose data scope you have checked.
- **The score series breaks at migration.** Changing wording, scale, channel or send timing moves NPS and CSAT even when nothing about the customer changed. **Guard:** import Delighted or GetFeedback history through Zonka's historical import, mark the cutover date on every chart, and run old and new surveys side by side for one cycle if you can.
- **Compliance claims are ISO-shaped, not SOC 2-shaped.** Zonka states ISO 27001:2022 certification plus GDPR and HIPAA compliance, but names no SOC 2 report, and its public security page returned a 404 when checked. **Guard:** request the ISO certificate, a recent penetration test and a signed DPA (and a BAA if health data is involved) before go-live, and confirm the hosting region in the contract.
