## What it is

SurePoint is practice and financial management software for mid-sized US law firms: time, billing, trust, general ledger, e-billing and firm analytics, plus a front-office matter layer and a market-intelligence product. It is built for the band SurePoint's own LLFI Mid-Sized 200 ranking defines as 50 to 150 attorneys, too big for a small-firm suite and too small to justify an [Aderant](/en/tools/aderant/) or [Elite 3E](/en/tools/elite-3e/) program. The company traces back to Rippe & Kingston in 1982. Aquiline Capital Partners has held a majority stake since June 2021, with ParkerGale keeping a minority. Jeff Steinberg, formerly a senior advisor to Aquiline, became CEO on 23 July 2026; Eric Thurston moved to executive chairman. SurePoint says more than 100,000 legal professionals use its products. It does not publish a firm count.

Most of the lineup arrived by acquisition and has since been renamed, which is the first thing to untangle in a demo:

- **SurePoint Pro**, launched 2 February 2026, is the cloud platform: **Practice Pro** (the former ZenCase, bought January 2025) for matters and front office, **Finance Pro** for billing and financials, and **Legal Insights** (the former Leopard Solutions, bought May 2024) embedded on top.
- **Finance Enterprise** is the long-running SurePoint Legal billing and accounting system (formerly LMS and LMS+), with GL, AP, trust, cost recovery, LEDES e-billing, multi-entity support and 500+ reports.
- **Finance Core** is the former Coyote Analytics (bought June 2022), aimed at smaller, growing firms. **SurePoint CRM** is the former ContactEase.

## Why it shows up in Legal Ops stacks

- **It is the named finance endpoint for AI time capture below the Am Law 200.** [Laurel](/en/tools/laurel/) announced its SurePoint integration in January 2026, [PointOne](/en/tools/pointone/) in October 2025 and [Billables AI](/en/tools/billables-ai/) in September 2025. For a mid-sized firm, that list decides whether an AI timekeeping pilot writes entries straight into billing or into a CSV someone re-keys.
- **Legal Insights is the unusual piece.** None of the mid-market finance systems ooligo covers ships lateral-hiring and competitor intelligence (practice-group movement, probability-of-move and projected-financials models) inside the financial system. For a managing partner weighing a lateral group, that is a real reason to shortlist SurePoint.
- **The document side is covered by partners, not by SurePoint.** The partner list names [iManage](/en/tools/imanage/), [NetDocuments](/en/tools/netdocuments/), [LawToolBox](/en/tools/lawtoolbox/) for Microsoft 365 calendaring, and [Filevine](/en/tools/filevine/). Payments run through SurePoint's own Stripe-based service; there is no LawPay listing.

## Pricing reality

There is no public price. Capterra and Software Advice list "contact vendor", and the per-user figures that show up next to SurePoint on directory pages belong to other products, so don't use them. Budget like a mid-market finance-system replacement: a subscription quote per timekeeper, plus a separately scoped data migration and implementation. For a firm moving its ledger and trust history, the migration is the bigger unknown. Ask for both numbers in the same proposal, and ask which product is being quoted. Finance Pro and Finance Enterprise are different systems with different feature sets.

## Best for

Firm administrators, CFOs and billing directors at US firms of 50 to 150 lawyers who are leaving PCLaw, Tabs3, Centerbase or an older SurePoint install, need real trust accounting and LEDES e-billing, and plan to add AI time capture. It fits best when the managing partner also wants lateral and competitor intelligence and would otherwise buy it separately.

## Versus the alternatives

[Aderant](/en/tools/aderant/) and [Elite 3E](/en/tools/elite-3e/) are the two platforms large-firm finance teams choose between. Pick one of them above roughly 150 lawyers, or when you have multi-office, multi-currency entities and a dedicated finance-systems team, because that is the scale their price and implementation load assume. [Clio](/en/tools/clio/) is the small-firm market leader and the fastest-moving entrant into this band: it reports well over 1,000 mid-market firms, bought ShareDo in 2025 and launched Clio Operate in North America on 9 March 2026. Pick Clio when matter workflow and client experience matter more than general-ledger depth, and pair it with dedicated accounting. Centerbase (10 to 200 attorneys) is the head-to-head rival SurePoint campaigns against directly; demo both. If none fit, a firm under 40 lawyers is usually better served by Clio plus QuickBooks than by any mid-market finance system.

## Watch-outs

- **Product renames hide which system you are buying.** "SurePoint" can mean Finance Pro, Finance Enterprise or Finance Core, and the published feature lists differ: GL, AP and LEDES are documented for Finance Enterprise but not on the Finance Pro page. Guard: put the product name and a feature schedule (GL, AP, trust, LEDES, multi-entity) into the order form, and see each one in a live demo on your own sample data.
- **New CEO, new platform, PE owner.** A July 2026 leadership change on top of a February 2026 platform launch means roadmaps and legacy support terms can move. Guard: get a written support and migration commitment for whichever legacy product you run today, and cap year-two and year-three price increases in the contract.
- **Review complaints cluster on performance and e-billing.** Reviewers of the legacy LMS and Finance Enterprise cite slow performance, weak e-billing vendor integrations and a department-based design that feels heavy for small teams. Guard: run a month-end close and a batch of LEDES submissions to your two biggest e-billing clients in a sandbox before signing.
- **No MCP server or public API reference.** AI arrives through partner integrations and Legal Insights models, not as a surface your own agents can call. Guard: if you plan agent workflows over billing data, ask for the API documentation and confirm read access to time, WIP and AR before you buy.
- **Security is attested, not detailed.** SurePoint states SOC 2 Type II and AWS hosting, with a trust center at trust.surepoint.com, but publishes no audit dates. Guard: request the current SOC 2 report and the AI subprocessor list before client data moves.
