# Owners and completion checks

| Observation | Owner in this example | Action | Completion evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sponsor has not been confirmed | CSM | Verify who owns the renewal decision | Named role and confirmation date in the account record |
| Weekly active seats fell | Adoption lead | Check the counting window, active-user definition and operational cause | Comparable counts plus an explanation or a documented unknown |
| Sponsor remains unknown at the review date | CS lead | Review the ownership gap with the CSM | Assigned owner and next internal review date |

Use your team's definitions of these roles. The renewal decision and any offer remain with the people authorized to make them.
