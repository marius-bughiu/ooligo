## What it is

Peony is a self-serve virtual data room (VDR) for M&A, fundraising and other diligence processes. You upload files, build a folder index, share one link, and control who sees what: email verification, signed NDA before access, dynamic watermarks, per-file permissions, an audit trail per visitor, and a Q&A module for bidder questions. An AI assistant answers questions across the documents with citations back to the page.

The company was founded in 2021 by Deqian Jia and Sean Yu, is based in San Francisco with a London office, and is backed by angels and small funds (Matt Clifford, Charlie Songhurst, Backed VC, Possible Ventures). It started as a general file product and moved into data rooms from 2023. Peony claims 8,000+ teams as users; that figure is self-reported.

The product ships fast. Its changelog lists PDF redaction and a three-step Q&A workflow (coordinator, expert, approver) in April 2026, a public REST API in August, private Q&A teams per bidder in September, and in October visitor anomaly alerts plus file and folder search on every plan (confirmed 2026-10-29). There is no MCP server; AI agents connect through the API on the Deal Team plan and above.

## Pricing reality

USD per **admin** per month, annual / monthly billing, checked 2026-11-25 at peony.ink/pricing. Viewers are unlimited and free on every plan, and Peony charges nothing per page, per room or for storage overage.

- **Free — $0.** 1 admin, 50 documents, tracked links, no data rooms.
- **Business — $30 / $44.** 1,000 documents, 3 data rooms per admin, 100 GB, acknowledge-only NDA, screenshot protection, AI document Q&A.
- **Data Room — $52 / $75.** Unlimited rooms and storage, dynamic watermarks, signed NDA, granular and group permissions, audit trail, basic Q&A, custom domain.
- **Deal Team — $64 / $89, minimum 4 admins.** Advanced Q&A workflow, redaction, API (enabled on request), webhooks, archive download, standard DPA.
- **Enterprise — quote.** SAML SSO, anomaly alerts, BYOK, EU hosting or self-hosting. Peony's docs say Enterprise is open to "selected pilot customers."

What a team really pays depends on how many people run the room. One deal lead on Data Room pays $624 a year; two pay $1,248. A sell-side process with four people needing the advanced Q&A workflow lands on Deal Team at $256 a month, $3,072 a year. A four-month process on monthly billing with two Data Room admins costs $600 in total. Against the institutional rooms the gap is large: Vendr's buyer data puts the Datasite median at $25,555 a year and Intralinks at $13,950.

There is a 7-day trial of the Data Room plan with no card, but it excludes AI features, and Peony's docs state that payments are not refundable.

## Best for

Legal ops managers, in-house deal counsel and founders who run one to four data rooms a year themselves (a fundraise, a small acquisition, an asset sale with a handful of bidders, a vendor or partner diligence pack) and want signed NDAs, watermarks, an audit trail and Q&A without a five-figure project quote.

**Do not use it** when the sell-side bank or the buyer's counsel requires a VDR with completed SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 audits, or when IT policy requires SSO for every SaaS tool and you cannot get onto the Enterprise pilot.

## Versus the alternatives

- **[Datasite](/en/tools/datasite/)** — the market-share leader for banker-run M&A, and owner of Firmex and Ansarada. Quote-based, with completed certifications and an MCP server. Pick Datasite when the adviser mandates it or the auction has dozens of bidders and a formal Q&A process.
- **[Intralinks](/en/tools/intralinks/)** — the other institutional VDR (SS&C). Pick it when your bank or fund already runs on SS&C or your legal AI is Harvey, which integrates with it.
- **[Papermark](/en/tools/papermark/)** — the fastest-growing low-cost entrant: open-source core, flat pricing per workspace, its own MCP server. Papermark Data Rooms costs $119 a month annual with 3 seats included, so it beats Peony's $52 per admin from the third admin on. Pick Papermark for three or more room operators or when you want Claude to work on the room through MCP; pick Peony for one or two operators.
- **iDeals** — the mid-market quote-based VDR with a mature Q&A module and certifications. Pick it when you need institutional controls but Datasite's quote is out of range.
- **[Box](/en/tools/box/)** — pick it only for ongoing document sharing with no bidder Q&A, NDA gating or page-level analytics, when the files already live there.

## Watch-outs

- **Certifications are in progress, not done.** On 2026-10-17 Peony described itself as "SOC 2 Type II-ready and ISO 27001-ready, with both audits underway." Guard: if a counterparty's security review asks for reports, request the audit timeline in writing before you open the room, and use Datasite or Intralinks for that deal if the reports are mandatory.
- **Access stops the moment the subscription ends.** Links and rooms go dark immediately; closed rooms are kept 30 days. Guard: export the archive (Deal Team download, or a $99 USB drive) and the Q&A log to CSV before you cancel, and schedule cancellation after closing, not at signing.
- **Every admin is a paid seat, charged without confirmation.** The pro-rated charge applies the moment an invited admin accepts, and there are only two roles, Owner and Member. Guard: add internal reviewers as viewers with group permissions, and keep the admin list to the people who build and run the room.
- **SSO, EU hosting and anomaly alerts are Enterprise-only.** Default hosting is AWS us-east-1 with standard contractual clauses for UK and EEA transfers. Guard: for EU sellers or targets, confirm with your DPO whether US hosting is acceptable before uploading personal data, or request Enterprise EU hosting.
- **Most coverage of Peony is Peony's own.** Its blog ranks for most data-room comparison searches, and independent review counts are small. Guard: run the 7-day trial on a sample room with a real counterparty, and test the AI separately, since the trial excludes it.
