## What it is

Workday VNDLY is a vendor management system (VMS): the software an enterprise uses to request, approve, onboard, track time for, invoice and offboard people who are not on payroll, from agency temps to statement-of-work (SOW) consultancies. VNDLY was founded in 2017 in Mason, Ohio, raised almost $58M (a $35M Series B led by Insight Partners in late 2019 was the largest round), and was bought by Workday for $510M in a deal announced on 18 November 2021. It is now sold as a Workday product. The old vndly.com domain redirects to workday.com.

Workday sells it as three modules. **Extended Workforce Management** covers the requisition-to-offboarding cycle: requisitions, approval chains, candidate shortlists, onboarding checklists, time and expense, rate cards and global digital invoicing. **Statement of Work** handles SOW contracts with redlining, milestones, change orders and multiple payment types, and turns only approved spend into invoice lines. **Worker Profile Management** keeps one record per external worker, including non-billable people such as interns and volunteers. Workday says VNDLY runs as one global instance across 130+ countries with 35+ language packs. The question it answers: **how do we manage contingent workers and SOW spend inside the same Workday record as our employees?**

## Why it shows up in recruiting stacks

The selling point is the worker record. VNDLY pairs with [Workday HCM](/en/tools/workday/), so contractors and employees sit in one view, and contingent workers can be provisioned into HCM for badging and building access. The 2026R2 release, posted on 24 August 2026, added a monthly timesheet experience, split SOW milestone payments across charge codes, and the ability to search for and close Workday positions from inside VNDLY.

The AI piece is the **Contingent Sourcing Agent**, now generally available. It has two skills. *Contingent talent rediscovery* recommends past workers and applicants already in your tenant for a new job, in a separate tab next to current applicants. *Contingent applicant grading* matches a résumé to the job requirements, assigns a grade and explains why. Program managers and MSP users can override both. Workday's admin guide says the first grades can take up to 48 hours and that staffing suppliers cannot use the agent in their own VNDLY view. Workday lists advanced vendor insights for late 2026 and conversational AI for 2027.

Analysts rate it in the top tier. Everest Group named VNDLY a Leader in its 2026 VMS PEAK Matrix in the Global, North America and EMEA segments and a Star Performer globally, its sixth straight year in the top tier. On the supply side, staffing ATS platforms such as [Ceipal](/en/tools/ceipal/) and [JobDiva](/en/tools/jobdiva/) sync requisitions and submittals with VNDLY, so agencies can work its jobs without re-keying.

## Pricing reality

Workday publishes no VNDLY price list, and the public review base is thin: G2 shows only a handful of reviews. Category economics apply. A VMS is either **supplier-funded**, where a fee comes off each staffing supplier's invoice, or **client-funded**, where you pay a subscription. The working benchmark for the technology fee is about 0.3% to 1% of managed spend, falling with scale, based on Beeline's published pricing examples. That works out to roughly $60K to $200K a year on a $20M program. A managed service provider (MSP) adds 2% to 3.5% of spend on top. Expect VNDLY to be quoted inside a wider Workday negotiation.

The agent is billed separately. The Contingent Sourcing Agent consumes Workday **flex credits** in production tenants, though not in preview or test tenants. Using it requires opting in to Workday's flex credits policy and its Universal Main Service Agreement (UMSA).

## Best for

Contingent workforce program leads and TA or procurement operations teams at enterprises that already run Workday HCM and want contractors in the same worker record as employees. That usually means a few hundred or more external workers, mostly agency temps and SOW consultancies, with an MSP running the program.

Skip it if HR does not run on Workday, since you would be buying VNDLY without its main advantage. Skip it too if your contingent population is mostly freelancers. Workday's product pages do not list independent-contractor classification or employer-of-record services, so an employer of record such as [Deel](/en/tools/deel/) is the better fit. Employee hiring stays in [Workday Recruiting](/en/tools/workday/), [Greenhouse](/en/tools/greenhouse/) or [iCIMS](/en/tools/icims/).

## Versus the alternatives

**[Beeline](/en/tools/beeline/)** is the main independent incumbent. In June 2025 it bought MBO Partners for independent-contractor engagement, and in September 2026 it launched Beeline MCP. Pick Beeline when your HR and finance systems are mixed or not Workday, or when freelancers and agency temps have to live in one platform.

**[SAP Fieldglass](/en/tools/sap-fieldglass/)** is the other incumbent. Pick it when procurement owns the program and you run SAP S/4HANA and Ariba.

**SimplifyVMS** is the fastest-rising independent, an Everest Leader and Star Performer in 2025 and 2026. Pick it for a vendor-neutral VMS with no suite attached. **[Magnit](/en/tools/magnit/)** sells its VMS together with its own MSP. Pick it when you want one firm to run both the software and the program.

## Watch-outs

- **Ownership risk.** Reuters reported on 13 August 2026 that Silver Lake was in talks to take Workday private. No deal had been announced as of this review. Guard: put a renewal price cap, a change-of-control clause and a full data-export right (worker, SOW and invoice history) in any multi-year VNDLY contract.
- **AI is metered on top of the platform fee.** The Contingent Sourcing Agent burns flex credits in production. Guard: get the credit rate and a monthly estimate in writing before you turn on the agent, and run it in a preview tenant first, where it consumes no credits.
- **The value depends on Workday HCM.** Without it, VNDLY is one capable VMS among several. Guard: if you are not on Workday, run a competitive RFP against Beeline and SimplifyVMS instead of accepting a bundle discount.
- **Freelancers fall outside the core product.** Workday's product pages do not list independent-contractor classification. Guard: map every worker type you engage before you sign, and contract a separate employer-of-record provider for 1099 and freelance workers.
- **Thin public reviews.** There are too few G2 reviews to read a pattern from. Guard: ask for two reference customers with a program the size of yours and the same MSP model, and ask them how long configuration changes take.
