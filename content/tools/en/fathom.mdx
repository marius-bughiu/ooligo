## What it is

Fathom is an AI notetaker that joins your Zoom, Meet, or Teams calls, records, transcribes, and writes a summary with action items. It is the freemium answer to [Gong](/en/tools/gong/) and Otter — free forever for individuals, with team plans that pipe summaries into [Salesforce](/en/tools/salesforce/) or [HubSpot](/en/tools/hubspot/).

The killer feature is that it actually writes good summaries out of the box. No prompt-tuning required.

## Why it shows up in RevOps stacks

- **Free for individuals.** Reps adopt it bottom-up before procurement gets involved. By the time RevOps notices, half the team is already using it.
- **CRM sync that works.** Summaries, action items, and call recordings auto-attach to the Salesforce or HubSpot opportunity without a clunky middleware step.
- **Recruiting crossover.** TA teams use it for candidate intake calls — same workflow, different ICP.

## Pricing

- **Free** — unlimited recording, transcription, and summaries for individuals
- **Premium** — $19/user/month, faster summaries and AI features
- **Team Edition** — $29/user/month, CRM sync and team views
- **Team Edition Pro** — $39/user/month, advanced AI and admin controls

## Best for

- Small to mid-market teams that want Gong-quality summaries at 1/10 the cost
- RevOps teams skeptical of enterprise conversation intelligence ROI
- Recruiting teams capturing structured candidate notes

## Watch-outs

- Not a coaching or revenue intelligence platform — no deal scoring, no scorecards
- Free tier is generous but does not include CRM sync; budget for Team Edition if you want pipeline integration
- Less granular admin controls than Gong or Chorus for compliance-heavy orgs
