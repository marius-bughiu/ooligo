## What it is

Findem is an AI talent acquisition platform built around "3D" search — you search not just titles and skills, but career attributes: tenure patterns, promotion velocity, remote experience, founder backgrounds, education trajectories. Pulls from 1B+ profiles across the open web.

## Why it shows up in Recruiting/TA stacks

- **Attribute search beats keyword search.** "Senior eng at a Series B who joined pre-PMF and stayed 4+ years" is a real query. Saves sourcers hours of LinkedIn boolean gymnastics.
- **Diversity sourcing without guessing.** Inferred attributes power compliant DEI sourcing in markets where self-ID is restricted. CHROs and DEI leads buy this directly.
- **Talent analytics.** Benchmark your team's skills, tenure, and gaps against competitors. Useful for workforce planning conversations with the CFO.

## Pricing

- **Sourcing** — custom, per-seat annual
- **Sourcing + Engagement** — adds outreach and CRM
- **Enterprise** — adds analytics, custom attributes, API access

No public pricing. Typical deals run $8–15K/year per seat. Annual contracts only.

## Best for

- Executive and senior IC sourcing where boolean search breaks down
- DEI-focused sourcing programs
- Mid-market to enterprise TA teams that want sourcing intelligence built in

## Watch-outs

- Overkill for high-volume hourly or junior roles — Findem shines at 60K+ comp bands
- Attribute inference is probabilistic — verify before relying on it for compliance-sensitive decisions
- Newer than Gem and SeekOut; ecosystem and integrations are still maturing
