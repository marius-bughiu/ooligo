## What it is

Chorus is the conversation intelligence platform [ZoomInfo](/en/tools/zoominfo/) acquired in 2021. It records calls and meetings, transcribes them, and surfaces moments — competitor mentions, pricing pushback, next steps — so reps and managers can review pipeline without rewatching every Zoom.

It is the closest direct competitor to Gong, with the strategic advantage that it lives inside the ZoomInfo data graph.

## Why it shows up in RevOps stacks

- **You already pay ZoomInfo.** If your data layer is ZoomInfo, Chorus is the path of least resistance — pricing leverage, single procurement, contact data already mapped to call participants.
- **Deal warning signals.** Chorus flags stalled deals, missing economic buyers, and silent stakeholders into Salesforce so RevOps can build forecast-quality alerts.
- **Coaching at scale.** Managers get scorecards on talk-time ratios, monologues, and competitor mentions without manually scrubbing recordings.

## Pricing

- **Custom only** — no public pricing. Typical deals land in the $1,000-$1,500 per rep per year range.
- Bundled discounts when bought with ZoomInfo SalesOS.
- Annual contracts; expect enterprise procurement timelines.

## Best for

- ZoomInfo customers wanting native conversation intelligence
- Mid-market and enterprise sales orgs with structured coaching motions
- RevOps teams piping call signals into Salesforce reporting

## Watch-outs

- Gong is still the category leader on AI summarization and deal inspection UX
- Roadmap velocity slowed post-acquisition; expect ZoomInfo bundling pressure
- Standalone value is weaker if you do not use ZoomInfo for data
