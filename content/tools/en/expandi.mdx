## What it is

Expandi is a cloud LinkedIn outreach tool billed per seat, where one seat drives one LinkedIn account. It runs on Expandi's servers rather than in your browser, gives each account a dedicated IP in the account owner's country, and sequences connection requests, messages, InMails, profile visits, post likes and email follow-ups in one campaign. The core is the Smart Campaign builder: if-then branches that react to whether a prospect accepted, replied, opened an email or engaged with a post. Expandi is run by ProfitSoft BV in Eindhoven and is independent and bootstrapped. Since September 2026 it also ships Expandi MCP, a beta server that lets Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor draft campaigns and inbox replies inside your account.

## Why it shows up in RevOps stacks

- **Branching that a simple sequencer does not have.** A linear tool sends step two whether or not step one landed. Expandi waits on conditions — connected, replied, viewed your profile, opened the email — and routes the prospect down a different path for each outcome. For founder-led and AE-led outreach, where a wrong follow-up costs more than a missed one, that logic is the reason to buy.
- **One rep, one account, one seat.** The bill follows the people sending, which suits teams where each AE owns their own LinkedIn presence. It is the opposite of [HeyReach](/en/tools/heyreach/), which bills per sender account and rotates one campaign across many.
- **The CRM and email handoffs are native.** HubSpot, Salesforce and Pipedrive sync directly, Salesforce with sentiment-based auto-sync and overwrite controls. [Smartlead](/en/tools/smartlead/) handles coordinated email steps. Webhooks and API credentials cover [Zapier](/en/tools/zapier/) and [n8n](/en/tools/n8n/).

## Pricing reality

There are two plans. **Business is $99 per seat per month, or $79 billed annually** (paid upfront, which is 20% off). **Agency** is a custom quote from 10 seats and adds roles and permissions, client reporting, a dedicated CSM and white-labelling. The trial is 7 days and converts to paid unless you cancel. Expandi says larger seat counts earn discounts, but below 10 seats nothing is published, so assume list price.

What the plan line leaves out: image and GIF personalization runs through Hyperise and video through [Sendspark](/en/tools/sendspark/), both paid add-ons with their own subscriptions. Most teams also pay for a [Sales Navigator](/en/tools/sales-navigator/) seat per account to build lead lists. A realistic all-in figure is $180-250 per rep per month on monthly billing. Five reps on annual Business cost $4,740 a year in Expandi seats alone.

## Best for

SDR managers and founders running LinkedIn-first outbound across 1-10 named accounts, where each person sends from their own profile and the sequences are long and conditional — event follow-up, multi-touch ABM, re-engagement of closed-lost accounts. It also fits GTM engineers who want an AI client to draft campaigns through MCP while a human approves every send.

**Skip it** if you need one campaign rotated across dozens of accounts (HeyReach), or if LinkedIn is a single touch inside an email-first cadence (lemlist).

## Versus the alternatives

**[HeyReach](/en/tools/heyreach/)** wins on multi-account economics: $79 per sender, or $999 for 25 senders on its Agency plan, against Expandi's $99 per seat. Pick HeyReach when an agency or team runs one campaign across many accounts. Pick Expandi when each rep runs their own sequences and the branching depth matters more than throughput.

**Waalaxy** has the largest installed base in LinkedIn automation and is the cheapest way for one person to send LinkedIn sequences. Pick it for solo prospecting with simple linear steps. It has no answer to Expandi's conditional branching.

**[lemlist](/en/tools/lemlist/)** is the fastest-growing pressure on standalone LinkedIn tools, because it puts LinkedIn steps inside an email sequencer. Pick lemlist when email carries the campaign and LinkedIn is one touch. Pick Expandi when LinkedIn carries it and email is the follow-up. See [Smartlead vs lemlist](/en/vs/smartlead-vs-lemlist/) for the email side of that decision.

## Watch-outs

- **Automation breaks LinkedIn's User Agreement, whatever the vendor's safety language says.** LinkedIn prohibits bots and automated methods for connecting and messaging, and in 2026 it began acting against vendors as well as users. Third-party roundups place Expandi among the tools whose heavy users reported restrictions. Dedicated IPs and warm-up lower the risk; they do not remove it. **Guard:** keep volumes well under Expandi's limit ranges, ramp new accounts over two to three weeks, and never put an executive's primary profile on an aggressive campaign.
- **A restricted account does not get you a refund.** Reviewers report that fees are non-refundable when LinkedIn restricts an account, and the annual plan is paid upfront. **Guard:** start on monthly billing for the first quarter, and only move to annual once your accounts have run clean at your target volume.
- **The AI is the MCP layer, not a built-in writer.** Expandi does not advertise a native AI message generator. Its AI story is Expandi MCP, which is in beta, drafts only, and never activates a campaign or starts new outreach. **Guard:** treat MCP as a drafting assistant and keep a human approving every campaign. If you want AI-written first lines at volume, generate them upstream in [Clay](/en/tools/clay/) and pass them in as placeholders.
- **Add-ons and seat counting drift.** Personalization add-ons bill separately, and one review reports being billed for more seats than the contract covered. **Guard:** reconcile seats against connected LinkedIn accounts on every invoice, and remove a seat the day a rep leaves.
