## What it is

Amplemarket is the AI-native outbound platform — bundles prospect data, multi-channel sequencing (email + LinkedIn + phone), AI-drafted personalized emails, and reply analytics into one platform. Targets SMB and mid-market outbound teams that don't want to stitch together [Apollo](/en/tools/apollo/) + [Outreach](/en/tools/outreach/) + Lavender. Used by lean RevOps teams whose outbound stack consolidation is itself a priority.

## Why it shows up in RevOps stacks

- **Single-platform consolidation.** Replaces ZoomInfo / Apollo data + Outreach / Salesloft sequencing + Lavender / Apollo AI assist. One contract, one onboarding.
- **Native AI personalization.** Personalized emails generated per prospect rather than templated — closer to manual quality than templated outbound, faster than manual.
- **SMB-friendly UX.** Built around the rep's daily prospecting flow, not around the RevOps admin's configuration view.

## Pricing reality

Amplemarket now publishes one entry price: the **Startup** plan at $600/month, billed annually — 2 users and roughly 27,000 contacts per year included, which works out to about $300/seat/month. Everything above it is custom-quoted. **Growth** (4 users) and **Elite** (10 users) scale with seats, credits, and contact volume, and every plan requires an annual contract — there is no monthly option. Additional seats run roughly $300-400/seat/month. Independent 2026 breakdowns put a small team's real-world spend in the $1,000-2,000+/month range once credits and the platform fee are included, with Growth landing materially higher again.

## Best for

- SMB and mid-market B2B SaaS (10-100 reps) with outbound as a meaningful pipeline source.
- Lean RevOps teams that want fewer vendors to manage.
- Sales-engagement greenfield deployments — easier to start with Amplemarket than to migrate off Outreach.

## Versus the alternative

- **vs [Apollo](/en/tools/apollo/).** Apollo is the closest competitor — also platform-bundled, similar pricing tier. Pick Apollo for the deeper free-tier-on-entry path and broader prospect database. Pick Amplemarket for stronger AI-personalization quality and tighter UX.
- **vs [Outreach](/en/tools/outreach/) / [Salesloft](/en/tools/salesloft/).** Those are enterprise sales-engagement platforms with deeper RevOps governance. Pick those for 100+ rep teams with mature processes; pick Amplemarket for the SMB / mid-market segment where the platform overhead doesn't pay back.
- **vs ZoomInfo + Outreach + Lavender stitched together.** The stitched stack is deeper but more expensive and more vendor-management overhead. Pick Amplemarket when the team can't manage the stack; pick the stitched stack when the team can.
- **vs [Regie AI](/en/tools/regie-ai/).** Regie AI is similar AI-native but more focused on personalization-as-overlay. Pick Regie if you stay on Outreach / Salesloft; pick Amplemarket for full-platform consolidation.

## Watch-outs

- **Migration cost from existing platform.** Migrating off Outreach or Salesloft to Amplemarket is non-trivial — sequence templates, reporting integrations, manager dashboards all rebuild. **Guard:** evaluate migration cost honestly before switching; greenfield is much cheaper than migration.
- **Prospect-data depth shallower than ZoomInfo.** **Guard:** for enterprise data needs, layer ZoomInfo or Cognism on top; treat Amplemarket's bundled data as starter.
- **AI-personalization can drift to formula.** Like all AI-email tools, optimizing hard for reply rate produces formulaic output. **Guard:** sample-audit AI-drafted emails for voice authenticity quarterly.
- **Annual-only contracts and data-credit caps surprise.** There is no monthly plan, and email/phone credits are capped per seat per year. **Guard:** model per-credit usage under the team's actual outbound velocity, and confirm the annual commitment fits before contracting.
