## What it is

HiBob sells Bob, an HCM suite for 200–2,000-person companies that runs core HR, onboarding, performance, compensation, time and attendance, workforce planning, and — through the Bob Hiring module — applicant tracking on one employee data model. It is not an enterprise HCM in the Workday sense, and it is not a recruiting-first ATS either: Bob Hiring is built to be good enough that a company already on Bob does not need a second system, not to beat Greenhouse at structured hiring. On 1 September 2026 HiBob announced a $166M round led by Salesforce, with Farallon participating, at a valuation of about $3.2B (Bloomberg, Reworked), up from $2.7B in 2023. The pitch behind the round is that Bob becomes the source of truth AI agents read for roles, reporting lines, and permissions. The product evidence for that pitch is Slackbot HCM, launched June 2026, which answers people questions in Slack from Bob data under Bob permissions, and a hosted MCP server first released 28 April 2026, still rolling out its OAuth version.

## Why it shows up in recruiting stacks

- **The requisition starts where the headcount plan lives.** HiBob describes a data flow from workforce planning into hiring: an approved position in the plan becomes the opening, the hire fills the position, and onboarding starts from the same record. The February 2025 acquisition of FP&A platform Mosaic pushes that further toward finance. For a company whose real hiring bottleneck is getting a role approved against budget, that link is worth more than any ATS feature.
- **Bob Hiring covers the inbound basics with auditable AI.** Branded career page with referrals, posting to what HiBob counts as 2,300+ job boards, candidate self-scheduling on Google and Outlook calendars, AI-written job descriptions, CV-to-skills matching that shows the evidence behind each score, AI scorecard summaries, and a hiring assistant that answers pipeline questions in chat. Brandon Hall Group calls the design a "glass box": recruiters see the prompts, adjust the criteria weighting, and get an audit log for every AI action. That matters if your legal team has to defend AI-assisted screening under NYC LL 144 or the EU AI Act.
- **Payroll moved inside the suite.** US payroll and benefits administration went GA on 1 January 2026, running on Gusto Embedded Payroll and PlanSource; UK payroll runs on Pento, the payroll automation company HiBob announced it was acquiring in February 2024. The signed offer, the employee record, and the first payslip can now sit in one vendor in those two countries.

## Pricing reality

HiBob publishes no prices. Bob is sold as a core HR platform plus modules, each priced per employee per month across your whole headcount — Hiring, Workforce Planning, Compensation, Learning, Payroll, and Time & Attendance are line items on the quote. Vendr's transaction data puts core HR at about $5–$10 per employee per month and each add-on module at roughly $1–$4, with a typical all-in rate of $10–$15 for 50–150 employees and $8–$12 for 150–500. Across 96 deals Vendr reports a median annual contract of $46,532, a range of $14,983–$118,022, and average savings of 16.7% off the first quote. Budget a one-time implementation fee on top: Vendr's bands run $3,000–$10,000 under 150 employees and $8,000–$20,000 for 150–500.

The number that decides the recruiting question: a 400-person company adding Hiring at $3 per employee per month pays about $14,400 a year for an ATS that perhaps six people use. That is often less than a standalone mid-market ATS quote, and it is the whole case for the bundled module.

## Best for

People Ops and TA leads at 200–2,000-person companies, distributed across the US, UK, and Europe, hiring 30–150 roles a year mostly from inbound applicants, where the hard part is headcount approval and onboarding handoff rather than interview discipline. Buy Bob for the HRIS and take Bob Hiring when your process fits its stages.

Do not buy Bob Hiring if your TA team runs panel interviews with enforced rubrics across dozens of hiring managers, sources outbound at volume, or depends on a wide assessment and background-check partner network. Keep Bob as the HRIS and run a dedicated ATS beside it.

## Versus the alternatives

[Greenhouse](/en/tools/greenhouse/) and [Ashby](/en/tools/ashby/) are the standalone-ATS answer to the "bundled module or dedicated ATS?" question. Both push hired candidates into Bob natively, and Greenhouse also offers a Bob workforce planning integration that opens Greenhouse jobs from approved Bob positions — so you can keep the headcount-to-requisition link without Bob Hiring, provided you pay for the Workforce Planning module. Pick Greenhouse when structured-hiring governance and its 500+ integration partners decide the evaluation; pick Ashby when TA wants analytics and sourcing in the ATS. Pick Bob Hiring when fewer than ten people touch recruiting and the per-employee module costs less than the ATS quote.

[BambooHR](/en/tools/bamboohr/) and [Personio](/en/tools/personio/) are the closest HRIS peers in this size band. BambooHR publishes its prices ($10–$25 per employee per month) and caps its built-in ATS at 5, 25, or 50 concurrent openings by tier; pick it for a US-centric company under 300 people that wants a known price. Personio is the European default for 50–2,000 employees; pick it when your workforce is mostly DACH and EU-payroll depth matters more than US coverage.

[Rippling](/en/tools/rippling/) is the fastest-growing platform in the segment and the stronger choice when HR and IT should share one trigger — the signed offer provisions the laptop and app access. Pick Rippling for that; pick Bob when culture, engagement, and workforce planning matter more than device management.

## Watch-outs

- **Modules are billed across every employee, not every user.** Hiring, Workforce Planning, and Compensation each add a per-employee fee for the entire headcount, and Vendr notes no fixed public tiers, so two quotes for the same company can diverge by the module mix alone. Guard: ask for the quote broken out by module at your current and 12-month-forward headcount, and price Bob Hiring against a Greenhouse or Ashby quote before accepting it as a bundle line.
- **The MCP server is new and its access model changed in its first two months.** The April 2026 release worked only through a Bob service user; the OAuth version with employee-level access followed in June on a gradual rollout. A service user typically sees more than any single employee, so an AI assistant connected that way reads more than the person asking it. Guard: connect assistants through the OAuth version scoped to the requesting employee, and audit which Bob permission groups can see compensation before enabling it.
- **Payroll coverage is country-specific.** Native payroll exists for the US and UK; everywhere else Bob's Payroll Hub passes changes to an external provider such as ADP Workforce Now, Paylocity, TriNet, or Xero. Guard: map employee locations before you scope the deal, and confirm the export format to your non-US, non-UK payroll provider in the proof of concept rather than after signature.
