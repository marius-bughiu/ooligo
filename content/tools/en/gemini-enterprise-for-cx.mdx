## What it is

Gemini Enterprise for Customer Experience (Gemini Enterprise for CX) is Google Cloud's suite for customer-facing AI agents and the contact centre around them. Google launched it at NRF 2026, with the press release dated 2026-01-11, and pitched it as one agent layer for both shopping and customer service. Google partners such as UJET describe it as the new name for what was sold as the Customer Engagement Suite and Contact Center AI (CCAI). If you bought Dialogflow CX, Agent Assist or CCAI Insights before 2026, this is where those products now live.

It is a bundle of separately priced parts, not one product:

- **CX Agent Studio** builds customer-facing chat and voice agents. It is built on Google's Agent Development Kit (ADK) and is described as the evolution of Dialogflow CX. Flow-based agents remain available for teams that need fixed, scripted steps.
- **Agent Assist** gives human agents generated replies, knowledge lookups, call summaries, smart reply and live translation during the conversation.
- **CX Insights** (formerly Conversational Insights) finds call drivers and sentiment and scores conversations for quality review across all interactions, not a manual sample.
- **Google Cloud CCaaS** (the CCAI Platform in Google's docs) routes voice and digital contacts. UJET says it has been the sole OEM provider of this product since 2022, so the contact-centre layer is UJET's software under Google's brand.
- **Shopping agent**, the retail piece announced at NRF, answers product questions and, with the customer's consent, adds items to the cart and completes checkout.

Early adopters named at launch were Kroger, Lowe's (for its Mylow home-improvement advisor), Papa Johns and Woolworths (for its Olive assistant in Australia). Google was one of four Leaders in Gartner's July 2026 Magic Quadrant for Conversational AI Platforms, alongside Salesforce, SoundHound AI and [Kore.ai](/en/tools/kore-ai/).

## Pricing reality

CX Agent Studio is the one part with a public rate card, which few vendors in this category offer:

- **$0.50 per session** at list price for chat and voice, falling to about $0.45 and $0.40 per session on one-year and three-year Flexible Savings Plan commitments. You may have to opt in to get the discounted rates.
- **A chat session ends after 50 turns or 30 minutes of inactivity**, and the next turn starts a new billable session.
- **A voice session covers the first 5 minutes.** After 300 seconds, billing switches to $0.0025 per second, which is $0.15 a minute. A 10-minute call therefore costs $0.50 + $0.75 = $1.25. A session that starts in chat and moves to voice is billed as one voice session.
- **Evaluations, AI augmentation and monitoring** are included in the session price.

The rest of the bundle is quoted separately. Agent Assist, CX Insights, telephony and speech run on Google Cloud consumption billing, and the CCaaS layer is sold per agent through Google or UJET. Worked example: 20,000 automated chat conversations a month that each fit in one session cost $10,000 a month at list, or $120,000 a year, dropping to about $96,000 on a three-year commitment, before agent-assist, analytics and telephony. Third-party reviews put real deployments at low six figures a year and describe go-lives of 3–6 months for enterprise builds.

## Best for

Heads of CX or contact-centre operations at retailers and consumer brands already on Google Cloud, with BigQuery as their data warehouse and an engineering team that can build in ADK, who want customer-facing agents, agent assist and QA analytics from one cloud contract and want to pay per session rather than per resolution. It also fits Dialogflow CX customers who have to plan a migration anyway.

Skip it if your support runs on Zendesk or Intercom with no Google Cloud footprint and no engineers to spare; a help-desk-native agent goes live in weeks without a cloud contract.

## Versus the alternatives

The market-share leaders it meets most often are [Salesforce Agentforce](/en/tools/salesforce-agentforce/), the pick when Service Cloud is your system of record and you want agents governed inside it, and [Kore.ai](/en/tools/kore-ai/), the pick when your contact centre spans several CRMs and CCaaS vendors and you need FedRAMP and PCI coverage with deterministic flows. AWS shops should look at [Amazon Connect](/en/tools/amazon-connect/) first for the same reason Google shops look here.

The fastest-growing entrant is [Sierra](/en/tools/sierra/), which raised a $950M Series E in May 2026. Pick Sierra or [Decagon](/en/tools/decagon/) when you want a vendor-built agent priced on outcomes and do not want to own the build. Pick Gemini Enterprise for CX over them when you have the engineers, want the agent logic in your own cloud project, and prefer a published per-session rate you can model in advance. If you need a full CCaaS on Google AI but your team is 50–500 agents, buy it through [UJET](/en/tools/ujet/) directly.

If none fit, turn on the AI layer of your existing help desk or CCaaS for your top ten intents and come back once those flows are documented and owned.

## Watch-outs

- **Long voice calls cost more than the headline rate.** Every minute past five adds $0.15, so a 20-minute call costs $2.75. Guard: price the pilot on your real handle-time distribution, and design the agent to hand over or close by minute five where possible.
- **Dialogflow CX agents do not move across as-is.** Google's own docs say tightly coupled playbooks and flows make direct replacement difficult, and CX Agent Studio steering agents cannot transfer between Dialogflow CX agents until the session ends. Guard: migrate one use case at a time with flow-based agents as the fallback, and get the Dialogflow CX support timeline from your account team in writing.
- **Product names and pages keep moving.** The suite has been CCAI, then the Customer Engagement Suite, then Gemini Enterprise for CX, and is easy to confuse with the employee-facing Gemini Enterprise seats. Guard: list every SKU by name and billing unit in the order form so procurement compares like with like.
- **The shopping agent can complete purchases.** Checkout with customer consent is the headline feature and the biggest risk. Guard: start with cart-add only, require explicit confirmation before payment, and log every agent-initiated order for review in the first 90 days.
