## What it is

Vervoe is the skills-assessment platform built around job-simulation tests rather than traditional knowledge questions. Founded 2017, the platform's differentiator is the AI grading engine that evaluates candidate responses to open-ended simulations (writing samples, presentations, situational judgment, mock client emails) at scale — providing assessment depth that multiple-choice tests can't and at speed that human-graded simulations can't.

## Why it shows up in Recruiting stacks

- **Open-ended simulation assessments.** Customer-service candidates handle a mock customer email; sales candidates respond to a mock objection; manager candidates triage a fictional team conflict. AI grades against rubric criteria the customer defined.
- **Better fit-signal than knowledge tests.** Job simulations correlate more strongly with on-the-job performance than abstract knowledge tests for many roles — the validity argument is well-supported in the industrial-organizational psychology research.
- **Per-role customization.** Customers build their own simulations specific to their roles rather than relying on generic test libraries.

## Pricing

- **Custom only.** Per-recruiter or per-test pricing depending on packaging; effective rates from low five figures annually for SMB.
- **Volume tiers** for high-volume hiring programs.
- Implementation typically 30-60 days including initial simulation design.

## Best for

- Customer-facing roles (sales, customer success, support) where job-simulation produces stronger signal than knowledge tests
- High-volume hiring programs where AI grading replaces hours of human reviewer time
- Companies pursuing [skills-based hiring](/en/learn/skills-based-hiring/) where job-relevance of assessment matters

## Watch-outs

- AI grading quality depends on the rubric the customer provides — vague rubrics produce vague grades
- Compete with [TestGorilla](/en/tools/testgorilla/) (broader test library), [HackerRank](/en/tools/hackerrank/) (coding-specific), and increasingly with custom AI-augmented assessments built directly via [Claude](/en/tools/claude/) Skills
- Bias-audit obligations under [EU AI Act](/en/learn/eu-ai-act-for-legal-teams/), NYC Local Law 144, Illinois AVDA apply to AI-graded assessments
- Senior or specialist roles often require more nuanced human evaluation than AI grading currently delivers; pair Vervoe with structured interviews for senior hires
