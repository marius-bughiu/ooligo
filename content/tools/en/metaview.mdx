## What it is

Metaview spent six years selling interview notes. It now sells a sourcing agent, and the pricing page is the tell: every published number on metaview.ai/pricing prices one agent — Sourcing — while the Notetaker that made the company's name is quote-only. The homepage line is "The Agentic Recruiting Platform built for radically efficient hiring, end to end," and the product is five agents: Sourcing, Application Review, Screening, Notetaker, Reports. The closest structural comparison is no longer [BrightHire](/en/tools/brighthire/). It is [hireEZ](/en/tools/hireez/) — a sourcing platform with interview capture attached, rather than the reverse.

## Why it shows up in Recruiting stacks

- **The sourcing agent runs against interview data you already own.** Metaview's own pitch for the agent is rediscovery: search the ATS and the screens you have already recorded, not just the open web. No pure sourcing tool has a transcript corpus to search, because none of them recorded the interviews. This is the one thing the reposition actually buys you, and it only pays off if you were a Notetaker customer first.
- **It ships an MCP server, and Metaview is a listed Claude connector.** Claude reads transcripts, AI notes, structured fields and scorecards directly from the workspace — compare candidates across a req, pull red flags from last week's screens, check comp expectations across open roles, without exporting anything. Among interview-capture vendors that is still rare.
- **Roughly 40 ATS integrations, self-serve.** The help centre documents Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever, Gem, SmartRecruiters, Workday, SAP SuccessFactors, iCIMS, Bullhorn, Workable, Teamtailor, Recruitee, Loxo, JazzHR, Pinpoint and about 25 more, set up from the admin panel rather than through a services engagement.

## Pricing

The published ladder covers **Sourcing only**, billed per user per month, with infinite concurrent searches on every tier:

- **Free** — $0. First 100 profiles sourced.
- **Pro** — $100 per user/month. 200 profiles sourced per month.
- **Max** — $300 per user/month. Unlimited profiles sourced.
- **Enterprise** — custom. Custom volume, dedicated support and training, enterprise security controls.

Notetaker, Application Review and Screening carry no published price and are quoted per agent, so a team running two agents pays two bills per head. Reports is a flat-fee add-on with no per-report charge; assistant questions and note regenerations are included at no extra cost.

The number that decides the deal is the one that disappeared. The old Starter plan at $30 per user is gone, so the paid entry point for a Metaview seat moved from $30 to $100, and the billing unit moved from interviews recorded to profiles sourced — output of the agent, not activity of the recruiter. Read 200 profiles a month as roughly two or three live searches. A recruiter carrying six open reqs will exhaust it, which means the real ladder for anyone sourcing full-time is $300, not $100. Five recruiters on Max is $18,000 a year at list — the same figure as BrightHire's median contract (Vendr, 57 transactions, February 2026), for a different product.

## Best for

In-house TA teams of 3–25 recruiters on Greenhouse, Ashby or Lever that already have a year of screens recorded in Metaview and want sourcing to draw on them. Concretely: a TA lead who asks Claude which candidates from the last two quarters of screens fit a newly opened req, then points the sourcing agent at the pattern to find more.

Not for you if interview intelligence alone is why you are here. The Notetaker is no longer the priced product, the roadmap is pointed at sourcing, and buying a quote-only module inside someone else's strategic pivot is a weak position at renewal. Not for you either if you need programmatic write access: there is no documented REST API and no webhooks, so MCP reads and Zapier are the entire surface.

## Versus the alternatives

- **[hireEZ](/en/tools/hireez/)** — $494/month for a Solo Recruiter seat, Enterprise quoted, median contract $13,000/year. Pick hireEZ when sourcing is the whole job and you have no interview corpus to mine; its candidate database is the product, where Metaview's is a by-product.
- **[LinkedIn Recruiter](/en/tools/linkedin-recruiter/)** — the volume incumbent, roughly £8,900 per seat per year at 1–2 licences on LinkedIn's published G-Cloud rate card, with Hiring Assistant a second per-seat line. Pick it when reach into passive candidates matters more than ranking quality, and accept that the discount curve barely moves below 250 seats.
- **[Juicebox](/en/tools/juicebox/)** — the fastest-growing entrant, $99 per seat/month annually with 500 contact credits, agents at $199 each. Pick Juicebox when you want natural-language search at a tenth of LinkedIn's cost and do not need ATS write-back on day one.
- **[BrightHire](/en/tools/brighthire/)** — acquired by Zoom in December 2025. Pick it when interview quality, not sourcing, is the problem you are funding, and you can absorb the integration risk of a product now inside a much larger roadmap.

## Watch-outs

- **The published price covers one agent out of five.** Teams budget $100 a seat off the pricing page and then discover the Notetaker is a separate quote. Guard: get every agent you intend to run quoted in the same conversation, before the Free tier convinces anyone the number is settled.
- **Profiles sourced, not seats, drives the bill.** A single recruiter on a heavy req load can consume the Pro allowance in a fortnight, and the next step is a 3x price move. Guard: run two weeks on Free, count profiles returned per search, and size against Max if the number lands above 50 per req.
- **The category moved under existing customers.** An entry bought in 2024 as scorecard automation is now a line item on a sourcing platform. Guard: re-check that scorecard write-back into your ATS still covers the fields your hiring managers use before you renew — the integration list is wide, but depth per ATS is not documented and varies.
- **MCP is read-only over interview data.** It answers questions well and cannot fix a record. Guard: keep the ATS as the system of record and treat Metaview MCP as an analysis surface, not a write path.
- **Data sits in AWS UK and defaults to two-year retention.** That is the right default for a UK or EU employer and a live question for a US-regulated one. Guard: set the retention window explicitly at contract time — Metaview will change it on request — and confirm the residency answer with your privacy reviewer rather than assuming SOC 2 Type II covers it.
