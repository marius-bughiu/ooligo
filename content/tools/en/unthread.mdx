## What it is

Unthread is a helpdesk that never asks the agent to leave Slack. The ticket is the Slack thread. Triage, assignment, SLA timers, escalation, and resolution all happen in the channel where the question was asked, and the inbox is a Slack surface rather than a web app the team tabs into.

That is the pole [Pylon](/en/tools/pylon/) and [Plain](/en/tools/plain/) leave open. Both ingest Slack Connect channels well, and both then pull the agent into their own workspace to work the queue. Unthread inverts it: the customer or the employee stays in Slack, and so does the person answering. Microsoft Teams gets the same native treatment, with email, a web portal, and a customer portal as secondary channels.

The company was founded in 2021 by Tom Bachant and Jake McCloskey, went through Y Combinator's Summer 2022 batch, and raised a $3M seed. Bachant's prior company, Dashride, was acquired by Cruise in 2018. Unthread was a Startup Battlefield Top 20 finalist at TechCrunch Disrupt 2025 with a team of about 10 in New York. Named customers include Intuit, Lemonade, SpaceX, Automattic, Sisense, BigPanda, Cribl, and Hopper.

## Why it shows up in Customer Success stacks

- **The agent's context switch is the thing being removed.** No browser tab, no second inbox, no re-typing an answer already written in the thread. For a support engineer who is in Slack eight hours a day, that removes the most common reason threads never become tickets — nobody wanted to open the ticketing tool.
- **It covers internal and external support on one license.** Lemonade brought Unthread in to replace Jira for IT, then extended it to Finance, Operations, People Ops, PR, and Talent. Most Slack-native competitors pick a side; Unthread sells both from the same seat.
- **The AI resolves rather than routes.** Grounded answers from the knowledge base, confidence scoring with human fallback, auto-assigned category, priority and team, and self-learning documentation that writes KB articles out of resolved tickets. Lemonade's published outcome is 40% of tickets auto-resolved, 3x faster resolution, and 12 hours saved per engineer per week.
- **The AI meter is the seat, not the resolution.** [Zendesk](/en/tools/zendesk/) bills $1.50-$2.00 per automated resolution and [Gorgias](/en/tools/gorgias/) $0.90-$1.00. Pylon's AI Agents start at $100/mo and scale with issue volume. Unthread prices automation into the per-agent rate. At 2,000 deflected conversations a month, that is a roughly $3,000/mo line item that does not exist here.
- **First-party MCP, and an official Anthropic partner listing since April 2026.** The server sits at `https://app.unthread.io/api/mcp` with browser OAuth, documented at `docs.unthread.io`.

## Pricing

Published and self-serve, which is worth something in a segment where Pylon has pulled its rate card behind a demo. Basic starts at $50 per agent per month and Pro at $75, both with a 5-seat minimum. Enterprise is custom. Every tier carries a 14-day trial.

The real floor is $250/month on Basic and $375/month on Pro. Basic caps at 100 conversations per month, and that number decides the tier for most teams well before the feature list does. A 10-agent team on Pro pays $750/month, $9,000 a year, with automation included. The same 10 agents against Pylon's last-published $89 Professional seat start at $890/month before AI Agents and Account Intelligence, landing closer to $1,300-$2,000/month all-in. Unthread is the cheaper seat and much the cheaper meter. It is not the cheaper entry point: the 5-seat minimum means a 3-person team pays for 5.

## Best for

Support, IT, or CX leaders at 200-2,000-employee companies where the queue already lives in Slack — internal employee requests across IT, HR, Finance, and Legal, or external B2B support running through Slack Connect channels — and where the measurable cost today is threads that never became tickets. It is the wrong shape when most volume arrives by email or a web widget, when the buyer is consumer-facing, or when support reporting has to satisfy a formal ITSM audit. Those belong to Zendesk or Jira Service Management.

## Watch-outs

- **Which tier the AI sits in is ambiguous in the vendor's own material.** The pricing page lists the AI automation builder and self-learning documentation under Enterprise, while Unthread's competitive content prices AI automation as included at Pro's $75 seat. The guard: make the order form name the specific AI capabilities and the tier that carries them, and refuse "AI is included" as a contract term.
- **The 100-conversation cap on Basic is a hard ceiling.** The guard: count last quarter's Slack support threads before choosing a tier. Above 100 a month, price Pro from day one and treat the $50 headline as a number you will not pay.
- **The 40% resolution figure is one customer, and it is internal-support-shaped.** Lemonade's 40% spans IT, HR, Legal, Procurement, and Finance — policy questions with documented answers, which deflect far more readily than external product questions do. The guard: run a paid pilot with a contractual floor defined as resolved-without-human-reply, measured on your own tickets, and report internal and external segments separately so one does not subsidize the other.
- **The MCP surface is narrower than the badge suggests.** The Unthread Admin MCP exposes five read-only survey-analytics tools — `list_surveys`, `get_survey_analytics`, `list_survey_responses`, `get_outbound_survey`, and `list_outbound_survey_responses`. There is no ticket read or write. The guard: if the plan is agent-driven triage from [Claude](/en/tools/claude/), build against the REST API and scope MCP to reporting only.
- **Reporting depth is the recurring complaint in reviews.** The guard: before signing, ask for a live walkthrough that builds the three reports your board or your audit actually needs. If two of them require an export, budget a BI seat alongside the license.

## Alternatives, and when to pick them instead

[Pylon](/en/tools/pylon/) is the segment leader and the default when support spans Slack Connect, Teams, Discord, email, and in-app chat, with account intelligence syncing to Salesforce or HubSpot. Pick Pylon when the account view matters more than the agent's context switch. Pick Unthread when the reverse is true and the team refuses to work a second inbox.

[Plain](/en/tools/plain/) wins when engineering owns support. Its GraphQL API and bring-your-own-agent model let you attach your own model to the queue and build the support surface into your product. Pick Plain when you have engineers to spend and a custom surface to build; pick Unthread when you want the queue working on Monday.

ClearFeed is the fastest-growing price entrant in Slack-native support: $24 or $49 per agent per month capped at 15 agents, or usage-based from $40 to $1,200 a month, with the AI Pack billed separately at $20/month plus $2 per 10 GPT requests on starter plans. Pick ClearFeed under 15 agents on a tight budget with predictable AI volume. Pick Unthread when you want automation on the seat meter instead of a per-request bill you cannot forecast.

[Zendesk](/en/tools/zendesk/) and Jira Service Management stay right when Slack is one channel among many, when the ticket has to survive a compliance audit, or when the install already has years of macros and reporting behind it. And if your team is under 5 people, none of these is the answer yet — a Slack channel and a shared label cost less than the seat minimum.
