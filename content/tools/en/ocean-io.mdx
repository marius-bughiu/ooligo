## What it is

Ocean.io is an account-discovery engine built on an index of more than 85 million companies. You give it seed domains, usually your best customers, and it returns ranked companies that look like them, with the reasons for each match. You can then filter by size, location, industry, technologies, hiring and keywords, and reveal contacts behind each account. It sits in the same prospecting category as [Clay](/en/tools/clay/) and [Apollo](/en/tools/apollo/), but it starts from the company graph, not from the contact.

Michael Heiberg founded the company in Copenhagen in 2017. It raised €6 million in January 2022 in a round led by Peak, and its legal entity is now Ocean Global, Inc. in San Francisco. We found no acquisition, new funding or wind-down news from the past 12 months. It is independent.

What changed in 2026 is the pitch and the price. The pricing page now sells "contextual intelligence for revenue teams" and calls Ocean.io "the data and ontology layer behind your AI". The cheap credit packs are gone. The entry plan is Growth at $250 a month, billed annually. A new top plan, Revenue Navigator, reads your won and lost CRM deals and turns them into segments. The question this page answers: now that Ocean.io costs more than Clay's entry plan, who should still buy it?

## Why it shows up in RevOps stacks

- **Lookalike search is the product.** Seed it with closed-won domains and you get a ranked list with match reasons. Most contact databases add similarity on top of filters. Ocean.io builds the list from similarity first.
- **Per-action costs did not move.** Per the API docs checked on 2026-11-25, a company or people search result costs 0.2 credits, an enrichment 0.1, a domain lookup 0.05, a revealed email 1 credit and a phone number 10 credits. Exports of loaded results are free.
- **One credit pool for the app, API and MCP.** The same balance pays for the web app, the REST API and Ocean.io's MCP server, which works with Claude, ChatGPT and any MCP client on every plan, including the trial. Ocean Agent (Ocean.io Chat in the app) runs search, scoring and export from a plain-English request.
- **Segments from your own deals (Revenue Navigator only).** It reads won, lost and open deals from Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Close or Attio, shows win rate, deal size and cycle time per segment, recommends Tier 1–4 accounts and builds buying committees.
- **Wider integrations.** Beyond the CRMs it pushes contacts into Salesloft, Apollo, Instantly and Lemlist, sends Slack alerts, and is still a lookalike provider inside Clay.

## Pricing reality

As checked on 2026-11-25, ocean.io/pricing lists three plans:

- **Free trial:** trial credits, company search, lookalikes, contact enrichment, integrations, REST API and MCP. No waterfall enrichment, phone numbers or free exports.
- **Growth: from $250/month, billed annually ($3,000 a year).** 60,000 credits valid for 12 months, waterfall enrichment across 16+ sources, double email verification, direct phone numbers and email support. That works out to $0.05 per credit.
- **Revenue Navigator: custom.** Everything in Growth plus segmentation, buying committees, segment scores, rule-based owner assignment, hiring signals in workflows and a custom credit volume.

Until September 2026 the yearly minimum was 9,000 credits for $567, and pay-as-you-go cost $0.069 per credit. Per credit, Growth is cheaper. The minimum spend is 5.3 times higher, and no monthly or pay-as-you-go option remains. Accounts that started before February 2026 may still be on legacy plans, where a verified email costs 3 credits in the app and API access is a paid add-on with its own credit pool.

What you get for $3,000 depends on how much of the 60,000 credits you use:

- **Discovery only:** 1,000 lookalike accounts a month is 2,400 credits a year, 4% of the allowance. You pay $0.25 per account found.
- **Discovery plus light contacting:** 5,000 accounts, 300 emails and 30 phone numbers a month is 19,200 credits a year, 32% of the allowance.
- **Full use:** 10,000 accounts and 3,000 emails a month is exactly 60,000 credits a year.
- **Phone-heavy SDR use:** 1,000 phone numbers a month is 120,000 credits a year, twice the Growth allowance. Extra credits are not priced publicly, so this buyer goes to Revenue Navigator or to a contact database.

For comparison, Clay Launch costs $167 a month billed annually ($2,004 a year) or $185 month to month, so Ocean.io Growth costs about 1.5 times Clay's entry plan. No public contract benchmark exists, so these bands are our math from list prices. A Black Friday offer adds 30% extra credits to plans bought by December 2, 2026.

## Best for

RevOps and growth leads at B2B companies with 20 to 300 employees, a clear set of best customers, and enough volume to use most of 60,000 credits a year. They rebuild a TAM or ABM list every quarter and want their AI assistant to query the same company data through MCP. Revenue Navigator is for teams with at least a few hundred closed deals in the CRM who want segments and account tiers worked out from those deals.

**Not for you** if you only need a few thousand accounts a year: you would use under 10% of the allowance. It is also the wrong buy if you want one vendor for contact data at volume, or if you have fewer than about 10 closed-won customers in one segment, because lookalike search needs good seeds.

## Versus the alternatives

- **[ZoomInfo](/en/tools/zoominfo/)** is the largest B2B data vendor by revenue. Pick it when you need US contact depth, intent and org charts under one enterprise contract. Pick Ocean.io when the account list is the job and the budget is $3,000, not tens of thousands.
- **[Apollo](/en/tools/apollo/)** is the default low-cost database with sequencing built in. Pick it when reps need contacts and outreach in one tool. Pick Ocean.io when list quality depends on similarity to your best customers, not on filter combinations.
- **[Clay](/en/tools/clay/)** is the orchestration layer, and it already offers Ocean.io lookalikes as a built-in provider. If you use under a quarter of 60,000 credits, run Ocean.io lookalikes through Clay instead of buying Growth. Buy Ocean.io directly when you need its MCP server, workflows or Revenue Navigator segments.
- **[Sumble](/en/tools/sumble/)** is the fastest-growing entrant. It came out of stealth in October 2025 with $38.5 million. Pick it when you target accounts by what they are building: tech stack, teams, live projects. Pick Ocean.io when you target by resemblance to your customers.

If none of these fits, the problem is probably the ICP, not the tool. Write down the three attributes your best 20 customers share. If you can't, no lookalike engine will fix it. Start with [ICP definition](/en/learn/icp-ideal-customer-profile/).

## Watch-outs

- **You prepay for credits you may not use.** Growth credits last 12 months and the plan is annual. A team that searches 1,000 accounts a month uses 4% of what it paid for. *Guard:* run the free trial on one real segment, count the credits a full quarter of your process would burn, and multiply by four before you sign.
- **Contact data is the weak layer.** Ocean.io rates 4.2 to 4.4 on Capterra and Software Advice from 16 to 17 reviews, but about 3.1 on G2 from 12, and the complaints repeat: bounced emails, stale phone numbers, missing key people. *Guard:* buy accounts from Ocean.io. Before you rely on its contacts, reveal 200 and compare the bounce rate with your current provider.
- **Bad seeds give bad lists.** Ocean.io's own docs warn that poor seed domains return poor results, and a June 2026 Capterra reviewer said about 80% of the returned companies missed their ICP. *Guard:* seed with 10 to 20 closed-won domains from a single segment, pull 100 results and score them by hand before you scale.
- **Agents burn search credits.** Every result an AI agent pages through costs 0.2 credits, and the agent decides how far to page. *Guard:* cap the result size in your prompts and check the balance endpoint (free) before large runs. The API returns a 402 error and charges nothing when credits run out.
- **Legacy accounts pay different rates.** On a pre-February 2026 plan, emails cost 3 credits in the app and API credits sit in a separate pool. *Guard:* check which plan you are on before you budget from this page, and use the "Move to credits" banner in Billing if the new rates suit you.
