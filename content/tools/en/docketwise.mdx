## What it is

DocketWise is case management software for one practice area: US immigration. The core of the product is its forms engine. A client fills in a multilingual online questionnaire, and DocketWise maps the answers onto the USCIS forms a matter needs, so the same date of birth or address history is typed once instead of on every I-130, I-485 and I-765 in a family-based package. Around that sit case and priority date tracking, a client portal with document requests, calendaring, time tracking, invoicing and, on higher tiers, e-filing, e-signature and texting.

It was founded in 2016 in Austin, bought by MyCase in May 2022, and came along when AffiniPay acquired MyCase the next month. AffiniPay renamed itself 8am in August 2025, so the product is now sold as 8am DocketWise, next to [MyCase](/en/tools/mycase/), [CasePeer](/en/tools/casepeer/) and [LawPay](/en/tools/lawpay/). The vendor says more than 10,000 immigration firms run on it.

## Why it shows up in Legal Ops stacks

- **Questionnaires do the data entry.** The client answers once, in their own language, from a phone. Reverse autofill pushes corrections made on a form back into the client profile, so the next form in the package starts from the fixed value.
- **It sits next to a general practice system instead of replacing it.** DocketWise lists sync integrations with MyCase, [Clio](/en/tools/clio/) and [PracticePanther](/en/tools/practicepanther/), so a mixed-practice firm can keep billing and non-immigration matters in its main system and run the immigration team in DocketWise.
- **There is an API and a small partner directory.** The REST API is documented publicly, but access goes through your customer success manager rather than self-serve keys. Partners include [Lawmatics](/en/tools/lawmatics/), [Zapier](/en/tools/zapier/), [QuickBooks](/en/tools/quickbooks/), translation services and Visalaw.ai, which drafts petitions from documents already stored in DocketWise.

## Pricing reality

docketwise.com/pricing, checked 2026-11-25, per user per month, unlimited cases on every tier:

- **Basic: $80 or $90.** Smart Forms, case management, multilingual intakes and questionnaires, case and priority date tracking, client portal and document requests, calendaring and time tracking, unlimited storage.
- **Pro: $100 or $120.** Adds e-filing, e-signatures, an HR portal for employer clients, CRM and lead management, custom attributes, text and bulk messaging, QuickBooks, and DocketWise IQ.
- **Advanced: $120 or $140.** Adds multiple branches, user permission groups, multiple admins, priority support, tailored setup, a 5 GB file size limit, and free data migration on the annual plan only.

The page has a Yearly/Monthly toggle but does not label which price set is which. The lower set is almost certainly the annual rate. Third-party listings still quote $79 / $99 / $119 or $79 / $109 / $129, so ignore them and get the rate in your order form. A five-person firm on Pro pays about $6,000 a year billed annually, or $7,200 monthly. A ten-person firm on Advanced pays about $14,400 a year annually. Basic and Pro do not include free migration, so ask for the migration quote before you compare totals.

## Best for

- **Best for:** managing attorneys and operations leads at immigration-focused firms of roughly 2-30 users doing family-based, naturalization, humanitarian and moderate employment-based work, who want questionnaire-driven USCIS forms at a published price.
- **Not for** corporate immigration teams running high-volume H-1B and PERM programs for large employers, firms that want an AI drafting agent inside the case system today, or firms where immigration is a small side practice that MyCase or Clio already covers.

## Versus the alternatives

- **vs LollyLaw.** The closest rival for small immigration firms, with 125+ USCIS, DOJ, EOIR and FBI forms, an e-file browser extension, questionnaires in 20+ languages and built-in payments. It publishes no prices; listings report about $120 per user per month. Pick LollyLaw if built-in payment plans and prebuilt workflows matter more than a published price; pick DocketWise if you want to know the cost before the demo.
- **vs eImmigration (Cerenade).** The other large small-firm incumbent, which cites 12,000+ users. It publishes cheaper tiers ($55 / $82 / $100 per user per month billed annually) and includes API access on its top tier. Pick eImmigration when budget is the deciding factor or when your team needs self-serve API access.
- **vs INSZoom (Mitratech) and LawLogix (Equifax).** The corporate immigration incumbents, built for employer programs, I-9 and E-Verify compliance, and contract-based pricing. Pick them for corporate immigration at volume; at under 30 users serving individuals and families, DocketWise is faster to buy and run.
- **vs Visalaw.ai.** The AI-native entrant, at $220 a month for Core on an annual plan, built for drafting petitions and support letters rather than managing forms. Pick it as an add-on through the DocketWise integration, not as a replacement for the case system.
- **vs [MyCase](/en/tools/mycase/) or [Clio](/en/tools/clio/) alone.** Neither has an immigration forms engine. If you file fewer than a handful of petitions a month, keep one system and fill forms by hand; once immigration becomes a practice line, add DocketWise on top through the sync.

## Watch-outs

- **The 2026 data breach.** On 2026-04-03 DocketWise filed a notice with the Maine Attorney General after unauthorized access to login credentials for a third-party partner repository. The first filing reported 116,666 affected people; a supplemental filing raised it to 143,480. Class-action firms began investigating the same month. **Guard:** ask 8am in writing what the root cause was, which partner connections were affected, and what changed since, and turn off any partner integration you do not use.
- **The AI is an assistant, not an agent.** DocketWise IQ is a writing assistant (rewrites, summaries, English and Spanish translation) and a document assistant that extracts names and dates from passports, green cards, EAD cards and I-94s into forms. It is Pro and above, there is no MCP connector, and 8am's newer AI launches went to MyCase. **Guard:** buy DocketWise for forms and case tracking; if you need petition drafting, budget for Visalaw.ai separately and test it on one closed case before you sign.
- **The pricing page is unlabeled.** Without knowing which price is annual, a quote can differ from the page by $10-20 per user per month. **Guard:** get the per-user rate, billing term and migration fee in the order form, and screenshot the pricing page on the day you sign.
- **Extraction errors flow into filed forms.** Data pulled from a scan or a questionnaire lands in USCIS forms, where one wrong date can trigger an RFE. **Guard:** keep attorney review of every generated form, and spot-check extracted fields against the source document for the first 20 cases.
