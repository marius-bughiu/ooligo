## What it is

ChurnZero is the workflow-focused customer-success platform with strong in-app engagement (notifications, surveys, walk-throughs) bolted onto core CS-platform health scoring and CSM workflow. Mid-market positioning between Gainsight (heavier) and Vitally (more flexible). Used by CS teams that want a ready-to-go CS platform without the implementation overhead of Gainsight.

## Why it shows up in RevOps stacks

- **Plug-and-play setup.** ChurnZero implementations are typically 4-8 weeks; faster than Gainsight or Catalyst.
- **In-app engagement bundled.** Walk-throughs, in-app surveys, NPS prompts — included rather than requiring a separate [Pendo](/en/tools/pendo/) / WalkMe.
- **Mid-market pricing.** Cheaper than Gainsight; price tiers visible-ish (still custom but more transparent than competitors).

## Pricing reality

ChurnZero publishes some pricing direction but ultimately custom-quotes. Mid-market deployments (10-30 CSMs) land at $25K-$70K annually; enterprise at $80K-$200K+. Per-CSM pricing plus product-MAU pricing if using in-app engagement at scale.

## Best for

- $10-150M ARR B2B SaaS with mid-sized CS teams (5-30 CSMs).
- Teams that want CS platform + in-app engagement bundled (not separate platforms).
- Smaller CS teams whose first-time CS-platform investment needs fast time-to-value.

## Versus the alternative

- **vs [Gainsight](/en/tools/gainsight/).** Gainsight is the enterprise default; deeper, more configurable, more expensive. Pick Gainsight at 50+ CSMs; pick ChurnZero for mid-market simplicity.
- **vs [Vitally](/en/tools/vitally/).** Vitally is more flexible (notion-style customization); ChurnZero is more ready-to-go. Pick Vitally for teams that want to build their own workflows; pick ChurnZero for teams that want the workflows out-of-box.
- **vs [Catalyst](/en/tools/catalyst/).** Catalyst is stronger on renewal-management; ChurnZero is broader on in-app engagement. Pick Catalyst when renewal-forecast is the priority; pick ChurnZero when in-app engagement matters too.
- **vs [Salesforce](/en/tools/salesforce/) + spreadsheets.** Default at small CS teams (under 5 CSMs).

## Watch-outs

- **In-app engagement depends on product instrumentation.** ChurnZero's walk-throughs need product-event integration. **Guard:** confirm Segment / [Mixpanel](/en/tools/mixpanel/) / direct event-pipeline is in place before contracting; without instrumentation the in-app feature is dormant.
- **Customization ceiling lower than Vitally / Gainsight.** **Guard:** for teams with non-standard CS workflows, validate ChurnZero's flexibility against the firm's actual processes before contracting.
- **Per-MAU pricing can surprise on high-engagement products.** **Guard:** model the in-app-engagement pricing under realistic MAU before signing.
- **Acquired by Insight Partners in 2023; product-direction uncertainty.** **Guard:** ask about roadmap commitments in procurement.
