## What it is

Grain is an AI meeting recorder for customer-facing teams. It joins Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams and Webex calls as a bot, or captures bot-free through its desktop app, which also records Slack huddles and in-person meetings. Each call produces a transcript, AI notes, action items and shareable video clips. On Business, the notes write into [HubSpot](/en/tools/hubspot/) or [Salesforce](/en/tools/salesforce/) fields. Grain Intelligence Inc. makes it, and the changelog shows steady releases through October 2026.

The clip is what sets Grain apart. Grain built its name on cutting a 40-second customer quote out of an hour-long call and sending it to the product team. That workflow is still its strongest one: the [Productboard](/en/tools/productboard/) integration pushes feature requests mid-call, and feedback tools such as [Canny](/en/tools/canny/) and [Enterpret](/en/tools/enterpret/) both list Grain as a call source.

## Why customer success teams pick it

- **The voice of the customer arrives as evidence, not paraphrase.** A CSM clips the moment a champion describes the workaround, and the PM watches the clip instead of reading a summary of a summary.
- **Agent access starts on the free plan.** Grain's official MCP server shipped in June 2025, and every plan gets basic MCP plus a personal API. Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor can search meetings, pull transcripts and notes, and create clips. Since the 2026-07-15 release, the MCP server also exposes the topics Grain tracks across your meetings, and Grain is listed in ChatGPT's plugin directory.
- **Business adds the team layer.** That covers a shared workspace, Slack, HubSpot, Salesforce and Aircall sync with CRM field autofill, AI coaching and talk-time metrics, recording-consent rules, and data retention policies. On Business and Enterprise, MCP also exposes coaching feedback and HubSpot deal data.

## Pricing reality

Checked 2026-11-03 against Grain's plans documentation. The public pricing page did not render its tier cards cleanly on that date.

- **Free: $0.** One person, unlimited meetings capped at 45 minutes each, 30-day history, 5 uploads in total. AI notes, clips, Zapier, basic MCP.
- **Pro: $19 monthly, $15 annual.** Still one person, with meetings up to 4 hours and unlimited history. Adds custom prompts, note templates and custom vocabulary. No CRM, no Slack.
- **Business: $39 monthly, $29 annual per seat, 2-seat minimum.** Adds the workspace, meetings up to 8 hours, CRM and Slack sync, coaching, advanced MCP and a workspace API. Viewer seats are free, and viewers can watch, comment and clip but cannot record.
- **Enterprise: quote-only.** SAML SSO, SCIM, audit logs, 1080p recording, and a bring-your-own model on Azure, Bedrock or Vertex.

What a team actually pays is the Business line: **$696 a year for the 2-seat floor**, and $3,480 a year for a 10-CSM team on annual billing. That is more per seat than its closest peers on their own ooligo entries: [Fathom](/en/tools/fathom/) Business at $25 annual (checked 2026-08-31) and [Fireflies](/en/tools/fireflies/) Business at $19 annual (checked 2026-09-01). The premium buys free viewer seats and the clip-to-product workflow. It does not buy better transcripts.

## Best for

CS leaders and product ops teams of 3-30 people at B2B SaaS companies who need customer calls to reach the roadmap as clips, with notes landing in HubSpot or Salesforce. It is a particularly good fit when PMs, designers and executives should watch customer moments without each taking a paid seat.

**Do not use it** as a free notetaker for a team. Free and Pro are single-person plans, and Free cuts recordings at 45 minutes, which ends most QBRs early. If the job is forecast inspection or a funded coaching program, buy a revenue-intelligence platform instead.

## Versus the alternatives

- **[Fathom](/en/tools/fathom/)**: the fastest-growing entrant and the cheaper team seat. Pick Fathom when summaries in the CRM are the whole requirement and price per seat decides. Pick Grain when clips and free viewer seats for product and leadership matter more.
- **[Fireflies](/en/tools/fireflies/) and Otter.ai**: the two highest-volume notetakers by seat count. Pick Fireflies for multilingual call volume and the lowest Business seat, if your team can live with metered AI credits. Pick Otter when a searchable transcript archive is all you need.
- **[Granola](/en/tools/granola/)**: pick it for internal meetings where you would rather type your own notes and have no bot in the room. Grain's desktop capture removes the bot, but its output is still a recording, not your notes.
- **[Gong](/en/tools/gong/)**: pick it when the deliverable is deal inspection and forecast risk across a large sales org. Grain's coaching and trackers are a notetaker add-on, not a revenue-intelligence system.

## Watch-outs

- **The free tier is a trial in practice.** The 45-minute cap and 30-day history mean a CS team cannot run on it. Guard: budget Business at the 2-seat minimum from day one, and use the 14-day Business trial, which needs no card, to test CRM field mapping before you pay.
- **MCP deal tools are HubSpot-only.** The deal-listing MCP tools read HubSpot. Salesforce shops get notes autofill but no deal context in Claude or ChatGPT. Guard: if Salesforce is your system of record, test the agent queries you need during the trial, not after signing.
- **Bot-free capture needs Apple Silicon or Windows.** Intel Macs are excluded from desktop capture. Guard: inventory CSM laptops before you promise customers there will be no bot.
- **Security controls sit on Enterprise.** SSO, SCIM and audit logs are quote-only. Guard: if IT requires SSO, price the Enterprise quote, not the $29 Business seat.
- **Plan names drift across Grain's own docs.** The changelog and developer docs still say "Starter" where the pricing page says "Pro". Guard: confirm feature-by-plan in writing on the order form.
