## What it is

UKG sells HR, payroll, and workforce-management software to employers with large hourly workforces. It formed in 2020 from the merger of Ultimate Software and Kronos. It has two suites: **UKG Ready**, for roughly 200-2,000 employees, and **UKG Pro**, for roughly 750 to 25,000+ (OutSail's sizing, July 2026). Both include a recruiting module: requisitions, job posting, an applicant tracker, offers, and a handoff into onboarding and payroll. UKG is private. Hellman & Friedman controls it, Blackstone is the largest minority investor, and Jennifer Morgan, formerly co-CEO of SAP, has been CEO since July 2024. UKG says 80,000+ organizations use its software.

For a hiring team, the important change is **UKG Rapid Hire**. UKG bought Chattr, a Tampa startup that built AI hiring for hourly roles, and launched Rapid Hire on its technology at UKG Aspire on 4 November 2025. It is a mobile, text-style flow. The candidate creates no account and fills in no application form. They answer role-specific screening questions, give their availability, and book an interview in the same conversation. UKG says it automates up to 90% of the repetitive steps: posting, follow-ups, scheduling, and onboarding paperwork. UKG's quarterly release on 27 August 2026 added AI job recommendations for hard-to-fill roles and a bulk-apply step that processes up to 100 candidates at a time. The same release added Bryte Voice, which handles 12 employee self-service actions through Siri and Google Assistant.

This page answers one question: **if you already run UKG, do you still need Paradox, Fountain, or Humanly for hourly hiring?**

## Why it shows up in Recruiting stacks

- **The hire is already in payroll and scheduling.** With UKG recruiting, an accepted offer becomes an employee record in the same system that runs time, scheduling, and pay. A bolt-on conversational tool has to write that hire back through an integration. Third-party connector vendors such as Flexspring and CloudApper still sell ATS-to-UKG syncs, which shows how common that integration work is.
- **Speed-to-interview for frontline roles.** Dutchman Hospitality Group runs about 1,500 frontline workers across 20+ locations in Florida, Indiana, and Ohio and gets 50-60 applications a day. On 26 May 2026, UKG reported that DHG schedules qualified interviews in under four minutes, moves 58% of qualified applicants into interviews, and often starts new hires two to three days after the offer. These are vendor-reported numbers with no independent audit. They are still the right test to run on your own funnel.
- **One vendor and one contract.** Procurement gets one DPA, one SOC 2 report, and one renewal, not an HCM contract plus a conversational-AI contract.
- **A large partner ecosystem.** Background-check vendors ([HireRight](/en/tools/hireright/), [First Advantage](/en/tools/first-advantage/)), assessment vendors ([Criteria](/en/tools/criteria-corp/)), and contingent-workforce systems ([Beeline](/en/tools/beeline/)) all list UKG integrations. So does [Humanly](/en/tools/humanly/), a conversational-AI competitor.

## Pricing reality

UKG publishes no prices. Every deal is a quote, priced per employee per month (PEPM) across the modules you license. Recruiting is not priced as a separate product. It is one module inside the suite rate. Third-party estimates (OutSail, 24 July 2026):

- **UKG Ready:** $21-27 PEPM for core HR, payroll, and time. $25-33 PEPM for the full suite, which includes recruiting.
- **UKG Pro:** $27-35 PEPM for base HCM. $32-41 PEPM for full HCM plus workforce management.
- **Implementation:** $20,000-$100,000+ over 3-5 months for Ready. $50,000-$500,000+ over 5-12 months for Pro, usually 40-70% of the first year's software fees.

Concrete numbers: a 300-employee employer on full Ready pays about $90,000-$118,800 a year. A 1,000-employee employer on Pro pays about $384,000-$492,000 a year, before implementation. We found no public price for Rapid Hire, and UKG has not said in public whether it is bundled or sold as an add-on. Ask that question first.

**PEPM pricing is the reason to look at this at all.** Recruiting software priced per employee costs the same whether you hire 50 people a year or 5,000. A frontline employer with 100%+ annual turnover gets far more hiring per dollar from a suite module than from a standalone tool priced per hire or per conversation. Humanly's mid-market deals run about $15-50K a year on top of the HCM.

## Best for

HR and talent-acquisition leaders at hourly-heavy employers (hospitality, retail, healthcare, field services, light manufacturing) that already run UKG Pro or UKG Ready for payroll and time, where most hiring is for frontline roles and managers at each site run their own interviews. Concretely: a 20-location restaurant group already on UKG Ready that hires 40 people a week and loses candidates while managers trade texts about interview times.

**Not for you if** your hiring is mostly salaried or technical. UKG's applicant tracker is built for volume, not for structured interview loops, scorecards, or sourcing passive candidates. Pair UKG payroll with [Greenhouse](/en/tools/greenhouse/) or [iCIMS](/en/tools/icims/). It is also the wrong pick if you are not on UKG. Rapid Hire's advantage is the native handoff, and without UKG you lose it. UKG also ships no MCP server, so an AI-assistant workflow has to go through its REST API.

## Alternatives — and when to pick them instead

- **[Paradox](/en/tools/paradox/)**: the share leader in conversational hourly hiring, owned by [Workday](/en/tools/workday/) since 1 October 2025. Pick it when you run a multi-brand rollout that needs Olivia's depth in scheduling, languages, and event hiring, and you will pay a separate enterprise contract for it. On UKG, you take the integration risk of a tool whose roadmap now belongs to a competing HCM.
- **[Fountain](/en/tools/fountain/)**: the other large standalone platform. Pick it when hiring runs to thousands a month across many locations and you want applicant-flow tooling deeper than a suite module. It is priced per hire or per location, so low-volume employers overpay.
- **[Humanly](/en/tools/humanly/)**: the fastest-growing entrant. It made three acquisitions in October 2025 and raised a $25M Series B in May 2026. Pick it when you want an HCM-neutral conversational layer with a documented UKG write-back, and voice screening that Rapid Hire does not advertise.
- **[Workday](/en/tools/workday/) or [SAP SuccessFactors Recruiting](/en/tools/successfactors-recruiting/)**: only relevant when the whole HCM is up for replacement. Do not switch suites to get a better hiring chatbot.

**The rule:** if you are on UKG and most of your hires are hourly, pilot Rapid Hire on one region before you sign a bolt-on. Buy Paradox, Fountain, or Humanly only if the pilot misses a number you set in advance, such as time to interview or the share of applicants who finish.

## Watch-outs

- **Rapid Hire's licensing is not public.** UKG has not published whether Rapid Hire is in the Pro and Ready recruiting modules or is a separate SKU, or whether it is equal on both suites. **Guard:** get the SKU, price, and suite coverage written into the order form, and ask whether your current recruiting module is being replaced or kept.
- **The success numbers are vendor-reported.** The 90% automation figure, the four-minute scheduling time, and Chattr's claim of "7x the industry average" conversion all come from UKG. **Guard:** before rollout, baseline your own application-to-interview and interview-to-start rates for one region, then compare after 60 days.
- **UKG is cutting staff while it ships AI.** HR Executive reported 950 job cuts on 16 April 2026, after a February 2026 restructuring and about 2,100 cuts in July 2024. **Guard:** put support response times and named implementation resources in the contract, and confirm your implementation partner's staffing before you sign.
- **AI screening puts the legal duty on you.** Ranked or filtered applicants fall under [NYC Local Law 144](/en/learn/nyc-local-law-144/), Colorado's AI Act, and the EU AI Act's high-risk hiring category. **Guard:** request UKG's model documentation and bias-audit support in writing, and give candidates a non-AI path.
