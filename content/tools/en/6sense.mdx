## What it is

6sense is an ABM platform built around predictive intent. It identifies in-market accounts using its own proprietary intent network, scores them by buying stage (target, awareness, consideration, decision, purchase), and pipes prioritized lists into your CRM, ad platforms, and sales engagement tools.

It is the leader in the "we tell you which accounts to chase this week" category, and it competes directly with Demandbase.

## Why it shows up in RevOps stacks

- **First-party intent network.** Unlike resellers of Bombora, 6sense built its own intent graph — broadly considered the strongest in the category for predictive signal.
- **Account identification on your site.** De-anonymizes visitor traffic and ties it to the account record, even before form fill.
- **Orchestration across paid + outbound.** RevOps can trigger LinkedIn ads, sales sequences, and SDR plays based on the same intent score.

## Pricing

- **Custom only** — contracts typically start in the $60K-$120K ARR range for full ABM
- Free intent tier exists for limited use; gateway to paid
- Annual contracts; pricing scales by accounts under management and modules

## Best for

- Mid-market and enterprise B2B with long sales cycles and clear ICP
- Marketing-led ABM teams pairing paid + outbound on the same accounts
- RevOps teams who want predictive scoring instead of rules-based MQLs

## Watch-outs

- Heavy implementation; budget for an integration partner or 90 days of internal lift
- Intent quality varies wildly by industry — get a POC against your historical pipeline
- Premium pricing means SMB and PLG motions rarely justify the spend
