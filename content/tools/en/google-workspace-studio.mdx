## What it is

Google Workspace Studio is the no-code builder for AI agents and automations inside Google Workspace. You describe a task in plain language ("every Friday, ping me to update my tracker"), [Gemini](/en/tools/gemini/) drafts a flow, and the flow runs on a starter (a new email, a schedule, a Drive or Chat event, or a manual start) through a chain of steps: summarize, classify, extract, draft, reply to an email, move or copy a [Drive](/en/tools/google-drive/) file, post a Chat reply. It replaced Workspace Flows: Google renamed it and announced it on 3 December 2025, and it reached every Rapid and Scheduled Release domain by 19 March 2026.

The product changed shape in 2026. Until August, Studio could help with a task, for example by drafting an email, but could not do it on its own, for example by sending one. On **17 August 2026** Google added enterprise controls that made autonomous flows acceptable to a security team. New flows run under the user's identity with least privilege and a unique, auditable ID. An agent-access dashboard lets admins suspend all flows, or individual OAuth scopes on a single flow. Admins can also require the user to confirm any step that shares data outside the domain. On **17 September** custom starters, custom steps written in Apps Script, outbound webhooks and beta connectors for Asana, Confluence, HubSpot, Jira, Mailchimp, QuickBooks, Salesforce and Slack arrived; all of them are off by default. In November came a "Wait until" step that pauses a flow for up to 14 days, plus skills: reusable Gemini instructions in the SKILL.md format that can trigger manual or scheduled flows.

## The question that brings ops leaders here

*We are a Google Workspace company. Do we automate ops work in Studio, which we already pay for, or buy Zapier, n8n or Copilot Studio?* For work that starts and ends inside Gmail, Drive, Sheets and Chat, Studio is the default answer. There is no new vendor, no new login and no new security review, and admins control it per organizational unit or group.

The cases where it earns its place: a legal-ops flow that watches a shared contracts inbox, extracts counterparty, contract type and renewal date into a Sheet, files the attachment in the right Drive folder and pings the reviewer in Chat. A recruiting coordinator flow that summarizes each new candidate email thread and drafts a scheduling reply for a human to send. A RevOps flow that reads a weekly pipeline export landing in Drive, writes a three-line summary and posts it to the sales leadership space.

## Pricing reality

Studio has no price of its own. It is listed on every Business and Enterprise plan on Google's pricing page and is also available on Education editions. US list prices per user per month are **Business Starter $7, Standard $14 and Plus $22** on an annual commitment, or about 20% more on flexible monthly billing ($8.40, $16.80, $26.40). The **AI Expanded Access** add-on raises Studio limits; Google publishes no list price for it, and you get it through a Workspace reseller or a Google sales quote.

The real cost is the limit, not the licence:

- **25 flows per user**, counting stopped flows, and no more than 25 active flows started by new email.
- **20 steps per flow.**
- **A daily cap on runs per user, across all of that user's flows.** When a user hits it, every one of their flows pauses until the next 24-hour cycle. Google does not publish the number. Since **1 November 2026**, according to Google's AI usage limits page as cited by SuperDupr (17 October 2026), the earlier promotional access has ended and limits are enforced per person, not pooled, resetting at midnight Pacific.

So a 60-person company on Business Standard pays about $10,080 a year for Workspace and gets Studio at no added cost, but cannot predict whether a 2,000-email-a-day triage flow fits under one user's cap. Plan as if it does not.

## Best for

An ops, legal-ops or recruiting lead at a **Google Workspace company of 20-1,000 employees** who wants people to automate their own Gmail, Drive, Sheets and Chat work without buying an iPaaS. It wins outright for personal and small-team flows: triage, summarize, file, notify, draft a reply for review.

## Alternatives, and when to pick them instead

- **[Zapier](/en/tools/zapier/)**: pick it when the flow is mostly outside Google. Studio has eight beta third-party connectors against Zapier's catalogue of thousands of apps, plus a published task-based price you can forecast.
- **[n8n](/en/tools/n8n/)**: pick it for high-volume or business-critical automations that need a published execution budget, retries, versioning and self-hosting. A flow that must not stop because one employee hit a daily cap belongs here.
- **[Microsoft Copilot Studio](/en/tools/microsoft-copilot-studio/)**: the equivalent for Microsoft 365 tenants, with more than 1,400 connectors and a credit-based meter. Pick it if your mail and files live in Microsoft.
- **[Gumloop](/en/tools/gumloop/)** or **[Lindy](/en/tools/lindy/)**: pick one of these when the work is a multi-step AI agent that browses, enriches and writes to a CRM, and a Studio flow cannot hold the logic in 20 steps.

## Watch-outs

- **The daily run cap is unpublished and per user.** One busy flow can pause all of its owner's other flows for the rest of the day. **Guard:** run high-volume flows from a dedicated service user, keep each person's critical flows few, and move anything that must run every time to n8n or Zapier.
- **Flows built before 17 August 2026 keep the old model.** The least-privilege identity, audit ID and agent-access dashboard apply to newly created flows only. **Guard:** rebuild older flows that touch Drive or send email, and turn on external-sharing confirmation in the Admin console.
- **Ask a Gem steps are being retired.** New ones can no longer be added since November 2026, and existing ones stop working no sooner than 1 March 2027 for Business and Enterprise. **Guard:** inventory flows that call a Gem now and rewrite them as skills or Ask Gemini steps before March.
- **Third-party connectors and webhooks are off by default and still beta.** **Guard:** enable them for a pilot OU only, and limit webhook targets with the URL allowlist, which is available on Business Plus, Enterprise and Education Standard and Plus, not on Starter or Standard.
