## What it is

Lusha is the freemium B2B contact-data provider — phone numbers and emails for prospects, with a Chrome extension that surfaces data on LinkedIn profiles. The free tier hands out 40 credits a month, which makes Lusha the gateway tool for individual SDRs and small teams. Used by SMB and mid-market teams whose budgets don't reach ZoomInfo and where Apollo's broader product surface isn't needed.

## Why it shows up in RevOps stacks

- **Free-tier-on-entry.** Individual reps can adopt without procurement; teams pay later when usage scales.
- **Chrome-extension UX in LinkedIn.** Surfaces data inline on LinkedIn profiles where reps are already prospecting.
- **GDPR posture better than ZoomInfo.** Lusha is Israel-based with EU compliance from early; less compliance friction than ZoomInfo for EU prospects.

## Pricing reality

- **Free** — 40 credits/month, single user
- **Starter** — $49.90/user/month, or $32.45/user/month billed annually; 400 credits/month
- **Professional** — $69.90/user/month, or $45.45/user/month billed annually; 600 credits/month
- **Premium** — $399.90/user/month, or $259.95/user/month billed annually; 3,400 credits/month
- **Scale** — custom; adds SSO, API access, CRM enrichment, and intent signals

The credit math is what actually bites: an email reveal costs 1 credit, a phone number costs 10. On Professional billed annually ($45.45/month for 600 credits) that's ~$0.08 per email but ~$0.76 per direct dial — a full contact with a phone number runs $0.80-$1.30 depending on annual vs. monthly billing, not the sub-$0.25 the headline credit count implies. Still cheaper than ZoomInfo per record; data quality is generally weaker.

## Best for

- SMB sales teams (1-20 reps) optimizing on per-credit cost.
- EU-aware teams that need contact data without ZoomInfo's GDPR overhead.
- Individual SDRs prospecting on LinkedIn who want inline data without leaving the page.

## Versus the alternative

- **vs [ZoomInfo](/en/tools/zoominfo/).** ZoomInfo wins on data depth (especially US) and integration breadth; Lusha wins on cost-per-contact and EU posture. Pick ZoomInfo for enterprise-tier data quality; pick Lusha for cost-conscious SMB.
- **vs [Apollo](/en/tools/apollo/).** Apollo bundles sales engagement + data + sequencing in one platform at competitive pricing. Pick Apollo for integrated workflow; pick Lusha for data-only at lower per-credit cost.
- **vs [Cognism](/en/tools/cognism/).** Cognism is the deeper EU-first option. Pick Cognism for serious EU outbound; pick Lusha for SMB EU + US blend at lower cost.
- **vs LinkedIn Sales Navigator alone.** Sales Nav has LinkedIn data but no contact details. Pick Lusha as the contact-detail layer on top of Sales Nav.

## Watch-outs

- **Data quality variance.** Lusha's data accuracy varies by region and seniority — better on US C-suite than on EU mid-management. **Guard:** sample-test data quality on the firm's actual ICP before scaling spend.
- **Phone numbers cost 10× an email.** A 600-credit Professional plan is 600 emails — but only 60 phone numbers. The headline credit count flatters email-only workflows and overstates dialing capacity. **Guard:** if the team dials, size the plan on phone reveals (10 credits each), not the credit headline, and monitor burn weekly.
- **Free tier is for trials, not production.** 40 credits/month covers a few dozen email reveals — or four phone numbers. **Guard:** don't structure the team's prospecting workflow assuming free-tier credits; budget for a paid tier from week one.
- **Chrome-extension permissions.** Lusha's Chrome extension reads LinkedIn page content. **Guard:** review with IT / privacy counsel; some firms restrict browser extensions for this reason.
