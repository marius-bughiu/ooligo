# Recruiter review before preparing the export

- Confirm that the proposed criterion relates directly to the work. Have the hiring team review the criterion and use it consistently.
- Remove names, photos, school prestige, demographic details, contact information and irrelevant personal history from the working file. Give each retained record a stable pseudonymous ID.
- Check source permissions, exclusions and contact holds in the system of record. Mark ineligible-to-review excerpts `hold` locally. Keep the identity lookup and contact list separate from the example.
- Inspect the generated cards before passing them to an approved tool. The formatter accepts the reviewer's markers. It does not validate permission, detect bias or certify compliance.
- A recruiter records corrections and next steps using the team's own reviewed process. Missing evidence leaves a question open.
