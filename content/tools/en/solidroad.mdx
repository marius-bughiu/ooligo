## What it is

Solidroad is a training-simulation platform for customer-facing reps. An AI persona plays the customer over phone, video, chat or email; the rep handles the conversation and, in the same session, works a replica of the tool they will use on the job — a helpdesk or a CRM. Each attempt is scored against a scorecard the trainer sets, with pass marks per criterion and written feedback straight after.

**This entry used to describe a different product.** As recently as August 2026 Solidroad was sold as a QA platform that graded live support conversations, human and AI-agent alike. The site now leads with "Training simulations for sales and service teams", and neither the homepage nor the 12 help-center collections describe scoring real tickets or evaluating an AI agent. If you came here for conversation QA, go to [Zendesk QA](/en/tools/zendesk-qa/), [Level AI](/en/tools/level-ai/) or [Intryc](/en/tools/intryc/), and ask Solidroad directly whether a legacy QA contract is still supported.

Mark Hughes and Patrick Finlay, both ex-Intercom, founded it in 2023. It went through Y Combinator's Winter 2025 batch and raised a **$25M Series A on 16 April 2026** led by Hedosophia, with First Round Capital and Y Combinator following on. Ryanair, Crypto.com, ActiveCampaign, Podium, Chime and Tech Mahindra are named customers.

## Why it shows up in Customer Success stacks

- **It trains the conversation and the software together.** Most AI roleplay tools rehearse talk only. Solidroad's system simulations are working copies of your helpdesk or CRM built from screen recordings you upload (the vendor quotes one to two hours per tool), so a new agent practices finding the order, applying the refund and writing the reply in one attempt. The interactive version is in beta and switched on per workspace; the older guided, click-path version is closed to new customers.
- **The scorecard reads your own policy.** Simulations pull from Notion, Guru, Confluence and Salesforce Service Cloud knowledge, so a rep is marked against the refund rule your team wrote rather than a generic rubric.
- **It gates go-live.** Crypto.com uses it to check agents meet its quality bar before they touch live tickets. Assignments carry a pass state, and Docebo receives completion and score when a learner passes — the only LMS connector today.
- **Coverage is wide for a company this size.** Phone and video run in 39 languages with regional accents; chat and email add Persian. SSO, SCIM and audit logs are documented, which clears most procurement checklists for a BPO or a multinational support org.

## Pricing reality

**No published prices.** /pricing redirects to a demo form, and every call to action is "Get a demo". The training page's FAQ says free trials are offered, so ask for one before the first pricing call rather than after.

What is known from outside is thin. One third-party directory shows a "from $149/month" figure on a listing that also says pricing is on request — treat it as unconfirmed. The comparable that is published in our catalog: [Second Nature](/en/tools/second-nature/) is tracked near $30-40 per seat per month with a floor around $20K a year, also unconfirmed by the vendor.

Size the business case on ramp time, not seat price. Solidroad's own Series A post claims Podium halved new-hire ramp and Crypto.com cut average handling time 18% — vendor-reported, unaudited. Multiply your weekly hiring volume by the cost of each week a new agent is below target; if the trial does not move that number for one cohort, the seat price does not matter.

## Best for

Support training and enablement leads onboarding **roughly 20 or more new agents a month** — in-house teams or BPO vendors — where the slow part of ramp is the tooling as much as the talk. Also a fit for contact-center hiring screens: Ryanair reports a 50% drop in interview time using it as a pre-hire assessment.

## Do not buy it if

You need QA on live conversations or an auditor for your AI agent. The product no longer sells that; buy a QA tool.

Skip it for an outbound sales team whose ramp lives in the CRM and the call recorder. [Hyperbound](/en/tools/hyperbound/) and Second Nature connect to Gong and Salesforce for that motion; Solidroad lists neither.

## Versus the alternatives

- **Zenarate** — the contact-center incumbent for AI simulation, voice and chat plus screen interaction, custom quote. Pick it for a large regulated floor that already runs scripted certification; pick Solidroad when you want trainers building scenarios and scorecards themselves, which is the reason Tech Mahindra gives for switching from a script-bound simulator.
- **[Mindtickle](/en/tools/mindtickle/)** — the enablement suite with roleplay inside it. Pick it when you want content, readiness and roleplay on one sales-led contract; it was built for revenue teams, not ticket queues.
- **[Second Nature](/en/tools/second-nature/)** — enterprise certification roleplay with video avatars and a credit model that favours cohort bursts. Pick it for sales certification at scale.
- **[Hyperbound](/en/tools/hyperbound/)** — the fastest-growing entrant, with a real free tier. Pick it for SDR and AE practice tied to Gong and the CRM; pick Solidroad when the rep's ramp runs through a helpdesk rather than a dialer.

## Watch-outs

- **The vendor already changed its product once.** The QA product buyers were evaluating in August 2026 is no longer on the site. **Guard:** name the modules you are buying in the order form, with a notification obligation and an exit right if any is withdrawn during the term.
- **System simulations are a copy of your tool, and copies drift.** A helpdesk UI change leaves reps practicing a screen that no longer exists. **Guard:** tie each simulation to an owner and re-record after every release that touches the agent workspace; budget the vendor's one to two hours per rebuild.
- **AI scores move when the scorecard moves.** Humans can correct a simulation score, which means the pass mark is also negotiable. **Guard:** hand-score 30 attempts at launch and re-run them after each scorecard edit before using pass rates for go-live or hiring decisions.
- **No public API or MCP server.** Scores leave through learner-data export and the Docebo connector. **Guard:** make a scheduled export at attempt-ID granularity a contract deliverable if pass rates feed your workforce or HR systems.
