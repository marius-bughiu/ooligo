## What it is

Questel is a Paris-based IP software and services group that sells the whole patent and trademark lifecycle from one vendor: **Orbit Intelligence** for patent search and analytics, **Equinox** as the IP management system (Invention, Corporate, Law Firm, and Law Firm+ editions), **Markify** for trademark and design search, plus a services arm that handles renewals, filings, and translation. **Qthena** is the AI assistant layered across it — drafting, office action responses, oppositions, appeals, prior-art analysis, and claim charts. Its own boilerplate claims more than 20,000 clients and 1.5M users across 30 countries; that is a vendor figure covering every product line, not an audited count.

Ownership is private equity. Eurazeo, IK Partners, and RAISE announced their takeover in November 2020, with IK Partners reinvesting after a first purchase in 2018. Equinox arrived by acquisition in late 2022, when it served about 250 clients; by March 2025 Questel said more than 1,000 law firms and corporations run it. In March 2024 Questel bought qatent, an AI patent-drafting and search startup out of the INRIA Paris research center, with CEO Charles Besson arguing that AI had to be built in-house rather than partnered.

That last point matters, because Qthena is the exception. Qthena is built by **ipQuants AG**, a Swiss company in Schaffhausen, under what ipQuants called a non-exclusive strategic partnership announced in March 2024. Questel sells it, brands it "a Questel solution," and integrates it into Equinox. The public record shows no Questel ownership of ipQuants.

## Why it shows up in Legal Ops stacks

- **One vendor for search, docket, and renewal payments.** Equinox pulls patent data from Orbit and trademark data from Markify, runs a renewal module with batch processing, and integrates with renewal providers such as PAVIS. An IP department that buys all three closes the gap between "what we own," "what it costs," and "what the competitor filed" without a CSV in the middle.
- **Qthena is a prosecution tool that ships with live file data.** It retrieves file wrappers and official communications from the EPO, EUIPO, and USPTO, which is what lets it draft an office action response against the actual examiner's text rather than a pasted excerpt.
- **The Japan update is real, not a localization pass.** On 3 August 2026 Questel added a Japanese interface, answers generated by reference to the JPO's examination guidelines, and Japan-specific patent and trademark skills that automate routine JPO correspondence. For a portfolio with heavy Japanese filings, that narrows a gap the US-built drafting tools have not closed.
- **A named European firm committed to it.** On 25 August 2026 HGF, one of Europe's larger IP firms, announced a long-term partnership after several years of using Qthena on client work for drafting, office actions, oppositions, appeals, and prior-art analysis. That is a production reference, not a pilot.
- **Security paperwork is current.** In September 2026 Questel extended its ISO 27001 certification to cover the Equinox editions and Orbit.

## Pricing reality

Questel publishes no prices. The Qthena pricing page lists three tiers — Essential (workspace, GenAI chat skills, document editing, SSO), Advanced (adds patent-search and drawing-generation allowances and research-assistant credits), and Premium (higher allowances, up to 5,000 blank and 5,000 patent projects) — but quotes no figures for any of them. Orbit Intelligence and Equinox are quote-only. One third-party listing shows Qthena at $29/month; we could not tie that number to Qthena rather than to an unrelated product, so do not budget against it.

The usable band comes from industry analysis rather than a vendor or customer source: IP management systems in this segment run from roughly $25K a year at the entry tier to $300K a year for large, customized deployments, and the same analysis places Questel below Anaqua on price for comparable scope. Renewal services are priced on top of the software, per act and per jurisdiction, so the platform quote is never the full annual spend.

The practical consequence: get a Questel quote and an [Anaqua](/en/tools/anaqua/) or [Clarivate](/en/tools/clarivate-ipone/) quote on the same portfolio, with renewal fees itemized separately in both. That comparison is the only price discipline available in a market with no list prices.

## Best for

IP operations managers and in-house patent counsel at European-headquartered companies, or law firms prosecuting heavily before the EPO and JPO, who want patent search, the docket, renewals, and an AI drafting assistant on one contract — and who have between a few hundred and a few thousand active families, where Anaqua's implementation weight is hard to justify.

**Not for you if** you only need an AI drafting tool: a dedicated drafting product will move faster than a suite and costs less to trial. Also not for you if your portfolio is US-only and analytics-heavy; Clarivate's analytics depth and US-centric interface fit that profile better.

## Alternatives — and when to pick them instead

- **[Anaqua](/en/tools/anaqua/)** — the largest IPMS consolidator, with AQX, RightHub, PATTSY WAVE, and the Patricia platform. Pick it when the portfolio is US-weighted, above roughly 500 families, and DMS sync into iManage or NetDocuments decides the deal.
- **[Clarivate IPOne](/en/tools/clarivate-ipone/)** — the other top-two incumbent, holding Derwent data, IPfolio, and CPA Global's renewals business. Pick it when you already license Derwent, or when you want an MCP-exposed patent data layer for Claude or Copilot.
- **[PatSnap](/en/tools/patsnap/)** — the search-and-analytics challenger with published entry pricing (Eureka Pro at $100/month). Pick it when the job is R&D landscaping and freedom-to-operate, not docketing, and you want to start without a sales cycle.
- **[DeepIP](/en/tools/deepip/)** — the fastest-growing drafting entrant, $40M raised across two rounds in twelve months, working inside Microsoft Word. Pick it over Qthena when attorneys will not leave Word and you want to keep your current docketing system.
- **[Solve Intelligence](/en/tools/solve-intelligence/)** — the best-funded drafting peer at $55M, with EPO and UPC workflows. Pick it when drafting throughput is the bottleneck and a browser editor is acceptable.
- **[Tradespace](/en/tools/tradespace/)** — the AI-native IPMS entrant, $15M Series A in January 2026. Pick it over Equinox when you want drafting, portfolio, and licensing in one young platform and can accept a startup's support model.

## Watch-outs

- **Qthena is a partner's product sold under Questel's name.** ipQuants built it, ipQuants signed the HGF deal, and the 2024 partnership was announced as non-exclusive. If that relationship changes, Qthena's place in your Equinox contract changes with it. **Guard:** confirm in writing which legal entity processes your invention disclosures and draft text, get ipQuants named in the data-processing agreement, and add a clause covering service continuity if the partnership ends.
- **Two AI drafting lines live under one roof.** Questel owns qatent's drafting technology outright and resells Qthena; the public record does not say which one carries the roadmap. **Guard:** ask which engine powers each drafting feature in your order form, and make the AI entitlements a named line item with the current release attached rather than "AI features as available."
- **The suite is several acquisitions stitched together.** Equinox, Markify, qatent, and Orbit each came from a different codebase. **Guard:** in the trial, run one real matter end to end — search in Orbit, docket in Equinox, draft in Qthena — and time the handoffs. Integration claims that hold in the demo do not always hold on your data.
- **PE ownership since 2020 means a renewal-pricing conversation is coming.** **Guard:** cap annual uplift at a fixed percentage for the full initial term, and price the renewal-services fees separately so a software discount is not recovered through per-act charges.
- **Tier quotas, not seat counts, drive the Qthena bill.** Patent-search, drawing-generation, and project allowances step up by tier. **Guard:** before choosing a tier, count last year's office actions and new filings per attorney, and ask in writing what happens when a quota runs out mid-month — hard stop, overage rate, or forced upgrade.
