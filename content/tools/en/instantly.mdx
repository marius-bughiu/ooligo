## What it is

Instantly is cold-email sending infrastructure with a B2B lead database and a light CRM bolted on: unlimited email accounts, unlimited warmup, a Unibox for replies, and an optional contact database (SuperSearch) under one login. The pitch is all-in-one — you find leads, send across many mailboxes, and track replies without wiring three tools together. The category leader it most resembles is Smartlead, with lemlist as the personalization-first alternative.

## Why it shows up in RevOps stacks

- **All-in-one under one login.** Sending, a built-in lead database, and a light deal tracker sit in one product, so a small team avoids stitching Smartlead + Apollo + a CRM together for early outbound.
- **Unlimited email accounts and warmup on every tier.** You rotate sends across dozens of mailboxes to keep per-mailbox volume low, with warmup running on all of them through Instantly's shared account network — the same flat-fee scaling story as Smartlead, with no per-inbox fee.
- **Shorter ramp than infra-first tools.** The UI and the Unibox master inbox are cleaner than Smartlead's, which is why solo founders and lean teams pick it when they want to launch this week instead of wiring an API.

## Pricing reality

Sending (Outreach) lists at three tiers: Growth $47/mo (1,000 contacts, 5,000 sends), Hypergrowth $97/mo (25,000 contacts, 100,000 sends, Unibox, A/B testing, API and webhooks), and Light Speed $358/mo (100,000 contacts, 500,000 sends, SISR dedicated-IP sharding). Annual billing cuts roughly 20% — $37.60 / $77.60 / $286.30 per month. Most teams that want the volume plus the API land on Hypergrowth.

The sticker is half the story. The lead database is a separate subscription — SuperSearch runs $37.60/mo (Growth) to $177.30/mo (Hyper Credits), credit-metered — so "all-in-one" is still two line items. On top of that, domains ($10-20/year), Google or Outlook inboxes (around $6 each per month), and verification are on you. Budget sending + data + mailbox infrastructure as one all-in number, not the $47 plan price.

## Best for

- Solo founders and lean outbound teams (1-10 reps) who want sending, data, and a light deal tracker in one tool with a same-week ramp.
- GTM teams running early outbound who'd rather buy a bundled lead database than wire a separate data vendor before they know their ICP converts.

## Versus the alternatives

Smartlead is the top alternative by share. Pick Smartlead if you run an agency with many white-label client workspaces ($29/client/mo) or you want API-first sending wired into Clay and n8n; pick Instantly if you want a cleaner UI, a bundled database, and a shorter ramp, and you don't need agency white-labeling.

lemlist is the fastest-growing entrant on the personalization and multichannel angle — dynamic images, email plus LinkedIn steps. Pick lemlist when multichannel sequences and rep-level personalization matter more than bundled data or raw send volume.

If you send under ~1,000 emails/month from a single domain, skip all three. Apollo's built-in sequencer or your existing engagement tool already covers it, and a dedicated sending layer is wasted spend.

## Watch-outs

- **The 450M-lead claim is not 450M deliverable contacts.** Instantly's pricing page advertises "450M+ B2B Leads," but independent triangulation puts the verified, reachable count far lower (~160M cited across reviews). Guard: treat SuperSearch as a convenience layer for early lists, verify before sending, and keep a dedicated data vendor (ZoomInfo, Apollo) for ICP-critical accounts.
- **Published price is not real cost.** The $47-97 sending plan is one of three bills once the SuperSearch database, mailboxes, domains, and verification stack on top. Guard: model the all-in monthly cost at the mailbox and credit volume you actually plan to run before you commit.
- **Unlimited mailboxes and warmup don't fix deliverability by themselves.** Shared warmup-pool reputation has degraded across every vendor in the category. Guard: run placement tests before scaling a campaign, keep per-mailbox volume conservative (around 20-30/day), and rotate domains rather than pushing one harder.
- **White-label and agency multi-tenancy are weaker than Smartlead's.** Guard: if you manage many client accounts, price Smartlead's per-client workspace model before committing to Instantly.
