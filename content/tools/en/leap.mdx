## What it is

LEAP is practice management for small law firms that ships with the legal content those firms would otherwise buy separately: jurisdiction-specific automated forms and precedents, legal accounting, time recording and a set of AI tools, all on one per-user subscription. It was founded in Sydney in 1992, is still privately held, and says it has more than 800 staff across Australia, New Zealand, the UK, Ireland, Poland, Canada and the US, over 2,600 UK firms on the product and 71,000 legal professionals worldwide (vendor-reported). It has grown by buying local incumbents: DivorceMate in Canada (2021), WealthCounsel and ElderCounsel in the US (April 2022) and Denovo, a Scottish case-management and accounts vendor, in 2025.

The difference from [Clio](/en/tools/clio/) is what comes in the box. Clio sells a lean platform and a large app marketplace; LEAP sells a firm that opens a conveyancing, probate or family-law matter and finds the forms for that jurisdiction already loaded and filled from matter data. That is why Clio runs dedicated "switch from LEAP" pages in the UK and Australia, and why LEAP's own pricing page lists migration from Clio, MyCase, PracticePanther, Actionstep and Filevine.

## Why it shows up in Legal Ops stacks

- **Forms and precedents per matter type.** Each matter type carries its own forms library, filled from the matter's contacts and fields. For property, wills and estates, and family work this replaces a separate precedent subscription; UK firms that also want By Lawyers guides and precedents pay for them on top.
- **AI included in the subscription.** LEAP states that all of its legal AI comes at no additional cost. **LawY** answers legal research questions with citations; at the 2024 US launch it ran on GPT-4 with retrieval, and one click sends an answer to a practising lawyer with at least five years in that area for verification, typically within 4-6 hours and never more than 24 (LawNext, July 2024). **Matter AI** answers questions over a matter's own correspondence and documents and cites its sources. **Generator** drafts letters, emails and forms from the firm's past work, LEAP forms or LawY templates.
- **Shipping cadence is real.** 2025-2026 releases include a Zapier integration (September 2025), LEAP Enterprise for larger firms (October 2025), a Lawmatics intake integration (US, 2026-01-22), AI Matter Summary with a choice of AI models (February 2026), the Matter AI Layout Filler that fills form data automatically (March 2026) and a Compliance Monitor app for conflict, ID, AML and source-of-funds checks (June 2026).
- **Accounting built for local rules.** Trust and office accounting is native, with LEAP Accounting Plus (January 2026) built for the SRA and Law Society Accounts Rules in the UK, plus Xero and QuickBooks links and a Microsoft-certified Power BI connector.

## Pricing reality

LEAP publishes no prices. Every regional pricing page describes a single per-user, per-month plan with no tiers, a one-time setup fee that "may apply" for migration, training and templates, and a quote form. Third-party figures are estimates: Costbench reports buyer-reported pricing around $149 per user per month, and Clio's UK comparison page cites review sites at £117 per user per month, against Clio's own £49 (annual billing). For a 5-lawyer firm that is roughly $9,000 a year on LEAP against about $3,700 on Clio's entry tier before add-ons (estimate). The gap narrows when the firm would otherwise pay for a forms library, a research tool and an AI drafting add-on; it does not narrow if it would not.

## Best for

Solo practitioners and firms of about 2-30 fee earners in Australia, the UK, Ireland, Canada or the US doing conveyancing, wills and estates, family or general practice work, where jurisdiction-specific forms make up most of the drafting and no one in the firm wants to stitch together separate forms, research and AI subscriptions.

**Not for:** firms whose practice is mostly bespoke drafting or litigation with few standard forms; firms that want published list prices and month-to-month terms; and multi-practice firms that need configurable per-matter workflow, where [Actionstep](/en/tools/actionstep/) fits better.

## Alternatives

- **[Clio](/en/tools/clio/)** — pick it when you want published pricing, the largest integration marketplace and a same-week rollout, and you will source forms and research separately.
- **[Actionstep](/en/tools/actionstep/)** — LEAP's main rival in Australia and the UK; pick it for a 20-150 fee-earner firm that needs configurable workflow per matter type more than bundled content.
- **[Smokeball](/en/tools/smokeball/)** — pick it for a small firm that puts automatic time capture first, with a forms library alongside; it is also quote-only, on 12- or 36-month terms.
- **[MyCase](/en/tools/mycase/)** or **[PracticePanther](/en/tools/practicepanther/)** — pick one for a US small firm that wants list-price billing and does not need jurisdictional forms bundled.

## Watch-outs

- **Quote-only pricing makes comparison hard.** *Guard:* get the per-user rate, setup fee, any third-party subscriptions (By Lawyers, InfoTrack searches) and the renewal terms in one written quote, then price the forms and research tools you would drop.
- **AI answers still need checking.** In October 2024 a Melbourne lawyer was referred to the state complaints body over fabricated case citations produced with LEAP's AI tools. *Guard:* require LawY's lawyer verification or a primary-source check for any authority cited to a court, and record that check on the matter.
- **Desktop heritage.** LEAP Desktop remains the full client, and LEAP Web was only described as moving closer to feature parity in December 2025. *Guard:* demo the exact tasks your browser-only and remote users perform in LEAP Web before signing.
- **Content lock-in.** Forms filled from LEAP's library live in LEAP's format. *Guard:* before migrating, ask how matters, documents and trust ledgers export, and run a test export of one closed matter.
