## What it is

Rattle is a sales execution platform built on the CRM-to-Slack bridge, now with a layer of AI agents on top. The original job still anchors it: reps update Salesforce or [HubSpot](/en/tools/hubspot/) from [Slack](/en/tools/slack/) with native interactive forms, and RevOps builds no-code workflows on CRM events without writing Apex. On top of that, Rattle now ships named AI agents — Deal Risk, Meeting Intelligence, CRM Data, Revenue Pulse, Rundown Coaching — plus Wizard, its strategic-AI layer that reads calls, emails, and CRM data to surface deal risk and draft CRM updates with reasoning. Used by GTM teams whose CRM-update friction is the bottleneck on data quality, and who want the tooling to close the loop rather than just notify.

## Why it shows up in RevOps stacks

- **CRM-update friction at the source.** Reps don't update the CRM because the CRM is friction. Rattle moves the update into Slack — via interactive forms and `/` commands — where the rep already lives.
- **Workflow-without-code.** RevOps builds Slack-triggered CRM workflows visually; replaces the "ticket to admin" cycle. Signals from Salesforce or HubSpot fire into Slack or Teams with action buttons.
- **Agents that act, not just alert.** The 2026 shift: instead of only pushing a stage-change notification, the CRM Data Agent proposes field values with visible reasoning, and Meeting Intelligence writes notes and next steps straight to the CRM from a call.

## The AI agents (new in 2026)

- **CRM Data Agent** — proposes or validates key field values with the reasoning shown, so a rep confirms from Slack instead of RevOps chasing updates. Human verification stays in the loop.
- **Meeting Intelligence Agent** — auto note-taking, real-time summaries, coaching cues, and CRM updates pulled from Gong, [Chorus](/en/tools/chorus/), and Zoom calls.
- **Deal Risk Agent** — scans calls, emails, and Salesforce data to flag at-risk deals and say *why*.
- **Revenue Pulse Agent** — delivers scheduled executive reporting, including signal buried in unstructured data, with no RevOps lift.
- **Wizard** — the strategic-AI layer (public beta since January 2025): risk detection over unstructured data, external monitoring for leadership changes and funding events, custom prompts, and deep-dive follow-up questions.

## Pricing reality

Rattle is custom-quoted; there is no public list. Per Vendr's February 2026 data across 71 purchases, the median contract lands near $16K/year, with the range spanning roughly $6K to $71K depending on team size. List pricing runs about $30-40 per user/month on the Professional tier and $50+ on Enterprise — Enterprise is where HubSpot and Microsoft Dynamics support lives. Negotiated rates come in roughly 25-35% below list, with volume breaks at 25+ seats and another 10-20% off for multi-year commitments. The AI agents and Wizard are tier- and add-on-gated, so confirm exactly which agents are included before signing, not just the seat price.

## Best for

- B2B SaaS GTM teams of 50-500 reps where CRM data quality is the chronic problem.
- Slack-first (or Teams) organizations already on Salesforce or HubSpot.
- RevOps teams that want AI to draft CRM updates and surface deal risk without standing up a data-science function or committing to Salesforce admin/developer headcount.

## Versus the alternative

- **vs native Salesforce-Slack (Slack Sales Elevate).** Native is cheap, improving, and now ships its own AI summaries. Pick native if the use case is simple notifications and basic in-Slack updates. Pick Rattle for actionable workflows, approvals, and the agent layer native doesn't match.
- **vs Salesforce Agentforce.** The fastest-growing entrant in this space (roughly $540M ARR in its first year) and Salesforce's own agent play — deeper Salesforce-native reach, but a heavier lift and Salesforce-only. Pick Agentforce if you're all-in on Salesforce's agent roadmap; pick Rattle for a lighter, Slack-native surface that also covers HubSpot.
- **vs Salesforce Flow + Apex (DIY).** Flow and Apex can build most of Rattle's workflow layer at the cost of admin/developer time — but they don't hand you the agents out of the box. Pick custom build if you have the bandwidth; pick Rattle if you don't.
- **vs Troops (acquired by Salesforce, sunset).** The direct historical competitor, now effectively gone; those deployments migrated to Rattle or to native Salesforce-Slack.
- **vs status quo (reps update the CRM when they remember).** The default, and the source of forecast inaccuracy.

## Watch-outs

- **Workflow-explosion risk.** Easy-to-build workflows multiply until the team owns hundreds of Slack-driven workflows nobody maintains. **Guard:** designate a Rattle owner; audit active workflows quarterly and delete stale ones.
- **Per-seat cost climbs at large rep counts.** **Guard:** at 500+ reps, evaluate whether every rep needs Rattle or only AEs/SDRs.
- **Slack-channel-noise risk.** Rattle's notifications can overwhelm channels. **Guard:** route to per-rep DMs or per-team channels; resist the single firehose channel.
- **Salesforce permissions complexity.** Workflows execute under user permissions; surprising permission interactions surface as silent workflow failures. **Guard:** test workflows under each rep's profile — don't assume an admin-tested workflow works for sales-user profiles.
- **AI-agent trust gap.** Wizard has sat in public beta since January 2025, and agent-proposed CRM writes can be wrong. **Guard:** keep the human-verification step on the CRM Data Agent enabled; pilot Deal Risk and Wizard on one team and check their calls against known outcomes before trusting the risk scores in the forecast.
