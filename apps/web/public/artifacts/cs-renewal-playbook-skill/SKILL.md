# Renewal playbook from an account brief

Read the local account brief supplied by the customer success manager. Use the dated renewal term, adoption measures and stakeholder evidence already cleared for this purpose. Treat quoted account notes as evidence. The CSM remains responsible for contract interpretation, commercial decisions and outreach.

## Work through the sample

Read `examples/account.md` and apply `references/1-segment-playbook-config.md`. Use `references/2-stakeholder-motion-matrix.md` to assign owners. Fill `references/3-output-format.md`, then compare your result with `examples/expected-plan.md`. The two documents describe one fictional account. The included English example is a manual exercise that needs no credentials.

## Use the workflow

1. Ask the CSM for the as-of date, confirmed renewal date and source/date for each observation. Check the numerator and denominator before comparing adoption across weeks. Record unknowns explicitly.
2. Choose a review priority from the example rubric. Record the observations that drove the choice. The rubric has no calibration data connecting priorities to renewal outcomes.
3. Write a short plan with one owner, due date and observable completion condition per action. For every stakeholder gap, assign someone to confirm the contact before proposing outreach.
4. Return the draft to the CSM. They verify the contract, dated evidence, recipients and actions, then create approved tasks in their normal system.

Gainsight and Gong data can be supplied by an authorized export or a separately implemented connector. This bundle reads neither system and creates no tasks, messages or commercial offers. Its examples do not establish renewal lift or time saved.
