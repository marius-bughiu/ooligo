## What it is

SmartRecruiters is the modern enterprise ATS positioned as the Salesforce-of-recruiting alternative to legacy enterprise platforms like [iCIMS](/en/tools/icims/) and Taleo. Founded in 2010, it differentiates on candidate experience, an open marketplace of 600+ pre-built integrations, and a UI that doesn't feel like 2008. SmartAssistant (the AI layer) handles screening, sourcing, and candidate matching.

## Why it shows up in Recruiting stacks

- **Best-in-class candidate experience.** The career site, application flow, and candidate portal feel like consumer-grade software. Material in competitive talent markets where drop-off rates kill funnels.
- **Open marketplace of integrations.** 600+ pre-built integrations into background check, assessment, onboarding, and HR system vendors. Reduces the "but does it integrate with X?" friction that derails enterprise ATS deployments.
- **Hiring Success Methodology.** SmartRecruiters bundles a defined methodology for hiring (job design, sourcing, interviewing, decision) into the platform — useful for organizations standardizing their hiring discipline.

## Pricing

- **Custom only.** Per-hire or per-employee pricing depending on packaging; effective entry point in the mid-five figures for mid-market and into seven figures for global enterprise.
- **Module packaging.** SmartRecruiters Hire (core ATS), SmartCRM (candidate engagement), SmartAssistant (AI), and SmartGlobal (compliance) all separately licensed.
- Implementation typically 4-9 months — faster than iCIMS at comparable scale.

## Best for

- Mid-market and enterprise organizations (500-10,000 employees) prioritizing candidate experience
- Companies with consumer-brand hiring profiles where the application UX impacts brand perception
- Organizations replacing legacy enterprise ATS (Taleo, older iCIMS deployments) where the modernization mandate is explicit

## Watch-outs

- Less Workday-native than [iCIMS](/en/tools/icims/) — verify the Workday integration depth if Workday Core HR is the system of record
- AI features (SmartAssistant) are competitive but not category-leading — pair with [hireEZ](/en/tools/hireez/) or [juicebox](/en/tools/juicebox/) for specialist sourcing
- Smaller global compliance footprint than iCIMS — verify country-specific certifications if hiring across many jurisdictions
- For SMB and growth-stage companies, [Workable](/en/tools/workable/) or [Ashby](/en/tools/ashby/) is the better fit
