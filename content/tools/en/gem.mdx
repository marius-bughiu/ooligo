## What it is

Gem started as a Talent CRM sitting on LinkedIn and your ATS. It now sells two packages: **Gem + Your ATS**, which keeps Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, or Workday as the system of record, and **Gem All-in-One**, which replaces the ATS with Gem's own. Both are priced on company FTE count, not on recruiter seats — the single fact that most changes the math for anyone who priced Gem before 2026.

## What changed

Three moves since the last time this entry was written, and each one breaks an old assumption about Gem:

- **Gem publishes a price.** The startup ladder on `gem.com/pricing` lists 1–10 FTE at $130/month billed yearly, against a $270/month list rate. Companies under 30 employees get Gem All-in-One free for six months, then 50% off on the paid plan. Companies at 30–100 employees get reduced published rates with no sales call. Above 100 FTE, both packages return to quote-only.
- **Gem ships its own ATS.** All-in-One puts Gem in direct competition with [Greenhouse](/en/tools/greenhouse/) and [Ashby](/en/tools/ashby/) instead of layering on top of them. That is a different purchase with a different switching cost.
- **GeMCP.** Gem runs a hosted MCP server at `mcp.gem.com/mcp`, so Claude or ChatGPT queries pipeline data directly, scoped to whatever permissions the user already holds in Gem. Recruiters ask "who did we source for this req last year and never contact" in the assistant instead of building a report.

## Why it shows up in Recruiting/TA stacks

- **LinkedIn extension that holds up.** One-click sourcing into a real CRM with enriched contact info, sequence enrollment, and dedupe against the ATS.
- **Pipeline analytics by source.** Pass-through rates, time-to-hire, and source ROI without exporting CSVs from Greenhouse and gluing them in a sheet.
- **Three named agents, not an "AI" checkbox.** The Sourcing Agent searches 800M+ profiles and drafts outreach; the Application Review Agent scores and ranks inbound applications with written reasoning per score; the Fraud Detection Agent screens applications across six signals. Gem's own figure for the sourcing agent is that 1 in 2 surfaced candidates draws recruiter action — vendor-reported, so treat it as a direction rather than a benchmark.

## Pricing

- **Startup program (under 100 FTE)** — published. 1–10 FTE is $130/month on annual billing, $270/month at list. Under 30 employees: six months free, then 50% off. 30–100 employees: reduced rates from day one, no sales call.
- **Gem + Your ATS** — custom, quoted on FTE count.
- **Gem All-in-One** — custom, quoted on FTE count. Includes the ATS.
- Every plan splits seats into **Standard** and **AI-Powered**; the agents ride on the AI-Powered class, so a quote that looks cheap per head is worth checking for the mix.

Gem's page renders the higher startup bands behind an FTE selector, and third-party trackers put the top of that ladder near $405/month at 76–100 FTE. Only the 1–10 band is directly readable, so treat the rest as approximate until it is in a quote.

Above the startup program, Vendr's buyer guide reports a **$25,700 median annual contract across 233 purchases**, with a $7,000–$73,184 spread and about 20% average negotiated savings. A 10–20-recruiter team should budget around $25K/year and expect headcount, not recruiter count, to drive the number.

## Best for

- Heads of Talent at 30–300-person companies who want sourcing, CRM, analytics, and the ATS on one bill and are willing to move the ATS to get it
- Companies under 30 employees — six months free followed by 50% off is the cheapest credible route into a real Talent CRM at that size
- Series B to public teams running outbound at volume (50+ candidates per req per recruiter) that stay on Greenhouse, Lever, or Ashby and want CRM on top without a migration

## Watch-outs

- **FTE pricing penalizes lean recruiting teams inside large companies.** A 400-person company with two recruiters pays against 400, not 2. Guard: get the FTE band and the renewal escalator — Vendr puts it at 3–7% — written into the contract, and price the identical scope against per-seat vendors before signing.
- **All-in-One is an ATS migration wearing a pricing page.** Guard: take All-in-One only if the ATS is already being replaced this budget year. Otherwise buy Gem + Your ATS, which is the same CRM and analytics without the data migration and the interview-workflow rebuild.
- **The startup free period expires into a real contract.** Six months free, then 50% off, then list — and by month seven the pipeline history lives in Gem. Guard: confirm the post-discount FTE rate and the data-export path at signup, not at renewal.
- **Raw search depth still trails the sourcing specialists.** [SeekOut](/en/tools/seekout/) and [hireEZ](/en/tools/hireez/) go deeper on Boolean and technical profile coverage, and [Juicebox](/en/tools/juicebox/) undercuts all three on price for AI-native search. Guard: if search is the job and CRM is not, buy the specialist and skip Gem.
