## What it is

Glider AI is a skills-validation platform built for the parts of hiring where someone other than the employer screens the candidate: staffing firms, managed service providers (MSPs) running contingent-labor programs, IT services companies and large enterprises hiring at volume. It does four jobs in one candidate record: skills tests and coding simulations, AI-run interviews (video, phone and chat), AI proctoring during those sessions, and identity verification that checks the person who applied is the person who interviews and the person who shows up on day one. The logo wall leans toward services and volume employers (Infosys, Wipro, EY, Hexaware, FedEx, Emirates, Intuit, Mastercard), and the vendor claims 500+ enterprise customers. Satish Kumar, co-founder, is CEO.

Two dated events shape the buying picture:

- **ID Verify (June 11, 2025).** Glider added document, liveness and face-match checks against photo IDs from 150+ countries, with real-time monitoring for proxy interviewers, deepfakes and unauthorized AI help, and a re-check on day one. It runs alongside the existing proctoring engine.
- **Findem acquisition (announced March 19, 2026).** Findem signed a definitive agreement to buy Glider; terms were not disclosed. Every glider.ai page now carries "Glider AI is now part of Findem," the brand continues, existing customers keep the same login, and Kumar still leads Glider. Findem positions itself as "Discover" and Glider as "Assess" and "Verify." The combined AI Recruiter sells as a full suite or as single agents (voice screen, chatbot, ID Verify, interview, assessment, scheduling), and Findem says it is introducing pricing tied to hires.

## Why it shows up in Recruiting stacks

- **It lives inside staffing and VMS systems.** Glider lists connectors for [Bullhorn](/en/tools/bullhorn/), [JobDiva](/en/tools/jobdiva/) and [Ceipal](/en/tools/ceipal/) on the staffing side, [Beeline](/en/tools/beeline/) and [Magnit](/en/tools/magnit/) on the VMS side, and [Greenhouse](/en/tools/greenhouse/), [Lever](/en/tools/lever/), [iCIMS](/en/tools/icims/), [Workday](/en/tools/workday/), [SmartRecruiters](/en/tools/smartrecruiters/) and SAP SuccessFactors for corporate TA. The developer-assessment leaders are built around the corporate ATS; Glider is one of the few that a recruiter at a staffing agency can run from inside Bullhorn and hand a validated candidate to an MSP.
- **Fraud is the product, not a setting.** Proctoring, ID matching and day-one re-verification come from one vendor. That matters for remote IT contractors, where proxy interviews and fabricated identities are the documented failure ([AI interview fraud](/en/learn/ai-interview-fraud/) covers the patterns). Glider's own research claims a 92% rise in candidate fraud over pre-pandemic levels; treat that as a vendor figure.
- **Coverage beyond engineers.** The same platform tests and interviews call-center, healthcare enrollment, finance and hourly roles. Published case studies include a large e-commerce company hiring 5,000 multilingual associates and Highmark Health hiring enrollment coordinators.

## Pricing

- **List price:** none. glider.ai/pricing redirects to a demo request form. Every deal is quoted.
- **Real-world band:** third-party listings (Capterra, GetApp, ITQlick) give an entry point around **$299 per month**, which fits a small staffing desk on a limited assessment volume. An AWS Marketplace listing titled "Glider Skill Assessment" shows a 12-month contract at **$60,000**, which is a reasonable marker for an enterprise or MSP program with interviews and proctoring. Plan for a range of roughly **$3,600 to $60,000+ a year**, driven by assessment and interview volume and by which agents you switch on.
- **What to expect after the deal:** Findem's announced pricing tied to hires may replace volume-based quotes for the combined suite. Get both models quoted on the same volume before you sign.

## Best for

A staffing firm, MSP or IT services company that submits technical and non-technical contractors to clients and needs to prove skill and identity on every submission, running Bullhorn, JobDiva or Ceipal, or feeding a Beeline or Magnit program. Second fit: an enterprise TA team running high-volume or contingent hiring that already buys or is evaluating [Findem](/en/tools/findem/) for sourcing.

Not for you if you hire under roughly 50 engineers a year with no staffing layer, if you need a published price and self-serve signup this week, or if the only goal is deep developer assessment.

## Versus the alternatives

- **[HackerRank](/en/tools/hackerrank/)** — the volume leader in developer assessment. Pick it when every role is software engineering and the assessment program runs from a corporate ATS.
- **[TestGorilla](/en/tools/testgorilla/)** — the broad-library share leader for SMBs, with published plans from $75 a month. Pick it when you need cognitive and role tests across many job families on a small budget and identity fraud is not your main risk.
- **[Codility](/en/tools/codility/)** — published prices ($1,200 and $6,000 a year) and deeper engineering task design. Pick it for engineering-only hiring where question quality and auditability matter more than staffing workflow.
- **[micro1](/en/tools/micro1/)** — the fastest-growing entrant, an AI interviewer that replaces the first-round human. Pick it when interviewer hours are the constraint and you do not need VMS hand-offs.
- **[HireVue](/en/tools/hirevue/)** — pick it for enterprise video interviewing and game-based assessments with an established audit history.
- **[Persona](/en/tools/persona/)** or [CLEAR](/en/tools/clear/) on top of an existing assessment tool when identity verification is the only gap.

The rule: if your candidates move through a staffing system or a VMS before they reach the employer, Glider is the default; if they go straight into a corporate ATS and are mostly engineers, pick HackerRank or Codility.

## Watch-outs

- **The Findem integration is still settling.** Glider product names now map to Findem names (AI Recruiter to ReadySlate, ID Verify to ID Verify Agent), and pricing is moving toward per-hire. Guard: write the product names, the per-unit price and a renewal cap into the order form, and add a clause that keeps your current module set at the same price if products are renamed or bundled.
- **Coding depth trails the specialists.** The most common G2 complaint is weak testing of practical coding skill. Guard: for senior engineering roles, run one Glider assessment and one Codility or HackerRank task on the same 20 candidates in the pilot and compare pass-through against interview outcomes.
- **Autonomous interviews are high-risk AI.** Scoring candidates with an AI agent falls into the EU AI Act's high-risk employment category, triggers [NYC Local Law 144](/en/learn/nyc-local-law-144/) bias audits for New York hires, and needs consent under the [Illinois AI Video Interview Act](/en/learn/illinois-ai-video-interview-act/). Guard: get Glider's bias-audit report and human-review settings in writing, and keep a recruiter as the decision-maker on every reject.
- **Biometric checks carry their own liability.** Face matching and liveness checks collect biometric data, which Illinois BIPA and the GDPR treat strictly. Guard: confirm retention periods, the consent text candidates see, and which subprocessor performs the match before you turn on ID Verify.
- **Vendor marketing numbers are not benchmarks.** "300x faster screening" and "90% less fraud" have no published method. Guard: measure your own baseline (time to submit, client rejection rate, fraud cases found after start) for 60 days before the pilot and compare.
