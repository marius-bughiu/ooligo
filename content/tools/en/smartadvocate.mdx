## What it is

SmartAdvocate is case management software for litigation firms, and in practice for plaintiff personal injury firms first. It started inside a national plaintiff's firm, has been sold for roughly two decades, and is still independent: headquartered in Melville, New York, with a second office in Bonita Springs, Florida, and no outside investor named on its site. The case file covers intake, medical treatment and records, negotiations and settlement, with modules marketed for mass torts, workers' compensation, Social Security and veterans' claims. The vendor says more than 20,000 legal professionals use it and lists 175+ integrations.

Two things set it apart from [CASEpeer](/en/tools/casepeer/) and [Filevine](/en/tools/filevine/). First, you choose the deployment: SmartAdvocate's cloud, or a server in your own office or data centre, both browser-based. Second, the October 2026 release put a built-in AI layer, SmartIntelligence, inside the case record instead of selling it as a separate product.

## Why it shows up in Legal Ops stacks

- **It is one of the spines the PI AI vendors connect to.** EvenUp's integrations page names SmartAdvocate alongside Litify and CASEpeer, and Supio published a SmartAdvocate connector in September 2025. Supio scans for cases you flag with a "Supio case sync" tag, task or note twice a day (9 a.m. and 6 p.m. ET) and pulls new documents. The sync runs one way, SmartAdvocate to Supio; writing extracted data back is listed as "coming soon". That is why the [personal injury AI stack](/en/stacks/personal-injury-ai-stack/) lists SmartAdvocate as a spine that keeps the AI options open.
- **The intake and money edges are already wired.** [Lawmatics](/en/tools/lawmatics/) shipped a live integration on 2026-03-17 that pushes contacts, notes, documents and custom fields into SmartAdvocate when a lead signs. [Confido Legal](/en/tools/confido-legal/) handles payments and disbursements, ClaimAngel's pre-settlement funding requests went live inside the case file on 2026-06-25, and LexitasConnect brought Lexitas services such as court reporting and records retrieval into the case workflow in April 2026.
- **SmartIntelligence covers the PI document grind.** The 2026 release names demand letters, medical chronologies, document filing and classification, an Intake AI Voice Agent, an intake coach, chat with a case, and drafted email and text replies. The feature page adds a settlement value assistant, a negotiation assistant, chat across the whole caseload, and an MCP server so tools like [Claude](/en/tools/claude/) can read the firm's case data.

## Pricing reality

SmartAdvocate publishes no price list and sells by quote. Capterra, Software Advice and GetApp all show a starting price of $109 per user per month. G2 notes that the licence is not the whole bill: server licences, installation, configuration, training and data migration are charged on top, and they vary by deployment.

Budget like this: a 10-user firm on the $109 floor pays about $13,080 a year in licences, before migration and training. A 40-user firm pays about $52,320 at the floor, still well under Litify's $150-300 per user per month all-in. The on-premise option has a higher start-up cost and, per SmartAdvocate's own comparison, is cheaper over time for firms with many users. Its pages do not say whether SmartIntelligence is included in the licence or priced separately. Get that in writing.

## Best for

- **Best for:** managing partners and operations directors at plaintiff PI firms of roughly 10-200 users who want a PI case file with demand and chronology AI included, and especially firms whose IT, data-residency or client rules require the system on their own servers.
- **Not for** a 3-person firm that wants a published price and a same-week start (CASEpeer is simpler), firms already running Salesforce, or a mostly transactional or corporate practice.

## Versus the alternatives

- **vs [Litify](/en/tools/litify/).** Built on Salesforce, roughly $150-300 per user per month all-in with a 3-6 month implementation. Pick Litify when the firm already runs Salesforce or wants a large intake call centre on the same platform; pick SmartAdvocate when you want PI depth without owning a Salesforce org.
- **vs [CASEpeer](/en/tools/casepeer/).** Published prices from $90 per user per month and a faster start. Pick CASEpeer for a small firm that will buy its AI from EvenUp or Supio; pick SmartAdvocate when you want AI drafting inside the case system or an on-premise deployment.
- **vs [Filevine](/en/tools/filevine/).** The fastest-growing platform in the segment after its $400M raise in September 2025, quote-only, with its own demand and chronology AI. Neither EvenUp nor Supio publishes a Filevine connector. Pick Filevine for heavy custom workflows; pick SmartAdvocate if outside AI vendors matter.
- **vs [Clio](/en/tools/clio/).** The overall market leader by firm count, but not PI-shaped. Pick it only if PI is a minority of the practice.

## Watch-outs

- **The AI is new and partly self-described.** SmartIntelligence shipped in October 2026, and its own pages disagree on scale: one says more than 40 further AI capabilities are in development, a graphic on the same page says 10+, and user counts read 20,000 in one place and 15,458 in another. **Guard:** in the demo, run demand letter and medical chronology generation on one of your closed cases and compare the result against what EvenUp or Supio produced on the same file.
- **Supio does not write back yet.** Supio's chronologies and case economics stay in Supio until its "Data Pushback" ships, so staff re-key provider names, visit dates and bill amounts. **Guard:** decide whether SmartIntelligence or an outside AI vendor owns chronologies, and run one tool for that job, not both.
- **On-premise means you own the upgrades.** Self-hosted upgrades are scheduled with your IT team, while cloud customers are upgraded together; Supio's own docs note that some self-hosted versions lack the tags its sync relies on. **Guard:** pick cloud unless a rule forces on-premise, and if you self-host, contract for a specific upgrade cadence.
- **The quote hides the total.** Services, migration and any AI charges sit outside the $109 headline. **Guard:** ask for a three-year total cost with licences, services and AI listed separately, and compare it line by line with Litify's.
