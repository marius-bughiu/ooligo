## What it is

Reply.io is a sales engagement platform that runs email, LinkedIn, calls, SMS and WhatsApp tasks from one sequence, with a contact database, email warm-up and a shared inbox built in. On top of that sits **Jason**, an AI SDR sold as its own plan: it builds an ICP from your website and knowledge base, finds contacts in Reply's B2B database, writes and sends multichannel sequences, answers replies and books meetings. You run it in copilot mode, where a person approves drafts, or autopilot. Reply has sold outbound software for more than ten years and claims 3,000+ customer companies. We found no acquisition or shutdown on record, and it ships product updates monthly.

The 2026 releases moved it toward an "execution layer" for outbound:

- **Agentic Chat** (July 2026, all plans) takes plain-language commands for 30+ actions, such as pausing sequences under a reply-rate floor or pulling campaign stats, in the app or in Slack.
- **Pre-warmed domains** (July 2026) are bought inside Reply and come with three ready-to-send mailboxes each, so a new sending domain skips most of the multi-week warm-up.
- **AI Enrichment** (November 2026, beta) turns one research prompt into up to six custom fields per contact that you can filter on and feed to Jason.
- **MCP server and a 200+ endpoint REST API**, plus n8n, Make and Zapier modules, so agents in Claude or Cursor and your own automations can enroll contacts and read results.

## Why it shows up in RevOps stacks

- **One vendor covers the human sequencer and the AI SDR.** Teams that want reps working warm accounts and an agent working the long tail can run both against the same contact records, inbox and reporting instead of buying [Outreach](/en/tools/outreach/) seats plus a separate [11x](/en/tools/11x/) contract.
- **Prices are published and month-to-month is available.** Outreach and Salesloft quote every deal; 11x and Artisan sell annual prepaid contracts. Reply lists every tier and lets you start monthly.
- **The integration surface is wide for the price.** Native two-way sync with [HubSpot](/en/tools/hubspot/), [Salesforce](/en/tools/salesforce/), [Pipedrive](/en/tools/pipedrive/), [Close](/en/tools/close/) and Copper, plus webhooks, so a [Clay](/en/tools/clay/) or [n8n](/en/tools/n8n/) workflow can push scored leads straight into a sequence.

## Pricing reality

Checked on reply.io/pricing on 14 November 2026, list prices in USD per month:

| Plan | Annual billing | Monthly billing | What decides the price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Email Volume | from $49 per user | from $59 per user | Active contacts: per user up to 5,000, then unlimited users ($159 annual at 10,000; $459 at 50,000) |
| Multichannel | $89 per user | $99 per user | Unlimited active contacts; one LinkedIn account and calls/SMS included per user |
| Jason AI SDR Starter | $500 | $800 | 1,000 active contacts a month, 2 LinkedIn accounts, unlimited users |
| Jason AI SDR Growth | $1,500 | $2,500 | 5,000 active contacts a month, 10 LinkedIn accounts |
| Jason AI SDR Enterprise | quote | quote | 25,000 active contacts, 25 LinkedIn accounts |

An "active contact" is a unique person who receives a first-step email in the month; follow-ups to that person are unlimited. Every plan includes 50 data credits a month; more are sold in packs.

**The add-ons decide which seat plan is cheaper.** On Email Volume, LinkedIn automation is $69 per account and calls/SMS $29 per account. One rep using all three channels on Email Volume costs $49 + $69 + $29 = $147 a month on annual terms, against $89 on Multichannel. Pick Email Volume only for email-only senders or for a team that wants unlimited users at 10,000+ contacts.

**Realistic bands.** A five-rep team on Multichannel annual is about $5,340 a year. Add Jason Starter and the total is about $11,300 a year, roughly a quarter of the ~$40,000 median 11x contract. Monthly billing costs 60% more for Jason Starter ($800 vs $500) and 67% more for Growth. When we checked, a Black Friday promotion was cutting outreach plans 40% and Jason 25%; budget on list price, because those codes expire.

## Best for

RevOps leaders and sales managers at SMB and lower mid-market B2B companies (roughly 3 to 30 sellers) who want multichannel sequences for their reps and an AI SDR for top-of-funnel volume under one contract, with published prices and a monthly option.

**Not for** an enterprise sales org that needs deep Salesforce-native forecasting, deal management and governance across hundreds of seats; that is Outreach or Salesloft territory.

## Versus the alternatives

**[Outreach](/en/tools/outreach/) and [Salesloft](/en/tools/salesloft/)** hold the largest share of enterprise sales engagement and win on forecasting, deal inspection, conversation intelligence and admin controls at scale. Expect roughly $75-160 per seat on quote-only annual contracts. Pick them when you have 50+ reps and the CRO wants pipeline management in the same tool; pick Reply when you have under about 30 sellers and care more about multichannel volume and AI prospecting than forecasting.

**[Apollo](/en/tools/apollo/)** bundles a much larger contact database with sequencing. Pick Apollo when data is the main purchase and sequences are secondary; pick Reply when LinkedIn and call steps in the same sequence matter more than database depth.

**[lemlist](/en/tools/lemlist/), [Instantly](/en/tools/instantly/) and [Smartlead](/en/tools/smartlead/)** are the fast-growing cold-email-first players. Pick Instantly or Smartlead for pure high-volume email at the lowest cost per send; pick lemlist for a similar multichannel seat ($87 annual) with stronger personalization templates. Reply's edge over all three is a more complete AI SDR.

**[11x](/en/tools/11x/), [Artisan](/en/tools/artisan/) and [AiSDR](/en/tools/aisdr/)** are AI-SDR-only vendors. 11x and Artisan sell annual contracts at a $26,000-40,000 median; AiSDR starts at $250 a month. Pick a dedicated AI SDR when you have no human sequencer to replace and want the agent with the best HubSpot fit (AiSDR) or voice (11x); pick Jason when reps and agent should share one inbox and one set of sequences.

## Watch-outs

- **Jason Starter's 1,000-contact cap is about 50 new prospects a working day.** That is enough to test a segment, not to replace an SDR's full monthly volume. **Guard:** before signing, divide your monthly new-prospect target by 1,000; if it is above 2, price Growth ($1,500 annual) or keep human reps on Multichannel for the overflow.
- **Contract and refund terms are where users complain.** Third-party reviews report minimum terms and no refunds, and the G2 and Trustpilot complaints cluster on billing rather than features. Some of those write-ups come from competitors, so treat them as directional. **Guard:** get the minimum term, renewal notice period and refund policy in writing on the order form, and run the first one or two months on monthly billing even at the premium.
- **"Unlimited mailboxes" sits under a fair-usage policy.** On Email Volume, the mailbox count you actually get is tied to your active-contact limit and sending activity. **Guard:** confirm the mailbox allowance for your tier with sales before you buy domains, and keep the pre-warmed mailboxes on a gradual volume ramp; Reply itself says pre-warmed does not mean full volume from day one.
- **LinkedIn steps run on your reps' personal accounts.** Automated invites and messages carry account-restriction risk, and Jason's plans bundle LinkedIn accounts that will be automated at scale. **Guard:** keep daily invite quotas conservative for the first two weeks, never run the same LinkedIn account in a second automation tool, and keep at least one non-automated channel per sequence.
