## What it is

Salesforce is the enterprise CRM, and since the Agentforce 360 re-architecture announced at Dreamforce in October 2025 it is sold under names most ops teams still don't use out loud. Sales Cloud is marketed as Agentforce Sales, Data Cloud is Data 360, and Agentforce is the agent runtime sitting on the same record graph as everything else. The object model, the permission system, and the ecosystem did not move; the SKU names on the pricing page did, which matters when you are matching a renewal quote against last year's contract. Every serious B2B software vendor integrates with it before any other CRM. It stays the default for $50M+ ARR organizations and most public-company sales orgs.

## Why it shows up in RevOps stacks

- **Configurability.** Custom objects, workflows, validation rules, Apex triggers, Flow automation. The scoped case where alternatives run out around the fourth requirement: territory assignment across multiple business units, each with its own approval matrix, its own forecast category mapping, and a shared account hierarchy that has to roll up cleanly for the board deck.
- **Integration ecosystem.** [Outreach](/en/tools/outreach/), [Salesloft](/en/tools/salesloft/), [Gong](/en/tools/gong/), [Clay](/en/tools/clay/), [ZoomInfo](/en/tools/zoominfo/), Marketo, Pardot — each ships a deep native Salesforce integration first and everything else second.
- **Hosted MCP servers, GA since 2026-04-29.** Enterprise Edition orgs and above enable prebuilt servers for the Agentforce 360 Platform, Tableau Next, and Data 360 SQL from Setup → API Catalog → MCP Servers, then connect [Claude](/en/tools/claude/) or Cursor over OAuth with PKCE. This is the biggest practical change here since the last revision of this entry: pulling pipeline out of Salesforce for an agent no longer requires a middle tier you own and maintain. Start with the read-only `platform/sobject-reads` server in a sandbox. Depth on the agent layer itself lives on the [Salesforce Agentforce](/en/tools/salesforce-agentforce/) entry.
- **Reporting depth.** Native reports and dashboards go deep, and the data model is mature enough to feed external BI (Tableau, Looker, Hex) without building an ETL layer first.

## Pricing

Per user per month, billed annually, checked on the Sales pricing page 2026-08-11:

- **Free Suite** — $0, capped at 2 users, no custom objects
- **Starter Suite** — $25, the only edition also sold monthly
- **Pro Suite** — $100
- **Enterprise** — $175, the realistic baseline for a sales org that needs the API, Sales Engagement, and Conversation Intelligence
- **Unlimited** — $350
- **Agentforce 1 Sales** — $550, which bundles 1M Flex Credits and 2.5M Data 360 credits per org per year, unmetered employee agent usage, Slack Enterprise+, Spiff, Sales Planning, Sales Programs, Maps, and Tableau Next

Two costs sit outside that ladder. The Web Services API is a $25/user/month add-on below Enterprise, so a Pro Suite org that wants Clay or ZoomInfo writing back is actually at $125. And customer-facing agent usage is metered separately from seats — Flex Credits at $500 per 100,000 credits, or a flat $2 per conversation — and an org picks one model, not both.

Real-world band: 2-4x the per-seat list once admin headcount (roughly one admin per 50-100 users), sandboxes, integration licenses, and implementation partners are counted. A 100-seat Enterprise org lists at $210K/year and lands nearer $400-700K all-in.

## Best for

RevOps leaders at $50M+ ARR B2B companies running more than one revenue motion, where replatforming costs more than operating Salesforce does, and where an audit or regulatory requirement makes the sharing model load-bearing rather than a nice-to-have.

## Watch-outs

- **The UX is dated** next to [HubSpot](/en/tools/hubspot/) and the newer wave ([Attio](/en/tools/attio/), Folk). Reps complain, and rep complaints show up later as unlogged activity and a forecast that misses. Guard: move the rep surface off Salesforce — Gong or Slack-based deal updates writing back through the API — instead of trying to make reps enjoy the native UI.
- **Agent spend has no seat ceiling.** Flex Credits and per-conversation billing scale with usage, not headcount, so a support-adjacent agent can outrun its own business case quietly. Guard: run the first agent on the Pre-Purchase model against a fixed credit balance and watch consumption in Digital Wallet for a full quarter before moving anything to PayGo.
- **The renames break your paper trail.** A quote that says Agentforce Sales and a contract that says Sales Cloud describe the same product; a quote that says Agentforce 1 Sales at $550 is not the same as last year's Einstein 1 Sales at $500. Guard: make procurement diff SKU-by-SKU against the prior order form rather than by product name.

Alternatives and when to pick them. Under $25M ARR, [HubSpot](/en/tools/hubspot/) is the better answer — pick it until you hit the third custom object or the second business unit, at which point the configurability argument flips. Microsoft Dynamics 365 Sales is the other enterprise-scale option; pick it when the organization is already standardized on Microsoft and the E5 licensing math swallows the CRM line. [Attio](/en/tools/attio/) is the fastest-growing entrant in the segment; pick it for teams under about 50 people who want an AI-native data model and will trade ecosystem depth for time-to-first-value.
