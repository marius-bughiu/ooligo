## What it is

Sweep is a metadata layer for the systems a RevOps team administers. It connects to a [Salesforce](/en/tools/salesforce/) org (and to [HubSpot](/en/tools/hubspot/), [Snowflake](/en/tools/snowflake/), Data 360 and [ServiceNow](/en/tools/servicenow/)), builds a continuously refreshed model of the configuration — objects, fields, flows, Apex, validation rules, permissions — and puts agents on top of it. The Documentation agent writes up what each piece does. The Metadata agent answers "what breaks if I change this field?" with a dependency graph. The Permissions agent explains who can see what and why. Monitoring agents flag configuration drift. A separate "Agentic Ops" toolkit adds lead routing, matching, deduplication, scheduling and Slack/Teams alerts.

Sweep was founded in Israel in 2021 by Ido Gaver and Eran Kirshenboim and raised $46M in disclosed funding, including a $22.5M Series B led by Insight Partners in May 2025. **ServiceNow confirmed that it completed the acquisition on 1 September 2026.** Neither company published a price; press reports range from "hundreds of millions" to about $400M. As of 9 November 2026 sweep.io still sells the product standalone, still advertises a free tier, and does not mention the new owner. Named customers include Brex, Wix, Mass General Brigham, NBC Sports and SailPoint.

What it is not: a DevOps release tool, a backup product, or a data-quality engine for record values at the scale of a dedicated dedupe platform. Its strength is the configuration, not the rows.

## Pricing reality

Sweep publishes list prices, all billed annually:

- **Agentic Starter** — $2,500/month ($30,000 a year), only for companies under 300 employees. Documentation agent and visual workspace, 2 users, one Salesforce org, one sandbox.
- **Foundation** — $5,000/month ($60,000 a year). Adds the Permissions and Monitoring agents and read-write workspace; 5 users.
- **Platform** — $7,000/month ($84,000 a year). Adds Build mode, process agents, the MCP server and "Agentforce Navigator"; 10 users, 3 sandboxes.
- **Enterprise** — quote-only. Multi-org (one org included), multiple platforms, SSO, SLA.

The ops toolkit is priced per user per month on top: routing and matching $40, alerts $20, scheduling $20, dedupe $10, or $75 for the bundle. An extra sandbox is $20,000 a year. The free tier has no published limits.

Realistic first-year spend for a single Salesforce org is **$30,000 to $100,000**: Foundation plus the ops bundle for 10 users lands near $69,000, and Platform with two extra sandboxes is about $124,000. Multi-org Enterprise deals sit above that range.

## Best for

A RevOps or Salesforce platform lead at a mid-market or enterprise company with a mature org — hundreds of flows and custom fields, two or three admins, little documentation — who needs to know what a change will break before shipping it, and wants that answer available to Claude or another assistant through MCP.

**Not for you if** your org is young enough that one admin knows every flow, or if your real problem is routing leads or merging duplicates. Specialists do those jobs for less.

## Versus the alternatives

The two names most Salesforce teams already shortlist for this work are Elements.cloud for metadata documentation and LeanData for routing; the entrant to watch is Salesforce's own agent layer.

- **Elements.cloud** — pick it when you need org documentation and impact analysis and nothing else. Core plans are priced on total Salesforce users, from $6,800 a year at 100 users to $29,440 at 1,000 users, well under Sweep's Starter tier, and the vendor is not owned by a Salesforce competitor.
- **[LeanData](/en/tools/leandata/)** — pick it when routing is the job. It is the incumbent for lead-to-account matching and routing on Salesforce and goes deeper there than Sweep's $40-a-seat routing module. See [LeanData vs Chili Piper](/en/vs/leandata-vs-chili-piper/) for that decision.
- **[Openprise](/en/tools/openprise/), [Insycle](/en/tools/insycle/), [DemandTools](/en/tools/demandtools/) or [Traction Complete](/en/tools/traction-complete/)** — pick one of these when the problem is record data (duplicates, normalization, enrichment) rather than configuration.
- **[Salesforce Agentforce](/en/tools/salesforce-agentforce/)** — pick it when security will only approve agents that run inside the Salesforce trust boundary. Sweep's Platform tier sits alongside it rather than replacing it.

## Watch-outs

- **Salesforce holds an off switch.** ServiceNow lists Salesforce as a competitor. Section 9.2 of the Salesforce Partner Program Agreement lets either party terminate on notice when the other announces a deal to be acquired by its competitor, and section 9.3 gives Salesforce termination for convenience on 30 days' notice. Termination ends the AppExchange listing (still live on 5 September 2026); by the agreement's terms it does not disable a package already installed, but customers have no rights under it, and Salesforce revoked Drift's and Gainsight's OAuth tokens in 2025 after security incidents. *Guard:* export Sweep's generated documentation and dependency maps every quarter, log the Sweep connected app in your OAuth usage audit, and get ServiceNow's commitment to standalone Salesforce support in writing, with a termination-for-convenience and refund clause if access is cut.
- **The useful features sit behind the expensive tiers.** MCP, Build mode and process agents start at Platform ($84,000 a year); multi-org starts at Enterprise. *Guard:* price the tier that contains the feature in your business case, not Starter, and get sandbox count named in the order form at $20,000 per extra sandbox.
- **Build mode writes to your org.** *Guard:* restrict Build mode to sandboxes and push changes through your existing release pipeline and change review. Sweep's FAQ says anything already deployed keeps running if you cancel, so treat Sweep-built automation as code you own.
- **Your metadata now flows to a new owner.** Sweep says it does not store Salesforce record data and is SOC 2 certified, without stating the report type. *Guard:* request the current SOC 2 Type II report and re-paper the DPA with the ServiceNow entity before renewal.
