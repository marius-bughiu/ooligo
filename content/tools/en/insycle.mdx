## What it is

Insycle is a no-code workbench for fixing CRM data in bulk: finding and merging duplicates, standardizing values (country names, job titles, phone formats, capitalization), mass-updating fields, and importing CSVs that match against existing records instead of creating new ones. It connects to [HubSpot](/en/tools/hubspot/), [Salesforce](/en/tools/salesforce/), and [Pipedrive](/en/tools/pipedrive/) as CRMs, plus Intercom, Mailchimp, Marketo, Yext, and Zendesk. The New York company was founded in 2016 by CEO Yonatan Lee, reports 700+ customers (PayFit, Oyster, and Oxford Nanopore among the names it lists), holds SOC 2 Type II, and rates about 4.6-4.7 across roughly 200 G2 reviews.

The work is organized into modules (Merge Duplicates, Cleanse Data, Transform Data, Magical Import, Bulk Operations, Group & Update, Grid Edit, and Data Validation), and every operation can run once by hand, as a saved template, on a schedule, or triggered from a HubSpot workflow or Salesforce Flow. In 2026 Insycle added a Data Integrity layer to every plan: Blueprints (versioned data standards), Data Logic (applying them to records), and Observability (tracking coverage and drift).

What it is not: an enrichment vendor or a router. Insycle does not fill empty fields with third-party data, and its Assign app is a paid add-on, not a replacement for [LeanData](/en/tools/leandata/). Despite the "agentic CRM" homepage, it is not an agent either. It governs the data your agents read, and its public documentation lists no API or MCP server for an agent to call.

## Why it shows up in RevOps stacks

The CRM's native tools stop short. HubSpot's Manage duplicates tool surfaces up to 10,000 duplicate pairs on Professional hubs (30,000 on Data Hub Professional), every merge is a manual click, and HubSpot states that merged records cannot be reverted. Salesforce duplicate rules block or flag records at entry but don't bulk-merge the backlog. Insycle closes that gap: fuzzy matching on any field combination, master-record rules you set once (for example, keep the oldest record but take the most recent non-empty phone), and bulk merges in one run, logged in an Activity Tracker that exports a before/after CSV per run.

The second reason is the price model. You pay per record, not per seat, so the one ops person running hygiene and the five marketers who fix imports cost the same as one.

## Pricing

Checked 2026-09-12 on insycle.com/pricing and Insycle's pricing support article:

- **Starter**: $1 per 1,000 records per month on annual billing, one module.
- **Growth**: $1.50 per 1,000 records, two modules.
- **Professional**: $2 per 1,000 records, all modules.
- **Enterprise**: custom, for databases over 500,000 records.
- Month-to-month billing costs 20% more. Users and operations are unlimited on every plan. The free trial processes up to 500 records with all modules and no card.
- **What counts.** HubSpot contacts, companies, and deals; Salesforce accounts, contacts, leads, and opportunities. Tickets and custom objects are optional, and every connected CRM adds its records to the total.
- **What it costs in practice.** A 100,000-record HubSpot portal costs $100 a month on Starter, $150 on Growth, and $200 on Professional, or $1,200-$2,400 a year. Growth is the realistic floor, because dedupe without standardization fixes only half the problem. The optional Assign and Associate apps add $0.50-$1 per 1,000 records, and certified-partner onboarding is $1,000 one time.

## Best for

- A RevOps or marketing-ops owner at a 10-200 person company on HubSpot or Salesforce with 25,000-500,000 records, a duplicate backlog from years of imports and form fills, and no budget for an enterprise data platform
- Teams merging two CRM instances after an acquisition, or moving from HubSpot to Salesforce, who need to match and normalize records before the cutover
- Not for Salesforce orgs over 500,000 records with a dedicated admin team (buy DemandTools or Openprise); not for teams whose real problem is empty fields (buy enrichment, such as [FullEnrich](/en/tools/fullenrich/)); and not for a 5,000-contact portal, where HubSpot's native tool and an afternoon cover it

## Alternatives — and when to pick them instead

- **HubSpot native duplicate management**: the default most portals already own. Pick it when the backlog fits within 10,000 pairs and email and domain matching find most duplicates. Pick Insycle when you need fuzzy matching, bulk merges, or a way back from a bad merge.
- **Validity DemandTools**: the long-standing Salesforce data-quality suite, priced per Salesforce user (about $10 per user per month on annual billing, per public listings). Pick DemandTools for large Salesforce orgs with an admin who wants the deepest dedupe and lead-to-account matching. Pick Insycle when the org is HubSpot-first, or when per-record pricing beats per-user pricing: a 50-seat org with 80,000 records pays about $6,000 a year for DemandTools and $1,440 for Insycle Growth.
- **Openprise**: a RevOps data orchestration platform (dedupe, normalization, enrichment, and routing across systems), with third-party reviews citing a $35,000-a-year floor. Pick it when data quality spans marketing automation, the CRM, and the warehouse, and the budget exists. [ZoomInfo](/en/tools/zoominfo/) Operations (formerly RingLead) plays the same role for ZoomInfo customers.
- **Dedupely**: dedupe only, $40 a month for up to 30,000 records. Pick it when duplicates are the whole problem and the portal is small.
- **[Traction Complete](/en/tools/traction-complete/)**: Salesforce-native matching and account hierarchies. Pick it when the data must never leave the Salesforce org.
- **An agent working against the CRM API**: the newest option in the category. The [HubSpot agent CLI cleanup skill](/en/workflows/hubspot-agent-cli-crm-cleanup-skill/) runs dedupe and standardization from Claude Code for the cost of tokens. Pick it when the hygiene owner works in a terminal; pick Insycle when they don't, and when you need the audit trail.

## Watch-outs

- **Merges are only partly reversible.** Insycle can recreate merged HubSpot records and strip the added emails from the master, but notes, emails, and tasks moved to the master stay there, and restored records get new IDs. Guard: before any bulk merge, run the same template on a 50-pair filtered sample, review the masters by hand, and keep the run's CSV export.
- **The bill follows your record count.** Record growth mid-term is billed each quarter, prorated, so a purchased-list import or a second connected CRM raises it. Guard: delete or archive dead records before you sign (the price drops when records are removed), and connect only the objects you clean.
- **Rules take time to learn.** G2 reviewers name a steep learning curve and rule-building that non-technical users struggle with. Guard: assign one named owner, start from Insycle's prebuilt templates, and budget the $1,000 partner onboarding if nobody on the team has written a merge rule before.
- **Bulk operations are slow.** Insycle's FAQ puts a simple update at about 200 records a minute, so a 100,000-record bulk change runs for more than 8 hours. Guard: schedule large runs overnight, and pause any integration or workflow that writes to the same fields while the run is going.

For the operating model around the tool (who owns hygiene, which fields to standardize first, how often to run it), see [CRM hygiene](/en/learn/crm-hygiene/).
