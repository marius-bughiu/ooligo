## What it is

Pillar was an independent interview-intelligence startup until Employ Inc. bought it on 5 March 2025. Employ owns three applicant tracking systems, [Lever](/en/tools/lever/), [Jobvite](/en/tools/jobvite/) and [JazzHR](/en/tools/jazzhr/), and the product now ships as **Employ's AI Interview Companion (formerly Pillar)**. The Pillar brand survives only in that parenthesis. It went live in Lever and Jobvite with Employ's Spring '25 release in June 2025, the first of three Employ AI Companions. The other two are a Screening Companion launched in November 2025 and a Sourcing Companion.

The product joins the video call, records and transcribes it, serves the interviewer skills-based questions while the interview runs, collects feedback in the moment, and writes a summary back to the ATS. After the call it flags coachable moments for the interviewer, tracks candidate sentiment and talk time, and lets the hiring team compare candidates side by side with video clips. Employ's own page sets the limit: it "does not provide candidate scoring or hiring recommendations". The category shape is [BrightHire](/en/tools/brighthire/) with the live interviewer prompt pushed to the front.

Ownership at Employ changed in 2026. Jerry Jao became CEO on 18 February 2026, followed by a new CTO, CRO and CFO by mid-March. No 2026 release from Employ mentions the Interview Companion. The company's 2026 announcements cover screening (the VONQ partnership) and candidate-fraud checks (ID.me).

## Why it shows up in recruiting stacks

- **Scorecard completion is the problem it targets.** Recruiters usually take notes. Hiring managers usually do not submit structured feedback after a panel. Capturing feedback during the interview, not in a reminder email two days later, is the mechanism behind Employ's claim of 90%+ scorecard completion.
- **Questions arrive during the interview, not in a prep doc.** Competency-mapped prompts on screen keep an interviewer who runs a loop once a quarter on the same criteria as the one who runs it weekly. That consistency is what [structured interviewing](/en/learn/structured-interviewing/) needs and what most panels lose.
- **It sits inside the Employ ATS.** On Lever or Jobvite it is configured in the platform you already run, with no separate login. Employ also lists bi-directional integrations with Greenhouse, Ashby, Workday, iCIMS, SmartRecruiters and ten other ATSs, plus Zoom, Google Meet and Teams and three calendars.

## Pricing reality

There is no published price. Jobvite's page for the companion carries its own "Request Pricing" button, separate from the ATS quote, so treat it as a priced add-on rather than an included feature. Expect the number to arrive as a line inside an Employ ATS renewal. That is also where you can negotiate hardest: ask for it to be bundled into a multi-year ATS commit.

For a reference band, Vendr's [BrightHire data](https://www.vendr.com/marketplace/brighthire) (60 purchases, updated February 2026) puts the median at $18,350 a year, with a low of $5,500 and a high of $46,083. BrightHire is no longer an independent vendor: Zoom acquired it in December 2025. [Metaview](/en/tools/metaview/) publishes its interview Notetaker at free for 25 calls a month and $60 per user per month for Pro. Ten Pro seats come to $7,200 a year at list. Its $100 and $300 tiers are the Sourcing product, not interview notes. If Employ's quote for the companion runs above the Metaview list price for the same seat count, you are paying for convenience inside the ATS. That can be worth it. Make sure it is a decision, not a default.

## Best for

Talent teams of roughly 5 to 50 recruiters already on Lever or Jobvite, whose failure mode is inconsistent or missing hiring-manager feedback rather than a lack of recordings. It fits best where hiring managers interview occasionally and need the prompt in front of them.

Do not buy it if you want an independent analytics layer you can carry across an ATS migration. Your recordings, summaries and coaching history live with Employ. Do not buy it for high-volume hourly hiring either. A conversational screener such as [Paradox](/en/tools/paradox/) is the right shape there. If you are not an Employ customer, the integrations page suggests it can connect to your ATS, but nothing Employ publishes says it is sold on its own. Confirm that in writing before you evaluate it.

## Versus the alternatives

[BrightHire](/en/tools/brighthire/), now a standalone brand inside Zoom, is the category's established leader for interviewer coaching and hiring-quality analytics. Its pitch is that Teams and Meet support stay, and that no Zoom license is required. Pick BrightHire when the analytics are the reason you are buying and you want them independent of your ATS vendor. Pick the Employ companion when you are on Lever or Jobvite and want one contract.

[Metaview](/en/tools/metaview/) is the fastest-growing independent option. It publishes prices, has an MCP server, and covers Ashby, Greenhouse, Lever and others. Pick Metaview when note quality and scorecard autofill are the job and you want to start without a sales call. Pick the Employ companion when the live in-interview prompt is the fix you need.

[Lavalier](/en/tools/textio/), launched by Textio in March 2026, is the newest entrant on the same live-guidance model, mapping interview evidence to a skills matrix. Shortlist it if you want live guidance without tying the tool to your ATS vendor.

## Watch-outs

- **The roadmap belongs to Employ's priorities.** Since acquisition, Employ's announcements have gone to screening, sourcing and fraud checks, and in 2026 the company replaced its CEO, CTO, CRO and CFO. The Interview Companion has had no dedicated release since June 2025. *Guard:* before you accept it as a reason to stay on an Employ ATS, get the next 12 months of companion features in writing, plus a price hold at renewal.
- **Data stays with the ATS vendor.** If you leave Lever or Jobvite, the coaching history and recordings do not move to the next system in any documented format. *Guard:* put an export clause for transcripts, summaries and recordings in the contract, and run one test export during the pilot.
- **The metrics are Employ's own.** The 90%+ scorecard completion, 12 minutes saved per interview and 32% lower first-year attrition are vendor-reported, and none of them is independently audited. *Guard:* pilot on two live requisitions and measure scorecard completion against your own prior quarter before you sign.
- **No MCP server or public API for the companion.** Employ documents neither. *Guard:* if an agent needs to read interview evidence, plan to pull it from the ATS record the companion writes to, and test that path during the pilot.
