## What it is

Crelate is a combined ATS, recruiting CRM and business-development tracker for agency desks — executive search, retained and contingent perm, and smaller staffing and consulting firms. It has been selling the same full-desk idea since 2012: candidates, client contacts, job orders, BD opportunities and placements in one record set, with client portals and a billing and invoicing module on top. The company is Series A, headquartered in Eagle, Idaho, run by CEO Aaron Elder, and claims 1,500+ recruiting teams and 25,000+ users. Tracxn put headcount at 52 in May 2026. It is independent; we found no acquisition or shutdown news in the last 12 months.

The AI layer is newer than the product. Crelate now markets a "Living Platform" with two agents: Discover Agent (launched July 2025), a conversational sourcing search the vendor says spans up to 1 billion people and 20 million companies, and Insights Agent (generally available 18 November 2025), which watches recruiter activity, refreshes stale records and surfaces hiring signals. A generative Co-Pilot sits inside records.

## Why it shows up in Recruiting stacks

- **BD and delivery share one pipeline.** A search firm tracks the client opportunity, the retained search and the candidate slate in the same system, so the report that answers "what is this client worth to us" does not need a second CRM. That is the core reason it lands on the Bullhorn-alternative shortlist next to [Loxo](/en/tools/loxo/), [Recruiterflow](/en/tools/recruiterflow/) and [Recruit CRM](/en/tools/recruit-crm/).
- **Reporting ships on the mid tier.** Reports and analytics, client portals and team portals are included in Business at $119 per user per month, the first plan without a user cap. For a 10-recruiter search firm that wants weekly desk reporting without a consultant-led implementation, that is the specific use case Crelate answers better than [Bullhorn](/en/tools/bullhorn/).
- **The back-office seam is wired.** The integrations page lists Xero, Bilflo, FoxHire, GorillaWorks and Ascen for payroll and back office, Checkr and E-Verify for screening, 20 job boards including Indeed, ZipRecruiter and [Appcast](/en/tools/appcast/), plus RingCentral, Zoom, Zapier and Merge. Crelate claims 100+ partners and an open REST API with webhooks.
- **Compliance paperwork exists.** SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001, per the vendor, which shortens a security review with enterprise clients.

## Pricing reality

Published at crelate.com/pricing, checked 24 September 2026:

- **Essentials — $85/user/month, annual only.** Capped at 2 users and 20,000 contacts. ATS, CRM, job portal, resume parsing, sourcing, AI support.
- **Business — $119/user/month, annual or monthly.** Adds the AI Assistant, client and team portals, reports and analytics, email connections and technical support.
- **Business Plus — quote only.** Adds the Advanced AI Assistant, BD workflows, automation and sequences, full API access and 50GB storage instead of 15GB. Third-party teardowns place Discover Agent here and above. Pin's pricing write-up cites G2 users reporting $140-160 per user per month; Crelate does not confirm that band.
- **Platform — custom user or site licence.** Crelate's own FAQ says Insights Agent and the Data Quality Agent are sold through Platform pricing.

Contact enrichment, email campaigns, text messaging and eSignature carry separate fees; Crelate Hire onboarding is priced per hire.

The arithmetic for a 10-recruiter search firm: ten recruiters on Business is $14,280 in year one at list. Ten Bullhorn seats run $34,000-$46,100 in year one per the third-party TCO trackers cited in our Bullhorn entry. The catch is renewal. Crelate's terms of service apply an "annual innovation price enhancement" at every renewal of the greater of 7% or US CPI, and state that discounts "are one-time and do not renew." At the 7% floor, those ten seats cost $15,280 in year two and $16,349 in year three — about $45,900 over three years before add-ons. Budget on that curve, not on the list price.

Third-party pricing pages also describe a 5-seat minimum and no free trial. Crelate's live page shows a 2-user Essentials plan and a free trial with sample data. Treat the order form as the only source of truth.

## Best for

Owner-operators and practice leads at executive-search and perm-placement firms of roughly 3-25 recruiters who run BD and delivery on the same desk and want client reporting and portals without a Bullhorn-scale implementation. Don't buy it as a sourcing engine — buy it as the system of record, and add sourcing only if the database you already own is exhausted.

## Versus the alternatives

[Bullhorn](/en/tools/bullhorn/) is the market-share incumbent. Pick it when you run contract staffing with timesheets, pay/bill, VMS requisitions and a large partner marketplace. Pick Crelate when you place perm and retained searches and the Bullhorn module stack is cost you do not use.

[Loxo](/en/tools/loxo/) is the other scaled agency platform and the stronger AI buy: $149 Core and $199 Professional per user per month, its own 850M+ sourcing graph and an MCP server on the entry tier. Pick Loxo when finding new people is the bottleneck. Pick Crelate when working your own client and candidate history is, and when Business at $119 covers your reporting needs.

[Recruiterflow](/en/tools/recruiterflow/) at $149 is the closest like-for-like on BD-plus-delivery with a more modern interface; pick it if your recruiters will judge the product in the first week of use. [Recruit CRM](/en/tools/recruit-crm/) at $95 is the budget pick for small desks.

[Juicebox](/en/tools/juicebox/) is the fastest-growing entrant in agency sourcing. It is not an ATS. Pair it with Crelate if external reach is the gap rather than paying for Business Plus to unlock Discover.

If none of these fit — because you bill on timesheets more than placements — scope the pay/bill system first and let it choose the ATS.

## Watch-outs

- **The 7% renewal floor compounds.** Price rises every renewal term, and first-year discounts vanish at renewal. *Guard:* strike or cap the innovation price enhancement clause in the order form, and get years two and three priced in writing before you sign.
- **You lose access to your data at termination.** The terms say that after termination you will have "no further access" to your content and Crelate may delete it; fees are non-refundable with no pro-rata credit. *Guard:* diary a full export — candidates, contacts, activities, placements, attachments — at least 60 days before the 30-day non-renewal notice deadline.
- **The AI you saw in the demo may not be on your tier.** Third-party teardowns place Discover at Business Plus, and Crelate's FAQ puts Insights in Platform pricing — both quote-only. *Guard:* make the rep map every demoed AI feature to a named plan on the quote.
- **Reporting runs one table at a time.** Reviewers report that reports pull from a single object — contacts, placements or opportunities — not joined across them. *Guard:* build your three most important weekly reports during the trial; if one needs a join, price a BI export before committing.
- **The interface shows its age.** G2 and Capterra reviewers cite dated navigation, extra clicks and a weak Chrome extension. *Guard:* have your most junior recruiter run a full req-to-submittal cycle in the trial and time it.
- **No MCP server yet.** The integrations page lists "MCP Server (Coming Soon)." *Guard:* score the product on the REST API and webhooks that ship today, and use the API if an agent needs access.
