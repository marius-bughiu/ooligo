## What it is

Verint is the workforce engagement suite of record for large contact centers: workforce management (forecasting, scheduling, adherence), call and screen recording, quality management, speech and interaction analytics, and a library of task-specific AI "bots" sold on top. Since late 2025 it is also Calabrio. Thoma Bravo took Verint private for about $2B in a deal that closed on 26 November 2025 and merged it with Calabrio, its mid-market WEM company. On 18 February 2026 the combined organization took the single name Verint, kept the Calabrio product name, and installed Calabrio's former CEO Dave Rhodes as Verint CEO.

The rough division of the merged installed base: Verint brings the upmarket and large-enterprise accounts (the company says more than 80 of the Fortune 100), Calabrio brings the mid-market. Verint's own executives claim the combination holds about 40% of the workforce-management market — a vendor number, not an audited one, but directionally consistent with Verint and NICE being the two names every enterprise WFM shortlist starts with.

The 2026 pitch is "agentic workforce orchestration." At Engage 2026 in June, Verint launched Agent Factory, an environment for building and governing AI agents next to human ones (OpenAI, Gemini, Anthropic and Llama models or your own, with MCP and A2A connectors), plus Workforce Intelligence, Desktop Intelligence and Quality Intelligence. Quality Intelligence is the interesting one: it checks whether the action promised on a call — a refund, say — was actually completed in the system of record, not just whether the agent used the right phrases.

## Why it shows up in Customer Success stacks

- **One suite for every seat that touches a customer.** WFM, recording, QM and analytics on one contract, scheduling in-house staff, BPO sites and back-office teams. For a 1,000-seat operation the alternative is four vendors and four data models.
- **The bots are where the ROI cases come from.** Verint's published customer numbers are self-reported but specific: an industrial distributor scoring 97% of 200,000+ monthly calls with the Quality Bot and avoiding 200+ analyst hires; BT attributing $9M in added annual cross-sell to coaching; an online bank cutting scheduling requests by 25% with a self-service scheduling bot.
- **Calabrio customers got the bots without migrating.** Since 10 March 2026 Calabrio customers have had direct access to Genie, TimeFlex, Copilot and the Intelligent Virtual Assistant, and Verint says it has no plan to sunset either product line or force migrations.
- **It sits behind the major CCaaS platforms.** Five9 and Cisco are named channel partners, and Five9's own bundles let you choose Verint or Calabrio as the WEM layer, so you can change telephony without re-implementing WFM.

## Pricing reality

Verint does not publish prices; everything is quoted, typically per named user per module on multi-year terms. The best external anchors:

- **Contract size.** Vendr's marketplace data puts the median Verint contract at **$49,451/yr**, ranging **$18,858 to $231,780**.
- **Per-seat list.** Calabrio's suite shows up on the UK G-Cloud 15 framework via reseller TTEC Digital at **£97.50 per user per month** for WFM, QM, analytics and recording together, with WFM alone at £30.50 — indicative list prices, negotiated down in practice.
- **Negotiation.** Buyers on Vendr report a 26% discount for signing a multi-year term at the vendor's quarter end, and a renewal uplift capped at 3% against a proposed 8%.

The real band for a 200–500 seat contact center running WFM plus QM is roughly **$50–$100 per seat per month before discount**, with AI bots, speech analytics and Agent Factory consumption priced on top. Professional services for implementation are a separate line, and on the Verint side they are rarely small.

## Best for

Contact-center and CX operations leaders running 300+ agents — often several thousand — across voice and digital, frequently with BPO sites and regulated recording requirements (financial services, insurance, utilities, government), who want WFM, quality and analytics from one vendor with a long compliance track record. Existing Calabrio customers in the 100–500 seat range are the second fit: you now get Verint's bot catalogue without a platform change.

## Do not buy it if

You run under about 100 agents on a modern helpdesk. The implementation weight and quoted pricing are built for enterprise contact centers, and [Assembled](/en/tools/assembled/) or Zendesk WFM will be live in weeks at published per-agent prices. Skip it too if what you actually want is AI that resolves contacts rather than AI that helps you staff and score them — that is a [Decagon](/en/tools/decagon/) or [Sierra](/en/tools/sierra/) purchase, not a WEM one.

## Versus the alternatives

- **NICE** — the other incumbent and the closest like-for-like: CXone Mpower bundles WFM, QM, analytics and a CCaaS platform. Pick NICE when you also want to replace telephony in the same deal. Pick Verint when you want to keep your CCaaS (Five9, Genesys, Cisco) and buy WEM independently.
- **[Genesys Cloud](/en/tools/genesys-cloud/)** — native WEM included in its higher CX tiers. Pick it when Genesys is already your contact center and good-enough WFM inside one console beats specialist depth.
- **[Assembled](/en/tools/assembled/)** — the fastest-growing entrant in support WFM, with published pricing from $25 per agent per month and an MCP server launched in May 2026. Pick it for 50–400 agents on Zendesk, Intercom or Salesforce, where time-to-value matters more than shift bidding and regulated recording.
- **[Level AI](/en/tools/level-ai/) or [Observe.AI](/en/tools/observe-ai/)** — AI-native QA. Pick them when the problem is scoring 100% of interactions and your WFM is fine; they usually trial faster than a Verint QM expansion.

## Watch-outs

- **Two overlapping product lines, one owner.** Verint WFM and Calabrio WFM, Verint QM and Calabrio QM now coexist. Verint says nothing is being sunset, but it laid off hundreds of staff in March 2026 and the roadmap beyond 2026 is described only broadly. *Guard:* write a product-continuity clause into any new or renewed contract — named product line supported through the term, plus a no-cost migration path and data export if it is retired.
- **Partner-hosted deployments are being retired.** Puzzel has told customers it will stop hosting Verint at the end of December 2026, in line with Verint's end-of-life for its partner-hosted (ASP) platform. *Guard:* if your Verint instance runs on a reseller's or CCaaS partner's infrastructure, ask now for the hosting model and its end date, and budget the move to Verint's own cloud before renewal.
- **Reporting and configuration are the recurring complaints.** Verint Workforce Management holds 4.2/5 across 216 G2 reviews, and the negatives cluster on missing features, weak reporting and complex configuration; Calabrio ONE rates higher at 4.4/5 across 433 reviews. *Guard:* name the reports your weekly ops review runs on and have them built in the proof of concept, not promised in the statement of work.
- **AI consumption pricing grows quietly.** Agent Factory and the bots are priced on usage, and model use climbs as you add agents and channels. *Guard:* negotiate a fixed consumption pool or a price ceiling per model call at signature, and start with one business unit or region before rolling out — Verint's own executives recommend production pilots on a subset of agents over proofs of concept.
