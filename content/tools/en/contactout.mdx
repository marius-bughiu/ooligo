## What it is

ContactOut is a contact finder built mainly for recruiters. Its Chrome extension sits on a LinkedIn profile and returns the person's email addresses and phone numbers; a separate search portal lets you filter its own database by title, location, skills, company size and years of experience, then export. Founded in 2015 by Rob Liu, it is privately held. The vendor claims 800M people profiles, 150M personal emails, 200M work emails and 100M direct dials, says it has 1.4M users, and lists SOC 2, GDPR and CCPA under compliance. Other ContactOut pages quote 350M and 700M+ profiles, so treat every database figure as marketing.

The category leader it resembles is [Lusha](/en/tools/lusha/), but the product is built around a different answer. Lusha and [Apollo](/en/tools/apollo/) are sold to sales teams that want work emails and direct dials. ContactOut leans on **personal** addresses: the Gmail or Outlook inbox a passive candidate actually reads, which also survives a job change.

## Why it shows up in recruiting stacks

- **It finds personal emails more than anything else.** Pin, a recruiting vendor that also sells enrichment, ran 1.3M+ email lookups between March and May 2026 with four providers active on the same candidate pool. ContactOut returned an email on 48.2% of lookups (monthly range 43–53%), behind [Wiza](/en/tools/wiza/) at 55.2% and ahead of RocketReach at 29.8%. Only 12.8% of ContactOut's hits included a professional-domain address, the lowest in the audit. That is a hit rate, not a deliverability rate, and it comes from a competitor, but it matches the product's positioning: you get the inbox a candidate checks on a Sunday, not the work address their employer monitors.
- **It runs inside LinkedIn Recruiter.** The extension works on LinkedIn Standard, Recruiter Lite and Recruiter Pro, so sourcers do not leave the tool they already search in. The free plan works on LinkedIn Standard only, and the pricing page lists "Works with recruiter Pro" under the Team plan.
- **It pushes to the ATS.** ContactOut names [Greenhouse](/en/tools/greenhouse/), [Lever](/en/tools/lever/) and [Bullhorn](/en/tools/bullhorn/), plus [Salesforce](/en/tools/salesforce/) and [HubSpot](/en/tools/hubspot/) for sales users and [Zapier](/en/tools/zapier/) for everything else. On the pricing page, "Salesforce + ATS integrations" sits in the Team column, not the self-serve plans.

## Pricing reality

The pricing page, checked 2026-11-17, defaults to an "Exclude US/UK Data" toggle that halves the price. Most recruiters hiring in North America or the UK need the other setting:

- **Free** — $0, 5 emails, 5 phones and 5 exports per day, one user per company.
- **Email** — $49 a month with US/UK data ($25 without), "unlimited" emails, 300 exports a month, email campaigns and a list builder.
- **Email + Phone** — $99 a month with US/UK data ($49 without), "unlimited" emails and phones, 600 exports a month, data enrichment and an AI email writer.
- **Team / API** — quote only. Team management and reporting, Recruiter Pro support, Salesforce and ATS integrations, API access, a dedicated account manager.

**"Unlimited" has a ceiling.** Section 10 of ContactOut's Terms of Use caps paid unlimited plans at 2,000 email credits and 1,000 phone credits per account per month unless an order form says otherwise. One full-time sourcer rarely hits that. A shared login across a desk of five will, and sharing breaks the one-user terms anyway.

What teams actually pay: a solo agency recruiter or in-house sourcer pays $99 a month on Email + Phone, which works out to under $0.10 a phone at the cap. A TA team on Recruiter Pro that needs ATS pushes is on the quote-only Team plan, and ContactOut publishes no seat price for it. Get a per-seat quote with the monthly credit limit written into the order form before you compare it with Wiza or Lusha.

## Best for

An agency recruiter or in-house sourcer filling technical or senior roles where the candidates are passive, employed and not reading InMail. ContactOut is the right pick when the outreach plan is a personal email or a call, the recruiter works out of LinkedIn Recruiter all day, and the volume is one person's worth of reveals, roughly 50–100 a day.

**Do not buy it** for outbound sales into companies (work emails and firmographics are where Apollo, Lusha and [Prospeo](/en/tools/prospeo/) are stronger), for bulk enrichment of an existing ATS database (a waterfall such as [FullEnrich](/en/tools/fullenrich/) is cheaper per found contact), or if most of your candidates are in the EU and your legal team has not signed off on a legitimate-interest basis for messaging them at personal addresses.

## Watch-outs

- **Personal-email outreach in the EU carries GDPR exposure.** ContactOut's privacy notice says it relies on the data being publicly available and on a legitimate interest in helping recruiters reach candidates. Regulators have tested that basis against tools like this: in December 2024 France's CNIL fined Kaspr, a LinkedIn contact-extraction extension, €240,000 and ordered it to stop collecting the details of people who had limited their contact visibility. Under GDPR Article 14, a recruiter who contacts a candidate with data collected elsewhere has to tell them where it came from, at the latest in that first message. **Guard:** for EU and UK candidates, put a source-and-opt-out line in the first message, log the source on the ATS record, and set a retention period for candidates who never reply. See [GDPR for legal teams](/en/learn/gdpr-for-legal-teams/) for the lawful-basis test.
- **LinkedIn does not approve of extensions like this.** LinkedIn's User Agreement bars third-party software that scrapes or copies profiles, and browser extensions that read profile pages are a documented reason for account restrictions. **Guard:** keep reveals at human pace from the recruiter's own account, never run it on a shared Recruiter seat, and do not give it the login of a seat you cannot afford to have restricted.
- **A found email is not a delivered email.** Pin's figures count lookups that returned any address, and ContactOut returns more than one per match on average. **Guard:** run every export through a verifier before it goes into a sequence, and watch bounce rate per source in the ATS or sequencer. Drop the source for a role family when bounces pass 5%.

## Alternatives, and when to pick them instead

- **[Wiza](/en/tools/wiza/)** — the higher hit rate in Pin's audit and stronger on work emails, with metered pricing from $49 a month. Pick Wiza when you source from Sales Navigator lists as much as from Recruiter, or need a mix of work and personal addresses.
- **[Lusha](/en/tools/lusha/)** — the share leader for sales prospecting, priced per user and per credit from $49.90 a month. Pick it when the same tool has to serve recruiters and an SDR team; take ContactOut when it is recruiters only and personal emails are the point.
- **SignalHire** — the closest like-for-like recruiter extension, with a similar capped "unlimited" plan. Run both on the same 100 profiles in a trial and keep whichever finds more verified personal emails for your role families.
- **[FullEnrich](/en/tools/fullenrich/)** — a waterfall that already queries ContactOut among 20+ sources, from $55 a month for 1,000 credits. Pick it when you enrich lists in bulk rather than one profile at a time in LinkedIn.
- **[hireEZ](/en/tools/hireez/) or [SeekOut](/en/tools/seekout/)** — sourcing platforms with contact data built in. Pick one when you also need search, CRM and sequencing; ContactOut is the cheaper add-on when you already have those.
