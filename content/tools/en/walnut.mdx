## What it is

Walnut is an interactive demo platform built for sales teams rather than website visitors. You capture your product as HTML or video, edit the captured data so a prospect sees their own logo, numbers, and use case, and send it as a guided demo, a sandbox, a playlist of demos, or a deal room. Founded in 2020 and headquartered in New York, it has raised $56M, most recently a $35M Series B led by Felicis in January 2022, and says it serves hundreds of B2B software companies. It holds 4.5/5 on G2 across 151 reviews and is SOC 2 Type II certified.

On 2026-09-02 Walnut shipped what it calls an enterprise agent platform: **Walnut Xpert**, a buyer-facing agent that answers questions and steers the prospect through the demo; **AI Mode for Playlists and Deal Rooms**, which builds a personalized buyer experience from one prompt; and **AI Center**, an admin layer that sets permissions and guardrails on how AI is used across the workspace.

## Why it shows up in RevOps stacks

- **Per-prospect demos without an SE rebuilding the environment.** Walnut lets reps change text, logos, charts, and figures in a captured demo so it matches the account. AI Mode generates that personalization from a prompt. Walnut says building a playlist or deal room used to take an AE or CSM about three hours per deal.
- **Deal rooms that carry the demo.** A deal room combines demos, content, and stakeholder activity in one link. If you already run a sales room in [Trumpet](/en/tools/trumpet/) or [Dock](/en/tools/dock/), Trumpet ships a native Walnut integration; Walnut's own Digital Sales Rooms are an add-on on Accelerate and included on Scale.
- **Demo engagement inside Salesforce.** The advanced [Salesforce](/en/tools/salesforce/) integration, from Accelerate up, lets reps launch demos from the record and see deal analytics and custom Walnut reports there. [HubSpot](/en/tools/hubspot/), Marketo, Pardot, and [Zapier](/en/tools/zapier/) are on every plan.
- **Read-only analytics in an AI assistant.** Walnut's help center documents an MCP server that wraps its Customer Data API and lets [Claude](/en/tools/claude/), [Cursor](/en/tools/cursor/), or VS Code query demo sessions, totals, and top demos. It reads analytics only; it does not build or publish demos. The Data API sits on the Scale plan.

## Pricing

Walnut publishes list prices, unlike [Navattic](/en/tools/navattic/). There is no free plan and no self-serve trial.

- **Ignite — $575/month billed annually** ($6,900 a year). 3 editor and 3 presenter seats, 50,000 AI credits a year, unlimited demos, AI Mode for demos, playlists, analytics, HubSpot and basic Salesforce. Sandbox demos, SSO, and Xpert are add-ons.
- **Accelerate — $1,550/month billed annually** ($18,600 a year). 6 editor and 6 presenter seats, 100,000 AI credits, sandbox demos, prospect personalization, video overlays, AI avatars, SSO, the advanced Salesforce integration, a dedicated CSM, and 10 onboarding hours. Deal rooms and Xpert are add-ons.
- **Scale — custom.** 200,000 AI credits, Digital Sales Rooms, translation into 243 languages, the Data API, and bring-your-own-key for AI models.
- **AI credits are metered.** Top-up packs run $1,000 for 50,000 credits ($0.020 each) down to $0.015 each above 200,000.
- **What teams pay.** Vendr puts the median Walnut contract at $17,000 a year across 36 purchases, in a $7,900 to $36,700 range, with buyers reporting 10-25% off list. The median lands just under Accelerate list, which matches the usual buying pattern: teams that need advanced Salesforce and sandboxes skip Ignite.

## Best for

- Sales and presales leaders at B2B SaaS companies selling five- and six-figure deals, where AEs and SEs send a personalized demo or deal room to a buying committee after the first call
- RevOps teams that want demo engagement reported against Salesforce opportunities and are budgeting $15,000-$20,000 a year for it
- Not for PLG teams whose job is a website "take a tour" button at the lowest cost, and not for a seed-stage company that needs a free or $100-a-month entry plan

## Alternatives — and when to pick them instead

- **[Storylane](/en/tools/storylane/)** — the broadest independent rival, with screenshot, video, and HTML capture, sandbox and presenter demos, and RepX, an AI SDR. Paid plans start at $40/month billed annually, but Salesforce waits for Premium at $1,200/month. Pick Storylane when marketing and sales share one demo library and you want a cheaper start.
- **[Navattic](/en/tools/navattic/)** — HTML-only website demos with Salesforce on its 5-seat Base plan, last published at $500/month, and a Vendr median of $8,500 a year, half of Walnut's. Pick Navattic when the demo lives on the website and feeds lead routing.
- **Supademo** — the fastest-growing entrant, ranked by G2 among its fastest-growing products, with its own AI Demo Agent since June 2026. HTML demos start on its Growth plan at $350/month for 5 creators. Pick Supademo when price per creator decides it and deep CRM reporting does not.
- **Consensus** — presales video and interactive demos with stakeholder-level analytics for buying committees. Salesforce, Gong, and Outreach arrive on Pro at $1,250/month for 10 users. Pick Consensus when the problem is mapping who on the committee watched what, rather than editing demo data per prospect.

## Watch-outs

- **The cheap plan is not the plan you will run.** Ignite has basic Salesforce and puts sandbox demos and SSO behind add-ons, and Vendr's $17,000 median sits close to Accelerate list. Guard: price Accelerate (or Ignite plus every add-on you need) before the first call, and bring a Storylane or Navattic quote to get into Vendr's 10-25% discount band.
- **AI credits are a second meter.** Personalization, Xpert, and AI Mode draw on a pooled annual allowance, and top-ups cost $0.015-$0.020 per credit. Guard: ask Walnut for credit consumption per AI Mode run and per Xpert session, and cap top-up spend in the order form.
- **Captures drift from the live product.** An edited capture shows your UI as it was on capture day, and data edits multiply the number of demo versions to maintain. Guard: keep one master demo per use case, personalize from it rather than re-capturing, and re-check every master after each major UI release.
- **The agent layer is weeks old.** Xpert, AI Mode for Playlists and Deal Rooms, and AI Center shipped on 2026-09-02, and Walnut's only result so far is its own claim that Xpert sessions more than double buyer time spent. Guard: pilot Xpert on one segment, measure stage conversion against reps sending standard demos, and set AI Center permissions before any rep can generate buyer-facing content.
