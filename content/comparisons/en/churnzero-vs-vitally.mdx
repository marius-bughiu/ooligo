[ChurnZero](/en/tools/churnzero/) and [Vitally](/en/tools/vitally/) both sit in the mid-market Customer Success platform band — between [Gainsight](/en/tools/gainsight/)'s enterprise heft and a CSM-owned spreadsheet. They compete for the same buyer: a $20-150M ARR SaaS company with 5-50 CSMs that has outgrown manual account tracking but can't justify a Gainsight admin on the org chart. The core split is build-versus-buy at the workflow layer. ChurnZero ships you a ready-to-run CS platform with in-app engagement bundled in. Vitally ships you a flexible, notion-style canvas you configure into the exact workflow your CS team runs. The routing question is whether your team wants the workflows out of the box or wants to build its own.

## Where ChurnZero wins

- **Out-of-box workflows and faster first value.** ChurnZero's plays, health scores, and lifecycle automations are pre-built; you turn them on rather than design them. For a first-time CS-platform team with no dedicated ops resource, that's the difference between live in six weeks and stalled in a configuration project. Vitally's flexibility is a cost here — a blank canvas needs someone to fill it.
- **In-app engagement is bundled.** Walk-throughs, in-app surveys, and NPS prompts ship inside ChurnZero. If your alternative is buying a separate Pendo or WalkMe to drive feature adoption, ChurnZero folds that into one contract. Vitally has no native in-app engagement layer — it reads product-usage signal but doesn't push in-app guidance back to users.
- **Lower configuration burden.** Because the workflows are standardized, there's less surface for configuration drift. Vitally's notion-style layouts mean different CSMs build different views, and the team's picture diverges unless an admin polices it. ChurnZero's narrower customization is a feature for teams that want consistency over expressiveness.

## Where Vitally wins

- **CSM-workflow depth and AI-native posture.** Vitally is built around the CSM's daily work surface, not a manager's dashboard — the CSM lives in Vitally to run their book without bouncing between Salesforce, the analytics tool, and Slack. It's also AI-native where ChurnZero is not, which shows up in account summarization and risk surfacing.
- **Customization without a RevOps ticket.** Vitally's notion-style flexibility lets the CS team build its own layouts, custom objects, and views without burning the RevOps roadmap. For teams whose CS motion doesn't fit a standard template, that autonomy is the whole reason to pick it. ChurnZero's customization ceiling is lower; non-standard workflows hit walls.
- **Product-usage signal as a first-class input.** Vitally pulls directly from Segment, Mixpanel, and Amplitude so health scores reflect what customers actually do in the product, not just login counts and ticket volume. ChurnZero can use product events too, but Vitally's health model is built around them.

## Pricing reality

Both are custom-quoted with no useful list price. Mid-market deployments (10-30 CSMs) land in the same band: ChurnZero at roughly $25-70K/year, Vitally at roughly $30-70K/year. Enterprise (50+ CSMs) runs $80-200K+ for both. At comparable scope the two are within ~10-20% of each other — this is not a 2-3× pricing decision the way [Gainsight vs ChurnZero](/en/comparisons/gainsight-vs-churnzero/) is. The one place the math diverges: ChurnZero layers per-product-MAU pricing on top of per-CSM when you use in-app engagement at scale, so a high-engagement product with millions of monthly active users can see the in-app component balloon past the seat cost. Model that MAU line before signing. Vitally is per-seat-plus-platform-fee, so its cost climbs with CSM headcount rather than end-user volume — more predictable, but it crosses over toward Gainsight territory past 50 seats.

## Implementation effort

ChurnZero: 4-8 weeks to live, vendor onboarding included, sustainable on a part-time CS ops resource. The bundled in-app engagement has one prerequisite — it needs product-event instrumentation (Segment, Mixpanel, or a direct event pipeline) in place, or the walk-throughs stay dormant.

Vitally: the platform installs fast, but the real work is health-score design. Budget 4-8 weeks for designing and tuning the health-scoring logic, because Vitally is only as good as the model the team configures — roll it out without that and you've deployed a dashboard with no signal. Vitally also depends on a mature product-analytics layer existing first; if the product team doesn't track meaningful events, its health scores fall back to lagging indicators. Both tools, then, assume you've already done the product-instrumentation work — neither creates that signal for you.

## Bottom line

- **Pick ChurnZero** if you're a first-time CS-platform buyer who wants workflows that work on day one, you need in-app engagement (walk-throughs, surveys, NPS) bundled rather than buying Pendo separately, and you value standardization over the freedom to build custom layouts. It's the lower-effort path for a team without dedicated CS ops.
- **Pick Vitally** if your CS team wants to own and shape its workflow, you have (or will hire) someone to design the health model and maintain layouts, product-usage signal is central to how you score health, and you want an AI-native surface where CSMs actually live day to day.
- **Pick neither** if you're under 5 CSMs — Salesforce plus a CSM-owned spreadsheet covers that scale, and both platforms' economics only work past ~10 CSMs. Revisit when manual tracking starts dropping accounts.

If you're choosing in a vacuum without the conditions above, pick ChurnZero. The faster time-to-value and bundled in-app engagement make it the lower-risk default for a team standing up its first CS platform. Switch to Vitally later if your workflows outgrow ChurnZero's templates or your CS team needs configuration autonomy the standardized platform can't give.
