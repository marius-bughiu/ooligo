For ten people running sales in Attio, start with the meeting record you need. If a rep needs the call and transcript in Attio, Pro already includes a recorder. If people also need a shared Fathom library, price those seats separately. Buying Fathom Business for everyone makes sense for a different requirement, such as syncing ten users to an existing HubSpot workspace. ## Annual software budget for ten users

These are illustrative subscriptions at list prices checked November 2, 2026. The four shared USD lines assume ten active users throughout the year, one Clay workspace and annual billing. The n8n subscription is billed in EUR.

| Shared subscription | Monthly rate used | Annual cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Attio Pro](https://attio.com/pricing/usd) | 10 × USD 79 | USD 9,480 |
| [Clay Launch](https://www.clay.com/pricing) | from USD 167 per workspace | from USD 2,004 |
| [Claude Team Standard](https://claude.com/pricing) | 10 × USD 20 | USD 2,400 |
| [Slack Pro](https://slack.com/pricing/pro) | 10 × USD 7.25 | USD 870 |
| [n8n Cloud Starter](https://n8n.io/pricing/) | EUR 20 for 2,500 monthly executions | EUR 240 |

**A. Ten people record, without automatic CRM sync.** Ten Fathom Team seats at USD 15 per month add USD 1,800 a year. The total starts at **USD 16,554 plus EUR 240**. Reps can record in Fathom and manually attach an approved summary to Attio. The three-users-per-domain limit applies to supported CRM sync. All ten people can record. If Attio’s own recorder covers the need, omit Fathom and the same base starts at **USD 14,754 plus EUR 240**. Connect the rep’s calendar, admit the recorder and confirm who can see the call. [Fathom plans](https://www.fathom.ai/pricing?plan=teams) · [Attio recording setup](https://attio.com/help/reference/productivity-collaborating/call-intelligence/set-up-and-manage-call-recording)

**B. All ten also need Fathom summaries in HubSpot.** For a team already paying for HubSpot, ten Fathom Business seats at USD 25 per month add USD 3,000 a year. The stack starts at **USD 17,754 plus EUR 240**, a USD 1,200 annual increase over A. Fathom documents a HubSpot Team connection for up to three users per domain and a Business connection for more. HubSpot’s own subscription is extra. Upgrading Fathom does not establish an automatic sync to Attio. [Fathom plans](https://www.fathom.ai/pricing?plan=teams) · [Supported CRM connections](https://www.fathom.ai/integrations-crm)

Both totals exclude tax, extra Clay data credits and actions, Attio credit packs, model/API usage beyond included allowances, and n8n capacity beyond Starter. Track implementation hours and recurring maintenance separately. For example, budget onboarding hours × your loaded hourly rate, then add 12 × monthly support hours × that rate. Use your billing exchange rate only if you need a single-currency total.

## How an approved lead reaches a rep

Attio holds the account and owner. Use Clay Launch for a small reviewed batch: export the enrichment results, check the source against the company domain and approve rows before importing them into Attio. Clay lists automatic CRM sync and webhook automation under Growth. If continuous enrichment matters, price Growth separately and confirm the specific destination. ## Test a Fathom note in Attio before automating it

Here is a proposed integration. We checked the API documentation and have not run the connection in a customer workspace. Fathom’s CRM guide documents Salesforce and HubSpot. For Attio, a RevOps owner would need to configure the following handoff and maintain it. [Fathom CRM integrations](https://www.fathom.ai/integrations-crm)

1. In a test workspace, agree which calls can be copied and who can see the destination note. A Fathom user key can read calls recorded by that user or shared with their team. Register a signed webhook for permitted recordings. Request the summary and keep the full transcript out of the payload. [Fathom API access](https://help.fathom.video/en/articles/8368641) · [Webhook options](https://developers.fathom.ai/api-reference/webhooks/create-a-webhook)
2. For a fictional recording `74201`, map the external attendee’s verified email to exactly one Attio person. Have the owner approve the summary. Create a note on that person using the returned Attio record ID, with the recording ID in the note. Save the resulting note ID. [Attio notes API](https://docs.attio.com/rest-api/endpoint-reference/notes/create-a-note)
3. Send the same event twice. Pass only if that person has exactly one approved note. With zero or multiple matches, expect a review item and zero writes. With a timeout after writing, look up the recording ID on the destination before retrying. If the outcome is unclear, hold it for review. Confirm that someone outside the permitted audience cannot read the copied note.

Keep the manual step until these cases pass in your workspace. If nobody can own failed matches and retries, start with Attio’s recorder or a manually reviewed note. For the weekly revenue meeting, the [forecast prep example](/en/workflows/forecast-meeting-prep-skill/) shows what can be produced from two CRM exports before connecting another system.

## When to adopt this stack and what to keep elsewhere

This design fits a sales team that wants Attio to own customer records and has someone responsible for imports and failed matches. If an existing CRM already routes leads, keep it and trial enrichment against an export before moving any records. If a developer already maintains an integration service, compare adding the meeting handler there with introducing n8n. Whoever owns the delivery log must be able to trace a single recording from receipt to the final note.

A note that says a buyer requested a discount is context for the rep. Pricing rules, discount approval and invoicing still belong in your quoting and billing systems. Likewise, use a separate reporting model for product usage and invoices if you forecast from both. The ten-seat budgets cover neither of those systems.
