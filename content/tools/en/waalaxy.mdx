## What it is

Waalaxy is LinkedIn prospecting automation for one person running their own account: import a list of profiles, pick a preset sequence (visit, invite, message, follow up), and let it run. Since 1 July 2026 it is cloud-only. The Chrome extension was pulled from the Chrome Web Store, campaigns now run from app.waalaxy.com in any browser, and they keep going with your laptop closed. An optional companion extension, Alien Copilot, only pre-fills imports from a LinkedIn search; it does not run anything. The closest comparison is [Expandi](/en/tools/expandi/), at a lower price and with less sequence control.

## Why it shows up in RevOps stacks

- **It is the cheapest serious entry point to LinkedIn automation.** A founder or a lone SDR gets server-side LinkedIn sequences for less than the price of a single [Sales Navigator](/en/tools/sales-navigator/) seat.
- **Setup takes an afternoon, not a project.** Sequences are preset templates, so nobody has to design branching logic before the first invite goes out. That simplicity is also the main limit (see below).
- **It plugs into automation tools on the middle tier.** Advanced and Business include API access and Make, Zapier and [n8n](/en/tools/n8n/) modules, plus an MCP server that lets Claude import prospects into Waalaxy lists and campaigns. The MCP only imports; it cannot read conversations, reply, or pull reports.

## Pricing reality

Waalaxy prices per user in euros, and the headline numbers on its pricing page are the annual-billed rates. Checked 14 November 2026:

| Plan | Monthly billing | Annual billing (per month) | LinkedIn invitations |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pro | €39 | €19 | 300 a month, about 11 a day |
| Advanced | €99 | €49 | 100 a day, 200 a week |
| Business | €139 | €69 | 100 a day, 200 a week, plus cold email |

Quarterly billing sits in between at 20% off. Every plan has a 14-day trial. The free plan still exists in the help center but cannot launch campaigns, so treat it as a demo.

**The plan most people need is Advanced, not Pro.** Pro caps invitations at roughly 11 a day, or roughly 77 a week, under 40% of the 200-a-week ceiling the higher plans allow. Advanced raises you to that ceiling and adds the API. So the real price for active single-seat prospecting is €49 a month on an annual commitment, or €99 month to month. The LinkedIn inbox is a separate €20-a-month add-on on every plan, and email-finder credits are thin below Business (25 a month).

## Best for

Founders, freelancers, and solo SDRs at small B2B companies who prospect from their own LinkedIn account and want simple invite-and-follow-up sequences without building anything. It fits best at one to three seats, where per-user pricing is still cheaper than the alternatives.

**Do not buy it to run many LinkedIn accounts against one campaign.** Team seats exist, but each seat keeps its own quotas, campaigns cannot be shared or duplicated across members, and managers cannot see each other's inboxes. Agencies and multi-sender teams outgrow it immediately.

## Versus the alternatives

**[Expandi](/en/tools/expandi/)** ($79 a seat annually) is the revenue leader in cloud LinkedIn automation and wins on conditional branching and personalization depth. Pick Expandi when reps write fewer, more considered sequences and need if/then logic; Waalaxy's preset sequences cannot branch.

**[HeyReach](/en/tools/heyreach/)** bills per connected sender and rotates one campaign across many accounts. Pick HeyReach from about three senders upward; below that, Waalaxy is cheaper and simpler.

**[lemlist](/en/tools/lemlist/)** is the fastest-growing pressure on standalone LinkedIn tools because it folds LinkedIn steps into an email sequencer. Pick lemlist when email is your primary channel. Pick Waalaxy when LinkedIn is the channel and email is occasional; its cold email sequences are Business-only.

## Watch-outs

- **Pro looks cheap because it is throttled.** At around 11 invitations a day, a Pro seat cannot keep a real prospecting pipeline full. **Guard:** model your weekly invitation target before buying; if it is above about 75 a week, budget for Advanced from day one.
- **Your LinkedIn session now lives on Waalaxy's servers.** Signup asks for your LinkedIn login before you see the app, and the cloud-move announcement does not say which IP address or location your account sends from. A login from an unfamiliar location is a signal LinkedIn can act on. **Guard:** connect with an authenticator app rather than SMS, keep daily quotas below the maximum during the first two weeks, and do not run the same LinkedIn account in a second automation tool at the same time.
- **CRM sync is mostly glue, not native.** Most CRM connections ([HubSpot](/en/tools/hubspot/), [Pipedrive](/en/tools/pipedrive/), [Salesforce](/en/tools/salesforce/)) run through Zapier, Make, or n8n, which means a second subscription, and message content does not sync. **Guard:** decide at setup which system owns the conversation record, and add the CRM-sync step to every campaign rather than relying on a manual export later.
- **Third-party guides from before July 2026 describe a different product.** Older reviews cover the extension, old plans, and the retired 80-free-invitations offer. **Guard:** check the live pricing page and help center before acting on any review dated earlier than July 2026.
