Eightfold and Findem both pitch themselves as "AI talent intelligence," but they aim at different buyers. Eightfold is an enterprise talent platform across recruiting, internal mobility, and workforce planning. Findem is a focused people-intelligence sourcing platform with attribute-based search and enriched candidate profiles. The shortlist makes sense only at large enterprises trying to decide between platform and best-of-breed.

## Where Eightfold wins

- **Platform breadth.** Eightfold covers recruiting + internal mobility + workforce planning + skills graphs. For a CHRO consolidating talent decisions, the breadth is the value. Findem is sourcing-focused.
- **Internal candidate matching.** Eightfold's internal mobility AI surfaces existing employees for open roles. Findem doesn't address this at all.
- **Enterprise integration depth.** Eightfold ties into Workday, SAP SuccessFactors, and major HRIS systems with deep bidirectional data flow. Findem integrates well but is less embedded in HR enterprise systems.

## Where Findem wins

- **Sourcing precision.** Findem's attribute-based search and enriched candidate profiles are sharper for hard-to-find external talent — executive search, DEI sourcing, niche technical roles. Eightfold's external candidate matching is broader and less precise.
- **Time to value for sourcing teams.** Findem deploys in weeks for a sourcing function. Eightfold is a multi-quarter enterprise rollout that touches HRIS.
- **Recruiter and sourcer UX.** Findem is built for sourcers to use daily. Eightfold's recruiter UX exists but isn't where the product invests its best work — that goes to the strategic and HR-IT layers.

## Pricing reality

Both are enterprise-priced but at different scales. Findem is six figures for a strategic sourcing deployment. Eightfold is mid-six to seven figures for a full talent intelligence rollout across recruiting, mobility, and planning. They serve different buyers — head of talent acquisition versus CHRO — which usually settles the conversation.

## Verdict

- **Pick Eightfold** if you're a 5,000+ employee enterprise, your buyer is the CHRO, and you're consolidating recruiting + mobility + workforce planning onto one talent intelligence platform.
- **Pick Findem** if your buyer is the head of talent acquisition or a sourcing leader, your bottleneck is finding hard-to-source external candidates, and you don't need internal mobility or workforce planning.
- **Use both** at the largest enterprises — Eightfold as the strategic talent platform, Findem as the precision external sourcing layer.

The single mistake to avoid: framing this as an apples-to-apples comparison. They're solving different problems for different buyers, even when the demos look similar.
