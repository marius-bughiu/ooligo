## What it is

HireVue is the dominant on-demand video interviewing and AI-assessment platform — used by 700+ enterprise customers including a substantial share of the Fortune 500. Founded in 2004, HireVue acquired Modern Hire in 2023 and now spans video interviewing, structured-interview-builder, technical assessments, and AI-augmented candidate ranking. Material political and academic scrutiny of its AI-scoring features over the years has shaped where the platform invests AI vs. where it offers traditional video tooling.

## Why it shows up in Recruiting stacks

- **Compresses high-volume screening.** On-demand video interviews let candidates record responses to standardized questions on their own schedule; recruiters review batches of recordings in a fraction of the time live screens take.
- **Strong enterprise integrations.** Workday, SAP SuccessFactors, Oracle HCM integrations are mature; reduces the integration risk that derails enterprise deployments.
- **Structured interview infrastructure.** Pre-built interview templates per role with rubric anchors; supports the [structured interviewing](/en/learn/structured-interviewing/) discipline at scale.

## Pricing

- **Custom only.** Per-recruiter or per-interview pricing depending on packaging; effective entry point in the mid-five figures annually for mid-market.
- **Volume tiers** for high-volume hiring programs.
- Implementation typically 60-90 days with HireVue services.

## Best for

- Enterprise organizations running high-volume hiring (1,000+ hires/year) where on-demand screening compresses recruiter time
- Companies with multi-jurisdictional hiring where standardized video interviewing aids compliance
- Hiring functions where on-demand asynchronous screening fits the candidate population (knowledge workers in mature talent markets)

## Watch-outs

- AI-scoring features have drawn scrutiny — many programs use HireVue for video infrastructure but rely on human review for the actual hiring signal
- On-demand video interviews damage [candidate experience](/en/learn/candidate-experience/) for senior or in-demand candidates who expect live engagement; right tool for high-volume early-stage screening, wrong tool for senior-leadership interviews
- AI bias-audit obligations under [EU AI Act](/en/learn/eu-ai-act-for-legal-teams/), NYC Local Law 144, and Illinois AVDA apply to AI-augmented features
- Compete head-to-head with [Spark Hire](https://www.sparkhire.com/) and increasingly with [BrightHire](/en/tools/brighthire/) on different parts of the interview-tech stack
