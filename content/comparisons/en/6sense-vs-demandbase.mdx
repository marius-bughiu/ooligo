6sense vs Demandbase is the duopoly battle of enterprise ABM platforms, and after years of feature parity claims, real differences have emerged. 6sense leans into AI-driven intent and predictive scoring. Demandbase leans into account-based advertising and a tighter CRM-led ABM workflow. Both will sell you a 100K-plus USD contract. The question is which one your team will actually adopt past month three.

## Where 6sense wins

- **Predictive intent and AI scoring.** 6sense's "buying stage" predictions and AI-driven account scoring are the most mature in the category. The data science team built moats here that Demandbase has not closed.
- **Sales-led ABM workflows.** 6sense's seller experience (Chrome extension, Salesforce embed, alerts) is what AEs actually open daily. Demandbase's seller tooling lags.
- **Scale and enterprise stickiness.** 6sense's customer base skews larger and stickier. Renewal economics favor it among Fortune 500 RevOps leaders.

## Where Demandbase wins

- **Account-based advertising.** Demandbase's roots in B2B display advertising mean their ad targeting and personalization at the company level is genuinely differentiated. 6sense's ad layer is less robust.
- **Marketing-led ABM workflows.** Demandbase's website personalization and ABM marketing tools (page experiences, campaign measurement) are more mature for marketing-driven orgs.
- **Pricing flexibility.** Demandbase has been more aggressive on pricing in deals against 6sense, especially for mid-market enterprise.

## When to use both / Pricing reality

You don't run both — they're direct substitutes and the budget can't support it. Pricing reality: both platforms start around 60K USD per year for entry-level deployments and scale to 250K-plus USD per year for full ABM stacks. The total cost is rarely the published list — negotiate hard, and watch the implementation services attach rate.

## Verdict

- **Pick 6sense** if your motion is sales-led ABM, your AEs need intent signals daily, and predictive scoring is your top priority.
- **Pick Demandbase** if your motion is marketing-led ABM, account-based advertising is core to your demand engine, and your CMO drives the budget.
- **Use both** essentially never. Pick one based on which department owns ABM.

The single mistake to avoid: buying either platform without a named ABM lead and a 90-day adoption plan. Both shelve fast in orgs that aren't operationally ready, and you'll be paying enterprise prices for a Salesforce field nobody trusts.
