## What it is

Factorial is a Barcelona HR platform for small and mid-sized companies, mostly in Europe. Every plan starts from Factorial Core (employee directory, onboarding and offboarding, document distribution, e-signature, payslip distribution), and you add modules from four groups: Time (time off, time tracking, shifts), Talent (performance, recruitment, engagement, training), Finance (expenses, projects, procurement, corporate cards) and IT (SaaS management, device inventory, MDM). In November 2026 the company reported 17,000 customer companies in more than 90 countries and over 1.3 million users. It is the closest like-for-like rival to [Personio](/en/tools/personio/), at a lower entry price and with a wider non-HR scope.

Three 2026 events change the buying picture:

- **Series D.** On June 3, 2026 Factorial raised $150M at a $2.5B valuation, led by General Catalyst, plus up to $540M from General Catalyst's Customer Value Fund to pre-fund sales and marketing. Germany is its fastest-growing market, at more than 100% a year and over 1,700 customers, with a new Munich office.
- **Factorial One.** The AI layer is built on two agents rather than dozens: an organisation agent that applies company policy across HR, finance and IT, and a personal agent for each employee that drafts documents, answers questions over company data, screens candidates, prepares offers and files expenses. Factorial's rule is that small actions run on their own and larger ones go to a human for approval.
- **Two acquisitions.** YepCode (May 2026) brings an integration and automation platform that Factorial says will let partners publish connectors to its marketplace. Empion (September 2026), a Berlin AI talent-assessment startup, adds behavioral, psychometric, cognitive and culture-fit tests, to be integrated in phases.

## Why it shows up in Recruiting stacks

- **The ATS is in the same record as the employee.** Factorial Recruitment posts to multiple job boards, parses CVs with AI and turns an accepted candidate into an employee profile with onboarding, time-off policy and device assignment attached. For a 50- to 300-person company hiring a few dozen people a year, that removes a separate ATS contract and its HRIS sync.
- **One vendor across HR, finance and IT.** Expenses, cards, SaaS spend and laptop management sit next to the people data, so onboarding can provision a device and close out access on exit without a separate IT tool. Personio and HiBob do not cover the IT side.
- **The agent model is concrete.** One screening candidates and drafting offers is shipped, per Factorial's own November 2026 description, not a roadmap slide. The Empion assessments are not yet in the product; price them as future value.

## Pricing

- **List floor:** "starts at $8 per month, per user" on factorial.com, €8 on the German site. No tiers, module prices, minimums or setup fees are published; every plan is a quote built from Core plus the modules you pick.
- **Real-world band:** third-party marketplace data puts individual modules at **$5.85 to $11.20 per user per month** ([Costbench](https://costbench.com/software/hr/factorial-hr/)). Core plus two modules (say Time and Talent) therefore lands around **$20 to $30 per user per month**, or roughly $24,000 to $36,000 a year at 100 employees, before negotiation. The recruitment module has also been reported as a flat add-on priced by number of open job posts (around $39 to $49 a month for ten), and sources disagree, so get it quoted on its own line.
- **Billing unit:** Factorial bills per user with access, which in an HRIS means effectively every employee. Monthly plans are charged on the headcount at the start of each month.

## Best for

A People Ops lead or founder-operator at a 30- to 500-employee company in Spain, Germany, France, Italy or Portugal who wants HR, a basic ATS, time tracking, expenses and IT on one bill, and hires under roughly 50 people a year. Second fit: a German Mittelstand firm that needs DATEV-ready payroll preparation and time tracking without Personio's price.

Not for you if most of your headcount is in the US (no US payroll or tax filing), if you hire 100+ people a year and recruiting is the main purchase, or if you need full payroll processing across several countries in-house.

## Versus the alternatives

- **[Personio](/en/tools/personio/)** — the DACH market leader and the most direct comparison. Pick Personio when native payroll in Germany and a mature works-council-reviewed AI story matter more than price, and when you do not need IT management.
- **[HiBob](/en/tools/hibob/)** — the other share leader in this band, stronger for distributed UK, US and Israel workforces and culture-heavy People teams. Pick HiBob when the workforce is not concentrated in continental Europe.
- **[Rippling](/en/tools/rippling/)** — the fastest-growing all-in-one, with full payroll, EOR, IT and spend in one record across many countries. Pick Rippling when US headcount is significant or you need payroll processed, not prepared.
- **[BambooHR](/en/tools/bamboohr/)** — published prices and an ATS in the entry tier for US-centric SMBs.
- **Specialist ATS** — [Teamtailor](/en/tools/teamtailor/), [Recruitee](/en/tools/recruitee/) or [Ashby](/en/tools/ashby/) on top of Factorial when hiring volume outgrows the built-in module; see [ATS vs recruiting CRM](/en/learn/ats-vs-recruiting-crm/).

## Watch-outs

- **"Payroll" usually means preparation.** In Germany Factorial prepares payroll and exports to DATEV; elsewhere it relies on local providers, and there is no US tax filing. Guard: for each country you employ in, ask whether Factorial calculates and files payroll itself or hands a file to a provider, and budget that provider separately.
- **Quote-only modules make year two hard to forecast.** Guard: put every module you expect in the first 24 months on the initial order form with per-module prices and a renewal cap.
- **Recruitment depth is SMB-grade.** G2 reviewers flag limits on reporting and customisation, and job-post-based pricing can bite during a hiring push. Guard: run your real pipeline report and a busy-month job count through the trial before you drop a specialist ATS.
- **Agentic screening is high-risk AI.** One screening candidates falls in the EU AI Act's high-risk employment category, and New York City hires trigger [NYC Local Law 144](/en/learn/nyc-local-law-144/) bias audits. Guard: get Factorial's risk classification and human-oversight documentation before switching agent screening on, and keep a recruiter as the decision-maker.
- **Two integrations in flight.** Empion and YepCode are still being folded in. Guard: price only what ships today, and if structured assessments are the reason to buy, keep your current assessment tool until Empion tests are live in your account.
