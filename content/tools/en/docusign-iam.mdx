## What it is

DocuSign Intelligent Agreement Management (IAM) is DocuSign's CLM offering, anchored by the 2024 acquisition of Lexion (the AI-native CLM startup) and integrated with DocuSign's dominant e-signature platform. IAM is positioned as the natural CLM extension for the millions of organizations already using DocuSign for signing — collapsing the contract repository, AI extraction, and workflow into the same vendor relationship rather than bolting on a separate CLM.

## Why it shows up in Legal Ops stacks

- **Native to DocuSign signing.** Every signed document already lives in DocuSign — IAM turns that repository into a structured, searchable, AI-extracted contract database without a separate import or migration.
- **Lexion AI inside.** The 2024 Lexion acquisition gave DocuSign credible clause extraction, summary generation, and obligation tracking competitive with [SirionLabs](/en/tools/sirionlabs/) and [Luminance](/en/tools/luminance/) on AI quality.
- **Procurement-friendly.** Adding IAM to an existing DocuSign contract is a much shorter procurement and security-review cycle than onboarding a new CLM vendor.

## Pricing

- **Custom only.** Sold as an add-on to existing DocuSign signing contracts; effective entry point typically in the mid-five figures annually.
- **Bundled packaging.** Most enterprise customers negotiate IAM as part of an expanded DocuSign agreement rather than a standalone purchase.
- Implementation is faster than greenfield CLM rollouts because the signed-document repository already exists in DocuSign.

## Best for

- Mid-market and enterprise organizations already on DocuSign signing
- Legal Ops teams whose primary CLM-driven win is post-signature visibility (renewal alerts, obligation tracking) over pre-signature workflow
- Organizations where vendor consolidation is a priority and the procurement bar for new CLMs is high

## Watch-outs

- Pre-signature workflow (intake, drafting, negotiation) is less mature than [Ironclad](/en/tools/ironclad/) or [Agiloft](/en/tools/agiloft/); IAM's strength is the post-signature side
- Lexion-acquired AI features are still being integrated into the broader DocuSign platform; verify the specific capability is generally available, not roadmap
- Less customizable workflow engine than Agiloft; less Salesforce-native than [Conga CLM](/en/tools/conga-clm/)
- Pricing leverage exists if you're an existing DocuSign customer; less attractive economics for organizations not already in the DocuSign ecosystem
