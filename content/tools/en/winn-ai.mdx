## What it is

WINN.AI sits in the sales call while it is happening. It listens, tracks which parts of your sales methodology the rep has covered, prompts for the questions still open, surfaces battlecard and knowledge-base answers when a buyer raises an objection, and then writes the qualification fields into the CRM when the call ends. The vendor's homepage names MEDDPICC, SPICED, Gap Selling, BANT and Solution Selling as supported playbooks.

That is a different job from [Gong](/en/tools/gong/), [Attention](/en/tools/attention/) or [Sybill](/en/tools/sybill/), which do most of their work after the call: recording, summarizing, scoring and filling fields from the transcript. WINN.AI's pitch is that a coaching note written on Thursday about a discovery call on Tuesday arrives too late to change the deal.

The company is Tel Aviv-based, founded in 2022 by Eldad Postan-Koren and Bar Haleva, and launched the product in June 2023. It raised an $18M Series A on 11 February 2026, co-led by Insight Partners, Mangusta Capital and S Capital, bringing total funding to $35M. Named customers include Deel, Kaseya, Cyera, Intercom and Snyk. We found no acquisition, shutdown or rebrand news through 19 November 2026.

## Why it shows up in RevOps stacks

- **Methodology adherence is measured where it happens.** Most teams pay for MEDDPICC or SPICED training and then audit adherence from CRM fields reps fill on Friday. WINN.AI tracks coverage during the call and fills the fields from what was actually said. The vendor cites Kaseya at 1,200 reps with 98% less admin and Cyera doubling CRM fill rate; treat both as vendor claims.
- **CRM hygiene without a second tool.** The Pro tier writes up to 25 CRM fields per call and drafts the follow-up email and next steps, so a team that buys it for live guidance usually does not also need a notetaker for writeback.
- **The rep sees the CRM in the call.** In-call CRM visibility and live answers from your knowledge base mean the rep does not alt-tab to Salesforce while the buyer waits.

## Pricing

WINN.AI publishes no prices. Checked on 19 November 2026, `winn.ai/pricing` lists two tiers, both behind "Talk to sales":

- **Pro** — live playbook guidance, knowledge-base answers, AI battlecards, live coaching, in-call CRM visibility, CRM updates for up to 25 fields, call summaries, follow-up emails, meeting prep, Slack updates and cross-call Q&A.
- **Enterprise** — minimum 20 seats. Adds unlimited CRM fields, custom objects, opportunity creation, Slack deal rooms, reporting, a leadership dashboard, SAML SSO through Okta and API access.

No free trial is advertised. The only third-party per-seat figure we found is about $69/user/month, published by a competitor (tl;dv) and originally dated 2024, so treat it as a floor to negotiate from, not a quote. At that rate a 20-seat Enterprise floor is around $16,500/year before any Enterprise uplift. For comparison, [Zoom Revenue Accelerator](/en/tools/zoom-revenue-accelerator/) Premium, which bundles live Sales Assist with recording, lists at $99.99/user/month, and Gong runs roughly $1,400-2,000 per seat per year plus a platform fee.

## Best for

AEs and their frontline managers at B2B companies of roughly 15-300 quota-carrying reps that have already committed to a named methodology (MEDDPICC, SPICED, Gap Selling) and need it executed on live calls rather than reconstructed in pipeline reviews. Strongest when ramping new hires, who benefit most from a prompt telling them which qualification gap is still open.

## Not for

Teams whose main need is a searchable call library, deal-risk scoring across hundreds of recorded conversations, or a forecast. That is post-call conversation intelligence and revenue forecasting, which Gong and [Clari](/en/tools/clari/) sell. It is also the wrong pick for teams without a defined methodology: live prompts against an undefined playbook produce noise. Phone-heavy inside-sales floors should check coverage first; the public pages describe virtual meetings, not dialers.

## Versus the alternatives

- **[Gong](/en/tools/gong/)** — the category leader by share. Pick Gong when the purchase is about call recording, coaching libraries, deal inspection and forecasting across 50+ reps. Pick WINN.AI when the gap is reps skipping qualification steps in the call itself. Some teams run both.
- **[Zoom Revenue Accelerator](/en/tools/zoom-revenue-accelerator/)** — Zoom shipped Sales Assist live guidance in July 2026. Pick ZRA when most calls run on Zoom and you want recording, guidance and published pricing on one contract. Pick WINN.AI when calls span Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams, or when methodology-specific CRM writeback is the deciding feature.
- **[Attention](/en/tools/attention/)** — the fastest-growing entrant in AI CRM writeback, also quote-only. Pick Attention when agentic post-call actions and recordings are the unit of value. Pick WINN.AI when the rep needs help before the call ends.
- **[Sybill](/en/tools/sybill/)** — $90/user/month annual on a public price page, with a first-party MCP server. Pick Sybill for a small team that needs autofill and agent access without a sales cycle.

## Watch-outs

- **No public price and no trial.** Every number in your business case comes from the sales call. Guard: get a written per-seat quote for Pro and Enterprise in the same cycle, ask whether the 20-seat Enterprise minimum is waived for a pilot, and benchmark against ZRA Premium's $99.99 list price before signing.
- **The 25-field cap on Pro.** MEDDPICC is eight fields before next steps, competitors and your own custom fields. Guard: write the exact field list before the demo; if it exceeds 25 or needs custom objects or opportunity creation, price Enterprise from the start.
- **Agent access is thin.** API access is Enterprise-only and we found no MCP server, so an AI agent cannot read WINN.AI call context the way it can with Sybill or Gong's API. Guard: if agent workflows are on your roadmap, make sure WINN.AI writes everything you need into CRM fields, where your agent can read it.
- **Thin independent review signal.** We could not find a meaningful G2 or Capterra review base; most proof points are vendor case studies. Guard: run a 30-day paid pilot with one team, and measure fill rate on your qualification fields and stage-to-stage conversion against a control team.
- **Recording consent and security details are on request.** The homepage claims SOC 2, ISO 27001, ISO 42001 and GDPR; the SOC 2 report type and how participant consent is handled are not public. Guard: request the SOC 2 report and the consent-notice behavior per meeting platform before rollout in EU or two-party-consent US states.
