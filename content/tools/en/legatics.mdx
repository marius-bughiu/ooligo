## What it is

Legatics is a UK-built transaction management platform for deal lawyers: the place where a closing checklist, the conditions precedent tracker, signature pages and the final closing binder live instead of in an Excel tracker and a reply-all chain. The product is five modules — **Lists** (collaborative checklists and CP trackers), **Signing** (e-signature and wet-ink packet management), **Binders** (closing sets), **Data Rooms** and **Admin** — plus an open API and, since July 2026, an MCP server. Legatics says more than 100 law firms use it, including 5 of the AmLaw top 10; logos on its site include Linklaters, A&O Shearman, Baker McKenzie, DLA Piper, Dentons and Pinsent Masons.

Legatics does not ship its own legal AI model. Its 2026 bet is the opposite: be the system of record for the live state of a deal, and let whichever assistant the firm already licenses read it. The MCP server (launched 15 July 2026, included for all customers at no add-on fee) exposes matters, lists, signing status, binders and data rooms. On 13 October 2026 Legatics joined Harvey's Connector Library, so a Harvey user can ask "what is still outstanding on Project Atlas, and who owes it?" across one deal or many. The knowledge base names Harvey, Copilot and Claude as tested clients.

## Why legal ops teams shortlist it

- **The checklist is the deal, and Legatics treats it that way.** Every row carries an owner, a status and the linked document, and every party — including opposing counsel and the client — works the same list. Legatics reports 50% fewer all-party status calls and 85% faster binder creation; both are vendor figures with no published method, so treat them as the ceiling, not the forecast.
- **The MCP server writes, not just reads.** The July launch was read-only. The current knowledge-base article lists write actions — updating statuses and owners, adding rows or parties, creating lists, marking documents for signing — gated by a `read_only` scope an admin can attach to any connected app. Access is three-layered: app scopes, the member's consent, and the member's existing Legatics permissions, over short-lived OAuth 2.0 tokens. An assistant can never do more than the lawyer could do by hand.
- **Fewer tools in the closing room.** The VDR-lite data room shipped in July 2026, and Syntheia document comparison runs inside Lists and data rooms from 25 August 2026, with files never leaving Legatics' infrastructure. For a mid-market financing, that removes the separate data room and the download-compare-reupload loop.
- **Security that passes a panel review.** ISO 27001:2022 certified by the British Assessment Bureau, AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.2+, AWS hosting across US, EU and Australian locations, and bring-your-own-key through AWS KMS on private instances. The security page lists no SOC 2 report, which US banks will ask about.

## Pricing

**Quote-only, verified 25 October 2026.** legatics.com/pricing is a form that promises "a pricing proposal," with no plans, tiers or rates. Third-party listings describe a subscription priced on users or matter workspaces, with enterprise agreements for large firms — Hogan Lovells, Bowmans and King & Wood Mallesons have been reported on firm-wide deals. No independent source publishes a dollar figure, and we will not invent one.

What moves the quote is the licensing unit. A firm-wide enterprise licence makes the marginal deal free, which is what drives adoption; a per-matter licence makes partners ration it to the largest deals, which is how transaction platforms die inside a firm. Ask for the firm-wide number first, even if you plan to start with one practice group. The MCP server and the Harvey connector cost nothing extra.

## Best for

**A legal ops or knowledge lead at a firm with a high-volume finance, M&A or real-estate practice that wants checklists and signings off email, and wants the firm's AI assistant to answer deal-status questions from live data instead of a stale export.** It is the strongest pick when the firm already runs Harvey, Copilot or Claude and does not want a second AI surface inside the closing tool.

**Skip it** if the practice closes a handful of deals a year: a single 2023 G2 review called it a slick demo that did not fit a smaller transactional practice, and the economics agree. A shared spreadsheet and DocuSign envelopes will do.

## Versus the alternatives

- **[Litera](/en/tools/litera/) Transact** — Litera's transaction module; Litera says its products reach 99% of the Am Law 100. Pick Transact when the firm already buys Litera Draft and Compare and wants Lito, Litera's bundled agent, across the whole document lifecycle. Pick Legatics when outside agents need to query or update deal data: Litera has no MCP server for third-party assistants.
- **iManage Closing Folders** — the other large incumbent, rated 4.8 on Capterra across 111 reviews. Pick it when [iManage](/en/tools/imanage/) Work is the firm's DMS and the closing set should file itself there with no second vendor.
- **SimplyAgree** — the US-focused challenger built around automated signature packets and same-day binders for venture and mid-market deals. Pick it for a US-only boutique that wants a narrower, faster signing tool rather than a firm-wide checklist platform.
- **[Datasite](/en/tools/datasite/)** — not a checklist tool, but the right buy when the deal needs a full sell-side data room with Q&A and buyer analytics. The Legatics data room is VDR-lite; it does not replace Datasite on a contested auction.

## Watch-outs

- **Write access turns a status question into a change on the deal.** An assistant that can mark a CP satisfied or add a party is editing the record opposing counsel also reads. Guard: create a separate connected app per AI tool, attach the `read_only` scope to all of them for the first 60 days, and grant write scopes only to named supervising associates once the audit log shows clean usage.
- **The AI tool's retention policy governs what it keeps, not Legatics'.** The knowledge base says so explicitly. Guard: before enabling the connector, confirm the assistant's zero-retention or tenant-isolation terms in writing and record them in the matter-intake risk assessment, especially for deals under NDA with a third-party data room.
- **Adoption depends on the other side.** A checklist only one firm updates becomes a prettier spreadsheet. Guard: put Legatics in the engagement letter or the first all-parties email as the system of record, and run the first two deals with a named associate who owns list hygiene.
- **No SOC 2.** Guard: ask for the ISO 27001 Statement of Applicability and the latest penetration-test summary before a US bank client's vendor review, not during it.
