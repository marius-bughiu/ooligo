## What it is

BrightHire is the interview-intelligence platform purpose-built for recruiting — the same product category as [Gong](/en/tools/gong/) for sales, but applied to candidate interviews instead of customer calls. Records, transcribes, and AI-analyzes every interview to surface decision-grade evidence, identify interviewer-bias patterns, and improve hiring quality through structured-interview adoption.

## Why it shows up in Recruiting stacks

- **Decision evidence on demand.** When the hiring committee debates a candidate, BrightHire surfaces the exact moments from the interview where the contested signal appeared. Decisions stop being "I felt it went well" and start being "here's the moment that supports the call."
- **Interviewer coaching loop.** AI flags interviewers who skipped required questions, talked over the candidate, or used leading-question patterns. The coaching feedback is specific, evidence-based, and actionable — not generic.
- **[Structured interviewing](/en/learn/structured-interviewing/) infrastructure.** Standardizes scorecards across interviewers, enforces required questions per interview type, makes the structured-interviewing discipline operational rather than aspirational.

## Pricing

- **Custom only.** Per-interviewer or per-interview pricing depending on packaging; effective rates from $50-150/interviewer/month at mid-market scale.
- **Annual contracts** typical for enterprise; pilot programs common for evaluating before broader deployment.
- Implementation is fast — typically 30-60 days.

## Best for

- Mid-market and enterprise companies hiring 100+ roles per year where interview quality directly impacts business outcomes
- Engineering, product, and sales-leadership hiring where decision-grade evidence matters more than raw throughput
- Companies under pressure to reduce hiring bias and produce defensible audit trails of interview decisions

## Watch-outs

- Less useful for high-volume, low-judgment hiring (warehouse, retail) where the cost-per-interview-analyzed exceeds the per-hire value
- AI features compete with [Metaview](/en/tools/metaview/), [Hume](https://hume.ai/), and increasingly direct [Claude](/en/tools/claude/) Skills built against meeting transcripts — BrightHire's lead is in the recruiting-specific workflow integration
- Privacy/consent considerations — recording interviews requires candidate notice in most jurisdictions; verify the consent flow per region
- Pair with the ATS ([Ashby](/en/tools/ashby/), [Greenhouse](/en/tools/greenhouse/), [Lever](/en/tools/lever/)) — BrightHire is the interview layer, not the funnel-tracking layer
