## What it is

Navattic is a no-code interactive demo platform. A Chrome extension captures your product's HTML and CSS, and the result is a clickable clone of the app that a prospect can use on your website, in an email, or through a shared link, with no login and no trial account. Capture is HTML-only: there is no screenshot-slideshow mode and no video export, though you can place uploaded media inside a demo. Every demo therefore behaves like the product rather than a recording of it. Navattic says it has more than 1,000 customers, and it holds a 4.7/5 rating on G2 across roughly 824 reviews. In 2026 it added Navattic Agents, AI agents that click through the captured demo while answering a visitor's questions, and an MCP server.

## Why it shows up in RevOps stacks

- **PLG website demos that behave like the product.** Because each demo is an HTML clone, a visitor can type into fields, open menus, and move between screens. Marketing puts it behind "Take a tour" next to "Book a demo" and reaches the self-serve evaluator who would never fill in a form.
- **Demo engagement becomes routing data.** Account identification (Base and above) names the companies viewing a demo. The integrations sync engagement to [HubSpot](/en/tools/hubspot/) Contacts and Companies and to [Salesforce](/en/tools/salesforce/) Leads, Contacts, and Accounts, checking for an existing account before creating one. Playbooks fire a [Slack](/en/tools/slack/) or email alert when a target account finishes a high-intent flow. Navattic routes its own product-qualified leads this way with [Default](/en/tools/default/), per a Default case study.
- **Salesforce on the 5-seat tier.** Navattic's pricing page lists Salesforce, Marketo, "and 25+ more" on Base, the $500/month tier. [Storylane](/en/tools/storylane/) holds Salesforce back until its $1,200/month Premium plan, and Arcade until Enterprise.
- **An MCP server with write access, from the $125 plan.** The Navattic MCP server is available from Startup upward. [Claude](/en/tools/claude/) and [ChatGPT](/en/tools/chatgpt/) connect over OAuth; [Cursor](/en/tools/cursor/) and other clients use a personal access token. Agents can read analytics, visitor data, and Navattic Agents conversations, and they can build, edit, publish, and archive demos and create one-to-one Launchpad share links.

## Pricing

Navattic took list prices off its pricing page in 2026; the page now shows plan contents with quote buttons. The last published figures, from Navattic's own June 2026 blog and a Supademo pricing teardown dated 2026-09-03:

- **Starter — $0.** 1 seat, 1 demo, basic Copilot demo generation.
- **Startup — $125/month billed annually** ($150 monthly), 1 seat. Unlimited HTML demos; HubSpot, Slack, email, and webhook with one playbook each; the MCP server. Limited to bootstrapped or seed-stage companies with 30 or fewer employees.
- **Base — $500/month billed annually** ($600 monthly), 5 seats. Adds Salesforce, Marketo, and 25+ other integrations with unlimited playbooks, account identification, A/B testing, pipeline-impact reporting, and a dedicated CSM.
- **Growth — $1,000/month billed annually** ($1,200 monthly), 10 seats. Adds [Gong](/en/tools/gong/), [Salesloft](/en/tools/salesloft/), [Outreach](/en/tools/outreach/), Gmail, Outlook, [Seismic](/en/tools/seismic/), and Highspot, buyer-circle discovery, AI avatar cloning, custom domains, and SSO.
- **Enterprise — custom.** Adds offline demos, audit logs, professional services, and priority support.
- **What teams pay.** Vendr puts the median Navattic contract at $8,500 a year across 99 purchases, in a $5,000 to $18,000 range, with 15-30% below list achievable on annual prepay and multi-year terms. That median sits between Base ($6,000 a year at list) and Growth ($12,000). Budget for Base if Salesforce is your CRM, and for Growth once sales-engagement or enablement sync is on the requirements list.

## Best for

- Product marketing and growth leaders at PLG or hybrid B2B SaaS companies who want a hands-on HTML demo on the website feeding HubSpot or Salesforce routing, from seed stage (Startup plan) up to teams of 5-10 demo builders
- RevOps and demand-gen teams who need the Salesforce integration on a 5-seat budget ($6,000 a year list) rather than a 10-seat one
- Not for teams that want video or GIF-style tours for launch posts and social channels (Navattic exports no video), not for enterprise presales teams whose SEs need data-edited sandboxes for every prospect (look at Consensus or Walnut), and not for products whose UI changes weekly, because HTML captures do not update themselves

## Alternatives — and when to pick them instead

- **[Storylane](/en/tools/storylane/)** — the rival in nearly every head-to-head, independent, with 5,000+ customer teams. It covers screenshot, video, and HTML capture in one library, starts at $40/month billed annually, and adds sandbox and presenter demos plus an AI SDR (RepX). Pick Storylane when marketing needs screenshot or video tours and sales needs live-call demos from the same tool. Pick Navattic when HTML website demos are the whole job and Salesforce has to be on a 5-seat plan.
- **Arcade** — the widest free footprint (20,000+ companies, per company-profile data). Growth is $42.50/month billed annually for up to 10 seats but has no HTML capture; Arcade reserves HTML and native Salesforce for Enterprise. Pick Arcade when the demo is a polished screenshot or video tour for launches and social posts rather than a pipeline asset.
- **Supademo** — the fastest-growing entrant, ranked by G2 among its fastest-growing products, with its own AI Demo Agent since June 2026. HTML demos start on its Growth plan at $350/month for 5 creators, against $500/month for 5 seats on Navattic Base. Pick Supademo when you want HTML and screenshot demos at a lower price and can live with a smaller integration list.
- **Consensus** — presales-first: video and interactive demos sent to the buying committee, with analytics on which stakeholders watched what. It agreed to acquire Peel, a conversational demo agent, in April 2026. Starter is $600/month billed annually for 5 users with marketing integrations only; Salesforce, Gong, and Outreach arrive on Pro at $1,250/month for 10 users. Pick Consensus when the buyer is an enterprise committee and SE capacity, not website conversion, is the bottleneck.
- **Walnut** — sales-led and enterprise-priced: a Vendr median of $17,000 a year, with no free tier. Pick Walnut when SEs need data-edited sandbox demos across a large sales team and budget is not the constraint.

## Watch-outs

- **List prices are no longer public.** Navattic removed them from its pricing page in 2026 and quotes each deal. Guard: bring the last published figures above and the $8,500 Vendr median to the first call, get the annual rate in writing, and run a competing Storylane or Supademo quote; Vendr reports 15-30% off list on annual and multi-year terms.
- **One capture type, no video.** Navattic outputs HTML clones only. Guard: if the same tour must also run as a video on LinkedIn, a GIF in a launch email, or a clip in the help center, budget a second tool (Arcade or Supademo) instead of forcing Navattic into that job.
- **Captures go stale when your UI ships.** An HTML clone shows the product as it looked on capture day, and re-capture is manual. Guard: give every live demo a named owner, re-capture after each major UI release, and retire any demo untouched for two quarters.
- **The Startup plan ends at Series A.** It is limited to bootstrapped or seed-stage companies with 30 or fewer employees, and the step to Base quadruples the bill ($1,500 to $6,000 a year at list). Guard: if you expect to raise or pass 30 people inside the contract term, negotiate the Base price at signing.
- **Agents are early, and the MCP server can publish.** Navattic has published no customer results for Navattic Agents, and the MCP server can publish and archive demos with the connected user's permissions. Guard: pilot Agents on one page against a baseline of meetings booked per 1,000 demo visitors, and connect MCP through a Viewer-role user, for whom Navattic disables write scopes, until a human reviews what agents change.
