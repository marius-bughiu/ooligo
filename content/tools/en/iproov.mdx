## What it is

iProov is a London biometric-verification company whose core product is face liveness: proving that a face presented to a camera belongs to a live person in front of that camera, not a photo, a mask, a replayed video, or an injected deepfake stream. Governments and banks use it for remote onboarding and account recovery. Its homepage lists the UK Home Office, NHS, ING, UBS and Rabobank among its customers, and it holds a US Department of Homeland Security contract. In March 2026 the company said it processed more than one million verifications a day during 2025.

The recruiting product is **Verified Meetings**, announced May 19, 2026. It is a plugin inside the video-conferencing client. During a call the host triggers a check, iProov analyses the participant's live video for deepfakes and presentation attacks, and it separately checks whether the feed comes from a physical camera or a virtual one. The host sees a red, amber, or green status, and a post-call summary is kept for audit records. Per the launch release, the check runs in the background and the participant is not told it is happening. Verified Meetings is the "pre-join" stage of iProov's Workforce Solution Suite, launched March 9, 2026, which also covers onboarding, privileged-access step-up, shared-device access, and help-desk account recovery.

## Why it shows up in recruiting stacks now

- **The virtual-camera check targets how interview fraud actually gets in.** A face-swap or proxy feed almost always enters a call through virtual-camera software. iProov's own threat report counted a 2,665% year-on-year rise in native virtual-camera attacks in 2024. That is a vendor figure about its own traffic, not a hiring fraud rate, but it explains why a hardware-integrity signal sits beside the image analysis.
- **Its liveness engine carries independent certification.** iProov lists ISO/IEC 30107-3 presentation-attack testing by iBeta, CEN/TS 18099 injection-attack testing at Ingenium Level 4, FIDO face-verification certification, and UK DIATF certification issued in October 2025. These certify the liveness products. None of iProov's pages say Verified Meetings itself has been tested, so treat that as an open question for the pilot.
- **It extends past the interview.** The same suite covers onboarding and help-desk resets, the two other places a fraudulent hire, or an attacker posing as an employee, has to get through. When IT already buys iProov for workforce MFA or account recovery, interview checks ride on that contract.

## What iProov has not published

The launch materials leave three buying questions open, so get each answer in writing before a pilot. **Platforms:** no iProov page names Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Webex, or Google Meet, only "video conferencing platforms." **Identity versus liveness:** nothing says whether Verified Meetings matches the face to an enrolled identity or an ID document, or only confirms that a live human is on a real camera. A live, real proxy interviewer passes a liveness-only check. **Timing:** the press release calls it pre-join, the product page describes an on-demand check during the call.

## Pricing reality

**iProov publishes no price for Verified Meetings.** The site offers demos only, with no trial and no self-serve plan. The one public price point is iProov's UK G-Cloud 14 price list, open to public-sector buyers only, which put Dynamic Liveness at roughly £0.61 to £0.91 per transaction depending on volume tier. Read that as the order of magnitude per check, not a quote for a meeting plugin.

For comparison, [Persona](/en/tools/persona/) starts at $250 a month for 500 verifications, [Checkr](/en/tools/checkr/) adds identity verification to a background report for $4.99, and [Reality Defender](/en/tools/reality-defender/) sells a Business API plan at $399 billed annually for 1,000 scans. On the first call, ask what the meter is: checks triggered, hosts licensed, or employees covered by the workforce suite. A recruiting team triggers a check on each final-round candidate, which is a small number next to a workforce MFA deployment.

## Best for

A TA leader at an enterprise or regulated employer, such as a bank, government contractor, or healthcare system, hiring remote staff into privileged IT, engineering, or finance roles, where the security or IAM team already runs iProov or is choosing a workforce identity vendor. For that buyer, Verified Meetings adds a camera-integrity signal in the final round without a separate detection vendor.

**Do not buy it** to run hiring on its own budget if you make fewer than about 50 remote hires a year, hire mostly in person, or have no identity check anywhere else in the funnel. A $4.99 check at offer does more for you first.

## Watch-outs

- **A check the candidate does not know about creates a consent problem.** Illinois roles fall under the [AI Video Interview Act](/en/learn/illinois-ai-video-interview-act/), which requires notice and consent before AI analysis of interview video. Facial analysis can also trigger Illinois BIPA and GDPR biometric-data rules. **Guard:** keep the silent mode switched off for candidates. Put a notice in every interview invite, collect consent, and get retention and deletion terms for post-call summaries into the contract.
- **A red status is a probability, and base rates make most flags land on genuine candidates.** **Guard:** route amber and red results to an identity re-check, never to a rejection. The [interview-fraud screening skill](/en/workflows/ai-interview-fraud-detection-skill/) works the false-positive arithmetic through.
- **Runway.** iProov's 2024 accounts show revenue of £28.3M, a £5.6M net loss (down from £9.6M in 2023), and cash of £21.1M, down about 35% in a year. The directors state there is enough working capital for at least 12 months. **Guard:** cap the first term at 12 months, ask for the 2025 accounts, and keep the interview check replaceable rather than wiring it into the ATS.

## Alternatives, and when to pick them instead

- **An identity gate first: [Persona](/en/tools/persona/), or [CLEAR](/en/tools/clear/) inside [Greenhouse](/en/tools/greenhouse/).** Pick this when you can fund one control. A document-plus-selfie check at a named stage proves who the person is, which a liveness check on a call may not. See [AI interview fraud](/en/learn/ai-interview-fraud/) for why the gate comes first.
- **[GetReal Security](/en/tools/getreal-security/).** Runs on Teams, Webex, and Zoom and, since May 2026, keeps verifying face and voice through the call after a consented enrollment, so it can tell whether the final round is the same person as the screen. Pick it when proxy interviewers between rounds are the threat.
- **[Pindrop](/en/tools/pindrop/) Pulse for Meetings.** Voice-led detection on Zoom, Teams, and Webex. Pick it when phone screens matter most or your contact centre already runs Pindrop.
- **[Reality Defender](/en/tools/reality-defender/).** The only vendor here with a self-serve API, so you can test detection on recorded interviews before any sales cycle.

Choose iProov over these when IT is already standardising on it for workforce identity. See the [hiring integrity stack](/en/stacks/hiring-integrity-stack/) for where a detection layer sits against the identity gate and post-offer screening.
