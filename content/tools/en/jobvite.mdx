## What it is

Jobvite is an established mid-market ATS, now part of the Employ Inc. portfolio (which also includes JazzHR and Lever post-2022 acquisitions). Founded in 2006, Jobvite was an early adopter of social recruiting and employee referrals; the platform now spans applicant tracking, recruiting marketing, video interviewing, and onboarding for organizations in the 500-5,000 employee range.

## Why it shows up in Recruiting stacks

- **Strong recruiting marketing layer.** Career site builder, candidate engagement campaigns, talent community management — the demand-side of recruiting that pure ATS platforms don't address well.
- **Employee referral depth.** Jobvite originated as a social/referral platform; the referral workflow is more sophisticated than most competitors offer (gamification, leaderboards, multi-touch nurture).
- **Mid-market enterprise integrations.** Workday, SAP SuccessFactors, Oracle HCM integrations are mature; reduces the integration risk that derails ATS deployments at this scale.

## Pricing

- **Custom only.** Per-employee or per-hire pricing depending on packaging; effective entry point in the mid-five figures annually for mid-market.
- **Module packaging.** Jobvite ATS, Jobvite Engage (CRM), Jobvite Video, Jobvite Onboard separately licensed.
- Implementation typically 60-120 days.

## Best for

- Mid-market companies (500-5,000 employees) outgrowing SMB ATS but not at enterprise complexity
- Organizations with strong employee referral programs as a hiring channel
- Companies replacing legacy ATS where Workday or SAP SuccessFactors integration depth matters

## Watch-outs

- AI features lag the modern leaders ([Ashby](/en/tools/ashby/), [SmartRecruiters](/en/tools/smartrecruiters/) post-AI investment); Employ Inc. ownership has slowed product velocity in some areas
- UI feels established-software dated next to AI-native challengers
- For SMB and growth-stage, [Workable](/en/tools/workable/), [Recruitee](/en/tools/recruitee/), or [Ashby](/en/tools/ashby/) typically better fits
- For enterprise with global complexity, [iCIMS](/en/tools/icims/) or [SmartRecruiters](/en/tools/smartrecruiters/) typically wins on governance and global capabilities
