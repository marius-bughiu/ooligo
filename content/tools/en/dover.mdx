## What it is

Dover is the AI-native recruiting platform built around the assumption that an early-stage startup's recruiting workflow should be largely automated — sourcing, outreach, scheduling, and CRM should all happen without dedicated recruiter headcount. Founded in 2019 by ex-Facebook and Stanford engineers, Dover targets the seed-to-Series-B segment that needs to hire fast but can't justify in-house recruiters yet.

## Why it shows up in Recruiting stacks

- **Free tier that actually delivers value.** Free Dover account includes AI-augmented sourcing, basic outreach automation, and ATS-style funnel tracking. The tool is genuinely usable without a paid contract — atypical in the recruiting category.
- **Founder-led recruiting workflow.** Built around the founder or ops generalist who's hiring without a recruiter — minimal setup, clear suggestions, opinionated defaults rather than infinite configurability.
- **Pre-vetted talent network on top of AI sourcing.** Dover Talent Network curates pre-screened candidates that match common startup hiring profiles (product manager, growth marketer, full-stack engineer); reduces the from-scratch sourcing burden.

## Pricing

- **Free** — basic AI sourcing, outreach, and CRM
- **Paid tiers** — custom pricing for advanced features (Dover Talent Network access, recruiting service, advanced analytics)
- Pricing model differs from per-seat ATS — closer to per-hire economics for paid features

## Best for

- Seed and Series A startups (under 75 employees) hiring 5-25 roles per year without dedicated recruiters
- Founders and ops generalists doing recruiting alongside their primary role
- Engineering and product hiring at early-stage tech companies

## Watch-outs

- Outgrown quickly by mid-market companies (50+ hires/year, dedicated recruiting team) — at that scale [Ashby](/en/tools/ashby/), [Greenhouse](/en/tools/greenhouse/), or [Workable](/en/tools/workable/) plus specialist sourcing ([Gem](/en/tools/gem/), [juicebox](/en/tools/juicebox/)) becomes the right stack
- Less mature ATS integrations than [Gem](/en/tools/gem/) — verify Dover-to-ATS data flow if running both
- AI sourcing quality variable for non-tech roles (sales, marketing, ops) where signal is sparser
- Dover's recruiting-as-a-service offering is a different product from the self-serve platform — verify which you're buying
