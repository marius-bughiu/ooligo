If you're considering moving off Paradox, you're usually a high-volume hourly or frontline employer who's hit one of two walls: the conversational experience is good but the candidate-pipeline analytics are thinner than your TA team needs, or the per-conversation pricing has become hard to justify as your AI screening volume grew. Paradox remains the dominant conversational AI for hourly hiring — but the alternatives have closed the gap.

## Sense

The other major candidate-engagement platform, with stronger CRM and nurturing capabilities. Sense's strength is the multi-channel engagement orchestration (SMS, email, voice) and the built-in analytics on candidate pipeline health, which Paradox treats more as a chat layer.

**Migrate from Paradox to Sense when:** your TA team needs deeper pipeline analytics, you run multi-channel nurture campaigns to passive candidates, or you have a meaningful redeployment motion (re-engaging former candidates) that benefits from CRM-style records.

**Don't migrate when:** your primary use case is high-volume hourly screening at the top of the funnel. Paradox's conversational experience is still the best in that segment.

## Gem

Gem isn't a true Paradox alternative for hourly hiring — it's a different shape, focused on professional sourcing and outreach. But for hybrid orgs running both hourly and corporate hiring, Gem can replace Paradox's professional-side use cases while you keep something else for hourly.

**Pick Gem over Paradox when:** your actual need is professional sourcing and CRM, and you adopted Paradox for the wrong job. **Don't pick when:** you're a high-volume hourly employer — Gem is not built for that motion.

## Stay on Paradox when

- You're a high-volume hourly or frontline employer (retail, QSR, healthcare, logistics)
- Your top-of-funnel screening volume is over 10,000 candidates per month
- Olivia's brand and conversational quality matter to your candidate experience
- Your ATS integrations (Workday, iCIMS, etc.) are wired into Paradox-specific events

## Verdict

- **Sense** is the right migration for ~30% of teams thinking about leaving Paradox — the ones that need richer engagement analytics
- **Gem** is right for ~10% — hybrid orgs where professional sourcing is the real job
- **Staying on Paradox** is the right answer for ~60% — pure high-volume hourly employers where the screening experience is the load-bearing capability

The single mistake to avoid: switching conversational AI tools because the chat scripts feel stale. Rewrite the scripts. The platform isn't the problem.
