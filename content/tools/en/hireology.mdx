## What it is

Hireology is an applicant tracking system for employers that hire the same frontline roles across many sites: car dealership groups, home-care and senior-living operators, and hospitality franchises. Adam Robinson co-founded it in Chicago in 2010, and he is still CEO. It has raised about $60.5M, the company's own total after a $27M Series D led by Blue Cloud Ventures in 2019, with Bain Capital Ventures and Baird among earlier investors. It is privately held and independent. Along the way it bought the referral tool EmployUs (2021) and the customer base of Hyrell, a franchise ATS (2022).

The data model is the reason to look at it. Every job, career site, report and dashboard sits in a location and organization hierarchy, so a dealer group can see which store is slow to call applicants back while each general manager hires for their own rooftop.

On 21 October 2026 Hireology made its AI Hiring Intelligence suite generally available. The open question for buyers is whether those features shorten hiring enough to justify the upper plan.

## What the AI features do

- **AI Match** scores every applicant at the moment they apply against attributes the employer sets for the role, including certifications and licenses. It shows the evidence behind each score and flags potentially discriminatory criteria during setup. Hireology says it is trained on role types from automotive, healthcare and hospitality.
- **AI Candidate Summary** turns each application into a short brief: strengths, gaps, and suggested interview questions.
- **AI Interview** is a voice screen candidates take by SMS or email link at any hour, in a supported language of their choice. Every applicant gets the same structured questions, and answers are scored against a rubric with a full transcript. Hireology states that it does not advance or decline anyone automatically.

The headline number is vendor data: across 614 customers evaluating 46,618 applicants in 2026, accounts using AI Match cut average time to hire from 15.5 to 12.4 days compared with their own pre-launch baseline. Service technicians went from 17.9 to 14.0 days and caregivers from 13.8 to 10.5. The comparison is before-and-after, with no control group, and it covers AI Match, not AI Interview. It does not show that the voice screen saves time. For how to measure this yourself, see [time to fill vs time to hire](/en/learn/time-to-fill-vs-time-to-hire/).

Hireology works with [Warden AI](/en/tools/warden-ai/) to test its AI features for disparate impact across race, sex, age and disability, and publishes the results in a Trust Center.

## Pricing reality

The [packages page](https://www.hireology.com/packages), checked 16 November 2026, shows no prices. It lists two plans, both with unlimited jobs and users:

- **Core**: branded career sites per location, job distribution, automated interview scheduling, bulk text and email, QR-code flyers, cross-location dashboards, and HRIS and scheduling integrations.
- **Perform**: everything in Core plus AI Match, AI Candidate Summary, and third-party fairness audits.

AI Interview is a paid upgrade sold only on Perform. Other add-ons are Enhanced Onboarding, background checks through Accurate Background, skills tests, reference checks through SkillSurvey, and payroll and HR through Netchex.

Third-party figures are estimates. Spendflo and Capterra list an entry price of $249 a month, and customer reports collected by Toolchase put typical deployments at $200–500 per location per month depending on hiring volume and modules. If the unit really is per location, a 10-store dealer group would pay roughly $24K–60K a year before add-ons. For scale, ooligo's own pages put [Fountain](/en/tools/fountain/)'s entry near $10,000 a year and small [Paradox](/en/tools/paradox/) deployments at about $1,000–2,000 a month plus implementation.

## Best for

Talent or HR leads at multi-location employers with roughly 5–200 sites, where general managers or administrators hire locally and someone at head office needs to see every location's pipeline. The clearest fit is a dealer group hiring service technicians and sales staff, or a home-care operator hiring caregivers and CNAs.

**Not for you if** you run fewer than three locations (a cheaper SMB ATS like [JazzHR](/en/tools/jazzhr/) covers it), if you want payroll and scheduling in the same system as hiring, or if Workday is already your HCM.

## Versus the alternatives

- **[Paradox](/en/tools/paradox/)**: the market leader in conversational hourly hiring, owned by Workday since October 2025. Pick it when Workday is your system of record or when you hire thousands of hourly workers a month through chat.
- **[Fountain](/en/tools/fountain/)**: pick it for gig, logistics and enterprise frontline hiring at very high volume, run by a central TA team.
- **[Workstream](/en/tools/workstream/)**: pick it for quick-service and retail franchisees who want hiring, onboarding and payroll in one product.
- **[Humanly](/en/tools/humanly/)**: the best-funded recent entrant, with a $25M Series B in May 2026. Pick it as an AI screening layer on an ATS you keep, not as a replacement for one.

**The rule:** if your roles need licenses or certifications and each location hires for itself, pick Hireology. If hiring is high-volume and centrally run, pick Fountain or Paradox. If the next step after "hired" is a payroll system you do not have, pick Workstream. For the full build, see the [high-volume recruiting stack](/en/stacks/high-volume-recruiting-stack/) and the [Fountain vs Paradox](/en/vs/fountain-vs-paradox/) comparison.

## Watch-outs

- **The AI scores still shape decisions.** No automatic rejection does not mean no regulatory exposure: a score a manager relies on can count as an automated employment decision tool under NYC LL 144, and Illinois has required notice of AI use in employment decisions since 1 January 2026. **Guard:** have a person review every low AI Interview or AI Match score before a rejection, give candidates the required notice, and ask for Warden AI's audit summary for your role types before going live in New York or Illinois. See [AI screening bias](/en/learn/ai-screening-bias/).
- **AI Interview costs extra on top of the upper plan.** Getting voice screening means paying for Perform plus an upgrade, and the time-to-hire evidence is for AI Match only. **Guard:** buy Perform first, measure time to hire for one quarter, and add AI Interview only for roles where phone screening is still the bottleneck.
- **Reviewers flag assessment and onboarding issues.** G2 rates Hireology 4.5/5 across about 1,370 reviews, but the most common complaints are unreliable assessments, limited candidate filters, technical problems in onboarding, and missing HRIS integrations. **Guard:** have Hireology run a live sync to your HRIS or payroll system during the trial, and get the integration written into the order form.
