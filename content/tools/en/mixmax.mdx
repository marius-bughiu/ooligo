## What it is

Mixmax is sales engagement that lives inside Gmail. Sequences (email, call, task and LinkedIn steps), open and click tracking, one-click meeting scheduling, templates, follow-up reminders, an AI meeting notetaker and CRM logging all run from the rep's inbox through a Chrome extension, instead of from a separate platform the rep has to open. It is an independent San Francisco company, not a sending-infrastructure tool and not a data provider: it has no contact database, and it assumes your reps already know who they are writing to.

That is the trade-off our [sales engagement roundup](/en/vs/best-sales-engagement-platforms-2026/) left out when it filed Mixmax under "wrong category". For an account-executive team or a small sales team that lives in Gmail, Mixmax covers most of what they would use from [Outreach](/en/tools/outreach/) or [Salesloft](/en/tools/salesloft/) at a fraction of the price and with almost no rollout. What it does not do is run a high-volume SDR floor.

## Pricing reality

Mixmax repackaged four times in 2026. The version live since October 2026 drops the old Inbox, Engagement, Suite and Teams tiers. There is now one paid plan with every feature included, priced per user by how many people that user can add to sequences each month:

- **Free** — 50 recipients a month, limited features, no MCP access
- **$29/user/mo** — 100 recipients a month
- **$89/user/mo** — 1,500 recipients a month (the tier the 14-day trial runs on)
- **$129/user/mo** — 3,000 recipients a month

Those prices are billed annually. Monthly billing costs more: Mixmax says annual saves 15-25% depending on tier, and the $29 tier is $34 month to month. The features table mentions up to 15,000 recipients a month, but anything above 3,000 goes through sales. Dialer, custom branding, advanced workspace rules and the Salesforce Insights package are each $9/member/mo on top ($12 monthly), and dialer minutes are billed separately.

The counting rules decide the bill. A recipient is one person added to one sequence: a five-step sequence to one person counts once, emails sent outside sequences do not count, and the same contact in two sequences counts twice. Allowances are per user, reset each calendar month, do not roll over and cannot be pooled across the team.

What that means in practice: five AEs who each enroll around 80 contacts a month fit the $29 tier, or $1,740/year for the team. Five SDRs enrolling 1,000 a month each need the $89 tier, or $5,340/year. That is still below [Apollo](/en/tools/apollo/) Basic at $49/user/mo plus a data line, and well below Outreach's reported ~$100-130/user/mo before implementation.

## Best for

- **AEs and founder-led sales teams of 2-25 reps on Google Workspace** who follow up on known contacts and want sequences, tracking and scheduling without a second app to live in.
- **RevOps leads who need activity in Salesforce or HubSpot** without asking reps to log anything: every send and reply goes to the CRM automatically.
- **Teams that want an assistant to run follow-up.** The MCP server lets Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor read sequence performance and meeting notes, draft sequences and enroll contacts from a prompt.

## The MCP server

Mixmax's MCP server went live as a beta on 13 April 2026, through Mixmax Labs, on paid seats and trials at no extra cost. It uses OAuth rather than API keys and shows each user only their own data. Reads are on by default: sequences and their insights, recipients, templates, meeting summaries and transcripts, tasks. Write actions are opt-in, one switch each: create and edit sequences, enroll recipients and activate a sequence, create templates and tasks, and send or schedule real email. Mixmax says capabilities may later move into specific plans and that the tool list is not a stability guarantee.

## Versus the alternatives

- **[Outreach](/en/tools/outreach/) and [Salesloft](/en/tools/salesloft/)** are the share leaders in sales engagement. Pick either when you run 30+ outbound reps who need a dialer floor, territory and team governance, deal inspection and manager coaching in one permissioned system. Pick Mixmax when reps work from the inbox and that governance would sit unused.
- **[Apollo](/en/tools/apollo/)** is the fastest-growing player in the segment and bundles a contact database with its sequencer. Pick Apollo when the bottleneck is finding who to write to; pick Mixmax when the contacts already exist in your CRM and the problem is follow-through.
- **[HubSpot](/en/tools/hubspot/) Sales Hub sequences** are the status-quo option for HubSpot shops. If you already pay for Sales Hub Professional, use its sequences first; add Mixmax only if reps reject working outside Gmail.
- **Yesware** is the closest like-for-like inbox tool and the default when your reps live in Outlook.

For raw cold email across many domains, Mixmax is the wrong tool: use [Instantly](/en/tools/instantly/) or [lemlist](/en/tools/lemlist/).

## Watch-outs

- **Recipient counting punishes overlapping sequences.** The same contact in two sequences burns two recipients, and unused allowance on one rep cannot cover another. Guard: before you buy, pull each rep's sequence enrollments for the last three months from the CRM, size each rep's tier on their own peak month, and merge sequences that target the same contacts.
- **It is built around Gmail.** The product is a Chrome extension for Gmail. An Outlook add-in is listed on Microsoft's marketplace, but independent 2026 reviews still describe Mixmax as Gmail-only in practice. Guard: if any reps use Outlook, put them in the 14-day trial and confirm sequences, tracking and scheduling work for them before you sign.
- **The MCP can send real email.** With "send email on your behalf" or sequence activation switched on, a bad prompt reaches real prospects. Guard: leave send switched off, have the assistant enroll recipients as drafts, and let a person activate the sequence.
- **Packaging keeps moving.** Four packaging changes in 2026, including a cap on the old Teams plan in July and a full model change in October. Guard: sign annually with the recipient tier and a renewal price cap written into the order form, and re-check the pricing page before every renewal.
- **No data underneath.** Mixmax does not find contacts. Guard: budget a data source separately, whether Apollo, a ZoomInfo seat or a Clay workflow, and compare that combined cost with Apollo alone.
