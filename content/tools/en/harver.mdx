## What it is

Harver is the talent-assessment platform formed by the 2022 merger of Harver (high-volume hiring assessment) and Pymetrics (game-based behavioral assessment). Now positioned as the assessment platform for high-volume hourly and frontline hiring — situational judgment tests, behavioral games, cognitive assessments designed for the populations that fill retail, hospitality, healthcare, and gig-economy roles at scale.

## Why it shows up in Recruiting stacks

- **Built for high-volume hourly hiring.** Where [HackerRank](/en/tools/hackerrank/) is for engineering and [Plum](/en/tools/plum/) is for behavioral fit on knowledge work, Harver targets the high-volume frontline hiring market — different candidate populations, different assessment formats, different validity considerations.
- **Behavioral fit for high-volume roles.** Customer-service, retail, hospitality roles benefit from behavioral and situational-judgment assessment in ways generic skills tests don't capture.
- **Pymetrics-acquired game-based assessment.** Game-based assessment delivers behavioral signal with materially better candidate experience than traditional questionnaire-style assessments.

## Pricing

- **Custom only.** Per-assessment or per-hire pricing depending on volume; effective rates favorable for high-volume programs.
- **Volume tiers** designed for organizations running thousands of assessments per month.
- Implementation typically 60-90 days including initial validation work.

## Best for

- Retail, restaurant, hospitality chains running high-volume frontline hiring
- Healthcare systems hiring nursing assistants, technicians, support staff at scale
- Gig-economy platforms screening contractor applicants

## Watch-outs

- Behavioral and game-based assessment validity is contested in the research literature — meta-analyses show modest predictive power; depends heavily on calibration and use case
- Bias-audit obligations under [EU AI Act](/en/learn/eu-ai-act-for-legal-teams/), NYC Local Law 144, Illinois AVDA particularly important for AI-augmented assessment used in high-volume hiring
- Wrong scope for senior knowledge-worker roles — for those use [HackerRank](/en/tools/hackerrank/), [Vervoe](/en/tools/vervoe/), or [structured interviewing](/en/learn/structured-interviewing/) instead
- Compete head-to-head with native screening features in [Fountain](/en/tools/fountain/) and increasingly with AI conversational screening tools
