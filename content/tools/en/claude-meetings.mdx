## What it is

Claude Meetings is Anthropic's meeting agent. You add Claude to a Google Meet call, it joins as a visible participant, posts a notice in the call chat, and leaves a meeting page in claude.ai with a summary, action items and a transcript. Anthropic announced it on November 10, 2026 and began rolling it out in beta on Team and Enterprise plans on November 12, in Claude on the web and Claude Desktop.

It does more than write notes. During the call, anyone can ask Claude something out loud or in the Meet chat, and by default it answers in writing in that chat, visible to everyone on the call. It can also build docs, whiteboards and slides while the meeting runs, and it can act through each attendee's connected tools, such as sending a [Slack](/en/tools/slack/) message, after that person approves it the first time. It keeps the transcript, summary and descriptions of shared screens. It does not keep audio.

## Why it shows up in ops stacks

- **The notetaker is already paid for.** A team on Claude Team or Enterprise gets notes, transcripts and in-call answers at no extra charge during the beta. For an org already paying for [Claude](/en/tools/claude/) seats, that makes a separate notetaker contract hard to justify on Meet calls.
- **Notes land where the team already works.** Meeting pages sit in claude.ai, org members on the invite can edit and share them, and past meetings can be queried from an ordinary Claude chat. No export step or MCP bridge is needed to get call history into the assistant, which is the integration work [Granola](/en/tools/granola/) and [Fireflies](/en/tools/fireflies/) customers build by hand.
- **It produces work, not only a record.** An account review can end with a draft plan on the meeting page instead of a summary that someone has to turn into one. That is closer to [Claude Cowork](/en/tools/claude-cowork/) running inside a call than to a transcription tool.
- **Admin controls exist from day one.** Owners turn it on for everyone or for specific roles, decide whether Claude joins calls with external guests, and decide whether members' connectors work in meetings.

## Pricing

Claude Meetings has no price of its own. It comes with the seat, from claude.com/pricing, checked 2026-11-27:

- **Team — $20 per seat per month billed annually, $25 monthly** (Standard seat), 2 to 150 seats. Premium seats are $100 annually or $125 monthly.
- **Enterprise — $20 per seat per month billed annually plus usage at API rates**, 20-seat minimum.

What the beta includes, per Anthropic's announcement and help center: notes, transcripts and in-call answers are free on Team and Enterprise. Any answer that uses a connected tool counts toward the plan's usage and can lead to charges. Docs, whiteboards and other artifacts made in a call are free through February 10, 2027. Anthropic has not published pricing for after the beta.

For comparison, a dedicated notetaker on annual billing costs $14 on Granola Business, $15 on [Fathom](/en/tools/fathom/) Team, $19 on Fireflies Business and $19.99 on [Otter](/en/tools/otter-ai/) Business. A 20-person team dropping Fireflies Business saves about $4,560 a year, but only if all of its recorded meetings run on Google Meet.

## Best for

- RevOps, CS and recruiting teams on Claude Team or Enterprise whose internal and customer calls run on Google Meet
- Ops leaders who want decisions from a call turned into a doc, plan or Slack message before the call ends, not a summary to rework afterward
- Teams that already ask Claude about their accounts and want call history in the same place, without an export pipeline

## Alternatives, and when to pick them instead

- **[Otter](/en/tools/otter-ai/)**: one of the two long-standing category leaders. Pick it when calls run on Zoom or Microsoft Teams too, or when you need a searchable transcript archive with no dependence on a Claude seat. Business costs $19.99 annually.
- **[Fireflies](/en/tools/fireflies/)**: the other incumbent. Pick it for the widest set of CRM and workflow integrations and for multi-platform coverage. Business costs $19 annually.
- **[Fathom](/en/tools/fathom/)**: the fastest-growing entrant. Pick it when you need recordings and clips, because Claude Meetings keeps no audio, or a free tier with unlimited recording for people without Claude seats.
- **[Granola](/en/tools/granola/)**: pick it when the people on your calls will not accept a visible bot, or for in-person conversations. Claude Meetings always joins as a participant.
- **[Gong](/en/tools/gong/)**: a different product. Pick it for deal scoring, rep coaching and forecasting from recorded calls, none of which Claude Meetings offers.

## Watch-outs

- **Google Meet only.** Anthropic says other platforms are "coming soon" and has given no dates. A Computerworld report quoted a spokesperson naming Microsoft Teams, but no Anthropic page does. Guard: count your recorded meetings by platform before you consolidate. If Zoom or Teams calls are a meaningful share, keep the existing notetaker contract and use Claude Meetings for Meet calls alongside it.
- **Team plans let Claude into client calls by default.** On Team, Claude Meetings is on for everyone and calls with external guests are allowed. Guests see a chat notice, and what they say goes into the notes, but they get no meeting page or recap email. On Enterprise, Claude stays out of calls with outsiders and leaves if one joins, until an admin allows it. Anthropic tells users to confirm everyone is OK with being transcribed. Guard: on Team, decide the external-guest setting before rollout, and where guests are allowed, add a transcription notice to the invite templates, and do not rely on the chat message alone where two-party consent laws apply.
- **The free beta has an end date and a meter.** Artifacts are free only through February 10, 2027, connector actions already count toward usage, and pricing after the beta is unpublished. Guard: do not cancel an annual Fireflies or Otter renewal on the strength of the beta. Run both through one renewal cycle and decide once Anthropic publishes the price.
- **Some orgs are excluded.** During the beta it is unavailable to organizations with zero data retention, a HIPAA-ready configuration or customer-managed encryption keys, among others. Many legal and healthcare-adjacent teams fall into that group. Guard: check your org's configuration with your Claude admin before planning a rollout, and keep the current tool where the exclusion applies.
- **No audio, and an eight-hour limit.** Claude saves text and screen descriptions, not recordings, and stays for up to eight hours. Guard: keep a recording tool such as Fathom or Gong wherever call playback is required for coaching, QA or dispute evidence.
