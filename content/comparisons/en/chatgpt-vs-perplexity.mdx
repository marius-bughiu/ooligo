[ChatGPT](/en/tools/chatgpt/) vs [Perplexity](/en/tools/perplexity/) is the general-purpose-LLM-versus-search-engine comparison, and the answer depends on what you actually do. ChatGPT is a general assistant: writing, code, image generation, voice, plugins. Perplexity is a search-first tool with an LLM front-end and inline citations. They overlap on Q&A, and that's about it. The 2026 reality: most knowledge workers should pay for one or the other plus Claude.

## Where ChatGPT wins

- **General-purpose breadth.** Writing, code, image generation, voice mode, custom GPTs, and the broadest plugin ecosystem. ChatGPT is a Swiss Army knife. Perplexity is one specialized blade.
- **Multimodal capabilities.** Image generation, voice mode, and video features are well ahead of Perplexity. Perplexity does answer with images now but does not generate them at parity.
- **Custom GPTs and ecosystem.** The GPT Store and team workflows around custom assistants are deeper. Perplexity has Spaces but the ecosystem is younger.

## Where Perplexity wins

- **Live web search with cited sources.** Perplexity's core job, and it does it better than ChatGPT's web browsing in most workflows. Citations are inline, sources are clickable, and the synthesized answer is search-tuned.
- **Speed for fact-finding.** "What is the current price of X" or "what did Y company announce yesterday" is faster in Perplexity than the equivalent ChatGPT browsing flow.
- **Trust through transparency.** When the source matters (compliance research, legal context, due diligence), Perplexity's citation-first UI is more defensible than a ChatGPT answer.

## When to use both / Pricing reality

Plenty of knowledge workers run both. ChatGPT for general work and creative tasks. Perplexity for research and lookups. Pricing: ChatGPT Plus and Perplexity Pro are both 20 USD per month. ChatGPT Pro and Perplexity Enterprise are 200 USD per month and 40 USD per seat respectively. The marginal cost of running both is real but small.

## Verdict

- **Pick ChatGPT** if you want one general assistant for writing, code, images, and voice with the deepest plugin ecosystem.
- **Pick Perplexity** if you spend more than an hour a day searching and synthesizing live information — analysts, sales reps doing account research, journalists, researchers.
- **Use both** if your work mixes creation and research, which describes most modern roles.

The single mistake to avoid: relying on ChatGPT's web browsing for citation-critical work. Perplexity is purpose-built for that and worth the second subscription.
