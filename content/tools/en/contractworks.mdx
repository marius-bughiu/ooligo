## What it is

ContractWorks is the budget-tier CLM optimized for the smallest end of the in-house market — companies that need a real contract repository, basic workflow, and e-signature but can't justify mid-market CLM pricing. Owned by Onit since 2020, it's positioned as a starter CLM for legal teams of one to five people.

## Why it shows up in Legal Ops stacks

- **Flat-rate pricing.** ContractWorks bills a flat monthly rate for unlimited users, not per seat. The economics flip the typical CLM math for organizations where the user count is broad (procurement, sales ops, HR all touching contracts) but the legal team is tiny.
- **Strong contract repository.** Tagging, OCR'd full-text search, automated reminders for renewal and expiration dates. Covers the 80% of contract management that small teams actually need.
- **Bundled e-signature with unlimited usage.** Removes the marginal cost of signing more contracts as the business grows.

## Pricing

- **Standard** — $700/month, unlimited users, up to 2,500 documents
- **Professional** — $850/month, unlimited users, advanced reporting and signature workflows
- **Enterprise** — $1,200/month, advanced security, SSO, custom integrations
- Flat-rate pricing makes ContractWorks dramatically cheaper than per-seat CLMs once you have 8+ users touching contracts

## Best for

- SMBs and seed/Series A companies needing first-time contract management
- Real-estate, professional services, and consulting firms with high contract volume but small legal teams
- Procurement-led organizations whose legal team is one person plus outside counsel

## Watch-outs

- Workflow engine is basic — approval routing exists but lacks the depth of Ironclad or Agiloft
- AI features are minimal; teams that want generative-AI drafting and review pair ContractWorks with [Spellbook](/en/tools/spellbook/) or [Claude](/en/tools/claude/)
- Outgrown quickly by companies that scale past 50 contracts/month or develop multi-stakeholder approval requirements; plan for migration to a mid-market CLM ([Concord](/en/tools/concord/), [Juro](/en/tools/juro/), [Ironclad](/en/tools/ironclad/)) within 18-24 months
