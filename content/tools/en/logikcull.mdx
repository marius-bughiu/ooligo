## What it is

Logikcull is cloud-native eDiscovery built for self-serve, mid-market work — drag in a PST, EML batch, or hard drive, and you're processing, searching, and producing within an hour. They sit deliberately downmarket from Relativity, optimizing for in-house counsel and small-to-mid law firms who need a usable product without an LSP partnership.

## Why it shows up in legal-ops stacks

- **Self-serve onboarding.** No professional services SOW just to run a small matter — important for in-house teams.
- **Per-matter pricing.** Spin up a project, pay for what you ingest, archive when done. Predictable for legal ops budgets.
- **Strong UX.** Search, redaction, production, and timelines that don't require a Relativity-certified specialist.

## Pricing

- **Per-matter** — ingest pricing, with subscription tiers for active matters
- Discount Discovery flat-fee option for predictable spend
- No public starting price; quote-based after trial

## Best for

- In-house legal ops handling investigations and small litigation
- Small-to-mid law firms without a dedicated eDiscovery team
- Employment, contracts, and IP disputes at modest data volumes

## Watch-outs

- Not the right fit for multi-TB cross-border matters — Relativity wins there
- AI/analytics layer is thinner than enterprise competitors
- Reveal acquired Logikcull in 2023; product roadmap is still settling post-merger
