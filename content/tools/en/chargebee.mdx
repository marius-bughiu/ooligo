## What it is

Chargebee is a subscription billing and monetization platform for SaaS and AI companies. The pricing page sells four products: **Billing** (catalog, invoicing, usage metering, dunning, 40+ payment gateways), **CPQ** (quoting on top of Billing), **RevRec** (ASC 606 and IFRS 15 revenue recognition, built from the RevLock acquisition) and **Growth** (the former retention product: pricing tables, checkout, cancel flows and churn experiments). **Receivables**, the AI collections product that came from the numberz acquisition, is sold separately.

The company is independent and VC-backed, and still buying: it acquired the payments-intelligence startup Inai in September 2025. On 17 August 2026 it announced a Leader placement in Gartner's 2026 Magic Quadrant for Recurring Billing Applications, its third year as a Leader. Zuora is a Leader in the same report.

## Why it shows up in RevOps stacks

Billing is where commission and partner-payout tools read "invoice paid". [QuotaPath](/en/tools/quotapath/) lists Chargebee among its Premium accounting connectors, [Everstage](/en/tools/everstage/) among its finance connectors, and [PartnerStack](/en/tools/partnerstack/) uses it as a billing-event source for partner commissions. If comp pays on cash collected, the billing system is the source of truth for comp, and RevOps inherits its data model.

The second reason is AI pricing. Chargebee runs **credit, action and outcome pricing on one catalog**. Customers buy a credit pool upfront and draw it down across features and agent actions, with rollover rules, overage rates and overdraft caps on the ledger. Chargebee's pitch is that most AI products launch on credits and move to per-outcome pricing later without a billing migration. Named users include CodeRabbit and Gorgias.

**Chargebee MCP** is hosted by Chargebee per site and region (US, EU, AU) and switched on by an admin under Agentic AI settings. There are three predefined servers. The **Knowledge Base** server answers docs questions and needs no auth. The **Data Lookup** server exposes read tools over subscriptions, invoices, quotes, credit notes, transactions and MRR history, plus an export-job tool. The **Onboarding** server writes catalogs and demo data, but only on test and sandbox sites. A **Custom** server lets you pick tools from the full catalog, including write tools. The docs name Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and Codex as clients, with a Claude connector and region-specific ChatGPT connectors.

## Pricing reality

Billing has two plans:

- **Flow**: either $0 plus 0.80% of invoicing, or $99/month plus 0.65%. The two cross at about $66,000 of monthly invoicing. Flow includes usage-based billing and 100M usage events a month.
- **Enterprise**: quote only. Adds multi-entity, account hierarchies and contract terms.

On the commit option, $100,000 of monthly invoicing costs about $749 a month ($8,988 a year). $500,000 a month costs about $3,349 a month (roughly $40,000 a year). The fee tracks revenue, not seats.

The real bill is Flow plus add-ons. RevRec, full CPQ, HubSpot CRM sync and eInvoicing are paid add-ons on both plans. Multi-entity, the NetSuite, Sage Intacct and Salesforce connectors, Avalara and SAML/SCIM are Enterprise-only and also extra. QuickBooks, Xero, Anrok and smart dunning are included.

Buyer data: Vendr reports a **median of $57,600 a year** across 65 purchases, with a range of $8,148 to $178,709 and average negotiated savings of 16% (page updated February 2026). Against peers:

- **[Zuora](/en/tools/zuora/)**: Vendr median $170,000, about 3x Chargebee.
- **[Nue](/en/tools/nue/)**: Vendr median $67,167, about 1.2x.
- **[DealHub](/en/tools/dealhub/)**: Vendr median $44,297, about 0.8x, though DealHub is quoting-first.

## Best for

A RevOps or finance-systems owner at a **SaaS or AI company between about $1M and $50M ARR, with one or two billing entities**, who needs usage or credit pricing live this quarter and wants to start self-serve. The scoped use case where Chargebee wins: launching prepaid credits for an AI product, with drawdown, rollover and overage caps, then adding per-outcome charges later on the same catalog.

## Not for

Groups with many entities and heavy mid-term amendments that need ASC 606 schedules updated in the same close. Zuora is built for that. Also not for Salesforce-native teams that want quoting and billing on Salesforce objects: the Salesforce CPQ and subscription connectors are Enterprise-only add-ons here, so [Nue](/en/tools/nue/) is the cleaner fit.

## Versus the alternatives

- **[Zuora](/en/tools/zuora/)**: the other Leader and the enterprise default. Pick Zuora when revenue recognition must live in the billing suite across many entities and currencies, and the budget is in six figures.
- **Stripe Billing**: the default when Stripe already processes your payments and contracts are simple. Stripe closed its Metronome acquisition in January 2026, which makes it the fastest-moving option for raw usage metering. Pick Stripe when you want one vendor for payments and billing. Pick Chargebee when you want gateway choice, since it sits on top of Stripe and 40+ other gateways, and you want retention flows and RevRec in the same tool.
- **[Nue](/en/tools/nue/)**: pick it for Salesforce-native quote-to-cash with credits on standard objects.
- **[Salesforce Revenue Cloud](/en/tools/salesforce-revenue-cloud/)**: pick it when procurement wants one vendor and billing is simple enough for native.

If none fit because you invoice a few dozen annual contracts, invoice from QuickBooks or your ERP and revisit when you add usage pricing. ## Watch-outs

- **The fee grows with your revenue.** At $1M of monthly invoicing, the Flow commit option is about $6,599 a month, roughly $79,000 a year, before add-ons. **Guard:** model the fee at 2x and 3x current invoicing before signing, and once you cross about $300,000 a month, ask for an Enterprise quote with a capped percentage or a flat platform fee.
- **MCP write actions are on you.** Chargebee's docs say the customer is responsible for anything an AI client does through its MCP servers, write actions included, and that approval depends on the client. An API key grants every enabled tool, and OAuth (which scopes access to the signed-in user's role) does not work on Multi-Business Entity sites. **Guard:** give agents the read-only Data Lookup server, use OAuth with a role scoped below the person's in-app role, and keep write tools out of any Custom server until your client enforces per-call approval.
- **Custom MCP servers need the latest product catalog.** Sites still on the Legacy Product Catalog cannot build them. **Guard:** check your catalog version before scoping agent work, and put the catalog migration in the renewal statement of work.
- **The headline price omits the ERP connectors.** NetSuite, Sage Intacct and Salesforce connectors are Enterprise-only paid add-ons. **Guard:** list every system Chargebee must sync with, get each line priced in the quote, and treat "we need NetSuite" as "we are buying Enterprise".
