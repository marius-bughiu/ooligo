## What it is

Coresignal is a public-web data provider that sells company, employee, job-posting and social-post records to people who write code. Its own figures on the pricing page: 70M+ company records, 907M+ employee profiles and 482M+ job postings, assembled from multiple sources (a professional network, Glassdoor, Indeed, Owler, Craft and others) into "multi-source" records of 600+ company fields and 300+ employee fields. It started in 2016 shipping flat-file dumps, launched self-serve APIs in July 2023, and now says it has 80+ staff and 1,000+ customers in AI search, HR tech, sales tech and market research. The contracting entity on its compliance page is Deeptrace Inc., a Delaware company.

What it is not: a prospecting app. There is no list builder for reps, no sequencer, no Chrome extension and no CRM sync. The dashboard's AI Data Search lets a non-engineer build and export a list, but the product is priced and documented for pipelines, warehouses and agents.

## Why it shows up in RevOps stacks now

- **Agents can call it directly.** Coresignal MCP v2 is hosted at `https://mcp.coresignal.com/mcp/v2` over streamable HTTP with OAuth sign-in, so no API key sits in a config file. Setup pages cover [Claude Code](/en/tools/claude-code/), Codex, [Cursor](/en/tools/cursor/), OpenCode and Antigravity CLI. Tools are priced in the docs: `entity_search` 20 credits per search, `entity_fetch` 1-20 credits per record, `email_enrich` 10 credits per email found, and the server asks for confirmation before expensive pulls.
- **Agentic Search API (spring 2026).** Natural-language search over employees, companies or jobs: `/fast` at 20 credits per call, `/reasoning` at 100 credits and capped at 10 requests an hour. In October 2026 it gained automatic entity detection.
- **A native [n8n](/en/tools/n8n/) node.** Company search, enrich-by-website and collect operations, plus employee, jobs and employee-posts collect and search, with a preview mode that returns up to 20 records. Company Posts is not in the node yet.
- **Posts and signals.** An Employee Posts dataset arrived in 2025, Company Posts in June 2026 (API in July), and company webhooks plus a Contact Enrichment API in October 2026. That turns a static record store into a feed you can trigger on.

## Pricing

Checked today on coresignal.com/pricing and the pricing docs. One universal credit covers every API; searches through the filter and Elasticsearch DSL endpoints are free, and you pay on collect or enrich.

- **Free trial:** 2,000 credits for 7 days, one per company email domain. Credits do not carry into a paid plan.
- **Monthly self-serve:** Mini $49 (2,500 credits), Starter $199 (12,000), Pro $499 (35,000), Growth $1,000 (150,000), Premium $1,500 (1,000,000), Scale $3,000 (4,000,000), Elite $5,000 (10,000,000). Credits reset monthly; Premium and up roll unused credits over for 3 months. Annual billing from Starter up is 10% off, paid upfront.
- **What a record costs in credits:** a job posting or a post is 1; a multi-source company or employee record is 10 for the core fields and 20 with every bundle; historical headcount is 10; a real-time employee fetch is 12 (Growth and up only); an email is 10.
- **Datasets:** bulk delivery in JSON, JSONL or Parquet on a yearly contract, from $1,000 a month.

What it costs in practice: enriching 10,000 CRM accounts with full company records is 200,000 credits, which overflows Growth and lands on Premium at $1,500 a month. Trim the key to core fields and the same job is 100,000 credits, inside Growth at $1,000. At list price a full record works out to about $0.39 on Mini, $0.13 on Growth and $0.03 on Premium. For scale, [People Data Labs](/en/tools/people-data-labs/) starts at $98 for 350 person credits, about $0.28 a match, and [Crustdata](/en/tools/crustdata/) does not publish what its credits cost.

## Best for

RevOps engineers, data teams and GTM engineers who need large volumes of firmographic, employee or job-posting data in a warehouse, an enrichment pipeline or an agent, and who want a published per-record price. The sharpest fit is hiring-signal and headcount work: job postings at 1 credit each and historical headcount by API make "which accounts are hiring for this role, and how fast are they growing" a cheap query.

## Not for

Reps who want to click through a database and send sequences: use [Apollo](/en/tools/apollo/). Teams that need mobile numbers: Coresignal's docs list email enrichment but no phone product. Buyers whose security review requires a SOC 2 report.

## Versus the alternatives

- **[ZoomInfo](/en/tools/zoominfo/)** — the category's share leader, with a rep-facing UI, intent data and phones on custom annual contracts. Pick ZoomInfo when salespeople are the users. Pick Coresignal when code is the consumer and you want to pay per record, not per seat.
- **[Apollo](/en/tools/apollo/)** — the other share leader: database, sequencer and dialer in one per-seat app. Pick Apollo when people prospect by hand. Pick Coresignal for bulk, programmatic company and jobs data, where per-seat pricing makes no sense.
- **[Clay](/en/tools/clay/)** — the fastest-growing platform in the segment, and a layer more than a rival: it runs many providers as a [waterfall](/en/learn/waterfall-enrichment/). Pick Clay when an operator designs enrichment in a table and wants CRM sync. Buy Coresignal directly when one provider covers most rows and you can write the code.
- **[People Data Labs](/en/tools/people-data-labs/)** — pick PDL for identity resolution, broad person enrichment and a SOC 2 report. Pick Coresignal for jobs, posts and historical headcount depth at a lower per-record price on the mid tiers.
- **[Crustdata](/en/tools/crustdata/)** — pick Crustdata when an agent needs live re-fetch and job-change webhooks at the moment of outreach. Pick Coresignal when you want published plan prices and bulk datasets.
- **[Sumble](/en/tools/sumble/)** — pick Sumble when the question is which accounts use a given technology, answered from job-post text, and you want a UI over it rather than raw records.

## Watch-outs

- **Bundles, not record counts, set your tier.** A full record costs twice the core record, and the jump from Growth (150,000 credits) to Premium (1,000,000) is 6.7 times the credits for 1.5 times the price. Guard: before you buy, set the API key's bundles to the fields your pipeline actually reads, multiply records by that credit cost, and pick the tier from the total; if you land just over Growth, Premium costs less per credit and per record.
- **Search is free in the API but not in the agent tools.** The filter and DSL searches cost nothing, while MCP `entity_search` and Search Preview each cost 20 credits and `/reasoning` costs 100. An agent left to explore can spend a Mini plan in an afternoon. Guard: keep exploratory agent sessions on a separate low-tier key, and move repeated queries into the free DSL endpoints once you know the filters.
- **Freshness depends on the endpoint.** Competitors' reviews report cached records months behind job changes, while Coresignal says API records update in real time and carry timestamps. Guard: pull 200 records of contacts you know changed jobs this quarter, check the timestamps and current employer, and route time-sensitive triggers through the Real-time Employee API, which needs Growth or higher.
- **The compliance story is self-attested.** Coresignal is a certified member of the Ethical Web Data Collection Initiative and says it follows ISO 27001 "without formal certification"; its site names no SOC 2 report, and `email_enrich` refuses EEA and UK people. Guard: send the security questionnaire and the DPA request before signing, and if you sell into Europe, plan a separate GDPR-compliant contact source rather than expecting emails from Coresignal. See [firmographic data](/en/learn/firmographic-data/) for what the company fields can and cannot stand in for.
