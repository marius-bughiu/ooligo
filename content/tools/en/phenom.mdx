## What it is

Phenom is an enterprise talent platform that sits above the [ATS](/en/learn/ats-vs-recruiting-crm/) and owns the surfaces candidates and employees touch: the career site, the talent CRM, onboarding, the internal talent marketplace and succession planning. The Philadelphia company no longer describes itself as a talent experience vendor. Its homepage now leads with "Phenom Applied AI", and its press releases call it "an applied AI company with the only AI infrastructure built specifically for HR". The product line is organized as agents running on two platform layers, Hypercell and WorkOps: an AI Interviewer, a Voice Screening Agent, a Fraud Detection Agent, a Career Coach Agent and an X+ Talent Advisor sit next to the older career site and CRM modules.

2026 changed the product. Phenom closed three acquisitions in fifteen weeks:

- **Included** (January 14, 2026), a Seattle people-analytics company, now the conversational layer of Phenom Workforce Intelligence. Included does not continue as a standalone brand.
- **Be Applied** (February 10, 2026), a UK cognitive and situational-judgement assessment vendor.
- **[Plum](/en/tools/plum/)** (April 28, 2026), a Kitchener, Ontario psychometric assessment vendor whose Role Model technology maps behavioral profiles against more than 40,000 jobs. Plum assessments are independently audited for adverse impact across demographic groups.

Be Applied and Plum together give Phenom its own assessment layer, so a buyer that used to pair Phenom with [SHL](/en/tools/shl/), [HireVue](/en/tools/hirevue/) or [TestGorilla](/en/tools/testgorilla/) now has an in-platform option to price against. The pitch for the bundle is candidate fraud: assessments measuring behavior that an AI-written résumé cannot fake. Gartner placed Phenom as a **Visionary**, not a Leader, in the 2026 Magic Quadrant for Talent Acquisition (Recruiting) Suites published May 8, 2026, with Workday and Oracle among the Leaders. Phenom says it serves "500+ leading global brands".

## Why it shows up in Recruiting stacks

- **It owns the candidate-facing surface the ATS does not.** The Intelligent Career Site personalizes job recommendations from profile and browsing behavior, and the same matching engine runs the internal Talent Marketplace for employees. Southwest Airlines reports $7M in annual savings and Varsity Brands 17,000 recruiter hours saved; both are vendor-published figures.
- **Screening, scheduling and assessment are now in one contract.** The AI Interviewer, voice screening, automated interview scheduling and the Be Applied and Plum assessments all run on the same candidate record.
- **It plugs into ServiceNow as an agent.** On June 10, 2026 Phenom announced hiring agents inside the ServiceNow AI Platform and ServiceNow Otto, built on A2A and MCP and sold through the ServiceNow Store. Hiring managers run intake, draft the job description, source, screen and move candidates to interview without leaving [ServiceNow](/en/tools/servicenow/).
- **There is a documented public API.** developer.phenom.com covers Jobs, Candidates, Applications, Applicants, Onboarding, Parsers, Tags, Prediction and SCIM-based user management.

## Pricing

- **Quote only.** phenom.com/pricing is a "Pricing & RFP Requests" demo form with no plans or figures.
- **Real-world band.** [Vendr](https://www.vendr.com/marketplace/phenom) puts the median buyer at **$98,313 a year**, across a range of $37,500 to $112,278. Vendr does not publish the deal count.
- **Per-employee estimates.** Broker estimates cited in third-party 2026 pricing guides run $7 to $13 per employee per month. At 5,000 employees that is $420,000 to $780,000 a year, well above the Vendr high. The two figures describe different purchases: the Vendr range is consistent with single-module deals, and the per-employee figure with a full candidate-plus-employee platform.
- **Modules are licensed separately.** Career site and CRM, onboarding, talent marketplace, workforce intelligence and the agent and assessment products are quoted line by line, and the assessments from the two 2026 acquisitions do not come free with the career site.

The old ooligo figure, an entry point "in the mid-six figures", was wrong for a single-module buyer: the Vendr median is under $100,000.

## Best for

A TA or talent-management leader at a 5,000-plus-employee employer in retail, healthcare, hospitality, manufacturing or airlines, who needs to raise career-site application conversion and fill roles internally before posting them, on top of Workday, SuccessFactors or Oracle. Second fit: a ServiceNow-standardized enterprise that wants hiring-manager intake and screening to run as agents inside ServiceNow.

Not for you below roughly 2,000 employees, where [SmartRecruiters](/en/tools/smartrecruiters/) plus [Gem](/en/tools/gem/) covers career site and CRM for a fraction of the contract.

## Versus the alternatives

- **[Workday](/en/tools/workday/) Recruiting** — the Gartner Leader and the default for Workday HCM shops, now with Paradox's conversational screening after the October 2025 acquisition. Pick Workday when the ATS-native experience is good enough and one HCM vendor matters more than career-site conversion.
- **[Beamery](/en/tools/beamery/)** — pick Beamery when the failing metric is pipeline and skills intelligence for sourcers rather than candidate conversion. Phenom's Included purchase narrows that gap on workforce analytics; ask both vendors to demo the same skills-gap question.
- **[Eightfold](/en/tools/eightfold/)** — the AI-native talent-intelligence rival. Pick Eightfold when matching and skills inference across the employee base is the core purchase and the career site is secondary.
- **[Avature](/en/tools/avature/)** — pick Avature when you need a configurable CRM and ATS for unusual processes such as campus, executive or agency hiring, and will trade polish for configurability.
- **[Radancy](/en/tools/radancy/)** — pick Radancy when employer-brand content and programmatic job advertising drive the purchase rather than AI screening.

## Watch-outs

- **Acquired assessment science is still being integrated.** Be Applied and Plum joined in February and April 2026, and Phenom says it is still folding both into a single agentic platform. Guard: ask for the validation studies and adverse-impact audit for each assessment as it runs inside Phenom, not as it ran on the standalone product, and pilot on one job family first.
- **AI screening carries hiring-law obligations.** NYC LL 144 requires an independent bias audit within the year before an automated employment decision tool is used on New York City candidates, plus advance notice; the EU AI Act classes recruitment AI as high-risk. Guard: get the current audit summary for the AI Interviewer and each assessment before switching it on, see [NYC Local Law 144](/en/learn/nyc-local-law-144/), and consult counsel for other jurisdictions.
- **Personalization depends on record quality.** Thin or stale job and candidate data produces poor recommendations on both the career site and the Talent Marketplace. Guard: audit job taxonomy and skills tags before go-live and make one TA-ops owner accountable for them.
- **No official MCP server.** MCP access exists only through the ServiceNow integration or third-party connectors such as StackOne's. Guard: if your own agents need Phenom data, build on the public API and scope it in the contract.
- **Module-by-module quoting hides the full cost.** Guard: get every module you expect to use in year two on the first order form, with renewal caps, before signing a multi-year term.
