## What it is

Google Antigravity is Google's agent-first development platform. You hand it a task, and agents plan the work, edit files, run commands, drive a browser to check the result and report back. It ships as a desktop app for running agents in parallel, an IDE, the Antigravity CLI and a Python SDK that exposes the same agent harness to your own code. Version 2.0, launched at Google I/O on 19 May 2026, added the standalone app, the CLI, scheduled background tasks and sandboxing with credential masking. It runs on macOS, Windows and Linux.

It is also now Google's only consumer coding agent. Gemini CLI and the Gemini Code Assist IDE extensions stopped serving free and AI Pro/Ultra users on 18 June 2026, and Google told them to move to the Antigravity CLI. Jules, the asynchronous coding agent, went read-only on 11 November and shuts down on 31 December 2026. If someone on your team built scripts on either, Antigravity is where Google wants them to land. It is closest in shape to [Claude Code](/en/tools/claude-code/) and [OpenAI Codex](/en/tools/openai-codex/): one agent engine behind an app, a terminal tool and an SDK.

## Why it shows up in ops stacks

- **The free tier is a real tool.** The $0 individual plan includes the app, CLI, IDE extensions, SDK, parallel background agents, browser use and scheduled tasks, on a weekly agent quota. A RevOps analyst can prototype a CRM cleanup script or an enrichment job without a purchase order.
- **Model choice inside one seat.** Paid plans run Google's Gemini 4 models and Anthropic's Claude Sonnet and Opus. On Gemini Enterprise, an admin must opt in to Claude models, which are billed by tokens.
- **`/teamwork` for long jobs.** On paid plans, `/teamwork` starts with a scoping interview, then an orchestrator splits the project into milestones for explorer and worker agents. Critic and auditor agents check each milestone before work continues. It suits a multi-day migration better than a one-off script.
- **MCP and agent access to SaaS.** The SDK connects MCP servers, and enterprise admins set MCP, sandbox and browser policies. [Greenhouse](/en/tools/greenhouse/) already lists Antigravity among the clients for its MCP server. Background on the pattern is in [what an MCP server is](/en/learn/mcp-server-explained/).

## Pricing reality

Antigravity has no price of its own. Access comes from the plan behind the Google account:

- **Individuals, $0:** baseline agent quota, refreshed weekly.
- **Google AI Pro, $19.99/mo:** quota refreshed every five hours with higher weekly limits, plus `/teamwork`.
- **Google AI Ultra, $100 or $200/mo:** 5× or 20× Pro's limits since I/O 2026, when the top tier dropped from $250.
- **AI credits:** sold since March 2026 at $25 per 2,500 for use past the quota.
- **Business via Google Cloud:** Gemini Enterprise Standard or Plus from $30 per seat per month, with $10 or $15 of monthly seat credits, or a pay-as-you-go plan with no seat fee and consumption billing. Enterprise access arrived on 21 August 2026 with pooled quotas, spend caps and audit logging.

Google's pricing page publishes no numeric quotas, and its plan docs say limits "are subject to modification." In March 2026 users reported that Pro quota ran out within days. Google tripled Gemini limits twice in May, and DeepMind's Varun Mohan still said heavy users could hit the weekly cap "after a couple work sessions." Budget AI Pro for an occasional scripter and Ultra at $100 for anyone using it daily. For a team, a Gemini Enterprise seat at $30–45 a month plus overage is the planning number.

## Best for

A RevOps or GTM engineer in a company already buying Google Cloud who maintains a handful of repositories: CRM-to-BigQuery syncs, enrichment jobs, internal MCP servers. They get a governed Gemini Enterprise seat with audit logs, and the CLI runs the same agent from cron or CI. It also fits a former Gemini CLI or Jules user who needs a supported replacement this quarter.

## Not for

Teams on Google Workspace alone who expect it to come with their licence. The Workspace AI Ultra Access add-on that bundled Antigravity was retired from 7 July 2026, and Workspace plans do not include Antigravity, so access means a separate Gemini Enterprise purchase or a personal Google account. It is also the wrong pick for anyone who wants to drive the agent from a third-party harness on a subscription login, as covered under watch-outs.

## Versus the alternatives

- **[GitHub Copilot](/en/tools/github-copilot/):** the largest installed base by seats, at $19 Business and $39 Enterprise per user per month with pooled credits. Pick it when the company buys GitHub Enterprise and most developers want inline help in the editor.
- **[Cursor](/en/tools/cursor/):** the leading coding-specific rival by revenue, reported above $2 billion annualized in early 2026. Pick it when the person lives in the IDE and wants to steer every edit visually.
- **[OpenAI Codex](/en/tools/openai-codex/):** the fastest-growing entrant, past 5 million weekly users by June 2026, included from ChatGPT Plus at $20. Pick it when the company standardized on ChatGPT Business.
- **[Claude Code](/en/tools/claude-code/):** Pro from $20/mo. Pick it when the work is multi-file changes to an existing codebase and you want published usage tiers rather than a quota Google can move.

Pick Antigravity over all four when the free tier must carry real work, or when Google Cloud is the procurement path.

## Watch-outs

- **Third-party access gets accounts suspended.** Antigravity's terms call it a breach to use "third party software, tools, or services to access the Service," naming OpenClaw with Antigravity OAuth as the example. In February 2026 Google cut off users who did so, including paying Ultra subscribers. Guard: run third-party agents on a Gemini API key or Gemini Enterprise, never a personal OAuth login. Keep the account behind your work seat separate from experiments.
- **Individual-plan data is used for training.** The individual terms let Google use interactions to improve its products and machine learning, with human reviewers, unless the user opts out. Gemini Enterprise says it does not train on prompts or outputs. Guard: do not let staff point a personal account at repositories holding customer or candidate data. Put those users on Gemini Enterprise, or confirm the opt-out is set.
- **Quotas move without notice.** Limits changed at least three times in 2026, and models rotate too: Google removes older Claude and open models from Free and Pro on 30 November 2026. Guard: give scheduled jobs a spend cap through Gemini Enterprise overage or credits. Pin jobs to a model you have tested, and re-test when the docs change.
