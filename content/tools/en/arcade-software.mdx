## What it is

Arcade, at arcade.software, is a different company from the MCP gateway covered at [Arcade.dev](/en/tools/arcade-dev/). It began as an interactive demo builder. You record a flow in your product with the Chrome extension, and Arcade turns it into a clickable screenshot or video tour that you can embed on a website, in a help center or in a launch email. In 2026 it rebuilt itself around a second product, AI video. The homepage now pitches "the AI video playground for marketing teams" and claims 40,000+ teams, a self-reported figure. The two products are priced separately. The interactive demo plans do not include AI video credits, and the AI video plans do not include paid demo features. Arcade is private and backed by Kleiner Perkins, which led a $14M Series A in November 2024. Funding to date is about $21.5M.

The changelog has moved fast. Arcade launched an MCP server in April 2026. Per-video model choice (Claude Opus, Sonnet or Haiku) arrived on 20 October. On 4 November it added editing over MCP from [Claude](/en/tools/claude/), [ChatGPT](/en/tools/chatgpt/) and [Gemini](/en/tools/gemini/), plus 60 new voices in 15 more languages. Arcade Apps followed on 23 November. Apps turn [Notion](/en/tools/notion/) docs, [Linear](/en/tools/linear/) tickets, [Google Drive](/en/tools/google-drive/) files and [Granola](/en/tools/granola/) call notes into videos.

## Why it shows up in RevOps stacks

- **One capture, three formats.** A demo recorded once can ship as an embedded tour, an MP4 or a GIF. [Navattic](/en/tools/navattic/) paid plans output HTML clones only. That makes Arcade the tool teams add when the same walkthrough has to run on LinkedIn, in a launch email and on the pricing page.
- **HubSpot on the self-serve plan.** On Growth, a demo form with an email field creates or updates a [HubSpot](/en/tools/hubspot/) contact in real time, and form answers land as timeline events. If Clearbit is enabled, viewer companies sync once a day.
- **Video from the tools reps already use.** The Arcade MCP and extension work on every plan, Free included. A product marketer can ask Claude for a 60-second feature video and see the credit cost in the chat before Arcade renders it.

## Pricing

**Interactive demos** are billed per seat, with 15% off for annual billing.

- **Free — $0.** 1 user, unlimited AI demo drafts, 1 published demo.
- **Growth — $42.50 per seat/month billed annually** ($50 monthly), for up to 10 users. Adds unlimited published demos, MP4/GIF export, watermark removal, AI translations, forms and CTAs, branching, and the HubSpot sync.
- **Enterprise — custom.** HTML capture, the API, custom domains, SSO, version history and the native [Salesforce](/en/tools/salesforce/) integration are only available here.

**AI video** is billed per workspace in credits, with up to 20% off for annual billing. Pro starts at $49/month billed annually ($59 monthly) for 1,000 credits and 10 seats. It scales to $119 for 2,500 credits and $469 for 10,000. On the default High model, a 1-minute video costs about 150 credits. Medium costs about three-quarters of that and Light about a quarter. AI-generated clips are metered on top, at about 1 credit per second for Standard and 3 for Premium. Free gives 250 credits once.

**What teams pay.** We found no independent negotiated-price data for Arcade, so these figures come from list prices. Demo Growth for 3 seats is $1,530 a year. Adding video Pro at 2,500 credits ($1,428 a year) brings both products to about $2,960. That buys roughly 16 one-minute videos a month on High. A 10-seat Growth team pays $5,100, still below Navattic's $8,500 Vendr median. Arcade offers startup discounts at Series A or earlier.

## Best for

- Product marketing teams at seed-to-Series B SaaS companies shipping launch videos, social clips and website tours from one recorder, with HubSpot as the CRM and a budget under $5,000 a year
- Not for: presales teams that need HTML clones with editable data, or Salesforce shops that want demo engagement scored on the lead without an Enterprise contract. Navattic or Storylane fits them better.

## Alternatives — and when to pick them instead

- **[Navattic](/en/tools/navattic/)** — a market-share leader for HTML website demos, with Salesforce on its $500/month Base plan. Pick it when the website demo is a pipeline asset routed into Salesforce.
- **[Storylane](/en/tools/storylane/)** — the other share leader, with the broadest independent feature set. HTML capture comes on Growth at $500/month, and sandbox and presenter demos on Premium. Pick it when SEs present live from demos.
- **[Supademo](/en/tools/supademo/)** — the fastest-growing entrant. HTML starts on Business at $199/month billed annually, and Salesforce is on its $38 Scale plan. Pick it when you need HTML or Salesforce without paying Enterprise prices.
- **[Walnut](/en/tools/walnut/)** or **[Consensus](/en/tools/consensus/)** — pick these when large SE teams personalize demo data, or when you need buying-committee analytics.
- **Synthesia or HeyGen** — avatar-led AI video. Pick one when the video needs a presenter on camera rather than product UI.

## Watch-outs

- **Two products, two bills.** A demo seat buys no video credits. Guard: decide which product carries the use case before trialing. Budget both only if launch videos and embedded tours are both on the plan.
- **HTML capture and native Salesforce are Enterprise-only.** Below Enterprise, Salesforce data has to pass through HubSpot or [Zapier](/en/tools/zapier/). Guard: if demo engagement must score Salesforce leads, get an Enterprise quote at the start and compare it with Supademo Scale.
- **Credits are a hard ceiling.** Arcade sells no one-off credit packs. Monthly plans do not roll credits over, and AI edits after a 5-minute gap cost about 50 credits each. Guard: draft on Light, render finals on High, and buy annual so unused credits carry over. Prices for existing customers change after 16 December 2026, so check the renewal price.
- **The 10-seat cap.** Both Growth and video Pro top out at 10 people. Guard: count every editor across marketing, sales and support before signing. Viewer and billing-only seats no longer count.
- **MCP spends the shared balance.** Anyone connected through Claude or ChatGPT draws on the same credit pool. Guard: limit MCP access to the people who own the video budget.
