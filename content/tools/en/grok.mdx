## What it is

Grok is the AI assistant built by xAI, which SpaceX acquired in February 2026; as of November 2026 the x.ai site carries SpaceX Super Intelligence branding, while the product, the grok.com app and the x.ai API keep the Grok name. Most people know it as the chatbot inside X. The part that matters for an ops team is the organizational offer announced at the end of December 2025: **Grok Business**, a self-serve team plan, and **Grok Enterprise**, a sales-led tier. Since May 2026 both can reach company data through connectors (Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Notion, GitHub, Linear, Salesforce and more) and through custom MCP servers an admin registers.

The question this page answers: is Grok Business a credible fourth option next to [ChatGPT](/en/tools/chatgpt/), [Claude](/en/tools/claude/) and [Gemini](/en/tools/gemini/) for a team's daily assistant, and what do its controls cover?

## Why it shows up in ops stacks

- **Admin-gated connectors.** On Business and Enterprise, a member cannot use a connector until a team admin adds it in the xAI cloud console (Grok Business → Connectors). Built-ins cover Gmail and Google Calendar, Google Drive, OneDrive, Outlook, Microsoft Teams, SharePoint and Salesforce; a catalog adds Box, GitHub, Linear, Notion, Canva, Gamma, Vercel, Meltwater and S&P Global. The launch post says SharePoint document editing runs on Grok 4.3.
- **Bring-your-own MCP.** Admins can register a custom MCP server by URL, so an internal tool exposed over MCP (a [Greenhouse](/en/tools/greenhouse/) MCP scope, a warehouse query server) becomes usable from the same chat window.
- **No-training terms by default.** xAI's Business announcement commits to no training on customer data. Vault, an Enterprise add-on, adds a dedicated data plane, application-level encryption and customer-managed encryption keys.
- **Frontier model at a low API price.** If the team also builds on the API, grok-4.7 lists at $2.00 input / $6.00 output per million tokens under a 200k-token prompt (doubling above it), with a 500k context window.

## Pricing

- **Free / X Premium / SuperGrok** — individual plans. Fine for trying the model; not for company data, since they carry no team admin, no connector governance and no business data terms.
- **Grok Business** — $30 per user per month, self-serve via "Create Team". Each seat gets SuperGrok-level access; upgraded licenses carry SuperGrok Heavy limits. Includes the admin console, team and seat management and consolidated billing. xAI does not publish a seat minimum or a Business usage number; paid plans draw on a shared weekly usage pool, with extra usage credits sold from $5.
- **Grok Enterprise** — custom pricing. Adds custom SSO, SCIM directory sync, advanced audit and security controls, custom retention policies and the option to switch off personal workspaces.
- **Vault** — Enterprise add-on, quote-only.

Real-world band for a 20-seat ops team: $600/month on Business. Treat Enterprise as a procurement conversation, budgeted at a premium to Business, the moment you need SSO.

## Best for

- **RevOps or ops teams already on X or on SuperGrok** that want to move existing personal use onto a governed team plan with no-training terms.
- **Teams that want a second assistant for model diversity** — for example, a RevOps lead cross-checking account research or a forecasting narrative against a different frontier model before it reaches leadership.

It is not the first pick for a legal ops or recruiting team whose security review needs SSO on day one and a documented compliance package; that means Enterprise and a longer procurement cycle.

## Alternatives — and when to pick them instead

- **ChatGPT Business / Enterprise** — the market-share leader. Pick it when you want the widest connector catalog, custom GPTs and the most third-party integrations.
- **Microsoft 365 Copilot** — the other incumbent. Pick it when your documents, mail and identity already live in Microsoft 365 and Entra; the data boundary and permissions come with the tenant. See [Microsoft 365 Copilot](/en/tools/microsoft-365-copilot/).
- **Claude Team / Enterprise** — the fastest-growing entrant in business seats. Pick it for long-document reasoning, contract and policy work, and agentic work through [Claude Code](/en/tools/claude-code/) and Skills.
- **Gemini for Google Workspace** — pick it when you are a Google Workspace shop and want the assistant inside Docs, Gmail and Drive without a separate connector setup.
- **[Perplexity](/en/tools/perplexity/) Enterprise Pro** — pick it when the job is cited web research rather than a general assistant.

The rule: Grok wins only when your team already uses it and wants governance on top, or when you deliberately want a second model family. Otherwise the incumbent tied to your identity provider is the cheaper rollout.

## Watch-outs

- **Brand and reputational review.** Grok Business launched in the middle of a public controversy over Grok's image generation producing sexualized deepfakes, which drew regulatory scrutiny in several countries. Guard: take the vendor to your security and comms review before rollout, and write an acceptable-use policy that limits team use to text and data work.
- **SSO and retention are Enterprise-only.** Business has no custom SSO, no SCIM and no custom retention policy. Guard: if your security policy requires SSO or a retention schedule, budget for Enterprise from the start instead of piloting on Business and migrating later.
- **Compliance evidence is not on the launch page.** The Business announcement makes no SOC 2 or ISO 27001 claim; the site links a DPA and a BAA. Guard: request the current SOC 2 report and signed DPA before any candidate, contract or customer data goes in.
- **Custom MCP must be public.** A custom MCP server has to be reachable over the public internet; local servers need a tunnel. Guard: put internal MCP servers behind OAuth and an allowlist, and scope them read-only until audit logs prove what Grok calls.
- **Usage limits are not published per seat.** The shared weekly pool makes heavy users hard to forecast. Guard: run a two-week pilot with your heaviest users and track credit spend before committing seat counts.
