## What it is

n8n is a workflow automation platform for technical teams. It gives you a visual canvas where each step is a node, JavaScript and Python code steps for when the canvas runs out, and AI agent nodes that connect any LLM to tools and memory. It has more than 1,000 integrations. You can run it on n8n's cloud or on your own infrastructure. Its GitHub repository has passed 210,000 stars, which makes it one of the most-starred automation projects anywhere. It sits between [Zapier](/en/tools/zapier/), which is easier for non-technical users, and enterprise iPaaS like [Workato](/en/tools/workato/), which costs far more.

It is source-available, not open source. The self-hosted Community Edition is free under n8n's Sustainable Use License, which allows internal business use and non-commercial distribution only. Files marked `.ee` need a separate n8n Enterprise License.

The company is independent and well funded. It raised a $180M Series C led by Accel in October 2025 at a $2.5B valuation, with NVIDIA's NVentures participating. In May 2026 SAP bought shares in a secondary sale that valued n8n at $5.2B, and signed a multi-year agreement to embed n8n in SAP's Joule Studio agent builder. n8n's press page puts total funding at $240M.

## Why it shows up in RevOps, Legal Ops and Recruiting stacks

- **Billing counts runs, not steps.** An execution is one run of a whole workflow, however many steps it has. A 20-step enrichment-and-routing flow that fires 500 times a month uses 500 executions on n8n and 10,000 tasks on a per-step meter. That one design choice is why teams move here when their workflows get longer.
- **MCP works in both directions, on every paid plan.** The instance-level MCP server lets Claude Code, Claude.ai, ChatGPT, [Cursor](/en/tools/cursor/), Codex, Gemini CLI and VS Code search workflows, run the ones you have exposed, and, from n8n 2.13, build and edit workflows and data tables. Clients connect over OAuth or a personal API key. The MCP Server Trigger node turns one workflow into a purpose-built MCP server, and the MCP Client node lets an n8n agent call outside servers. n8n's pricing page lists all three on Starter, Pro and Enterprise.
- **Data can stay in your perimeter.** When you self-host, customer records never leave your infrastructure. On n8n's cloud, data sits in Frankfurt, which settles the residency question for EU Legal Ops and Recruiting teams handling candidate and contract data.

## Pricing reality

n8n.io/pricing quotes in euros only, checked 2026-11-22. Every plan includes unlimited users, unlimited workflows and every integration. Prices below are per month, billed annually. The page advertises annual billing as "Save 17%", which puts monthly billing about 20% higher.

- **Community Edition — free, self-hosted.** No execution cap. No SSO, Git version control or environments. Registering an email unlocks folders and debugging in the editor.
- **Starter — €20/month** (about $23). 2,500 executions, 5 concurrent, 1 shared project, 1,600 Assistant credits a month, 1 day of workflow history. Cloud only.
- **Pro — €50/month.** 10,000 executions, 20 concurrent, or 50 on the larger Pro size. 3 shared projects, admin roles, global variables, 5 days of workflow history, 7 days of insights, 4,000 or 9,600 Assistant credits depending on plan size. Cloud only.
- **Business — €667/month.** 40,000 executions, 6 shared projects, SSO and LDAP, Git version control, multiple environments, queue mode, 30 days of insights. Self-hosted only; a cloud version is waitlisted. It is aimed at companies under 100 employees and comes with forum support only.
- **Enterprise — custom.** Cloud or self-hosted, 200+ concurrent executions, log streaming, audit logging, external secret stores, 365 days of insights, and support with an SLA.
- **Start-up Plan — €333/month.** This is Business at 50% off, for companies with fewer than 20 employees and less than €5M in total funding. It lasts a year, or longer while you still qualify.

What changed since our September check: the prices and execution allowances held. Assistant credits fell by about 30%, from 2,300 to 1,600 on Starter and from up to 13,700 to up to 9,600 on Pro. The larger Pro size now allows up to 50 concurrent executions. The published overage rate of €4,000 per 300,000 extra executions is gone. Starter and Pro overage is now listed as "Capped", and the Business FAQ says only that overage charges "may apply" if you don't upgrade.

The real-world price band for a team of 5 to 50 builders: about €600 a year on Pro if cloud and no SSO are acceptable, about €4,000 a year on the Start-up Plan, or €8,004 a year on Business. The expensive step is Pro to Business. It buys SSO and Git-backed environments, and it also means moving from n8n's cloud to running n8n yourself. Our estimate for self-hosting on a mainstream cloud is $40–80 a month in infrastructure (one small VM, managed Postgres, Redis for queue mode), plus engineer time.

## Best for

- **Best for:** RevOps and GTM engineers at 20–200 person companies who build multi-step automation across CRM, enrichment and messaging, and want code steps and agent nodes in the same tool. The payoff is clearest once workflows run past 10 steps, because the per-execution meter keeps them cheap.
- Legal Ops and Recruiting teams that need contract or candidate data self-hosted or kept in the EU.
- Teams standardising on Claude, Codex or Cursor that want one orchestration layer those assistants can call and edit through MCP.
- **Not for** marketing or sales teams with no one who can read JSON or debug a failed HTTP node. Not for anyone planning to resell n8n-powered automation as their own product either, which the license forbids without a separate agreement.

## Versus the alternatives

- **[Zapier](/en/tools/zapier/)** — the market-share leader, with the widest connector catalogue and the gentlest learning curve, from $19.99/month. Pick it when the builders are non-technical and the flows are short. Pick n8n once flows get long, because Zapier bills every step as a task.
- **Microsoft Power Automate** — the other share leader, bundled into many Microsoft 365 agreements. Pick it when the company runs on Microsoft 365 and IT wants automation inside that tenant's governance.
- **[Make](/en/tools/make/)** — the cheapest paid entry, Core at $9/month, billed in credits, with a strong visual builder for complex branching. Pick it when budget is tight and nobody needs to self-host.
- **[Gumloop](/en/tools/gumloop/)** — the fastest-growing AI-native entrant, with a $50M Series B led by Benchmark in March 2026. Pro is $37/month for 20,000 credits. Pick it for teams without engineers who run document- and model-heavy agent pipelines. Pick n8n when you want code steps, self-hosting and a pricing unit that doesn't meter model calls.
- **[Activepieces](/en/tools/activepieces/)** — the open-source alternative, with an MIT core and Plus at $16/month. Pick it when the license has to be genuinely open source, or when you intend to embed automation in a product you sell.
- **[Workato](/en/tools/workato/)** — enterprise iPaaS, with a Vendr median of $64,544 a year. Pick it for governed CRM-to-ERP order-to-cash at enterprise scale, where n8n's forum-support Business tier is too thin.

## Watch-outs

- **The license is not open source.** The Sustainable Use License allows internal business use and non-commercial distribution only. **Guard:** if n8n will run workflows for your customers, or ship inside a product you sell, get n8n's Embed or commercial terms in writing before you build. If that is the plan from day one, evaluate Activepieces as well.
- **The Pro-to-Business step is a price jump and a platform move.** SSO, Git version control and environments are Business features, and Business is self-hosted only. **Guard:** at the pilot stage, decide whether security will require SSO within 12 months. If it will, budget for Business, or the Start-up Plan if you qualify, from the start rather than building on Cloud Pro and migrating later.
- **Overage is no longer priced in public.** Business and Enterprise workflows keep running past the quota, but n8n's FAQ says overage charges may apply, and annual Business customers hear from sales near 80% of their quota. **Guard:** get the per-execution overage rate written into the order form before you sign, and set an alert in the usage page at 60% of the annual allowance.
- **Polling triggers use up executions.** A schedule that fires every 5 minutes runs 288 times a day, about 8,640 executions a month. That is more than three times Starter's 2,500 from one workflow. **Guard:** use webhooks wherever the source app supports them, and check execution counts in the usage page during the first two weeks.
- **MCP exposure is not scoped per client.** Workflows are opt-in, one by one or by project and folder. But every connected client, Claude and ChatGPT alike, sees everything you have enabled, and the search tool returns previews of every workflow the user can see. **Guard:** expose only workflows that are safe for any connected assistant to trigger. Keep write-heavy or destructive ones off MCP or on a separate instance. On self-hosted instances, `N8N_DISABLED_MODULES=mcp` turns the server off entirely.
- **Self-hosted paid plans contact n8n daily.** Business and Enterprise license keys ping n8n's license server once a day with usage counts. **Guard:** allow outbound traffic to the license server in your network policy, and tell your security review before they find it themselves.
