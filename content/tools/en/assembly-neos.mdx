## What it is

Assembly Neos is cloud case management for plaintiff personal injury firms, sold by Assembly Software. It is the successor to Needles, the Windows PI system that began in 1985 as PINS and was renamed Needles in 1995. Needles and Trialworks came together as Assembly Software in 2019, and Neos is where Assembly now points Needles customers. Assembly says 1,450+ PI firms and 15,000+ attorneys and staff use its products. Neos runs on Microsoft Azure and is SOC 2 Type II certified. Jonah Paransky, formerly of Wolters Kluwer ELM and LexisNexis, became CEO on 2026-01-07.

The reason to look at it now is the AI. NeosAI, on the top tier, summarizes records, extracts data into case fields, generates documents and answers questions about a case. On 2026-11-10 Assembly released Demands Plus to general availability: it builds a medical chronology from the records already in the case, keeps it updated as new records arrive, flags treatment gaps and missing records, and drafts a settlement demand modelled on the firm's own letters, with citations that open the source record in Neos.

## Why it shows up in Legal Ops stacks

- **It is the installed base.** Thousands of plaintiff firms learned case management on Needles. For them the realistic choice is not "which PI system in the abstract" but "move to Neos, or migrate off Assembly entirely".
- **It answers the build-or-bolt-on question for demands.** Most PI firms buy demand letters from [EvenUp](/en/tools/evenup/) or [Supio](/en/tools/supio/). Neither vendor publishes a Neos connector: EvenUp's integration page names Litify, [SmartAdvocate](/en/tools/smartadvocate/) and [CasePeer](/en/tools/casepeer/), and Supio names Litify, MyCase and CasePeer. A Neos firm therefore either moves records by hand into an outside tool or uses Demands Plus inside the case file. Assembly also posts the Demands Plus cost to the case as a recoverable expense rather than firm overhead.
- **The intake and phone edges are wired.** Intake forms and an intake CRM are on every tier, and the top tier adds a dialer with call tracking through [RingCentral](/en/tools/ringcentral/) and Zoom Phone. [LawPay](/en/tools/lawpay/) handles payments, and DocuSign, [Zapier](/en/tools/zapier/), Case Status and YoCierge (medical records) are listed partners.

## Pricing reality

assemblysoftware.com/pricing, checked 2026-11-15, per user per month, billed yearly, 3-user minimum:

- **Essentials: $109.** Intake forms and CRM, lead tracking, document assembly and storage, email and calendar sync, reports, expenses and invoicing.
- **Premium: $139.** Adds OCR, PDF editing, redaction and Bates stamping.
- **NeosAI Platinum: quoted.** Adds NeosAI, automated workflows, Intake Pro, Neos Texting, the dialer and e-signature integrations.
- **Demands Plus:** an add-on that requires Platinum, priced on caseload. Assembly publishes no number.
- **Add-ons:** two-way texting on lower tiers, digital disbursements and medical records retrieval.

Directories (TrustRadius) still list $99 / $129 / $179, so budget off the live page. A 3-user firm on Essentials pays $3,924 a year. A 10-user firm on Premium pays $16,680 a year. Platinum plus Demands Plus is a sales conversation; get the per-demand or per-case rate in writing and set it against EvenUp's benchmarks of roughly $300 per demand before add-ons. Migration is run by an assigned CSM and Assembly says most land in 60 to 90 days.

## Best for

- **Best for:** managing partners and operations leads at plaintiff PI firms of 3-50 users, especially firms still on Needles, who want demands and chronologies drafted inside the case file instead of in a separate vendor.
- **Not for** solo attorneys (the 3-user minimum), mass-tort shops that need Filevine-grade workflow customization, or firms that want an open API or an MCP connector for Claude today; Assembly's pricing page lists neither.

## Versus the alternatives

- **vs [Filevine](/en/tools/filevine/).** The fastest-growing platform in plaintiff case management after its $400M raise in 2025, quote-only, with its own MedChron and DemandsAI. Pick Filevine for mass tort or heavy custom workflows; pick Neos for published seat prices and a migration path from Needles.
- **vs [CasePeer](/en/tools/casepeer/).** Published prices from $90 per user per month, with connectors to EvenUp and Supio. Pick CasePeer if you plan to buy demand AI from an outside vendor; pick Neos if you want it inside the system.
- **vs [SmartAdvocate](/en/tools/smartadvocate/).** Quote-only from about $109, with SmartIntelligence AI and an on-premise option. Pick SmartAdvocate if you must keep the system on your own servers.
- **vs [Litify](/en/tools/litify/).** Salesforce-based, roughly $150-300 per user per month all-in. Pick it only if the firm already runs Salesforce.
- **vs [Clio](/en/tools/clio/).** The market leader by firm count, but not PI-shaped. Pick it only if PI is a minority of the practice.
- **vs adding [EvenUp](/en/tools/evenup/).** Pick EvenUp when you want valuation analytics and a human review layer on each demand; Demands Plus does not estimate case value.

## Watch-outs

- **The AI only arrives on the top tier.** Every NeosAI feature, and Demands Plus, requires Platinum, which has no published price. **Guard:** ask for the Platinum quote and the Demands Plus rate before you compare Neos with CasePeer or Filevine at list price.
- **Demands Plus is new and the speed claims are the vendor's.** The 30-minutes-per-demand figure, against 3 to 10+ hours by hand, comes from Assembly's beta reports. **Guard:** run 5 closed cases through it during the trial and time the attorney review, not the draft.
- **No published API.** The pricing page lists no API access, which limits custom reporting and outside AI tools. **Guard:** confirm in writing what data export you get and how you would leave.
- **Migration runs a quarter, not a week.** 60 to 90 days is Assembly's own estimate. **Guard:** freeze custom field changes in Needles during the move and test one full case lifecycle before cutover.
