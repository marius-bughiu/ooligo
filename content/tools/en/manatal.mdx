## What it is

Manatal is the AI-augmented ATS-and-recruiting-CRM that has gained substantial traction in APAC and emerging markets through aggressive pricing and AI-native features. Founded in Bangkok, Manatal serves both corporate hiring teams and recruitment agencies on the same platform — a structural difference from Western ATS vendors that segment those markets.

## Why it shows up in Recruiting stacks

- **AI candidate recommendations.** ML-based matching of candidates against open roles using the platform's training data; usable enough to be the daily default rather than a side feature.
- **Aggressive pricing for the capability.** Per-user pricing starting at $19/month delivers ATS-and-CRM functionality that competitors charge 5-10x for in similar feature ranges.
- **APAC strength.** Localization, support hours, and feature priorities reflect the APAC corporate and agency markets in ways US-built tools don't.

## Pricing

- **Professional** — $19/user/month, basic ATS and CRM
- **Enterprise** — $39/user/month, advanced AI, custom workflows
- **Custom** — for large enterprise and agency-scale deployments
- Per-user model with low entry point makes Manatal accessible for solo recruiters and small agencies

## Best for

- APAC-headquartered companies and recruitment agencies
- Recruitment agencies running both client-side and corporate-side hiring on one platform
- Cost-sensitive SMB teams that need real ATS+CRM capability without enterprise pricing

## Watch-outs

- Less brand recognition in US/EU than [Workable](/en/tools/workable/), [Greenhouse](/en/tools/greenhouse/), or [Recruitee](/en/tools/recruitee/)
- AI quality is competitive but not category-leading vs specialist sourcing tools ([juicebox](/en/tools/juicebox/), [hireEZ](/en/tools/hireez/))
- Mid-market and enterprise buyers in mature markets tend to prefer locally-headquartered vendors despite price advantage
- Compete head-to-head with [Recruitee](/en/tools/recruitee/) (EU-strong) and [Workable](/en/tools/workable/) (US-strong) — fit depends largely on geographic alignment
