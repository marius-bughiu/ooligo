## What it is

Lexroom is a legal AI workspace built for civil-law Europe: research over national statutes and case law, analysis of uploaded contracts and case files, and drafting from scratch or from templates, with a Microsoft Word add-in. The Milan company was founded in 2023 by Paolo Fois, Martina Domenicali and Andrea Lonza, and sells today in Italy, Spain and Germany. Its pitch is "data-first": answers are grounded in a curated corpus of official sources rather than a general-purpose model's memory, which is the failure mode that makes US-trained tools weak on Italian, Spanish or German law. The vendor put that corpus at 6M+ verified sources when it raised a $50M Series B led by Left Lane Capital on 2026-05-19 (tech.eu), and at 13M+ after its first acquisitions.

The closest analogue is [Harvey](/en/tools/harvey/) for a single civil-law jurisdiction, sold at a solo-lawyer price instead of an AmLaw one.

## Why it shows up in Legal Ops stacks

- **It is local where the big platforms are generic.** Lexroom builds one corpus per country and hires local teams to tune features to each legal system. In Italy that includes support for the Processo Civile Telematico (PCT), the court e-filing system — a feature no US-built platform offers.
- **It is buying its way into new markets fast.** In September 2026 it acquired Query Juriste (France, about 5M documents, ~300 customers) and Praven Intelekt (Bulgaria, about 1.2M documents), then Brocardi, an Italian legal portal online since 2004 with roughly 1M registered users (Milano Finanza, 2026-09-16). That takes it to five markets.
- **The scale is real for a 2023 company.** It reports 20,000+ customers, 200+ employees and over €20M ARR in summer 2026 (Pulse 2.0, 2026-09-08). Most of the base is small firms: an earlier count had about 6,500 of 7,000 customers at 1-10 professionals (Startupbusiness).
- **EU-only hosting is the default.** ISO 27001, GDPR, EU-only servers, SSO, and a stated policy of never training on client documents. The German site also claims compliance with the BRAO §43a duty of confidentiality.

## Pricing reality

Lexroom publishes a starting price, which is rare in this category, but the number differs by market. Each tier covers 1 user, a private library of up to 200 documents and the Word add-in, on an annual contract, VAT excluded:

- **English site:** from €75/month, one module.
- **Italian and Spanish sites:** from €99/month.
- **German site:** from €150/month, with all modules included.

Read that as roughly €900-1,800 per user per year depending on country and module count — roughly a third to two-thirds of Legora's ~$3,000 per user per year with a 10-seat minimum. Firms above a handful of seats, or that need more than 200 stored documents, move to custom quotes; budget the 200-document cap as the first thing that forces an upgrade.

## Best for

Solo lawyers and small firms (roughly 1-20 professionals) in Italy, Spain or Germany, plus in-house teams at mid-market companies there, who want research and drafting grounded in local law at a published price, without a 10-seat minimum.

**Not for:** US or UK common-law work, firms whose documents must stay inside iManage or NetDocuments, or large firms that need multi-step agent workflows across deal rooms. Lexroom's coverage is national and its document workflow is a 200-document private library, not a DMS integration.

## Alternatives

- **[Legora](/en/tools/legora/)** — the fastest-growing platform in European legal AI. Pick it when you have 10+ seats, work across common- and civil-law matters, and need Tabular Review over thousands of documents.
- **[Harvey](/en/tools/harvey/)** — the market-share leader. Pick it for AmLaw-scale procurement and custom agent workflows when budget is not the gate.
- **[Noxtua](/en/tools/noxtua/)** — pick it in Germany when beck-online content and self-hosted European models matter more than price; it starts near €350 per user/month.
- **[Libra by Wolters Kluwer](/en/tools/wolters-kluwer-libra/)** — pick it when your firm already pays for Wolters Kluwer content in the Netherlands, Belgium, Italy or Central Europe.
- **[vLex Vincent AI](/en/tools/vlex-vincent-ai/)** — pick it when matters cross many jurisdictions and breadth beats national depth.

## Watch-outs

- **Pricing and coverage are not uniform across markets.** The same entry tier costs €75, €99 or €150 depending on which country page you land on, and Spain and Germany are newer than Italy. *Guard:* get the quote for your jurisdiction in writing, and test 10 real research questions from your own practice area during the trial before signing the annual contract.
- **The French and Bulgarian products are mid-migration.** Query Juriste and Praven Intelekt are being folded into Lexroom over time. *Guard:* if you buy either today, put a clause in the contract that fixes price and data export if the brand switches during your term.
- **There is almost no independent review base.** Capterra showed zero user reviews when we checked, and most user counts are vendor-reported. *Guard:* ask for two references at firms of your size in your country and confirm their actual seat price.
- **The 200-document library caps serious analysis work.** Litigation files and due diligence blow through it quickly. *Guard:* size your upload volume in the trial and price the next tier before rollout, not at renewal.
