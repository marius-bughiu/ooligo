## What it is

Attio is an AI-native, fully customizable CRM built for modern GTM teams. Where Salesforce makes you hire an admin and HubSpot pushes you into their opinions, Attio gives you a Notion-like data model — define objects, attributes, lists, and views the way your business actually works.

It is the CRM that startups graduate to from spreadsheets, and the CRM that ops-savvy series-A teams pick over HubSpot.

## Why it shows up in RevOps stacks

- **Schema flexibility without admin pain.** Build custom objects (deals, accounts, partners, investors) in minutes. No declarative-vs-flow debates, no AppExchange.
- **AI research and enrichment baked in.** Attio enriches contacts and companies on creation, summarizes activity, and supports custom AI prompts as fields.
- **Email + calendar as the source of truth.** Activity is captured automatically — no rep has to log a call.

## Pricing

- **Free** — up to 3 users, basic CRM
- **Plus** — $34/user/month, custom objects and views
- **Pro** — $69/user/month, advanced reporting and automations
- **Enterprise** — custom, SSO, audit logs, premium support

## Best for

- Series A-C startups picking their first real CRM
- RevOps teams who want a CRM that does not require a dedicated admin
- Teams running custom GTM motions (PLG, partnerships, investor CRM)

## Watch-outs

- Reporting is improving but not yet at Salesforce parity for complex orgs
- Smaller integration ecosystem than HubSpot or Salesforce — verify your stack works
- Mobile experience is still catching up to web
