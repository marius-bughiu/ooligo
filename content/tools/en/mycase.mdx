## What it is

MyCase is a cloud practice management platform for solo and small law firms, sold on published per-seat prices. It covers matters, contacts, calendaring, time entry, invoicing, trust accounting, a client portal and document storage, plus intake forms, a legal CRM and e-signature on its upper tiers. Its center of gravity is consumer-facing practice — personal injury, family, criminal defense, immigration — where intake volume and client communication weigh as much as the matter record. The vendor says more than 19,000 firms use it.

The owner changed its name, not the product's direction. AffiniPay, the LawPay payments company, bought MyCase from Apax-advised funds in June 2022, together with CasePeer (personal injury), Docketwise (immigration), Woodpecker (document automation) and Soluno (billing). In August 2025 AffiniPay renamed itself 8am, and the product is now sold as 8am MyCase. Its built-in AI ships as 8am IQ, running on OpenAI models: a Writing and Document Assistant on Pro, and a Case Assistant plus an OCR-backed Discovery Assistant on Advanced.

The September 2026 change is the one that moves it in an AI stack. At its Kaleidoscope conference 8am launched 8am MyCase MCP, an official, read-only connector that lets [Claude](/en/tools/claude/) search a firm's MyCase data, and put it on every plan, Basic included. 8am calls it the first official Claude connector for legal practice management. The category leader MyCase most resembles is [Clio](/en/tools/clio/); MyCase is the one you can price without a sales call.

## Why it shows up in Legal Ops stacks

- **Every tier has a published price.** Basic, Pro and Advanced all carry a number on mycase.com/pricing, with month-to-month billing, no setup fee and a 10-day trial without a credit card. A managing partner can size a budget in minutes. Clio publishes only its entry tier; Smokeball publishes none.
- **Claude can read the matter data on every tier.** The MCP connector answers questions such as overdue deadlines by lawyer, unbilled hours, invoices more than 30 days late, caseload by practice area or a case summary with upcoming dates, from live data with nothing to export. It honors each user's MyCase permissions and cannot create or change a record. Before it, getting MyCase data into an agent meant the open API, which only Advanced includes.
- **Payments and trust accounting come from the payments company.** LawPay costs $0 per month with processing fees on top, and because 8am owns both products, a payment lands in the matter and the trust ledger without a third-party sync.
- **The built-in AI works on the firm's own files.** The June 2026 update added OCR because, per 8am, 35% of documents uploaded to MyCase are scans or images that search could not read. The Case Assistant answers questions across a matter's documents and communications, and since November 2026 Advanced firms can test, in beta, drafting client messages and creating calendar events, tasks and notes from a Case Assistant chat. None of it is a legal research tool, and 8am does not claim it is.

## Pricing reality

mycase.com/pricing, checked 2026-11-21, per user per month:

- **Basic — $50 billed annually, $60 monthly.** Case and contact management, time entry, invoicing, trust accounting, online payments with next-day deposits, customizable reporting, unlimited storage, a limited client portal and the MCP connector. No third-party integrations.
- **Pro — $100 annually, $120 monthly.** Adds unlimited e-signature and document generation, automated workflows, intake forms and legal CRM, 2-way texting, chat and call log, premium billing, the 8am IQ Writing and Document Assistant and 70+ integrations.
- **Advanced — $130 annually, $150 monthly.** Adds the 8am IQ Case Assistant and Discovery Assistant (5,000 OCR pages per user per month, pooled across the firm; another 5,000 pages for the whole firm costs $30 and expires with the subscription period), advanced document automation, a desktop drive, split billing and the open API.
- **Add-ons.** MyCase Accounting is $39 per user per month and is now sold only on Pro and Advanced. LawPay carries no monthly fee.
- **The MCP connector has a second bill.** It is included in MyCase, but it requires a Claude Team or Enterprise plan, paid separately. Claude Team lists at $25 per seat monthly or $20 billed annually.

Annual billing saves 13-17%, and nothing public points to negotiated discounts at small-firm seat counts, so list price is close to what firms pay (estimate). In practice a solo lawyer on Pro pays $1,200 a year, a five-lawyer firm on Pro pays $6,000, and the same firm on Advanced with accounting pays $10,140. A firm buying MyCase for intake or built-in AI needs Pro at minimum, because Basic has neither.

The MCP connector changes the cheapest route to AI over matter data. Five lawyers on Basic plus Claude Team, both billed annually, cost $4,200 a year ($70 a seat a month), against $7,800 for the same five on Advanced. The trade is real: the Basic-plus-Claude route reads structured records and documents but gets no OCR on scanned files, no 8am IQ drafting inside MyCase and no API. A firm whose files are mostly scans still needs Advanced.

## Best for

- Managing partners and firm administrators at consumer-facing firms of 1-20 lawyers — personal injury, family, criminal defense, immigration — who want intake, billing, payments and a client portal in one system at a price they can read off a web page.
- Firms already paying for Claude Team or Enterprise that want partners to ask questions of the firm's matters and billing without building an integration.
- Firms already processing payments through LawPay.
- **Not for** in-house legal teams (this is firm-side software built around time and trust accounting), transactional or corporate practices that need deep document management, or firms that want legal research inside the matter.

## Versus the alternatives

- **vs [Clio](/en/tools/clio/).** The market-share leader. After its $1B vLex acquisition, Clio Work puts legal research and drafting inside the matter, and accounting is included from Starter at $49. Clio also now lists its own connector in Claude's directory, so an official MCP route is no longer what separates the two. Core, Signature and Elite — the tiers with AI — are quote-only. Pick Clio when research and drafting are part of the work you are automating, or the firm is heading toward 50 lawyers; pick MyCase when a predictable price and intake matter more. The full breakdown is in [Clio vs MyCase](/en/vs/clio-vs-mycase/).
- **vs PracticePanther.** The closest match on published pricing: Solo $49, Essential $69, Business $89 and Business Pro $114 per user per month billed annually ($59 to $124 monthly), with accounting bundled into Business Pro. Pick it when full accounting inside the subscription matters more than AI; pick MyCase when you want 8am IQ or the Claude connector.
- **vs Smokeball.** Quote-only across four plans (Bill, Boost, Grow, Prosper+), strongest for document-heavy practices, with its Archie AI assistant sold as an add-on. Pick it for automatic time capture and document automation, and expect a sales process to get a number.
- **vs [Filevine](/en/tools/filevine/).** Built for plaintiff firms running thousands of active matters on templated workflows. At that volume MyCase is the wrong shape; 8am's own CasePeer is its answer for personal injury.
- **Alongside [EvenUp](/en/tools/evenup/) or [Eve](/en/tools/eve-legal/).** The fastest-growing entrants in this buyer's budget are plaintiff AI, not practice management: EvenUp was valued above $2B in October 2025 and Eve above $1B a month earlier. They draft demands and medical chronologies; MyCase holds the matter, the calendar and the trust ledger. A personal injury firm that buys one still needs the other.

## Watch-outs

- **The MCP connector works only with Claude, and only on a business plan.** 8am names no other client, and Claude Pro or Max accounts cannot connect it. It is read-only, so an agent can find the overdue invoice but cannot send the reminder, log the time or update the matter. **Guard:** budget the Claude Team seats alongside MyCase before promising partners the feature, and keep any write-back in MyCase's own workflows or the Advanced API.
- **Claude sees what the user sees.** Access follows each user's MyCase permissions, which means a loosely permissioned firm hands Claude the same loose access, including to privileged matters. **Guard:** audit MyCase roles before connecting, and check Claude's data terms under your Team or Enterprise agreement against your engagement letters.
- **The API and the stronger built-in AI sit on the top tier.** The Case Assistant, OCR and the open API are Advanced-only. A firm that buys Pro and later needs to sync with a system outside the 70+ integrations pays $30 more per seat. **Guard:** list every integration and AI use case before the trial, and pick the tier against that list.
- **8am IQ sends documents to OpenAI, and OCR cannot be undone.** Customer data does not train third-party models, but 8am's FAQ reserves the right to use inputs and outputs to improve its own products, and OCR already applied to documents cannot be reversed. **Guard:** check the FAQ and data-processing terms against your engagement letters, and switch 8am IQ off in Firm Settings before migrating sensitive files until that review is done.
- **Accounting is no longer available on Basic.** The $39 add-on is sold only on Pro and Advanced, so a firm that needs a general ledger inside MyCase pays at least $139 per seat. **Guard:** if you need operating accounting, price Clio Starter and PracticePanther Business Pro, where it is included, before assuming MyCase is the cheaper option.
