## What it is

Calendly is a scheduling platform that lets prospects, candidates, or anyone with your link book time on your calendar without email back-and-forth. The free tier handles individual one-on-one booking; the paid tiers add round-robin distribution, routing forms that qualify and direct leads based on form responses, CRM integration, and team administration. Since August 19, 2026 it also sells two AI tiers, Standard Plus and Teams Plus, which bundle Calendly Notetaker (recording, transcripts, and recaps across Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams) with Callie, a beta assistant that negotiates meeting times over email. Its growth model is product-led: individuals adopt it for free, their booking links spread to every contact they email, and those contacts bring it to employers who eventually buy Teams or Enterprise. Calendly raised $352M at a $3B valuation in its 2021 Series B, and Sacra estimates it reached about $349M ARR at the end of 2024, up roughly 40% year over year; there has been no new round since. In a RevOps stack it most often replaces email scheduling; for revenue routing it competes with [Chili Piper](/en/tools/chili-piper/) and [Default](/en/tools/default/), and for interview scheduling with [GoodTime](/en/tools/goodtime/).

## Why it shows up in RevOps and recruiting stacks

- **The free viral loop puts it on every team's calendar before anyone approves it.** Calendly links spread via email signatures, LinkedIn messages, and outreach sequences. By the time RevOps writes a scheduling policy, reps and recruiters already have active accounts, so standardizing on Calendly is usually a consolidation move, not a greenfield purchase.
- **Teams-tier routing covers most inbound qualification workflows.** The $16/seat/month Teams plan includes routing forms that qualify leads from [HubSpot](/en/tools/hubspot/), Marketo, Pardot, or a native Calendly form, round-robin across reps, routing by HubSpot or [Salesforce](/en/tools/salesforce/) assignment, and meeting records written back to Salesforce. For teams that don't need account-ownership lookup, this does the job without a dedicated routing platform.
- **The API, webhooks, and MCP server are now open on every plan.** Since November 6, 2026, webhooks and the Scheduling API (`POST /invitees`) work on the Free plan; previously Free accounts got a 403. Free accounts are capped at 5 API bookings per user per day and 50 requests per user per minute, paid plans keep higher limits, and three data-deletion and activity-log endpoints stay Enterprise-only. Calendly's hosted MCP server at `https://mcp.calendly.com` (OAuth 2.1 with Dynamic Client Registration, no personal access tokens, no self-hosting) maps natural-language requests onto API v2 calls for event types, availability, and booking. For GTM engineers wiring Calendly into outbound sequences or AI agents, the integration layer no longer forces a plan upgrade. Zapier is the exception: it still requires a paid plan.
- **The [Greenhouse](/en/tools/greenhouse/) and [Lever](/en/tools/lever/) integrations close the interview scheduling loop.** Candidates self-schedule from a Greenhouse or Lever email, picking a slot from the interviewer's Calendly availability, and the confirmed event lands in the ATS without recruiter intervention. For a recruiter running 20-plus open roles, that removes most of the back-and-forth per interview round.

## Pricing reality

List prices are published and unchanged since the last check; the packaging around them is what moved:

- **Free:** 1 event type, 1 calendar connection, one-on-one booking. Now includes webhooks and the Scheduling API at the Free rate limits above.
- **Standard:** $10/seat/month billed annually. Unlimited event types, multiple calendars, reminders and automations, HubSpot, Mailchimp, Stripe and PayPal, Zapier.
- **Standard Plus:** $18/seat/month billed annually ($24 monthly). Standard plus Notetaker and Callie, English only.
- **Teams:** $16/seat/month billed annually ($20 monthly). Round-robin, routing forms, Marketo and Pardot, Salesforce record sync, centrally managed event types, optional SSO add-on.
- **Teams Plus:** $24/seat/month billed annually ($32 monthly). Teams plus Notetaker, Callie, shared recaps, and advanced admin controls. Since September 2026 the free trial runs on Teams Plus, not Teams.
- **Enterprise:** starts at $15,000/year with a 50-seat minimum, USD only. Adds Salesforce lookup routing, Microsoft Dynamics, SSO/SAML, domain control, audit logs, the Data Deletion API, and dedicated support.

The two Plus prices come from Calendly's pricing page as tracked by usagepricing.com and cross-checked against two independent pricing write-ups; the base prices were read directly on calendly.com/pricing on 2026-11-25. All users in one organization must sit on the same plan, so adding Notetaker for five AEs means upgrading every seat.

The real-world band: a 10-seat RevOps team pays $1,920/year on Teams or $2,880/year on Teams Plus. [Chili Piper](/en/tools/chili-piper/) no longer sells per-seat modules; its entry Growth bundle is $15,000/year for 15 seats, billed annually only. At 15 seats Calendly Teams costs $2,880/year, about one-fifth of that.

## Best for

RevOps and TA teams at companies with 25–500 employees who need routing forms and round-robin at a predictable per-seat price, run under roughly 1,000 inbound leads or candidate handoffs a month, and have no dedicated routing engineer. The Teams plan at $16/seat/month is the target; buy Teams Plus only if you want the notetaker and don't already pay for [Gong](/en/tools/gong/), [Fathom](/en/tools/fathom/), or [Fireflies](/en/tools/fireflies/).

It is also the right pick for a recruiting coordinator scheduling interviews through Greenhouse or Lever: candidate self-scheduling removes the email loop and reduces drop-off between rounds.

Do not buy Calendly if you need Salesforce account-ownership routing at fewer than 50 seats, since that capability is Enterprise-only and the Enterprise floor is $15,000/year and 50 seats. Skip it if you need enrichment-based qualification before booking (Calendly routes on self-reported form answers) or a per-lead routing audit trail inside your marketing platform.

## Versus the alternatives

[Chili Piper](/en/tools/chili-piper/) is the main competitor for revenue-team routing. Its Growth bundle ($15,000/year, 15 seats, $45 per extra seat per month) covers form-to-calendar booking, Salesforce ownership lookup, weighted round-robin, and handoff SLAs, with AI credits included. Pick Chili Piper when you run 1,000-plus inbound leads a month through Salesforce, have a RevOps engineer to maintain rules, and need a decision log for every handoff. Pick Calendly when volume is lower and you want routing that works without a configuration owner.

[Default](/en/tools/default/) is the newer challenger in inbound routing, pitching form enrichment plus routing as one workflow. Pick it over Calendly when enrichment before the booking step matters more than scheduling UX.

[GoodTime](/en/tools/goodtime/) is built for high-volume structured hiring: panel scheduling, multi-timezone coordination, and interviewer load-balancing at a depth Calendly's round-robin does not reach. Pick GoodTime when your TA team books 50-plus panel interviews a week; pick Calendly in the 5–30 interviews a week range.

Cal.com is the open-source alternative, with Teams at $12/user/month billed annually and webhooks on its free tier. Pick it when you want to self-host or need to own the scheduling data; pick Calendly when the integration catalog and invitee familiarity matter more.

## Watch-outs

- **Salesforce account-ownership routing is Enterprise-only.** Teams that buy Calendly Teams expecting to route inbound leads to the AE who owns the account find it gated behind Enterprise, which now carries both the $15,000/year floor and a 50-seat minimum. Guard: map every routing rule before choosing a tier; if any rule depends on CRM record ownership and you have fewer than 50 seats, price Chili Piper's Growth bundle against Calendly Enterprise before signing.
- **The AI tiers are all-or-nothing per organization.** Notetaker and Callie only come bundled in the Plus tiers, and every user in an org must be on the same plan, so a pilot for a few reps becomes an $8/seat/month uplift for the whole account. Callie is still beta and both features are English-only. Guard: run the pilot on a separate trial org, and check what your existing call recorder already captures before paying for a second one.
- **Round-robin lacks routing transparency.** Calendly does not expose a log of why a given rep received a meeting. If a lead goes to the wrong rep and the deal is lost, RevOps cannot reconstruct the decision. Guard: for high-value inbound, use a CRM workflow to stamp the assigned rep and timestamp on the record at booking time.
- **Free-plan API limits are easy to hit in production.** Five Scheduling API bookings per user per day is enough to prototype an AI-agent booking flow and not enough to run one. Guard: load-test against your expected daily booking volume and move the booking user to a paid plan before launch.
- **Marketing attribution breaks on hosted booking pages.** Booking through a standalone Calendly link usually drops the UTM chain from the originating campaign. Guard: embed Calendly inline on your own domain and verify UTM pass-through to the CRM in staging before sending paid traffic to it.
