## What it is

Contentsquare is the digital-experience analytics platform that already owns three of the products a Customer Success team is probably running. It bought Hotjar in 2021, [Heap](/en/tools/heap/) in 2023, and Loris AI on 2025-10-23, then merged them into one platform: `hotjar.com/pricing` now redirects to `contentsquare.com/pricing`, and Heap is a product line inside Contentsquare rather than a company you can buy from. If you are evaluating Hotjar or Heap in 2026, you are evaluating Contentsquare and you are negotiating with one vendor.

The company was founded in Paris in 2012 and reports $1.4B raised, roughly 1,500 employees across 14 offices, 3,000 enterprise and mid-market customers, and technology deployed on 1.3 million websites (vendor press page, checked 2026-08-31). Nine acquisitions to date.

What it does, in the shape a CS leader cares about: session replay and heatmaps (the Hotjar lineage), account-level product analytics with retroactive event definition (the Heap lineage), surveys and feedback widgets (Voice of Customer), and — since the 2026-03-17 release — conversation intelligence over support tickets, calls, and in-product chat (the Loris lineage).

## Why it shows up in Customer Success stacks

- **It is the only platform in this catalog carrying web behavior and support conversations under one roof.** Loris was a support-side conversation-intelligence vendor, a Gartner Cool Vendor in Customer Service and Support Technology, with models trained across more than a billion customer interactions. Contentsquare bought it to connect *why the customer contacted support* to *what they did in the product first*. That is the join most health scores fake by bolting two disconnected tools together.
- **Retroactive analysis, inherited from Heap.** Autocapture lets you ask "how many renewal-quarter accounts opened the billing page and bounced?" against data nobody instrumented in advance. Precision filtering applied retroactively is a Pro-tier feature, not a Growth one.
- **MCP is documented, metered, and present on every tier.** Contentsquare ships an MCP server with per-tier tool-call budgets — 300 calls/month on Free, 36k/year on Growth, 72k/year on Pro, 108k/year on Enterprise — and appears in Anthropic's Claude Connectors Directory. A CSM can ask Claude for the friction data instead of filing a ticket with the analytics team. The quota is the real constraint, not the connection.
- **Sense Analyst runs analysis on a schedule.** Launched 2026-03-17, it builds a configurable "Newsroom" against your KPIs and emails findings without being asked. Contentsquare-published data from 2026-01-29 puts blended human-plus-AI resolution at 57% of inquiries versus 29% for bot-only support — vendor-sourced, so treat it as directional rather than as a business case.

## Pricing reality

List price, from the vendor pricing page (checked 2026-08-31):

- **Experience Analytics Free** — $0. 200k monthly sessions, 10k session replay captures, 1 project, unlimited heatmaps, MCP capped at 300 tool calls/month.
- **Experience Analytics Growth** — from $49/mo billed annually. 3 projects, 13 months of data access, zone-based heatmaps, journey analysis, Sense Chat and AI summaries, MCP at 36k tool calls/year.
- **Experience Analytics Pro** — custom quote, sized from 1M+ monthly sessions. Up to 6 projects, retroactive precision filtering, multi-session replay summaries, revenue goals, Sense Analyst available as a paid add-on.
- **Enterprise** — custom. Unlimited projects, experience monitoring, error summaries, data feeds, dedicated support and SLAs.
- **Voice of Customer** — Free at 100 monthly responses across 3 surveys; Growth at $99/mo billed annually for 500–100k monthly responses; Pro custom.
- **Product Analytics** (the Heap lineage) — every tier is a custom quote, sized from 10k to 1M+ monthly sessions.

What teams actually pay is a different number. Vendr's transaction data puts the median Contentsquare contract at **$20,000/year**, with observed deals spanning $14,674 to $92,136. By deployment size: 500k–2M monthly sessions across 1–2 properties lands at **$30k–$80k/yr** with 10–20% off the first quote; 2–5M sessions at **$80k–$200k**; 5–20M+ sessions at **$200k–$500k+** with 20–30% discounts available. Multi-year commitments come in 15–30% lower on effective annual price.

The distance between "$49/mo" and "$20k/yr median" is the budgeting problem on this page. Free and Growth are the old Hotjar business, priced for a growth marketer. Everything a CS org buys this platform for — account rollups, retroactive filtering, conversation intelligence, Sense Analyst — sits above the paywall in custom-quote territory.

## Best for

CS and CS-ops leaders at 200+ employee companies running a self-serve or hybrid motion, where renewal risk shows up in product and web behavior weeks before it surfaces in a QBR, and where the support queue is a large enough signal source to be worth mining. Best economics when CS, product, and digital/e-commerce share a single contract — session-metered pricing punishes you for buying the platform twice.

## Do not buy it if

Your CS team is under ten people and all you need is product-usage signal. The Free tier is a fine pilot, but the Pro quote you graduate into is not defensible at that headcount — [PostHog](/en/tools/posthog/) on usage pricing or [Amplitude](/en/tools/amplitude/)'s free tier gets you most of the health-score inputs without a five-figure commit. Skip it too if your product is not a web or mobile app: Contentsquare measures interfaces, and if your customers' work happens over API or inside a shared Slack channel, there are no sessions to analyze. Feedback-aggregation buyers whose signal is text rather than clicks are better served by [Enterpret](/en/tools/enterpret/).

## Versus the alternatives

- **Quantum Metric and Glassbox** hold the enterprise pole — heavier deployments, high-traffic digital estates, compliance-driven capture. Pick one of those if you are a bank, airline, or telco with a regulatory replay requirement and a dedicated digital-analytics team. Contentsquare concedes some enterprise capture depth in exchange for a much wider product surface.
- **FullStory** is the closest mid-market substitute on session replay alone. Pick it when replay quality is the entire purchase and you do not want product analytics or surveys inside the contract.
- **[Amplitude](/en/tools/amplitude/) and [Pendo](/en/tools/pendo/)** own the product-analytics decision this competes against. Pick Amplitude when the primary consumer is a product team running experiments; pick Pendo when you want in-app guides and NPS in the same tool as the analytics. Contentsquare wins when the question is "what did this customer experience," not "what did this cohort convert at."
- **[PostHog](/en/tools/posthog/)** is the fastest-growing entrant and the price discipline in this segment — open source, self-hostable, usage-priced, replay and product analytics in one product. Pick it when engineering owns the analytics decision and the five-figure commit needs justifying to a CFO.
- None of these replace the CS platform itself. Contentsquare feeds signal into [Gainsight](/en/tools/gainsight/), [Vitally](/en/tools/vitally/), or [Catalyst](/en/tools/catalyst/); it does not run playbooks, own renewals, or manage the customer health workflow.

## Watch-outs

- **Three acquired products, three data lineages, one migration still in flight.** Hotjar merged into Contentsquare in July 2025; the Heap and Loris layers are still being folded in through 2026. *Guard:* during the trial, take one named account and confirm that its Heap-lineage usage rollup, its Hotjar-lineage session replay, and its Loris-lineage support conversation all resolve to the same customer identity in the same interface. If they do not, you are buying three tools on one invoice.
- **Session-metered pricing bills you for traffic growth, not for value received.** A good quarter for marketing becomes a budget overrun for CS. *Guard:* negotiate a session ceiling with the overage rate written into the contract itself rather than referenced from a rate card, and take the multi-year commitment for the 15–30% effective discount if you are confident past year one.
- **Conversation intelligence is the newest capability here and the main reason a CS team would buy the platform.** Loris closed 2025-10-23 and shipped inside Contentsquare on 2026-03-17 — under two quarters of production history at Contentsquare scale as of this writing. *Guard:* ask for a reference customer in your industry running it longer than two quarters, and keep it out of load-bearing renewal-risk scoring in year one. Human review stays in the loop.
- **Sense Analyst is a paid add-on, not a tier feature.** The scheduled-analysis capability that makes this attractive to a lean CS team is priced separately on Pro. *Guard:* require the add-on price in the same proposal as the base platform. Pricing it afterwards is how a $30k deployment becomes a $45k one.
- **The AI layer is retrofit, not native.** This is a 2012 analytics company that added agents in 2026, which is why `ai_native` is false in this entry's frontmatter. *Guard:* evaluate Sense Chat and Sense Analyst against your own event data during the trial, never the demo dataset — retrofit AI layers degrade fastest on messy real-world event taxonomies.
