Five sales engagement platforms worth considering in 2026, ranked by who they're actually right for. The category has been commodified at the bottom and AI-rebuilt at the top — pick accordingly.

## 1. Outreach — the enterprise outbound default

Outreach is the platform for serious outbound orgs at 50+ SDRs/AEs. Sequence depth, conversation intelligence (Kaia), forecasting, and the broadest integration ecosystem. ooligo score: 8.7.

**What it replaces:** spreadsheet sequence tracking, Salesloft at the upper end, the custom Python scripts your RevOps team built to track step-level reply rates.

**Where to start:** if you're scaling past 25 reps and Salesloft is creaking, run a 60-day Outreach pilot on one team. Migrate sequences carefully.

**[Full Outreach review →](/en/tools/outreach/)**

## 2. Salesloft — the strong number two

Salesloft is functionally on par with Outreach for most teams under 100 reps. Cleaner UX, faster onboarding, slightly less raw power at the very top of the market. ooligo score: 8.5.

**What it replaces:** Outreach for teams that don't need its complexity, the spreadsheet-driven outbound at smaller orgs.

**Where to start:** if you're picking new and don't have a strong reason for Outreach, default to Salesloft.

**[Full Salesloft review →](/en/tools/salesloft/)**

## 3. Apollo — the all-in-one for SMB outbound

Apollo bundles data + sequences + enrichment + dialer at a price point Outreach and Salesloft can't match. The right answer for SMB and early-stage teams. ooligo score: 8.3.

**What it replaces:** ZoomInfo + Outreach + a separate dialer, at roughly 1/3 the cost.

**Where to start:** if you're under 25 reps and outbound is a meaningful motion, Apollo is the default starting point.

**[Full Apollo review →](/en/tools/apollo/)**

## 4. Default — the AI-native inbound + outbound platform

Default is the AI-first inbound routing and outbound platform. If you're rebuilding the GTM stack from scratch in 2026, it's worth a serious look. ooligo score: 8.6.

**What it replaces:** Chili Piper + Outreach + the manual lead routing work, with AI workflows as the connective tissue.

**Where to start:** pilot Default for inbound routing first. Expand into outbound sequences once you've seen the AI workflow ergonomics.

**[Full Default review →](/en/tools/default/)**

## 5. Regie.ai — AI agents for outbound

Regie.ai is building the AI sales agent layer — autonomous prospecting workflows that run sequence drafting and follow-up with humans in the loop. ooligo score: 8.0.

**What it replaces:** SDR-drafted email copy, the playbook handoff that breaks when an SDR leaves.

**Where to start:** layer Regie.ai on top of your existing engagement platform for one team. Measure reply rate and meeting-set rate against the human-only baseline.

**[Full Regie.ai review →](/en/tools/regie-ai/)**

## What's not on this list (and why)

- **Mixmax, Yesware** — fine email tools, but they're not really sales engagement platforms.
- **HubSpot Sequences** — works if you're already on HubSpot Sales Hub Pro/Enterprise. Don't buy a SEP on top of it unless you've outgrown native sequences.
- **Smartlead, Instantly** — cold email infrastructure tools. Different category — they're for high-volume cold senders, not full sales engagement.

## The minimum viable choice

If you're picking new in 2026:

1. Under 25 reps: **Apollo**
2. 25-100 reps: **Salesloft**
3. 100+ reps and complex motion: **Outreach**

Layer Clay underneath whichever one you pick. The data quality of your sequences matters more than the engine.
