## What it is

Bullhorn is the ATS-and-CRM platform for staffing firms, contract recruiters, and search agencies — the recruiting segment that places candidates into roles at client companies rather than hiring for one employer. Founded 2002, it runs the recruiting operations of more than 10,000 companies, 4,500+ of them on the small-agency plans. The data model is what separates it from corporate-side ATS products (iCIMS, Greenhouse, Workable): it assumes many client relationships, contract placements, and a billing cycle, not a single hiring org.

Two things moved in 2026. Bullhorn began publishing list prices for small agencies after two decades of quote-only selling, and it consolidated its AI under one name, Amplify. At Engage Boston on 2026-05-28 the company expanded Amplify to nine named digital-worker skills — Enrich, Match, Screen, Outreach and Present, joined by Prospect, Verify, Audit and Transcribe — plus Amplify Chat, a plain-language layer that queries live Bullhorn data and triggers multi-step workflows.

## Why it shows up in Recruiting stacks

- **Built for staffing-firm workflow.** The record model assumes the user places candidates with multiple client companies. Client management, contract management, billing and timesheet tracking are first-class objects, not modules bolted onto a corporate ATS.
- **Deep VMS integration.** Vendor management systems are the platforms enterprises use to run their staffing suppliers. That integration is required infrastructure for any firm working Fortune 500 accounts, and it is the reason large agencies stay on Bullhorn even when they dislike the interface.
- **Recruiter CRM depth.** Long-cycle candidate relationship tracking, communication history, placement history, and re-engagement workflows that a corporate ATS never has to model.
- **The AI now acts on the record rather than beside it.** Amplify's agentic skills run against Bullhorn's own data. The figures Bullhorn published at Engage are customer-reported and single-firm: Procom cited a 65% increase in submission-to-placement rate, IDR reported 200 hours saved per week, and early adopters averaged a 39% increase in placements per recruiter. Read those as direction, not as a number you can budget against.

## Pricing

Small-agency plans are published per user per month, VAT excluded:

- **Starter — $99/user/month.** Applicant tracking, resume parsing, client management, job posting with a career portal, email and calendar sync, live support. Implementation included. No AI.
- **Core — $165/user/month.** Adds the marketplace (350+ pre-integrated partners), custom fields and workflows, LinkedIn RSC and Apply Connect, and the email sidebar. Implementation included. Still no AI.
- **Pro — quote only.** The first tier with any AI: recruitment CRM, AI search and match, Amplify Chat, AI data enrichment, process automation, resume formatting, call transcription, real-time analytics, and a named account manager.
- **Max — quote only.** The agentic tier: agentic screening, matching and enrichment, omni-channel candidate engagement, multi-step outreach, a website chatbot, and interview scheduling. Implementation is scoped into the quote rather than included.

Larger agencies buy Front Office Growth, Front Office Enterprise, or Bullhorn One, all quote-only. Onboarding, Middle Office, Messaging and Analytics are licensed separately at every size. There is no free trial.

**The list price is not the invoice.** Bullhorn's page says "No setup fees. No hidden costs," and on Starter, Core and Pro that holds for implementation. Third-party TCO trackers still put a ten-seat first year at $34,000-$46,100 — roughly $283-$384 per user per month all-in — once training ($100-$500 per user) and the separately licensed modules land, with renewal uplift reported near 20% a year and uncapped by default. Model the modules and the renewal, not the seat.

Against the rest of the segment: Loxo publishes $149 (Core) and $199 (Professional) per user per month on annual billing, with its agentic workforce and 2,500 pooled monthly credits inside the $199. Recruiterflow publishes $149 per user per month for its platform and quotes its AIRA agents separately. Bullhorn has the cheapest published entry price of the three and is the only one of the three that publishes no price for AI at all.

## Best for

- **Owner-operators and recruiters at 1-30-seat staffing firms and search agencies** who want a working ATS/CRM at a known number on day one — Starter is a complete applicant tracking system, not a limited trial.
- **Staffing ops leads at 50+ seats** who need VMS integration and a back office in the same system as the front office. That is the quote-only Front Office and Bullhorn One line, not the published tiers.
- **RPO providers** running placements across many client orgs, where contract, timesheet and billing data has to reconcile back to the placement record.

## Watch-outs

- **The published tiers contain no AI.** Every Amplify capability — search and match, Amplify Chat, agentic screening — starts at Pro, and Pro carries no published price. *Guard:* ask for the Pro and Max quotes in the first sales call and price them against the $165 Core line. Scoping a deal on the $99 headline and then discovering the AI sits two quote-gated tiers above it is the standard failure on this vendor.
- **Cost is driven by modules, not seats.** Onboarding, Middle Office, Messaging and Analytics are priced apart from the per-user rate. *Guard:* get every module you expect to use quoted at signature, and negotiate a written renewal cap — third-party data reports roughly 20% annual uplift with no cap by default.
- **Wrong product category for in-house corporate hiring.** *Guard:* if you hire for one employer, use [Greenhouse](/en/tools/greenhouse/), [Ashby](/en/tools/ashby/), [Lever](/en/tools/lever/), [Workable](/en/tools/workable/), [iCIMS](/en/tools/icims/) or [SmartRecruiters](/en/tools/smartrecruiters/) instead. Bullhorn's client, contract and billing objects will sit empty and you will pay for them anyway.
- **No official MCP server.** Bullhorn ships a documented REST API, but agent access runs through third-party connectors such as StackOne, or community MCP servers built on that API. *Guard:* if an outside agent has to reach Bullhorn records, budget it as an integration project against the REST API, and confirm the connector's auth model before it touches candidate PII.
- **The interface shows its age.** Newer staffing challengers — [Loxo](/en/tools/loxo/), Vincere — compete directly on speed of use. *Guard:* there is no free trial, so run the demo with your own two heaviest recruiters and time a full submission cycle before signing a multi-year term.
