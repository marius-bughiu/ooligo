## What it is

Fathom is an AI notetaker that records, transcribes, and summarizes Zoom, Meet, and Teams calls, then pushes the summary and action items into [Salesforce](/en/tools/salesforce/), [HubSpot](/en/tools/hubspot/), Slack, Notion, or Asana. It is the freemium answer to [Gong](/en/tools/gong/) — free forever for one person, paid only when you need CRM writeback or search across a team's calls.

Two things changed in 2026. Fathom added bot-free capture and a desktop app, so a call can be recorded without a visible bot participant. And it now publishes a public API and an MCP server, with first-party ChatGPT and Claude integrations on top, so an agent can query your call history instead of a person digging through it. The vendor also moved from fathom.video to fathom.ai; the old domain 301s to the new one.

## Why it shows up in RevOps stacks

- **The free tier has no recording cap.** Reps adopt it bottom-up before procurement hears about it. Unlimited recording, transcription, and summaries at $0 is the reason it spreads.
- **Summaries are usable on the first call.** 15+ summary templates from Premium up, and no prompt-tuning pass before the output is worth reading.
- **Agent access is first-party now.** Public API and MCP ship on every paid plan, so call transcripts wire into a Claude or ChatGPT workflow without a third-party connector in the middle.
- **Recruiting crossover.** TA teams run it on candidate intake calls — same workflow, different ICP.

## Pricing reality

Per user per month, checked 2026-08-31 at fathom.ai/pricing.

- **Free** — $0. Unlimited recording, transcription, summaries, clips, and search. No CRM sync.
- **Premium** — $20 monthly, $16 annual. Advanced summaries with 15+ templates; Ask Fathom restricted to one call at a time. Single seat, no minimum.
- **Team** — $19 monthly, $15 annual, 2-seat minimum. CRM sync, and Ask Fathom unlimited across your own calls.
- **Business** — $34 monthly, $25 annual. Adds CRM field sync, which maps extracted insights into named CRM fields, plus custom summary templates.
- **Enterprise** — quote-only. SSO, Okta SCIM provisioning, custom data retention, dedicated success manager, priority support SLAs.

Two things the price list does not say out loud. **Team costs less per seat than Premium** — $19 against $20 monthly, $15 against $16 annual — and it unlocks CRM sync as well, so any pair of users belongs on Team. A solo operator is the only reader for whom Premium is the right line item.

The second is the real budget trap. CRM sync on Team and Business is capped at **3 users per domain**. A 20-rep team that wants pipeline writeback for everyone is not paying $25 a seat; it is on an Enterprise quote. Price the quote before you model the rollout.

## Best for

RevOps and sales leaders at 5-50 rep teams who want call summaries attached to the opportunity record and are not buying deal scoring. Also TA teams capturing structured intake notes without signing an interview-intelligence contract.

**Do not use it if** you need coaching workflows, scorecards, or deal-risk scoring. Fathom has no revenue-intelligence layer, and no tier upgrade adds one. Same answer for compliance-heavy orgs that need SSO and enforced retention: those sit behind the quote-only tier, so the entry price stops being the price.

## Versus the alternatives

- **[Gong](/en/tools/gong/)** — Fathom records and summarizes; Gong scores. Pick Gong when a VP Sales owns the budget and wants deal inspection, forecast risk, and rep coaching, and can defend a five-figure annual contract against Fathom's $180-300 per seat per year.
- **Otter.ai and Fireflies** — the two highest-volume notetakers by seat count. Otter has repositioned as an org-wide knowledge layer rather than a per-user notetaker; Fireflies meters AI usage against a monthly credit ceiling and tiers storage. Pick Fireflies when you want cheaper seats and can live with the credit ceiling; pick Fathom when you want per-seat cost flat and AI usage uncapped inside your own calls.
- **[Granola](/en/tools/granola/)** — the fastest-growing entrant, built around augmenting the notes you type yourself, with no bot in the room. Pick Granola when the meetings are internal and a recording bot is socially awkward. Pick Fathom when the calls are external, need CRM writeback, and someone will ask for the recording later. Fathom's bot-free capture narrows that gap without closing it: Granola is note-first, Fathom is recording-first.

## Watch-outs

- **The 3-user-per-domain CRM sync cap on Team and Business.** Guard: count the seats that genuinely need writeback before signing. Above 3, get the Enterprise quote up front instead of finding the ceiling in month two.
- **No revenue intelligence, at any tier.** Guard: if a forecast-accuracy or coaching mandate is anywhere in next year's plan, treat Fathom as the interim tool and keep the export path open — the public API and MCP server make the call history portable to [Gong](/en/tools/gong/) or [Chorus](/en/tools/chorus/) later.
- **Bottom-up adoption outruns recording policy.** Guard: reps switching on a notetaker with no policy behind it is how two-party-consent states turn into a legal problem. Write the consent and retention rule before the free tier spreads, not after.
- **Admin controls below Enterprise are thin.** SSO and SCIM exist only on the quote-only tier. Guard: if IT mandates SSO, model the Enterprise quote, not the $25 annual Business rate.
