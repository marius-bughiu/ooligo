## What it is

Recruitee is the modern ATS most commonly chosen by European mid-market companies — Amsterdam-headquartered, GDPR-native, and built around a clean Kanban-style hiring board UX. Owned by Tellent (the parent that also owns KiwiHR and Javelo) since 2022. Serves 6,000+ companies across 90+ countries with strongest concentration in EMEA.

## Why it shows up in Recruiting stacks

- **GDPR-native by design.** Built in the EU under the GDPR regime; data residency, candidate consent flows, and right-to-be-forgotten workflows are first-class — not retrofitted onto a US-built product.
- **Kanban-first UX.** The hiring funnel renders as drag-and-drop cards across pipeline stages. Smaller learning curve for hiring managers who don't live in the ATS daily.
- **Multi-language candidate-facing surface.** Career site and application flow localize across 20+ languages; useful for organizations hiring across European jurisdictions.

## Pricing

- **Launch** — $224/month, up to 10 active jobs
- **Scale** — $359/month, unlimited jobs, advanced reporting
- **Lead** — $479/month, custom workflows, advanced integrations
- All plans include unlimited users; per-active-job model contrasts with per-employee pricing of [iCIMS](/en/tools/icims/) or [SmartRecruiters](/en/tools/smartrecruiters/)

## Best for

- European-headquartered SMB and mid-market companies (50-1,000 employees)
- Multi-country hiring teams across EMEA where GDPR compliance is a procurement gate
- Hiring-manager-led organizations where the hiring manager (not the recruiter) is the daily ATS user

## Watch-outs

- Less brand recognition in US than [Greenhouse](/en/tools/greenhouse/) or [Lever](/en/tools/lever/); American buyers default elsewhere
- AI features lag specialist tools — pair with [juicebox](/en/tools/juicebox/) or [hireEZ](/en/tools/hireez/) for sourcing
- For high-volume hourly hiring, [Fountain](/en/tools/fountain/) or [Workable](/en/tools/workable/) is a better fit
- Outgrown by enterprise scale (1,500+ employees, multi-business-unit complexity); migration to [SmartRecruiters](/en/tools/smartrecruiters/) or [iCIMS](/en/tools/icims/) typical at that point
