## What it is

Reachdesk is a B2B gifting and direct-mail platform. Reps, marketers and HR teams send physical gifts, eGifts, branded swag and event kits from [Salesforce](/en/tools/salesforce/), [HubSpot](/en/tools/hubspot/), [Marketo](/en/tools/adobe-marketo-engage/), [Outreach](/en/tools/outreach/) or [Salesloft](/en/tools/salesloft/), and Reachdesk sources, stores, packs and ships them. It runs its own fulfillment centers in the US, Canada, the UK, Ireland (for EU sends) and Australia, over 1.1 million square feet in total, and delivers to more than 180 countries. The company has offices in London, New York and Lisbon, raised a $6 million Series A in 2020 and a $43 million Series B in September 2021 (Highland Europe leading, HubSpot Ventures participating, per TechCrunch), and holds a SOC 2 Type 2 report. We found no acquisition, shutdown or wind-down in the past 12 months.

The question this page answers: **Reachdesk or [Sendoso](/en/tools/sendoso/) for a global ABM gifting program, and how do their billing models differ?** If a large share of your sends land outside the US, Reachdesk is the better default. If you are US-only and high-volume, the two are close enough that the quote decides it.

## Why it shows up in RevOps stacks

- **Native warehouses outside the US.** The Ireland site ships EU sends from inside the EU, so they skip UK–EU customs, and Canada and Australia are served locally. Reachdesk quotes 1–5 days for domestic sends with a 3-day SLA on standard sends, and 3–10 days inside the EU (3–5 express). For a European or APAC account list, that is the difference between a gift arriving before the meeting or after it.
- **Sends trigger where the pipeline lives.** Salesforce and Marketo log sends at campaign level, so a gift shows up in campaign influence. HubSpot, Outreach, Salesloft, [Gong](/en/tools/gong/) and [Slack](/en/tools/slack/) log to tasks or notes. [Chili Piper](/en/tools/chili-piper/) and [Calendly](/en/tools/calendly/) can fire a gift when a meeting is booked, and [6sense](/en/tools/6sense/) and [Demandbase](/en/tools/demandbase/) are listed as ABM integrations. HR systems ([BambooHR](/en/tools/bamboohr/), [Workday](/en/tools/workday/), [HiBob](/en/tools/hibob/)) cover employee gifting from the same account.
- **eGifts bill on redemption.** Reachdesk charges an eGift only when the recipient claims it, not when it is sent or opened. Standard packaging and handwritten notes are included. Its catalog lists 8,500+ gift and eGift options plus direct Amazon sends in the US, UK and EU.
- **Agent sending with a human in the loop.** The Reachdesk MCP server works from [Claude](/en/tools/claude/) and [ChatGPT](/en/tools/chatgpt/) (the two clients Reachdesk says it tested), keeps existing role permissions, and requires a preview and human confirmation before every send. Gifty, the in-app assistant, suggests gifts by role, location, budget and goal and drafts notes in the recipient's language.

## Pricing reality

Reachdesk publishes a floor, not a price list (checked 2026-11-23). Plans start at $20,000 a year:

- **Core**: 5 users, 1 gifting territory (Americas, EMEA or APAC), 1 fulfillment center, sized for about 50 gifts a month.
- **Plus**: 10 users, 2 territories, 2 fulfillment centers, event fulfillment, about 100 gifts a month.
- **Premium**: 15 users, 3 territories, 3+ fulfillment centers, creative services included.

Every plan includes unlimited integrations, a dedicated CSM, an onboarding manager and a solutions engineer. Gifts, eGifts and swag are billed separately from the platform fee, a pick-and-pack fee applies to every physical send, and swag sourcing has a $2,500 minimum order (print-on-demand has none).

The per-send answer: at Core's floor and its 50-gifts-a-month sizing, 600 sends a year carry about $33 of platform fee each before the gift, postage and pick-and-pack. Double the volume on the same contract and that falls to about $17. Like Sendoso, Reachdesk only gets cheap at volume.

## Best for

ABM and field-marketing leaders at B2B companies selling into Europe, the UK, Canada or Australia, running at least 50 physical sends a month against a named account list, who need sends tied to Salesforce or Marketo campaigns for attribution.

**Not for you** if you send fewer than about 30 gifts a month, ship almost entirely inside the US and already have a Sendoso quote you like, or cannot commit $20,000 a year before the first gift ships.

## Versus the alternatives

- **[Sendoso](/en/tools/sendoso/)**: the market-share leader and category consolidator (Alyce, Postal, Merch). Its median platform contract is about $25,500 a year per Vendr, close to Reachdesk's floor. Pick it for US-heavy programs and when you want SmartSend's gift picks from Gong calls. Reachdesk's own comparison says Sendoso bills eGifts on open rather than redemption and charges extra for handwritten notes; treat that as a competitor's claim and confirm it in the order form.
- **Goody**: the fastest-growing entrant. Unlimited gifts to the US, Canada and the UK are free and Pro is $20 per user per month billed yearly. Pick it for low-volume sales gifting and employee programs where a five-figure platform fee makes no sense.
- **DIY**: a corporate card, Amazon Business and a spreadsheet. Pick it under about 20 sends a month; you give up warehousing and attribution.

The direct answer: if more than a third of your sends cross a border, start with Reachdesk. Otherwise get both quotes at the same 12-month send plan and compare the fully loaded cost.

## Watch-outs

- **The ROI guarantee has terms.** Reachdesk promises 5x pipeline ROI in the first year or a refund, with "terms and conditions apply" on every page that states it. *Guard:* get the definition of attributed pipeline, the measurement window and the refund mechanics written into the order form before you sign.
- **The floor is not the bill.** Gift spend, pick-and-pack, premium wrapping, event upgrades and storefront add-ons all sit outside the $20,000. *Guard:* ask for a fully loaded quote built on your actual send mix by region, and cap pick-and-pack and storage rates in the contract.
- **Territories are plan-gated.** Core covers one region and one warehouse, so an EMEA team on Core cannot ship from the US site. *Guard:* map next year's account list by country before choosing a plan; upgrading mid-term is a renegotiation.
- **Agents can spend your budget.** MCP and Gifty move sending into AI clients. *Guard:* keep the per-send human confirmation on, set user-level budget limits, and review the send log weekly for the first quarter.
