## What it is

HackerEarth is a technical-assessment platform that sells two things on separate price cards: Skill Assessments, a coding-test product with FaceCode live interviews bundled in, and OnScreen, an AI interviewer launched in April 2026 that runs first-round technical interviews with a video avatar at any hour. The company is independent, backed by DHI Group (the owner of Dice), and claims a developer community of more than 10 million and coverage of 1,000+ skills across 40+ programming languages.

OnScreen is the reason HackerEarth matters again in 2026. Every session starts with government-ID verification, a face match against that ID, and a liveness check. Proctoring runs during the interview rather than as a post-hoc review, and flags proxy attempts, tab switching, and identity mismatches. Scoring is rubric-based with automated code execution, and answers the model is unsure about are routed to a human reviewer instead of being scored down. HackerEarth scopes it to first-round interviews for individual-contributor engineers. Senior, system-design, and whiteboard rounds stay with people.

## Why it shows up in Recruiting stacks

- **It answers the 24/7 first-round question directly.** A candidate who applies at 11 p.m. on a Friday can finish a structured technical interview before Monday. HackerEarth cites one enterprise customer that screened more than 2,000 candidates in a weekend. That is a vendor claim, but the capacity logic holds: the bottleneck in a high-volume funnel is engineer calendar time, and OnScreen removes it from round one.
- **Identity checks are built in, not bolted on.** Proxy interviewing and deepfake candidates are the fraud pattern teams now plan for (see [AI interview fraud](/en/learn/ai-interview-fraud/)). Running KYC-style ID, face match, and liveness inside the same session as the interview closes the gap a separate identity vendor leaves open.
- **It has the lowest published per-candidate price among the major platforms.** Both assessment tiers and both OnScreen tiers sit on the public pricing page, with a 30-day trial, so a first technical hire does not wait on a sales cycle.

## Pricing

Verified against [hackerearth.com/recruit/pricing](https://www.hackerearth.com/recruit/pricing) on 2026-10-25.

**Skill Assessments**

- **Growth — $990/year billed annually** (120 credits, about $83/month) or **$99/month billed monthly** (10 credits/month). One admin, a 5,000+ question library, FaceCode, plagiarism detection, full-stack and GenAI skills, and psychometric tests.
- **Scale — $3,990/year billed annually** (300 credits, about $333/month) or **$399/month billed monthly** (25 credits/month). Unlimited admins, a 20,000+ question library, video responses, AI-based plagiarism checks, ATS integrations, and calendar integrations.
- **Enterprise — custom.** Adds custom credits, the full 25,000+ question library, global candidate benchmarking, API access, roles and permissions, and SSO.

**OnScreen (AI interviewer)**

- **Growth — $1,290/year** (120 interview invites) or **$129/month** (10 invites/month).
- **Scale — $4,990/year** (600 invites) or **$499/month** (50 invites/month). Adds custom roles, realtime code evaluation, premium support, and SSO.
- **Enterprise — custom.** Adds custom question development, a dedicated account manager, and SLAs.

**The per-candidate math.** Growth works out to $8.25 a credit, below [Codility](/en/tools/codility/)'s $10 and well below [HackerRank](/en/tools/hackerrank/) Starter's $15.80. Scale is $13.30 a credit, and at $3,990 it is the cheapest published tier in this group that carries ATS integrations: HackerRank Pro costs $5,028 and [CodeSignal](/en/tools/codesignal/) Grow costs $5,748. OnScreen Scale comes to $8.32 per AI interview, against $9.98 on monthly billing.

## Best for

- Talent acquisition leads at companies hiring 20–50 engineers a year who want ATS write-back without an enterprise quote, using Scale
- High-volume or campus hiring teams whose first technical round is gated by interviewer availability across time zones, using OnScreen
- Teams that have seen proxy candidates and want identity verification inside the interview itself

## Alternatives and when to pick them

- **[HackerRank](/en/tools/hackerrank/)** — the category leader by customer count. Pick it when your process assumes candidates use AI and you want to score how they direct it: its agentic IDE and AI Fluency score have no HackerEarth equivalent.
- **[CodeSignal](/en/tools/codesignal/)** — the other major platform. Pick it when you need AI interviewers for product, design, and go-to-market roles as well as engineering; OnScreen covers engineering only.
- **[Karat](/en/tools/karat/)** — pick it when you want human interviewers on the first round instead of an avatar, and the budget to pay per interview for them.
- **[Codility](/en/tools/codility/)** — pick it when you need MCP task authoring below enterprise pricing; HackerEarth has no MCP server, and its API sits on Enterprise.

## Watch-outs

- **Growth has one admin seat.** A hiring panel of five engineers cannot each open reports on their own login. Guard: if more than one person reviews submissions, budget for Scale's unlimited admins from the start rather than discovering the cap in week two.
- **OnScreen's evaluation model is not disclosed.** HackerEarth's own materials say the model architecture and training data are not public, which matters under NYC LL 144 and the EU AI Act if the score informs a hiring decision. Guard: request a bias audit and the rubric for each role before rollout, keep a human reviewer on every reject, and pair OnScreen with [structured interviews](/en/learn/structured-interviewing/) in later rounds.
- **Assessments and OnScreen bill separately.** Running both on Scale costs $8,980 a year. Guard: price the combined line before comparing against a single-product quote from a competitor.
- **Credit consumption rules are not spelled out on the pricing page.** Guard: confirm in writing whether an invite a candidate never opens consumes a credit, and what the per-credit overage rate is, before choosing annual billing.
- **AI-assistant detection is less documented than identity checks.** HackerEarth's 2026 materials mark the scope of Smart Browser's AI-assistance detection as pending confirmation. Guard: run a pilot cohort with a known AI-assisted test-taker and check what the integrity report actually flags.
