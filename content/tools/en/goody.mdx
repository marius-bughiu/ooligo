## What it is

Goody is a gifting platform where the recipient does the work. You pick a gift, or a collection to choose from, and send a link by email, text or custom URL. The recipient accepts it, enters their own shipping address and size, and can swap it for anything of equal or lesser value. No address list, no warehouse, no kitting. Goody was founded in 2020, raised about $32 million (a $13.1 million Series A led by NEA, then a $15 million round from SoftBank's Latin America Fund in November 2021) and now claims 25,000+ companies. We found no acquisition, shutdown or wind-down in the past 12 months.

The question this page answers: **do we need [Sendoso](/en/tools/sendoso/) or [Reachdesk](/en/tools/reachdesk/) at all, or does Goody's free tier cover low-volume sales gifting?** For most teams sending under about 50 gifts a month inside the US, Canada and the UK, Goody's free plan covers it, and the platform fee you skip is the whole saving.

## Why it shows up in RevOps stacks

- **You pay for accepted gifts, not sent ones.** If you pay by card, Goody charges only after the recipient accepts, so a gift that expires unopened costs nothing. If you prefunded a Balance, the value goes back to it on expiry, and unused value from a cheaper swap returns too. For cold outbound, where many gifts are never claimed, that changes the math more than any discount.
- **No address collection.** Because the recipient types their own address, a rep can send to a prospect they only have an email for. That is the step that kills most physical-gift outbound programs.
- **Gifting from an AI client, with a spend cap.** Goody's MCP server (announced 25 June 2026) exposes 28 tools: catalog search, card and message drafting, pricing, sending by link, email or direct shipping, contacts and autogifts. [Claude](/en/tools/claude/), [ChatGPT](/en/tools/chatgpt/) and [Cursor](/en/tools/cursor/) connect over OAuth; [n8n](/en/tools/n8n/), [Zapier](/en/tools/zapier/) and Make use a scoped token. A send cannot run until the agent has shown the full price and you have confirmed it, autogifts are created paused, and a rolling daily spend limit sits in account settings. AI Gifting, a chat assistant that picks a gift and drafts the note, has been on every plan, free included, since 8 December 2025.

## Pricing reality

Goody publishes three plans (checked 2026-11-28):

- **Starter, $0**: unlimited gifts within the US, Canada and the UK, 600+ brands, bulk sends to 1,000+ recipients, branded swag with no minimums, scheduling and tracking, Google or Microsoft sign-in. You pay only for the gifts.
- **Pro, $20 per user per month billed annually**: gift cards from hundreds of brands, sends to 140+ countries with customs and duties capped at $55 per gift, [Calendly](/en/tools/calendly/) or [HubSpot](/en/tools/hubspot/) meeting booking, sending from your own Gmail, advanced reporting.
- **Team, priced per company by organization size**: unlimited seats, team budgets, [Salesforce](/en/tools/salesforce/) or HubSpot gifting from the CRM, 200+ HRIS integrations ([Rippling](/en/tools/rippling/), [Gusto](/en/tools/gusto/), [BambooHR](/en/tools/bamboohr/), [Workday](/en/tools/workday/), [ADP](/en/tools/adp/) and others) for birthday and anniversary autogifts, company swag stores, SAML SSO.

What a sales team really pays: five reps on Pro is $1,200 a year plus the gifts. At 300 sends a year that is $4 per send of platform cost, against roughly $83 per send for a $25,000 Sendoso contract at the same volume. Team pricing is quote-only; get the quote before you promise CRM attribution to anyone.

## Best for

SDR and AE managers, and founders doing their own outbound, at companies sending up to about 50 gifts a month, mostly to North America and the UK, who want meeting-booking gifts without a five-figure contract. It also fits People Ops teams running birthday, anniversary and onboarding gifts from an HRIS.

**Not for you** if you run tiered enterprise ABM with stored, kitted inventory shipped from regional warehouses, or if every send must log to a Salesforce campaign and you will not pay for Team.

## Versus the alternatives

- **Sendoso**: the market-share leader, now owning Alyce and Postal. Pick it when you send 100+ physical gifts a month, need stored swag kitted and shipped, and finance wants attribution on every send.
- **Reachdesk**: the European-warehouse option, from $20,000 a year. Pick it when a large share of your accounts sit in the EU or APAC and you need local fulfillment.
- **Snappy**: Goody's closest model in employee gifting, with curated recipient-choice collections. Pick it when the buyer is HR and the program is recognition at scale rather than pipeline.
- **DIY**: a corporate card and Amazon Business. Pick it under about 10 sends a month; you lose recipient-entered addresses and pay-on-accept.

The direct answer: start every new gifting program on Goody Starter. Move to Pro when a rep sends outside North America and the UK, and to Sendoso or Reachdesk only when volume and attribution needs justify the platform fee.

## Watch-outs

- **Budgets and CRM logging sit on the quote-only Team plan.** On Starter and Pro nothing caps a rep's spend except the card. *Guard:* fund sends from a prepaid Balance sized to the month's plan, and request a Team quote before rolling Goody out past one team.
- **Unattended agents can spend money.** The MCP's unattended mode treats the Send permission, your automation and the daily cap as consent. *Guard:* keep humans in the loop for prospect gifts, set the daily spend limit to one week's budget, and review sends weekly.
- **Gift links expire and direct shipping is US-only via MCP.** The standard link window is about 10 weeks, and agent-driven direct shipping covers US addresses only. *Guard:* send by link for anything outside the US, and set a shorter expiry on prepaid gifts so unclaimed value returns to your Balance sooner.
