## What it is

Salesforce Service Cloud is the customer service application built on the same platform as the [Salesforce](/en/tools/salesforce/) CRM. It covers case management, a self-service help center, omnichannel routing, digital messaging, voice through Salesforce Voice (formerly Service Cloud Voice), field service, and the contact-center modules for workforce and quality management. It is also the base that [Salesforce Agentforce](/en/tools/salesforce-agentforce/) runs on for service: the AI agent reads and writes the same Case, Contact and Account records your agents already use.

The question it answers: **we already run Salesforce, so do we put support on the same platform, or buy a standalone helpdesk like [Zendesk](/en/tools/zendesk/) or [Intercom](/en/tools/intercom/)?**

Two 2026 changes reshape that question. On 2026-09-03 Salesforce launched three new editions (Core, Advanced and Max) that bundle Agentforce, Slack and Tableau Next into the seat price. On 2026-09-10 Salesforce closed its acquisition of Fin, formerly Intercom. That means the most common "standalone AI helpdesk" alternative is now a Salesforce product too.

## Why it shows up in Customer Success stacks

**One customer record.** When a case, an open opportunity and a renewal sit on the same Account, the CSM sees the escalation before the QBR. A health score in [Gainsight](/en/tools/gainsight/) can read case data without a sync job. With a separate helpdesk, someone has to own that integration and its field mapping indefinitely.

**The agent inherits your permissions.** Agentforce for Service acts on Case records under the same sharing rules and field-level security as your human agents. Salesforce's hosted MCP servers went GA in April 2026 for Enterprise-tier orgs and above, and calls run as the signed-in user. That is the cleanest way to give [Claude](/en/tools/claude/) or another MCP client governed read access to case history.

**Contact center on the same data.** Max bundles workforce management and quality management. Salesforce Voice runs telephony through [Amazon Connect](/en/tools/amazon-connect/) or partner carriers such as [Five9](/en/tools/five9/) and [Talkdesk](/en/tools/talkdesk/), and a call becomes a case without a CTI bridge.

## Pricing reality

List prices from the Service Cloud pricing page, checked 2026-10-26, per user per month, billed annually:

- **Starter Suite**: $25. The small-business suite shared with Sales Cloud.
- **Pro Suite**: $100. Adds real-time chat and customization.
- **Core**: $195. Case management, self-service help center, Slack Business+.
- **Advanced**: $395. Adds the Casey help agent, Premier Success Plan, full sandbox, Backup & Recover.
- **Max**: $550. Adds the full Agentforce for Service suite, contact-center features (WFM, QM), Tableau Next, and 2.75M Flex Credits per org per year.

Launch coverage (SalesforceBen, 2026-09-03) put Core and Advanced at 500,000 and 1M Flex Credits. The pricing page itself names only Max's allocation, so get the credit count in your quote. Enterprise ($175) and Unlimited ($350) are no longer listed for new buyers, but existing contracts keep legacy pricing.

Customer-facing Agentforce is metered on top of seats. The Agentforce pricing page lists Flex Credits at $500 per 100,000 credits ($0.005 each), and a standard action burns 20 credits (about $0.10). The alternative is $2 per conversation. Employee-facing Agentforce add-ons for Service are $125 per user per month on editions below Max; Max includes them.

What that means for 50 agents at list price: Core runs about $117,000 a year, Advanced $237,000 and Max $330,000. Compare Zendesk Suite Professional plus Copilot at $165 per agent per month, about $99,000 a year. Then add the AI meter. At 10,000 AI-handled conversations a month on per-conversation pricing, that is another $240,000 a year. Implementation partners, telephony usage and Data 360 storage come on top of both.

## Best for

Support and CS operations leaders at companies of roughly 200+ employees that already run Sales Cloud as the system of record. These teams need cases, renewals and account context on one object model, and they have (or will fund) a Salesforce admin. It is the right default when the support team also handles complex B2B escalations, entitlements and field service, not just high-volume chat deflection.

Skip it if you are not on Salesforce CRM. Skip it if you have fewer than about 20 agents and no admin, or if your volume is mostly simple B2C questions where an AI-first helpdesk resolves the majority without case workflows.

## Versus the alternatives

**[Zendesk](/en/tools/zendesk/)** is the largest standalone helpdesk. Pick it when support runs independently of the CRM, when you do not want a Salesforce implementation project, or when total seat cost has to stay under about $170 per agent per month.

**[ServiceNow](/en/tools/servicenow/) Customer Service Management** wins when customer issues routinely become internal IT, engineering or field workflows that already run on ServiceNow.

**Microsoft Dynamics 365 Customer Service** lists Professional at $50 and Enterprise at $105 per user per month; third-party figures for Premium and Contact Center disagree, so quote those directly. Pick it for a Microsoft-standard shop already on Dynamics or Teams. Enterprise costs roughly half of Salesforce Core.

**[Decagon](/en/tools/decagon/) and [Sierra](/en/tools/sierra/)** are the fastest-growing entrants. Each is an AI agent layered over whatever helpdesk you keep. Pick one when AI resolution rate is the buying reason and you want the agent decoupled from your seat contract.

**Fin**, formerly Intercom, is now Salesforce-owned. Salesforce says Fin will keep serving its existing customers, including those on other helpdesks. Buy it on that assurance only with a contractual commitment to non-Salesforce helpdesk support.

## Watch-outs

- **The bundle hides the meter.** Seat editions now include Flex Credits, which makes Agentforce look free until volume arrives. *Guard:* model your monthly AI-handled conversations at $2 each and at about 3 actions times $0.10, then negotiate the cheaper meter and a rate cap into the order form.
- **Admin dependency.** Routing rules, entitlements, flows and permission sets are configuration, not settings, so a misconfigured omnichannel queue strands cases quietly. *Guard:* fund a named Salesforce admin or a partner retainer before go-live, and run a weekly report on cases with no owner older than your SLA.
- **Open CTI is on a clock.** Salesforce's developer docs put Open CTI in maintenance mode, unavailable for new Agentforce Service orgs and scheduled for retirement in February 2028. *Guard:* if your telephony connects through Open CTI, scope the move to Salesforce Voice or a supported partner connector in your next renewal, not in 2027.
- **Renewal pressure toward new editions.** Legacy Enterprise and Unlimited pricing holds for existing contracts, but the new editions carry higher list prices. *Guard:* price your renewal on both legacy and new editions, and sign before Salesforce's January 31 fiscal year-end, when discounting is most available.
