## What it is

Juicebox (branded PeopleGPT) is the AI-native sourcing tool that replaces Boolean search with natural-language queries against a 800M+ candidate database compiled from LinkedIn, GitHub, conference attendee lists, paper authorship, and other public signals. Launched in 2023, Juicebox is the leading example of LLM-native recruiting tooling — built around a generative-AI primitive rather than retrofitting it onto a legacy product.

## Why it shows up in Recruiting stacks

- **Natural-language sourcing.** "Find me senior Python engineers in Berlin who have contributed to open-source ML libraries and worked at fintech startups" produces a ranked candidate list in seconds. The Boolean queries that used to take a senior sourcer 30 minutes to construct are now a single sentence.
- **Multi-source candidate enrichment.** Pulls signal from LinkedIn (where most candidates live) plus GitHub (depth on technical roles), patent databases, conference speaker lists, paper authorship — sources Boolean LinkedIn searches can't access.
- **Built for AI-first recruiting workflows.** Pairs naturally with [Claude](/en/tools/claude/) for outreach personalization and [n8n](/en/tools/n8n/) for ATS sync; the assumption is that the recruiter's stack is itself AI-augmented.

## Pricing

- **Starter** — $79/month, individual recruiter use
- **Pro** — $399/month, team features, ATS integrations, advanced enrichment
- **Enterprise** — custom, SSO, advanced controls, dedicated CSM
- Per-seat economics work for SMB and growth-stage; enterprise teams negotiate volume tiers

## Best for

- Growth-stage tech companies hiring engineers, designers, ML researchers (technical roles where deep signal matters)
- Recruiting teams that have already adopted AI in adjacent workflows ([sourcing](/en/learn/ai-sourcing/), screening, interview)
- Companies replacing LinkedIn Recruiter as their primary sourcing tool to reduce LinkedIn lock-in

## Watch-outs

- Compete with established sourcing players ([hireEZ](/en/tools/hireez/), [Gem](/en/tools/gem/), [Findem](/en/tools/findem/)) — Juicebox's lead is in the natural-language UX; established players have stronger ATS integrations and CRM workflow
- Less effective for roles where candidate signal is sparse online (sales, operations, traditional management roles)
- Quality of database varies by geography — US/UK/EU best; sparser in some APAC and LATAM markets
- Not a replacement for an [ATS](/en/learn/ats-vs-recruiting-crm/) — pair with [Ashby](/en/tools/ashby/), [Greenhouse](/en/tools/greenhouse/), or another ATS for the funnel-tracking layer
