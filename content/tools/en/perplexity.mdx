## What it is

Perplexity is an AI search engine that returns synthesized answers with inline citations, plus a Spaces feature for sustained research projects, a Comet browser, and an Enterprise tier for teams. It's the default tool for ops folks who want grounded answers — not pure model output — when researching companies, regulations, or candidates.

## Why it shows up in ops stacks

- **Grounded by default.** Every answer cites the sources, which matters for ICP research, competitor intel, and any output going to a CRO or GC.
- **Spaces for project research.** Pin sources, run repeated queries, share with the team — better than ChatGPT for ongoing research workstreams.
- **Real-time web.** [ChatGPT](/en/tools/chatgpt/) and [Claude](/en/tools/claude/) have web search, but Perplexity's UX is built for it from the ground up.

## Pricing

- **Free** — basic search, limited Pro features
- **Pro** — $20/mo, advanced models, unlimited Pro searches
- **Enterprise Pro** — $40/user/mo, SSO, admin, data controls
- API access via Perplexity Sonar, usage-based

## Best for

- RevOps building account research dossiers
- Recruiters sourcing candidates and validating market data
- Legal ops scanning regulatory updates and case summaries

## Watch-outs

- Citations look authoritative; still verify before quoting in board decks
- For long-form reasoning and writing, Claude/ChatGPT remain stronger
- Integration catalog is thin — pair with Zapier or Make for distribution
