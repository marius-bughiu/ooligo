## What it is

Personio is a Munich HR platform for European small and mid-sized companies: the employee record, time and absence tracking, payroll, recruiting, performance reviews and compensation in one system. Founded in 2015, it reports more than 16,000 customers covering 1.6 million employees, and its own pricing FAQ targets companies of 10 to 10,000 employees. Its press boilerplate draws the core band tighter, at 25 to 5,000.

2026 changed the company more than the product. Three events matter to a buyer:

- **Profitability.** On April 22, 2026 Personio reported its first profitable quarter, which it says lets it fund product work "independent of external capital cycles". For a buyer signing a multi-year HR contract, that lowers vendor risk.
- **aurio** (announced the same day), a Munich recruiting-AI startup whose agents search for candidates and score incoming applications before a recruiter opens them. The team joined Personio's product and engineering groups. Personio said initial customer launches would follow "later this year"; as of November 5, 2026 the aurio features have not shipped as a named Personio Recruiting release.
- **Circula** (October 6, 2026), a Berlin spend-management platform with more than 3,000 customers, covering expenses, corporate cards, accounts payable and tax-optimised benefits. It now runs as "Circula by Personio" and is still sold standalone to companies that do not use Personio.

Fosway named Personio a Core Leader in its 9-Grid for Cloud HR for the second year running in September 2026.

## Why it shows up in Recruiting stacks

- **Recruiting writes straight into the employee record.** Personio Recruiting is used by 9,000 customers. A hire becomes an employee profile with onboarding, payroll and the first performance cycle attached, with no sync job between an ATS and an HRIS to maintain.
- **European employment law is the default, not a localization.** German labor law, works-council requirements and DATEV handoffs are built in, and the AI layer runs on EU-hosted models in Frankfurt and Dublin with no US data transfer. For a German works council reviewing an AI feature, that is the argument that gets it approved.
- **Payroll is native.** Personio Payroll grew 80% year over year and serves more than 1,500 customers. Circula moves expense and benefit data into the same payroll engine.
- **AI today is assistive, not agentic.** The Personio Assistant answers questions over HR data ("how has headcount changed?"), Recruiting drafts job descriptions and screens CVs, and performance reviews get AI summaries. Specialized agents for analytics and performance are on Personio's own roadmap as "future", not shipped.

## Pricing

- **Published floors exist, on the German site only.** personio.de/plaene lists HR from **€7.60 per employee per month** (Core), an HR-plus-payroll platform from **€14** (priced for Core Pro with the Expert payroll tier, annual billing, excluding a separate tax-advisor fee), and Payroll from **€5**, sold only on top of an HR subscription. The English pricing page shows the same two plans with no figures.
- **Two plans, then Apps.** Core covers one legal entity and caps e-signature workflows at 8% of employees. Core Pro adds unlimited entities and e-signatures, position management, workforce planning, OAuth, Okta and Microsoft Entra ID, and **API access for custom integrations**. Recruiting, Surveys, Performance & Development, Compensation, Whistleblowing, Employer of Record and Premium Support are each separately priced Apps.
- **Real-world band.** Third-party estimates put core HR at €5 to €8 per employee per month and the broader suite at €12 to €20 ([ERP Research](https://erpresearch.com/erp-add-ons/hcm-extensions/personio/pricing)). At 100 employees that is €14,400 to €24,000 a year for the suite. Vendr reports 15 to 30% discounts off the first quote on multi-year or multi-module deals but publishes no median.
- **Fixed terms.** Annual plans carry a 12-month minimum, plus a one-time setup fee that scales with company size.

## Best for

A People Ops or TA lead at a 50- to 2,000-employee company headquartered in DACH or elsewhere in the EU, who wants hiring, the employee record and payroll on one vendor and needs a works council to sign off on the AI. Second fit: a German mid-market finance team that already runs Circula and wants payroll and expenses on one tax logic.

Not for you if most of your headcount is in the US, or if recruiting volume is high enough that the ATS is the primary purchase.

## Versus the alternatives

- **[HiBob](/en/tools/hibob/)** — the closest rival in this size band, also quote-only and sold as core HR plus modules. Pick HiBob when the workforce is spread across the UK, US and Israel rather than concentrated in DACH.
- **[BambooHR](/en/tools/bamboohr/)** — publishes its prices, includes the ATS in the entry tier, and ships an MCP connection. Pick BambooHR for a US-based workforce; see [Personio vs BambooHR](/en/vs/personio-vs-bamboohr/).
- **[Rippling](/en/tools/rippling/)** — the fastest-growing all-in-one, with IT, device and spend management in the same record. Pick Rippling when you employ across many countries and want EOR, payroll and IT under one admin.
- **Specialist ATS on top** — [Teamtailor](/en/tools/teamtailor/), [Recruitee](/en/tools/recruitee/), [Ashby](/en/tools/ashby/) or [Greenhouse](/en/tools/greenhouse/). Pick one when you hire more than 100 people a year and need structured interviews, sourcing CRM and pipeline analytics deeper than Personio Recruiting offers.
- **[Workday](/en/tools/workday/)** — the move above roughly 5,000 employees or when HR spans jurisdictions outside Europe.

## Watch-outs

- **The aurio agents are promised, not shipped.** Guard: price Personio Recruiting on what runs today, and if agentic sourcing is the reason to buy, pilot [Juicebox](/en/tools/juicebox/) or [hireEZ](/en/tools/hireez/) alongside it until aurio ships.
- **Recruiting AI is high-risk under the EU AI Act.** CV screening falls in the Act's high-risk employment category, and NYC Local Law 144 requires a bias audit for US hires in New York City. Guard: get Personio's risk classification and human-oversight documentation for each Recruiting AI feature before switching it on; see [NYC Local Law 144](/en/learn/nyc-local-law-144/).
- **API access is a Core Pro feature.** Core customers cannot build custom integrations. Guard: if any system needs Personio data, scope Core Pro from the start; the plan is priced per employee, so upgrading later reprices the whole headcount.
- **No official MCP server.** Third-party connectors from Knit, Apideck and Truto, plus community servers, wrap the v2 API. Guard: run agents through a connector you control and scope the API credential to the read endpoints they need.
- **App-by-App quoting hides the year-two bill.** Guard: put every App you expect to use in year two on the first order form, with a renewal cap.
- **Two acquisitions in one year means integration work.** Guard: for Circula, ask which DATEV and payroll flows are live today versus planned before you consolidate vendors.
