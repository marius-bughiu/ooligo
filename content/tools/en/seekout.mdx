## What it is

SeekOut is a talent-search and sourcing platform built on a very large index of public candidate profiles — SeekOut cites 1B+ across seven talent verticals (public profiles, GitHub, academic, healthcare, nursing, your ATS, internal talent), enriched with GitHub activity, academic papers, patents, and clearance/work-authorization data. Six named agents (Rubric Creator, Candidate Finder, Profile Analyst, Outreach Author, Market Researcher, Engagement Tracker) sit on that layer and build a hiring rubric, search, score fit, and draft outreach. The change that matters most for evaluating it: since **June 2026 SeekOut sells self-serve**. The Recruit Core plan publishes $149/mo for three seats, ending years of enterprise-only, call-sales pricing. Closest category neighbors are [hireEZ](/tools/hireez) on sourcing depth and [Eightfold](/tools/eightfold) on the talent-intelligence framing.

## Why it shows up in Recruiting/TA stacks

- **Depth on technical and hard-to-fill roles.** The GitHub Coder Score, patent and paper coverage, and skills enrichment surface engineers, researchers, and security/clearance candidates that title-keyword search in an ATS or [LinkedIn Recruiter](/en/tools/linkedin-recruiter/) misses. This is the scoped use case where SeekOut earns its price: a team filling recurring senior-technical or specialized US reqs that a sourcer can't fill from LinkedIn alone.
- **An entry price that skips procurement.** Recruit Core ships the full index, Smart Match search, workspaces, verified contacts, and outreach on a credit card — 14-day trial, no card to start, no implementation fee, cancel any time. A two-person team can test it against real reqs this week instead of next quarter.
- **SeekOut MCP.** SeekOut runs an MCP server that puts candidate search inside Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or Copilot — 14 guided workflows across the seven verticals, wired to your existing license, workspaces, power filters, and credits, so work done in the assistant lands in Recruit and the reverse. Rare among sourcing tools and the reason `mcp_available` is true here.
- **Sam covers the inbound side.** SeekOut Sam runs structured, rubric-based screening asynchronously and returns side-by-side comparable results. It is the answer to SeekOut's own "44% of great hires are already in your ATS" framing, and it ships on the Full Recruiting Funnel tier or as an AI Inbound Screening add-on lower down.
- **Spot sells the outcome, not the seat.** Spot pairs the agents with human recruiters to deliver an interview-ready slate — dossiers, scorecards, screening recordings — in roughly 14 days, priced per role or contractor with no annual commit, positioned at ~70% below a contingency agency.

## Pricing

Two published numbers, then quotes.

- **Recruit Core** — $149/mo paid annually ($1,788/yr upfront), or $179/mo billed monthly. Three seats included, 500 contact credits/mo, 1,000 exports/mo, 14-day free trial with no credit card.
- **Recruit Sourcing** — custom, billed annually, per-seat and adjusted with volume. 750 contacts and 5,000 exports per seat per month, custom power filters, advanced diversity filters, SSO, dedicated customer success.
- **Sourcing + Integration** and **Full Recruiting Funnel** — custom, billed annually. The first adds ATS rediscovery, one search across external and ATS profiles, market intelligence, and recruiter benchmarks; the second adds inbound evaluation, AI screening, agentic workflows, benchmark dashboards, and hands-on implementation.
- **Spot** — custom per engagement across three outcome-aligned models, separate from platform seats.

For the custom tiers there is still no list price. Third-party trackers and buyer reports put real deals in a wide $10–30K/year-per-seat band with a median annual contract around $20K; multi-year or prepaid commits pull the per-seat figure toward $4.5–7K, and implementation runs another $2–10K. Treat that band as triangulation from resellers and buyers, not vendor-published, and cap the 5–7% annual escalator at signing. Healthcare search and AI Inbound Screening are sold as add-ons on the lower tiers.

## Best for

Two segments now, which was not true a year ago. **Solo recruiters and one-to-three-person teams** filling technical or specialized reqs can start on Core and pay $1,788/yr for the same index enterprise buyers get, with no procurement cycle. **Enterprise and scale-up TA teams** (Series C through Fortune 500) filling recurring **technical, engineering, security-clearance, or diversity-target roles in the US/Canada** still buy the custom tiers — that is the segment where a dedicated sourcing function already exists and the bottleneck is candidate discovery rather than pipeline management.

**Do not buy SeekOut if** your hiring is mostly non-technical, high-volume, or international outside North America — the data thins out fast in EMEA and APAC — or if you want one tool to run reqs end to end. SeekOut sources and screens; it is not an ATS, and it assumes you have one.

## Versus the alternatives

- **[hireEZ](/tools/hireez)** — hireEZ publishes $494/mo for its solo plan and prices enterprise against the stack it replaces rather than per seat. On published entry pricing SeekOut Core is now well under a third of that, and for three seats instead of one. Pick hireEZ anyway when pipelines are global — APAC and Asian-language sourcing is where its data runs deeper — or when you want it to absorb CRM, scheduling, and analytics too. SeekOut wins back on US technical depth, clearance data, and Spot.
- **[Gem](/tools/gem)** — pick Gem when the constraint is the outreach-and-CRM motion (sequences, source analytics, ATS-synced pipeline) rather than raw discovery. Many teams run Gem as the system of action and reach for SeekOut only when a req is genuinely hard to source.
- **[Eightfold](/tools/eightfold)** — pick it when the mandate is enterprise talent intelligence and internal mobility on top of Workday/SAP, not external sourcing. Eightfold is the platform play; SeekOut is the sharper sourcing tool.
- **[Juicebox](/tools/juicebox)** — the fast-growing lightweight entrant. Juicebox Starter is $99 per seat/mo for one seat and 500 contacts; SeekOut Core is $149/mo for three seats and the same 500 contacts. The price argument that used to favor Juicebox now only holds for a genuine solo recruiter. Pick Juicebox when natural-language candidate search is the whole job; you give up the clearance data, the agents, and the managed service.

If none of these fit and you fill a handful of reqs a quarter, LinkedIn Recruiter plus a contingency agency on the hard roles remains the honest answer — but note that the older version of that argument rested on SeekOut having no entry price, and that is no longer the case.

## Watch-outs

- **International coverage drops off a cliff.** Reviews consistently flag SeekOut as US/Canada-oriented. Guard: run live test searches on your actual hard reqs in your real geographies during the 14-day trial and compare hit rates against hireEZ — don't trust the global profile count, trust the searches you ran. Since June 2026 that test costs nothing, which was not true before.
- **The index figure is the vendor's own.** SeekOut cites 1B+ profiles; independent reviews have put the indexed number closer to 780M. Guard: size the tool by results on your reqs, not by the headline count.
- **On Core, credits bind before seats do.** 500 contact credits and 1,000 exports a month shared across three seats is about 167 contacts per sourcer per month. Guard: measure your contacts-per-hire before committing annually — if a sourcer burns 300/mo you are already on Sourcing-tier economics (750 contacts per seat) and should price that rather than stack overages.
- **Pricing climbs and escalators compound on the custom tiers.** Modules, seats, and the 5–7% annual bump add up. Guard: cap the escalator at signing, right-size seat count to your actual sourcers (it's per seat, not per recruiter), and revisit the Spot-vs-platform split annually — if you're only filling a few hard reqs, the managed service may beat paying for idle seats.
- **It's sourcing, not an ATS.** Guard: keep your system of record ([Greenhouse](/tools/greenhouse), [Lever](/tools/lever), Workday) as the source of truth and confirm the two-way sync covers stage updates before you rely on SeekOut for pipeline reporting.
