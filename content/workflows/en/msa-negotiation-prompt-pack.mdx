A vendor cuts its nonrenewal notice from 90 calendar days to 60. The buyer asked for 30. Who can offer 45? In this exercise that authority belongs to commercial counsel Priya Shah, and her approval is still pending. Use the eight prompts to prepare the next draft without giving away that fallback.

## Start with the internal record

In fictional matter **JH-042**, Juniper Harbor is buying a SaaS subscription from Alder Relay. Procurement requester Maya Chen supplies the proposed start and first renewal dates, December 1, 2026 and December 1, 2027. October 9 is the requested internal review date. The [legal intake guide](/en/learn/legal-intake/) shows why the request went back to Maya before it reached Priya.

Open the [case record](/artifacts/msa-negotiation-prompt-pack/examples/case-record.md) beside the [eight prompts](/artifacts/msa-negotiation-prompt-pack/msa-negotiation-prompt-pack.md). The pack contains English Markdown files. You can follow the entire example without a model account. To use a prompt on an approved matter, copy the common instruction and one prompt, then supply the relevant versions and your own counsel-approved rules. Keep the internal playbook separate from anything intended for the vendor.

## Work two rounds of the renewal clause

| Step | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| First pass, R1 and R2 | Vendor V1 §7.1 asks for at least **90 calendar days** of notice. Rule REN-01 supports the buyer's **30-day** replacement. The excerpt lacks a delivery method. Request the full notices section and order form before calling it missing from the agreement. |
| Counteroffer, C1 and C2 | Buyer B1 asks for 30. Vendor V2 offers **60 calendar days** and email delivery. Keep the email sentence subject to verifying the address. Return the notice period to Priya for review. |
| Fallback and trade, F1 and F2 | The buyer can propose paying undisputed invoices within **20 days** if the notice period stays at **30 days**. The vendor offered to discuss shorter notice, without agreeing to 30. Rule PAY-01 does not authorize combining 20-day payment with a 45-day notice. |
| Compare versions, P1 and P2 | Cite the actual notice and payment clauses in each version. Record what moved, what the vendor expressly said and which question remains. Its motives are unknown. |

Read the [worked responses for all eight prompts](/artifacts/msa-negotiation-prompt-pack/examples/review-notes.md). The final entry holds the 45-day exception for Priya, leaves the 60-day counter unaccepted and assigns Maya to obtain the order form. No third-round response or signature is part of the example. These are authored drafting notes, so they give you an answer to check without implying that a particular model produced it.

## Use the result in a real review

Choose the draft, playbook and reviewer your team authorizes for the matter. Verify each quoted clause, internal rule and approval against those sources. A gap in an excerpt is a reason to ask for the rest of the documents. It is not enough to declare a clause missing.

The pack returns proposed text. A reviewer moves approved wording into a separate document and controls what leaves the team.
