Sales readiness breaks into four jobs: **record** what reps say to buyers, **score** it against a standard, **rehearse** the parts that score badly, and **coach** the rep from the gap to the next attempt. The four products on this page each sell at least three of those jobs. [Gong](/en/tools/gong/) records, scores, and through Gong Enable now rehearses. [Attention](/en/tools/attention/) records, scores and writes the result into the CRM. [Sybill](/en/tools/sybill/) records and fills CRM fields. [Hyperbound](/en/tools/hyperbound/) rehearses, and its Perform license scores real calls.

Buy all four and you pay for three recorders and three scoring engines, put two or three bots on every call, and end up with scorecards that disagree about the same rep. This stack is a rule for choosing: **one recorder of record, one scorecard, one rehearsal surface, and a manager who owns the loop.**

## The overlap map

| Job | Gong | Attention | Sybill | Hyperbound |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Record real calls | Yes, core | Yes, core | Yes, core | No; Perform scores calls it ingests |
| Score real calls | Yes, scorecards | Yes, scorecards | Light: summaries, autofill | Yes, Perform license |
| Write results to CRM | Yes | Yes, agentic write-back | Yes, Autofill capped at 10 fields on Business | Lists Salesforce, HubSpot |
| AI roleplay | Gong Enable (AI Trainer; Dry Run announced 24 June 2026 for July) | No | No | Yes, core (Practice) |
| Agent / MCP access | MCP, read-only | MCP, 68 tools with a write scope | First-party MCP on Business | No MCP |

Read across a row and the waste becomes visible. The "record real calls" row has four sellers and you need one.

## How the pieces fit

- **The recorder of record is a swappable slot, chosen by headcount and by what the output is for.** [Gong](/en/tools/gong/) is the pick at 40 or more quota-carrying reps where coaching is a funded program with an owner. It is quote-only, runs about $1,400-$2,000 per seat per year plus a mandatory platform fee reported between $5,000 and $50,000, and has a practical floor of 15-25 seats. [Attention](/en/tools/attention/) is the pick when the deliverable is the follow-up sent and the CRM field written, not the dashboard; it is quote-only, and our estimate is $70-$140 per seat per month. [Sybill](/en/tools/sybill/) is the pick under about 15 reps, because it publishes its price: Business at $90 per user per month annual, with no seat minimum and no platform fee.

- **[Hyperbound](/en/tools/hyperbound/) is the rehearsal surface.** It turns an ICP description into an AI buyer a rep can call, scores the call against your scorecard, and supports multiparty roleplays in more than 25 languages. The free tier carries 45 prebuilt roleplays and example scorecards. Practice and Perform are separate per-user licenses, and both are quote-only; our estimate puts Practice at $30-$60 per seat per month and the two together at $85-$140, which is Gong money.

- **The scorecard lives in one system, and the other systems read from it.** This is the decision teams skip. If Gong scores discovery calls on a MEDDPICC-shaped card and Hyperbound scores roleplays on a card someone in enablement built separately, a rep can pass practice and fail the real call on criteria nobody reconciled. Write the scorecard once, name its owner, and copy it verbatim into the rehearsal tool.

- **The coach is a person with a weekly slot.** Gong's AI coaching, Attention's agents and Hyperbound's Kota assistant all produce coaching suggestions. None of them assigns the next roleplay and checks it was done, unless a manager configures that and reads the completion list.

### Named handoffs

1. **Call ends → recorder scores it → gap logged against the rep.** The recorder of record scores every recorded customer call on the single scorecard. The lowest-scoring criterion per rep per week is the gap.
2. **Gap logged → roleplay assigned → due date set.** The manager assigns a Hyperbound roleplay built for that criterion, using Manager Assignments, with a date. A gap with no date is a report, not a coaching loop.
3. **Roleplay passed → certification recorded → next live call re-scored.** The rep passes the roleplay at the threshold. The recorder scores the next three live calls on the same criterion. If the live score does not move, the roleplay is testing something the buyer does not do; rewrite the persona from real call transcripts.
4. **Score written → CRM field → pipeline review.** Whichever recorder you chose writes the score or the risk flag to the opportunity. The manager reviews roleplay completion in the weekly forecast call, not in a quarterly enablement review.
5. **Agent query → one source.** If deal reviews happen inside [Claude](/en/tools/claude/), connect the MCP server of the recorder of record only. Two connected recorders give the assistant two transcripts of the same call and two answers.

## The seats that duplicate each other

- **Hyperbound Perform on top of Gong or Attention.** Perform scores real calls. So does the recorder you already pay for. Buy Practice alone and let the recorder score live calls.
- **Gong Enable on top of Hyperbound Practice.** Gong's AI Trainer runs on Enable, and Dry Run builds a roleplay from the account context of an upcoming meeting. If you buy Enable, you have a rehearsal surface; a second one splits practice history across two systems.
- **Sybill alongside Gong or Attention.** Two recorders means two bots on the call, two consent notices, two retention policies and two transcripts that drift. Sybill is a recorder choice, not an add-on to another recorder.
- **Attention alongside Gong.** The agentic write-back is the argument for Attention. If Gong's scorecards and forecasting are why you bought it, Attention duplicates the recording and the scoring to add one capability. Pick on that capability.

## Cost reality

Annual cost for 30 reps. Gong's platform fee is assumed at $5,000-$15,000 for this size; Attention and Hyperbound ranges are our estimates, because neither vendor publishes a price.

| Configuration | Annual | Per rep per year |
|---|---|---|
| A. Gong + Hyperbound Practice | $57,800-$96,600 | $1,930-$3,220 |
| B. Attention + Hyperbound Practice | $36,000-$72,000 | $1,200-$2,400 |
| C. Sybill Business + Hyperbound Practice | $43,200-$54,000 | $1,440-$1,800 |
| All four, Practice and Perform | $135,200-$208,200 | $4,510-$6,940 |

The last row is the version this stack exists to prevent: roughly $77,000-$112,000 a year more than configuration A for capability you already own. Configuration C stops being the cheap option above about 30 reps, because Sybill's flat per-seat price does not discount while Attention's quote can.

The payback test is ramp time. Worked example with your numbers in place of these: an AE carrying an $800,000 annual quota produces about $15,400 of quota per week at full productivity. Configuration A at its top end costs $3,220 per rep per year, so it pays back if it shortens [ramp time](/en/learn/ramp-time/) by about one week per new hire, or lifts conversion on existing reps enough to cover the difference. Measure ramp before you buy; a tool that cannot show the week it saved has nothing to renew on.

## Common variations

- **Bundled roleplay instead of Hyperbound.** [Zoom Revenue Accelerator](/en/tools/zoom-revenue-accelerator/) Premium, at $99.99 per user per month, includes Sales Roleplay paid from a pooled 1,500 AI credits per user per month. Gong Enable includes AI Trainer and Dry Run. Take the bundle when pre-call rehearsal is the need and nobody runs a certification program. The swap rule: if you need certification gates, multiparty roleplays or languages beyond English, keep a dedicated rehearsal tool.
- **[Mindtickle](/en/tools/mindtickle/) as the whole stack.** One vendor for roleplay, certification, call scoring and a per-rep Readiness Index. Take it at 100 or more reps when enablement, not RevOps, owns the budget and practice scores have to feed one number. If Gong already records every call, buy Mindtickle without its Call AI module.
- **[Second Nature](/en/tools/second-nature/) instead of Hyperbound.** Take it when the buyer is corporate training and the rollout spans service roles as well as sales. Keep Hyperbound when the scorecard has to match your sales methodology.
- **Hyperbound free tier, no paid rehearsal.** Under 20 reps, the 45 prebuilt roleplays cover onboarding. Pay for Practice only when you need custom personas built from your own calls.

## Failure modes and guards

- **Roleplay adoption decays after onboarding.** Reps practice in the first month and stop. *Guard:* tie each roleplay to a certification with a date, and put completion on the weekly forecast agenda.
- **Two scorecards drift apart.** *Guard:* one named owner, one scorecard document, copied into the rehearsal tool on every change, and a quarterly check that the same call scores within a point in both systems.
- **Two bots on one call.** *Guard:* one recorder of record, written into the recording policy, and the other recorders' calendar access revoked during the switch.
- **AI scores leak into compensation decisions.** Roleplay and call scoring process recorded voice and personal data. *Guard:* keep AI scores out of compensation and termination decisions until HR and legal have reviewed them, and sign DPAs with explicit recording retention periods.
- **Credit meters on the agent surface.** Sybill's MCP deal analyses cost roughly 300-400 credits each against 5,000 per seat per week on Business. *Guard:* multiply planned agent runs per rep per week by 350 before signing, and price overage bundles into the annual number.

## What this stack does NOT replace

- **A content library.** Highspot or [Seismic](/en/tools/seismic/) hold the decks and battlecards reps send; this stack trains the conversation around them. See [sales enablement](/en/learn/sales-enablement/).
- **A forecasting tool.** Deal-risk flags are not a forecast. [Clari](/en/tools/clari/) owns that number at scale.
- **A manager's 1:1.** The loop assigns practice; it does not decide why a rep is stuck.
- **Hiring.** A bad-fit hire does not ramp faster with more roleplays.
- **The full conversation-intelligence setup.** Synthesis into Slack and Notion is covered in the [conversation intelligence stack](/en/stacks/conversation-intelligence-stack/).

## Match rules

**Use this stack when:**

- You hire at least 6 new reps a year, so ramp time is a number you can measure and move.
- A named manager or enablement lead will run the weekly loop of score, assign, rehearse and re-score.
- You already record calls, or you are buying a recorder now and can choose it on this page's rule.

**Do not use this stack when:**

- You have fewer than 10 reps and no hiring plan. A notetaker such as [Fathom](/en/tools/fathom/) plus Hyperbound's free tier covers it.
- Nobody owns the scorecard. Buying rehearsal before the standard exists trains reps to pass a test nobody agreed on.
- The problem is pipeline volume, not conversion. Coaching better calls does not create more of them; start from the [outbound calling stack](/en/stacks/outbound-calling-stack/).
