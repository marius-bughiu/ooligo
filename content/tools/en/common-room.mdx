## What it is

Common Room captures signals from outside your CRM — Slack communities, Discord, GitHub, LinkedIn, podcasts, job changes — and ties them to people and accounts. It started as a community platform for dev-tools and PLG companies and grew into a serious signal-driven prospecting layer.

If your buyers live in Slack groups, on GitHub, or job-hop frequently, Common Room is where you find them before your CRM does.

## Why it shows up in RevOps stacks

- **Buying signals outside the CRM.** Champion changing jobs, account starring your repo, dev signing up for a competitor — Common Room captures it and routes it as a sales trigger.
- **Person-level + account-level resolution.** Identifies which signals belong to your ICP and merges them into a single timeline.
- **Built for PLG and bottom-up motions.** Free user activity, community engagement, and product usage all feed the signal layer alongside CRM events.

## Pricing

- **Free** — limited members and integrations, good for starter use
- **Starter** — from ~$625/month annual, full integrations
- **Team** — custom, higher limits and AI features
- **Enterprise** — custom, advanced security and admin

## Best for

- PLG and dev-tools companies with a free user base
- RevOps teams running signal-based outbound (job changes, GitHub stars, community joins)
- Marketing teams owning community-led growth motions

## Watch-outs

- Less useful for traditional enterprise outbound where buyers do not engage publicly
- Signal volume can be overwhelming without clear routing rules
- AI features are catching up; competitors like UserGems are stronger on specific signals
