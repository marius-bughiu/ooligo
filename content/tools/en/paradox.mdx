## What it is

Paradox makes Olivia, a conversational AI assistant purpose-built for high-volume hiring. Candidates apply, screen, and self-schedule interviews entirely through chat — no apply forms, no recruiter back-and-forth. McDonald's, Chipotle, and CVS run their hourly hiring on it.

## Why it shows up in Recruiting/TA stacks

- **Apply-by-chat.** Olivia replaces the application form with a 2-minute SMS or web conversation. Apply-to-hire times drop from weeks to days; conversion rates often double.
- **Self-scheduling that actually works.** Olivia syncs to manager calendars, handles reschedules, sends reminders, and chases no-shows. This is the killer feature most TA teams underestimate.
- **Workday / SAP / iCIMS depth.** Deep ATS integrations mean Olivia writes back into the system of record cleanly — critical for enterprise.

## Pricing

- **Olivia Hire** — custom, typically priced per requisition or per applicant volume
- **Olivia Capture, Schedule, Onboard** — modular add-ons
- **Enterprise** — custom MSAs with implementation services

No public pricing. Six- to seven-figure annual contracts for Fortune 500 deployments. Mid-market deals typically start at $50K+.

## Best for

- Enterprise high-volume hiring (retail, restaurants, healthcare, distribution)
- Teams hiring 5K+ hourly workers per year
- Workday and SAP SuccessFactors shops needing a conversational front-end

## Watch-outs

- Overkill for low-volume professional hiring — pick Metaview or Gem instead
- Implementation is a real project (8–16 weeks) with change management for managers
- Per-requisition pricing punishes spiky hiring cycles; negotiate floors and ceilings
