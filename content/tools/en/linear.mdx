## What it is

Linear is opinionated project tracking built for software teams — fast, keyboard-driven, with a clean data model around issues, projects, cycles, and roadmaps. Ops teams have adopted it for internal initiatives because it's faster than Jira and more structured than a Notion database.

## Why it shows up in ops stacks

- **Speed.** Sub-100ms interactions, command palette everywhere — the velocity of the tool actually changes how ops teams work.
- **Structured by default.** Cycles, status, priority, labels, and projects come out of the box. RevOps initiatives stop sprawling across spreadsheets.
- **MCP and API.** AI agents and automations slot in cleanly — Linear was early to MCP support.

## Pricing

- **Free** — up to 250 issues, 10 users
- **Standard** — $8/user/mo, unlimited issues, integrations
- **Plus** — $14/user/mo, advanced cycles, AI features
- **Enterprise** — custom, SAML SSO, advanced security

## Best for

- RevOps and Talent teams running structured initiatives
- Companies already using Linear in engineering — extend, don't add Jira
- Cross-functional projects that need an issue model, not a doc

## Watch-outs

- Not built for marketing or finance workflows — they'll bounce off
- The opinion ("the right way to plan work") is a feature and a tax
- Custom fields and views are improving but still trail Jira's flexibility
