## What it is

Pinpoint is the modern ATS designed for SMB and mid-market companies, with strongest concentration in UK and EMEA markets. Founded in 2019, it competes against [Workable](/en/tools/workable/), [Recruitee](/en/tools/recruitee/), and [Ashby](/en/tools/ashby/) on the same buyer profile — companies that have outgrown spreadsheet-tracked hiring but aren't at enterprise scale yet. Differentiates on candidate-facing experience and a powerful careers-site builder.

## Why it shows up in Recruiting stacks

- **Best-in-class careers site.** Pinpoint's careers-site builder produces clean, conversion-optimized career pages that integrate natively with the ATS — no separate CMS needed.
- **GDPR-native.** UK-headquartered, designed under GDPR from inception. Data residency and consent flows are first-class.
- **Per-job pricing scales sensibly.** Flat-rate per-active-job model maps cost to actual hiring activity rather than penalizing user growth.

## Pricing

- **Starter** — $600/month for up to 5 active jobs
- **Growth** — $1,000/month for up to 25 active jobs
- **Enterprise** — custom for unlimited jobs and advanced features
- Per-job model contrasts with per-employee pricing of [iCIMS](/en/tools/icims/) and [SmartRecruiters](/en/tools/smartrecruiters/)

## Best for

- SMB and growth-stage companies (50-500 employees) prioritizing candidate experience
- UK and EMEA companies where GDPR-native vendor relationships matter
- Hiring teams that need a strong careers site as part of [recruitment marketing](/en/learn/recruitment-marketing/)

## Watch-outs

- AI features lag specialist tools — pair with [juicebox](/en/tools/juicebox/) or [hireEZ](/en/tools/hireez/) for sourcing
- Less brand recognition in US than [Workable](/en/tools/workable/) or [Greenhouse](/en/tools/greenhouse/); American buyers default elsewhere
- Outgrown by enterprise scale (1,500+ employees, multi-business-unit complexity)
- For high-volume hourly hiring, [Fountain](/en/tools/fountain/) is a better fit
