## What it is

Lever is an ATS + native CRM, mostly serving mid-market companies. After being acquired by Employ Inc. (Jobvite, JazzHR), product velocity has slowed, but the core ATS is still solid and the CRM remains one of the few ATS-native options.

## Why it shows up in Recruiting/TA stacks

- **CRM in the ATS.** Nurture campaigns, talent pools, and silver-medalist workflows live in the same product as your reqs. For teams not buying Gem, this is real value.
- **Mid-market sweet spot.** Pricing and complexity sit between Ashby and Greenhouse. Series B–D companies that don't need full Greenhouse muscle often land here.
- **Decent reporting.** Lever Insights covers funnel, source ROI, and DEI without forcing a BI tool, though it trails Ashby's depth.

## Pricing

- **LeverTRM** — ATS + CRM, custom annual
- **LeverTRM for Enterprise** — adds advanced reporting, data warehouse sync
- Add-ons — Nurture, Analytics, Automation

No public pricing. Typical mid-market deals run $15–40K/year. Negotiation leverage has improved as competitive pressure from Ashby grows.

## Best for

- Series B–D mid-market companies
- Teams that want ATS + CRM in one without adopting Greenhouse + Gem
- Orgs prioritizing nurture and silver medalist workflows

## Watch-outs

- Product velocity has slowed materially under Employ Inc. ownership — fewer net-new features than Ashby or Greenhouse
- AI roadmap is thin; expect to pair with Metaview and outside sourcing AI
- Marketplace is smaller than Greenhouse — verify your critical integrations before signing
