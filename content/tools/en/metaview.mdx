## Which part of Metaview to buy

Metaview serves several recruiting tasks, with separate buying units. [Notetaker](https://www.metaview.ai/interview-notes) captures interviews, produces candidate summaries and syncs notes or scorecards to supported ATSs. [Sourcing](https://www.metaview.ai/sourcing) searches for candidates from a role brief. [Application Review](https://www.metaview.ai/application-review) evaluates incoming profiles against criteria your team can edit. Buying one does not establish that you need the others.

Start with Notetaker when interviewers struggle to produce usable, attributable notes for a hiring debrief. Sourcing volume is a different problem. You do not need a year of recordings or a Sourcing subscription merely to evaluate interview notes. Skip a recruiting platform if your requirement is only general customer-meeting summaries; evaluate [Fathom](/en/tools/fathom/) first. For sales coaching and revenue reviews, compare [Gong](/en/tools/gong/) and [Chorus](/en/tools/chorus/) in our [conversation-intelligence shortlist](/en/vs/best-conversation-intelligence-tools/).

## Prices depend on the module

These public list prices were checked on November 2, 2026. They are not negotiated quotes or a single price for the whole platform.

| Module | Published entry and paid options | Billing unit to check |
|---|---|---|
| [Notetaker](https://www.metaview.ai/pricing?product=notes) | Free: 25 calls/month. Pro: $60/user/month with unlimited calls. Enterprise: custom. | Users recording interviews |
| [Sourcing](https://www.metaview.ai/pricing) | Free: first 100 profiles sourced. Pro: $100/user/month for 200 profiles/month. Max: $300/user/month for unlimited profiles. | Users and sourced profiles; the free allowance is not stated as monthly |
| [Application Review](https://www.metaview.ai/pricing?product=application-review) | Free: 50 applications/month. The displayed Pro selection is $150/month for 500 reviews/month, with unlimited users. Enterprise: custom. | Selected review volume, not a fee per recruiter |

For illustration, two Notetaker Pro seats total **$120/month** at the displayed rate, before any tax or additional products. Adding Application Review requires its own volume choice; multiplying every agent price by recruiter headcount would misstate the budget. Confirm modules, allowances, billing term and renewal conditions together before ordering. We have no independently verified discount or typical customer spend to report.

## Setup and an evaluation worth running

Use an organization-approved interview sample, not a vendor's best-looking demo. Define your [structured interviewing](/en/learn/structured-interviewing/) rubric first. Then check whether notes retain the evidence needed for each criterion, distinguish speakers and preserve uncertainty instead of inventing an answer. Measure correction minutes alongside completeness; a longer summary is not automatically a better record.

Choose one ATS and one interview format for the first trial. The [integration documentation](https://support.metaview.ai/integrations/integrations-overview) is the starting point, not proof that every field in your configuration syncs correctly. Verify the destination candidate, scorecard fields, access permissions and duplicate-record behavior with an approved test record before wider use.

Our [interview debrief template](/en/workflows/interview-debrief-summary-skill/) and [interview-loop planning template](/en/workflows/interview-loop-builder-skill/) offer structures for downstream work. They are separate instructions, not a tested Metaview integration. The [interview-intelligence guide](/en/learn/interview-intelligence/) explains the category beyond this product.

## Limits to resolve before rollout

- **An AI summary is not an independent hiring judgment.** Check important statements against the interview and keep responsibility for the decision with the hiring team. Inspect omitted and contradictory evidence, not just polished wording.
- **Recording and sharing need an agreed process.** Have your organization approve notices, access and retention settings before recording. Test sharing and deletion using non-sensitive material. This review does not certify legal compliance.
- **MCP is not read-only.** The [official MCP documentation](https://support.metaview.ai/integrations/mcp-integration/mcp-overview) describes live changes, including ATS updates and Application Review decisions that can send rejection emails. Inspect account permissions and available actions before connecting an assistant; require confirmation before writes or candidate messages.

Choose Notetaker if the trial improves usable interview records at an acceptable review cost. Evaluate Sourcing and Application Review separately against their own workloads. Keep your current process if it already produces the required evidence and the new subscription does not improve it.
