## What it is

Pipedrive is a sales-first CRM built around a drag-and-drop Kanban pipeline. It was founded in Tallinn, Estonia in 2010, has been majority-owned by Vista Equity Partners since 2020, and says it serves more than 105,000 companies in 180+ countries. The product covers deals, leads, contacts, email sync, activity tracking, automations, forecasting and reporting. It does not do service ticketing or product analytics. It is a CRM for salespeople, not a customer platform.

Three things changed in 2026 that a buyer needs to know before reading any older review.

- **An official MCP server.** Pipedrive launched a native MCP server on 30 June 2026, on every plan. Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor or a custom client can read deals, contacts, organizations and leads and can create or update records, convert leads, and log activities and notes. It respects the connected user's permissions and keeps an audit trail. Pipedrive added leads, quotes and the product catalog in its September updates, and the connector has been in Claude's official directory since 18 August 2026.
- **Nova, an AI meeting assistant.** Nova went generally available on 16 September 2026 on all plans at no extra cost. It writes pre-call briefs, records and transcribes meetings, and drafts CRM updates that a rep approves before they are written. Pipedrive positions it as assisted, not autonomous. There is no agent that works deals on its own.
- **The largest restructuring in the company's history.** On 29 October 2026 CEO Paulo Cunha announced fewer teams, layers and locations, affecting the "majority of the roles". He cited free search traffic collapsing as SMBs pick software through AI assistants. Estonian press reported 267 jobs cut in Tallinn and Tartu, about 70% of local staff; Pipedrive has not confirmed the number. The stated new focus is core CRM quality and an "AI-native sales system", with Nova as the first step. In August 2026 Pipedrive also acquired Outfunnel, a marketing-automation app from its own marketplace.

## Why it shows up in RevOps stacks

- **Reps actually use it.** The pipeline board is the main interface, not a report. Each card shows stage, value and next activity, so a team under 30 reps can go live in days without an admin designing permission trees. In SMB sales the usual cause of CRM failure is reps not logging activity, and that is the problem Pipedrive is built to solve.
- **Per-seat pricing with no stated minimum.** No platform fee and no seat floor on any plan. A three-person team pays for three seats. HubSpot Sales Hub Professional adds a required $1,500 onboarding fee, and Salesforce's useful tiers assume an admin.
- **The cheapest CRM to put behind an AI assistant.** The MCP server is included on Lite at $14/seat. For a RevOps lead who wants Claude to update deals after calls or pull the stale-deal list on Monday morning, Pipedrive has the lowest entry price among the major CRMs. Usage is capped by plan-based token allowances that Pipedrive does not publish, and extra tokens can be bought.
- **API on every plan.** The REST API is on Lite and up, with rate limits that scale by plan. That covers BI exports, warehouse sync and Zapier or Make automations without upgrading.

## Pricing

Pipedrive renamed and repriced its plans in July 2025 (Essential became Lite, Advanced became Growth, Professional and Power merged into Premium, Enterprise became Ultimate). Prices have moved again since. Per seat per month, as of November 2026:

- **Lite**: $14 annual, $24 monthly. Pipeline, leads, calendar, AI report creation, Nova, MCP. No email sync, no automations.
- **Growth**: $39 annual, $49 monthly. Two-way Gmail and Outlook sync with tracking, automations (up to 3 if/else steps), Sequences, forecasting, meeting scheduler.
- **Premium**: $59 annual, $79 monthly. Adds LeadBooster (chatbot, live chat, Prospector, web forms), Smart Docs (quotes and e-signature), Projects, AI email writing and summaries, team inbox, required and formula fields, permission sets, phone support.
- **Ultimate**: $79 annual, $99 monthly. Adds security rules, IP and time-based access limits, contact enrichment, a sandbox and extended phone support.

There is no free plan. The trial is 14 days with no credit card. Monthly billing costs up to 42% more, so the annual figures are the ones to budget with.

Add-ons are mostly billed per company: LeadBooster from $32.50/month, Smart Docs from $32.50, Web Visitors from $41, Campaigns from $13.33, and Projects from $6.67 per user. Campaigns and Web Visitors scale with contact and visitor volume. The upgrade math is that Premium costs $20/seat/month more than Growth, while LeadBooster plus Smart Docs cost $65/month for the whole company. Above three seats it is cheaper to buy those two add-ons on Growth, so only move to Premium if you need its permission controls, required fields or AI email.

What a team really pays: a 10-rep team on Growth with annual billing pays **$4,680/year** (10 × $39 × 12), and the same team on Premium pays **$7,080**. That is 63% more than the $2,880 this plan cost on the old $24 Growth price. Vendr does not publish a median for Pipedrive. Its February 2026 bands put teams of 1–10 users at $2,000–$8,000/year and 11–50 users at $10,000–$40,000, with multi-year terms 15–30% lower. For comparison, HubSpot Sales Hub Professional at 10 seats is $10,800/year on annual billing plus the $1,500 onboarding, and Salesforce Pro Suite is $12,000/year before implementation.

## Best for

Best for a sales-led SMB with 2–50 reps and no dedicated CRM admin, selling a repeatable deal motion. Typical buyers are a founder-led B2B company moving off spreadsheets, or a mid-market sales division that wants a CRM it can run itself. The specific use case where it now wins is the small RevOps team that wants reps' pipeline hygiene enforced by the board and wants an AI assistant writing back to the CRM through MCP without paying an enterprise CRM's entry price.

Not for you if you need marketing automation, service ticketing or product analytics in one system. Pipedrive's Campaigns is basic email and Outfunnel is not yet folded into the core product. Also not for you if you sell complex multi-stakeholder enterprise deals that need account hierarchies, deep custom objects or CPQ; [Salesforce](/en/tools/salesforce/) earns its overhead there.

## Versus the alternatives

**[HubSpot](/en/tools/hubspot/)** is the most common alternative and the usual upgrade path. Pick HubSpot when marketing, sales and service need one record graph; its breadth is worth the price at that scope. Pick Pipedrive when the job is pure pipeline management: at 10 seats, Growth at $4,680/year against HubSpot Professional at $12,300 in year one is a 2.6× gap, and Growth already covers the automation and forecasting most sales managers buy Professional for. HubSpot Starter at $20/seat month-to-month is cheaper than Growth but lacks the automation depth. The full breakdown is in [Pipedrive vs HubSpot](/en/vs/pipedrive-vs-hubspot/).

**[Salesforce](/en/tools/salesforce/)** is the other market-share leader, and below about 50 seats it is the right pick only when enterprise integration, compliance or territory governance requires it. Starter Suite at $25/user is cheap, but the Pro Suite at $100/user and the admin it needs are what most teams end up paying for.

**[Attio](/en/tools/attio/)** is the fastest-growing challenger. Pick Attio when you need a flexible data model and custom objects for a PLG or non-standard GTM motion, and you have someone technical to build it; custom objects sit on Pro at $79/seat annual. Pick Pipedrive when a five-rep team needs to be live this week. **[Zoho CRM](/en/tools/zoho-crm/)** is the budget option, with a free plan for three users and paid tiers from $14.

## Watch-outs

- **The vendor is mid-restructuring.** Cutting the majority of roles, announced on 29 October 2026, puts support response times, roadmap delivery and account management at risk. Pipedrive's support already drew G2 complaints about after-hours response before the cuts. *Guard:* before signing a multi-year term, get the support SLA for your plan in writing, ask who your account contact is after the restructuring, and prefer annual terms with a price cap at renewal over a deeper multi-year discount until the new structure has shipped a quarter of releases.
- **Prices have moved twice in 16 months.** Growth went from $24 to $39 and Premium from $49 to $59, so renewal quotes based on 2025 pricing will surprise finance. *Guard:* build the budget on today's annual price, not the price you signed at, and negotiate a renewal cap of 5–7% into the order form.
- **Automation hits a ceiling.** Growth allows 3 if/else steps per automation, and Premium caps active automations at 150. Multi-branch or cross-object logic ends up in Zapier or Make, which adds cost and something else to maintain. *Guard:* map your three most complex automation requirements against the builder during the trial; if any needs more than three conditions, budget for a Make or Zapier layer from day one.
- **MCP and AI usage caps are not published.** The MCP server and AI features run on plan-based token allowances, and Pipedrive sells top-ups without publishing amounts or prices. *Guard:* run your heaviest assistant workflow, such as post-call updates for every rep, for a full billing cycle on the plan you intend to buy, and ask sales for the allowance and top-up price in writing before rolling it out.
