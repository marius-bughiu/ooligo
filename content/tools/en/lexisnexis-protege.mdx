## What it is

LexisNexis Protégé is the legal AI assistant at the center of **Lexis+ with Protégé** — the platform LexisNexis renamed from Lexis+ AI in February 2026 when it stopped being an assistant bolted onto research and became an end-to-end drafting, research, and analysis workflow tool. Every answer is grounded in the LexisNexis library — primary law, exclusive secondary sources, and Practical Guidance — and citations run through Shepard's for validation, status, and treatment. The pitch to a lawyer: personalized AI that learns from your firm's matter history and drafting style, with authority you can defend in front of a partner or a judge.

## Why it shows up in Legal Ops stacks

- **Citations grounded in authoritative content, checked by Shepard's.** In May 2026 LexisNexis added Shepard's Verify Trust Markers, which scan AI-generated *and* attorney-drafted text, check every legal citation against Lexis sources, and flag any it can't verify. For output that has to survive review — or the hallucinated-citation sanctions that keep hitting the news — that check is the whole point.
- **Protégé Work turns a request into a plan.** The May 2026 release added an orchestration layer: describe a goal in plain language (contract comparison, complaint analysis, research synthesis, due diligence, compliance review), and Protégé routes it across 300+ pre-built skills, shows a structured plan, then returns review-ready work product in Word, Excel, PowerPoint, or PDF.
- **Vault and agents for matter-grounded drafting.** Protégé Vault holds up to 100,000 documents per matter and takes multimodal input — PDFs, spreadsheets, images, audio, video — with every output linking back to the exact passage, spreadsheet row, or timestamp. Purpose-built drafting agents produce contracts, motions, briefs, and deal documents grounded in Lexis content, firm templates, and that matter's own materials.

## Pricing

- **Custom, quote-only, and priced outside every base Lexis tier.** Protégé is a separate line item on top of your research contract, not bundled into it — budget for both.
- **Real-world band (third-party reported, not vendor-published):** the AI add-on runs roughly **$125–$275/user/mo**, and a full standalone Lexis+ with Protégé bundle **$128–$494/user/mo**. Enterprise deals with the full agentic feature set land higher. There's also a transactional option — about **$99 per AI Ask** and **$250 per AI Drafting use** — for firms that don't want a per-seat subscription.
- BYOK encryption (customer-managed keys via AWS KMS, Azure Key Vault, Google Cloud KMS, or HashiCorp Vault) ships for regulated buyers who need to hold their own keys and revoke access.

## Best for

- Litigation and regulatory practices that need cite-checked, Shepard's-verified output
- Firms and in-house teams already standardized on Lexis+ primary and secondary content
- Teams that want research, drafting, and diligence under one grounded platform instead of stitching point tools together
- Regulated and public-sector legal teams that require customer-held encryption keys

## Alternatives and watch-outs

- **Pick a different tool if you're not already on Lexis content.** The value concentrates for firms paying for the Lexis library; if your research spine is Westlaw, [Thomson Reuters CoCounsel](/en/tools/thomson-reuters-cocounsel/) is the like-for-like grounded alternative. For a firm-wide drafting and workflow suite that isn't anchored to one research publisher, [Harvey](/en/tools/harvey/) fits better. [Legora](/en/tools/legora/) is the fastest-growing entrant to weigh — European-origin collaborative drafting and review, Harvey's chief rival after its 2026 raise — when you want content-agnostic drafting.
- **AI is a separate spend on top of research.** The effective per-attorney cost stacks the Protégé add-on onto a base Lexis contract that already isn't cheap. Guard: get the AI add-on quoted separately from your research renewal, and model the combined three-year per-seat cost — including renewal uplift — before signing.
- **Grounding reduces hallucination; it doesn't eliminate it, and Shepard's checks cites, not reasoning.** Verified citations can still sit inside a wrong argument. Guard: keep Shepard's Verify on every AI-drafted citation *and* an attorney review on the substance, not just the cite list.
- **The agentic layer is new.** Protégé Work, agents, Vault, and Workrooms shipped in May 2026, so firm-wide maturity is still early. Guard: pilot Protégé Work on one bounded workflow — a single diligence matter or clause-comparison run — and check the output against your own baseline before rolling it out to every practice group.
