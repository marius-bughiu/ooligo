## What it is

Dovetail is the interview and conversation corpus that sits underneath a feedback stack. Where [Canny](/en/tools/canny/) collects feature requests and [Productboard](/en/tools/productboard/) turns them into a roadmap, Dovetail holds the raw material those decisions are supposed to rest on: call recordings, interview transcripts, support tickets, survey responses and sales calls, stored as searchable, tagged, quotable evidence with the timestamp and the speaker still attached.

The company was founded in Sydney in 2017 and spent its first years as the default UX research repository. It now sells itself as a "Customer Context Layer": the corpus is meant to feed agents and other teams' tools, not only researchers. The Summer Launch on 14 July 2026 shipped **digital twins** (a queryable stand-in for a segment, built from that segment's own calls and tickets) and **AI Agents** that watch incoming signal and write back into Salesforce, Slack, Linear or a ticket. **Channels 2.0** went GA on 21 October 2026. On 6 November 2026 Dovetail replaced its whole plan lineup with **Dovetail One**, priced on platform, users and AI credits.

## Why it shows up in Customer Success stacks

- **The MCP server is what lifts this above a searchable archive.** The Dovetail MCP server is included on every Dovetail One plan and connects Claude, Copilot, Codex, Cursor and Atlassian Rovo; Dovetail's agents can in turn call external MCP connectors such as Salesforce, Notion, Hex and Canva. A CSM drafting a QBR narrative pulls three verbatim quotes from that account's own calls inside the session where the drafting is already happening, rather than in a tool someone has to remember to open.

- **Revenue weighting is what makes it a Customer Success tool rather than a research tool.** Channels classifies tickets, surveys and sales calls into ideas, and CRM enrichment attaches account and ARR data from Salesforce or HubSpot. A theme raised by 40 trial users and a theme raised by four accounts worth $2M in renewal do not deserve the same roadmap treatment, and that ranking, not the clustering, is the output a CS leader takes into a prioritisation meeting.

- **Evidence stays bound to the claim.** Every theme resolves back to the highlight, the transcript timestamp and the person who said it. In an escalation or a renewal, that is the gap between "customers are asking for this" and a named quote from that customer's own director, which is the version that survives being challenged.

## Pricing reality

**There is no free plan any more, and every plan is metered.** Dovetail One bills three things together, annually and upfront: a platform fee, users, and AI credits at **$0.15 each on every plan**. Processing costs 1 credit per data point (transcription, translation, summarisation, classification, CRM enrichment, indexing); redaction adds another credit per data point. Chat answers typically cost 1-10 credits, deep-research chat 5-30, a digital-twin or agent response 3-25, a scheduled agent run 5-50. Search is free.

- **Bronze**: $4,800/yr for the platform and a fixed 5 users, plus credits, paid by card. On 19 November 2026 its button still reads "Coming soon", so you cannot buy it yet. It also leaves out the Public API, SSO, digital twins and the Slack and Teams app.
- **Silver**: 25+ users, sales-quoted, pay by invoice. Adds SSO, access control, audit log, data residency (US, Europe or Australia), digital twins, API and CLI.
- **Gold**: 100+ users, sales-quoted, with a 30-day free trial, the only free entry point left. Adds SCIM, AI redaction, custom retention, choice of LLM provider and a dedicated CSM. HIPAA is a Gold-only add-on.

The meter is what changes the arithmetic. Loading 10,000 tickets and calls a year costs 10,000 credits, or $1,500; redacting them doubles that to $3,000, and historical backfill is charged the same as new data. Credit overages are on by default and billed monthly; unused credits expire at the end of the contract. For the negotiation, Vendr's marketplace data (February 2026, 45 purchases, all on the pre-Dovetail One plans) put the median contract at **$21,600/yr**, ranging **$10,800 to $61,200**. Use that as the reference band until Dovetail One deals show up in the data, and add your credit forecast on top. Legacy Professional, Business and Enterprise workspaces get a credit grant on 11 January 2027; after that, Channels usage draws on credits.

## Best for

Customer Success and product leaders at roughly 200 employees and up, where customer conversations are already being recorded through Zoom, Google Meet or [Gong](/en/tools/gong/), and the recurring failure is that nobody can locate the quote six weeks later. The fit sharpens when a research or insights function already exists and Customer Success wants to read from the same corpus instead of standing up a second one, and when 25 or more people will use it, which is where Silver starts. Atlassian, Canva, Okta, Itaú Unibanco, Breville and AWS appear among the public customer logos.

## Do not buy it if

Your feedback is already structured and your question is "what do we build next." Canny answers that with a public board and vote counts for a fraction of the price, and Dovetail's strength, unstructured conversation, is wasted on a queue of tidy feature requests. The same holds if you record no calls: the differentiation starts with transcripts, and without them you have bought a costly tagging tool with a meter attached. It is also not a survey platform or a participant recruiter: Dovetail reads Qualtrics, [Sprig](/en/tools/sprig/) and Usersnap; it does not run the study or source the panel. And a team of three or four researchers now has no Dovetail plan it can buy without a sales cycle until Bronze opens.

## Versus the alternatives

- **UserTesting**: the largest install base in the segment, holding UserZoom and EnjoyHQ after Thoma Bravo and Sunstone Partners took it private in a $1.3B deal. Pick it when you need the participant panel and moderated testing on one contract; there the repository is a feature of a testing platform, not the product.
- **Qualtrics**: the largest budget line in experience management and the one most likely to already be in the building. Pick it when survey collection, program governance and case management have to sit on one contract. Pick Dovetail when the material already exists and the problem is reading it.
- **[Enterpret](/en/tools/enterpret/)**: the fastest-growing entrant, backed by a $20.8M Series A led by Canaan Partners (4 December 2024), now pitching the same "customer context" idea and running headless over MCP. Pick Enterpret when the volume is support tickets and reviews at scale and nobody is running interviews. Pick Dovetail when the highest-value evidence is a conversation a human had.
- **Condens**: still publishes flat prices: €15 per contributor per month on Lite, €500 a month (€6,000/yr) for Business with five contributors and unlimited viewers, no AI credit meter, and free migration from Dovetail on Business. Pick it when a small research team wants a repository with a number on the website and a predictable bill.
- **Marvin**: Free, Starter, Pro and Enterprise plans, with the paid tiers sold by demo. Pick it when research ops (scheduling, participant tracking, incentive handling) matters more to you than corpus depth.

## Watch-outs

- **The bill is now a forecast, not a price.** Platform and users are fixed, but every transcript, ticket, chat answer and agent run burns credits, overages are on by default, unused credits expire, and prepaid credits are not refunded. *Guard:* before signing, run your real annual volume through the per-data-point and per-action rates, double it if you need redaction, decide whether overages stay on, and get the overage rate and a renewal uplift cap written into the order form.
- **Backfill costs the same as new data.** Migrating three years of call history into Dovetail One is charged at the full processing rate. *Guard:* load the last 12 months first, prove the use case, and only then price the archive.
- **Agents and digital twins write into other systems, and every run is metered.** An agent that updates a Salesforce record or opens a Linear ticket on a false positive becomes somebody's backlog, and a scheduled agent can cost 5-50 credits per run. *Guard:* run agents in report-only mode into a private Slack channel for two weeks, count the false-positive rate and the credits consumed, and only then grant write scopes.
- **A digital twin models your corpus, not your customer.** If the recorded calls skew toward the accounts your CSMs enjoy talking to, the twin reproduces that bias fluently and without warning. *Guard:* check segment coverage of the underlying calls before quoting a twin to an exec, and require every twin answer to cite its source highlights.
- **Legacy customers have a deadline.** Professional, Business and Enterprise workspaces move onto credits for Channels from 11 January 2027, and AI features are limited once the grant runs out. *Guard:* pull your current Channels volume now and price the Dovetail One switch before the grant, not after.
