## What it is

Loxo is the modern recruiting platform that combines ATS, recruiting CRM, and AI-augmented sourcing in one product — primarily targeting staffing firms, recruitment agencies, and executive search firms but increasingly used by corporate recruiting teams as well. Founded in 2014 in Denver, Loxo positions against [Bullhorn](/en/tools/bullhorn/) on UX modernization and against pure ATS platforms on integrated sourcing depth.

## Why it shows up in Recruiting stacks

- **Modern UX vs legacy staffing platforms.** Cleaner interface, faster workflow, mobile-friendly — material productivity advantages for recruiters working in the platform daily.
- **AI-augmented candidate matching.** ML-based scoring of candidates against open requisitions; surfaces candidates the team's manual search would have missed.
- **All-in-one for staffing firms.** Where Bullhorn requires multiple modules (ATS, CRM, Back Office) at additional cost, Loxo bundles much of the same functionality at lower TCO.

## Pricing

- **Custom only.** Per-recruiter pricing typically; effective rates from $80-200/recruiter/month depending on volume and modules.
- **Annual contracts** standard.
- Implementation typically 30-60 days — faster than Bullhorn for similar scope.

## Best for

- Staffing firms, executive search firms, and contract recruiters
- Mid-market recruitment agencies that find Bullhorn too heavy and Crelate too lightweight
- Corporate recruiting teams with high contract-to-hire or specialty-search workflow alongside corporate hiring

## Watch-outs

- Smaller installed base than [Bullhorn](/en/tools/bullhorn/) in the US enterprise staffing market — for the largest staffing operations, Bullhorn ecosystem advantages may matter
- Less suited for pure corporate hiring than dedicated corporate ATS ([Greenhouse](/en/tools/greenhouse/), [Ashby](/en/tools/ashby/), [Workable](/en/tools/workable/))
- AI features compete with specialist sourcing tools ([juicebox](/en/tools/juicebox/), [hireEZ](/en/tools/hireez/)) on candidate-discovery quality; Loxo's strength is in the integrated workflow
- Pricing is opaque; verify TCO against Bullhorn before commitment for staffing-firm deployments
