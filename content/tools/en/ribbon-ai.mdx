## What it is

Ribbon is a voice AI interviewer for first-round screens in high-volume hiring. A candidate applies, Ribbon invites them by SMS, WhatsApp, or email, and its interviewer (branded "Bonnie") runs a structured, two-way voice conversation at any hour, in browser or by phone-in. Answers are scored against a rubric you configure, candidates are ranked, and the transcript, summary, and score go back to your ATS. Video interviews, language-proficiency scoring, skill assessments, cheating and off-screen detection, and a meeting notetaker sit around that core.

The Toronto company was founded in 2023 by Arsham Ghahramani (CEO) and Dave Vu (COO), and raised US$8.2M led by Radical Ventures in March 2025, with Cadenza Ventures and GD1 among the other investors. It says it has run more than 1 million interviews and lists Tesla, Deloitte, Extra Space Storage, and BGIS among its customers (vendor claims). Its target buyers are the frontline employers that hire on repeat: manufacturing, warehousing, trucking, self-storage, hotels, restaurants, home care, contact centers, and staffing agencies.

Ribbon is **not** a scheduler, a conversational apply flow, or an ATS. It runs the first screen and hands a ranked shortlist to a recruiter.

## Why it shows up in Recruiting stacks

- **The price is on the website.** Ribbon is one of the few AI interviewers that publishes a rate card, with a 7-day free trial. A TA lead can model the cost before the first sales call, which no one can do with [Paradox](/en/tools/paradox/), [HireVue](/en/tools/hirevue/), or [HeyMilo](/en/tools/heymilo/).
- **Wide ATS coverage.** Ribbon claims two-way integration with 60+ systems, including [Greenhouse](/en/tools/greenhouse/), [Workday](/en/tools/workday/), [Ashby](/en/tools/ashby/), [Workable](/en/tools/workable/), [Bullhorn](/en/tools/bullhorn/), [BambooHR](/en/tools/bamboohr/), [SmartRecruiters](/en/tools/smartrecruiters/), [JazzHR](/en/tools/jazzhr/), UKG, Paycom, and SAP SuccessFactors. The HRIS-style systems on that list (UKG, Paycom, BambooHR) are the ones hourly employers already run.
- **Candidate-side voice quality.** The pitch is a natural-sounding interviewer that hourly applicants will finish on a phone, plus white-label branding and custom voices so the screen looks like your company, not a vendor.

## Pricing

Published on 2026-10-04, all self-serve tiers billed annually:

- **Growth:** $499/month, 2 seats, 2 active roles, 100 interviews/month included, $4.00 per extra interview.
- **Business:** $999/month, 5 seats, 5 active roles, 400 interviews/month, $3.00 overage.
- **Scale:** $1,999/month, 10 seats, 10 active roles, 1,000 interviews/month, $2.50 overage, plus implementation help and ATS field mapping.
- **Enterprise:** custom, unlimited interviews, SSO (SAML), sub-teams, a dedicated CSM and SLA, and SOC 2 documentation.

**What that means at scale:** the annual floor is $5,988. If you use the full bucket, the effective rate is about $4.99 per interview on Growth, $2.50 on Business, and $2.00 on Scale. Against a 20-minute recruiter phone screen at a $35/hour loaded cost (about $12, estimate), Ribbon pays back from the first month if you actually run the volume. The real constraint is the **active-role cap**, not the interview count: a team with eight open requisitions is on Scale, whatever its volume.

## Best for

TA teams and staffing desks screening 100 to 1,000+ hourly or frontline applicants a month for roles with a repeatable rubric (warehouse, driver, store associate, front desk, caregiver, contact-center agent) who want to buy on a credit card and be live in a week.

**Do not buy Ribbon if** you hire senior or specialist roles, where an AI-only first round costs you candidates; if you have more open requisitions than your tier's role cap allows and cannot justify Scale; or if your security review requires SOC 2 Type II and SSO today.

## Versus the alternatives

- **[Paradox](/en/tools/paradox/)**: now part of Workday, and the market-share leader for hourly hiring. Pick it when you want conversational apply, screening, and self-scheduling as one enterprise deployment and can absorb a $25K–150K+ contract. Ribbon wins on entry cost and time to live.
- **[HireVue](/en/tools/hirevue/)**: the enterprise assessment incumbent. Pick it when a Fortune 500 legal team needs validation studies and published assessment science. Ribbon is faster and cheaper for a single screening step.
- **[Apriora (Alex)](/en/tools/apriora/)**: the fastest-growing conversational interviewer, with a $17M Series A. Pick it for adaptive follow-ups, calendar coordination, and SOC 2 Type II on an enterprise contract.
- **[HeyMilo](/en/tools/heymilo/)**: per-interview pricing, roughly $4–8 by third-party estimates, and deep staffing-ATS write-back (Bullhorn, Avionté, JobDiva). Pick it for an agency desk that needs explainable per-question scores in Bullhorn notes.
- **[Sapia.ai](/en/tools/sapia-ai/)**: text-chat interviews. Pick it when candidates will not take a voice call.

If none fit because volume is under about 50 screens a month, a recruiter with a phone-screen script and an ATS scorecard is cheaper than the $5,988 floor. For screening rules you can defend, see [candidate screening](/en/learn/candidate-screening/).

## Watch-outs

- **The bias audit is gated and dated.** Ribbon advertises a 2025 NYC Local Law 144 bias audit, but the report sits behind a demo request and the public page names no auditor. *Guard:* get the full report before signing, check the auditor's name, the audit date, and that it covers the scoring you will use. Under [NYC Local Law 144](/en/learn/nyc-local-law-144/) the audit must be under a year old when you use the tool, and posting the summary and candidate notices is the employer's job, not Ribbon's.
- **Security paperwork trails the price card.** Ribbon is SOC 2 Type I with Type II "in observation," and SSO is Enterprise-only. *Guard:* ask for the Type II completion date and bridge letter, and do not route candidate data from a regulated client through a self-serve tier without a signed DPA.
- **Annual billing behind a 7-day trial.** The self-serve tiers are billed annually, so the trial is your only exit. *Guard:* run the trial on one live requisition with real applicants, and measure interview completion rate and ATS write-back before you pay.
- **"60+ integrations" varies in depth.** Third-party reviews report recruiters still moving information by hand, and field mapping is listed only on Scale and above. *Guard:* in the trial, confirm that score, transcript, and stage change all land in your ATS without a copy-paste.
- **Voice latency and rubric bias are yours to watch.** Reviewers mention pauses while the AI responds, which can push hourly applicants to drop off, and a fluent candidate can outscore a strong, terse one. *Guard:* offer a human-screen path, never auto-reject on score alone, and check score gaps by segment with the [screening score variance audit](/en/workflows/screening-score-variance-audit-skill/). See also [AI screening bias](/en/learn/ai-screening-bias/).
