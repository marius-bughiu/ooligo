HubSpot vs [Attio](/en/tools/attio/) is a generational fork in the road. HubSpot is the mature, marketing-led CRM that scales from solopreneur to mid-market with everything bundled. Attio is the modern, data-model-first CRM built for product-led startups that want their CRM to feel like Notion meets a graph database. Pick the wrong one and you'll either outgrow it in 18 months or pay enterprise prices for a system your reps refuse to log into.

## Where HubSpot wins

- **Marketing and content engine.** HubSpot's marketing hub, landing pages, forms, email nurtures, and SEO tooling are still best-in-class for inbound-led GTM. Attio doesn't ship a marketing automation layer at all.
- **App ecosystem and integrations.** Thousands of native integrations, a mature partner directory, and millions of agency hours of expertise. If you want a contractor to fix something on Friday, HubSpot wins on day one.
- **Sales hub maturity.** Sequences, deal pipelines, forecasting, conversation intelligence add-ons, and quote-to-cash all live inside one bill. Attio is bring-your-own engagement layer.

## Where Attio wins

- **Data model flexibility.** Custom objects without pricing penalties, bidirectional relationships, and a workspace that bends to weird B2B graphs (multi-entity orgs, partners, investors). HubSpot's custom objects start at Enterprise tier and feel bolted on.
- **Speed and UX for product-led teams.** Attio is the CRM your engineers and PMs will actually open. Real-time collaboration, fast search, keyboard-first navigation. HubSpot feels like 2015 SaaS by comparison.
- **Pricing predictability for small teams.** Attio's per-seat pricing is honest. HubSpot's tier jumps (Pro to Enterprise) hide critical features behind walls and surprise you at renewal.

## When to use both / Pricing reality

You generally don't run both. The honest pricing reality: HubSpot's Pro Sales Hub at around 100 USD per seat looks cheap until you need custom objects, advanced reporting, or workflow extensions, which all push you to Enterprise at roughly 150 USD per seat with a 5-seat minimum. Attio is around 34 to 75 USD per seat depending on plan, with custom objects in the lower tier.

## Verdict

- **Pick HubSpot** if marketing is more than 30 percent of your pipeline source, you need an integrated content and email nurture engine, or you have non-technical operators running RevOps.
- **Pick Attio** if you're a product-led B2B startup with a developer or RevOps engineer in-house, and your data model is too weird for a vanilla CRM.
- **Use both** rarely. Some teams run Attio as a relationship graph for partnerships while HubSpot runs the demand engine, but it's a maintenance tax.

The single mistake to avoid: picking Attio because it's prettier and then realizing you needed marketing automation six months in.
