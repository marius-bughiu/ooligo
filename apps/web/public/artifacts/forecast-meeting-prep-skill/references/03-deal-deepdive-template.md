# Manager's optional deal review

Use this worksheet after reading the generated briefing. The runner supplies the snapshot facts. The manager fills the remaining fields from authorized evidence during the meeting.

| Field | Fictional example or entry prompt |
|---|---|
| Opportunity | D-101 · Pine Mill |
| Observed change | best_case → commit, $48,000 → $50,000, close moved from Nov 6 to Nov 20 |
| Question | Which customer evidence supports the category change? |
| Rep response | Record the rep's account with its source and date |
| Decision | Manager confirms, revises or leaves open |
| Owner and next review | Assign a person and date |

A date change establishes the movement in the exported record. Customer intent, deal outcome and forecast probability need separate evidence. Preserve unknowns in the worksheet. Keep access to the completed note consistent with access to the underlying opportunity.
