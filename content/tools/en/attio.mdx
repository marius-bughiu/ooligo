## What it is

Attio is a customizable CRM that now sells itself as "the CRM for agentic revenue." The data model has not changed — you define objects, attributes, lists, and views the way your business runs, without the admin tax Salesforce charges or the opinions [HubSpot](/en/tools/hubspot/) hands you. What changed is everything around it.

Two things moved in 2026. Attio shipped a hosted MCP server and put it on every plan, Free included. And it repriced: Plus went from $34 to $44 per user/month and Pro from $69 to $99, while custom objects — the reason most teams picked Attio in the first place — moved behind the Pro tier.

## Why it shows up in RevOps stacks

- **Agent access is first-party and starts at $0.** The server sits at `https://mcp.attio.com/mcp`, authenticates over OAuth with no API keys, and exposes 40+ tools across records, lists, notes, tasks, emails, meetings, and read-only SQL. It inherits your own workspace permissions rather than running through a shared service account: reads auto-approve, writes ask for confirmation. Per-workspace limits are 100 reads/sec and 25 writes/sec.
- **Workflows are agent blocks, not just if-this-then-that.** Triggers fire on record changes, schedules, webhooks, or manual runs; the library ships 20+ pre-built agents including scoring against your ICP, lead routing, pre-call briefing, post-call follow-up, and enrichment. Logic blocks handle loops, branches, and fallbacks, and you can drop in custom JS or call an external MCP server from inside a run.
- **Enrichment and interaction history are native.** Company records carry employee count, ARR, funding raised, and category; contact records carry connection strength and first/last/next interaction across email and calendar, with no rep logging a call.

## Pricing

Per user/month, with annual billing 20% below monthly:

- **Free** — $0. Up to 3 seats, 3 objects, 50,000 records, 200 emails/mo
- **Plus** — $44 monthly, $35 annual. Up to 10 seats, 5 objects, 250,000 records
- **Pro** — $99 monthly, $79 annual. Unlimited seats, 12 objects, 1,000,000 records, plus Call Intelligence, Sequences, and custom objects
- **Enterprise** — custom, billed annually. Unlimited objects, SSO, SCIM, CSV export limits

Seats are only half the bill. AI runs on credits, in two pools that both reset monthly. **Seat credits** — 100/500/1,000/2,500 per user/month by tier — are personal and fund Ask Attio. **Workspace credits** — 250/1,500/10,000/custom per workspace/month — are shared and fund workflows, AI attributes, and Ask Attio once a member's seat credits run out. Inside a workflow, data writes and integration blocks cost 1 credit each while triggers, logic, and data lookups are free, so cost tracks how much a run actually does rather than how complex it looks.

Extra workspace credits are sold as packs from Plus upward: +5,000/month at $85 ($70 annual), +10,000 at $150 ($120), +25,000 at $330 ($260), +50,000 at $595 ($475). A ten-seat Pro team on annual billing lands at $790/month before credits, about $9,480/year; add a 10,000-credit pack and it is roughly $10,920/year. Free gets no add-on packs at all — when the 250 workspace credits are gone, the automation stops until the month turns.

## Best for

RevOps teams of 5–30 at Series A to C companies that want the CRM and the agent layer on one bill, and that want an agent to read and write pipeline over MCP without a middleware hop.

## Not for

Firms that need Salesforce-grade approval chains and territory governance. Teams whose deciding requirement is SSO or SCIM — both are Enterprise-only, so a 12-person startup with a security questionnaire jumps straight from $79/seat to a quote. And teams buying primarily for outbound: Sequences and Call Intelligence both start at Pro, so Plus is a records tier, not a selling tier.

## Versus the alternatives

- **[Salesforce](/en/tools/salesforce/)** — pick it when the evaluation turns on governance, partner apps, or a data model your finance and support orgs already share. Attio wins on time-to-first-useful-schema; Salesforce wins on what happens at year three with 200 users.
- **[HubSpot](/en/tools/hubspot/)** — Sales Hub Starter is $20/seat/mo against Attio's $44, and Professional is $100 against $99. Pick HubSpot when marketing automation and CRM have to sit on one bill; pick Attio when the object model is the thing you are buying.
- **[Day.ai](/en/tools/day-ai/)** — the fastest-growing entrant in AI-native CRM, and the one that prices the opposite way: per agent at $24/$60/$200, with non-agent users free. Pick Day.ai when the number you want on the invoice is agents rather than people; pick Attio when 10+ humans need real records, reporting, and permissions.

## Watch-outs

- **Custom objects are a Pro feature now.** Plus caps you at 5 objects and blocks custom ones outright, which breaks the classic Attio use case of modeling partners or investors on the cheap tier. Guard: list the objects you need before quoting Plus — if the count is over 5 or any of them is custom, you are buying Pro at $79/seat annual, not Plus at $35.
- **Credits are the variable that bites.** A single high-volume routing workflow charges 1 credit per write and 1 per integration call, and Plus ships 1,500 workspace credits a month. Guard: start on monthly billing, run one full cycle, read the burn in workspace settings, then convert to annual — and use the admin "Set a limit" control to cap how much of the shared pool Ask Attio can drain.
- **The price moved 29–43% in one cycle.** Plus rose $34 to $44 and Pro $69 to $99 against the ladder we tracked in May 2026. Guard: take the 20% annual discount and negotiate a multi-year rate at signing rather than assuming next year's renewal holds.
- **Plus stops at 10 seats.** Crossing that line is not an add-on, it is a tier change to Pro at more than double the per-seat rate. Guard: if headcount plan puts you past 10 seats inside twelve months, price Pro on the first call.
- **MCP inherits the permissions of whoever authorizes it.** An agent connected under an owner account can write anything that owner can write. Guard: give agents their own Attio user with scoped access rather than OAuthing from an admin seat.
