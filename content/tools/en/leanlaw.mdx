## What it is

LeanLaw is time, billing and trust accounting software for law firms that keep their books in QuickBooks Online. It does not replace QuickBooks. It writes invoices, payments and trust transactions into the firm's QuickBooks Online ledger through a two-way sync, so the accountant keeps working in the system they already know. The company is based in Boise, Idaho, and says more than 1,000 law firms use it.

The product now comes in two flavours. **LeanLaw** handles hourly and flat-fee billing. **SettleFlow** handles personal-injury and contingency work: settlement statements, lien tracking and disbursements paid out by ACH. Both include IOLTA trust accounting and payments processed by [Confido Legal](/en/tools/confido-legal/).

LeanLaw is not practice management. It has no docketing, calendaring, document management or intake. The homepage says so plainly: if you run QuickBooks Desktop, another accounting system, or no accounting system, LeanLaw "is not the right fit today."

## Why it shows up in Legal Ops stacks

- **It answers the "keep QuickBooks, skip Clio's price" question.** Small firms whose bookkeeper already lives in QuickBooks Online want legal billing and trust controls without moving the general ledger into a practice management suite. LeanLaw is built for exactly that split.
- **Trust accounting is in every license.** Trust deposits, transfers and three-way reconciliation post to QuickBooks Online, which is the part most generic billing tools get wrong.
- **It sits beside the practice management system instead of fighting it.** LeanLaw says it works next to [Clio](/en/tools/clio/), [NetDocuments](/en/tools/netdocuments/) or other practice management software, and it sells its trust module on its own by exception for firms that keep billing in Clio or Filevine.

## Pricing reality

The live pricing page (checked November 16, 2026) lists:

- **LeanLaw Core / SettleFlow Core:** $40 per user per month, plus the required Trust Compliance module at $25 per user per month. That is **$65 per user per month**, billed annually upfront, with no monthly option.
- **LeanLaw Pro / SettleFlow Pro:** $65 per user per month for firms with 1-2 users, $75 at 3 or more users, on annual terms. Pro adds matter-based accounting, LEDES billing, a fixed-fee desk, the Clerk AI Agent Desk and an MCP connector.
- **Elite:** $100 per user per month for both LeanLaw and SettleFlow at once, with custom reporting and a dedicated customer success manager. Contingency-heavy firms can instead pay $5 per $1,000 of settlement disbursements.
- A one-time onboarding fee, quoted separately. A 3-year prepaid term and a monthly term are also offered for Pro and Elite.

Payments run through Confido at 2.95% for standard cards, 3.45% for American Express and 1% for ACH capped at $30.

In practice, a 5-attorney firm with 2 billing staff on Pro is 7 users × $75 = **$525 a month, or $6,300 a year**, plus onboarding and the firm's own QuickBooks Online subscription paid to Intuit. On Core the same firm pays $455 a month. LeanLaw's own Core-vs-Pro blog post from September 2026 still quotes $55 and $75 per user, month-to-month, so get the current figure in writing.

## Best for

Managing partners and firm administrators at 1-to-30-lawyer US firms, hourly, flat-fee or contingency, whose bookkeeper or outside CPA already runs the firm on QuickBooks Online, and who need trust-compliant billing and collections without buying a full practice management suite.

## Versus the alternatives

**[Clio](/en/tools/clio/)** is the largest practice management vendor. Pick Clio Manage when the firm also needs matters, calendaring, documents and a client portal in one system and is willing to let Clio hold the trust ledger. Pick LeanLaw when practice management is already solved, or not needed, and the real pain is billing and trust in QuickBooks. LeanLaw's homepage claims that firms leaving Clio Manage plus Clio Payments recover $8,000-10,000 a year; treat that as a vendor claim and run your own seat math.

**[MyCase](/en/tools/mycase/)**, from 8am, is the other large suite. It bundles [LawPay](/en/tools/lawpay/) payments and its own accounting. Pick it for the same all-in-one reason as Clio, especially if the firm wants LawPay. [PracticePanther](/en/tools/practicepanther/) and [Smokeball](/en/tools/smokeball/) follow the same logic: their accounting tiers replace QuickBooks rather than feed it.

**AI time capture is the fastest-moving part of this segment**, and it is arriving as add-ons rather than new billing systems. [Billables AI](/en/tools/billables-ai/) and [PointOne](/en/tools/pointone/) draft time entries from email, calendar and documents. Billables AI is LeanLaw's named partner, so a LeanLaw firm adds capture without changing billing.

If none fit, you are either on QuickBooks Desktop or Xero, which LeanLaw does not support, or outside the US. Firms in that position look at billing tools that sync with those ledgers, such as TimeSolv, or at software with its own built-in accounting, such as CosmoLex.

## Watch-outs

- **LeanLaw's own pages disagree on price and terms.** The September 2026 blog says $55 Core and month-to-month on every plan. The live pricing page says $65 all-in Core, annual only. *Guard:* before signing, get a written quote that names the tier, the term, the per-user rate at your seat count, and the onboarding fee.
- **It only works if QuickBooks Online is the system of record.** QuickBooks Desktop and Xero are out, and a messy chart of accounts flows straight into your trust reports. *Guard:* have your CPA clean the QuickBooks Online chart of accounts and trust liability accounts before go-live, and reconcile trust three ways for the first two months.
- **You will still need practice management.** LeanLaw has no calendar, docketing or document system. *Guard:* decide where matters live first. If that is Clio, confirm which system creates the matter and which holds trust, so client money is never tracked in two ledgers.
- **The AI story is mostly partner or beta.** Time capture is Billables AI, a separate product. The MCP connector is in private beta and, like the Clerk AI Agent Desk, is Pro-only. *Guard:* buy LeanLaw for billing and trust. Price Billables AI separately if you want automated capture, and don't pay for Pro on the strength of beta features.
