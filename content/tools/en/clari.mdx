## What it is

Clari is no longer a company. It is **Clari Forecast**, the forecasting product inside [Salesloft](/en/tools/salesloft/). The two companies merged on 3 December 2025 with Steve Cox as CEO, traded for nine months as "Clari + Salesloft", and on 2 September 2026 announced a single brand: Salesloft is the company and the platform, and Clari Forecast is the only product that keeps the Clari name. Since then `clari.com` returns a permanent redirect to `salesloft.com`, and `clari.com/pricing` lands on Salesloft's demo-request page (both checked 13 November 2026).

The rest of the old Clari line has been folded into Salesloft's menu. Inspect is still sold under that name for pipeline visibility; Copilot call intelligence now sits under Salesloft Conversations; Groove is still reachable as a login but is no longer marketed as a separate product; Capture and the Guide action hub no longer appear as named products. If you are evaluating "Clari" in late 2026, you are evaluating a Salesloft contract with a forecasting module in it.

The forecasting method itself has not changed: Forecast rolls up rep, manager and executive calls against snapshotted pipeline, Inspect flags deals whose activity contradicts their stage, and an AI Forecast Agent now summarizes where the number moved and why. What changed is that the product is now sold, roadmapped and supported by the engagement vendor.

## Why it shows up in RevOps stacks

- **Forecast accuracy is what RevOps gets graded on.** Boards judge whether the quarter lands within a few points of the CRO's call. Clari's deal-level variance signals surface slippage early enough to act on, and that is the whole purchase.
- **Activity-grounded scoring, not stage-driven scoring.** Email and calendar activity are captured automatically. A deal with no buyer-side activity in 14 days gets flagged whatever stage the rep set.
- **Forecast data is reachable from an LLM.** The Salesloft MCP server launched on 14 April 2026, was listed natively in [Claude](/en/tools/claude/)'s connector directory in July, and its summer expansion added forecast and deal-inspection data alongside Cadence activity and call intelligence. [ChatGPT](/en/tools/chatgpt/), Microsoft Copilot and Gemini connectors followed. Aviso and BoostUp have no equivalent today.

## Pricing reality

Nothing is published. `salesloft.com/pricing` is a "Talk to Sales" form with no tiers or numbers (checked 13 November 2026). The figures below are third-party procurement estimates; use them as negotiating bands, not list prices.

- **Clari Forecast**: approximately $100-$120 per user/month on annual billing (Docket, April 2026), or roughly $1,200-$1,500 per user/year.
- **Total contract value**: Vendr's data across 291 Clari purchases puts the median annual contract near $76,000, in a $19,065-$415,001 range, with average negotiated savings near 15%.
- **Implementation**: approximately $15K-$75K of professional services over 8-16 weeks, at the top of the band for heavily customized Salesforce instances.
- Annual terms only; multi-year commitments are where the larger discounts sit.

Post-rebrand, the realistic quote is a Salesloft bundle. Forecast-only deals still happen, but expect the account team to price engagement and conversation intelligence against it. Below roughly $10M ARR the economics do not work: the RevOps lead can hold the pipeline in their head, and a median contract of $76,000 is a material share of GTM budget.

## Best for

- RevOps leaders at $50M-$5B ARR B2B companies with multi-region sales teams and a board-visible quarterly forecast.
- Teams already on Salesloft for engagement, for whom Clari Forecast is now an add-on to an existing contract rather than a second vendor.
- Teams that want forecast and pipeline data queryable from Claude or ChatGPT this quarter.

**Not for you if** you run fewer than 50 reps, if your forecasting problem is really a CRM hygiene problem, or if you run [Outreach](/en/tools/outreach/) for engagement and do not want your forecast owned by its direct competitor.

## Alternatives — and when to pick them instead

- **[Gong](/en/tools/gong/)** — the market-share leader in revenue intelligence and the fastest-growing company in the segment, past $500M ARR in May 2026. Pick it when call analytics is the lead requirement and you want forecasting from a vendor that does not also sell your sequencer. See [Gong vs Clari](/en/vs/gong-vs-clari/).
- **[Aviso](/en/tools/aviso/)** — the closest pure-play rival on forecasting depth, with predictive models over historical pipeline behaviour. Pick it when you want a forecasting vendor independent of your engagement stack. See [Clari vs Aviso](/en/vs/clari-vs-aviso/).
- **[BoostUp](/en/tools/boostup/)** — the mid-market price option. Pick it when forecasting is the only job you are buying. See [Clari vs BoostUp](/en/vs/clari-vs-boostup/).
- **[Salesforce](/en/tools/salesforce/) native forecasting** — included with Sales Cloud. Pick it under $50M ARR. The gap is activity-grounded scoring: the native forecast moves on stage changes a rep controls.

## Watch-outs

- **Your forecasting vendor is now your engagement vendor.** If you run Outreach, Clari Forecast's roadmap is set by Outreach's main competitor, and the commercial pressure will be to consolidate. **Guard:** get a written commitment on supported CRM and engagement integrations for the full term, and price a Forecast-only renewal before the bundle conversation starts.
- **Packaging is still being re-cut.** Groove, Copilot and Capture have been renamed or folded in within a year, and Groove and Salesloft Cadence still overlap. **Guard:** list exact SKUs and entitlements in the order form, and add a clause that holds price and scope if modules are repackaged mid-term.
- **MCP scope varies by tenant.** Some third-party reviews still describe the connector as read-only, and forecast data arrived later than engagement data. **Guard:** before you plan an agent workflow, have the AE confirm in writing which forecast and deal objects your tenant exposes and whether write actions are enabled.
- **Integration debt on custom Salesforce objects.** Standard objects map out of the box; heavy customization needs scoped work. **Guard:** budget the full 8-16 weeks, not the one-month number from the sales cycle.
- **Forecast-accuracy theater.** Teams use the tool to manage the number rather than improve the estimate. **Guard:** measure rolling four-quarter forecast variance, not in-quarter convergence, which rewards sandbagging in the final week.
