## What it is

ServiceDesk Plus is the ITSM product of ManageEngine, the enterprise IT division of Zoho Corporation. It is one of the few mid-market service desks still sold in both a cloud edition and an on-premises edition you install on your own Windows or Linux servers, and the two share the same three editions and list prices.

It is a full ITIL desk: incidents, service requests, problems, changes and releases, a service catalog, SLAs, projects, a CMDB and IT asset management, plus enterprise service management instances for HR, facilities and other teams. ManageEngine says it is a Challenger in Gartner's 2026 Magic Quadrant for IT Service Management Platforms, which it describes as the first edition since the report was retired in 2023.

The 2026 releases moved it from assistive AI toward agents. **Zia Agents**, launched in the first half of the year on the cloud Enterprise edition, ship as five prebuilt agents plus Zia Agent Studio, a no-code builder; agents can be started from the Ask Zia assistant or by workflow triggers, and a master agent can hand work to sub-agents. The same releases added CMDB data-quality rules for orphaned, incomplete and stale configuration items, baselines, and CI impact analysis. Cloud customers can connect ServiceDesk Plus to MCP-compatible AI clients through Zoho MCP, and can bring their own Google AI Studio key; on-premises customers can bring Azure OpenAI.

## Where it fits

- **IT desks that must stay on-premises.** Regulated, air-gapped or data-residency-bound organisations that cannot put tickets and a CMDB in someone else's cloud. Freshservice is cloud-only and Atlassian has ended new Data Center sales; ServiceDesk Plus self-hosts at the same list price as its cloud edition.
- **Price-sensitive teams that still want ITIL.** The Enterprise edition includes change, release, problem, projects and CMDB out of the box at a list price below most rivals' middle tiers.
- **Shops already on ManageEngine.** If Endpoint Central, OpManager or AD Manager already run the estate, ServiceDesk Plus links assets, patches and tickets without a third-party connector.
- **Very small desks.** The Standard edition is free for up to five technicians on one instance, which covers a lot of single-site IT teams.

## Pricing reality

ManageEngine's pricing page, marked current as of September 2026, lists three editions with "starts from" per-technician rates: **Standard $13, Professional $27 and Enterprise $67 per technician per month**, billed monthly or annually with a discount for annual. End users are free. Standard is priced on technicians only; Professional and Enterprise also count managed IT assets, so the real figure rises with your estate. Above 10,000 assets you go through sales, and a perpetual on-premises licence is available on request.

What each edition buys matters more than the headline:

- **Standard** is the incident and request desk. No CMDB, and service catalog, problem and project are add-ons.
- **Professional** adds IT asset management. On-premises, problem, service catalog, project and CMDB are each about $1,195 add-ons, and change and release management is $2,395.
- **Enterprise** bundles change, release, problem, projects, service catalog and CMDB, and is the only edition with Zia Agents. Predictive, generative and conversational Zia features are included in every edition at no extra charge.

Real numbers: a 25-technician desk pays about **$3,900 a year on Standard, $8,100 on Professional and $20,100 on Enterprise** at the entry rates, before asset-count uplifts. Against [Freshservice](/en/tools/freshservice/) for the same team (about $14,700 on Growth, $29,700 on Pro, plus Copilot), ServiceDesk Plus Enterprise gets ITIL depth and agentic AI for roughly two-thirds of Freshservice Pro. Annual support on the free edition is optional at $35 per technician; 24x5 support is included in paid plans.

## Best for

IT service-desk managers at 300 to 5,000-employee organisations with 10 to 150 technicians who need ITIL change and CMDB, want the lowest licence cost in the category, or must self-host, and who accept a dated agent interface in exchange.

## Versus the alternatives

The two largest platforms are [ServiceNow](/en/tools/servicenow/) and [Jira Service Management](/en/tools/jira-service-management/). Pick ServiceNow above roughly 300 fulfillers, or when IT, HR, security and customer service must share one data model and you can fund a partner-led rollout. Pick Jira Service Management when engineering already lives in Jira and service requests must link to engineering work items natively.

The closest head-to-heads are [Freshservice](/en/tools/freshservice/) and [HaloITSM](/en/tools/haloitsm/). Pick Freshservice when agent experience and go-live in weeks matter more than price or self-hosting. Pick HaloITSM when you want every module and the AI on one flat per-agent licence and you need FedRAMP Moderate.

The fastest-growing entrants are Slack-native desks such as [Unthread](/en/tools/unthread/). Pick Unthread when most requests start as Slack threads, the team is under about 20 agents, and you do not need change management or a CMDB. For customer-facing support rather than internal IT, use Zoho's own [Zoho Desk](/en/tools/zoho-desk/) instead.

## Watch-outs

- **The interface and speed draw the most complaints.** G2, Capterra and community-forum reviewers describe a busy, dated UI and slow screens, with hosted instances singled out more often than self-hosted ones. Guard: in the 30-day trial, load a realistic ticket and asset volume on the deployment model you will actually run, and time agents on their ten most common actions before you commit.
- **On-premises means you own the patching.** In January 2023 CISA added CVE-2022-47966, a pre-authentication remote code execution flaw in many on-premises ManageEngine products including ServiceDesk Plus with SAML SSO, to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog. Guard: if you self-host, put ServiceDesk Plus behind your VPN or a reverse proxy, subscribe to ManageEngine security advisories, and write a patch window measured in days into your runbook.
- **Edition and add-on maths hides the real price.** The headline $13 buys a desk with no CMDB, and Professional plus add-ons can cost more than Enterprise. Guard: price your required modules and asset count on all three editions in one written quote, and default to Enterprise if you need change management plus CMDB.
- **The newest AI is cloud and Enterprise only.** Zia Agents do not ship on Standard, Professional or on-premises, and MCP access runs through Zoho MCP on cloud. Guard: if agents or MCP are part of the business case, test them on a cloud Enterprise trial instance, not on a demo.
- **Microsoft retired Exchange Web Services for Exchange Online from October 2026.** Older mail-fetch configurations that use EWS stop working. Guard: move incoming mail to the Microsoft Graph connector before migrating or upgrading, and test ticket creation from email on day one.
