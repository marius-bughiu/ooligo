## What it is

Krisp is a bot-free AI meeting notetaker built on top of its noise-cancellation app. You select "Krisp Microphone" and "Krisp Speaker" as the audio devices in Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Slack Huddles, or any other calling app, and Krisp transcribes, records, and summarizes from that audio path. Nothing joins the participant list unless you ask it to — an optional bot covers meetings you cannot attend.

The noise-cancellation heritage is what separates it from the other bot-free notetakers. The same seat that writes the notes also strips background noise from both sides of the call and, since February 2026, converts accents in real time: speaker-side for Indian, Filipino, South African, and Latin American English, and listener-side (what you hear) from March 2026. A separate Call Center AI line sells the same audio stack per agent, and a developer SDK licenses it to voice-AI builders.

## Why it shows up in ops stacks

- **No bot, and it works in every calling app.** Because capture sits at the audio-device layer, Krisp covers dialers, softphones, and browser calls that bot-based tools cannot join. Desktop apps run on macOS and Windows; iOS and Android apps cover in-person meetings and record outbound phone calls.
- **MCP on the cheapest paid seat.** Krisp's hosted MCP server (`https://mcp.krisp.ai/mcp`, OAuth) is included on Core and Advanced. It exposes 27 read-and-write tools — search across meetings and transcripts, read summaries, create and complete action items, manage folders and tags, list upcoming meetings — to [Claude](/en/tools/claude/), [ChatGPT](/en/tools/chatgpt/), [Cursor](/en/tools/cursor/), Gemini, and others. Granola puts MCP on its $14 Business seat; Krisp puts it on an $8 one.
- **Audio quality as a transcription input.** Cleaning the audio before it reaches the transcriber helps most on noisy calls: shared offices, field reps, contact-centre floors.
- **On-device by default for English.** English transcription runs on the machine; noise cancellation and accent conversion never leave it. Recordings and summaries sync to Krisp's AWS cloud (US by default) only once saved.

## Pricing

Per user per month, from krisp.ai/pricing, checked 2026-11-27. Annual billing halves the monthly rate:

- **Free trial — 7 days.** All premium features, no card. There is no permanent free plan any more.
- **Core — $8 annual, $16 monthly.** Unlimited bot-free note-taking, transcription, and noise cancellation; 1 hour a day of accent conversion each way; [HubSpot](/en/tools/hubspot/), [Slack](/en/tools/slack/), [Notion](/en/tools/notion/), [Zapier](/en/tools/zapier/), and Microsoft Teams integrations plus webhooks; MCP; 10 GB storage.
- **Advanced — $15 annual, $30 monthly.** Adds [Salesforce](/en/tools/salesforce/) and ConnectWise, a manager view and company deal grouping, 4 hours a day of speaker-side and unlimited listener-side accent conversion, admin controls on link sharing and note-taking mode, and 60 GB storage.
- **Enterprise — custom.** SOC 2 report access, SAML SSO and SCIM, on-device private mode, unlimited storage, and HIPAA with a BAA for 100+ seats.

Transcripts and summaries cover 16 languages. Teams above 50 seats are routed to sales for volume pricing.

## Best for

- SDR, AE, and CS teams who call from dialers or softphones and need notes, noise suppression, and CRM sync on one $8–$15 seat instead of a notetaker plus a separate noise tool
- Offshore or nearshore teams — BPOs, global support, distributed SDR pods — where accent conversion is a real throughput lever
- Teams that want meeting history readable by an agent over MCP at the lowest paid seat in the category

## Alternatives — and when to pick them instead

- **[Granola](/en/tools/granola/)** — the other bot-free option, and the better-funded one ($192M raised). Pick it when note quality and shared team context matter more than audio: Granola merges your typed notes with the transcript, and its Spaces model is stronger for account history. It also keeps a free tier.
- **[Otter.ai](/en/tools/otter-ai/)** — pick it when the org already owns it or needs a large searchable archive at $19.99 annual on Business. It joins as a bot.
- **[Fireflies.ai](/en/tools/fireflies/)** — pick it for deal intelligence and CRM autofill inside a $19 annual seat, and 100+ transcription languages. Also a bot.
- **[Fathom](/en/tools/fathom/)** — pick it when a free tier must carry real usage; its unlimited $0 plan beats Krisp's 7-day trial.

The rule: pick Krisp when the call happens outside Zoom/Meet/Teams or audio quality is the problem; pick Granola when the notes are the product.

## Watch-outs

- **Nothing announces the recording.** With no bot visible, consent is entirely yours, and two-party-consent states and GDPR require notice. Guard: put a recording notice in the invite template and the call opener before rollout; the optional bot is the visible alternative for external calls.
- **"On-device" has exceptions.** Non-English desktop transcription, mobile recordings, file uploads, and phone-call recordings are transcribed in the cloud by a sub-processor (Baseten). Guard: if on-device processing is the reason you are buying, restrict the rollout to English desktop use or price Enterprise private mode.
- **SSO tier is inconsistent across Krisp's own pages.** The pricing cards list SSO/SCIM under Advanced; the security page and MCP page place it on Enterprise. Guard: get the SSO tier in the order form before you sign Advanced.
- **No free plan to pilot on.** Seven days cannot show whether the archive earns its seat. Guard: run the trial on a defined cohort of 5–10 users with the CRM integration on from day one, then buy monthly for one month before committing annually.
