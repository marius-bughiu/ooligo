## What it is

Remote is a global employment platform: employer of record (EOR), contractor management, global payroll, a US PEO and an HRIS, sold per person per month. Its EOR covers 90+ countries, and Remote says it hires through entities it owns and operates rather than a network of local partners. It is the closest like-for-like rival to [Deel](/en/tools/deel/), and the two publish the same entry EOR price.

Remote is also the EOR you may already be buying without knowing it. [BambooHR](/en/tools/bamboohr/)'s Global Employment add-on and [Personio](/en/tools/personio/)'s EOR (announced 18 March 2025) are both "powered by Remote": hiring starts in the HRIS and Remote is the legal employer. On 27 August 2026 Remote's EOR and contractor products became a Workday Certified Integration on the Workday Marketplace, and Remote runs managed global payroll inside [Workday](/en/tools/workday/) through Global Payroll Connect.

## Why it shows up in recruiting stacks

The recruiting decision Remote answers is the last mile: you found the candidate in Spain or Brazil and have no entity there. Remote issues a compliant local contract, runs payroll and benefits, and handles termination rules. Since 2025 it has also pushed up-funnel with **Remote Recruit**, which searches what Remote calls 800M+ profiles with AI matching, runs AI-assisted first interviews and sends an offer that converts straight into an EOR hire.

Treat Recruit as a sourcing tool, not an ATS. Remote announced a "Recruit ATS" for 2026; as of 26 November 2026 we found no launch. remote.com/recruit returns a 404, the pricing page lists no Recruit price, and Recruit is missing from the site's product menu, although a global-HR overview page still lists it as available. Do not retire an ATS on the strength of it.

In 2026 Remote bought Atlas (expense management, January) and Bravas (identity and device management, April), added in-product AI agents, an MCP server and a CLI, and shut down its standalone Remote Equity Advanced product on 18 March 2026.

## Pricing reality

List prices on remote.com/pricing, checked 26 November 2026, all "starting at":

- **EOR:** $599 per employee per month on annual contracts. Third-party comparisons quote $699, which appears to be the rate without an annual commitment; budget $699 if you will not sign for a year.
- **Global Payroll** (your own entities): $19 per employee per month.
- **Contractor Management:** $29 per active contractor per month. **Plus** at $99 adds Remote-drafted contracts and up to $100,000 indemnity per contractor, capped at $1M per company. **Contractor of Record** at $325 puts the contract with Remote and carries uncapped indemnity.
- **PEO (US only):** $99 per employee per month.

Remote states no setup fees and no standard deposit, but it may ask for a refundable reserve. The real cost of EOR is employer on-costs: statutory contributions, mandatory benefits and the 13th or 14th month salary in some countries, often well above the platform fee. A team of 10 EOR employees pays about $71,880 a year in Remote fees on annual terms before any of that. Volume discounts start in sales conversations, not on the page.

## Best for

Heads of People and TA leaders at 20 to 500-person companies hiring their first few people in countries where they will not open an entity, especially if they already run BambooHR, Personio or Workday and want EOR employees to appear in that system. It also fits finance-led teams that prefer owned-entity EOR and fixed, published per-head fees.

Do not pick Remote if you hire mainly in one country with your own entity (use local payroll), if you need recruiting software (buy an ATS), or if your volume is in contractors in markets where a cheaper contractor-payments tool suffices.

## Versus the alternatives

[Deel](/en/tools/deel/) is the largest player by reported revenue (more than $1.5B on its press page), matches Remote's $599 EOR entry price, covers more countries on its own claims (150+) and now bundles a native ATS on Deel HR. Pick Deel when you want recruiting and EOR in one vendor and a wider country list; pick Remote when owned-entity coverage, Workday or BambooHR embedding, or published contractor indemnity matters more. On G2, Deel Hire rates 4.8/5 on about 5,900 reviews against Remote's 4.5/5 on about 4,000.

G-P (Globalization Partners) is the other incumbent, quote-only and enterprise-oriented; pick it for large, procurement-heavy rollouts. [Rippling](/en/tools/rippling/) is the fastest-growing entrant, adding EOR to its HRIS and IT stack on a quote; pick it when US-heavy teams also want device and app provisioning on the same hire event. Oyster ($699 published) and Multiplier (third-party reports of about $400 to $459) bracket Remote on price.

For sourcing and tracking candidates, a standalone ATS still wins: [Greenhouse](/en/tools/greenhouse/) for structured hiring, [Ashby](/en/tools/ashby/) for analytics and outbound CRM, [Workable](/en/tools/workable/) for SMB budgets.

## Watch-outs

- **Recruit's roadmap is unclear.** The promised ATS has not shipped and the product page is gone. Guard: keep your ATS, and use Recruit only on a monthly term you can drop.
- **The $599 headline assumes annual terms.** Guard: get the monthly-billing rate and the reserve amount in the quote, and model year one at $699 per head if you may offboard early.
- **Embedded EOR splits the experience.** In BambooHR and Personio, payroll and benefits run in Remote, and Personio's flow finishes in a Remote browser tab. Guard: map which system owns time off, payslips and terminations before the first hire, and test one employee end to end.
- **Platform fee is not total cost.** Guard: request Remote's employer-cost estimate per country and compare it with an entity-plus-payroll quote once you pass about 10 people in a single country.
- **Contractor indemnity has limits.** The Plus cover pays third-party claims from Remote's breach of its service warranty, capped at $1M per company. Guard: read the warranty and use Contractor of Record or EOR for workers who look like employees.
